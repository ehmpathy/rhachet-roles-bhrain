## .what

a **doc.kernel** is one concept, distilled from a doc to its atomic form: one idea, irreducible,
portable, and fertile (it can grow into a brief). a doc holds many entangled ideas; to kernelize
it is to harvest them one by one.

- a kernel may define (`a triangle has three sides`) or assert (`backoff alone dedupes a replay`)
- a kernel that asserts a fact a test can refute is a **claim** (`term=doc.kernel.claim`)
