# rule.forbid.claims-with-no-evidence

> **no claim is asserted with no evidence beside it, and no extraordinary claim (the best, the only, the root, never seen) on ordinary evidence.**

razors: `razor=hitchens` · `razor=russell` · `razor=sagan`.

## .the shape

| 👎 | 👍 |
|---|---|
| "this is the root cause" | "this is the cause of the 3 fails read; others unread" |
| "this is the fastest approach" | "this beat the two others tried, on one benchmark" |
| "no one uses that flag" | "no call in the repo passes that flag (`read:` grep); callers outside: unread" |

## .the test

> **what evidence sits beside this claim, and is it as strong as the claim is wide?**

## .enforcement

blocker: a superlative or a root cause asserted with no evidence, and a peer acted on it
nitpick: a superlative where a comparison against the cases read would serve
