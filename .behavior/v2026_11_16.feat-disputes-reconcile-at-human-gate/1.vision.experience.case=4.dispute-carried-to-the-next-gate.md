## .narrative

on `3.blueprint` (no human gate) the driver disputed `rules/i003/blocker.2` at 0.70 onto F12, and
drove on (`case=1`). two stones later, `5.3.verification` holds `approved?`. it reaches the gate.

the halt lists F12 — though F12 was raised two stones back — because the halt reads every open
`owed: human` fulcrum on the route, not only those raised on this stone. the line says where F12
was raised, so the human knows which artifact to open.

the human upholds it. the debt that rode silently through two stones settles at the first place a
human looks.

the same F12, but the human opens it and finds the driver was wrong: two of its rows name things
badly. they concede it. the halt now names the repair owed and the driver's next command. the driver
repairs the names **in the current diff** — never a rewind of `3.blueprint`, which would void every
approval at or after it (`judges/getOneStoneGuardApproval.ts:13`), the very gate the human stands at.

```
when [t1'] the human rules F12 conceded

when [t2'] the driver repairs in the current diff and re-arrives
  then 3.blueprint is not rewound
  then the human approves 5.3.verification once the repair is read
```
