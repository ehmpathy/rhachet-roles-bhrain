## .settled

### the definition sharpens

S05 defined subversive prose as **unfalsifiable**. S06 adds the second half:

> **subversive prose works on the reader rather than informs them.**

⇒ two faces, one defect — prose that spends the reader's attention on aught but a transferable concept:

| face | what it does |
|---|---|
| **unfalsifiable** (S05) | commits to no position, so no reader can disagree |
| **persuasive** (S06) | commits to a position and then **works the reader toward it** rather than lets them judge |

### the mechanism — and it is why this rule cannot fold into the density rules

> **rhetoric and density are indistinguishable on the page. both are short. both are punchy.**

⇒ a terseness pass **does not reduce rhetoric — it concentrates it.** *"make it terser"* optimizes for punch, and punch is persuasion.

**this is what makes the rule non-redundant.** every other rule in the set makes prose shorter, and every one of them makes this defect worse. a rule that grades density cannot grade voice, because after compression the two look identical.

### the taxonomy of instances

| shape | what it does |
|---|---|
| **the invented metaphor** | carries the diagnosis in place of the mechanism, so the reader assents to an image rather than a fact |
| **the absolution** | answers a charge nobody made, which plants the charge as settled |
| **the pre-emptive concession** | raises a shield before the reviewer can strike |
| **the aphorism** | converts a limitation into a philosophy, so a gap reads as design |
| **the self-issued verdict** | ✅/🔴 on the author's own calls — the glyph decides what the reviewer is there to decide |
| **the read-order directive** | steers the review toward the author's strongest exhibit |
| **humility as advocacy** | *"I judged X and took it"* — the modest frame is what makes the call hard to challenge |

### the recursion the wisher named

> *"a reviewer who reads bolded aphorisms and green checkmarks takes the cheaper of two reads. I built the gate and then wrote the artifact in a voice designed to get past one."*

⇒ the cure was authored in the disease's voice. that is the same recursion this vision already names about overflow — *"the reviewer most likely to overflow is the reviewer whose job is to prevent it"* — and **that sentence is itself an aphorism**, so the vision states the recursion in the register the recursion is about.

### the fix — five moves, and it is NOT more cuts

1. **strip every metaphor to its mechanism**
2. **delete every ✅/🔴 attached to my own calls** — verdict glyphs belong to the reviewer
3. **bold only what a reader must not miss**, never what the author wants believed
4. **kill the aphorisms** — state the limitation flat
5. **no directives about what to read first**

🟡 **move 1 has a bound the fix list does not state, and it is worth a line.** the ban is on a metaphor that **carries a diagnosis**. a metaphor that *illustrates* an already-stated mechanism is the `an example illustrates, it does not prescribe` carve-out telepath's seam brief already grants. the defect is a metaphor **in place of** the fact, never beside it.
