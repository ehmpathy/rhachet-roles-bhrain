# domain.term.reason: education.pursuit.course.vitro

## .etymology

latin `in vitro`, in glass: a test outside the live organism, where one variable is held and
the rest are still. its pair `in vivo` names the test inside the live system. the pair names
exactly the two stages a course runs: claims tested alone, then the role assessed on a real task.

rejected:
- `sandbox` — names the place, not the stage; and it carries no pair
- `lab` — names the room; a lab holds both vitro and vivo work
- `playground`, `scratch` — imply no verdict is owed; a vitro claim always earns one

## .evidence

- the narrative move maps "practice joints on scrap wood" to vitro: practice apart from the
  cabinet, before the cabinet
- the vitro verdicts land in the pedigree as held / refuted / untested counts, so a refuted
  claim stays on record rather than vanish

## .invariants

- every claim a course tests in vitro earns one verdict: held, refuted, or untested
- a vitro verdict cites its evidence
