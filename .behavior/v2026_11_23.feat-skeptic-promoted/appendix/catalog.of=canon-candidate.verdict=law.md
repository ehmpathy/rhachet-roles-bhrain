# catalog.of=canon-candidate.verdict=law

three lessons no extant rule holds. each applies trust-no-one, so the foundation test passes: each is an application, and lands as a rule, never beside the basis. all three fill one gap: what a read is worth once it is in hand.

| # | candidate | source | the gap in the canon | proposed rule | its check |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | weigh a read by what it rules out | Heuer, ACH: evidence consistent with every hypothesis has no diagnostic value; weigh each item across all hypotheses, and work to disprove. Sagan: spin more than one hypothesis | `assume-the-first-thought-is-wrong` asks for two ways a take fails. it never asks whether the read that "confirmed" the take would have looked the same if the take were wrong | `rule.always.weigh-a-read-by-what-it-rules-out` | name the rival take; would this read differ under it? no → the read confirms naught |
| 2 | one burden for every claim | Yudkowsky, evenness: "beware lest you place huge burdens of proof only on propositions you dislike"; Wilson on the pseudoskeptic, "a disguised dogmatist", whose doubt guards a preconceived position | the canon doubts the self and the peer. it never asks whether the doubt itself is even: a skeptic who doubts only what it dislikes is a pseudoskeptic with good tools, and every flaw it learns to detect makes it more wrong | `rule.always.weigh-each-claim-with-one-burden` | would this read have moved me as far if it had pointed the other way? |
| 3 | a true fact can mislead by its implication | Feynman, "Cargo Cult Science": the oil that "doesn't soak through food" — true, and no oil does at that heat; the implied contrast is false | `prove-or-mark-each-claim-in-a-yield` proves what a claim SAYS. it does not ask what a reader will infer from it | cue in `prove-or-mark`, or `rule.always.prove-the-implication-not-only-the-fact` | what will a reader conclude from this true line, and is that conclusion true? |

## .what each still owes

- **a foundation test, written.** each row above states the test; the rule file must carry it (`rule.always.razor-the-foundation-from-the-application`)
- **a measure before a mechanism.** ACH itself has little evidence that it reduces bias. so none of these ships a hook until a false-refusal measure exists (the research 12 shape)
- **row 3 may be a cue, not a law.** if the execution seat finds `prove-or-mark` can carry it in one cue row, it lands there. the vision leaves that fork open at clean rework (F22)
