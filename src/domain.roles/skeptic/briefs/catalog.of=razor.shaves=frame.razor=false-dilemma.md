# razor: false dilemma

shaves: a choice or frame · settled by: logic

## .the question

> **do these options cover every case, and which case sits outside them?**

the law is exhaustiveness. a contradictory pair (P or not-P) covers every case; two contraries do not.

## .the source

- a classical fallacy; Sagan lists "the excluded middle, or false dichotomy" in the baloney detection kit, *The Demon-Haunted World*, 1995 (exact text `guess:`)
- read via https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baloney_detection_kit

## .fails when

- the fork is truly binary; say why
- a third option is invented to dodge a real fork
