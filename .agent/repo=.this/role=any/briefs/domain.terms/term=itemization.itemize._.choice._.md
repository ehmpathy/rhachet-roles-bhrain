# domain.term: itemize

term.synonyms.forbidden:

- register
- inventorize
- tabulate

## .what
to **itemize** is to give each entry of a concept its own addressed home — one item, one file, at
its coordinates. it is the act; **inventory** is what it produces.

it sits in the librarian's family of materialization verbs, each paired with the artifact it makes:

| verb | produces | each entry carries |
|---|---|---|
| **itemize** | an **inventory** | a reference |
| **catalogize** | a **catalog** | a reference **+ a preview** |
| **articulate** | an **article** | — (one concept, defined) |
| **demonstrate** | a **demo** | — (one instance, shown) |
| **lessonize** | a **lesson** | — (one concept, taught) |
