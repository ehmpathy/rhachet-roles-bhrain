# 1.vision.experience.dimensions

the experience space of role=achiever, factored into orthogonal axes, then walked. every cell carries
one of four verdicts: **demoed** (a `case=N` file) · **itemized** (a row in `case=_`, no demo) ·
**forbidden** (a rejection invariant, why stated) · **impossible** (cannot arise, why stated).

## .the axes

| # | axis | positions | why it is an axis | why it is orthogonal to the rest |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | actor | commander · achiever · subordinate | each meets the role from a different seat | any actor can meet any duty, moment, or store state |
| D | duty | track · unblock | the two duties the wish names | a duty is what the actor does; the other axes say when, where, and in what state |
| K | input kind | order · correction · constraint · investigate-question · pause/cancel · remark-or-one-line-answer | the extant itemize rule settles which inputs are asks; each kind closes differently | kind is what was said; it is independent of when (M) and of the store (T) |
| M | moment | at talk · mid-work · after compaction · at stop | the four moments a record can be lost or a blocker can be first seen | a moment is when; it applies to both duties (a blocker can arise at any of them) |
| R | record state | absent · open · closed-with-proof · closed-without-proof | lossless means absent and closed-without-proof are both not-closed | applies to both duties: an ask has a record, and so does a blocker (the goal it bears on). independent of C: an open blocker may carry any cure route, none included, so the R × S walk splits `open` by C rather than fold C into R |
| C | cure route | run · dispatch-with-hour · above-charter · none | the wish names three cures and forbids the fourth | a cure is chosen per blocker, whoever's it is and whenever it arose |
| H | dispatch hour | before · past · none (forbidden) | a dispatch is a promise with a due time | only a dispatch has an hour; H is a sub-axis of C=dispatch, walked under it |
| S | whose blocker | self · subordinate | the wish says "self and subordinates" | ownership is independent of the cure chosen |
| T | store state | present · absent dir · scope forbidden (on main/master) · corrupt line · unwritable | the record lives on disk; each state is a way the disk can lose it | the store's state is the environment, independent of what was said or done |
| P | topology | one session · two sessions on one scope · subordinate on its own scope | the store is shared on disk; the role boots into "any clone" | how many writers share a store is independent of what each writes |

### duty = track — K × R (at stop, store present, one session)

| order | demoed (case 2) | demoed (case 1) | demoed (case 1) | demoed (case 3) |
| correction | itemized — same path as order (case 2) | itemized — as case 1 | itemized — as case 1 | itemized — as case 3 |

| pause/cancel | demoed (case 10, before it is covered) | demoed (case 10: `deferred` with a when); itemized (`declined` with a why, the same path) | forbidden — a close by proof is refused on a check-less kind; it closes by the goals it names | forbidden — a close with no when (defer) or no why (decline) is refused (case 10, `[t2]`) |

the two forbidden columns are the latent sub-axis the walk surfaced: **has a done check?** a
check-less kind closes by a stated why (no-work) or by the goals it names (pause), never by proof.

## .what the walk found that a free-list would miss

- **closed-without-proof** (case 3): the extant store lets `fulfilled` carry any reason
- **has a done check?** a latent sub-axis of K: pause and remark close by a why, never by proof
  (cases 10, 11)
- **scope forbidden on main** (case 12): the store refuses, the talk hook crashes, and the stop passes
- **a gate that fails open**: a corrupt line or an unwritable store crashes a hook with exit 1, which
  claude code reads as permission. both now fail closed
- **a subordinate report with no goal** (case 13): no hook reads the radio, so lossless needs the stop
  to read inbound radio tasks
- **past the hour across a compaction** (case 8): an overdue dispatch is first seen at boot
