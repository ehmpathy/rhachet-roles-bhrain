> **the two axes of a brain get the same treatment on every surface.** the test is parity with the
> choice: where the choice is recorded and rendered, the effort is too; where it is not, neither is.

the choice IS recorded (`DriveBrainInheritance`) and rendered (`brain = <slug>`) on an inherited
stone, so the effort is now both.

🟡 the axes still carry through on different terms, and that is a fact about the domain rather than
an exception to parity: a slug outlives an `/effort`, but a level does not outlive a `/model`, since
an effort is model-scoped (`S10`).
