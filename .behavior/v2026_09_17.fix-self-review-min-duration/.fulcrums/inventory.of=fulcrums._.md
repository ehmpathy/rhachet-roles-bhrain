## 🔴 .`F06` is the round's live proof that a MOOT marker is the dangerous one

`r5` left a check here: *"for every resolved fulcrum, ask what open fulcrum could change what its
answer is worth."* **`F06` is its first catch, and it fired within a day.**

`F06` was marked 🌙 **moot** on one premise — *"`S11` removes the elapsed measurement, so no threshold
remains to configure."* `S13` restored the measurement, so a 30s threshold exists again and `F06`'s
fork has its operand back, unchanged.

⇒ 🟡 **a 🌙 MOOT marker is more dangerous than a ✅ RULED one.** a ruled fulcrum invites a re-read of
its *verdict*; a moot one asserts *the question itself is gone*, so nobody re-checks the premise that
killed it. **mootness is a claim about another fulcrum's answer, and it must be re-verified whenever
that answer moves.**

🔴 **the rule that covers both: when a fulcrum's status moves, re-read EVERY term in its question,
not merely the one the latest utterance touched.** a fulcrum is a sentence, and an utterance rarely
addresses more than one of its nouns.
