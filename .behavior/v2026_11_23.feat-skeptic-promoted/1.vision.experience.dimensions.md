# 1.vision.experience.dimensions

## .two spaces, not one

the role meets two kinds of actor at two kinds of moment, and the pairs do not cross:

- **runtime** — a seat (a brain in a turn) meets the role at each hook event of a turn
- **lifecycle** — a maintainer or a consumer repo meets the role as files: boot spec, seeds, release

a seat never edits seeds or ships a release; a repo never has a turn. one product of actor × moment
would bar those cells by construction, and a barred block that large hides a split. so each space
gets its own product, and every axis inside it is orthogonal to the rest.

## .runtime space — actor = seat

| axis | positions | why orthogonal |
|---|---|---|
| moment | boot · prompt (the turn opens: the human's words, or an instinct flag delivered by `rhx clone say`) · take (a diagnosis, design, or claim forms) · act (a tool runs) · reply (the turn ends at Stop) | the five points a turn passes through. the skeptic ships no hook at any of them; boot is the brief load, and a take is cued by a brief (`case=4`, `case=6`). the instinct reads at reply and speaks at the next prompt. a seat reaches every moment whatever roles sit beside skeptic and whatever the outcome |
| feel | happy · sharp | any moment can pass or go wrong, under any co-tenant set: a +driver seat meets a second onStop hook, yet still passes (`case=8` `[t2]`); a +subconscious seat is flagged (`case=2`) or met with silence (`case=3`). the outcome fixes neither the moment nor the roles beside it |
| co-tenant | alone · +learner · +driver · +subconscious | which roles share the seat is set at enroll, before any moment; it changes what boots and which hooks run beside the skeptic, never which moments occur |

co-tenant inclusion rule: a co-tenant is a position only if it changes what the skeptic meets. four
seams qualify — the boot cues, a second onStop hook that holds a turn, a second onTool hook, and an
outside reader of the reply — and four positions close the set:
- **+learner** — the skeptic's cues cite learner rules; without it they point at briefs not booted.
  learner also registers an onTool guard (`getLearnerRole.ts:27`), so this position carries the act
  seam too
- **+driver** — stands for every role that registers a second hook at reply or act: driver (onStop
  and onTool, `getDriverRole.ts:46, 72-77`), telepath (onStop, `getTelepathRole.ts:35`), achiever
  (onTool, `getAchieverRole.ts:32`). a seat with any of them meets a peer hook beside the skeptic's
  at the same event, the seam `case=8` walks at reply, so each collapses into this position
- **+subconscious** — the skeptic's instinct. its onStop hook never holds a turn; it spawns a reader of
  the reply's output tokens, and the reader's flag opens the next turn (`case=2`, `case=9`). it is the
  only co-tenant that reads what the seat said and answers it. a seat with +subconscious beside
  +driver meets both seams at once, which `case=8` walks
- any other registry role registers no hook at reply or act and adds no cue the skeptic cites, so it
  folds into whichever position it sits beside

product: 5 × 2 × 4 = 40 cells, none barred.

## .lifecycle space — actor ∈ {maintainer, consumer}

| axis | positions | why orthogonal |
|---|---|---|
| actor | maintainer (edits the role in bhrain) · consumer (a repo that enrolls the release) | both touch the boot spec, the seeds, and the release; neither position excludes a moment |
| moment | boot (the boot spec is measured) · seed (a seed is added or checked) · release (a version ships or is swapped in) | each moment applies to each actor |
| feel | happy · sharp | each actor meets each moment's pass and its refusal: a maintainer's seed is refused (`case=5`) or passes; a consumer's installed-copy check is refused or passes. the outcome fixes neither the actor nor the moment |

product: 2 × 3 × 2 = 12 cells, none barred.

total: 52 cells across both spaces. walked in `1.vision.experience.case=_.md`.

## .latent state, named

**resource state** (a `.seeds` dir in a role present / absent) shows up only on lifecycle × seed. it is
folded into `feel` there, since each such cell has one sharp form.

**instinct state** shows up only on runtime × +subconscious, at reply and at the next prompt. it is
folded into `feel`, each state walked in the case named:

| state | feel | walked |
|---|---|---|
| a claim backed by a cited record, hash matched | happy | `case=10` `[t0]` |
| a claim with its basis stated (`read: … · open: …`, `guess: …`) | happy | `case=3` |
| a claim with no basis, no record | sharp | `case=2`, `case=6` |
| a cited record whose source has moved | sharp | `case=10` `[t1]` |
| a cited record that does not exist; no `.skepticism/` yet | sharp | `case=10` `[t2]` `[t3]` |
| a false flag on a backed claim | sharp | `case=9` `[t3]` |
| no live clone, or the instinct's call faults | sharp | `case=9` `[t4]` `[t5]` |
| a flag rate below the bar | sharp | `case=2` `[t5]` |

## .boundaries (where sharp cells cluster)

- what the instinct reads: the reply's output tokens, never a tool call, input, or prompt
- a claim that moves work or reaches a peer vs. one that does neither (the instinct asks of the first only)
- a cited record vs. its source: a hash compare, deterministic, before any brain
- the flag rate vs. the bar the replay set: below it, flags go to the log only
- the onStop seam: the subconscious never holds a turn; a co-tenant's onStop may (`case=8`)
- delivery: a live clone gets the flag; any other seat gets the log, and no error
- the loop guard: a flag opens a turn whose reply wakes the instinct again; one flag per claim per session, no chain (`case=9` `[t2b]`)
- the boot budget vs. the clamp
- a seed's landed path vs. the role dir after a rename
