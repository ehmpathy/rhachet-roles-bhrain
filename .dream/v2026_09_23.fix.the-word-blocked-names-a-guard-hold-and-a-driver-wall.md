# 🌙 dream: part the guard hold from the driver wall — they share the word `blocked`

- kind  = fix
- owner = `ehmpathy/rhachet-roles-bhrain`
- caught 2026-09-23

## .the cue that fired

`has-ergonomics-validated` @ `5.3.verification` scored the captured cli i/o against
`def.ergonomic`'s *unambiguous* criterion, and one word failed it in both directions at once.

the live capture, verbatim:

```
$ rhx route.stone.set --stone 5.3.verification --as passed

🗿 route.stone.set
   ├─ stone = 5.3.verification
   └─ passage = blocked (review.self required)
   …
🪨 run solid skill repo=bhrain/role=driver/skill=route.stone.set
   └─ ✋ blocked by constraints
```

⇒ one command, the word twice, ~100 lines apart, and **the two senses summon different parties.**

## .the gap, precisely

`'blocked'` names **two concepts** on one surface:

| the sense | what it means | who must act | how it is written |
|---|---|---|---|
| a **guard hold** | the guard's conditions are unmet; the driver's own next command clears it | the **driver**, at once | `status: 'blocked'` **plus** a `blocker` |
| a **driver wall** | the driver hit a wall and cannot proceed (`--as blocked`) | 🔴 a **human** | `status: 'blocked'` with **no** `blocker` |

the checkable evidence, all first-party:

- `src/domain.objects/Driver/PassageReport.ts` — `status` accepts `'blocked'`, and `blocker?:
  RouteStoneGuardBlockerType` is the only discriminant between the two senses
- `src/domain.objects/Driver/RouteStoneGuardBlockerReport.ts:24-31` — `RouteStoneGuardBlockerType`
  holds **seven** values (`review.self`, `review.peer`, `review.peer.exhausted`,
  `review.peer.unabsorbed`, `review.peer.undeclared`, `judge`, `approval`) and 🔴 **the driver wall
  is not among them**
- `src/domain.objects/Driver/RouteStoneDisposition.ts:11` — `'blocked' → a driver wall (--as blocked);
  a human must clear it (✋)`, so the **disposition** type reads the word in sense 2 only
- `src/contract/cli/routeStatusLine.acceptance.test.ts:411,443,471,500` — four fixtures write
  `status: 'blocked'` with `blocker: 'review.self' | 'review.peer' | 'approval' | 'judge'`, so the
  ledger genuinely carries sense 1 far more often than sense 2

### the harm

`passage.jsonl` is durable and human-read. a reader cannot part *"the guard holds, carry on"* from
*"a human is needed"* without a second field, and no surface prints that field. so a ledger scan,
a status line, or a hook that keys on `status === 'blocked'` conflates a routine state with an
escalation.

⚠️ and `rule.forbid.domain-term-ambiguity` is exact about the root: **an overload hides an ABSENT
DISTINCTION.** the second sense was never granted its own word.

## .the shape of the fix

two candidate directions, and the choice is the fulcrum this dream carries:

**A — split the word.** keep `'blocked'` for the guard hold; coin a distinct status for the driver
wall (`'walled'`, `'halted'`, or `'stuck'` — `rule.forbid.domain-term-synonyms`' dispute procedure
settles which). the cleanest read, and the most expensive:

- `PassageReport.status` gains a member; every reader of the union must handle it
- every route's `passage.jsonl` on disk carries the old word ⇒ a **read-time** compatibility shim,
  or a migration, is owed
- `RouteStoneDisposition.why`, the status-line renderer, the hook disposition, and every snapshot
  that prints the word all move together

**B — qualify in the RENDER only.** leave the stored status alone; make the two senses visually
distinct wherever they print — e.g. `passage = held (review.self required)` for sense 1 and
`passage = blocked (driver wall)` for sense 2, derived from the `blocker` field's presence. cheaper,
reversible, and it leaves the ledger ambiguous for any consumer that reads the file rather than the
render.

⇒ 🔴 **the discriminant already exists and is reliable** — `blocker` present = the guard held the
road, absent = the driver did. so either direction is a read of a field that is already correct;
what is owed is a **name** for what that field already distinguishes.

🟡 **start with the glossary, not the code.** `domain.terms/` has no `term=route.stone.passage.blocked`
cluster. `rule.require.domain-term-itemization` wants the word itemized and its two senses
enumerated **before** a split is chosen — and `rule.forbid.domain-term-ambiguity`'s own measured
lesson applies here in full: *"enumerate the senses of the word you keep BEFORE the inconsistency
settles"*, because afterward the word you would have reached for is forbidden.

## .why it is not done in this round

the SAFE/CLEAN test (`rule.always.fix-forward-under-scouts-honor`), answered honestly:

| question | answer |
|---|---|
| is it **SAFE**? | 🔴 **no.** a rename of a `PassageReport.status` value changes a durable on-disk ledger format that every prior route already wrote |
| is it **CLEAN**? | 🔴 **no.** it ripples into the status-line contract, the hook disposition, the judge, and every snapshot that prints the word — files this round never opened. that is the *smuggled refactor* shape |
| is it inside this round's **delta**? | 🔴 **no.** the word predates this round in every file that holds it; this round wrote no new `blocked` semantics |

⇒ so it is a **dream plus a fulcrum** (`F23`), never a silent pass. and the ONE half that did pass
both questions was fixed in-round: `PassageReport.ts:12`'s docblock declared `'blocked'` to mean the
driver wall **alone**, which is false and misleads every reader of the ledger. that comment now
states both causes and names the `blocker` test. **a one-line comment with zero ripple is a fix; a
ledger format is a dream.**
