# case=4 · the lane that goes quiet

- **coordinates** = `reviewer-runs × disputed × live`
- **feel** = happy · **care** = critipath · **verdict** = impossible → demoed

**acceptance #2's cell**, and the one F04 turns on. *"the disagreement consumes no budget."* this
demonstrates what that costs and what it buys.

## .the narrative

*driver, on `5.1.execution`, round 3, right after a dispute*

I disputed the architect on round 2. now I re-arrive to let the mechanic re-check a fix.

```
🦉 the path is clear

🗿 route.drive
   └─ review round 3
      ├─ architect (l1) : disputed — F007, skipped this generation   2 rounds, 4 budget
      ├─ mechanic  (l1) : ran                         3 rounds, 4 budget  ███░ approved
      └─ ergonomist(l1) : ran                         2 rounds, 4 budget  ██░░ approved
```

🟡 **the rows above are a SKETCH; the live render is multi-line.** `formatGuardReviewerTree.ts:126-134`
emits `r$index: $slug (l$level, $rounds/$budget)` as a header, then a detail block beneath it —
verdict, blockers, nitpicks, `given:`, `taken:`. the single-line columns and the `███░` bar are this
document's shorthand for the meter, never the engine's shape.

🔴 **and beneath that shorthand sits a real gap: a skipped lane has NO state to render as.**
`ReviewerTreeState.state` is a **closed union of six** — `inflight | awaits | queued | finished |
malfunction | constraint` (`formatGuardReviewerTree.ts:61-87`) — and a lane the driver took a stance
on is none of them. the nearest is `queued`, which renders *"awaits arrival"*: **exactly the misread
this case's own `.why it is a happy path` forbids.** ⇒ the emit requirement stated below is a
**contract change to a shared formatter** (`review.by` reuses it via `hideMeter`), never a copy
tweak. `F04` now carries that cost.

the architect **did not run**. it is not exhausted — it has two rounds left. it is quiet because I
took a stance on it, and a stance ends that conversation **for the generation it answers**.

**what I gained:** this round costs three lanes rather than four. the point I disputed is not
re-argued, and the argument I already made is not re-typed.

**what I gave up, and I want it on record:** until I change the artifact, I can edit
`setStoneAsDisputed.ts` freely, break exactly the boundary the architect was there to guard, and no
lane will raise it. ⚠️ **the window is one generation wide, never the stone** — the moment I touch
the artifact, the architect returns and reads what I did.

🔴 **and that is what makes the trade legible rather than merely survivable.** the blindness ends on
**the same event that creates the risk**: an edit. ⇒ the seam is unwatched exactly while it is
still, and watched again the instant it moves.

**the aha:** *a dispute is not a mute button on a critic. it silences one ANSWERED point, for one
generation of the artifact — and the critic returns to grade whatever I do next.*

## 🔴 .the same cell, from the REVIEWER's side — where `happy` becomes `sharp`

⚠️ **one cell, two actors, opposite feels** — and the catalog's actor × feel grid names this the
sharpest edge in the behavior. the driver's walk above is a `happy` path. the same walk, told from
the lane that goes dark, is the `sharp` one — and until `experience-coverage` nitpick.3 it was
argued in the catalog's prose rather than rendered here, where a reader of this case would meet it.

*architect lane, `5.1.execution`, round 2 → the close*

I read the diff and I found a real seam: `setStoneAsDisputed` writes a **guard** artifact and sits
in `stones/`, so the next hand to add a guard invariant will not think to look there. I raised one
blocker and I named the file and the line.

the driver answered me — a genuine `.taken`, with seven peer paths cited. **I do not get to answer
back on this generation.** the driver declared a stance, and on round 3 I am not summoned. I have two
rounds of budget left and neither is spent this round.

**what I still hold:** my `.given` sits on disk, unedited, and the fulcrum names me. the council will
read the driver's argument beside my blocker, and one of us will be wrong on the record.

✅ **and what I get back, which `S03` restored:** the moment the driver changes the artifact, I run
again. I read the new state, and I raise whatever is **new** — including, if it is there, the very
break the driver made in the seam I flagged. **the room did not empty; it recessed.**

**what I still lose, and it is what remains of `F04`:** for the span of one generation the driver may
edit the file I flagged and I will not see it until the next. and I may not re-raise **the disputed
point** — the driver re-declares against it, at the cost of one command.

🔴 **the aha, from this side:** *I was not overruled and I was not deleted. I was answered on one
point, and I keep every other point I have not yet made. what I cannot do is repeat myself — which
is the only thing the driver was trying to stop.*

⚠️ **its fail-safe is now partly mechanical, and that is the change worth recording.** three
mitigations: the fulcrum row (social — the council reads it) · the **per-generation return**
(🔴 mechanical — I come back on the event that creates the risk) · the **per-stone horizon**
(mechanical — the next stone re-enrolls me at full budget). ⇒ under the per-stone skip only the
third was hard, and that is why `F04` carried `dirty`. **the label stands, on a smaller hole:** what
is `dirty` now is the **repeated** dispute, where a driver re-declares every generation and reaches
per-stone blindness one round at a time.

## .the bdd timeline

```gherkin
given a stone `5.1.execution` with peer reviewers `architect`, `mechanic`, `ergonomist`, budget 4
  and the mechanic and ergonomist are approved
  and the architect's latest given raises exactly ONE concern     # 🔴 so "the lane" and "the concern" coincide
  and that concern is `disputed` at rounds 2                      # B = disputed, C = live

[t0] given a fresh stone where every lane's given is clean
     when the driver runs --as passed
     then the round runs, and every lane executes                 # the baseline, unchanged

[t1] given the architect is `answered` and NOT yet disputed
     when the driver runs --as contemplated --that architect, then --as passed
     then the architect DOES run                                  # ✅ answered × live runs
      and its meter reads rounds 2

[t2] given the driver then runs --as disputed --with architect --about blocker.1
     when the driver runs --as passed, with the artifact UNCHANGED
     then the architect does NOT execute                          # 🔴 impossible cell — acceptance #2
      and its meter still reads rounds 2, budget 4                # no budget consumed
      and the emit says "disputed — F007, skipped this generation"
      and it NEVER says "exhausted"                               # 🔴 a distinct word

[t3] given a human then overrules the architect's level
     when the driver runs --as passed, artifact still unchanged
     then the architect STILL does not execute                    # 🔴 the skip survives a forgive
      and the fulcrum row remains for the council

[t4] given the driver edits the very file the architect flagged
     when the driver runs --as passed
     then the review hash moves, so a NEW generation begins
      and the architect DOES execute                              # ✅ S03 — the lens returns
      and it reads the edit, and may raise it                     # 🔴 the blindness ENDS here
      and it mints a NEW .given, so the old stance no longer covers
      and the driver must answer, then declare again              # ⇒ one command, and the SAME --why path

[t4b] given the driver re-disputes on that new given, and again on the next
      when this repeats every generation
      then the lane is dark on every round it would have spoken   # ⚠️ per-stone blindness, one round at a time
       and passage.jsonl holds N `disputed` rows for one slug      # 🔴 countable
       and NO gate reads that count                                # ⚠️ the residual F04 carries

[t5] given the route advances to the NEXT stone
     when its guard runs
     then the architect executes at full budget                   # the outer horizon, unchanged
```

🔴 **`[t2]` versus `[t3]` is the shape of the claim.** a *forgive* is the human's word; a *dispute*
is the driver's. they are independent, they compose, and neither undoes the other. the council reads
both.

## 🔴 .the question this case's `given` had to DODGE — and no artifact answers it

the `given` above pins the architect to **one** concern so that *"the lane"* and *"the concern"*
coincide. **at two concerns they come apart, and F04's skip predicate has no stated answer:**

| the candidate | what it does at 1 disputed of 2 concerns |
|---|---|
| **the lane is skipped once ANY concern is disputed** | 🔴 the undisputed concern goes unheard for a generation — a driver mutes a live point by a quarrel with its neighbour |
| **the lane is skipped only once its residual tally CLEARS the threshold** | ✅ coherent with `S06`'s tally exclusion — the lane goes quiet exactly when it no longer holds the road |

⇒ **the second is almost certainly right**, since `S06` made the mechanism a tally exclusion and a
skip is downstream of *"does this lane still reject?"*. ⚠️ **but `F04` was argued and confidence-rated
under the per-LANE grain, before `S07`**, so its 55% prices a predicate the design no longer has.

🔴 **this is a fulcrum the board does not carry.** filed as `F024` — *does the per-generation skip key
on a concern or on the lane's residual verdict?* — and it is **owed before 2.1.criteria**, since the
skip is what acceptance #2 is measured on.

⚠️ **and they do not share a grain.** a dispute is per-REVIEWER (`F01`); a forgive is per-LEVEL —
`getOverruledReviewerSlugs.ts:31-36` filters on `review.level`, never on a slug. so `[t3]`'s
*"overrules the architect's level"* lifts the **mechanic and ergonomist too**. the skip still
survives, which is what `[t3]` asserts; what the line understates is the blast radius. ⇒ the
invariant is stated at the right grain already — *"a stance is owed iff … its level is unforgiven"*
— and this note exists so a reader of this case alone does not model a per-lane forgive.

🔴 **`[t4]` is this case's whole subject: the lane RETURNS on the edit.** it goes quiet for exactly
one generation, and *how long* is the quantity every argument about this cell turns on.

🔴 **`[t4b]` is the cost that survives, and it is why `F04` keeps `dirty`.** a driver who re-declares
at every generation reaches per-stone blindness one round at a time. **the ledger can see it** — N
`disputed` rows, one slug, one stone — and no gate reads that count, so the restraint is social. the
wisher priced the per-command friction as the brake (*"so it'll have to mark `--as disputed` each
time"*); `[t4b]` is what that friction does **not** buy.

🟡 the confidence itself lives in `F04` and in the inventory's row, and is deliberately **not copied
here** — it has already moved once (65% → 55%, on measured evidence), and a copy goes stale on the
next move with no signal to the reader.

⚠️ **`[t5]` is the OUTER of two horizons.** blindness ends at the next generation (`[t4]`) and,
failing that, at the next stone. ⇒ the per-stone bound is the **backstop**, never the mitigation.

## .why it is a happy path

the driver is told, plainly, what did not run and why. `rule.require.status-feedback` (ergonomist):
*"a mutation that does not report what changed"* is a blocker — so the emit names the skip with its
own word, never as a silent absence and never as `exhausted`.

⚠️ **`disputed` and `exhausted` must not share a rendered word.** they look identical on the meter —
a lane that did not run — and they differ in every respect that matters: one is the meter's verdict
and reversible by a top-up, the other is the driver's and is not.

🔴 **and that requirement lands in ONE operation.**
`formatGuardReviewerTree` is the single render source for every reviewer row on every surface, and
`exhausted` earns its distinction there — a `🌙` glyph at `:200` plus the `terminal — does not block
higher levels` line at `:225-227`. **a `disputed` lane owes the same treatment in the same place**, so
this line is a claim about a seventh union member and its detail block, never about prose. ⇒ the
reuse table now carries the operation.
