# razor: law of the instrument

shaves: a choice or frame · settled by: rivals (cures from other layers, named)

## .the question

> **did the tool I hold pick the shape of the problem I see, and what would a cure from another layer look like?**

## .the source

- Abraham Kaplan, *The Conduct of Inquiry*, 1964, owns the law: "Give a small boy a hammer, and he will find that everything he encounters needs pounding"
- Abraham Maslow, *The Psychology of Science*, 1966, owns the hammer-and-nail line
- neither coined the image (*Once a Week*, 1868; Tomkins, 1963)
- read via https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_of_the_instrument

## .fails when

- the tool at hand is the right one. the bias sits in the first reach, not the tool

## .run by

`rule.always.skepticize-the-layer-of-a-cure`
