# domain.term.choice.reason: externalize.entrait

## .etymology
`en-` + `trait` — the family's own prefix, applied to a noun this repo already declares
(`term=artifact.trait`).

⇒ **it is derived, never invented.** the wisher supplied the pattern and the member fell out of it,
so this cluster records a derivation where most record an argument.

## .the law it was derived from — 2026-09-06

the wisher stated the family's construction rule in five words:

> enbrief -> brief; entrait -> trait

⇒ generalized: `en$X` produces a `$X`.

| verb | produces | declared before the law was stated? |
|---|---|---|
| `enbrief` | a brief | yes |
| `enskill` | a skill | yes |
| `entool` | a tool | yes |
| `entrait` | a trait | no — it is the law's first application |

🟡 **three members obeyed a rule nobody had written.** the law was latent in the extant set, and the
utterance did not create it — it read it off.

## .why not each forbidden synonym

| rejected | why |
|---|---|
| entrain | 🟡 the sharpest collision, because it is already spoken here — *"entrained onto all technical prose"* (`define.simplified-technical-english`, mechanic). it names the act of a rhythm imposed by an outside driver, so its subject is the behavior; `entrait` names the artifact the behavior falls out of. and it breaks the law: `en$X` produces a `$X`, and there is no `trai` |
| instill | names a slow, repeated transfer with no artifact at the end. an entraitment is one file, written once |
| habituate | names acquisition by repetition, which is what a declared trait is written to avoid |
| internalize | names what happens in the reader, never what the author produced. every verb in this family names an act the author performs |

🟡 **`entrain` is the one a careful reader will still reach for**, and the law is what settles it
rather than taste: a verb whose product has no name cannot join a family named for its products.

## .the amendment it forces on `enumerate-before-you-name`

`rule.require.enumerate-before-you-name` bars a word tested against one instance. `entrait` was
graded n=1 on a first read — one utterance, no second traveler — and deferred.

⇒ **that read was wrong, and the law is why.** `entrait` is not tested against its own single use;
it is tested against a declared pattern with three prior members, and it satisfies the pattern
exactly.

> **a member of a declared family is not an n=1 coinage. the family IS the enumeration, and it was
> walked before the member was named.**

🟡 so the rule's test needs a second branch: *"list every instance the word must cover"* assumes the
word is the unit under test. where the *pattern* is the unit, the enumeration is the family's
extant members, and a candidate passes when it fits them.

⇒ caught as a dream rather than amended here — a canon rule belongs behind `1.vision` approval.

## .the boundary against its three peers

| verb | the climb | it retires |
|---|---|---|
| `enbrief` | 0 → 1 · 🧠 → 📚 | the derivation |
| `enskill` | 0 → 2 · 🧠 → 💪💧 | the sequence |
| `entool` | 2 → 4 · 💪💧 → 💪🪨 | the execution |
| `entrait` | **no climb — an orthogonal axis** | the recall |

🟡 the tidy read is that `entrait` is rung 5, since its imagine cost per use is zero. it fails on
direction: the three above move work OUT of the brain into an artifact a reader invokes; entraitment
moves it IN, below decision, and the artifact is never touched again after boot.

⇒ **an artifact you never touch cannot sit on a scale whose unit is cost per touch.** the full
argument is `term=artifact.trait._.choice.reason.md`.
