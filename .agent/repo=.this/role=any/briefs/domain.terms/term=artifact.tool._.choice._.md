## .what

any externalized product a future traveler uses instead of re-derives. **the umbrella over every
rung of the entoolment ladder** — a 📚 brief, a 💧 fluid skill, a 🔩 rigid skill, a 🪨 solid skill.

| the word | what it names |
|---|---|
| **tool** | any rung. **the umbrella** |
| **skill** | the mechanized rungs, 2 through 4 |
| **brief** | rung 1 — prethought knowledge, applied by a brain every time |

🟡 **`tool` is never the contrast against a brief.** a brief IS a tool, so *"a brief and a tool"*
reads as *"a brief and a brief"*. the word that contrasts with a brief is **skill**.

## 🟡 .the rungs differ by IMAGINE COST — never by a coined unit

the ladder ranks its rungs by rhachet's own measure: **imagine cost = time + tokens**, plus the
**risk** a probabilistic step lands wrong. name those directly.

🔴 **`brain-turn` is forbidden.** it reads as a unit of imagine cost and is not one: turns differ
by orders of magnitude, so a count of them carries neither the time nor the tokens — the two
quantities you need to shorten a call. it is an indirection between the reader and the root cost
(`.reason`, the 2026-08-30 dispute).

⇒ the tell that the two senses have drifted: **one sentence in which the word appears twice, in
both senses.** it reads as coherent until a reader asks which sense each is.
