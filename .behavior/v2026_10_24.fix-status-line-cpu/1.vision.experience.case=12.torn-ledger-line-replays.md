# case=12 — a degraded line replays its row on each hit (sharp · critipath)

## .narrative

a peer meter ledger holds a torn line. the uncached line rendered a degraded phase and printed one
stderr row that names the torn ledger. the cached line must say no less: the degraded line is
cached WITH its row, so each hit prints the line and the row again, never a line that reads clean
over a broken ledger. the ledgers sit in the fingerprint, so when the driver repairs the line the
next repaint misses at once and renders clean, with no row.

## .bdd sketch

```
given('[case12] a bound seat whose peer meter ledger holds a torn line')
  when('[t0] the seat repaints twice')
    then('the second repaint renders naught, and prints the degrade row again')
  when('[t1] the ledger is repaired')
    then('the next repaint misses at once, and prints no row')
```
