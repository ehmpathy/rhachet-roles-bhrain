# F9 — switch visibility is one output line, never a per-stone ledger

**rework** = clean · **status** = open · **confidence** = 85%

## .the fork, stated fairly

case 8 makes the switch a **critipath**: a prescription nobody can verify is a preference, not a
control. how much record does that require?

- **a line** — the stone-entry output names the brain and its provenance
- **a ledger** — a durable per-stone brain record, beside `passage.jsonl`, queryable after the drive

## .taken, and why at the time

**a line.**

- `rule.require.status-feedback` already demands it and bounds it: *"a mutation that does not report
  what changed = blocker"*. the line satisfies the rule exactly; the ledger exceeds it
- neither the wish nor the issue asks for a ledger. to add one is a **scope leak**, and
  `rule.forbid.scope-leaks` is a live rubric on this route
- `rule.prefer.wet-over-dry` — the durable-record need has one usecase today. wait for the second

## .the counter-case, stated fairly

⚠️ **a line lives in a transcript, and a transcript is not queryable.** case 8's foreman asks their
question *later* — after a defect ships, possibly after the session is gone. a line answers a reader
who was there; a ledger answers one who was not.

and the route already keeps `passage.jsonl` — a per-stone append-only record. the brain is arguably a
property of a passage, so the ledger is less invented than it first appears.

⇒ that is a genuinely strong counter, and it is the reason this sits at 85% rather than higher.

## .rework, and why

**clean, and additive.** a ledger can be added later with no change to the line, the guard schema, or
the hook. naught is hardened against its absence.

⚠️ one caveat: `rule.forbid.timestamps-in-route-artifacts` binds any such record — a brain ledger may
carry a date, never a time.

## .confidence, and why 85%

the scope argument is strong for **this route**. the 15% is the counter above: if a foreman's real
question is asked days later, the line does not answer it and the ledger is the honest deliverable.

⇒ caught as a dream rather than dropped, so the next traveler finds the argument already made:
`dreams/v2026_09_09.amend.a-passage-record-cannot-attribute-a-brain-to-a-stone.md`.
