# domain.term.choice.reason: prose.condensation

## .etymology
latin `con-` (*together*) + `densāre` (*to make dense*) — **to make dense by a gather-together**.

⇒ the physical sense is exact and it carries the whole claim: vapour condenses to water. **the same
substance, less volume — and the vapour that did not condense is gone.** so the word already names a
lossy concentration, which is what parts it from `compression`.

## .the boundary against `compression` — and why it is not a synonym

this was the sharpest call in the cluster, and the two words both survive:

| | its subject | what it removes | lossy? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **compression** | a line | words that carried no distinction | ❌ lossless by construction |
| **condensation** | a passage | lines that rank below the bar | lossy by design |

⇒ **they cannot be one concept, because their lossiness runs opposite.** a lossy compression is a
defect; a lossy condensation is the act performed correctly.

so the repair for each is opposite too:

| the defect | the repair |
|---|---|
| a compression that dropped a distinction | a **shorter, more precise** cut — never a restored paragraph |
| a condensation that kept a below-bar line | cut the line |

the wisher settled the compression half outright, 2026-09-04, verbatim:

> this is false; lossless compression does not increase ambiguity. infact, rambled prose increases ambiguity

⇒ `compression` is therefore owed its own cluster and is named in the arrears at
`term=prose._.choice._.md`. this cluster declares the boundary from its side so the next traveler
does not merge them.

## .why not each forbidden synonym

| rejected | why |
|---|---|
| summarization | a summary is a derived artifact that sits beside the original. condensation replaces the draft; there is no second file |
| distillation | a **declared term** as of 2026-09-06 — `term=prose.distillation`, which names decompose **plus** condense. condensation is that word's second move alone, so to reuse it here would name a whole for one of its halves |
| abridgement | names a shortened edition of a whole that still exists. same defect as `summarization`, in a formal register |
| prune | a horticultural verb for the removal of what is dead or harmful. condensation cuts lines that are true, tight, and merely useful — alive, and cut anyway |
| reduction | carries the pejorative *"reduced to less than it was"*, and it names a magnitude rather than a rank |

## .the two senses inside `condense`, and why they are ONE concept

the word appears twice in the canon, and a reader may take them for two:

| where | the act |
|---|---|
| `distillation`'s second move | state each fundamental in the fewest words that keep it whole |
| `reflexive-condensation` | cut each line below the 95% bar |

⇒ **one concept, two subjects.** both are *"gather what carries the concept; drop what does not"* —
the first applied to a node in a tree, the second to a line in a draft.

🟡 what parts them is *when* they fire: the first runs as the concept forms, the second on a draft
that already exists. that is a difference of moment, never of act — so
`rule.forbid.domain-term-ambiguity` does not fire, and one cluster serves both.

### the measured lossy cut, which is what forced the compression boundary

| the artifact | what it says |
|---|---|
| `contract.reviewer-output.md:102` — the source | *"a skip writes no **log** artifacts, so it advertises no log dir"* |
| its `.md.min:77` — **what boots** | *"a skip writes no artifacts"* |

⇒ two words removed, one distinction destroyed. the min is what boots, so the source stayed correct
and every reader held the false form.

🟡 **that cut would read as a successful condensation and it is a failed compression.** without the
boundary declared, the bar would have been cited to defend it.

## .invariants
- the bar grades importance, never length
- a cut that drops a distinction is a failed compression — the bar never licenses it
- a line that is true, tight, and merely useful is still below the bar
- a caveat a reader must act on is not the author's to rank
