## narrative

a director seat starts. it boots role=any, learner, driver, librarian. before, those three roles
cost 66.6k of the seat. now each `.md.min` holds only the rule's cue: the claim, the test, the
when/then table, and the enforcement line. the seat does not notice a change, and that is the
point: no rule it used to see is gone, and the bill for each call is about half.

against the file list, not against the seat's feel.
