## .the gap, precisely

- after a driver lands a conceded repair, `route.drive` prints the same halt. the driver cannot tell
  "not repaired" from "repaired, awaits the human". an annotation in the fulcrum would change its
  content hash and reopen the verdict
- `read:` prints a path relative to the route dir; from the repo root on a real route
  (`.behavior/v…/`) a human cannot open it as printed

## .the shape of the fix

- a driver lever, e.g. `rhx route.fulcrum.repaired --case F<n>`, appends a passage row; the halt
  then reads "repair landed, awaits approval" and asks only the human. the row never lifts the
  debt; an approval after it still does
- the dispute lines take the route path and print `read:` joined to it; one snapshot at a
  non-`.` route turns red first (i007 r010 nitpick.5)
- the lever's stdout names what is left for the human: the count of disputes still owed, and
  "now approve" at the last one; its conceded line speaks to the human, not the driver (i007
  r010 nitpick.3)

## .why it is not done in this round

the lever is a new contract with its own journey; the path change ripples into every halt snapshot.
