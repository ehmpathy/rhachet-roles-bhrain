# 🌙 dream: route.drive onStop re-blocks a halt only a human can lift

kind: fix · owner: rhachet-roles-bhrain, role=driver · caught 2026-11-03

## .the cue that fired

on `feat-achiever-no-excuses`, stone `1.vision` sat with every peer lane approved, judge 1 passed,
and judge 2 at "wait for human approval". each stop, `route.drive --when hook.onStop` exited 2 and
re-printed the whole stone, three times in a row, while the clone had no move left.

## .the gap, precisely

the onStop hold does not tell a driver-actionable halt from a human-only one. when the only open
gate is `approved?`, a re-block spends a turn and a full stone render per stop, and buys naught.

## .the shape of the fix

when the stone's only open gate is a human approval (and the driver has already arrived, with every
lane absorbed), the onStop hook prints one line with the approve command and exits 0. clamp: a route
whose only open judge is `approved?` → onStop exits 0, and the line names
`rhx route.stone.set --stone <s> --as approved`.

## .why it is not done in this round

it is the driver role's code, not the achiever's, and this route is nano and scoped to the achiever.
