# F04 · the reviewer is a PEER review, never a self review

- **rework** = clean · **status** = open · **confidence** = 70%

## .the fork, stated fairly

the wish leaves it open: *"whether the reviewer is a peer review or a self review"*.

| option | what a guard does with it |
|---|---|
| **peer** | runs `rhx review.by`, tallies a numeric verdict, spends a per-reviewer budget |
| **self** | halts and demands the driver articulate findings into a named path, then `--as promised` |

## .taken, and why at the time

peer.

- a self review has no overrule and no budget — `rule.always.bear-every-self-review` states the count is the work, never a budget, and no lever exists to waive one
- ⇒ a prose self-review on *every* stone that emits a yield is an unbounded demand across the whole fleet
- the defect this reviewer catches is precisely the one a self review cannot catch. `case=3` measures it: six rounds of author re-read caught none of what a reviewer caught in seven passes for under five cents
- ⇒ **a self review here would ask the author to supply the very attention whose absence is the defect**
- a peer verdict is numeric and parseable, so a guard tallies it with no sub-brain (`contract.reviewer-output`)

## .rework, and why

**clean.** the reviewer's kind is a guard declaration. a switch to self is an edit to the guard's review block.

## .confidence, and why it is not higher

70% — the lowest of the structural calls — because:

- a peer reviewer costs a brain call **per arrival**, and `case=4` shows the cost is this reviewer's tightest constraint. a self review costs zero brain
- the wisher may want the cheaper shape on low-value stones and the peer shape only on `1.vision` and `5.x`
- ⇒ **an unpriced third option is what holds this at 70%** — a hybrid: self on the early stones, peer on the yield-heavy ones
