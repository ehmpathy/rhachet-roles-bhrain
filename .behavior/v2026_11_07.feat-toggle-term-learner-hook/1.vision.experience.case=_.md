# 1.vision.experience.case=_

the inventory over `1.vision.experience.dimensions.md`. keys: V verb · S prior state · T target ·
L layout · K caller · R session role · X concurrency · E after-event. an axis left out of a row is
at its default: L `split`, K `any`, R `repo actor`, X `solo`.

## .actors × feel

| actor | happy | sharp |
|---|---|---|
| **human** who wants the glossary kept fresh in their repo | one `enable`, one commit; every later learner session nudges on a stale stop (critipath · case=1, case=3) | a target it cannot safely write is refused with the fix, no write (critipath · case=6) · a bad verb names the three (critipath · case=7) |
| **clone** in a session — runs a verb, or is nudged by the hook | `get` answers in one short tree (the state, the file, and while on the hook, its cost, whose stops it reaches, and when it takes effect), no write (critipath · case=2) · a stale stop holds with the distill guide (critipath · case=3) | the change it makes does not reach its own live session; the output says so (critipath · case=1 t2) |
| **human or clone** who turns it off | `disable` removes it, and a re-run is a no-op (critipath · case=4, case=5) | a stray hand copy of the withdrawn hook under the learner's author is removed too (alterpath · case=5) |
| **developer or clone** in a repo that unlinked the learner after `enable` | — | the leftover entry costs one shell test per stop and never holds or errors; `get` and the fix name the cleanup (critipath · case=9) |
| **developer or clone** in a repo that never asks | sessions stop exactly as they did before the upgrade (critipath · case=8) | — none: no verb runs, no write, no refusal |

## .impossible cells

| cell | why | kind |
|---|---|---|
| `S ≠ off · T = absent` | an absent file holds no entry | nature |
| `S = any · T ∈ {link, dir, outside-repo, unreadable, not-a-dir, malformed, unwalkable}` past the refusal or the fault | the verb refuses before it reads S, so S is never observed | nature |
| `L = old · T = link` | the old layout means `.claude/settings.json` is the real file | nature |
| `V = get · E ≠ none` | `get` writes naught, so no after-event follows from it | nature |
