# 🌙 dream: a lane's tally scales with its partition count, not with its defects

kind: repair · owner: bhrain · caught 2026-10-12

## .the cue that fired

5.3 i003, after the guard split each lane into ~22 partitions. `ergo-contract-snapshots` went from
2 blockers and 2 nitpicks (i002) to 9 blockers and 17 nitpicks (i003). most of the new concerns are
first raises on code no earlier round graded, one or two per partition. the stone tally went from
23 to 54 blockers on one round, and no defect in the diff explains that rise.

## .the gap, precisely

- each partition reviewer is told to find concerns in its slice, and a slice of a few files yields
  one or two concerns of its own almost always — the floor per call is not zero
- the merge sums them, so the lane's count scales with the number of partitions
- the judge reads one stone-wide tally, so a finer split raises the bar the driver must clear,
  with no change to the code under review
- ⇒ the split meant to cut cost and overflow also multiplies driver work per round

## .the shape of the fix

1. measure first: per lane, concerns per partition vs partition size, over i002 and i003
   (`rhx review._.cost --of run` holds the per-partition rows; extend it with a concern count)
2. candidates, to weigh against the measurement:
   - pack small partitions together up to a token floor before the pour, so a lane runs as few
     calls as fit the window (fewer calls also cut the common-prefix re-read)
   - dedupe concerns across partitions in the merge by rule + location
   - ✅ state in each partition prompt that zero concerns is a valid and expected verdict for a
     slice — landed in `asPromptTailVariable` (the peer partitions block), clamped in its test.
     whether it moves the tally is unmeasured; the measure in step 1 now reads its effect
3. clamp: a fixture lane split in N vs 2N partitions over the same defects yields the same tally

## .why it is not done in this round

SAFE yes, CLEAN no: the pack and the dedupe change the partition contract and the merge, and the
measure must come first so the fix is chosen on evidence. this round's debt is owed first.
