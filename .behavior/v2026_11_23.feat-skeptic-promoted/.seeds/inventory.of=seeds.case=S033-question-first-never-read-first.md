## .settled

- the role overfit on reads: it framed every doubt as "find the read". the unit of doubt is the question, not the read
- to skepticize a claim is to ask the question that would show it wrong, then answer it by what settles it: a read, logic alone, or the rivals weighed
- some questions leave one answer (logic forces it); others leave rivals, which are named
- a read is one answer, never the frame; a read can itself imply a single solution, which is questioned in turn
- the persona is rewritten structurally on this philosophy, not patched line by line
- every skeptic brief is skepticized for coherence, density, and consistency, against that philosophy
- razors are tactics of skepticism, each a question put to a claim
