# domain.term: archive

term.chosen   = archive
term.kind     = noun
term.boundary = route
term.synonyms.forbidden:
- rejected
- discarded
- graveyard
- attic

## .what

the route directory that holds **the ideas considered and ruled OUT**.

```
$route/archive/
```

a yield states what was decided. the forks that were weighed and not taken live here, so a
later reader can see that a question was asked, and answered, rather than never raised.

## 🔴 .the pair — it is defined against `appendix`, never alone

| dir | holds | the decision it backs |
|---|---|---|
| `appendix/` | the evidence beneath a decision | one that **IS** in the yield |
| `archive/` | the ideas considered | ones that were **REJECTED** |

⇒ **the axis is the fate of the decision, never the age of the document.**

⚠️ argue the two together or the opposition is lost. this file and
`term=route.appendix._.choice._.md` are one settlement in two files.

## ⚠️ .an archive is not a deletion, and not a chronicle

it holds a **rejected idea**, stated as an idea. it does not hold the story of the round
that rejected it — git holds that (`rule.always.yield-the-output-not-the-archaeology`).
