# domain.term.choice.reason: enbrief

## .etymology

**`en-` + brief** — the english prefix that makes a verb of transformation into a target form
(*encode*, *enshrine*, *entrust*). to enbrief is to make a brief of what was only known.

`brief` is already this org's declared artifact kind (`.agent/repo=*/role=*/briefs/`, and the
`say`/`ref` boot tiers), so the target form is not a metaphor — it names a real directory, a real
boot manifest entry, and a real delivery path into every consumer's context.

## 🔴 .the boundary gap — `term.boundary = UNSETTLED`, and it is shared by the whole family

`rule.require.boundary-qualified-terms` asks *"$word, of WHAT?"* and demands a one-word answer.
**for `enbrief` there are two candidates, and neither is settled:**

| candidate | the frame it takes | what it gets right | what it gets wrong |
|---|---|---|---|
| `knowledge.` | the **artifact** — what the brief holds | matches the librarian's taxonomy, where a brief is a document kind | says not one word about the *act*, and the `en-` family is verbs |
| `externalize.` | the **act** — a move from one head into a shared artifact | matches the learner's extant `rule.always.externalize.*` rules | names a broader act than the ladder does; a `sweep` externalizes too |

⚠️ **do not settle this per-term.** the same gap binds `enskill`, `entool`, `pave`, and `learn` —
they share one ladder and one **verb-shape**, so a boundary guessed for one would fracture a family
that is coherent today. `rule.require.boundary-qualified-terms` says an unnameable boundary is a
**discovery gap, not a name gap**; this is that, and it is recorded rather than guessed.

⇒ **and the gap is free to carry today**, because none of these words holds two senses in this
repo. the filename stays flat until one does (`rule.require.boundary-qualified-terms`, `.what`).

## ✅ .2026-08-31 — `seed`, `tool`, and `skill` LEFT this family, and the reason sharpens it

three clusters carried this same *"the externalization family shares one gap"* note and did not
belong to the family. `term=artifact` was paved and each settled at once:

| the term | its kind | its boundary |
|---|---|---|
| `seed` | **noun** — a file the repo keeps | `artifact` |
| `tool` | **noun** — the artifacts a traveler reuses | `artifact` |
| `skill` | **noun** — the mechanized rungs | `tool` |

⇒ **the family is verbs.** the shared gap is real for `enbrief`/`enskill`/`entool`/`pave`/`learn`
because a verb's boundary names *the act's own subdomain*, and the fork there — `knowledge.` (what
the artifact holds) vs `externalize.` (the act) — is genuinely open. **a noun in the same
neighbourhood answers in one word**, and each of the three above did.

⚠️ **the lesson is `rule.require.enumerate-before-you-name`'s, one level up:** a gap claimed from
**adjacency** is a gap that was never checked. read the cluster's own `.what`, never the family it
sits near.

## .the family — enbrief, enskill, entool

⚠️ **the SET's index lives at `catalog.of=externalization._.md`, never here.** the table below is
kept because it carries this cluster's own *why* — the pattern `enbrief` is eldest in. a reader who
wants *"which verb do i reach for?"* opens the catalog, which also records the `enroute` gap
(`rule.require.catalog-is-an-index`).

`enbrief` is the **eldest**, and the pattern the other two were coined to join (2026-08-13). the
family is not a stylistic rhyme; it is a **partition of the externalization surface by which climb
each verb names**:

| verb | the climb | retires | brain cost that remains per use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **enbrief** | 0 → 1 · 🧠 → 📚 | the derivation | read + interpret + apply |
| **enskill** | 0 → 2 · 🧠 → 💪💧 | the sequence | follow the steps |
| **entool** | 2 → 4 · 💪💧 → 💪🪨 | the execution | **none** |

⇒ they are three concepts, never synonyms, because **each retires a different cost**. see
`philosophy.entoolment-is-the-pinnacle` for the ladder this table is the spine of, and
`term=externalize.entool._.choice.reason.md` for the enskill/entool pair judgment.

⚠️ **two claims this table must NOT be read to make.**

1. **that `entool` targets "a tool".** it does not, because **every rung is a tool** — a brief is
   pavement a traveler uses instead of re-derives, exactly as a solid skill is. the rungs differ in
   imagine cost per use, never in tool-hood (`term=entool`)
2. **that each verb supersedes the one above it.** a higher rung does not retire the rungs below
   it — many solid skills rest on extensive briefs, or on a prior less-deterministic skill. so a
   brief still carries weight at rung 4, and *"we entooled it, so the brief is spent"* is the
   misread this note exists to block

**why the family matters for the glossary:** a coherent prefix family teaches itself. a traveler
who meets `entool` for the first time and already knows `enbrief` infers the shape without a
lookup — which is itself the dereference benefit `term=inventory` argues for, applied to
vocabulary rather than filetrees.

## .why not the rejected synonyms

- **summarize** — names a *reduction of an extant text*. enbrief often materializes a concept that
  was never written down at all (a lesson, a settled dispute, a discovered constraint). the input
  is comprehension, not a document
- **document** — the sharpest line, and the same one `enskill` draws. `document` is generic across
  all three family members, so it cannot mark any one of them; it also reads as prose-about-a-topic
  rather than the durable artifact itself. it is a **forbidden synonym of both `enbrief` and
  `enskill`**, on the same grounds
- **writeup** — informal, and names the act's output casually rather than its form. breaks the
  `en-` family shape (`rule.prefer.symmetric-term-pairs`)
- **distill** — the near-miss worth an explicit note. `distill` names the **concentration** step —
  what the learner does to reduce a round's noise to a lesson — which is a *precondition* of
  enbrief, not a synonym for it. one can distill and never enbrief: the lesson is understood, and
  no file exists. that gap is precisely the arrears this org's learner role exists to pay, so the
  two must stay distinct

## .disputes

none. the verb predates this capture — it composes the thinker's declared skill operations
(`brief.articulate`, `brief.catalogize`, `khue.instantiate`) and names the librarian's
`kno301.doc.enbrief.*` tactic cluster. the glossary merely records, at last, a word the code
already carried across its contract surfaces.

## .evidence

- **code**: `stepArticulate.ts`, `stepCatalogize.ts`, `stepInstantiate.ts`, and
  `getThinkerBrief.Options.codegen.ts` all compose the verb in declared thinker skills;
  `librarian/boot.yml` declares the `kno301.doc.enbrief.*` tactics at say-level
- **the librarian's own taxonomy** names the four materialization mechanisms — *articulate*
  (articles), *catalogize* (catalogs), *demonstrate* (demos), *lessonize* (lessons) — which
  `enbrief` sits above as the general act. this is why the `en-` prefix was already productive
  here before `enskill` and `entool` were coined to join it
- **the wisher confirmed the family (2026-08-13)**: *"enbrief, enskill, entool are all common
  terms"* — which surfaced this cluster's absence. **the eldest and most-used of the three was the
  one with no glossary entry**, exactly the drift `rule.require.domain-term-itemization` predicts:
  a word so common it is never questioned is a word nobody thinks to itemize
- **invariant:** `enbrief` is a verb of THIS org's practice vocabulary — it composes declared
  thinker operations and names a librarian tactic cluster — not generic english like `write`
