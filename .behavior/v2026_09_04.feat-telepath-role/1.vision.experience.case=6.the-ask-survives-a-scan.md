# case=6 · the ask survives a scan

**cell** = `reader · brief · sharp · complete · message`
**feel** = sharp · **care** = critipath (`freq` = every message that carries an ask · `cost` = the work halts, silently, with no party aware)
**issues** = #407

## .why the ask earns a privilege no other passage gets

every other passage is information. miss it and the reader is merely less informed.

**miss the ask and the work stops — and neither party knows.** the sender believes the ask was made; the reader saw a wall of analysis and priced the whole message as analysis. both then wait on each other.

that asymmetry is the whole argument.

## .why terseness does not close this

telepath's output half already mandates *"maximize the point, minimize the words."* a message can satisfy that in full and still bury its ask.

**brevity compresses the prose; it does not promote the ask out of it.** a dense message hides an ask as well as a bloated one — arguably better, since there is less visual variation for it to stand out against.

⇒ this is a **separate axis**, and **no extant telepath brief reaches it** — the full booted set is enumerated below, so a reader can check the claim rather than take a count on faith:

| brief | its axis |
|---|---|
| `rule.require.answer-what-they-meant` | the input half — read the actor correctly |
| `rule.require.place-a-passage-by-its-subject` | **where** a passage sits |
| `rule.require.generic-governs-structure-never-voice` | the structure/voice seam |
| `rule.forbid.subversive-prose` | whether a passage asserts a **disputable claim** at all (wired at `i018`) |
| **absent** | **what a passage must LOOK like when it is an ask** |

## .the narrative

the human has a broken tmux colour and four other tabs open. a message arrives from the supervisor. they do not read it — they scan it, and that is the whole condition this cell is written under.

```
🦉 diagnosed. the pane inherits a 256-colour term from the outer shell.

⭐ ASK — run this, then tell me what it prints:
   tmux display-message -p '#{client_termname}'

the detail, if you want it: `2.7.tmux` sets `default-terminal` but the outer
shell exports `TERM` after it, so the inner value loses.
```

**one glyph in the message carries a treatment, so the scan lands on it.** the human reads six words and knows the shape of what is wanted: run one command, report back.

⇒ the decision they make is whether this is worth an interrupt, and they can make it in the two seconds a scan affords. one command, one line back, and the trailer says outright they may skip the reason.

🟡 they choose to run it — and the choice is theirs rather than extracted. the message did not hide the cost or bury the ask under the evidence for it; it put the price on the surface and let them price it.

what the human is spared is a second read. in the before-state below they refused three times, and the refusal was never unwillingness — **they could not find what was asked of them**, so the message read as work dumped on them rather than a question put to them.

## .the fail-safe — two of three are reachable, and the third is a platform limit

a sharp critipath owes fast · loud · teaches-the-fix.

### the mechanism, verified 2026-09-04

a `Stop` hook receives **`last_assistant_message`** — *"the complete text of Claude's final response message in this turn"* (`code.claude.com/docs/en/hooks.md`, Stop Hook Reference). the slot is live in this repo with two users and a `timeout: 5` budget convention (`.claude/settings.json:253-271`).

⇒ so a watcher can read the emitted message, detect an unstarred ask, and **block the stop** — which forces a correction turn rather than a silent end.

| obligation | met | how |
|---|---|---|
| **loud** | | the stop is blocked. the turn does not end quietly, which is the exact inversion this cell suffers |
| **teaches the fix** | | the nudge names the rule and the treatment |
| **fast** | ❌ **unreachable** | `Stop` fires **after** emit; `MessageDisplay` fires at display time; a `PreRender` hook is an open feature request (`anthropics/claude-code#61152`), not a shipped surface |

### why `fast` is a platform limit, never a scope choice

**no shipped hook reaches a message before a human reads it.** so the ask is buried, the reader prices it as analysis, and the earliest any mechanism can intervene is after that has already happened.

⇒ **an unreachable obligation is not an undischarged one.** the fail-safe this cell shows is the strongest that exists: the failure is caught within one turn and corrected in the next, rather than left to surface *"minutes to days later"* when nobody replies.

🟡 **and the correction is real, not cosmetic** — a blocked stop means the sender re-sends with the ask starred, so the reader's *second* read carries the treatment. the loss is one turn, never the whole exchange.

### the D5 consequence, stated plainly

per `1.vision.experience.dimensions.md`, a reviewer reads files. this cell's subject is a message. so:

| mechanism | reaches a live message? |
|---|---|
| a **reviewer** | ❌ structurally — there is no file to grade |
| a **brief** | 🟡 yes, and it **demonstrably failed** — three consecutive asks, all with headers, all missed |
| a **subconscious** (#414) | the only one that reaches a message at all — **after** it is emitted, never before |

🟡 the before-state above is the disproof of the brief-only answer:

- telepath's canon was booted for all three messages
- ⇒ a booted rule did not fire under load, three times in a row
- ⇒ and a human had to repeat the correction

⇒ **so the brief is not the fail-safe. the nudge is.** the brief is the rule; the nudge is what makes it fire.

## 🟡 .the harder half, left open on purpose

**an ask is not merely an imperative sentence.**

| utterance | is it an ask? |
|---|---|
| *"run X and tell me the output"* | yes — an act |
| *"i ran X"* | ❌ no — a report |
| *"you may want to check X"* | yes — softened, still an act |
| *"i could not do X"* | **yes** — an ask in a report's clothes |
| *"which do you prefer, A or B?"* | yes — a decision |

that fourth row is the one that hides. a blocked report **is** an ask, and it reads as information.

⇒ this taxonomy is real work. whether it splits into two briefs — *what counts as an ask* and *how it must appear* — is settled in `1.vision.yield.md`.

## 🟡 .the seam this must not cross

`⭐` in the narrative above is **this repo's voice in an example**. the rule itself must prescribe a structural property — *the ask occupies a dedicated block a scanner cannot mistake for analysis* — and say no word about which glyph fills it.

per `rule.require.generic-governs-structure-never-voice`: a telepath example written in the adopter's voice is a **false positive**; a telepath **rule** that prescribes a glyph is a **blocker**.
