## .the cue that fired

two peer lanes (`mech-failhides` r002, `behavior-intent-coverage` r008) refused at i010 on a context
overflow. the diagnosis put the target corpus at **471.8k tokens** against a reported **75.2%** of a

## .the gap, precisely

`runStoneGuardReviews` expands `$conversation` to a comma-joined list of every prior `.given`,
`.report`, and `.taken` for the stone, minus the current `{iteration, hash}` generation. **there is
no depth flag, no count bound, and no byte bound.**

checkable evidence, on this branch today:

```sh
rhx grepsafe --pattern 'conversation' --path src/domain.operations/route/guard/review
tree --du -h .behavior/v2026_09_20.review-cost-meter/.reviews/peer
```

`.reviews/peer` measures **~1.4M chars**. at `estimateTokenCount`'s `Math.ceil(chars / 4)` that is
**~350k tokens** — **33% of the window**, before a single rule or target byte is added.

⇒ and it is **monotone in rounds**: every round appends a `.given`, a `.report`, and a `.taken` per
lane, so the floor rises on every iteration and never falls.

🔴 **this is the branch's own subject, one layer out.** `0.wish` is *"make the review prompt's spend
MEASURABLE, then ATTRIBUTABLE, then CHEAPER"*, and the census already names `conversation` as one of
its nine inputs. what it does not do is **bound** it — so the one input that grows without limit is
measured and unclamped.

🟡 **and `--focus pull` cannot reach it.** pull collapses the *target* payload to a path list; the
conversation rides in whole under either focus. so the lever that repaired i010 buys headroom and
does not remove the trend.

## .the shape of the fix

🟡 **the cheapest cut comes first, and it needs no depth policy:** collapse a malfunctioned
`.given` to its one-line header (`malfunction — no verdict`, round, lane). it drops ~60% of the
measured conversation mass and removes zero argument. then a depth clamp, as below.

a `--conversation-depth <n>` clamp on `rhx review`, defaulted, that keeps the **most recent `n`
generations** and drops the rest.

three properties it must have, and the third is the one that is easy to miss:

1. **recency-ordered** — the newest generation is the one a reviewer needs to judge a repair; the
   oldest is the one whose code no longer exists
2. **whole-generation granularity** — a truncation mid-`.taken` hands the lane an argument with its
   evidence cut off, which is worse than its absence
3. 🔴 **it must SAY it truncated.** a reviewer that re-raises a point answered three rounds ago is a
   reviewer that never saw the answer, and it will read as a reviewer defect rather than a bind
   defect. the prompt owes a line: *"N earlier generations omitted."*

🟡 **the cache angle makes the default non-obvious, and it cuts the way a first read does not
expect.** `getAllTargetsByCacheStability` sorts the corpus so stable content leads; the conversation
rides in the **tail** (lever D). a depth clamp that drops the OLDEST generations keeps the tail's
*newest* content — which is the content that changes every round. ⇒ a clamp could raise the hit rate
(less mass behind the prefix) or lower it (the retained slice is the volatile one), and **nobody has
measured which**. that measurement is part of the fix, not a footnote to it.

## .why it is not done in this round

- **SAFE?** 🔴 **no.** it changes what every reviewer on every lane can see. a clamp set too tight
  makes lanes re-raise answered points, which costs rounds and reads as a reviewer malfunction
- **CLEAN?** 🔴 **no.** it ripples into `parseReviewArgs`, `compileReviewPrompt`, the composition
  census (`conversation` is a named row whose sense would shift), the guard's expansion in
  `runStoneGuardReviews`, and every `--conversation` snapshot

⇒ both answers are no, so a dream is owed rather than a fix-forward
(`rule.always.fix-forward-under-scouts-honor`). and the deferral is for **dirt**, not size, so a
fulcrum rides with it — the ripple estimate above is a judgment, and a judgment made alone is what a
fulcrum list exists to surface.

## .deferred again — 2026-10-13

## .partly served — 2026-10-15

the cheapest cut landed: `asConversationEntryCondensed` collapses a prior peer given that holds no
verdict (no count read, no `blocker.N` / `nitpick.N` named) to one line that names its cause and the
chars it dropped. it is applied once, in `getOneReviewScopeSettled`, so every lane and partition
reads the condensed form. a `.taken` and any given that names a concern ride whole. the depth clamp
below is still owed, and still unmeasured for its cache effect.

🔴 **measured, and it is the SMALL lever.** over this route's 5.1 stone: 39 of 1,409 `.md` givens
condense, 129.6k of 4.59M given chars (2.8%). on one real run (`repo-rules`, i016) the conversation
was 122 entries and 277.9k chars, and the cut saves 3.6k (1.3%). verdictless givens are short —
the largest is 6.9k. ⇒ the mass is in readable givens and takens, so the depth bound is the lever
that matters; the condense cut is not a substitute for it.
