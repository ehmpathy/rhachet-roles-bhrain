## .the gap, precisely

`define.wick-dense` declares three tenets. **an aphorism satisfies all three.**

| tenet | *"an unnameable harm is not a harm"* |
|---|---|
| few words | ✅ six |
| point first | ✅ no wind-up at all |
| no time-ordered log | ✅ none |

⇒ 🔴 **it passes every check the lane runs, and gives the reader no act to take.** they must first
decode the identity before they learn what to do. that decode is the cost the whole overlay exists to
remove, relocated from *length* into *shape*.

**four more, all from the one brief, all authored under the wick-dense persona:**

- *"three local calls in one test file are the shape without the cost"*
- *"the line is the test made visible, and its absence is the tell"*
- *"the severity is not a measure of how right you are"*
- *"a nitpick loses none of the record"*

each is terse, loaded, and oblique. **`rule.require.brevity` actively rewards them**, since each is the
fewest words the author could reach — the compression went into obliquity rather than into cuts.

## .why the extant rules do not catch it

the lane guards **one** pole:

| pole | the prose | guarded by |
|---|---|---|
| verbose | accretes, masks sense beneath words | `rule.forbid.rambles`, `rule.forbid.chronological-accretion`, and the three subversive-prose asks (`bhuild#358`, `bhuild#360`, `bhrain#428`) |
| 🔴 **oblique** | **terse, dense, and unactionable** | 🔴 **unguarded** |

⚠️ **`bhrain#428` is not this.** `v2026_09_04.amend.subversive-is-accretion-not-compression.md` settled
that subversive prose **is** accretion — *"only subversive actors accrete narratives and mask sense
beneath mountains of words."* that ask names the verbose pole on purpose. this is its opposite, and a
rule against accretion does not reach it.

⇒ **so the lane has two failure poles and a guard on one**, and the unguarded one is the pole the
persona pushes an author toward.

## .the shape of the fix

a rule in `lang.prose/`, beside `rule.forbid.rambles` as its second negative peer. the name is that
repo's call; `rule.forbid.aphorisms` fits the extant `forbid.$topic` grammar.

**the test:** *"can a reader act on this sentence without a decode pass?"*

- states a subject, a verb, and what to do → a claim. keep it
- states an identity, a paradox, or a negation the reader must unpack → an aphorism. restate it

**the tells:**

| tell | example | plain form |
|---|---|---|
| `X is not X` | *"an unnameable harm is not a harm"* | *"you cannot name it → nitpick"* |
| the elided noun | *"the shape without the cost"* | *"it matches the rule's shape and carries none of its cost"* |
| the revealed-truth close | *"…and its absence is the tell"* | *"if you cannot write that line, grade it a nitpick"* |
| the negated abstraction | *"severity is not a measure of how right you are"* | *"severity measures harm, never correctness"* |

🔴 **the bound, and it is the whole reason this is delicate: the repair must not add words.** every
plain form above is the same length or shorter. a rule that reads as *"be less terse"* would reverse
`rule.require.brevity` and re-open the verbose pole. **the ask is a change of shape at constant
length** — state the claim rather than gesture at it.

## .why it is not done in this round

**not clean.** `lang.prose/` and `define.wick-dense` are `ehmpathy/rhachet-roles-ehmpathy`'s, and a
rule that lives away from the persona it bounds is one its readers never boot
(`rule.always.scope-onetime-lessons-to-the-behavior`). it also touches three published briefs
(`define.wick-dense`, `rule.require.brevity`, `rule.forbid.rambles`) whose readers span every repo that
enrolls the mechanic.

⇒ this route's scope is the contemplation gate. the evidence is measured, the repair is scoped, and it
belongs upstream.
