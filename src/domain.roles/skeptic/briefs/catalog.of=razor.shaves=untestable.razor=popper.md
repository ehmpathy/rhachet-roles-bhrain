# razor: popper

shaves: a claim that cannot be tested · settled by: logic, then observation

## .the questions

> **1. what observation, if it came, would show this claim wrong?** (logic)
> **2. was that observation run?** (observation)

no answer to 1 → the claim says naught. an answer to 1 and no run → the claim is untested, not confirmed.

## .the source

- Popper, *Logik der Forschung*, 1934 (imprint 1935); English, 1959
- read at https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/popper/

## .fails when

- one counter-instance is never sufficient alone: the observation can err, and a read tests a bundle (`razor=duhem-quine`)

## .run by

`rule.always.reach-less-wrong-via-questions`
