# razor: rapoport

shaves: a cause among rivals · settled by: rivals

## .the question

> **can I state the rival so well that its holder would say "thanks, I wish I'd thought of putting it that way"?**

every rivals razor shaves a rival. this one asks whether the rival was weighed at its strongest before the shave.

## .the source

- Anatol Rapoport, *Fights, Games, and Debates*, 1960
- the rules as listed by Dennett, *Intuition Pumps*, 2013
- read at a secondary page: https://www.themarginalian.org/2014/03/28/daniel-dennett-rapoport-rules-criticism/

## .fails when

- it is slow; reserve it for a rival a decision hangs on
- a bad-faith rival has no strongest form to rebuild

## .run by

`rule.always.weigh-each-claim-with-one-burden`
