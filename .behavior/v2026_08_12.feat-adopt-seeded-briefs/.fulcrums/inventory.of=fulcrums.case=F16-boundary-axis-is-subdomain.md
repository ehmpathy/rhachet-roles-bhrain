## .the evidence found this round

🔴 **`rung` is genuinely overloaded, and the overload is live.**

| the sense | where declared | what it means |
|---|---|---|
| a gate-position | `term=guard.rung._.choice._.md` | a single gate-position in a guarded stone's one ordered ladder — peer levels + the judge |
| a determinism position | `term=entoolment.rung`, `philosophy.entoolment-is-the-pinnacle` | 0 🧠 tribal → 1 📚 brief → 2 💪💧 fluid → 3 💪🔩 rigid → 4 💪🪨 solid |

**this is the case that decides the axis.** under A they become `driver.rung` / `learner.rung`,
which does **not** disambiguate — a driver cares about both ladders, so the role name resolves
neither. under B they become `guard.rung` / `entoolment.rung`, which does.

⇒ **ambiguity is a property of context, never of ownership.** only the subdomain axis names the
context.

## .why the rework is dirty

a rename touches the filename, every `.refs` line, every `.see also`, and the boot globs that catch
them. and a **bulk find-and-replace is forbidden here specifically** — it cannot tell a *citation*
of a flat term from an *instance* of one, which this repo has already watched happen once.

so the mitigation is the one the rule itself prescribes: **fix forward on touch, never sweep.** an
untouched flat cluster stays flat, and the reversal cost never accrues on clusters nobody visits.

## .what would overturn it

a case where two senses of one word are separated by **role** and not by subdomain — i.e. where the
same subdomain means different things to two roles. no such case has been found.
