# seed S04 — the severity is the driver's own rank

**kind:** settlement · **settles:** `F06`, reqs 2/3/4 as a set, and informs `F07`

## .said

> the driver marks them --as conceded with severity ; its based on the drivers rank of severity

## .settled

> **the gate reads a RANK the driver assigns, and that is the design rather than its weakness.**

three consequences, and the vision had each of them backwards or unresolved:

| # | what is settled |
|---|---|
| 1 | 🔴 **req 4 is severity-based.** the input is the driver's grade on the concession, never the concern's kind. `F06` ruled |
| 2 | 🔴 **req 3 is satisfied AS INTENDED.** the wish asked for *a fact the tool reads, never a judgment a clone asserts* — and the fact the tool reads IS the recorded rank. the judgment is the driver's by design; the record is what makes it a fact |
| 3 | **a `[nitpick][urgent]` pair lifts the refusal, and that is correct.** a nitpick that ships nameable harm has earned the round; a blocker that ships none has not. the two axes stay orthogonal, and the gate reads the harm axis |

### 🔴 what this REVERSES in the vision

the vision graded reqs 2, 3, and 4 as *"served in intent and not in letter"* and called that a
pattern a council should rule as a set. **the set is ruled, and the verdict is that the letter was
the misread.**

- req 4 names a **kind** and means a **harm**. the mechanism was right
- req 3 names an **external fact** and means a **recorded** one. a rank on the record is what was
  asked for
- req 2's freshness gap is untouched by this — it is `F10`'s, and it stays open

⇒ **two of the three are closed as author error in the wish's own prose, not as design divergence.**

### 🟡 what it does NOT settle, and the row it makes sharper

`F07` asks whether a driver refused at `better` may re-absorb the same concern at `urgent`.

- this settlement says the rank is the driver's to assign ⇒ **a re-grade is a re-rank, and re-ranks
  are what the verb is for**
- it does **not** say a re-rank taken **in response to a refusal** is honest

⇒ so `F07` is informed and not closed. what it now asks is narrower and better: **is a re-rank
under refusal a re-thought, or a purchase?** the vision's own answer holds — the defence is
visibility, never a lock — and the answer is now the design's stated posture rather than its
apology.
