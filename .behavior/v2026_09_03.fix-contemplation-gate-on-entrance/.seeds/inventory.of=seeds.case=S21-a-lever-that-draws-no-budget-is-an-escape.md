# seed S21 — a lever that draws no budget is not a lever

## .said

> and we should remove the encouragement to run `rhx review` by hand

> completely eliminate that recommendation

> they should never

> as it defeats the budget system

## .settled

> **a review that draws no budget is not a cheaper review. it is an escape from the meter the
> whole ladder runs on.**

the meter is the mechanism. every sanctioned review costs a round, mints a `.given`, and that
given is what a judge tallies and what a contemplation gate reads. a review summoned **outside**
the guard skips all three at once:

| the guard summons it | you summon it |
|---|---|
| a round is drawn from the budget | 🔴 free, and unlimited |
| a `.given` is minted | no artifact the engine can read |
| the judge tallies its verdict | it gates naught |
| the guard picks the rubric, the bind, the brain, the focus | 🔴 **the party under review picks all four** |

⇒ **row 4 is the isomorphism, and it is why this is a forbid rather than a preference.** a commit
changes *what the reviewers can see*; a hand-run review changes *who reviews, and under what
terms.* **both are a review-scope change made by the party under review**, and both are attractive
for the identical reason — they work, they are free, and they feel like diligence.

## ⚠️ .the sharp part — it reversed an edit made thirty minutes earlier

`rule.always.spend-own-levers-before-escalation` had just gained a **"scoped re-run | driver"**
row, added by this very drive as the sanctioned answer to an overflowed lane. it read as an
obvious win: the lane was dark, the re-run was in the driver's own hands, and it produced a real
report.

⇒ **the argument was sound and the premise was wrong.** the table sorts levers **by owner**, and
a hand-run review is not a lever at all — it draws no budget, so it belongs to no owner. it was
struck the same day, and the strike is recorded in the brief rather than quietly applied.

⚠️ **the tell was already on the page and unread: every other row of that table names a command
that COSTS a resource.** a row whose command is free was the anomaly, and its freeness read as an
advantage rather than as the disqualification it was.

## .the lever it reached for instead

the same overflow has a sanctioned remedy, and S13 had already put it in the table:
**`rhx route.mutate.guard`** — narrow that lane's `--paths-with`, trim its `--conversation`, and
let the **guard** run it. the lane still costs its budget round, still mints a `.given` that
gates, and still runs on the guard's terms.

⇒ **one repair, two symptoms.** the recipe existed *because* the guard lever was absent from the
owner table; a driver who believed the bind was not theirs took the only tool left.
