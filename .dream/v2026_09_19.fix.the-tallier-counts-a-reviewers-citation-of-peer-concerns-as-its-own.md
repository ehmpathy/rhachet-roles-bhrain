# 🌙 dream: the tallier counts a reviewer's CITATION of peer concerns as its own concerns

- **kind** = `fix`
- **owner** = whoever next touches the review tallier (`asPeerGivenVerdict` / the sub-brain tally path)
- **caught** = 2026-09-19, from `.behavior/v2026_09_17.fix-budget-grant-needs-urgent-concession`,
  stone `5.3.verification`, observed on the `i004` walk

## .the cue that fired

the `i004` guard walk tallied **6 nitpicks stone-wide**. a read of the four reports that held them
found only **3 distinct concerns** — and the gap is one reviewer's report counted twice over.

`enroll-verif-test-intent` (`r012`) was tallied `0 blockers · 2 nitpicks`. its own verdict line says
the opposite, verbatim:

> *"## Verdict: no test-intent violations found. No blocker."*

and its body raises no concern at all. the two nitpicks the tallier read come from this sentence,
where the reviewer **reports what a PEER found**:

> *"i004 (latest, unaddressed) carries only **2 nitpicks** on domain-term … (`increase`/`top-up`
> vs `grant`) — cosmetic, not test-intent."*

⇒ those are `repo-rules`' two nitpicks, cited by `r012` as **context it read and dismissed**. the
tallier attributed them to `r012` as findings of its own.

## .the gap, precisely

the tallier's job is to read a reviewer's prose and extract *"how many concerns did **you** raise?"*
it instead matched *"how many concern-counts does this text mention?"* — and a reviewer that reads the
prior conversation will mention peer counts by design.

🔴 **the failure mode is structural, not a one-off.** the `--conversation` bind is what makes a lane
aware of prior rounds, so **every reviewer with conversation depth is a candidate**: the deeper the
context a lane is given, the more peer counts its prose holds, and the more it over-tallies.

⇒ so the instrument that measures convergence gets **noisier exactly as a route converges**, which is
the inverse of what a tally is for.

### .what it costs

| harm | how it lands |
|---|---|
| the stone-wide tally **overstates** | 6 read where 3 stood. a route with a tight `--allow-nitpicks` floor can be **rejected on concerns nobody raised** |
| the driver is sent to a phantom | the guard mints a `.taken` path for `r012`, so the driver owes an answer to a lane that asked naught |
| 🔴 it **inverts** with convergence | the cleaner the round, the more prior counts a conversation-bound lane recites |

⚠️ **it is safe in one direction only.** an over-count can hold a stone that should pass; it cannot
pass a stone that should be held. ⇒ a failhide this is not — but `rule.forbid.overzealous-blockers`'
cost argument applies in full: a driver that answers two phantom concerns reads the third with less
care.

## .the shape of the fix

three parts, and the first is the cheap one:

1. **bound the tally to the reviewer's own verdict block.** `contract.reviewer-output` already
   demands a numeric line per dimension; the tallier should read **the report's own summary**, never
   a count that appears mid-prose. a count inside a quote, a table of peer findings, or a sentence
   with another lane's slug in it is not this lane's
2. 🔴 **prefer the reviewer's explicit verdict where the two disagree.** `r012` wrote *"No blocker"*
   under a structured `## Verdict:` header. where a reviewer states a verdict AND a count is scraped
   that contradicts it, the stated verdict is the stronger signal — and today it is ignored
3. **census the corpus.** any lane with `--conversation` depth may hold the same over-count. the
   check is a read of each `i00N` report against its tallied figure, which is mechanical

## .why it is not done in this round

the SAFE/CLEAN test (`rule.always.fix-forward-under-scouts-honor`):

- **safe?** 🔴 **no.** the tallier gates **every** peer review in this repo. a change to what it
  counts can flip a verdict on a suite this route cannot test, and `rule.forbid.failhide` makes the
  failure direction load-carrying — a tallier that under-counts passes a stone that should be held
- **clean?** 🔴 **no.** it touches the shared verdict path, its snapshot corpus, and the
  `contract.reviewer-output` brief that documents the parse. the blast radius is every route in the
  fleet, not this behavior's diff

🟡 **and the defect is in the HARNESS, never in this behavior's surface.** this route changed the
budget gate; it did not touch the tally. ⇒ a repair here would be an execution-grain change smuggled
into a verification stone, which `rule.require.review-test-changes` forbids by name.

## 🔴 .what WAS done, so the boundary is exact

the over-count was **detected and corrected by hand** before any concern was absorbed: the three
distinct concerns were read from their reports, and `r012`'s two were ruled a double-count rather
than answered. ⇒ the walk's own record stands at 6; the true residual is 3, and this dream is where
the difference is kept.
