## .settled

**1. the pair.** enskill is the DISCOVER half (rung 0 → 2), entool the UPGRADE half (2 → 4). they
are two concepts, not synonyms: a tactic can be enskilled and never entooled, which is the common
state. **the repeat is the trigger, not a premonition** — `rule.prefer.wet-over-dry` applies, so
the *second* run is the evidence. `SOP` recorded as the human-side alias, never a rival term.

**2. the family.** the confirmation surfaced a gap: **`enbrief` — the eldest and most-used of the
three — had no glossary cluster**, while the two words coined hours earlier had theirs already.

**3. the route rung — and this corrected a real hole.** the driver's ladder assumed brain cost
always falls to zero, so work with an irreducible judgment floor had nowhere to go; it sat at rung
2 and read as a failure. the correction:

> **a tool removes the brain. a route removes the brain's overhead** — the order, the omissions,
> the rediscovery of a path someone already walked.

a route is not a fifth rung; it is what rungs 1–3 look like when the residual work is genuinely
judgment. and the value axis differs: a tool's benefit is the work costs **zero**; a route's is
that it costs **the same every time**.

`declapract.upgrade` makes the point precisely **because the brain still performs every repair**.
what it retired is the need to recall that reflection follows repair, or that hazards are detected
before either.
