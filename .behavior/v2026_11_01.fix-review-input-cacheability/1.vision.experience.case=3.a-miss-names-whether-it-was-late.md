an edit between the two rounds does not fork this case: past the reach naught was cached, so an

## .narrative

the grove reports `🔴 380 missed`. a driver reads that as 380 layout defects to hunt. but a heavy
lane's rounds arrive 1h–3h apart, and the cache reach is ~66m, so most of those rounds met an evicted
prefix however perfect their bytes. today the verdict cannot say which: `getOnePrefixVerdict` reads
no clock, and grades any zero read `missed`.

**after.** a pair whose round began past the reach of its prior grades `lapsed` — a cadence fact,
no layout act owed. `missed` is left to the pairs that came in time and still read naught, which is
the count a driver can act on.

## .[tn] sketch

the timeline is one lane's life in the meter; the gap at `[t1]` forks it.

```

    when it began 5 minutes after round 1

    when the gap cannot be read from the run dirs

```
