## .the gap, precisely

`[case10]` spawns the REAL `rhx clone whoami` and times it against `WHOAMI_TIMEOUT_MS`. it shares a
jest worker with peers that spawn their own children, so the elapsed carries the worker's contention
as well as the binary's boot.

measured 2026-09-18, at the then-cap of 2_000ms, six runs, no production edit:

| what ran | wall | `[case10]` |
|---|---|---|
| the 7-file brain suite | 61s | 🔴 `timed-out` |
| this file alone | 29s | 🔴 `timed-out` |
| this file alone | 13s | ✅ |
| `[case10]` alone | 5s | ✅ |
| the 7-file brain suite | 24s | 🔴 **`took = 2032`** |

⇒ 1.6% over the cap: the red measured the host, never the binary.

## .what is taken, and what is owed

- ✅ the overrun is a third determinate arm: `timed-out` is a designed render
  (`getCloneAddress.ts` kills the child and releases the hook), so the test asserts its contract —
  `address === null` and `took >= cap` — and bounds the wall clock at `took < 2 × cap`
- ✅ the cap is now 10_000ms, bound to the hook budgets on disk by `[case8]`
- 🟡 **owed:** a genuinely slow `whoami` lands on the overrun arm and stays green; `2 × cap` bounds
  the drift and no further. a true budget clamp needs the probe to run alone
- 🟡 the overrun arm has not been observed to fire

## .the shape of the fix

**move `[case10]` into its own file**, so jest gives it a worker with no peers — the live hook's
exact condition. one file, plus a header that says why it is split out.

rejected: a warm-up spawn before the measured one. it removes a cold-boot cost the live hook does
pay.
