# 🌙 dream: a clone cannot read its own identity without a multi-second subprocess

**kind** = reseed · **owner** = `ehmpathy/rhachet` · **caught** 2026-09-20

## .the cue that fired

the wisher asked: *"does the brain set work? i.e., do we reliably set the brain from route?"*

the `5.4` dogfood closed the chain end to end, then measured 2 timeouts and 1 success. n=3 cannot
part a hard floor from a noisy cap, and the two take opposite repairs.

## .the gap, precisely

`applyStoneBrainOnEntry` gates its dispatch on `getCloneAddress`, which spawns `rhx clone whoami`
each tick, with no cache. measured with `.agent/.notes/tool.probe-rhx-latency.js`:

| invocation | trials | p50 | inside the then-2s cap |
|---|---|---|---|
| `rhx clone whoami --output json` | 12 | 11224ms | 0/12 |
| `rhx clone list --output json` | 6 | 19904ms | 0/6 |
| `rhx --version` | 8 | 1520ms | 0/8 |

⇒ a hard floor. the cost is general to an `rhx` invocation — `whoami`'s own work is one env read,
one disk lookup, and a socket probe capped at 1000ms.

🔴 **the value is already in the caller's own environment.** `RHACHET_CLONE_SERIAL` sits in
`process.env`, but its key is rhachet-internal (`utils/cloneEnvKeys.js`), and no SDK export
(`rhachet`, `rhachet/actors`, `rhachet/brains`, `rhachet/keyrack`) surfaces a clone-self accessor.

## .the local half — raised, and the clamp is red

`WHOAMI_TIMEOUT_MS` is now `10_000` (`getCloneAddress.ts:19`), and its `.note` records p50 = 5537ms
on rhachet 1.47.6. its `.why` claims a 25s hook budget.

🔴 the budget on disk is 5s.
- `getDriverRole.ts` declares `PT5S` on both `route.drive` hooks, and a boot re-stamps
  `.claude/settings.json` from it
- ⇒ `getCloneAddress.integration.test.ts [case8]` reads the settings and fails, verified
  2026-09-27: `Expected: < 5000 · Received: 10000`

⇒ the upstream ask is the only fix that fits a 5s hook. a local fix is a role budget raise to
`PT25S`, which is a call about every adopter's hook bound.

## .the family — one pattern, three instances

a clone has surfaces to read a **peer**, and none to read **itself**:

| the read | the peer instrument | the task |
|---|---|---|
| another clone's live brain | `clone whoami` | `#528` |
| another clone's transcript | `clone get` — a cwd heuristic that refuses on a tie | `#544` |
| its own identity | `clone whoami` — a subprocess | `#545` |

⇒ the tell of a self-read routed through a peer instrument: a cost that buys no information. a
cwd heuristic answers *"which of these is yours?"*; a caller that already holds the answer needs
neither.

## .the shape of the fix

upstream, three bars on `#545`, each with a test:

1. **no subprocess** — an SDK accessor returns `{ serial, slug, actorHash }`; assert no exec fires
2. **inside a hook budget** — 12 trials, p100 < 50ms
3. **fails loud outside a clone** — `ConstraintError`, never a synthesized serial

the cheaper alternative clears bars 1 and 2 by construction: publish `CLONE_ENV_KEYS`.

once either lands, `getCloneAddress` reads in-process first and keeps the spawn as its fallback.

## .why it is not done in this round

**not clean.** a read of `process.env.RHACHET_CLONE_SERIAL` today couples this repo to an undeclared
key in another repo's private convention.

## 🔴 .the durable lesson

the dogfood reported *"the chain closed"*, which was true, and read as *"the feature works"*. the
mechanism worked; the gate in front of it did not clear on that host.

⇒ **a success rate quoted from 3 samples is a story, not a measurement.**
