🔴 **the `case` cell is a LINK, and that link IS the dereference this case is about.** the real board
(`.fulcrums/inventory.of=fulcrums._.md`) renders it exactly so. **a bare `F09` would not be enough**,
because an entry's filename is `case=F$n-$slug` and **the slug sits in neither column** — so a human
who sorts on `rework` and then reaches for the argument would have to read the directory and match a
title by eye. ⇒ that is the *"linear hunt"* `rule.forbid.itemization-without-coordinates` §
*the coordinates ARE the dereference mechanism* names as the defect the coordinate form exists to
remove.

⚠️ **and the ordinals above belong to this DEMO's route, never to the live one.** the fictional `F09`
here and this route's real `F04` argue one subject — the skip across a hash move — under two numbers.
every `F$n` outside this code fence, `.see also` included, cites the live board.

I sort on `rework`. **F09 is the only dirty row**, so I read that entry first. it names the fork,
what the driver took and why, the reviewer it disputed, the review hash at declaration, and the
current hash. **they differ, so some artifact moved after the lane went dark.**

🔴 **and that is ALL two hashes tell me — one bit, never a distance.** the review hash covers the
stone's whole artifact set (`src/**/*`, at an execution stone), so a `.taken` written for a
*different* reviewer flips it exactly as an edit to the disputed seam would. ⇒ **so I must open the
diff myself; the row points, it does not measure.** the mitigation's real strength, and the cheap
repair that would give it a distance, are argued at `F04` § *what the review hash actually is*.

that is the one I rule on. F03 and F07 I read in twenty seconds each and let stand.

**today** those three disagreements would reach me as: a stone that took eleven rounds, a budget
topped up twice, and a `--as blocked` whose artifact I must read in full to learn what was actually
contested.

**the aha:** *the dispute did not skip my review. it scheduled it — into the one review I would have
done regardless, in the form I can act on fastest.*

🔴 **and the measurement makes that aha both stronger and dearer.** on the prior route, **5 of 6
adjudicated calls were reversed** (`1.vision.yield.md` § `.what is awkward` § 7). so:

- **stronger** — a council that reverses this often is one whose read is worth a schedule at all. an
  unscheduled dispute is a wrong call nobody catches
- 🔴 **dearer** — the same rate is what makes `[t2]`'s `--as rewound` the *likely* branch rather than
  the tail. **this narrative's driver rules on one of three and lets two stand; the data says that
  when a council does engage, it usually turns the call.** ⇒ every stone built on a disputed decision
  between `[t3]` and here is exposed

⚠️ **`[t2]` is therefore the branch to read first in this case**, over the pleasant one where the
council agrees and the stone stands. it is demoed, and its *frequency* is the part this narrative's
tone understates.

🔴 **this line cited a `[t5]` that this timeline does not have** — its steps end at `[t4]`, and the
agreement outcome is numbered nowhere in this case. it is walked at **`case=9` `[t10]`**, and the
citation now names the branch rather than a coordinate. ⇒ **a citation to an ABSENT step is the same
class as one to the wrong step**: both are claims about a step, and only a read of the timeline
falsifies either. the round-4 sweep that found it read the timeline; the round-3 audit read the
matrix.

## .the bdd timeline

```gherkin
given a route with 8 stones, closed
  and 3 stances of `disputed` across 2 stones
  and .fulcrums/inventory.of=fulcrums._.md with 3 rows and 3 entries beside them

[t0] given a lane the driver CONCEDED rather than disputed
     when the council reads .fulcrums/
     then that lane has NO row                                    # ⬛ impossible — a concede records no judgment
      and its record lives in passage.jsonl alone                 # the count, not a claim

[t1] when the council reads inventory.of=fulcrums._.md
     then every disputed lane has exactly one row                 # 🔴 acceptance #4
      and every row cites an entry that exists                    # the census claim, by construction
      and the rows carry `rework` so a human can sort on it
      and each entry carries the reviewer, its blockers, the driver's --why, and the hash

[t2] given the council disagrees with a dispute
     when it rules
     then the entry's `.the verdict` field is filled, verbatim
      and the driver may be sent back with `--as rewound`         # 🔴 ✅ rewind × disputed × live
      and the reviewer's original .given is still on disk, unedited

[t3] given the driver had disputed and written no fulcrum entry
     then it could not have                                       # 🔴 --why is required, and the path must EXIST
      and an unwritten path is refused at the boundary            # ✅ F018 — before any state moves
      and an entry whose sections argue naught is admitted        # ⚠️ the hole — see below

[t4] given 11 lanes and 11 disputes on one stone
     when the council reads
     then it sees 11 rows on one stone                            # 🔴 the abuse signal is VISIBLE
      and passage.jsonl shows 11 `disputed` and 0 `conceded`
```

🔴 **`[t4]` is the only restraint on abuse, and it is a social one.** a driver that disputes every
lane passes every stone. the design does not forbid it — it makes it **countable**, and a count of
eleven disputes with zero concedes is a signal no reader can miss.

⚠️ **`[t3]` is the same hole `case=3` `[t5]` names, one level up.** `--why` is required to **point at
an entry that exists**, never to point at one that argues. no gate reads prose, and ✅ **the wisher
ruled that acceptable** — the field's action completes in the **author**, before any reader arrives
(`S04`, `F014`). ⇒ argued in full at `1.vision.yield.md` § *what is awkward* § 2.

✅ **`F018` raised the floor twice over, and the second raise is the larger one.** the boundary
refuses an unwritten path outright — 🔴 **rung 1 of `rule.prefer.prevent-over-correct`, a constraint
rather than a check-after.** and behind the path sits a **fulcrum entry**, whose contract demands the
fork, the counter-argument, the `rework` grade, and what would settle it. ⇒ **the reachable failure
is an entry with its sections filled and empty**, which is far harder to author than the one-word
`--why` this case first named.

🔴 **`[t0]` is why the impossible slab is a design claim rather than an omission.** a concede leaves
no fulcrum **on purpose**: it accepts a judgment rather than asserts one, so no claim exists for a
council to rule on. its record is the count.

## .why it is a happy path

the human does the review they were always due to do, on an artifact built for exactly that read:
one row per judgment and no narrative about any one of them (`rule.require.catalog-is-an-index`),
sorted by the column that names reversal cost, with the argument **one dereference away** —
and that last clause is a property of the `case=F$n-$slug` coordinate, never of the prose
(`rule.forbid.itemization-without-coordinates`).

⚠️ **the council is not new and this behavior does not invent it.**
`rule.always.defer-fulcrums-to-last` already gathers every best-guessed fork into one end-of-route
review. **the wish's insight is that a disputed verdict was already bound for that review** — the
change only routes it there directly, instead of through a budget that ends by exhaustion.

## .the cells this case covers

🔴 **`[t2]` is a `rewind`, never a `forgive`, and the difference is the outcome itself.**
`--as rewound` **revives** the lane; a forgive — `--as overruled` / `--as approved` — **leaves it
dark** (`case=4` `[t3]`). ⇒ `rewind` is a move of its own (dimensions § *`rewind` is a value SPLIT
OUT of `forgive`*).
