# 🌙 dream: the review tally fallback is pinned to one vendor

kind = fix · owner = bhrain/role=driver (route guard review) · caught 2026-10-22

## .the cue that fired

stone 5.1 of `v2026_10_02.feat-transcript-token-inventory` halted twice (i004, i005) on the same
`💥 malfunction: review tally fallback failed`. the l3 lane wrote a legible prose review; only the
count could not be read.

## .the gap, precisely

- `src/domain.operations/route/genReviewBrainSupply.ts` declares
  `FIXED_FALLBACK_BRAIN = 'fireworks/deepseek/v4-flash'`, documented as not configurable
- the fireworks account answers 412 (suspended)
- ⇒ every prose reviewer on every route malfunctions until a human repairs one vendor account,
  while the review brains themselves (openrouter) are healthy

## .the shape of the fix

- let the fallback follow an ordered list of brains, tried in turn on a build or ask fault
  (`fireworks/deepseek/v4-flash`, then an `openrouter/...` peer of the same model)
- or derive the fallback from the brain the lane itself ran on, which is by construction reachable
- keep one declared default; a fault on each brain in the list stays a malfunction (never a
  silent zero)
- clamp: an integration case where the first fallback brain throws and the second tallies

## .why it is not done in this round

safe, but not clean: it changes a shared guard contract and its snapshots, outside the token
inventory this route builds.
