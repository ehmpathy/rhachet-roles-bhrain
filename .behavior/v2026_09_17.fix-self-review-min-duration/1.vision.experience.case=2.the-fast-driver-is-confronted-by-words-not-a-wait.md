## .the narrative

a driver is asked for a self review. eight seconds later they promise. the articulation file is at
the owed path and it says `looks good`.

the guard renders `patience, friend` — the identity reframe, the four when-then cues, the path it
read. **no duration is mentioned, and no wait is demanded.**

this driver, unlike the one in `case=1`, has naught to confirm. they read the four cues, recognize
that `looks good` answers none of them, and go back and actually read the artifact. eleven minutes
later they rewrite the articulation with what they found, and promise again. **it clears.**

### 🔴 .the gate DOES tell them apart — and it charges the one it catches naught

this demo asserted the opposite for part of the round, and `S13` reversed it. **the gate now parts
these two drivers, and does it without a wait** — which is the property neither `B` nor the unbounded
`E1` could deliver:

| | the thorough driver (`case=1`) | the fast driver (here) |
|---|---|---|
| when they promise | T+39min | T+8s |
| `elapsed < 30s` | ✗ | ✓ |
| the guard's verdict on promise 1 | **`allowed`** | **`challenge:first`** |
| commands to clear | **1** | 2 |
| what they are held for | naught | 🔴 **naught either** — one keystroke, no wait |

⇒ **so the claim is the strongest form this case has carried:** *the gate confronts the right driver,
and holds neither.* `B` bought the first half at the price of a wait; the unbounded `E1` bought the
second half and forfeited the first. **the conjunction was in no option the drive tabled** (`F11`).

🟡 **and the prompt's own words still do the work.** the gate decides only **who hears a paragraph**;
what turns this driver around is the four cues inside it, which `looks good` answers none of.

### 🔴 .and the driver is never stuck — for a new reason

under the clock, liveness came from a **hatch**: three attempts on one slug cleared regardless. today
that hatch is **unreachable for the driver who repairs**, because attempts are counted per hash and a
repair mints a new hash.

**under option E the hatch is retired rather than repaired: the attempt count IS the gate.** the
second promise clears by construction, so there is no stall for a hatch to relieve.

⇒ the wish's requirement 3 — *"no driver is hard-stuck"* — is satisfied twice over: the hashless
report makes any count accumulate per slug, and E's count-gate caps the attempts at **two** rather
than three.

### .the "aha"

> **the prompt is the encouragement. the wait was the friction. they are separable, and only one of
> them is expensive.**

`research.selfreview-effectiveness` rates the prompt's two mechanisms — forced articulation
(`d = 0.55`) and situational cues (`d = 0.65`) — and rates the wait with no effect size at all.
⇒ **option E keeps both measured levers and drops the only unmeasured one**, which is the exact trade
the research supports and option B declined.

🔴 **and the trade `S11` made is REFUNDED by `S13`.** this section read *"the trade is not free — the
research also says 'target interventions — only confront on repeated rapid attempts', and E confronts
universally"*, and that cost is gone:

| the research's rule | `B` | unbounded `E1` | 🔴 `S13` |
|---|---|---|---|
| *preserve autonomy — guide but don't block* | 🔴 blocks 30s | ✅ one keystroke | ✅ one keystroke |
| *target interventions — only confront on repeated rapid attempts* | ✅ | 🔴 universal | ✅ **only within 30s of the ask** |

⇒ **`S13` is the first option in this round that satisfies every row of that table.** the drive's

## 🔴 .why `[t2]` and `[t3]` are in this demo at all

they assert **the two bypasses**, deliberately. a demo that omits them would claim a guarantee this
design does not make.

| the bypass | cost to the driver | who concedes it |
|---|---|---|
| `[t2]` — promise twice, fast, no read | **2 commands** | the unbounded `E1` — it exists under `S11` too |
| 🔴 `[t3]` — `sleep 31`, promise once | **1 command + a wait spent idle** | 🔴 **`S13` itself.** the bound it added is what gets gamed |

🔴 **`[t3]` is the sharper admission, and it is new.** `S13` re-admitted duration as a signal about
review, so a driver who waits without a read is now **indistinguishable from `case=1`'s thorough
driver** — by construction, since elapsed time is all the gate sees.

⇒ **the bound on both is what the refusal COSTS.** 🔴 the haste cue does refuse the promise —
`challenge:rushed` → exit 2 — but it charges **one command and no wait**, at most **once per slug**.
so `[t3]`'s driver evades one re-run and one paragraph; what still refuses them is **the articulation
gate** (`case=4`, `case=6`), which is a separate read on every promise. ⚠️ **it does not read the
prose**, so a two-word articulation at the right path with a fresh mtime clears. **that is the
residual risk of this round**, and `F01` marks it as the reason requirement 2 carries the most load.

🟡 **and `[t3]` is cheaper than what `B` charged, which is the honest comparison.** under `B` every
driver paid the 30s; under `S13` only a driver who *wants* to evade the prompt pays it, once per slug.
⇒ the drive measured its own five reviews on this stone and found the review work complete **before**
the clock ever mattered — so the 30s `B` bought was **idle**, never attention. `[t3]` makes that
idleness a choice rather than a tax.
