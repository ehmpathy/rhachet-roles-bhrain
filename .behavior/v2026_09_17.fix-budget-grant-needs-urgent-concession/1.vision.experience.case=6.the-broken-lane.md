## .the narrative

*driver, on `5.1.execution`, round 2, `ergo-coverage` lane returned no verdict*

`ergo-coverage` blew its context window. its `.given` carries no numeric count, so the tallier scores
it **one blocker** (`contract.reviewer-output`: *"where it finds no numeric count it can NOT assume
zero"*). the halt offers me the budget lever. i reach for it out of habit.

```
$ rhx route.guard.budget --for review --add 2 --peer ergo-coverage --stone 5.1.execution
```

```
      │  │        └─ trim its --paths-with, or its --conversation depth
      │  ├─ 2. answer its given — a malfunction still owes a .taken
      │  │     └─ rhx route.stone.set --stone 5.1.execution --as absorbed --that ergo-coverage
      │  └─ 3. re-arrive and let the GUARD run it
      │        └─ rhx route.stone.set --stone 5.1.execution --as passed
      │
      └─ ⛔ do NOT hand-run `rhx review` — it draws no budget and its verdict gates naught
```

🔴 **the refusal is the right answer here, and it is BETTER than a carve-out.** a permitted grant
would buy the driver another broken run and another wasted round. ⇒ req 6's *"its repair must not
need a grant"* is met because **the repair was never a grant** — it is a guard edit, which
`rule.always.diagnose-reviewer-malfunctions` and `rule.always.spend-own-levers-before-escalation`
already name as the driver's own lever.

⚠️ **this cell is where the gate is most likely to be MIS-READ as a regression**, so the copy carries
the diagnosis rather than a bare refusal. the difference between *"refused, go away"* and *"refused,
and here is the repair you actually wanted"* is the whole of `rule.require.errors-name-the-fix`.

🔴 **the `⛔` line carries real weight.** a driver refused a budget grant on a broken lane has exactly
one attractive wrong move left — a hand-run `rhx review` to see what the lane *would* have said. that
is forbidden outright (`rule.forbid.hand-run-reviews`: no budget drawn, no `.given` minted, gates
naught), and this refusal is the moment a driver most wants it.

**the aha:** *i asked for a round. the engine told me the lane was broken and handed me the wrench —
which is what i needed and not what i asked for.*

## .the bdd timeline

```gherkin

  and NO live absorption on the stone                                  # B = none

[t0] when the driver runs route.guard.budget --add 2 --peer ergo-coverage
     then it is REFUSED                                                # 🔴 no carve-out for malfunction
      and the guard's budget is UNCHANGED
      and stderr names the MALFUNCTION, not merely the absent concession
      and stderr prints route.mutate.guard as step 1
      and stderr forbids a hand-run `rhx review`

[t1] when the driver narrows the lane via route.mutate.guard
     then the guard's --paths-with is tightened
      and NO budget was needed for the edit                            # 🔴 req 6, satisfied
      and the MALFUNCTIONED run drew no round either                   # 🔴 the CLAMP — runStoneGuardReviews.ts:654-656
      and so the meter still reads 2/3, never 3/3                      #    a malfunction is neither passed nor constraint

[t2] when the driver writes the .taken and re-arrives
     then the guard runs the narrowed lane within its EXTANT budget    # 1 round left, and it fits
      and the lane returns a readable verdict

[t3] given the lane is ALSO exhausted at 3/3 when it malfunctions
     when the driver runs the top-up
     then it is still REFUSED                                          # the gate reads B; C is copy, not verdict
      and stderr names BOTH the malfunction and the dry meter
      and the remedy order is unchanged: narrow, answer, re-arrive

[t4] given the driver concedes the malfunction's phantom blocker
     then the concede is REFUSED at the absorb gate                    # 🔴 F030 — an unreadable given owes a re-run
      and so no B value can ever be manufactured from a malfunction
```

🔴 **`[t4]` is the cell's safety property, and it falls out of extant machinery.** a driver cannot
concede against an unreadable given (`F030`), so a malfunction can never be laundered into an
`urgent` concession that lifts the gate. ⇒ **the malfunction column is refused at B = `none` and
cannot reach B = `urgent` by way of the broken lane itself.**

⚠️ **it can still reach `urgent` from a DIFFERENT lane**, since B is stone-scoped — that is `[t4]`'s
bound and `F10`'s subject, not a defect in this cell.

🔴 **`[t3]` pins that C is a COPY branch, never a verdict branch.** the gate's answer is identical at
`malfunctioned`, `exhausted`, and `live`; what changes is which diagnosis the refusal leads with.
that separation is what keeps the gate one predicate wide.

## .why it fails safe

**loud** — the malfunction is named ahead of the refusal, so the driver diagnoses rather than retries
· **teaches the fix** — three numbered steps, the guard edit first, each a command the driver owns ·
**closes the attractive wrong move** — the `⛔` line names the hand-run review and says why it is
worthless · **unlaunderable** — `F030` makes the malfunction unable to mint its own lift.
