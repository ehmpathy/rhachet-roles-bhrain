# 🌙 dream: the PreToolUse exception in `rule.require.qualified-error-headers` must part safety gates from advisory guards

- kind: reseed
- owner: ehmpathy/rhachet-roles-ehmpathy (role=mechanic)
- caught 2026-11-01

## .the cue that fired

the mechanic standards review of `fix-memory-guard-fails-open` read `rule.require.qualified-error-headers`. its `🔴 .the PreToolUse exception` says a malfunction in a PreToolUse hook prints `💥 MalfunctionError:` and still exits 2, because exit 1 fails the gate open. for an advisory guard such as the bhrain memory.guard, that rule is the stall itself: a fault that exits 2 halts every call. this is root cause R4 in `.behavior/v2026_11_01.fix-memory-guard-fails-open/root-causes.md`.

## .the gap, precisely

the rule treats every PreToolUse hook as a safety gate, whose open state is the harm. some hooks are advisory: their open state costs one missed nudge, while their closed state halts every Bash, Write and Edit call. for those, exit 2 on a fault is the defect — it is the exact stall that froze two crews in ehmpathy/rhachet-roles-bhrain#653. the rule, read as written, would have a reviewer flag the fix as a blocker.

## .the shape of the fix

split the exception by what the gate protects:

| the hook guards | on a fault |
|---|---|
| a harm that ships if the call proceeds (secrets, destructive ops, permissions) | exit 2, `💥 MalfunctionError:` — fail closed |
| a nudge whose miss costs one unguarded act (memory capture, style) | exit 1 (non-block notice the human sees), one line that names the fault and its fix — fail open |

plus the test: *if this hook faults on every call for a day, which costs more — the calls it let through, or the work it halted?*

## .why it is not done in this round

the rule lives in another repo. a reseed there is the cure; an edit here would be a local fork of an org rule.
