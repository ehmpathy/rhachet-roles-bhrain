## .settled

**the canon carries a require/forbid pair per trait**, in the shape the ehmpathy canon already uses
(`require.brevity` ⇄ `forbid.rambles`). the require names the target; the forbid names the smell.
**either alone leaves half the cue.**

### the default inverts — prose is the exception

> *"unless we litteraly sked for a narrative, it must be bulletized"*

⇒ **the licence is the human's ask, and it must be spoken.** an implied ask is not one: *"explain
this to me"* asks for a clear transfer, and an outline is the clearer one.

three further exemptions, each narrow: a **verbatim quote** · a **narrative in a `.demo=` artifact**
· a **single claim**.

### the tier settlement, and it is a say/ref economy claim

| artifact | tier | why |
|---|---|---|
| both **rules** | `say` | they govern the **container**, chosen before a word is typed |
| the **demos** | `ref` | depth is free there; a reader dereferences a worked pair on demand |

⇒ **the rule that decides a container must arrive before the container is chosen.** a rule that
lands after a paragraph exists asks for a rewrite; the same rule known up front costs one decision.
