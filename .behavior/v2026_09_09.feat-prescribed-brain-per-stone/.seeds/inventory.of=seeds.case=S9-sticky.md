# S9 — sticky

**source** = the wisher, 2026-09-16 · **settles** = `F6`

## .said

> sticky

## .settled

> **a switched brain STAYS until a later stone changes it — it is not restored on stone exit.**

that is the whole concept. a prescribed tool, once applied, persists across the steps that follow
until another step prescribes a different one; the default is CONTINUITY, not reversion.

### 🔴 the cost of the persistence is contained ELSEWHERE, not by reverting the tool

the counter-case was that a rich brain set at a hard stone bills every mechanical stone after it —
"the majority of the drive's economy" (the vision's own words). restore-on-exit would cap that by
reverting to the inherited brain when each stone ends.

⇒ the verdict is that the containment belongs in the **route template** (cheap brains stamped on the
mechanical stones by bhuild's templates), NOT in a per-stone auto-revert. **the tool does not undo
itself; the next step that cares sets what it needs.**

🟡 the shape: continuity is the cheaper default to reason about (a driver need not track "what will I
snap back to?"), and the cost is a property of what each stone DECLARES, not of a lease the mechanism
enforces. a sticky tool with per-stone declarations is a garden the template tends, not a stack the
mechanism pushes and pops.
