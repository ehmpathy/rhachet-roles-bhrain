# case=7 — a boot over budget halts

feel: sharp · care: critipath

## .narrative

a maintainer adds `rule.always.question-your-first-take` at say. the boot payload grows past the
declared budget. `roleBootClamps` runs in the integration suite and halts:
`✋ ConstraintError: boot payload exceeds its declared budget`, with the fix (condense a min, ref an
entry, or raise `budget.tokens` deliberately). the maintainer tightens a min's cue rows, or sets the
budget to the least multiple of 250 above the new payload. the snapshot diff shows the change to
the reviewer.

## .bdd sketch

```
given role=skeptic with a declared budget.tokens
  when a new say brief pushes the seat payload past the budget
    then [t0] roleBootClamps fails, names the payload and budget, prints the fix
  when the budget is set to the least multiple of 250 above the payload, under 10,000
    then [t1] the clamp passes; the snapshot shows the new figure
  when the budget is set looser than that
    then [t2] the clamp fails: a loose budget is refused too
```
