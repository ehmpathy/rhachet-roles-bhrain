# 1.vision.experience.case=_ — the inventory

the coverage contract. two grains:

- **the cell grain** — every cell of the product, verdicted demoed / itemized / forbidden / impossible,
  lives in the walk tables of `1.vision.experience.dimensions.md` (one table per crossed pair). each
  demoed cell names its case
- **the experience grain** — below: each distinct experience those cells add up to, with its feel,
  care (freq × cost), and boundary count (the seams one walk crosses)

| case | experience | actor | feel | care | boundaries | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | a batch of orders lands, each is tracked, each closes by its done check | commander, achiever | happy | critipath (every session) | 3: talk hook, store, stop hook | demoed · `case=1.orders-tracked-to-proof.md` |
| 2 | an order arrives mid-work, the achiever jumps to it, the first is never covered; stop goes red | achiever | sharp | critipath (the new-ground jump is the most common drop) | 3: talk hook, store, stop hook | demoed · `case=2.untracked-order-reds-the-stop.md` — **clamp 1** |
| 3 | a goal is marked done with no proof of its done check; it stays open | achiever | sharp | critipath (a false done is worse than none) | 2: store, stop hook | demoed · `case=3.done-without-proof-stays-open.md` |
| 4 | a blocker the achiever can cure itself (a locked key, a stale build) is cured at once | achiever | happy | critipath (most blockers are this) | 2: store, the cure tool | demoed · `case=4.self-cure-now.md` |
| 5 | a blocker owned by another repo is dispatched to its owner with an hour; past the hour it reds | achiever, subordinate | happy → sharp | critipath | 4: store, radio, stop hook, clock | demoed · `case=5.dispatch-with-an-hour.md` |
| 6 | the achiever writes "blocked on X" and tries to stop; stop goes red and names the three cures | achiever | sharp | critipath (the exact excuse the wish forbids) | 2: store, stop hook | demoed · `case=6.blocked-on-x-reds-the-stop.md` — **clamp 2** |
| 7 | the one act truly above charter (a human grant) is named as a runnable command, and the stop is allowed | achiever, commander | happy | critipath (the only honest halt) | 3: store, stop hook, a human | demoed · `case=7.above-charter-names-one-act.md` |
| 8 | context compacts; the boot re-reads open orders, and an overdue dispatch is shown first | achiever | happy → sharp | alterpath (rare per session, high cost) | 3: boot hook, store, clock | demoed · `case=8.record-survives-compaction.md` |
| 9 | a dispatched subordinate reports a blocker; the achiever cures it or routes it, the dispatch goal holds the record | achiever, subordinate | happy | critipath — the densest walk | 5: radio, store, cure tool, stop hook, clock | demoed · `case=9.unblock-a-subordinate.md` |
| 10 | the commander says "pause"; open goals are deferred with a when, and the stop passes | commander, achiever | sharp (a hold that traps the commander is the worst feel) | critipath (every session that ends early) | 3: talk hook, store, stop hook | demoed · `case=10.commander-says-pause.md` |
| 11 | a remark or quick question lands in the inventory; it closes as no-work with a why, and the stop passes | commander, achiever | sharp (a stop held over "nice" is noise) | critipath (every session) | 3: talk hook, store, stop hook | demoed · `case=11.remark-closed-in-one-line.md` |
| 12 | an order given on main: the store forbids main, so the order is held and the stop reds until the clone branches | commander, achiever | sharp | critipath (fresh clones and human sessions start on main) | 3: talk hook, store, stop hook | demoed · `case=12.order-on-main-fails-loud.md` |
| 13 | a subordinate pushes a report no goal covers; the stop reads inbound radio tasks and reds | subordinate, achiever | sharp | critipath (the one seam the talk hook never sees) | 3: radio, store, stop hook | demoed · `case=13.subordinate-report-with-no-goal.md` |
| — | a goal with no world check (a taste call) closes by a self-attested why | achiever, commander | happy, with a sharp edge (the soft spot: a cheap why is a new excuse) | alterpath (rare: most asks have a world check) | 2: store, triage view | itemized, with `[t2]`/`[t3]` in case 3 — the close is marked self-attested and listed with its why for the commander; an empty why is refused |
| — | a corrupt inventory line (a crash mid-append) | achiever | sharp | alterpath (rare, but today it fails the gate open) | 2: store, stop hook | itemized — the hooks exit 2 with a `💥 MalfunctionError` that names the file and line, never exit 1 |
| — | an unwritable store | achiever | sharp | alterpath (rare) | 2: talk hook, store | itemized — the talk hook names the lost order and the cause to the clone; the stop exits 2 |
| — | two sessions on one scope | achiever ×2 | sharp | alterpath | 2: store ×2 | itemized — appends are O_APPEND; slug writes are last-writer-wins; a close re-runs its check, so a lost write reads as open (fails safe) |
| — | the achiever games a done check (`echo ok`, `true`) | achiever | sharp | alterpath (rare, costly: a false done) | 2: store, stop hook | itemized, out of this round's scope — the commander can read every check in the record; the refusal is caught as a dream (`dreams/v2026_11_03.feat.a-done-check-can-be-gamed-with-echo.md`) |
| — | correction, constraint, investigate-question tracked | achiever | happy | alterpath | 3 | itemized — same path as case 1; a constraint closes with the work it bounds |
| — | a non-ask remark ("nice") | — | — | — | — | forbidden to require a goal: an over-eager list is noise. it closes as no-work (case 11) |
| — | a dispatch with no hour | — | — | — | — | forbidden: a dispatch with no due time is a drop under a marker, the shape of "blocked on X" |
| — | the commander re-orders or cancels an order | commander | happy | alterpath | 2 | itemized — the order closes as `declined` (with why) or `deferred` (with when), statuses the role adds; marked, never dropped (extant via-systematic-execution rule, "the bound") |
