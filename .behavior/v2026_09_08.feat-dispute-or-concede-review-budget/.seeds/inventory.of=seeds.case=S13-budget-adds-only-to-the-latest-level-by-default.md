## .settled

### 1. 🔴 budget adds only to the LATEST level by default — this REFINES S09

`S09` established the negative half: an exhausted lower level **stays exhausted** unless explicitly
rewound or budgeted. `S13` states the positive half: a plain `route.guard.budget --add N` lands on
the **latest (current) level alone**, never on every level.

⇒ **no contradiction with `S09`** — `S09` governs the reach *beyond* the current level (naught,
unless named); `S13` governs where the add *does* land (the latest level). my `F022` distillation
*"the default reach of a top-up: none"* was over-broad, and this corrects it.

### 2. 🔴 every one of the wisher's directives OVERRIDES the wish — on purpose

the wish is `.what` / `.why` / `.acceptance`; every other line is ground, and a wisher directive
outranks it (`rule.require.wish-outcome-over-proposal`). so each ask this session — the
latest-level budget default, the S12 autonomy nudge, the densification — is a **deliberate
override**, and the vision must frame it as one, in plain words, never as an incidental design call.

### 3. the `reviewed?` judge asks for its OWN budget at end-of-ladder exhaustion — for max autonomy

extends `S12`. once every level is terminal AND every exhausted lane carries a live concession, the
halt is the driver's: it says *"halted on more budget, to address concessions"* and the top-up is
the driver's own lever. a concession on l1 does not hold l3 — l3 runs before any human is named.
