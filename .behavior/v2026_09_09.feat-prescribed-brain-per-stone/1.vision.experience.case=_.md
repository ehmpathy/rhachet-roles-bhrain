## 🔴 .the axis-`E` cell — added at self-review r3

`1.vision.experience.dimensions.md` gained a fifth axis, **entry ordinality**
`{first entry, re-entry after a halt, re-entry after a rewind}`. it collapses onto the 108 walked
cells everywhere but one region, which is itemized here per
`rule.require.experience-catalog-evolution`:

| cell | verdict |
|---|---|
| `driver-clone × apply × absent-after-switch × live × re-entry-after-rewind` | 📋 **itemized** sharp·alter — a rewind into a stone with **no `brain:`** keeps the LATER stone's richer brain. `case=7`'s leak, on the one direction `case=7` cannot reach |

🔴 **the `C` coordinate is `absent-after-switch`, never the `absent-key` union.** it read `absent-key`
until peer review r3, and that was imprecise rather than merely terse:

- the path's own narrative **settles** `C`'s fact 3 — *a later stone declared opus, then the drive
  rewound into a stone that declares none*
- ⇒ a prior stone **has** switched, by construction of the path. `absent-from-launch` is unreachable
  here: with no prior switch there is no inherited brain, so the rewind carries naught forward and
  the cell holds no experience at all
- ⇒ the union label hid that a settled coordinate was settled, so a reader who audited the walk could
  not tell **which** of `C`'s two absent values this cell occupies

⚠️ **and the union is exactly what `C`'s fact 3 was repaired to make visible one round earlier.** to
declare a third fact and then address a cell by a coordinate that spans it un-does the repair in the
peer artifact — the one place a reader checks the walk.

**why itemized rather than demoed**, stated so the skip is checkable:

- **freq** — low. a rewind is an exceptional act, not a step of a normal drive
- **cost** — one stone at the wrong price, and it self-corrects at the next stone that declares one
- **fix** — 🔴 **identical to `case=7`'s**: bhuild's templates stamp the cheap brain on routine stones.
  a demo would restate a demoed critipath with the arrow reversed

⇒ per `rule.require.experience-coverage`'s proportionality clause, **an alterpath may stay itemized.**
its absence is a nitpick, and its presence here is what keeps the coverage contract honest.

⚠️ **the other two ordinals genuinely collapse**, and that is argued in the dimensions doc rather than
asserted: the hook's act is the same on every entry, so a re-entry is either the walked cell or
`case=6`'s same-value dispatch.

🔴 **and `F14` strengthened that collapse rather than disturbed it.** the argument read *"the hook's
act is the same on every entry"*; a convergent applier makes that true **by construction** rather
than by an argument about the hook's branches — there is one act, and it runs at every entry.

## 🔴 .the probe-malfunction family — added at peer review r5 (verification)

`slice 2`'s `apply × {other-known, other-unknown, same-as-live} × {live, deaf}` region assumed the
probe (`clone whoami`) and the dispatch (`clone say`) either succeed or fail with the one clean,
typed rejection `case=2` demos (`unenrolled`, exit 2). the shipped `StoneBrainHaltCause` type names
six more causes — the probe dies by signal, times out at its 2s cap, answers with an unreadable
clone/address/payload, or the dispatch step itself never launches after a good probe.

✅ **demoed — case 11.** all six share the family's argument for why they are one case rather than
six: each renders through the same halt shape `case=2` already demos, and the property none of the
other cases can show is that **the fix line must differ by cause**, on pain of repeating `case=4`'s
exact harm (a reader handed the wrong remedy).

per `1.vision.dimensions.md`'s own discipline for a region that does not multiply the product (the
dispatch-mechanism cell, axis `E`): this is argued there as a dimension orthogonal to `reach` —
`reach` names the driver's TRUE state; this family names whether the INSTRUMENT observing that
state functioned. see `1.vision.experience.dimensions.md` § *the probe-malfunction dimension*.

## 🔴 .the reviewer-brain-readability states — added at peer review r5 (nitpick.1)

`case=9`'s `[t3]`–`[t6]` walk three reviewer rows, each a readable `--brain` value (`opus`,

- **the tool's default** — a `run:` line with no `--brain` flag at all. `rhx review`'s own default

would restate `case=9`'s `[t4]` with two more row shapes and no new claim.
