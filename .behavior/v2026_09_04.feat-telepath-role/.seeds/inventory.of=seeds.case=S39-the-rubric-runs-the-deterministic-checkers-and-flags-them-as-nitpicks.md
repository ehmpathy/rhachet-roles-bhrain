## .settled

**a rubric's deterministic checkers are RUN by the review skill, and their hits are graded
`nitpick`.**

two clauses, and each is separable:

- **run, over cite.** a rubric that merely NAMES its checkers leaves the run to a brain, so the
  cheapest half of the review is the half most likely to be skipped
  - the skill runs them itself, then hands the brain only the residue
  - ⇒ the entoolment ladder pointed the right way: a 🪨 solid skill settles what it can settle, and
    a brain grades the judgment that is left
  - it also shrinks the brain's subject, which is the failure mode the whole canon opens on
- **nitpick, over blocker.** a checker reads a shape and cannot read intent, so a hit is
  mechanically correct and substantively wrong whenever its exempt set is short a case
  - at `nitpick` the driver disputes it in one `.taken` line, or repairs it
  - at `blocker` an imperfect exempt set halts a stone, and the pressure is then to weaken the
    checker rather than to widen the exemption

🟡 **the measured instance is the checker that was live when this was said.**
`review.for.width-ambiguous-glyphs.js` flagged four lines in
`.dream/v2026_09_07.entool.a-telepath-hook-refuses-a-blocked-glyph.md` — a dream
whose DECLARED SUBJECT is that glyph, so every one of the four is a specimen it cannot state its
subject without. the predicate was right and the verdict was wrong.

⇒ so the severity is not a courtesy. it is a property of what a mechanical predicate can know.
