kind: fix · owner: rhachet-roles-bhrain (review/prime) · caught 2026-10-22

## .the cue that fired

`genReviewPrefixPrime.integration.test.ts` `[case7]` (five lanes, one head) went red once, while a
full acceptance run loaded the host:

- `.log/role=mechanic/skill=git.repo.test/what=integration/2026-10-22T21-09-52Z.stderr.log`
- `exactly one prime is sent across all five` → received 2
- outcomes `["primed", "primed"]` — two leaders, no `timeout`, no `lapsed`

unloaded, the same suite passed 4 of 4 (`rhx git.repo.test --what integration --scope
path://genReviewPrefixPrime --mode apply`).

## .the gap, precisely

`genPartOfMeet` decides each lane's part under `withReviewWatermarkHold`. `asPartOfMeet` hands back
`leader` on two paths, and only one of them is the design:

| path | what it is |
|---|---|
| no claim, or a claim older than `ms.await` (an abandoned claim) | design. a lane that waits on the hold past `msOfAwait` (5s in the test) takes over a live claim |
| two holders admitted at once by `withSimpleMutex` over `simple-on-disk-cache` | a defect in the lock or its store, in another package |

the failure does not say which. no log names how long each lane waited on the hold.

### fixed beside it — a solo leader stamped its release twice

found in the same round, and fixed there, so it is no longer owed. a solo close released the
claim in `getOneGatherOfLeader`, then `genReviewPrefixPrime` released it again via
`setReleaseOfClaim` with a later `primedAt`. a trailer that read the first stamp could pour before
the review it trailed departed. `genReviewPrefixPrime` now returns `solo` before the second
release; `[case11]` clamps one stamp (red at 30ms re-stamp with the fix reverted).

## .the shape of the fix

1. record each lane's hold wait (acquire start → acquire end) in the test's event log
2. re-run `[case7]` under induced load (e.g. a CPU-bound peer process) until it reproduces
3. a wait ≥ `msOfAwait` → the takeover is by design. make the case's `msOfAwait` comfortably larger
   than any hold wait a loaded host shows, and assert on it
4. a wait < `msOfAwait` with two `primed` → a double hold. reseed it to `with-simple-mutex` /
   `simple-on-disk-cache` with the reproduction, and clamp it here

## .why it is not done in this round

- SAFE: yes — diagnosis only touches a test
- CLEAN: no — this route clamps role boot budgets. the prime meet is shipped code on main that this
  PR does not touch, and the diagnosis may land in another package
- the harm, if it ships as is: one extra poke (max one output token) per meet that hits it. no
  verdict, no tally, and no review is affected
