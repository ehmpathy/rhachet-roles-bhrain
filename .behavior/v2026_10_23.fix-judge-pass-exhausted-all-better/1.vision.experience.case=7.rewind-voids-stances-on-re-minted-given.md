# case=7 — a rewind re-mints the given; the old stances do not ride along

cell: lane=either · stance=(any, before the rewind) · path=either · row=filename · given=re-minted

feel: sharp · care: critipath · the cure must not let a filename key revive a voided stance

## .narrative

the driver conceded every concern on a lane `better`, then rewound the stone to rework the
artifact. the rework landed byte-identical to before, so the lane's next round mints a given with
the same filename as the one the old stances named.

the driver runs `--as passed` and is asked to declare each concern again — the rewind wiped the
slate, as it did before the cure. had the filename key revived the old stances, the driver would
have passed on concessions made about a stone they chose to redo; instead they answer this round's
given on its own terms.

## .bdd
