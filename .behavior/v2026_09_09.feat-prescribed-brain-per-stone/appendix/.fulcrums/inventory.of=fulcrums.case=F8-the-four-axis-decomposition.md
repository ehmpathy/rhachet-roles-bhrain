# F8 — the experience space is factored on four axes, plus a fifth that collapses

**rework** = clean · **status** = open · **confidence** = 75% ⬇️ (was 80%, lowered at self-review r4)

## .the fork, stated fairly

the decomposition is an **invented taxonomy**, and `rule.always.itemize-the-fulcrums-you-best-guess`
names that as a fulcrum trigger in its own right, whether or not an alternative was weighed.

the axes taken: `actor(2) × goal(3) × declaration(6) × reach(3)` = 108 cells.

## 🔴 .the fifth axis, and why the product stays 108

self-review r3 found a fifth axis — **entry ordinality** `{first, re-entry after a halt, re-entry
after a rewind}` — that sat in neither the axis list nor the rejected list, and was **used by two
demos** (`case=2`'s `[t3]`, `case=3`'s `[t4]`).

it is admitted in `1.vision.experience.dimensions.md` under axis `E`, and it does **not** multiply
the product: the hook's act is identical on every entry, so the cell's verdict does not move — except
on **re-entry after a rewind**, where the live brain is a later stone's and sticky keeps it. that one
region is itemized as `📋 sharp·alter` in `case=_`.

## 🔴 .so the fulcrum's SHAPE changed

| | before r3 | now |
|---|---|---|
| the axes | four, walked | four walked, plus one admitted and argued to collapse |
| the claim | *"four axes is the right decomposition"* | *"four axes walked, plus one that collapses with a named exception"* |
| what a reviewer checks | are these four orthogonal? | 🔴 **and: is the collapse argument sound?** |

⇒ the second row is the material change. the fulcrum once asked whether the axis **set** was right; it
now also asks whether an axis was correctly argued **out of** the product. those are different
questions, and the second is the one r3 opened.

## .taken, and why at the time

- **`reach` earns its place** because its `unenrolled` value is measured, is today's common case, and
  is the value most likely to be dropped by a free-list
- **`declaration` carries six values, not three**, because `absent-from-launch` vs
  `absent-after-switch` is what makes stickiness (`F6`) visible as a cell rather than a footnote, and
  `misspelled-key` is what makes case 4 visible at all
- **`actor` earns its place** because it surfaces that a driver authors guards too, and because it
  splits the `audit` slice into two readers with different levers

## .the axes rejected, and why

recorded in `1.vision.experience.dimensions.md`: switch-outcome (derived from `C × D`), stone-kind
(no variance), concurrency (out of this behavior's scope), brain-vendor (collapses into
`other-unknown`).

🟡 **that list is a copy, and the copy went stale once** — the rejected table gained an
`entry ordinality` row at r3 marked *not rejected*, and this fulcrum still read *"four axes"* for a
full review round after. **a citation is not a link.** the list above is now a pointer rather than a
restatement, so the next amendment lands in one place.

## .the counter-case, stated fairly

⚠️ **`actor` is the weakest axis, and the walk proves it.** slice 4 (`human × prescribe`) mirrors
slice 1 (`driver-clone × prescribe`) **exactly** — 18 cells with identical verdicts, because at
author time a guard is text on disk and text does not know who typed it.

⇒ a three-axis decomposition would have produced 54 cells with no loss of demoed critipaths. the
actor axis pays for itself only in the `apply` and `audit` slices.

it is kept because the mirror is itself a finding worth stating, and because dropping the axis would
hide that author time is actor-blind — which is the same blindness `F3` is about.

## .rework, and why

code, and no later stone hardens against the axis set.

## .confidence, and why 75%

the axes are orthogonal and the hidden-dimension check was run and answered on the page. two doubts,
and the second is new:

| the doubt | weight |
|---|---|
| the `actor` axis above — a reviewer could reasonably call an 18-cell exact mirror evidence of a non-orthogonal axis rather than a finding | 20% |
| 🔴 **a fifth axis was absent from both lists until r3.** the hidden-dimension check ran and did not catch it, because that check tests declared axes for invariance and cannot report an axis nobody declared | 5% |

would be found the same way, and nobody has run that grep on the four axes above.
