- kind = fix
- owner = `ehmpathy/rhachet-roles-bhrain`

## .the cue that fired

a `has-consistent-mechanisms` self review found `formatTreeBucket` and `formatSelfReviewRoster`
each derive one concept under two names — `labelMarker`/`cont` against `elbow`/`stem`. the repair
was an owner, `asTreeBranchMarkers`, and both were wired to it.

🔴 **then the grep that should have come FIRST came second.** the derivation is not a two-site
duplication. it sits in **19 further prod files**, and `src/contract/cli/goal.ts` alone carries a
dozen of them.

`isLast ? '   ' : '│  '` for the column beneath it:

**checkable:** `rhx grepsafe --pattern "'└─' : '├─'" --glob '*.ts'` over `src/`, minus the two
wired call sites and `asTreeBranchMarkers` itself.

🟡 **the variants are the sharp part**, because they are what a mechanical sweep would get wrong.
they do NOT all agree:

| variant | seen at |
|---|---|
| `isLast ? '   ' : '│  '` — the common stem | most sites |
| `isLast ? ' ' : '│'` — a ONE-space stem | `contract/cli/reflect.ts:304,514` |
| `total === 1 ? '' : isLast ? '   ' : '│  '` — a third arm for the singleton | `contract/cli/goal.ts:262` |
| a constant `├─` with no elbow arm at all | `formatHashbarRetired.ts:24` (deliberate — a `│` follows) |

⇒ so the owner's current signature covers the common case and **not** the three variants. a sweep
that assumed one shape would silently re-indent two cli surfaces.

## .the shape of the fix

1. widen `asTreeBranchMarkers` to carry the variants, or add a peer for the one-space stem — do
   **not** force one shape onto call sites that deliberately differ
2. convert per subsystem, never repo-wide in one commit — `contract/cli/goal.ts` alone is a
   reviewable unit by itself
3. **bite-check each subsystem**: alter the shared owner, confirm that subsystem's snapshots go
   red, restore, confirm green. these are snapshot-heavy surfaces, so a wrong stem is caught
   loudly — but only if the snapshots actually cover the converted lines
4. the singleton arm at `goal.ts:262` and the one-space stems at `reflect.ts` want a deliberate
   verdict, never a silent normalization

## .why it is not done in this round

**SAFE ✅ / CLEAN 🔴.**

the fix is safe — it is pure glyph derivation, and these surfaces are snapshot-covered. it is
**not clean**: it opens `contract/cli/goal.ts`, `reflect.ts`, `route.ts`, `learn.ts` and a dozen
domain operations that this round — a self-review-trigger repair — never touched.

⇒ that is the **smuggled refactor** `rule.always.fix-forward-under-scouts-honor` names outright:
*"a large change that ripples ridden along under scouts honor, which is what the CLEAN question
refuses."*

🟡 **and the round's yield already flags scope as its own live concern** — it delivers four
independent repairs to one gate and states that as a question the council may want to split. a
fifth subsystem would worsen exactly that.

## 🔴 .the lesson this one paid for

> **an extraction's VALUE and an extraction's SCOPE are answered by the same grep, and I ran it
> after I had already written the claim.**

the owner shipped with a `.note` that asserted *"this is the fourth, so it gets an owner rather
than a fifth."* **the number was invented.** it named three specific prior copies from memory and
did not check. the real count is 21.

⇒ the correction is in the file, and the general form is sharper than the fix: **a count in a
docblock is a claim, and a claim needs the same evidence a test does.** this is the round's
**second** false claim shipped in my own new prose — the first was a dream that asserted a build
sequence `package.json:65` disproves.

⚠️ **both were TRUE-SOUNDING and neither was load-bearing for correctness** — which is why neither
tripped a test, a type, or a lint. the only instrument that catches them is a reader who checks.
