# philosophy.entoolment-is-the-pinnacle.concepts-vs-capacity

## .what

a **zoom-in** of `philosophy.entoolment-is-the-pinnacle._.md` onto the one distinction the whole
ladder rests on:

> 📚 a **brief** confers **prethought concepts** — what a brain thinks *with*.
> 💪 a **skill** confers **prethought capacity** — what a brain no longer has to *do*.

both are **knowledge**. that is the point of the pair, and the reason `knowledge` alone cannot name
either half.

## .why `knowledge` is the wrong word for a brief

the obvious gloss is *"a brief holds knowledge, a skill holds execution."* it reads plainly and it
is wrong, for a mechanical reason:

> **`knowledge` is the genus, not the differentia.**

a skill carries knowledge too — of the sequence, of the order, of the path, of which trap to avoid
at step 4. so `knowledge` names what both halves **share** and parts them not at all. a word that
applies equally to both sides of a contrast is not a contrast word.

`concepts` and `capacity` are two **species** of that one genus, and each names what only its own
half has:

| | the artifact | what it confers | what the reader gains |
|---|---|---|---|
| 📚 | a **brief** | prethought **concepts** | a thought to reason **with** |
| 💪 | a **skill** | prethought **capacity** | a step they need not **do** |

## .`concepts` is adopted, never coined

the librarian already declares it. `kno101.primitives.1.ontology.[article].frame.docs_as_materializations`
sets out three layers:

> **concepts** (patterns in reality) → materialized via → **documents** (organized arrangements of
> instances) → grounded in → **instances** (atoms of raw data/experience)

and it states the relation directly: *"documents are concept-detection and transmission devices."*

⇒ **a brief is a document.** so *"a brief confers prethought concepts"* is not a new claim — it is
the librarian's ontology, read at the rung the ladder cares about. the word was already settled
here; `knowledge` was the drift off it.

## .the test — for an artifact you are about to write

> **"does the reader end up with a thought to REASON WITH, or a step they no longer have to DO?"**

| the answer | the artifact |
|---|---|
| a concept they now hold — a distinction, a rule, a why | 📚 a **brief** — rung 1 |
| a step they no longer perform — the sequence is written, or run | 💪 a **skill** — rungs 2–4 |
| **both** | 🔴 the common case. write both, and pair them |

🟡 **the third row is not a fence-sit.** it is what most real work produces, and the pair is the
strongest artifact the practice makes — see below.

## .capacity without concepts is a black box; concepts without capacity is a lecture

the two failure modes are symmetric, and each is a real defect:

| you shipped | the reader gets | the failure |
|---|---|---|
| a 🪨 solid skill, no brief | a call that works | correct until the day it is wrong, and **unarguable** when it is — the reason it was shaped that way lives nowhere |
| a 📚 brief, no skill | a grasp of the why | they must still perform every step, **every time**. the derivation is amortized; the execution never is |

⇒ so the pair is the artifact: **a brief carries the judgment and the reach-for-it knowledge that
no crystallized branch holds.** a skill nobody knows to reach for is unreachable pavement.

## .this is why the climb never retires a rung

`philosophy.entoolment-is-the-pinnacle._.md` states that a higher rung does not retire the rungs
below it. **the concepts/capacity split is the reason.**

the climb mechanizes **capacity** — one branch at a time, as the operation crystallizes. it
mechanizes **no concept at all**. so the brief that explains *why the skill does what it does* is
untouched by every rung the skill climbs, because it was never on that axis.

⇒ *"we entooled it, delete the brief"* trades a concept away to buy capacity that was already
bought. the rung tells you **what a use costs**, never **what may now be thrown away.**

## .the cues — when → then

| when… | then… |
|---|---|
| you are about to write *"prethought knowledge"* | 🔴 stop — the genus. say `concepts` or `capacity` |
| you finish a skill and feel done | ask what concept a reader needs to know it exists, and when to reach for it. that is its brief |
| you finish a brief that describes a sequence | ask what a reader must still *do* every time. that is its skill |
| you are tempted to delete a brief after a skill lands | 🔴 the skill mechanized capacity, never the concept. the brief stays |
| a skill fails and no one can say what it was *supposed* to guarantee | the absent brief is the defect, never the skill |

## .enforcement

- `knowledge` used as the word a brief is contrasted **against** = **blocker** — it is the genus
  both halves share, so the contrast it draws is empty
- a 🪨 solid skill shipped with no brief behind it = **blocker** — a black box, correct until it is
  wrong and unarguable when it is
- a brief deleted because its subject was entooled = **blocker** — the rung measures cost, not
  what may be discarded
