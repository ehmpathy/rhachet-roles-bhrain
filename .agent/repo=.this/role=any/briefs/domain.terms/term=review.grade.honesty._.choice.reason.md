# domain.term.choice.reason: honesty

## .etymology

the meter's own declaration names it: `ReviewRunGrade`'s doc says the gap between "naught was
spent" and "we did not record it" *"is the whole honesty of this meter."* the family word was
already spoken at the type; this cluster gives it an address beside its two peer families,
`attribution` and `cache`.

rejected:

| rejected | why |
|---|---|
| `accuracy` | grades a figure against the truth. an `unknown` run has no figure to be accurate or not |
| `certainty` | reads as a scale. the family is three positions, each a different kind of claim |
| `coverage` | names the census's share of the corpus — the count beside a sum — never one run's grade |
| `confidence` | forbidden already at the genus `grade`: a degree of belief, where each position here is a fact about what was recorded. carried at the family so a reader of this cluster alone meets the ban |

## .the evidence — three positions, and why not two

two positions (`priced` / `unpriced`) would merge `refused` with `unknown`. both lack metrics,
and they render as opposites: a refusal never reached the brain, so `$0` is a fact; an unknown
run may have been billed, so `$0` would be a lie (`rule.forbid.failhide`). the third position is
what keeps a known nil apart from an absence.

## .the boundary

"honesty, of WHAT?" — of a run's spend, as the meter grades it. one answer, so the cluster sits
under `review.grade` beside `review.attribution` and `prompt.cache`, the other two families.

## .disputes

none.
