# domain.term.choice.reason: lesson

## .etymology
`lesson` is the word a surf instructor and a surfer both say for one booked unit of
instruction. chosen over `class` (implies a group) and `session` (also names an auth login,
so it would overload one word onto two concepts).

## .disputes
none.

## .evidence
- discovery: the surf school's own flow — a surfer books a lesson, an instructor teaches a lesson
