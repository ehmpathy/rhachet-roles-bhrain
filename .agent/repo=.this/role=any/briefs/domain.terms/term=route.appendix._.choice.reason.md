# domain.term.choice.reason: appendix

## .etymology

latin *appendere*, "to hang upon". an appendix hangs upon the body it belongs to — it is
**subordinate and attached**, never free to stand alone. that is exactly the relation a
route's evidence has to its yield: the yield states the claim, the appendix carries the
weight under it, and neither reads correctly alone.

the word is borrowed from print, where it has meant this for centuries. a reader who has
ever opened a book already knows what to expect behind the name — the fact behind the
claim, not a second claim.

## .why each synonym is forbidden

| the candidate | why it loses |
|---|---|
| `evidence` | names the CONTENT, so it cannot also name the container. and a `.given` carries evidence too — the word is already in use one boundary over |
| `backup` | reads as a copy kept against loss. an appendix is not a copy of the yield; it is the part the yield deliberately did not carry |
| `support` | too weak to part from `archive`. a rejected idea also "supported" the decision, when it was ruled out |
| `annex` | a near-synonym with no distinct sense, so it is pure drift (`rule.forbid.domain-term-synonyms`) |

## 🔴 .why it must be argued with `archive`, and never alone

the two dirs are declared in the same rule, with **opposed** senses. settle one alone and
the reader inherits a word with no boundary: *"evidence about the drive"* covers both, and
the next author files a rejected fork under `appendix/` in perfect good faith.

⇒ this is the `given`/`taken` lesson applied before the cost was paid rather than after.
those two were argued together, and the pair is why neither drifted. the same shape holds
here, so the same discipline is owed.

**the test, at the moment a file is written:** *"is the decision this backs IN the yield?"*

- yes → `appendix/`
- no, it was ruled out → `archive/`
- there is no decision, only a record of the round → 🔴 neither. git already holds it

## .the boundary — `route`, not repo-wide

both dirs live under `$route/`, and their sense is bound to a yield. a repo-level `docs/`
dir that carries background material is **not** an appendix in this sense, and must not
borrow the word.

## .evidence

- declared: `rule.always.yield-the-output-not-the-archaeology` (bhrain/driver) states both
  dirs and both senses, in one table
- first instance: `.behavior/v2026_09_03.fix-contemplation-gate-on-entrance/appendix/`
  **created 2026-09-04** — which is what made the gap visible. a word declared in a booted
  brief and unpaved until a drive reached for it
- precedent for the pair discipline: `term=route.guard.review.given` /
  `…taken` — argued together, and neither has drifted since

## .invariants

- an appendix is **cited by path** from the yield. an uncited appendix is unreachable, so
  it is archaeology under an appendix's name
- an appendix never holds a rejected idea. that is `archive/`
- a route may hold both dirs, either, or neither
