# domain.term.choice.reason: concede

## .etymology

latin *concedere* — *to yield, to give way* (`con-` fully + `cedere` to give ground). the english
sense carries a cost: one concedes **a point in an argument one was party to**.

⇒ that cost is the reason the word fits. a concession here is not neutral assent — **it is an
admission that the driver holds a debt**, and the tally keeps the concern precisely so the debt
stays visible until the repair lands.

## .why `accept` and `agree` lost

| candidate | why not |
|---|---|
| `accept` | too passive, and ambiguous about **who acts next**. one accepts a delivery and owes naught; one concedes a point and owes a repair |
| `agree` | names a state of mind. the design needs a **declared act** the gate can read |
| `acknowledge` | ⛔ the sharpest miss: to acknowledge a concern is to say it was **received**. a driver who acknowledges has not said whether they think it right |
| `admit` | carries guilt rather than debt, and reads as a verdict on the driver |
| `own` | ⛔ overloaded — ownership is already the route's word for who holds a lever (`rule.always.spend-own-levers-before-escalation`) |

🟡 **`acknowledge` is the one worth the row.** the whole gate turns on a driver who has taken a
position; a word that means *"seen"* would satisfy it while the position stayed undeclared.

## .why it must symmetrize the dispute

a driver who reads four concerns and argues one is honest. a driver who reads one and declares about
the lane is not — and **the two look identical unless a concession exists at the same grain as a
dispute.**

⇒ so the concession is not ceremony. **it is the artifact that makes the silence detectable**, and
without it the gate can only ever ask *"did you declare at all?"* rather than *"did you declare on
each?"*

## 🔴 .the mirror — an over-claim is the same defect, run backwards

| the move, at coarse grain | what it does silently |
|---|---|
| **dispute** a lane | **suppression** — drops concerns the driver never argued |
| **concede** a lane | **over-claim** — commits the driver to concerns they never read |

⇒ a rule aimed at a dispute alone catches half of it, which is why
`rule.forbid.suppression-of-undeclared-concerns` is stated over **declarations** rather than over
disputes. the twelve-row walk: `1.vision.experience.case=11`.
