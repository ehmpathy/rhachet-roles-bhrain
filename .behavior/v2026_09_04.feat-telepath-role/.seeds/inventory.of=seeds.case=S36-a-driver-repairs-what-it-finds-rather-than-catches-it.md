## .settled

**a driver's default on a found defect is REPAIR. a caught dream is what the default falls back to
when the fix fails the safe/clean test — never the first move.**

- the two are not peers a driver picks between
  - repair is the rule; a dream is the exception's receipt
- ⇒ so a round that catches more than it repairs has inverted the default, whatever each individual
  call looked like

🟡 **the claim is about the DEFAULT, not about the deferral contract.** a genuinely dirty fix still
defers, still owes a dream, and still owes a fulcrum. what is corrected is which of the two a driver
reaches for first.

## .the cause was a SPLIT, and the repair is DELIVERY rather than a third rule

both halves already existed and neither was wrong. what was wrong is where each lived:

| the half | where it sat | did a driver boot it? |
|---|---|---|
| repair — `rule.always.fix-forward-under-scouts-honor` | the achiever | no |
| catch — `rule.always.catch-dreams-for-followups` | the learner | yes, via the inherited learner traits |

⇒ so mid-drive the only cue a driver held was a `.dream/` template, and a template is a stronger
pull than a clause in a rule it does not carry.

🟡 **the first repair drafted was a third rule in the driver's own coordinates, and the wisher
refused it** — *"why wouldnt we just put this under the driver?"*, then *"and the dream one too, out
of the learner, and into the driver"*.

- `rule.require.specialize-a-rule-its-readers-look-past` fires on a reader who READ a rule and
  looked past it
  - its own boundary table says so
- ⇒ a driver never read either half, so the trigger was never met
  - the defect was **absence**, and the cure for an absent rule is to deliver it
- ⇒ and the act is the driver's on its merits: a defect found mid-drive, priced against the diff
  already open. a dream is a deferred TASK rather than a retained lesson
