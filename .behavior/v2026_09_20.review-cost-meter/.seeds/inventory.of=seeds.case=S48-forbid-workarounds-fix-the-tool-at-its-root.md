## .settled

- a workaround is forbidden. no record, dream, or `.taken` makes one acceptable: the extant rules
  that let a workaround stand once it is written down are narrowed by this one
- a failure is diagnosed to its root cause before any input is patched. "my input was wrong" is a
  hypothesis to test, never the default read
- the dogfooder carries the strongest form of this duty. when the tool that fails is the tool under
  construction, its failure IS the defect report, and a patch to the input hides the report from
  the one party able to act on it
- a defect in a tool under construction is never "acceptable" and never "the tool did its job." a
  refusal that fires at the costliest moment, or a contract that needs hand upkeep to stay sound,
  is a design defect even where the refusal itself is correct
- a pit of success is the bar: correct use asks no questions and meets no friction. the tool
  guides its caller, and the right move is the easy move. the wrong state is unreachable, or
  caught once at the cheapest moment with the exact fix printed
- any friction is a critical defect, fixed in the round that surfaced it — never a nitpick, a
  follow-up, or a dream
- the driver's own conduct is held to it too: a pile of stale background waiters is friction the
  driver made, and it is cleaned up, not left to accrue

- the rule boots at `say`: the moment it must fire recurs every round, and a rule fetched on
  demand is never fetched at that moment
- it belongs upstream too, in ehmpathy's mechanic and architect, dispatched once radio returns
