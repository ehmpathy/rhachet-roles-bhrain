# rule.always.build-never-relay.example=the-nineteen-hour-relay

2026-11-19, repo uladkasach/nheuron, a director seat. for 19 hours the seat relayed word between seats and moved the hour of goals, and built naught. the human named the pattern.

| the tell | what a read of the transcript shows | the law's cure |
|---|---|---|
| a goal re-houred with no act between | the hour moved, no file, verb, tree, or pr made since the last hour | the second re-hour owes a builder seated that turn: a tree, a fork, or the skill owner |
| a relay as the turn's output | a peer's word restated, no read behind it | the read first; then the act it calls for |

what a later read must show: each hour moved on a goal beside the artifact made since the hour before.
