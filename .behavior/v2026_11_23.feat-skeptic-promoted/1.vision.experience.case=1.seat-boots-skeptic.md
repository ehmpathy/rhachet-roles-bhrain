rule's min. the boot stats line reads under budget. the role ships no hooks: the word checks
the pioneer held were cut (F042), and the instinct is opt-in, added only by `rhx instinct.of --role
skeptic set --as enabled`. the seat starts work with one reflex: before a take leaves, name its
counter-take and run one read.
