# define.skeptic.mechanisms-ruled-out

## .what

the mechanisms this role has weighed for doubt of one's own thought, and rejected — each with the reason it fails. read it before you propose a mechanism for this role; a proposal that matches a row here must answer that row's reason first.

the subject is doubt of **your own** thought, in any domain. a mechanism tied to one command, or aimed at acts against the world, is not this role's.

## .the rows

| mechanism | why it fails | what holds instead |
|---|---|---|
| a Stop check that reads the reply's words (a state word, a superlative, a question shape, "I do not know") | it grades words, never whether the thought was checked. it flagged honest replies as often as stale ones; a reader learns to skip a check that cries wolf, so it catches none, as a block or as a notice | the stance at boot; any future check flags a contradiction with a recorded fact |
| "backed" = a tool call of this turn | a new prompt opens a turn, so every prior read reads as unread with no source moved | staleness belongs to the source; a cited evidence file whose source has not moved is a read (`rule.always.trust-but-verify`) |
| a prediction attached to one command (`--expect pass` on a test, `--expect 0 blockers` on a review) | no one runs a test or a review that they expect to fail, so the prediction is fixed and carries no information; it is also ad hoc, tied to a command rather than to thought | predict what you are unsure of — a cause, a file, a count — wherever a look follows (`rule.always.predict-before-you-look`) |
| a fresh-context counter-take as a skeptic tool | it is review: another context judges your claim. the reviewer role owns review | the self's own counter-take, named and tested by a read (`rule.always.trust-no-one-especially-not-yourself`) |
| a bare "are you sure?" prompt, or a re-read of your own answer | doubt with no outside signal grows no more right, and often less (Huang et al. 2023, arXiv 2310.01798) | doubt that ends in a read: the read is the outside signal |
| debate between copies of one model | it does no better than a vote, and raises confidence without accuracy | a read; or another role's review |
| a gate before an irreversible act | it guards acts against the world, not one's own thought | out of scope here; a guard role's subject |

## .the test, before a new proposal

> **is it general (any claim, any domain), fed by a signal from outside the self, and silent unless a recorded fact contradicts you?**

any no → name the row above it matches, and why that row's reason does not apply.

## .the direction chosen

how a doubt becomes a reflex rather than a recall, for a mind whose only levers are its context and its tools: a subconscious brain that asks, per turn, which claims should have been skepticized, against a durable record of the ones that were (`define.skeptic.instinct-via-subconscious`). it differs from row 1 in what it asks: row 1 asked whether a thought was checked, which words cannot show; the subconscious asks whether a basis is stated and cited, which words do show.
