# F038 · `absorb` composes `contemplate` and the concern disposition

- **rework** = clean (the noun/verb move) + **dirty** (the added gate + `contemplated`→`absorbed`) · **confidence** = wisher-ruled · **status** = ruled 2026-09-15

## .the fork

the wisher renamed the concern-disposition verb to `absorb` (`F007`), then asked whether `absorb`
should also swallow `contemplate`. two shapes were on the table:

| shape | what it does |
|---|---|
| **A — rename only** | `stanced`→`setStoneAsAbsorbed` at the concern grain; `contemplate` (the per-reviewer `.taken`) stays a separate, unchanged gate |
| **B — composition** | `absorb` names two grains: absorb a concern (disputed\|conceded) AND absorb the feedback (the `.taken`, renamed `absorbed`), with the coarse gated on the fine |

## .taken, and why

**both — A and B, ruled by the wisher.** and B is a **composition**, never a merge:

> *"its not a teardown. its an ADDITIONAL gate before the `.taken` file will even be accepted"* ·
> *"its a rename and added gate: before you can absorb the feedback, you must absorb each concern"*

so B is precisely two moves, and the `.taken` is retired by neither:

| the move | grain | what it is |
|---|---|---|
| the rename | feedback | `contemplated` → `absorbed`. the `.taken` stays |
| the added gate | composition | the `.taken` is REFUSED until every concern of that given is absorbed |

⇒ `feedbackAbsorbed(given) ⟺ ∀ concern : absorbed(concern)` — the whole is composed of its parts,
and the added gate is the precondition that makes the composition hold. the law is
`define.invariant.review.peer.absorb`.

## .rework, and why

**split.** the noun/verb rename (`stance`→`absorption`, `setStoneAsStanced`→`setStoneAsAbsorbed`) is
`clean` — a sedreplace of a standalone word plus ~8 function/type renames. the `contemplated`→
`absorbed` rename and the added precondition gate are **dirty**: they touch the contemplation
subsystem, `converge-with-reviewers.via-a-taken-per-point`, `rule.forbid.unanswered-exits-from-a-blocker`,
and a large snapshot surface.

## .confidence

**wisher-ruled** — the shape is the wisher's, verbatim (`S20`). the DIRT grade is mine.
