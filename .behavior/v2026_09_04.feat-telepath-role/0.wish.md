# wish: the telepath role — a bhrain that writes terse, and grades prose that is not

## .the outcome

`ehmpathy/rhachet-roles-bhrain` carries a **telepath** role: the generic communication role for
bhrains. it holds the vocabulary and the rules for prose that a reader parses in one pass, and it
is reachable as a reviewer so a route can grade its own artifacts against them.

done when:

- the role exists and enrolls — `rhx enroll --roles telepath` yields an actor with briefs + skills
- its briefs carry the terse-prose canon the ten open issues below ask for
- a **reviewer** exists that grades an artifact against those briefs and renders a verdict the
  route guard can read (`contract.reviewer-output` — two numeric lines)
- the reviewer is cheap enough to run on every yield-producing stone

## .why now

prose bloat is not a tidiness problem here — it broke a route. measured on
`ehmpathy/test-fns @ .behavior/v2026_08_31.feat-tempdir-autoprune`: a **4.5k-token wish** grew
**155.8k of yields** and a **366k behavior dir** — a 35x amplification into the yields alone. four
reviewers overflowed their context gate for 9+ iterations and rendered **no verdict at all**,
and an unreviewed reviewer is indistinguishable from a clean one.

so terse prose is a correctness property of the review pipeline, not a style preference. the rules
already exist (`rule.require.brevity`, `rule.forbid.rambles`, `rule.forbid.chronological-accretion`,
`define.wick-dense`) — what is absent is a role that owns them and a reviewer that grades against them.
