# catalog.of=glyph.axis=rung

## .what

the glyphs that mark a position on the **determinism spectrum** — how much of an artifact runs
with no brain at all.

rungs 2–4 and their glyphs are **rhachet's**, adopted rather than coined. rungs 0–1 are ours.

## .the members

| glyph | rung | what it is | what a brain still spends |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🧠 | 0 | tribal knowledge — it lives in one head | all of it, every time, per traveler |
| 📚 | 1 | a brief — the derivation is externalized | the whole application of it |
| 💧 | 2 | a fluid skill — *probabilistic throughout* | the whole execution |
| 🔩 | 3 | a rigid skill — *deterministic entrypoint, mixed operations + orchestration* | the probabilistic branches only |
| 🪨 | 4 | a solid skill — *deterministic throughout* | none |

the quoted definitions are `ehmpathy/rhachet`'s own words, from
`define.term.skill.thought-routes`.

## .what the ladder ranks by — imagine cost, and risk

rhachet declares the measure:

> **imagine cost** = time + tokens to have brain figure out what to do · **compute cost** = cpu
> cycles to execute deterministic logic … to harden a route = to shift work from imagine-cost to
> compute-cost.

⇒ **that is the axis, and it needs no second vocabulary.** time and tokens are the root cost; a
unit layered on top of them is indirection that hides which of the two a call actually spent.

🟡 **and there is a third axis the cost words do not carry: RISK.**

- a probabilistic step can be *wrong*, never merely slow
- ⇒ so a climb up this ladder buys two things at once — less imagine cost, and less variance in the
  outcome
  - the second is often the larger win
