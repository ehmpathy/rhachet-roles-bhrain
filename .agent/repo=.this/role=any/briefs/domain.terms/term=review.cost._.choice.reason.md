# domain.term.choice.reason: cost

## .etymology

settled 2026-09-20, against `budget`, when three cost surfaces were grouped under one name.

the wisher proposed `budget` twice. it was taken as a best guess on the **aspirational**
reading — that an allowance was intended and a `set` half would follow — then overturned.

## .the argument that settled it

two readings pick different words:

| reading | the word it earns |
|---|---|
| an allowance IS intended (`--max '$5'`, a guard that halts a lane which would blow it) | `budget` |
| read-only: a figure of what was already spent | **`cost`** |

today's surfaces are read-only, and `budget` promises a constraint that does not exist.

the decisive ground was the overload, not the tense. `route.guard.budget` already owns `budget`
for the round allowance. two commands a driver types in one session, one word over two
concepts — `rule.forbid.domain-term-ambiguity` outright.

the driver first raised that collision, then judged it weaker than stated — on the ground that
rounds and dollars are both scarce resources, so it might be one concept over two resources.
that retreat was wrong. an allowance and a record of spend are not one concept, whatever the
resource.

## .the rejected peers

| word | why not |
|---|---|
| **budget** | an allowance. overloads `route.guard.budget` at the same driver's fingertips |
| **spend** | reads as a verb first, and the surfaces are nouns (`review.cost.tree`) |
| **ledger** | implies a transaction log a reader can walk; these surfaces report aggregates |
| **bill** | implies an invoice owed to someone. the money is already gone |

## .the invariant

> a cost figure is measured or it is absent. it is never inferred.

no rate card is applied to a total, and an unrecorded run is graded `unknown` and **excluded**
from every sum — never rendered `$0.00`. the distinction between *"charged naught"* (`refused`,
a fact) and *"not recorded"* (`unknown`, an absence) is the whole reason the grade exists.

## .disputes

### dispute: budget — raised 2026-09-20 — status: RESOLVED (keep `cost`)

- claim = the surfaces should be `review.budget.*`, since an allowance half is a natural
  follow-on and the name may lead the feature
- counter = `route.guard.budget` owns `budget` for the round allowance; the overload lands
  across two commands one driver types in one session
- resolution = keep `cost`; record `budget` as a forbidden synonym. ⇒ if an allowance ships, it
  is `review.cost.*.set --max`, which keeps one word for one concept
