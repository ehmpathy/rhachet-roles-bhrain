## .the gap, precisely

this repo ships the mechanism: a `.guard` may declare `brain: <arg>`, and the driver hook applies it
on stone entry (`parseStoneGuard` → `applyStoneBrainOnEntry` → `setStoneBrain` →
`dispatchBrainSwitch`).

⇒ **the mechanism has no callers.** every guard in the wild is stamped from a bhuild template, and no
template emits `brain:`. the vision's economy claim — mechanical stones run a cheap brain — is the
unshipped half.

## .the shape of the fix

in the bhuild route-template stamper:

1. **pick the axis** — reflect, repair, and verification-rerun stones take a cheap brain; vision,
   criteria, and blueprint stones do not
2. **stamp the key** — a top-level inline `brain: <arg>` beside `artifacts:` / `reviews:` /
   `judges:` / `protect:`
3. 🟡 **the value is the brain-cli's own `/model` argument, verbatim** — never a rhachet brainslug
   (`F10`). `claude-sonnet-5[1m]` is right; `anthropic/claude/sonnet` reaches `/model` unchanged and
   fails
4. **leave the key absent elsewhere** — an absent `brain:` keeps the inherited brain at zero I/O
