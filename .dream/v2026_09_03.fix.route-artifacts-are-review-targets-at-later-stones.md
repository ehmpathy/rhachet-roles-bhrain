- **kind** = `fix`
- **owner** = `ehmpathy/rhachet-roles-bhrain`
- **caught** 2026-09-03, on the `v2026_09_03.fix-contemplation-gate-on-entrance` route @ `1.vision`

## .the cue that fired

the wish fenced four items out of scope and this was its fourth. it stayed uncomfortable through
five self-review rounds without a home, which is the tell that it is a real followup rather than a
stray thought.

## .the gap, precisely

`0.wish.md` and `1.vision.yield.md` are `targetFiles` for peer reviewers at later stones — the wish
cites `5.1.execution`. so an execution-stage reviewer grades the **frozen output of a stone that
already passed**.

two costs, and the second is the one that compounds:

1. **the driver cannot act on it.** a blocker raised against `1.vision.yield.md` at stone 5.1 asks
   the driver to edit an artifact whose stone is closed. the honest response is *"out of scope"*,
   which is a `[REFUTE]` the driver must author every round.
2. 🔴 **it manufactures exactly the debt this behavior now enforces.** once the entrance gate lands,
   an unanswerable blocker against a frozen artifact **holds the stone** rather than dies on the
   next hash change. the fix makes this defect louder, never quieter.

⇒ **the interaction is why this cannot stay merely noted.** before the contemplation fix, a
reviewer's complaint about a frozen artifact died with the next edit. after it, the driver owes a
`.taken` for it every round, forever, or a human overrules.

## .the shape of the fix

the scope is per-stone and already declarative — each guard's `review.peer` entry carries its own
target globs, and `input.scope.json` records the resolved `targetFiles` per reviewer. so the
candidate shapes, cheapest first:

- **narrow the globs per stone** — a `5.x` guard targets `src/**` and its own stage artifacts,
  never `0.wish.md` / `1.vision.*`. purely a template change; no engine change at all
- **mark passed-stone artifacts read-only to reviewers** — supply them as *context* rather than as
  *subjects*. `define.review-ubiqlang.supplies-vs-subjects` already draws this exact line, so the
  vocabulary exists and the distinction is paved
- ⚠️ **do NOT drop them from the prompt.** a reviewer needs the wish and the vision as **supplies**
  to judge whether execution matches intent. the defect is that they are **subjects**, never that
  they are present

⇒ the second shape is likely right, because the first re-derives per stone what the supplies/
subjects split already states once.

### 🔴 measured 2026-09-07 — BOTH shapes are already in-tree, on one guard

every peer lane's `--paths-with` was read from the `5.1` and `5.3` guards of this route:

| stone | the binds | route artifacts in `targetFiles`? |
|---|---|---|
| **5.1** | every lane binds `'**/*.{ts,sh,md,snap}'` | 🔴 **yes** — `**/*.md` sweeps `0.wish.md`, `1.vision.yield.md`, every `.taken` |
| **5.3** | `src/**` · `{src/contract,blackbox}/**` · `blackbox/**` · `**/*.test.ts` · `**/*.snap` · `**/__snapshots__/**` · `{src,blackbox}/**/*.test.ts` | ✅ **no** — not one lane |

⇒ **shape 1 is proven to work, and it landed by accident.** the 5.3 binds were narrowed to stop a
context overflow, and they fenced the route artifacts out as a side effect. **nobody chose it and
nobody recorded it** — this route's own yield asserted the exposure was live at 5.3 for three stones.

⚠️ **which is the argument AGAINST shape 1, now with evidence.** a fence that exists as a side effect
of an unrelated repair is reopened by the next person who widens a glob for an unrelated reason, and
no rule fires. it is not a fence; it is a coincidence that currently points the right way.

⇒ **shape 2 is likewise in-tree, deliberately, and it is the pattern to generalize.**
`behavior-experience-coverage` supplies route artifacts as refs:

```
--refs '$route/1.vision.experience.case=*.md' --refs '$route/1.vision.experience.dimensions.md'
```

**the reviewer reads the vision to judge the code, and cannot raise a blocker against the vision.**
that is precisely the supplies/subjects split, already written, already run, on one lane of one
guard — so the fix is to **make that lane's pattern the rule**, never to invent a mechanism.

⚠️ **and the 5.1 half is worse than its bind reads.** `'**/*.{ts,sh,md,snap}'` puts a brace in the
**extension** slot, which `parseReviewArgs` drops silently — so those lanes ran **unbounded**, wider
than `**/*.md`. the route artifacts were subjects there by the bind's *failure*, not its intent. ⇒
that defect is its own dream (`v2026_09_04.fix.review-multi-glob-flags-do-not-comma-split.md`), and
**the two compose**: a glob written to exclude route artifacts would have been dropped anyway.

## .why it is not done in this round

**safe? yes. clean? no.**

- the wish fences it explicitly — *"do NOT let these expand it"*, item 4 — so to fix it here defies
  a stated bound
- it touches **route templates across every behavior**, not the contemplation gate. the diff would
  share no file with this one
- it wants its own experience walk: which artifacts are supplies at which stones is a matrix, and a
  guess made mid-drive would be the kind of best-guess that ripples

⇒ dirty, so it is caught rather than fixed — and the fulcrum for the deferral is recorded with it.
