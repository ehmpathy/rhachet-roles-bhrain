# 🌙 dream: bound the boot mass a pedigree adds to a role

- kind: followup
- owner: rhachet-roles-bhrain, role=learner (education suite)
- caught 2026-11-26

## .the cue that fired

the i005 peer review of `5.1.execution.from_vision` named it; the learner's own consumer-seat boot reads 9,955 of 10,000 tokens.

## .the gap, precisely

`education.pedigree set` names `briefs/education/pedigree._.md` under `always.briefs.say`. that index gains one row per graduated pursuit, so each graduation adds boot mass to every session of that role. a role near its boot budget (the learner itself, at 100%) breaches its budget clamp after a few pursuits. no check bounds it.

## .the shape of the fix

- the index moves to `ref`; `say` carries one fixed line that points at it (constant mass), or
- the index caps the rows at `say` (latest N) and the rest sit in a `ref` peer
- `setPedigreeEntry` measures the boot spec's payload after the splice and refuses, names the cut, past budget
- clamp: a role at 9,900 tokens graduates three pursuits; the boot stays under budget

## .why it is not done in this round

it changes the boot-spec contract that F5 settled (index at `say`) for every role a pursuit graduated, and it needs a rule on what the director sees at boot. that is a design call for the foreman, not a repair inside stone 5.1 (fulcrum F19).
