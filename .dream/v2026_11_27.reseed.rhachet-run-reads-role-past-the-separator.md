# 🌙 dream: `rhx run` must stop its own flags at `--`

- kind = reseed
- owner = ehmpathy/rhachet (`src/contract/cli/asRunTargetFromArgs.ts`)
- caught 2026-11-27

## .the cue that fired

`blackbox/review.by.dispatch.acceptance.test.ts` `[case-rhx1]` went red on the rhachet 1.51.0 →
1.54.1 bump: `rhx review.by --repo bhrain --role reviewer -- --role ghost` now refuses with
`no skill "review.by" found with --repo bhrain --role ghost`, exit 2, before the base skill runs.

## .the gap, precisely

`asRunTargetFromArgs` reads the long `--repo` / `--role` anywhere in argv, last-wins, and never
stops at `--`. so a `--role` meant for the skill, past the separator, retargets the dispatch. the
`--` convention (all tokens after it belong to the callee) is broken for run's long flags.

harm: a caller who dispatches the base `review.by` and overrides its target role after `--` gets a
refusal. the everyday `rhx review.by --role <role>` path is unharmed (bhrain and ehmpathy wrappers
exec node directly).

## .the shape of the fix

in rhachet: `asRunTargetFromArgs` returns its target at the first bare `--` token, so no token after
it can set skill, init, repo, or role. a clamp: `run review.by --role reviewer -- --role ghost`
targets role `reviewer`.

then in this repo, once the bump lands: restore `[case-rhx1]` to its contract —

- `exit 1`
- stderr holds no `unknown flag`, `--skill`, or `--repo`
- stderr holds `role not found` and `role=ghost`
- the refusal snapshot reads `✋ ConstraintError: role not found at .agent/repo=*/role=ghost`

and drop the `🔴 F26` mark from its title.

## .why it is not done in this round

the defect lives in rhachet, another repo. this pr pins the current refusal in plain view (F26, the
F14 precedent) and names the regression in its body.
