# domain.term.choice.reason: prose.distillation

## .etymology
`distil` is latin *dēstillāre* — *to trickle down* — and it names separation into components plus
their concentration. **both moves sit inside the word**, which is the axis every other candidate
failed on.

⇒ it is adopted, never coined:

- *domain distillation* and *system distillation* already name this act at other scales
  - so the word arrives with precedent rather than a need for one
- this cluster settles only the boundary — `prose.distillation` is the conceptual scale

## .the rename — 2026-09-06

the term was `fundamentalism` from 2026-09-04, a coinage of the wisher's assembled across four
utterances (`.evidence`). it was renamed after a dispute the wisher raised and then settled.

### why the prior word was retired

in common english, `fundamentalism` names religious literalism — strict adherence to a surface text.
**the repo's sense was its exact inverse**: *drill past the surface to the root*.

⇒ the analysis on record priced that a *comprehension* hazard and kept the word, on the ground that
the rule body corrects a misread in one line. it was incomplete:

- it never priced the **valence**
  - a reader who understands the word perfectly may still decline to say it
- telepath is a published role
  - ⇒ so the cost recurs per adopter rather than once

### why `distillation` won where three candidates had failed

each earlier candidate named **one** of the two moves. that was the pattern, and it is what the
`-ism` had bought:

| candidate | the move it named |
|---|---|
| `decomposition` | decompose only — and already overloaded in this canon |
| `rootward` | neither. a direction, not a practice |
| `drill-to-root` | decompose only, and the discipline sense is lost |
| **`distillation`** | **both** — separation and concentration, in one word |

### the collision that was answered rather than overruled

`distillation` was raised in review and set aside on one objection: the learner already spends
`distill` on a different act — raw source into a durable brief.

⇒ the wisher answered it: *"it is the same direction as the learners distill"*. so the two are **one
act at two scales**, and `rule.forbid.domain-term-ambiguity`'s own carve-out applies — where two
senses differ by *context* rather than by concept, the repair is a boundary, never a second word.

🟡 **the learner's `distill` is undeclared**, and this settlement makes it load-bearing. it is now a
named gap rather than a silent one.

## .why not each forbidden synonym

| rejected | why |
|---|---|
| fundamentalism | the prior canonical word. retired on valence, never on sense — see the rename above |
| first-principles | a method to derive a conclusion. distillation governs what you say once you hold it — a different act, at a different moment |
| root-cause | diagnostic — *why did X fail?* distillation is definitional — *what does X stand on?* a healthy concept has fundamentals and no root cause |
| essentialism | a philosophical position about whether categories have essences. it makes a claim about the world; this makes one about prose |
| reductionism | carries the pejorative sense — *reduced to less than it was*. distillation condenses without loss; a fundamental that needs a paragraph was two fundamentals |
| depth | a property, not a practice. *"write with depth"* names no act and grades no line |

## .the boundary between its two moves and its peers

| the act | owned by |
|---|---|
| drill the concept to its root | **distillation** — this term |
| drill the passage — *why must this be said at all?* | `purpose-first` |
| cut each line below the 95% bar | `condensation` |

🟡 **the first utterance carried both the content drill and the passage drill**, and the wisher later
asked for the passage drill as a separate enbriefment — so the two were parted rather than fused.

## .disputes

### dispute: the coinage itself — raised 2026-09-06 — status: RESOLVED (renamed to `distillation`)
- raised.by = the wisher, verbatim: *"did we say theres a better word than fundamentalism?"*
- claim = the ordinary-english collision is priced as a reader hazard and left unresolved. a coined
  word whose common sense *inverts* its repo sense costs every first-time reader a correction — and
  telepath is a published role, so that cost recurs per adopter rather than once
- counter = the five rejections each hold, and none of the rejected words names both moves. the
  `-ism` suffix is what earned it: `fundamental` names a node, `fundamentalism` names the practice.
  a replacement must carry decompose *and* condense, or it names half the term
- candidates weighed = `decomposition` · `rootward` · `drill-to-root`. each named ONE move, which is
  what the counter predicted, so the counter was correct about every candidate on the table
- resolution = the wisher supplied a fourth the table had set aside. **`distillation` carries both
  moves and is adopted rather than coined**, so it defeats the counter on its own terms. the one
  objection against it — the learner's `distill` — was answered rather than overruled: the two are
  one act at two scales, so the repair is a boundary. see the rename section above

🟡 the counter was right and still lost, which is the shape to keep. it correctly barred every
one-move candidate; it did not bar the two-move word nobody had put on the table. **a rejection list
is a filter, never a search — it can only judge what is offered to it.**
