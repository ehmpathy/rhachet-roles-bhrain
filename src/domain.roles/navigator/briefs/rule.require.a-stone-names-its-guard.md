# rule.require.a-stone-names-its-guard

> **every stone states how a traveler KNOWS it is done** — a check, a measurement, or an
> artifact. a stone with no guard passes on vibes.

## .why

a route's whole claim is that progress is **computed**, not argued. the guard is where
that claim lives. strip it and a stone becomes a title over some work, and "am I
done?" returns to the one place a route exists to take it out of: the driver's mood.

🔴 **the failure is asymmetric and it favors the wrong answer.** a tired driver with an
unguarded stone passes it. a tired driver with a guarded one cannot.

## .the test — answer it on the page, before the stone lands

> **"what would a traveler point AT to show this stone is finished?"**

| the answer | the guard |
|---|---|
| a file that now exists | `artifacts:` |
| a number that cleared a bar | a judge command that exits non-zero under the bar |
| a review that came back clean | `reviews:` |
| 🔴 *"you'd just look at it"* | **not a milestone yet.** split it until a pointer exists |

⇒ that last row is the whole rule. an un-namable guard is not an absent guard — it is a
**mis-cut stone**, and the repair is to split it, never to wave the guard away.

## .a guard that cannot FAIL is not a guard

the second failure, and the quieter one: a guard whose check passes under every outcome.

| shape | why it gates naught |
|---|---|
| `artifacts:` that names a file the stone creates up front | present before the work starts |
| a judge that reports a number with no bar | reports, never refuses |
| a review with no rules glob behind it | returns 0 blockers because it read no rubric |

> the test: **could this guard report that the stone is NOT done?** no → it is a receipt,
> never a gate.

## .the guard belongs to the stone, not to the end

write it when you cut the stone. a guard added at the end is written by someone who
already knows the answer, and it will be shaped — unconsciously — to accept what was
built rather than to test what was asked.

| when… | then… |
|---|---|
| you add a stone | 🔴 its guard is part of that same act |
| you cannot name the guard | the cut is wrong. split the stone |
| the guard restates the stone title | it checks naught. name the artifact or the bar |
| a stone's work landed before its guard was written | suspect the guard. would it have failed yesterday? |
| a stone is genuinely exploratory | its artifact is the WRITE-UP. that is still a guard |

🟡 an exploratory stone is the case that most often skips this, and it should not: *"what
did you find?"* has a pointer — the yield file — even when the answer was "no signal".

## .enforcement

blocker: a stone with no guard and no stated check · a guard that cannot report failure ·
a guard authored after the stone's work landed, unexamined.
nitpick: a guard that restates the stone title rather than names an artifact or a bar.
