## .the cue that fired

5.3 i002 r001 `repo-rules` nitpick.16 (F229), per `rule.forbid.serial-reviews-that-could-pour`.

## .the gap, precisely

`src/domain.operations/route/judges/runStoneGuardJudges.ts` awaits each judge in a `for … of`. no
judge reads another's output: each has its own index, its own cached artifact, and the iteration is
computed once before the loop.

## .the shape of the fix

1. map the judges to `Promise.allSettled`, bounded like the peer pour
2. sort the returned artifacts by `index` before the guard-tree render
3. a settled rejection becomes that judge's failed artifact, never a throw that drops its peers
   (`rule.forbid.dropped-partial-review-progress`)
4. clamp: two slow judges finish in max, not sum, of their times; the render order holds

## .why it is not done in this round

SAFE yes, CLEAN no: the render and the judge log read completion order today, and the guard
snapshots pin both. a guard holds one or two judges, so the wall-time win is small.
