## .settled

### 1. 🔴 the hard region of a route is FRONT-LOADED and contiguous

**vision and blueprint are where every decision is decided.** execution stamps what the blueprint
already settled; verification checks what the criteria already named. neither judges.

⇒ so the expensive region is **short and early**, and every stone from the roadmap onward is
mechanical.

🔴 **that shape decides the economy of any per-stone cost control, and it inverts which half pays:**

| the region | its property |
|---|---|
| hard — vision, blueprint | short and early. one lift, one drop, so the switch count is near zero |
| mechanical — roadmap onward | 🔴 **the body of the route**, and where the whole economy lives |

⇒ **a sticky expensive brain set at the blueprint bills the majority of the route, never a tail.**
the lift reads as the point; **the drop back is where the money is.**

#### 🔴 the error came from the SEED, and the seed archive is what proves it

`S1` preserves issue `#377` verbatim, and it reads:

> *"hard-thought stones (blueprint, execution, verification) want an **Opus** driver"*

⇒ **the vision inherited that list and never tested it.** it restated the seed's claim in its own
day-in-the-life, and it survived five self-review rounds because every round checked the distillate
**against the seed** — where the seed was the source of the error.

🔴 **that is the archive's whole purpose, discharged in reverse.** `rule.always.archive-the-wishers-words-verbatim`
justifies the archive as *"the seed is what they check the distillate against"* — and here it proved
the opposite direction: **the distillate was faithful and the source was wrong.**

⚠️ **so a faithful distillation is no guarantee at all**, and a self-review that grades fidelity
cannot catch this class. ⇒ **a seed's claims are archived, never ratified** — an inherited premise
needs its own check, exactly as an authored one does.

### 2. 🔴 a GUARD guards the BUDGET a stone may spend — beside the schema

the guard's subject is not validation alone. it meters what a stone is allowed to cost:
`route.guard.budget` caps review rounds, and each peer review's `--brain` prices a reviewer's round.

⇒ **so a cost-control key belongs to the guard's own concern rather than borrows its file.**

🔴 **the durable half is the contrast with the argument it replaced.** the mechanical case —
*"a `.stone` has no schema, so the guard is the only structured surface"* — is true, and it prices
the guard as a **convenient host**. it invites the question *"and once a stone grows a schema?"*

⇒ **a motive argument does not carry that weakness.** it names why the home is right rather than why
the alternative is unavailable. ⚠️ the first draft reached the mechanism and stopped one layer short
of a motive that was available — `rule.forbid.surface-speak`, committed inside a vision's own
reasoning.

### 3. 🔴 a forbid list catches the words it lists and LICENSES their nearest synonyms

`stage` was used three times as a stone-synonym and survived five self-review rounds.
**`phase` was on the forbidden list and `stage` was not** — one sense, two words, and only the listed
string ever fired a match.

⇒ **a forbid list reads as a semantic guard and behaves as a lexical one.** when you forbid a word,
forbid its near-synonyms in the same edit.

🟡 **and the reach was a real distinction with a wrong word.** the passage wanted *the slot in the
route template* against *one drive's transit of it* — and the glossary already held both: **stone**
and **passage**. ⇒ **a synonym is often reached for to name a distinction the canon already covers**,
so the first question is *which extant term names this?*
