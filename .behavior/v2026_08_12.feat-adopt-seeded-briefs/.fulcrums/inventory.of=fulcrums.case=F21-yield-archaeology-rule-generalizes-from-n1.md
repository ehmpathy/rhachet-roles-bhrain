## .why the confidence is low — the brief says so itself

its own `.provenance` section:

> ⚠️ **one route, one stone.** the measurement above is a single case, and this rule generalizes
> from it.

the measurement is real and sharp — `ehmpathy/sdk-aws-lambda`, `v2026_08_03`, stone `1.vision`,
1909 lines, one fulcrum narrated **six times**, ~two thirds of the document not yield. but it is
**n=1**, and `rule.prefer.wet-over-dry` grades a generalization from one instance a nitpick by
default.

## .the evidence that arrived AFTER it was written, and it is the strongest available

**this behavior's own vision yield is a second instance.** it runs 800+ lines, and a grep for its
three core results returns **24 hits** — the `dist/` result alone appears in groundwork, the
timeline, the edge-case table, the assumptions list, and the awkwardness section.

⇒ recorded in the vision as **F11**, and the honest verdict is that **n is now 2**, both measured,
both the same shape, and the second was produced by the author of the rule while the rule sat in
context.

⚠️ that raises the confidence and does **not** close it. two instances of one shape, both from
routes in this org, both authored under similar conditions — that is a pattern, not yet a law.

## .the part with the least support

the `appendix/` vs `archive/` split is the rule's own contribution and **no route has used it
yet**. it is a proposal about where history should land, tested nowhere:

| dir | holds |
|---|---|
| `$route/appendix/` | elaboration on a decision that IS in the yield |
| `$route/archive/` | ideas considered and **rejected** |

⇒ **the diagnosis is measured; the prescription is not.** a route that tries the split and finds
the boundary unusable would refute the prescription and leave the diagnosis intact.

## .what would overturn it

- a third measured route with a long yield and **no** repetition → the shape is conditional
- a route that uses `appendix/`/`archive/` and finds the split arbitrary → keep the rule, drop the
  two dirs
- the parent rule adds its own ejection target upstream → this rule becomes a citation
