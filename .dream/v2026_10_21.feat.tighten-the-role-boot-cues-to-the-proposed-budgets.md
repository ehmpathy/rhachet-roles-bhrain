# 🌙 dream: tighten the learner and driver cue lines to reach their 12k proposals

kind: feat · owner: rhachet-roles-bhrain · caught 2026-10-21

## .the cue that fired

`v2026_10_21.fix-clamp-role-boot-budgets` moved the argument out of every learner and driver min
and kept each cue line byte for byte (fulcrum F10). both roles landed above the 12k the director
proposed, and each budget was set at what it reached.

## .the gap, precisely

the seat pays 20,657 (learner) and 20,003 (driver). the gap to 12k is ~42% of the learner's boot
and ~40% of the driver's. the vision's markup floor (~14.3k · ~12.9k) counts each prose line as
argument, but most resident prose lines are one-line claims and tests, so it understates what a
cue-intact cut can reach. the gap closes only by phrase choice inside cue lines (a shorter row,
one claim phrased once, a table cell trimmed to its noun) and by a catalog for a set booted whole;
a 40% tighten is a full pass over every min, not a polish.

## .the shape of the fix

1. per min, tighten cue rows; never drop a row, a test, or an enforcement clause
2. the boot snapshot (`src/domain.roles/roleBootClamps.integration.test.ts`) shows each changed
   line as a diff, which is the review surface: every row before has a row after with the same sense
3. lower each `budget.tokens` to the least multiple of 250 strictly above what it reaches

## .why it is not done in this round

SAFE but not CLEAN: a tightened row is a changed cue line in a pr that already moves ~60 mins,
so a real cut would hide among hundreds of reworded rows. alone, with the snapshot as its guard,
each reword is reviewable.
