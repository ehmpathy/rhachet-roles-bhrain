# wish: a review budget grant is refused unless the last round earned it

## .the outcome

**`rhx route.guard.budget --for review --add N` stops to be a free lever.** it is refused by
default, and it is permitted only where the **immediately prior round** carried an explicit
**urgent concession** — a recorded signal that one more round converges, rather than a reflex at
the moment a lane runs dry.

no concession on the last round → the grant is refused, and the refusal names what to do instead.

## 🔴 .the cue that fired — drivers churn against NITPICK reviewers, and top off to keep going

observed across the fleet, 2026-09: **drivers spend round after round on lanes that raise
nitpicks, and reach for `--add N` each time the meter runs dry.** the loop is self-feeding —

```
lane raises a nitpick  →  driver edits  →  lane raises another nitpick
   →  budget runs dry  →  --add N  →  lane raises another nitpick  →  …
```

⇒ **every round of that costs grove tokens, and not one of them was a blocker.** the lane was never
stuck; it was never done either, because a nitpick lane is never done — it grades taste, and taste
has no floor. the budget was the only bound on that loop, and the top-up removes it.

⚠️ **the reviewer is not at fault here.** `rule.forbid.overzealous-blockers` already tells a
reviewer to grade a cosmetic point a nitpick, which is correct. the defect is that a **nitpick
costs the driver a round to answer and costs the grantor no thought to fund.**

## 🔴 .why — the budget is the only bound that makes a round COST

`rule.always.spend-own-levers-before-escalation` lives in this repo and argues the opposite, in the
strongest terms it has:

> *"budget is not scarce, and to treat it as scarce is the error."*
> *"`rhx route.guard.budget --for review --add N` — owner: **driver**"*

**that is the rule this wish reverses.** it is cited by name so no reader is caught between two
briefs.

three costs, and the third compounds:

| cost | what it is |
|---|---|
| **gain.cash** | the grove is token-capped. every granted round is metered spend, on a box that oom-killed twice in 14.7d |
| **gain.time** | a lane that failed to converge in N rounds is rarely one round short. it re-raises the same point at 45/107 as at 105/107 — measured, `ehmpathy/rhachet` @ `beav/fix-node-pty-install`, stone 5.1 |
| 🔴 **gain.rank** | **the budget is what makes a round cost, and the cost is what forces convergence.** remove the bound and the whole scarcity argument the ladder rests on is void |

⇒ it is the `spiral` shape: **the remedy that resembles the defect.** eighteen rounds that did not
converge are not repaired by a nineteenth of the same kind.

## 🔴 .and a top-up buys its way out of the ARTIFACT the canon most wants

`rule.always.converge-to-terminal` says an exhausted level **owes a record** — what was tried each
round, and what would fix that reviewer for the next traveler.

a top-up is the one move that prevents a level from ever arrival at that record. the budget runs
out, the record comes due, and `--add N` makes it **not due yet**.

⇒ so the cheap lever displaces the expensive work, and it sits right beside it and looks like
progress. that adjacency is the mechanism, never a lapse of discipline.

## .what must be true of the answer

| # | requirement | why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | a budget grant is **refused by default** | that is the outcome. the route author chose `budget: N` with the whole rubric in view; the clone at round 19 has one blocker in view |
| 2 | 🔴 the refusal is **lifted only by an explicit URGENT concession on the LAST round** | not "somewhere in the history". the evidence must be fresh, or it is a permission that never expires |
| 3 | 🔴 the concession is **recorded and machine-readable** — a fact the tool reads, never a judgment a clone asserts | a self-asserted concession is the same self-grant one layer out. an artifact on disk, or it did not happen |
| 4 | 🔴 a **nitpick-only** round can never be the concession that lifts the refusal | that is the measured churn above. if taste can fund a round, the loop has no floor |
| 5 | the refusal **names the sanctioned move** | converge — a `.taken` per open `.given`, then re-arrive (`rule.always.converge-with-reviewers`) |
| 6 | a **malfunction** is untouched by this | a broken lane is not an exhausted one. it is repaired, and its repair must not need a grant |
| 7 | a **human** may still grant, with the route in view | a human decision on record is the carve-out. this binds the bot |
| 8 | the extant tests and route snapshots are **updated, never deleted** | they encode the behavior under change; a deleted case hides the change from every reviewer |

## 🔴 .the fulcrum this wish RESERVES — do not settle it silently

**what counts as an "urgent concession", and who makes it, is the wisher's call and is NOT yet
made.** the candidates, stated fairly:

| candidate | reads as |
|---|---|
| a **reviewer** narrowed or dropped a point, on the record | the lane converges — the extra round has somewhere to land |
| a **reviewer** marked an open blocker urgent-only | the disagreement is bounded, so one more round can close it |
| a **driver** conceded a contested point rather than re-argue it | the driver gave ground, so the next round meets a changed artifact |
| a **human** typed an urgent concession into the round | the human carve-out, recorded rather than reflexive |

⇒ **itemize this as a fulcrum, best-guess it, and drive on** (`rule.always.defer-fulcrums-to-last`).
the rework is clean — it is one predicate over a recorded fact. do **not** halt on it, and do not
quietly pick one and leave the choice unrecorded.

## .what is NOT wanted

- 🔴 **a larger default budget.** the same lever with a bigger number, and it bloats back the moment
  somebody argues a lane was cut short
- **a self-asserted concession** — a clone that writes *"the reviewer conceded"* and then grants
  itself the round has laundered the grant, not gated it (`rule.forbid.self-grant-human-gates`)
- **a silent wait-out.** exhaustion discharges the ROUNDS, never the DEBT: a blocker with no paired
  `.taken` is not terminal, whatever the budget reads. a change that lets a driver merely SURVIVE N
  rounds re-opens the exit `rule.forbid.unanswered-exits-from-a-blocker` closed
- **a deleted test.** update the snapshots; do not drop the cases

carries a paired `.taken` ⇒ the stone passes.** requirement 2 above must not break it.

## .why it matters

a bound nobody can raise is a promise. a bound anybody can raise is a suggestion.

⇒ the review ladder's whole argument — that a round COSTS, so convergence is forced — rests on one
number, and today any clone at the keyboard can change it. **that is not a budget; it is a
formality with a counter attached.**
