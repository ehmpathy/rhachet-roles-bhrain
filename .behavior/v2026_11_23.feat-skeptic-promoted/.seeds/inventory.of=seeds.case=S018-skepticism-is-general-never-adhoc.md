## .settled

- the role promotes doubt of one's own thought, in any domain; never a mechanism tied to one command
- a prediction whose answer no one doubts (a test expected to pass, a review expected clean) carries no information
- a fresh-context counter-take is review; the reviewer role owns review, never the skeptic
- action gates and guardrails against the world are not this role's subject
- the mechanism waits on independent research, gathered before the human's own idea is shared
