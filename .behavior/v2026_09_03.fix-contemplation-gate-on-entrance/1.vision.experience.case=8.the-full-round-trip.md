## .the narrative

*driver, one drive on `5.1.execution`, two reviewers: `mechanic` at l1, `architect` at l3*

**I start where the old world let me cheat.** the mechanic raised two blockers three iterations ago
and I have edited plenty since. under the old rules its critique would have evaporated with the
hash; instead the door names it and quotes the hash it was raised at.

so I answer — one repair with the lines quoted, one refutation with a path. the tail reads *"your
response is recorded"*, which tells me it paired a real critique rather than shrugged at an empty one.

I re-enter, and here is the sharp part: **the mechanic reads my answer and drops both blockers.**
but the same round unlocks l3, the architect runs for the first time, and raises one of its own —
**after the door had already let me in.** so the exit gate catches me on the way out.

out of habit I try `--as passed` again. this time **the door itself stops me**: same blocker, same
hash, no edit between — but a new invocation, so the entrance check now sees it. **two gates, one
debt, and which one fires is decided purely by when the blocker appeared.** I try to end the session
instead; **the stophook holds me**, and it is right — a `.taken` is entirely within my power.

I answer the architect. it re-runs, disputes, sharpens. on the fourth round it is **exhausted** and
its blocker still stands. I reach for `--add 2`. the architect is live again — **and the door still
holds me**, which is exactly right and briefly irksome. more breath for the critic is not an answer
from me. it re-runs and holds its ground: the fix wants a rename that belongs to another repo. no
honest move left, so I escalate. the human agrees and overrules **l3**. I re-enter and pass.

**the aha:** ten boundaries, and never once a cheap way past one. every exit was *answer it* or *ask
a human*. that is what the invariant feels like from the inside — not a wall, but the absence of a
shortcut.

## 🔴 .the interactions no spotlight reaches

| interaction | steps | the risk it exposes |
|---|---|---|
| **a discharged debt stays discharged across a level unlock** | `[t3]`→`[t6]` | the mechanic's `.taken` sits at `9f2a1c`; by `[t6]` current is `c30d77`. re-anchor the pair to "current" and the mechanic resurrects as `stale` — the driver answers a critique nobody re-raised |
| 🔴 **the two gates partition by WHEN, never by WHICH debt** | `[t5]` vs `[t6]` | the identical architect blocker is caught by the **exit** gate in `[t5]` and the **entrance** gate in `[t6]`. the seam is the invocation boundary, not the debt's age — a reviewer creates a debt *after* the entrance check has already passed, so only the exit gate can catch it that time round |
| **a level unlock mid-drive introduces a debt from a reviewer that never spoke** | `[t5]`→`[t6]` | the architect has **no prior given at all**. a latest-per-slug read over an empty set must yield "no debt", never a crash or a phantom |
| **exhaustion and budget are separate levers** | `[t8]`→`[t9]` | the shortcut that tempts is to let `--add` clear the debt, since it makes the reviewer live again. `[t9]` forbids it |
| **the overrule must be scoped while another rung is live** | `[t11]` vs `[t12]` | per-level scope is observable only with two rungs in flight |

⇒ **`t3→t6` is the sharpest** — the one walk where P2's benefit and P2's risk sit together: the
carried `.taken` must stay valid across three hash moves, and the new blocker must still gate. get
the key wrong in either direction and exactly one of those breaks.

⚠️ **this does not replace the spotlights.** a boundary *exercised* here is not a sharp critipath
*proven* to fail safe. cases 1, 3, and 5 remain the focused proofs. **dense journey for breadth,
spotlights for safety — both, never either.**
