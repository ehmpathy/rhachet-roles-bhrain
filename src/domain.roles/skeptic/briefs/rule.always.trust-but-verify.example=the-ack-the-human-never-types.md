## .the order

the mechanism asked the human to type `ack <key>` or `ack all`. the human declined to type it, and set the rule: infer the ack from their responses, skeptically, lean unacked, and press until each response is acked.

## .the cure

no typed ack. an answer stays lit until the human's own later words engage it, inferred skeptically (lean unacked), and is confirmed only with a quote of those words. every reply leads with each unacked answer.

`commander.inbox ack --at <id> --why '"<their words>"'` carries the proof: the tool refuses a quote that is not in a message of theirs after the answer, and a bare reaction. a nod, a message on another subject, and silence never ack.
