# domain.term.choice.reason: verdict

## .etymology

a **verdict** is what a judge returns on a dispute put before them. the fit is exact: a dispute is
argued by the driver against a reviewer, and a human hears it and returns one of two outcomes.

| word | what it loses |
|---|---|
| `ruling` | it names the act; the artifact the gate reads is the outcome. the verb `route.fulcrum.rule` keeps the act |
| `decision` | any call is a decision, the driver's own included. a verdict is the human's alone |
| `resolution` | it reads as a fix landed, where a `conceded` verdict owes a fix not yet made |

⇒ the verb and the noun split on purpose: a human **rules** (`route.fulcrum.rule`); the file holds
the **verdict**.

## .evidence

the vision fixed the shape (`1.vision.md`, contract table): a human-only lever, two outcomes, and a
proof that a lever wrote it. the outcome words `upheld` and `conceded` reuse the driver's own
`--as conceded` vocabulary, so one word names one stance on both sides of the gate.

## .the invariant

`verdict ≠ null ⟹ the newest ledger row for its case matches it`. enforced at the onStop linter and
at every human gate (`define.invariant.review.peer.dispute.the-fulcrum-is-the-one-home`).
