# case=9 — a concession after an approval holds passage

cells: R57 (sharp, critipath) — invariant 2, the approval must follow the repair it answers

## .narrative

the human approves `3.blueprint`. later in the same round the driver disputes a concern, and cites
F52. the stone reaches its gate again, and the human reads F52 and rules it `conceded`: the
reviewer was right, the repair is owed.

the earlier approval never saw that repair. if it still stood, the driver could pass the stone with
the repair undone, on an approval the human gave before the debt existed. so passage reads the
order: an approval counts only if it follows the latest verdict. the refusal names the repair, and
the two commands that close it: the human approves again, then the driver passes.

## .[tn] sketch

```
given 3.blueprint approved, then a dispute cited F52, then a human ruled F52 conceded
when [t0] the driver runs rhx route.stone.set --stone 3.blueprint --as passed
  then refused, exit 2: a conceded repair awaits a human approval after it
  then stderr names F52, the repair owed, and the two commands that close it
when [t1] the human runs rhx route.stone.set --stone 3.blueprint --as approved
  then a new approved row is appended — the earlier one never saw the verdict
  and the driver runs --as passed
  then the stone passes
when [t2] the human approves again, with no verdict since
  then no row is appended; the approval is idempotent
```
