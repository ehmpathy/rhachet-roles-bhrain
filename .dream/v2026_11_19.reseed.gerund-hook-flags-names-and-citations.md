# 🌙 dream: the gerund hook must not flag a surname, a cited title, or a url

- kind: reseed
- owner: ehmpathy/rhachet-roles-ehmpathy (role=mechanic, hook=pretooluse.forbid-terms.gerunds)
- caught: 2026-11-19

## .the cue that fired

each brief of the absorbed achiever cites its sources. the hook refused six writes in one round on
words that are no gerund in use: the surname `Deming`, the titles `Getting Things Done`, `The
Knowing-Doing Gap`, `Making things happen`, `The Essence of Winning and Losing`, the word `thing`,
and a url path (`Andon_(manufacturing)`). each write passed only on a retry of the same operation.

## .the gap, precisely

the hook matches any `-ing` token, so a proper noun, a quoted title, a url, and a word whose `-ing`
is no suffix (`thing`, `Deming`) all read as gerunds. the retry door works, but it trains a writer
to retry past the hook, which blunts it for the real gerund it exists to catch (one real gerund,
`doing`, sat in the same write and was fixed).

## .the shape of the fix

- skip tokens inside a url, inside a markdown link target, and inside a `## .sources` section
- skip a capitalized token mid-sentence (a proper noun or a title word)
- keep an allowlist of non-suffix `-ing` words (`thing`, `string`, `ring`, `king`, `bring`, …) and
  of surnames met in briefs (`Deming`)
- clamp: a fixture brief with each case above passes; a fixture with `doing` in prose still blocks

## .why it is not done in this round

the hook lives in another repo.
