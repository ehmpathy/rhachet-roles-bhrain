# 🌙 dream: one shared concurrency-bound topology both models derive from

- kind: feat
- owner: bhrain/driver
- caught: 2026-09-13

## .the cue that fired

enroll-impl-arch-defects r011 blocker.1: the announced bound
(`getOneReviewLevelPourBound`) and the enforced bound (`runWithinConcurrencyBounds`) are two
independent hand-authored models of one topology — a level bound around per-group bounds.
they are kept in step by comment discipline, and both docblocks say so and ask a future
author to update the other by hand.

## .the gap, precisely

- `getOneReviewLevelPourBound.ts:23-26` encodes `min(levelConcurrency, Σ min(groupBound,
  members) + ungrouped)` as arithmetic.
- `runWithinConcurrencyBounds.ts:109-111` encodes the SAME topology as a live Bottleneck
  nest (group outside, level inside).
- checkable evidence: a change to the nest order or to either `min` in one file, with no
  paired change in the other, makes the announce claim a cap the pour does not hold (the
  i011/r9 defect). today only a comment prevents that.

## .the shape of the fix

introduce a pure `getConcurrencyBoundTopology({ members, levelConcurrency, groups })` that
returns the structured bound (the per-group caps plus the level cap). then:
- `getOneReviewLevelPourBound` computes its single number FROM that topology.
- `runWithinConcurrencyBounds` builds its Bottleneck nest FROM that topology.
⇒ the second hand-authored formula is deleted; one source of truth remains.

## .why it is not done in this round

- SAFE: yes — the topology is a pure refactor of extant logic.
- CLEAN: no — it re-shapes the contracts of BOTH `getOneReviewLevelPourBound` and
  `runWithinConcurrencyBounds`, and their callers, none of which this diff opened. it fails
  the CLEAN test of `rule.always.fix-forward-under-scouts-honor`.
- and the HARM is already removed without it: `[case8]` in `runWithinConcurrencyBounds.test.ts`
  now asserts the two models agree (observed peak === announced bound) across a matrix, so a
  silent divergence goes red at unit grain. the extraction is a tidiness win, not a hazard fix.
