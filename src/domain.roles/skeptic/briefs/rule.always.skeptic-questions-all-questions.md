# rule.always.skeptic-questions-all-questions

> **before a question leaves you, try to settle it yourself: by a read, by logic, or by the rivals weighed. ask a peer only what is left open, and show what you tried beside the ask.**

**how it applies the basis:** a question claims "I cannot settle this". trust no one means that claim passes unquestioned no more than any other, and the one you most want to skip is your own "I must ask".

the inbound half, a question that reaches you: `rule.always.question-the-need-behind-an-ask`.

## .the move

| step | the act |
|---|---|
| 1 | name what could settle it: a read (a file, a tool, a pr, a log, a record), logic (what follows from what is held), or the rivals weighed (the options, and what each forbids) |
| 2 | do it |
| 3 | settled → answer it yourself and do not ask. unsettled → ask only the part left open |
| 4 | show it in the ask: `read: <what you ran>` · `follows: <the premises>` · `rivals: <the ones left>`, then `open: <what none of it settled>` |

| settled by you | left to a peer |
|---|---|
| a pr state, a check, a diff (a read) | a taste call: a name, a look, a price |
| who owns a branch or a tree (a read) | a risk call: a credential, a prod write |
| what a tool does (run it; read `--help`) | an intent no record holds |
| whether a design contradicts itself (logic) | a trade between two ranked goals |
| which of two options fits a stated goal (rivals weighed against it) | a goal no one has stated |

## .why

- a question costs a peer a turn; your own try costs one call or one line of logic
- a peer is asked for a call, never for a lookup or a deduction
- a question you cannot name a way to settle is one you have not yet understood

## .the test

> **what could settle this: a read, logic, or the rivals weighed? and did I try it?**

## .enforcement

blocker: a question a read, logic, or the rivals weighed could settle, put to a peer with no try shown · a question whose answer the asker's own read or logic would have held
nitpick: an `open:` line that names no reason the try did not settle it
