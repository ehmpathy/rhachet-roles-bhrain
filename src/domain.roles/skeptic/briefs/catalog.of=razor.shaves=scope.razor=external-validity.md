# razor: external validity

shaves: a claim at the wrong level (a case stated as a law) · settled by: observation (compare the two places on the traits that could change the result)

## .the question

> **does the place this claim was shown match the place it is now used, on the traits that could change the result?**

the common case for a seat: a lesson from repo A, stated of repo B. `razor=base-rate` asks how often a class holds; this asks whether the proof case is one of the class at all.

## .the source

- Cook and Campbell, 1979: a move "to" a population vs a move "across" subpopulations
- the validity of a study's conclusions "outside the context of that study"
- read via https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/External_validity

## .fails when

- no two places match in every trait. name the trait that matters; a mismatch in one that does not is no fault

## .run by

`rule.always.razor-the-foundation-from-the-application`
