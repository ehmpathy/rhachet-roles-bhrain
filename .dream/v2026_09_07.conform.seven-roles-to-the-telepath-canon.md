## 🟡 .why the count went unmeasured until now

`rule.require.bulletize` shipped with a blocker line that no instrument read.

| the peer instrument | the grain it grades |
|---|---|
| `review.for.emphasis-noise.density.js` | emphasized LINES per section |
| `review.for.emphasis-noise.spans.js` | a STACK on one line |
| `review.for.diffusion.flat.js` | a section with no needle |

⇒ a section of one 60-word paragraph that chains four claims passes all three. **a rule with no
instrument is enforced only where an author happens to look.**

## .the shape of the fix

- run `review.for.narration.paragraphs.js` per role, at `--bar=5` first, then drive `--bar=3` to zero
- repair by hand — the tool reports and never repairs, because **which claims nest under which is the
  argument itself**
- repair the `.md.min` where one exists, since that is the artifact a session pays for

⇒ the full census, the encoded exemptions, and the per-role order are in the issue body at
`.agent/.notes/dispatch.bulletize-the-rest-of-the-roles.md`.

## .why it is not done in this round

**safe? yes. clean? no.**

`rule.prefer.scouts-honor` bounds a fix-forward to prose the change already touched, and grades a
doc-wide rewrite smuggled into an unrelated change a **blocker**. this route's contact with those
seven roles was zero — the diff never opened one of their briefs.

🟡 and the repair is not mechanical. each of the 90 is a tree that must be authored, so the sweep is
a round of its own rather than a pass at the end of this one.

## .the fulcrum

> **a canon is not adopted when it is written. it is adopted when the artifacts that cite it
> conform.** the author's own compliance is the weakest possible evidence, because the author is the
> one reader who cannot be surprised by it.

rework: **clean**. the deferral is on SCOPE, never on dirt.

## .the one call it does not settle

**does `rule.require.bulletize` bind a `.behavior/` `case=N` narrative?**

- the rule's boundary exempts *"a narrative in a `.demo=` artifact: its declared subject IS the
  sequence"*, and a `case=N` file is that in all but filename
- the instrument encodes that read today, by **header** rather than by filename
- ⇒ read the other way, the scope grows by roughly the same count again
