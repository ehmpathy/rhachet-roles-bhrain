## .the cue that fired

r11 (enroll-impl-arch-defects, i002 #1 and i003 #3) on `v2026_10_23.fix-judge-pass-exhausted-all-better`.

## .the gap, precisely

`ReviewAbsorption.given` and `RouteGuardReviewPeerGiven.pathGiven` are bare strings that hold a
path in one place and a key in another. the match is safe only because every reader calls
`isReviewAbsorptionOnGiven`; a reader that compares the raw strings re-opens the defect this route
cured.

## .the shape of the fix

cast `given` to its key once, at the ledger read in `getStoneReviewAbsorptions` (the only producer
of `ReviewAbsorption`), and add `key` to `RouteGuardReviewPeerGiven` at its read. then
`isReviewAbsorptionOnGiven` becomes `===`, `asReviewAbsorptionKey` drops its `basename`, and a
branded type (`ReviewGivenKey`) makes a raw path unassignable to the key slot.

## .why it is not done in this round

it re-types two shared shapes and every peer caller that reads `pathGiven` — a contract change past
this fix; the one predicate holds the match today, with clamps on both row forms. fulcrum F4.
