# domain.term.choice.reason: unknown

## .etymology

plain english, chosen because it is plain: the run's cost is not known. the wish states the law
in these words — a run whose cost cannot be known is `unknown` and renders absent, never `$0`.
`genReviewMeterStdout` pins the word against drift: any other word for this grade is a second
name for one position.

rejected:

| rejected | why |
|---|---|
| `unmetered` | a `refused` run is unmetered too. the word cannot part the two nils, and to part them is the family's whole job |
| `zero` / `$0` | the precise lie this position exists to prevent |
| `n/a` | reads as "does not apply". the cost applies; it was not recorded |

## .the evidence

a grove mine once read ~2.8gb of prompt files and died at a 2gb heap. a disk-conscious prune of
prompt files would flip every `unknown` row to `refused` and turn "unrecorded" into "measured
nil". so the compiled metrics and the brain's answer are read as witnesses too, beside the
prompt: any one of the three proves the brain may have been reached.

## .the boundary

"unknown, of WHAT?" — of a run's spend, by the honesty family. the word names one shape of
claim — an absence the instrument will not dress as a zero — never one overloaded concept.

none.
