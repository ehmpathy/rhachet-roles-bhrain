shape:
- l1: basic-checker (always passes, 0 blockers)
- l3: premium-checker (always passes, 0 blockers)
- judge: a raw judge process that exits 1 (malfunction)

malfunction must be recoverable: a human overrules the judge rung (JUDGE_LEVEL), and the
stone then passes as overruled. before this fix, no path could target the judge rung on a
peer+judge stone, so a judge malfunction was a permanent block.
