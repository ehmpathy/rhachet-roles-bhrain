# F11 — the brain line carries no marker at the vision; a glyph claim is deferred to execution

**rework** = clean · **status** = open · **confidence** = 80%

> raised at self-review r1, after `🧠` was found to be **claimed** for rung 0, tribal knowledge.

## .the fork, stated fairly

every render this feature adds is a line a human triages at a glance:

```
🗿 5.2.execution.phase1
   └─ brain switched → claude-sonnet-5[1m]   (prescribed by 5.2.execution.phase1.guard)
```

the extant surfaces put a **marker** at that position — `🗿` for the driver, `✋` for a wall, `👋`
for a grant. so the fork is: **does the brain line get its own glyph, and which one?**

## .taken, and why at the time

**no marker at the vision. the line reads as prose, and the claim is deferred to execution.**

- a glyph claim is not a render choice — `catalog.of=glyph._.md` requires a **palette entry**, an
  **index row**, and an **axis-file row**, and a role marker requires two published faces updated
  besides. that is an execution act with real artifacts, and a vision that performs it half-way
  leaves the register drifted, which is the exact defect the catalog exists to prevent
- 🔴 **the obvious pick is taken.** `🧠` is claimed for `rung` 0, tribal knowledge. it is apt for a
  brain, and aptness is not the test — *"a glyph that names two **unrelated** concepts is the
  collision"*
- an unmarked line is **honest**: it makes no claim on the register. a wrong marker is a contract
  on a surface, and the catalog measures its repair at **68 occurrences across 10 files** when `🔮`
  collided

## .the counter-case, stated fairly

**an unmarked line is harder to scan, and scan-ability is the whole point of `case=8`.** that case
argues a switch must be visible; a line with no marker sits in a tree beside marked peers and reads
as the odd one out.

⇒ and the catalog itself says which way that asymmetry resolves:

> *"the 'lone outlier' read is the trap, and it inverts the repair. three bare halt headers beside
> one marked header reads as one site to strip; it is in truth three sites owed a marker."*

so **the counter is real**: absence is the defect the register wants fixed, and this fulcrum ships
the absence deliberately. it is a defensible defer, not a defensible end state.

## .rework, and why

**clean.** to add a marker later is one glyph inserted at a fixed position in a handful of render
sites, plus the three catalog rows the claim owes. no caller parses these lines, and no contract
depends on the position.

⚠️ it is clean **only while the sites are few**. once the render ships and downstream guards produce
these lines in logs and snapshots, a marker insert becomes a snapshot churn across every route.

## .confidence, and why 80%

the **defer** is high-confidence — a vision stone should not claim a glyph, and the extant register
is explicit about what a claim costs.

the open 20% is that the defer may simply never be picked up: an unmarked line that ships once is an
unmarked line forever, and the catalog's own evidence is that **absence propagates** more quietly
than a collision does. ⇒ so this fulcrum is as much a **reminder** as a decision.

## .where

`1.vision.experience.case=1` · `case=6` · `case=7` · `case=8` — every render with a brain line ·
`1.vision.experience.case=8.the-switch-is-visible.md` is the case whose argument the counter rests on.

## .the verdict

open — for the fulcrum council. ⚠️ **it is the cheapest call in this inventory to answer**, and the
one most likely to be forgotten, because an absent glyph raises no error.
