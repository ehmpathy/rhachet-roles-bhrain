# rule.forbid.cases-with-no-point

> **no passage stays on the low rungs of the ladder with no claim the cases add up to.**

razor: `razor=ladder` (dead-level abstraction, stuck low). twin: `rule.forbid.claims-stuck-high`.

## .the shape

| 👎 | 👍 |
|---|---|
| forty log lines pasted, no line on what they show | "the retries all hit one host: the fault is that host, not the client" + the three lines that show it |
| a list of every file touched, no word on why | "the change moves auth into one module" + the files |
| a step-by-step of what was tried | what holds now, and the one step that settled it |

## .the test

> **what one claim do these cases support? is it stated?**

none stated → stuck low; the reader must climb the ladder alone.

## .enforcement

blocker: a report a peer had to decode to find its claim, and decoded wrong
nitpick: a dump of cases with no claim line above it
