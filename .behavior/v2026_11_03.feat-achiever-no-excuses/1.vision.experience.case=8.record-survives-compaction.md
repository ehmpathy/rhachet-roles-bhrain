# case 8 — the record survives compaction

feel: happy · care: alterpath (rare per session, costly when it bites) · actor: achiever · seams: boot hook, store

## .narrative

four hours in, context compacts. the summary mentions two of the commander's five open orders. the
boot hook re-reads `.goals/` from disk and prints all five open goals, inflight first. the achiever
works from the record, not from the summary. naught lived only in context, so naught was lost.

## .[tn] — a sketch

```
given  five open goals on disk, a summary that names two
when   [t0] the session compacts and restarts
then     goal.triage.next --when hook.onBoot prints all five (extant hook, exit 0)
     and goals with a dispatched cure show their hour
given  one of the five is a dispatch whose hour passed while the session was compacted
when   [t1] the session restarts
then     the boot lists it first, marked overdue, with the two moves (log the chase, or close by check)
when   [t2] the achiever tries to stop before it acts on the overdue dispatch
then     goal.triage.next --when hook.onStop exits 2, as case 5 past the hour
```

the overdue dispatch is first seen at boot, never first at a stop that has already moved on.
