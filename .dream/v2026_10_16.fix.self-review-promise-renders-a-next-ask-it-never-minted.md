# 🌙 dream: a self-review promise renders the next ask, but never mints it

kind: fix · owner: rhachet-roles-bhrain (driver / route guard, self review) · caught 2026-10-16

## .the cue that fired

on stone `1.vision`, three times in a row: `--as promised` for review N succeeded and rendered
`review.self N+1/5`, its slug, its guide, and its `articulate into` path. the driver wrote that
review and promised it — and was refused:

```
🍃 no ask on record
   ├─ this review was never asked for, or its ask was rewound
   └─ ask for it, and the guard hands you the path
      └─ rhx route.stone.set --stone 1.vision --as passed
```

a run of `--as passed` then minted the ask, and the file (written before that) had to be re-saved
to clear the freshness bar. the haste cue then fired on the short gap from the late ask.

## .the gap, precisely

the promise render and the ask ledger disagree: the render shows the driver an ask the ledger does
not hold. so every review after the first costs a refusal, a `--as passed`, a re-save, and often a
haste cue that measures the guard's lag rather than the driver's care.

## .the shape of the fix

either (a) a successful promise mints the next ask in the same transition that renders it, or
(b) the promise render shows only `run --as passed for the next review` and no path. (a) is the
pit of success — the render is already the ask in every way but the ledger. clamp: promise review
1 of 2, then promise review 2 with no `--as passed` between → green.

## .why it is not done in this round

not clean: the guard's self-review flow is outside this route's wish (the status-line init), and
its tests are a separate surface.
