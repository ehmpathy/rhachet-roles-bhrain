# 🌙 dream: review.by grades a caller-fixable refusal as a malfunction

**kind** = fix · **owner** = `ehmpathy/rhachet-roles-bhrain` · **caught** 2026-10-03

## .the cue that fired

the openrouter cutover's level-3 peer reviewers traced vision case 1 (a guard drives
`review.by --for <rubric>`) with the key absent, and found the refusal framed as a breakage.

## .the gap, precisely

measured, pinned by `blackbox/review.by.key-absent.acceptance.test.ts`:

- with `OPENROUTER_API_KEY` absent, `review.by --role demo --for demo-arrow-only` and
  `review.by --role demo` both exit **1**, under `💥 rubric malfunctioned: demo-arrow-only`
- the child review's own `✋ ConstraintError` and its fix
  (`rhx keyrack set --owner ehmpath --key OPENROUTER_API_KEY --env prep`) still render inside it
- cause: `ReviewVerdict.outcome` is `passed | rejected | malfunctioned`
  (`src/domain.operations/review.by/ReviewByResult.ts`). `asReviewVerdict.ts` maps every
  `!run.detected` to `malfunctioned`, with no exit-code branch, though `getExitCodeClass` already
  sorts exit 2 to `constraint` at the guard layer
- so a guard that drives review.by reads exit 1 and grades the stone `💥 malfunction`, where the
  raw `rhx review` path refuses `✋`, exit 2

## .the shape of the fix

1. add `constraint` to `ReviewVerdict.outcome`; `asReviewVerdict` consults
   `getExitCodeClass({ code: run.exitCode })` and maps exit 2 with no tally to `constraint`
2. `genReviewByStdout` + `asReviewByReviewerTreeState` render a `✋ rubric refused: <slug>` row,
   with the child's refusal text beneath it
3. `review.by.ts` exits 2 when every non-passed rubric is a `constraint`, else keeps exit 1 — and
   revise review.by's blackbox criteria, which pin every error case to exit 1
4. flip the two `GAP (F5)` clamps in `review.by.key-absent.acceptance.test.ts` to exit 2 and `✋`

## .why it is not done in this round

- **not clean** — it changes review.by's published exit-code contract and its criteria, the stdout
  render and its snapshots, and how a guard grades a review.by run. the wish is a vendor cutover
- the gap predates the cutover; the cutover neither caused nor worsened it, and the fix text reaches
  the human today (pinned). raised as fulcrum F5
