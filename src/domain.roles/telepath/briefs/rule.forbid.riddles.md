# rule.forbid.riddles

> **a riddle spends the reader's time for the author's pleasure. it is rude.**

a **riddle** is any prose whose meaning must be **deciphered** to be understood — encoded, implied,
or carried by an image, rather than stated.

⇒ it is a species of `rule.forbid.decode-friction`, never a peer. that rule counts the mental steps
between the words and the claim; this one grades the steps an author **chose** to add.

## 🟡 .why it is rude, and rude is the accurate word

| | the author | every reader |
|---|---|---|
| the craft | enjoyed **once**, at the keyboard | — |
| the decipher | costs **zero** — they already hold the answer | paid in full, on **every** read |
| the review | — | paid again, by every reviewer, human or robot |

⇒ **that asymmetry is the whole objection, and it is `philosophy.pavement-saves-nature` run
backwards.** pavement is paid for once by one traveler and saved forever by all who follow. a riddle
is *enjoyed* once by one author and *paid for* forever by all who follow.

the cost is real and metered, never a matter of taste:

- extra tokens to parse — a robot reader pays them literally, per read, per review round
- extra time to understand — a human reader pays them in attention, the scarcest resource in the loop
- extra rounds to review — a reviewer who must decipher a claim cannot grade it in one pass

🟡 **and the reader cannot opt out.** they came for the subject; the puzzle was added without their
consent, and they must solve it before they learn whether the artifact even concerns them.

## .the bound — a riddle is misplaced, never wrong

> **riddles are fun if you have the time for them.**

that is the exact test. in a puzzle, a poem, or a game, the decipher **is** what the reader came
for, and to state the answer plainly would be the defect.

| the reader came for | a riddle is |
|---|---|
| the puzzle | the point |
| the subject | theft of their attention |

⇒ so this rule is not an objection to wit. it is an objection to wit **charged to someone who came
for a fact**.

## .the test — a COUNT, never a verdict

> **how many mental steps from these words to the subject?**

zero → it states its subject · one or more → a riddle, and each step is nameable.

an author cannot answer *"is this clear?"* honestly, because **for them the count is always zero**.
a number forces a rank a second reader can check.

### the worked count

`the-canons-author-is-the-only-role-that-obeys-it` — a real filename in this repo's `.dream/` queue:

1. *"the canon"* — which one? → telepath's prose canon
2. *"the author"* — who? → the telepath role
3. *"obeys it"* — obeys what? → its own rules
4. *"the only role"* — implies others exist → the seven other roles in the repo
5. *"only one that obeys"* — invert it → the others do **not** obey
6. so what is owed? → make them obey
7. how? → conform their briefs to the canon

**seven steps — and after all seven you still do not know the scope, the size, or the action.**

⇒ `conform.seven-roles-to-the-telepath-canon` — **zero steps.** the kind is `conform`; the slug
names what to conform. the reader has the task from the filename, with no file opened.

🟡 **the intermediate repair was still wrong.** `seven-roles-carry-90-unbulletized-paragraphs`
states the *symptom* rather than the *action*, and narrows a whole-canon conform to one rule. a
label names the subject, and for a task artifact the subject is **the work**.

## .the shapes

| shape | 👎 as written | 👍 stated |
|---|---|---|
| the observation title — a remark *about* the content | `the-canons-author-is-the-only-role-that-obeys-it` | `conform-seven-roles-to-the-telepath-canon` |
| the metaphor as claim — a diagnosis carried by an image | *"the cure catches the disease"* | *"the reviewer's subject grows with the defect it detects"* |
| the aphorism — a limitation converted into a philosophy | *"the engine guarantees it happens; the reviewer judges if it was real"* | *"the engine does not check whether the review was read. that gap is unfixed"* |
| the epigram — a blockquote that restates its section in fancier words | *"the one reader who cannot be surprised by it"* | delete it. it adds no claim the section lacks |
| the teaser header — names an emotion, never a fact | `## .the aha` | `## .the constraint the design turns on` |
| the withheld referent — names a kind and hides which | `## .the fix that looks like the one we were warned against` | `## .the reviewer's scope drops src/, never .behavior/` |
| the coy lead — announces a claim it declines to make | *"there is one call this does not settle"* | *"it does not settle whether a `case=N` narrative is exempt"* |
| the mislabeled header — promises one kind, carries another | `## .the fulcrum`, with an aphorism under it | `## .the fulcrum`, with the call and its rework grade |

⇒ **every repair is the same move: state the subject.** not one is a cut, and not one asks for more
words — four of the eight are shorter after the repair.

## 🟡 .a LABEL is the sharpest instance, because it is the retrieval surface

a riddle in a body costs a reader who already opened the file. a riddle in a **filename, a title, or
a header** costs every reader who scans and never opens.

- a reader who globs a directory, reads a `.see also`, or triages a queue sees **only labels**
- ⇒ so a riddle label fails at the one moment a label is the sole artifact available
- and it defeats the index outright: a `.dream/` queue of observations must be opened entry by entry
  to learn which holds the work. the queue stops to be a queue and becomes a pile

## .why an author will not cut their own

`rule.forbid.decode-friction`'s other species — a fetch — reads as an **oversight**, so an author
who finds one cuts it without argument.

a riddle reads as **the good part**. it is the sentence they were pleased with, so a sweep for
friction passes straight over it.

### the measured case is this canon's own author

`rule.forbid.subversive-prose` was booted, read, and agreed with — and every one of its ten shapes
is illustrated as a **body sentence**. so a filename that was a pure aphorism drew no cue, and the
defect ran the length of a route unchecked.

⇒ that is `rule.require.specialize-a-rule-its-readers-look-past`'s exact trigger, and its exact
repair: a rule aimed at the surface the reader occupied, in that surface's own vocabulary. a louder
`.what` on the parent would have caught nobody.

## .when it fires

| when… | then… |
|---|---|
| you name a file, a dream, a section, or an issue | the strongest cue. state the subject, never the remark about it |
| a phrase reads well aloud | the alarm, never the goal. a good label is *findable*, and often dull |
| you reach for a metaphor to carry a diagnosis | a metaphor beside a stated mechanism is fine. a metaphor **as** the claim is a riddle |
| you write a blockquote that restates the section above it | an epigram. it adds no claim, and it charges a re-read |
| a header promises a kind — `.the fulcrum`, `.the gap` — and carries another | the header lied. move the content, or rename the header |
| you would write *"there is one call…"* | name it in the same sentence |
| you would defend a phrase as *"more apt"* | aptness is not a property prose is graded on here |
| you feel pleased with a sentence | 🟡 count the steps. the pleasure and the step count are correlated |

## .the boundary

| a violation | not a violation |
|---|---|
| a title that is an observation about the content | a title that states the content, however dull |
| a metaphor **as** the claim | a metaphor beside a stated mechanism |
| a header that promises one kind and carries another | a header that names its section's claim |
| a coy lead that defers the claim it announces | a lead that states the claim, then its ground |
| an epigram that restates its own section | a verbatim quote, attributed |
| wit charged to a reader who came for a fact | wit where the reader came for the puzzle |

**the line that parts them: did the reader ask for the decipher?**

- yes → it is what they came for
- no → a riddle, however apt the phrase

## .the axis

a structure rule, so telepath owns it (`rule.require.generic-governs-structure-never-voice`). prose
that must be deciphered must be deciphered in every register, so it prescribes no member of that
rule's voice row.

blocker: a filename, title, or header that is an observation about its content rather than the
content · a metaphor or aphorism that carries a claim with no mechanism stated beside it · a header
that promises one kind and carries another · an epigram that restates the section it sits in.
nitpick: a coy lead that defers its claim by one sentence.
false positive: a dull label that names its subject · a metaphor beside a stated mechanism · a
verbatim quote · a declared artifact-kind header (`## .the cue that fired`) whose contract fixes its
content · a riddle in an artifact whose declared subject IS the puzzle.
