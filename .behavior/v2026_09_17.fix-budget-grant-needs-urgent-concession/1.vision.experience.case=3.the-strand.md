🔴 **the refusal must say *"your fix ships"*.** without that clause a driver reads the refusal as a
blocked passage and escalates. the concession was already **shed from the residual tally**
(`getStoneConcededBetterConcernCounts`), so the stone passes on its own arithmetic — what is lost is
the **confirmation**, never the passage.

## .what is actually lost, precisely

| | before this gate | after |
|---|---|---|
| the fix lands | ✅ | ✅ |
| the concern is shed from the tally | ✅ | ✅ |
| the stone passes | ✅ | ✅ |
| 🔴 **the lane that raised it confirms the fix** | ✅ | ❌ **never** |

⚠️ **and the loss is permanent, never deferred.** budget is cumulative rounds —
`isReviewPeerSkippedForBudget` reads `rounds >= budget` — so an artifact edit mints a new given and
restores no round. ⇒ the fix is unverified by the only party that ever objected to it, for the life
of the stone.

🔴 **that is the honest cost, and `#458` §3a named it before this wish existed:**

> under a hard cap, an empty `.taken` buys **passage** rather than one more round, silently. **the
> budget is what makes the re-raise possible.** remove the rounds and you remove the only mechanism
> that ever caught a token answer.

⇒ **the same argument applies to a token FIX.** nobody re-reads it. this gate accepts that trade for
`better`-graded concerns and refuses it for `urgent` ones — which is exactly where the harm test
draws the line, and is the best defence the design has.

**the aha:** *i wanted the round to prove i was diligent. the grade i chose already said the work was
maintenance — and maintenance does not get to buy attention.*

```gherkin
[t3] given the driver re-absorbs the SAME concern --severity urgent
     when the top-up runs again
     then it is GRANTED                                                  # ⚠️ see the flip-flop note

[t4] given the driver's harm claim at [t3] names no shipped harm
     then a reviewer grades it the mis-grade                             # rule.require.grade-a-concession-by-its-harm
      and the council reads it at the end-of-route fulcrum board          # the social bound
```

🔴 **`[t3]` is the loophole and it must be walked, never hidden.** a driver refused at `better` may
simply re-absorb at `urgent`. three bounds hold it, none mechanical:

1. **a contrary second stance is refused** — the predecessor's `F09` already forbids a flip-flop on
   one concern against one given. whether a *severity upgrade* counts as contrary is **`F07`**, open.
2. the re-grade is a **written harm claim** a council reads.
3. an `urgent` grade with no nameable harm is a **blocker** by two extant rules.

⚠️ **if `F07` rules that a severity upgrade is permitted, this gate is one command wide.** that is
the single most important open call in this vision, and it is stated as such.

## .why it fails safe

**loud** — the refusal names the live concession and its grade, so the driver sees exactly which
written claim of its own produced the refusal · **teaches the fix** — `--as passed` is printed as the
default exit, so the driver is not stranded · **prevents the false escalation** — the *"your fix
ships"* clause is what stops a driver from a `--as blocked` on a stone that would have passed.

🔴 **the failure mode this cell must avoid is a driver who reads the refusal as a wall.** it is not a
wall — `rule.always.raise-a-blocker-a-taken-cannot-close`'s test asks *"is there a change I am
PERMITTED to make that would close this?"* and the answer is **yes: `--as passed`**. the copy above
is what makes that answer visible.
