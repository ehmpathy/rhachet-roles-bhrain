# rule.always.razor-the-foundation-from-the-application

> **split each lesson, cure, or claim into its foundation (the law that holds in any domain) and its application (the one act it owes here), before it lands. miss the foundation and you overspecialize: the general lesson is lost. miss the application and you overgeneralize: the specific cure is lost.**

**how it applies the basis:** a lesson as first written is trusted in the shape it arrived in, usually one half. that shape is your first thought about the lesson, and it is doubted too: the questions test whether the law survives without today's words, and whether the act survives without the law.

## .the questions

| question | a miss looks like |
|---|---|
| what law holds here with no word of today's domain? | the lesson names today's tool, row, or metric as its subject |
| what one act does it owe here? | a principle with no concrete cure |
| is this law already held by some role? | a new law that restates an extant one in new words |
| where does each part live? | foundation in the general role whose readers span domains; application in its domain's role, which cites the foundation |

where a cure lands (its role, layer, and depth): `rule.always.skepticize-the-layer-of-a-cure`.

## .the worked pair

- foundation: every symptom is the trace of a defect upstream; cure the root of roots, and a refusal that holds work is never the cure
- application: lost bandwidth on a box is answered by a cure of the per-unit cost (one process per hook event, no respawn loop), never by fewer crews
- so a hold reported as the cure owes two things beside it: the owner of the root cure, and the dated hour the hold ends. a hold with no end is the same defect as no cure

## .the test

> **can I state this lesson with no word of today's domain, and the one act it owes here?**

both yes → land each part in its home. either no → it is not yet a lesson.

## .enforcement

nitpick: a lesson landed with no foundation named · a lesson landed with no concrete application · a new foundation that restates an extant law

peer: librarian `rule.prefer.decompose-a-subject-via-suffixes` and learner `rule.require.specialize-a-rule-its-readers-look-past`: the same split at the grain of a brief file · telepath `rule.forbid.absolutes` (a foundation stated over a set: what is the set, and did you walk it?)

forbid twin: `rule.forbid.lessons-at-one-level`
