## .the gap, precisely

- `after` is a bare line count into `passage.jsonl`. both logs are git-tracked, so a merge or a
  rebase that shifts rows could make an old approval read as one after the verdict
- an entry no live dispute cites (a dispute since rewound, or a `held` design call) has no sealed
  anchor: the driver can retype its `status` or delete it, and no gate sees it
- a conceded entry that no live dispute cites never reads as answered, since the approval fold
  tracks only cases a live dispute opened

## .the shape of the fix

- the lever records `afterHash`, the hash of the last passage row it read; the fold finds that row
  and fails closed (repair still owed) where it is absent
- a ruled entry's anchor is its ledger row: the gate lists every ledger case, live dispute or not,
  so a deleted or retyped entry with a row reads as unreadable
- the approval fold seeds a case from its ledger row as well as from a dispute

## .why it is not done in this round

each changes the ledger contract or the fold's seed set, with a migration for extant rows; the
round's blockers took the budget.
