### the record as it stood


`StoneBrainOutcome.outcome` uses `'undispatched'` as the discriminant for ANY halt (all six causes)
— `setStoneBrain.ts:47-60`. and `StoneBrainHaltCause` separately declares a SPECIFIC cause also
named `'undispatched'` — the "confirmed address, but the child process never launched" arm
(`StoneBrainHaltCause.ts:36`). so a failed spawn produces `{ outcome: 'undispatched', cause: 'undispatched' }`.

this is a domain-term overload (`rule.forbid.domain-term-ambiguity`): one word, two concepts. a
grep, a log line, or a `.cause === 'undispatched'` read is ambiguous against the broader
`outcome === 'undispatched'` check. everywhere else in this module the discriminated-union tag and
its inner-enum values were carefully kept distinct (the whole point of the split that keeps
`unenrolled`/`unreadable-clone`/`timed-out`/`unreadable-payload`/`unreadable-address` apart).

### .the shape of the fix

rename the CAUSE `'undispatched'` → `'spawn-failed'` (the outcome `'undispatched'` stays). touches:
`StoneBrainHaltCause.ts` (union member + comment), `setStoneBrain.ts:189` (the assignment),
`formatStoneBrainUndispatched.ts:91` (the HALTS record key), and the cause assertions in
`formatStoneBrain.test.ts`, `setStoneBrain.integration.test.ts`, `applyStoneBrainOnEntry.test.ts`.
the RENDER is unchanged (the HALTS key is not printed), so no render snapshot moves.

## .gap 2 — retry-after-failure has no backoff (a stated-tradeoff gap)

the i015 fix to `applyStoneBrainOnEntry` correctly does NOT mark entry on a failed dispatch, so the
next onStop tick retries rather than goes silent forever (the wish's "fail loud, never a silent
no-op" bound). but there is no circuit breaker: for the two causes that pay the full
`WHOAMI_TIMEOUT_MS = 2_000` probe (`unenrolled`, `timed-out`), EVERY onStop tick for the whole
duration a human works a misconfigured stone re-runs a 2s probe + a fresh spawn — up to 40% of the
hook's 5s budget, on every tick, for as long as the stone is open.

each retry is individually justified (fail-loud over efficiency, per the wish). the gap is that the
tradeoff is IMPLICIT, not STATED — the reviewer's ask is "a decision record even if the answer is
'acceptable, given F-a says unenrolled is rare'."

### .the shape of the fix

state the tradeoff in a `.note` on `applyStoneBrainOnEntry` (retry-every-tick is deliberate,
prioritizes fail-loud, bounded by F-a's "unenrolled is rare"), OR add a cheap circuit breaker: skip
the probe for N ticks after a failure whose cause is `unenrolled`/`timed-out` (a persisted
last-fail-tick beside the entry marker). the note is the minimum; the breaker is the fuller fix.
