# 1.vision.experience.case=_ — the inventory

dimensions: `1.vision.experience.dimensions.md`. every cell verdicted: **demoed** · **itemized** ·
**forbidden** (nurture — attemptable, rejected; an invariant) · **impossible** (nature — no attempt).

the space is walked as four sub-products. each is a full product: every tuple has a row. which axes
are crossed, and why the rest are not, is argued in `dimensions.md` `.the walked product`.

## .A — the dispute's path: gate (D2) × owed × carry (D7) × verdict (D4) = 2·2·2·3 = 24

`ground × band × reflect` tuple to the `owed` A row it lands in. every dispute reaches the gate as
one of the two `owed` values — a 95% **dirty** dispute is `owed: human`, and walks the `human` rows
here (R5–R8, R34, R35), blocks included. actor: the driver raises, the human rules.
