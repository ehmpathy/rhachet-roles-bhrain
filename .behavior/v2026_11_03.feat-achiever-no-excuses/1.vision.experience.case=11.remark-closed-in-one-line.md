# case 11 — a remark or a quick answer closes in one line

feel: sharp (a stop held over "nice" is noise that costs the turn the role exists to save) · care: critipath (every session) · actor: commander, achiever · seams: talk hook, store, stop hook

## .narrative

the talk hook writes every commander message to the inventory: it cannot tell an order from "nice"
or from "what does --hour mean?". today coverage only links an ask to a goal
(`src/domain.objects/Achiever/Coverage.ts`), so once onStop is wired, a remark is an uncovered ask
and the stop goes red. the clone would either set fake goals for remarks (the over-eager list the
itemize rule forbids) or loop.

so coverage gains a second form: close the entry as no work, with a why the commander can read. a
quick question closes with the answer it got; a remark closes as a remark. the stop then sees every
entry covered: by a goal, or by a stated "no work here".

the sharp half: a close with no why is refused, so "no work" cannot silently swallow a real order.
and the commander can see every such close in the triage view, beside the goals.

## .[tn] — a sketch

```
given  the inventory holds "nice" and "what does --hour mean?"
when   [t0] the achiever answers the question in one line, then stops
then     goal.triage.infer --when hook.onStop exits 2: uncovered asks = 2
when   [t1] rhx goal.memory.set --covers $hash1 --as no-work --why 'a remark, no work implied'
     and rhx goal.memory.set --covers $hash2 --as no-work --why 'answered: --hour is the follow-up deadline'
then     the stop passes
when   [t2] rhx goal.memory.set --covers $hash3 --as no-work   (no why)
then     the set exits 2: ✋ ConstraintError: a no-work close owes a why the commander can read
when   [t3] rhx goal.triage.infer
then     the view lists no-work closes with their whys, beside the goals
```
