# domain.term.choice.reason: reflect

## .etymology

the word reuses this repo's own `reflect` family: `rhx reflect` turns feedback into rules. a sure
misread is feedback on the **reviewer**, and its home is a reflection on that reviewer's rubric.
one word for one move, whether a human or a driver starts it.

| word | what it loses |
|---|---|
| `upstream-issue` | it names the container, not why the issue is owed |
| `defect-report` | it implies the artifact under review is defective, the opposite of the claim |

## .evidence

wish #747 invariant 3: a dispute the driver is sure of owes a reflection upstream, never a human's
time. the vision set the bar at `wrong` ∧ ≥ 0.90 (F4, wisher-ruled in S01).
