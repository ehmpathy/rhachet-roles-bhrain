# case=6 — a second round after a rubric edit

feel = happy · care = alterpath · cells = S1.across `warm` × `tail-edited`

the lever the wish pictures: a second round over the same lane reads its stable inputs from cache.
measured, it is the rarer lever — most second rounds arrive past the reach (C5) — so it is an
alterpath. it is demoed because it is the wish's named outcome, and because cure 1 changes it.

## .narrative

a driver answers a reviewer's blocker, and in the same pass a human edits one line of that lane's
rubric. the driver re-arrives 20 minutes later, well inside the ~66m reach.

**before.** the rubric sits at the top of the head. the edited line is the first byte that differs
from round 1, so the whole corpus behind it is paid fresh. a one-line rubric edit voids ~88% of the
prompt.

**after.** the rubric sits in the tail, behind the corpus. round 2's head matches round 1's up to
the first target the driver changed — and the mtime sort puts the files edited this round last
(`getAllTargetsByCacheStability`), so the untouched corpus leads and reads from cache. the rubric
edit costs only the tail.

## .[tn] sketch

```
given a lane whose round 1 ran 20 minutes ago
  [t0] the driver edits one target file, and the lane's rubric gains one line
  [t1] the guard runs round 2
    then the head matches round 1 up to the first edited target, which sorts last
    then the edited rubric sits in the tail, behind the corpus
  [t2] round 2 asks the brain
    then the untouched corpus reads from cache
    then only the edited target, the rubric, the refs, and the conversation pay the full rate
  [t3] the driver runs `rhx review._.cost --of tree`
    then the pair grades `served`
```
