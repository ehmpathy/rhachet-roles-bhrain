# case=11 · the dispute that buried three concessions

- **coordinates** = `dispute × answered × live` — the same cell as `case=10 [t1]`, reached on a lane
  that carries **four** concerns rather than one
- **feel** = sharp · **care** = critipath · **verdict** = the design's own defect, demoed

**this case exists because every prior demo assumed a lane carries ONE concern.** none said so, and
none had to — the design's finest named grain was the lane, so the assumption was invisible
(`dimensions.md` § 5). ⇒ `S07` names the atom, and with the word in hand the defect is one table.

## .the narrative

*driver, on `1.vision`, round 4, an `experience-coverage` lane at 0 blockers and 4 nitpicks*

the lane files four nitpicks. I read all four, and I am not of one mind:

| # | the nitpick | my honest position |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | a `.see also` points at a file that moved | ✅ **concede.** it is right, and I will repair it |
| 2 | two counts drifted from their source | ✅ **concede.** right again |
| 3 | a header names an emotion | ✅ **concede.** right a third time |
| 4 | *"`case=10` adds no cell, so it should be deleted"* | 🔴 **dispute.** it re-walks a cell the grid reached by the WRONG road for two rounds |

I write the fulcrum for #4, and run:

```
rhx route.stone.set --stone 1.vision --as disputed --with experience-coverage --why <path>
```

🔴 **and the stone passes.**

## 🔴 .the mechanism, and why it is a defect

`S06` settled the mechanism as a **tally exclusion**, reusing the seam a human forgive already
walks — `getNonOverruledReviewFiles`, which drops a **review FILE** from `reviewFilesToCount`. so:

| the grain | what leaves the tally | what the judge sees |
|---|---|---|
| per-**file** (as `S06` landed) | all 4 | 🔴 a clean lane. **my own three concessions are erased** |
| per-**concern** (`S07`) | 1 | ✅ 3 nitpicks, still counted, still holds the stone |

⚠️ **the driver's admission of fault was deleted by their disagreement with a neighbour.** I did not
argue the three away. I never mentioned them. they left the arithmetic because a **file** was the
unit the mechanism could address.

⇒ and it breaks the wisher's own stated purpose for a fulcrum — *"the purpose is a guaranteed later
review"* (`S05`). a guarantee that covers 1 concern in 4 guarantees naught about the other 3.

🔴 **`S05` had already ruled the grain, and the mechanism did not follow it.** *"each fulcrum is a
single dispute … a disagreement is about one point."* ⇒ **the settlement reached the row it was
written on and no mechanism** — the same class the fulcrum board records at `F15 → F17` and the seed
inventory at its gap list.

## .the repair

a stance names **one concern**, by position within that reviewer's own report:

```
rhx route.stone.set --stone 1.vision --as disputed  --with experience-coverage --about nitpick.4 --why <path>
rhx route.stone.set --stone 1.vision --as conceded  --with experience-coverage --about nitpick.1
rhx route.stone.set --stone 1.vision --as conceded  --with experience-coverage --about nitpick.2
rhx route.stone.set --stone 1.vision --as conceded  --with experience-coverage --about nitpick.3
```

🟡 **no reviewer is asked to mint an id.** `nitpick.4` is an ordinal within that reviewer's report,
and `contract.reviewer-output` already fixes the two severities the ordinal indexes into. ⇒ the
driver names the position; the reviewer's contract is untouched.

⇒ **one fulcrum may back N disputes** (`S07`). the entry is the argument; the dispute is one use of it.

## 🔴 .the boundary walk — every declaration the engine accepts, against ONE law

> **a concern is discharged only by a party with STANDING over it.**

three parties hold the authority, and no other move may discharge one:

- the **reviewer** that raised it — by not re-raising it next round
- a **human** — by a forgive, which is an exercise of authority
- the **driver**, **by name** — one concern at a time, disputed or conceded

⇒ **every value `PassageReport.status` accepts — all ten — plus the two this behavior adds:**

| # | the declaration | who declares it | does it discharge a concern it did not name? | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `passed` | driver | no — it discharges naught | ✅ |
| 2 | `arrived` | driver | no — it opens a round | ✅ |
| 3 | `blocked` | driver | no. `rule.always.raise-a-blocker-a-taken-cannot-close` states the halt is an **OR** over points, so the closeable ones stay owed | ✅ |
| 4 | `exhausted` | the **engine** | no. it ends a reviewer's rounds; the blockers it raised outlive its budget (`rule.always.converge-to-terminal`) | ✅ |
| 5 | `malfunction` | the **engine** | no. an unreadable verdict scores as **one blocker** and still gates | ✅ |
| 6 | `rewound` | driver / human | no — it clears state for a **fresh** evaluation, so each concern is re-raised or not by its own reviewer | ✅ |
| 7 | `approved` | **human** | no — a sign-off on the stone, never on a concern | ✅ |
| 8 | `overruled --level N` | **human** | **yes — and permitted.** the breadth IS the grant | ✅ **by authority** |
| 9 | `promised --that <slug>` | driver | ⚠️ **out of class.** a self-review's concerns are the driver's own, so no other party's concern exists to suppress. 🟡 it shares the *shape* — the gate checks the articulation EXISTS, never what it says | ✅ |
| 10 | 🔴 `contemplated --that <slug>` | driver | **yes.** one `.taken` file discharges a slug that carried 6 points. the gate counts REVIEWERS | 🔴 **breaks it** |
| 11 | 🔴 `disputed --with <slug>` (per-file) | driver | **yes.** 1 dispute sheds 4 concerns — this case | 🔴 **breaks it** |
| 12 | 🔴 `conceded --with <slug>` (per-lane) | driver | the **mirror**: it over-claims rather than suppresses — the driver asserts agreement with concerns they never read | 🔴 **breaks it** |

⇒ **nine of twelve obey the law, and the three that break it are the three at LANE or SLUG grain.**
the pattern is exact: **every move that names a PARTY obeys; every move that names a FILE or a SLUG
does not.**

🟡 **one mechanism outside that set belongs in the walk, and it is the sharpest ✅ on the page.** a
reviewer's **fresh given** supersedes its whole prior given — `getLatestPeerGivensPerSlug` keys
latest-per-slug by iteration — so every concern the reviewer declines to re-raise is discharged
wholesale. **permitted, by the strongest authority there is: the party that raised it is the party
that dropped it.**

⚠️ **the twelve rows are enumerated from `PassageReport.ts`, never from this walk's own recall.** a
hand-listed set collapses `passed`/`arrived`, drops `approved`, `promised`, and `malfunction`, and
smuggles in a non-status mechanism. ⇒ **a completeness claim is verified against the declaration**
(`.dream/v2026_09_05.enbrief.a-census-claim-is-verified-against-disk-never-against-its-own-summary.md`).

### 🟡 the two failure shapes are a symmetric pair

| the move, at coarse grain | what it does silently |
|---|---|
| **dispute** | **suppresses** — drops concerns you never argued |
| **concede** | **over-claims** — commits you to concerns you never read |

⇒ a rule aimed at dispute alone catches half of it. the law above is stated over **declarations**
for that reason.

### 🔴 the extant hole this walk found beside the new one

`contemplated --that <slug>` has **the identical defect and it ships today.**
`rule.always.converge-with-reviewers.via-a-taken-per-point` already states the duty — *"for EVERY
`.given.by_peer` point you MUST author the paired `.taken`"* — and names its own gap: *"the gate
counts REVIEWERS. this rule counts POINTS … the door is a floor; this rule is the work."*

⇒ **so the concern was already the real grain of the domain, admitted in a booted rule, with no term
and no mechanism.** the per-concern stance is the first move that gives that grain an address —
which makes `--about` a lever the `.taken` gate could later reuse, over a cost this behavior pays
alone.

🟡 **out of scope for this stone.** recorded so the next traveler finds it named:
`.dream/v2026_09_10.fix.the-taken-gate-counts-reviewers-not-concerns.md`.

### 🟡 the near-miss that is NOT a member of this class

`runStoneGuardReviews.ts:430` cache-skips on raw `cachedReview.blockers === 0`, so a lane at 0
blockers and 8 nitpicks never re-runs. **its 8 nitpicks are frozen, and they are not suppressed** —
the cached file is still pushed into `reviews`, so every one of them still counts.

⇒ a different defect (`case=10`), and the boundary is worth the line: **this class is about what
leaves the ARITHMETIC, never about what stops to be RE-EXAMINED.**

## .the bdd timeline

```gherkin
given a stone `1.vision` with peer reviewer `experience-coverage`
  and the guard declares --allow-blockers 0 --allow-nitpicks 2
  and the lane's latest .given raises 0 blockers and 4 nitpicks
  and the driver disputes exactly ONE of them and concedes the other three

[t0] when the driver runs --as disputed --with experience-coverage --why <path>   # per-FILE
     then the whole review file leaves reviewFilesToCount
      and totalNitpicks falls 4 → 0
      and the stone PASSES                                       # 🔴 the defect
      and the three conceded nitpicks are never repaired

[t1] when the driver runs --as disputed … --about nitpick.4    # per-CONCERN
     then exactly ONE nitpick leaves the tally
      and totalNitpicks falls 4 → 3, which exceeds the allowance of 2
      and the stone is HELD                                      # ✅ the three still gate
      and the halt names the three concerns still owed

[t2] given the driver then concedes nitpick.1 and repairs it
     when it runs --as passed
     then the hash moves, the cache misses, the lane re-runs
      and its new .given raises 3 nitpicks                       # the repair landed

[t3] given a driver who disputes one concern with a fulcrum that argues a DIFFERENT one
     when it runs --as disputed --about nitpick.4 --why <a fulcrum about nitpick.1>
     then it is ADMITTED                                         # ⚠️ no gate reads the entry (S04)
      and the mismatch is a residual, recorded at dimensions.md § 3

[t4] given ONE fulcrum entry and eight concerns the driver disputes
     when it runs --as disputed eight times, each --about distinct, each --why <the same path>
     then all eight are ADMITTED                                 # ✅ S07 — 1 fulcrum : N disputes
      and totalNitpicks falls by exactly 8
```

🔴 **`[t0]` is the step this case exists to make legible, and it is the design as `S06` left it.**
every artifact on this route said *"per point"* in its prose while the mechanism shed a file.

⚠️ **`[t3]` is the residual, and it is NOT a new hole.** `S04` ruled that no gate reads a `--why`
entry's content — *"a reason is a nudge to reconsider"* — so a fulcrum that argues the wrong concern
is the same unmechanized class as a fulcrum that argues naught. the per-concern grain **narrows** it
(the concern is now named, so a reader can check the pairing) without a gate.

## .why it fails safe

**fast** — the arithmetic is at the gate, before any subprocess · **loud** — the halt names each
concern still owed, by position · **teaches the fix** — the residual count says how many more
declarations are required, and of which kind.

⚠️ **the ergonomic hazard.** four declarations where one used to do is more typing, and a driver will
ask for a `--about all`. ⇒ **that flag reintroduces this defect under a friendlier name**, and the
walk above is why it must not exist: a driver who types `all` has not read all.
