## .settled

### 1 · a claim is not taught until its ENTRY POINTS carry it

a concept can be fully argued in a repo and still be missed, because a reader meets the depth last
and the entry point first.

measured on the learner role. the entoolment ladder — `0 🧠 tribal → 1 📚 brief → 2 💪💧 fluid →
3 💪🔩 rigid → 4 💪🪨 solid`, all five rungs tools, `entool` naming the climb across all five —
was stated correctly in four places and absent from two:

| the artifact | carried the claim? |
|---|---|
| `philosophy.entoolment-is-the-pinnacle._` and its two zoom-ins | yes, in full |
| `term=externalize.entool._.choice.*` | yes |
| `rule.always.enskill-the-tactics-you-discover` · `rule.always.entool-the-skills-you-touch` | yes |
| the role's `readme.md` | no — its `triggers` list read `enbrief` / `enskill` / `entool` as three peers |
| `im_an.obsessive_learner` — the identity brief, and its `.md.min` | no — one `externalize when` block, headed by the genus |

⇒ so a reader who booted the role and never dereferenced the philosophy would take the three verbs
as three families. the depth refuted that; not one artifact they read did.

### 2 · the entry points are the two a reader cannot skip

a role's `readme.md` and its identity brief are read on every enrollment. a `ref`-tier philosophy is
read when someone reaches for it. so a claim the depth carries and the entry points omit is a claim
that reaches only the reader who already suspected it.

⇒ the repair is not a stronger argument in the philosophy. it is one fence and one table at each
entry point, which is what landed.

### 3 · a lossy `.min` and its precise source can both boot, at the same tier

found while grading the repair. `.agent/repo=.this/role=any/briefs/im_an.obsessive_learner.md` is a
symlink to the full source, and it boots at `say`; `repo=bhrain/role=learner` boots the `.md.min` at
`say` as well. one brief, two say-tier boots, one compressed and one not.

⇒ it also makes the tier exemption in a density audit misfire: the source has a `.md.min` beside it
in `src/`, so it grades `ref`, and via the symlink it has no min beside it, so it grades `say`. both
verdicts are correct about their own path, and only the second is correct about what a session pays.
