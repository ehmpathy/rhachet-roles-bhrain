# seed S01 — measure the yield for terseness

## .said — verbatim

> is the vision yield terse? dense? direct? maximally ruthlessly terse?

## .settled

**a yield's terseness is measured, never assumed.** the question was asked of an artifact that had
already passed peer review, and the honest answer was **no** — 615 lines, with 18 instances of
*"an earlier draft"*.

⇒ the reviewers were not at fault. **no lane graded length**, so length grew unchecked while every
graded property stayed clean. an ungraded property drifts to whatever the author's habit produces.

⚠️ the sharper half: **the author cannot answer this question about their own artifact without a
count.** each line was added deliberately and reads as necessary from the inside. the measure — a
line count, plus a grep for the repeated phrase — is what made the answer decidable.
