# catalog.of=externalization 🦉📜

the index over the externalization verbs — the `en-` family, plus the two words that bracket
it. one row per verb; the depth lives in each verb's own term cluster
(`rule.require.catalog-is-an-index`).

🟡 **this catalog exists because its index had been parked inside a member.** the family table sat
in `term=externalize.enbrief._.choice.reason.md` — one verb's ref file — so a reader who wanted the *set* had
to already know which member to open. that is the defect this artifact retires.

## .the question this answers

> **"i have a lesson / a tactic / a half-mechanized artifact. which verb do i reach for?"**

## 🔑 .the family's construction law

> **`en$X` produces a `$X`.**

| verb | produces |
|---|---|
| `enbrief` | a brief |
| `enskill` | a skill |
| `entool` | a tool |
| `entrait` | a trait |

🟡 **the law was already true across three members and had never been stated** — declared by the
wisher on 2026-09-06, which is why the fourth member needed no argument. it is also the membership
test: a verb whose product has no declared noun is not one of these
(`term=externalize.entrait._.choice.reason.md`).

## .the ladder the family partitions

every `en-` row below names a **climb on the entoolment ladder**
(`philosophy.entoolment-is-the-pinnacle._.md`):

```
0 🧠 tribal  →  1 📚 brief  →  2 💪💧 fluid  →  3 💪🔩 rigid  →  4 💪🪨 solid
```

**all five rungs are tools** (`term=artifact.tool`). they differ in imagine cost per use, never
in tool-hood.

## .the rows

| verb | the climb | it retires | the cue that fires it | the cluster |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **enbrief** | 0 → 1 · 🧠 → 📚 | the derivation | a concept surprised you, or was hard to discover | `term=externalize.enbrief._.choice._.md` |
| **enskill** | 0 → 2 · 🧠 → 💪💧 | the sequence | you ran the same sequence a second time | `term=externalize.enskill._.choice._.md` |
| **entool** | 2 → 4 · 💪💧 → 💪🪨 | the execution | you called a skill and still finished by hand | `term=externalize.entool._.choice._.md` |
| **entrait** | — an orthogonal axis, never a rung | the recall | the cue arrives before the artifact does — a form you must already be in | `term=externalize.entrait._.choice._.md` |
| **pave** | any climb | — the umbrella verb | you laid a reusable path for a future traveler | `term=externalize.pave._.choice._.md` |
| **learn** | — | — the retention side, never a climb | a lesson must not be re-derived | `term=externalize.learn._.choice._.md` |

🟡 **`pave` and `learn` are no `en-` verbs, and they are here on purpose** — a reader who asks
*"which verb?"* picks among all six, never among four. `pave` names the act at any rung; `learn`
names durable retention, which is what an externalized artifact makes possible rather than a
climb of its own.

## .`entrait` moves the OTHER WAY, which is why it has no rung

the three climbs move work OUT of a brain into an artifact a reader invokes. entraitment moves it IN:

| the act | where the work goes | what a reader does with the artifact |
|---|---|---|
| `enbrief` · `enskill` · `entool` | OUT — into an artifact | invokes it, at some cost per use |
| **`entrait`** | **IN — below decision** | never touches it again after boot |

⇒ zero cost per use reads as *"rung 5"* and is not one: **an artifact you never touch cannot sit on
a scale whose unit is cost per touch.** so the ladder and entraitment are orthogonal axes.

🟡 this is a **third row** owed to `philosophy.entoolment-is-the-pinnacle.concepts-vs-capacity`,
which today states a two-way split — a brief confers concepts, a skill confers capacity. a trait
confers a default, and that file does not yet say so.

## .`enroute` — SPOKEN, UNDECLARED, and not a fourth peer

`philosophy.entoolment-is-the-pinnacle._.md` uses it twice as a participle:

> *"the fluid operation's branches are **enrouted** and hardened one at a time"*

**it has no term cluster.** that is a `rule.require.domain-term-itemization` gap — a verb this
repo's published prose already speaks.

🟡 **and it is no peer of the three** — F15 settled that a route IS a 🔩 rigid skill, rung 3, so:

| candidate | verdict |
|---|---|
| a fourth peer verb, beside enbrief/enskill/entool | false symmetry — it would claim a fourth artifact kind where the spectrum has three mechanized rungs |
| `enskill`, aimed at rung 3 | a specialization — the same act, with the target rung named |

⇒ so the settlement owed is whether a specialization earns its own word at all, given
`rule.forbid.domain-term-synonyms`. recorded as a gap rather than guessed.
