# domain.term.choice.reason: trace

## .etymology

trace = to follow a path from point to point, and the mark that path leaves. both senses
are wanted: the report follows one lane across its rounds, and what it renders is the mark the
rounds left behind. the word is also already the industry's for exactly this shape — a
distributed trace is a sequence of spans read for where the time went.

rejected candidates, each for a reason that outlives the choice:

| candidate | why not |
|---|---|
| **history** | names the archive, not the read. every artifact here has a history; only one report differences it |
| **timeline** | a render shape, not an intent. a timeline could serve `observe` just as well |
| **series** | true and empty — it says the rows are ordered and not what a reader does with them |
| **breakdown** | taken, and dangerously. the by-input census is already called a breakdown, and it is an `observe` surface. one word over two intents is the ambiguity `rule.forbid.domain-term-ambiguity` forbids |
| **drilldown** | a buzzword (`rule.forbid.buzzwords`), and it names depth where the axis measures intent |

## .disputes

### dispute: whether `trace` is an axis POSITION or merely a flag — raised 2026-09-23 — status: RESOLVED (a position)

- raised.by = the `enroll-impl-behavior-intent` peer lane, at i005
- claim     = `--trace` is one more render option on the cost report, so it owes a help line
  rather than a term and an axis position
- counter   = an open debt on this branch is the evidence it is a position. the demo
  `case=6 [t3]` asserts that a second round's cache share exceeds the first's — a difference
  — and it sat open for the whole branch with no one able to name the surface that would
  close it. it had been filed at `observe` for want of a third position, and no observe
  surface can serve it: the three marginals average a lane's rounds, so a series like
  `0, 0, 8.4, 41.2, 7.3` reports as one 29.7% and the step is gone
- resolution = `trace` is the third position on `intent`. a demo whose cell does not exist
  obliges no instrument, which is exactly why the debt could sit unclosed and unassigned

## .evidence

- discovery: a walk that missed it, twice. the `intent` axis was decomposed with two
  positions in two separate passes, and `case=6` was filed at `observe` both times. the term
  was surfaced by a demo that would not fit its own cell — the sharpest kind of evidence an
  axis is short a value (`rule.require.dimensional-decomposition`)
- the measured series that proves a marginal cannot serve. one lane's per-round cache
  shares on this branch: `0.0, 87.9, 101.7, 87.4, 87.5, 0.0` — a marginal over those reports
  one figure, and every step that matters is invisible in it
- the bound is declared, not assumed. `--trace` is `tree` breadth only. at `grove`, two
  trees can share a `<stone> i<NNN> · <slug>` key, so the cross-tab collides — a ruled
  decline rather than an absent surface (`F22` fork c)

## .the boundary

`trace` is an intent, never a breadth. a reader who takes it for "more detail" will look for
`--trace` at `grove` and find it refused. the refusal is correct: the axis position is served
only where its key is unique.
