# S01 · star the ask

## .said

> *"teach the telepath to always make the ask crystal clear. **star the ask.** never hide the ask in bloated prose"*

🟡 verbatim. the emphasis is the wisher's own.

## .settled

when a message carries an **ask** — an act the reader must perform, a decision they must render, or a datum they must supply — that ask takes a treatment the prose around it cannot wear.

the concept, stripped of this round:

- the ask is the one passage whose miss **stops the work**, silently, with neither party aware
- every other passage is information; miss it and the reader is merely less informed
- ⇒ that asymmetry is what earns the ask a visual privilege no other passage gets
- **brevity does not close it.** terseness compresses prose; it does not promote the ask out of it
- a header is **not** a treatment — a shape the reader's eye already filters as ordinary structure does not solve this

🟡 *"star"* fixes the **outcome** (unmissable), never the **mechanism**. a glyph, a promoted block, or a fenced trailer are all open, and the choice is voice — so it belongs to the adopter, never to the generic rule.
