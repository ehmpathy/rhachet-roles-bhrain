# domain discovery: the education suite's terms

the territory is real-world education: a student, a course, a canon, a lab, a credential. the map (this repo) already holds `learn`, `enroll` (rhachet), `lesson` (`kno201`), `research`. each new term below is grounded in the territory and vetted against the map.

## .move 1 — narrative, then bdd

> a guild master sees an apprentice botch a joint. he sends the apprentice to the joiner's course. the apprentice reads the masters' treatises, cuts practice joints on scrap wood at the bench, then builds a cabinet. the guild inspects it and writes the course and the masters into the apprentice's record. next week the apprentice cuts the joint right.

| narrative noun / event | dobj / dop |
|---|---|
| guild master sends | director → `education.pursuit init` |
| the joiner's course | `course` (one subject, bounded) |
| the masters' treatises | `greats` (the canon) |
| practice joints on scrap | `vitro` (isolated practice, before the live work) |
| the cabinet | `entool` (the climb on the extant ladder; no new word) |
| the guild's record | `pedigree` |
| writes it in | `education.pedigree set` |

```
given a role with a gap a director names
when  the director opens a course on the gap        → route + bind
when  the student reads the greats                  → kernels with citations
when  the student practices in vitro                → held / surprised per kernel
when  the student entools                           → briefs + skills in the role's dir
when  the student graduates                         → pedigree entry
then  the role boots with the new briefs and its pedigree
```

## .move 2 — five whys on "send a role to study"

send a role to study → why? → it lacks knowledge a task needs → why not let it learn on the job? → on-the-job lessons arrive one defect at a time (`learn`) → why is that slow? → the domain's greats already paid for those lessons → so what is the act? → **acquire the greats' lessons on purpose, test them, keep them.** that is education, distinct from `learn` (retention of one's own).

## .move 3 — dimensional decomposition of the unit

| axis | values |
