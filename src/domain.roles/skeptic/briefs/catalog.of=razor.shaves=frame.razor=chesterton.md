# razor: chesterton

shaves: a choice or frame · settled by: observation (history, blame, tests), plus rivals (candidate purposes)

## .the question

> **do I know why this fence was built, and what would break if it went?**

## .the source

- G. K. Chesterton, 1929, the chapter "The Drift from Domesticity": "If you don't see the use of it, I certainly won't let you clear it away. Go away and think."
- read via https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chesterton%27s_fence

## .fails when

- it is read as a veto. once the use is known, the fence may go
- the fence has outlived its purpose; the purpose, once read, says so
- it is used to guard the status quo

## .run by

`rule.always.ask-why-then-ask-can-we-delete`
