# case=6 — a dispute buys no budget

cells: R20 (happy, critipath) · R21 (sharp, critipath) — invariant 6

## .narrative

the logic lane has run its budget dry: exhausted, one blocker left open. the driver disputes it at
80% and links fulcrum F21. the dispute sheds the concern from the tally, and that is all it does.
no round is added, the exhausted lane is not re-run, and no paid read happens.

the driver then reaches for `rhx route.guard.budget --add 1` to "let the reviewer see the
argument." the meter refuses: the only warrant for budget is a live `urgent` concession, and a
dispute is the opposite of a concession. the refusal names the doors that DO settle a dispute,
read from its ground — a human verdict at the gate always; a reflection upstream only for a sure
misread, never for a dirty dispute.

## .[tn] sketch

```
given the logic lane is exhausted with blocker.1 open
when [t0] the driver disputes blocker.1 at 0.80, --why F21
  then the lane's budget is unchanged
  then the lane is not re-run
  then F21 is owed: human
when [t1] rhx route.guard.budget --for review --add 1 --peer logic --stone 5.1.execution
  then refused: no live urgent concession on this stone   # today's bytes: formatBudgetGrantRefusalLines.test.ts.snap:91
  then (new, not built — F19 keeps today's bytes; the doors render at the dispute and the gate)
       where the target lane carries a dispute, the refusal adds the doors that settle it,
       read from the fulcrum's ground:
       wrong ground → a human verdict at the gate · or, at ≥ 0.90, a reflection (--reflect)
       dirty ground → a human verdict at the gate, only (a --reflect there is refused, case=7)
```
