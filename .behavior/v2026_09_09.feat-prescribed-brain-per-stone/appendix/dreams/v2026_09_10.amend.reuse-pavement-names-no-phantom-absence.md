# 🌙 dream: `reuse-pavement` names the phantom PATH and not the phantom ABSENCE

- **kind** = amend
- **owner** = `bhrain/role=learner` — `rule.always.reuse-pavement-before-improvise`
- **caught** = 2026-09-10

## .the cue that fired

a wisher asked *"we ourselves can verify it was accepted via clone get followup, no?"* — and the
answer was **yes**, against a design record that had graded that route out.

the record's line: *"parse the brain-cli's answer from the transcript — brittle, a prose format
nobody promised to keep."*

⇒ that is the correct grade for an **ad-hoc scrape of a terminal**. it is the wrong grade for
`rhx clone get`, which the dependency ships, versions, contracts, and emits as directioned JSON.
**one `--help` would have settled it, and the grade was written without one.**

## .the gap, precisely

`rule.always.reuse-pavement-before-improvise` names three anti-patterns:

| anti-pattern | its shape |
|---|---|
| the **braided trail** | a second path laid beside a serviceable one |
| the **rut** | a found path conformed to with no test |
| the **phantom path** | a **cited** artifact that does not exist |

🔴 **the fourth is absent, and it is the phantom path's exact inverse:**

> **the phantom absence — an artifact asserted NOT to exist, with no look.**

they are mirror failures of one act, and they fail differently:

| | the claim | how it surfaces |
|---|---|---|
| phantom **path** | *"X exists"* — and it does not | a reader globs and finds naught. **loud** |
| 🔴 phantom **absence** | *"X does not exist"* — and it does | 🔴 **naught surfaces.** the option is simply never weighed |

⇒ **the phantom absence is the more expensive of the two**, because the phantom path fails *at the
moment a reader reaches for it* and the phantom absence fails **silently, forever** — nobody
dereferences a road not taken.

## .the second shape it takes — a grade of the GENUS, applied to the INSTANCE

the measured case did not assert *"no such surface exists."* it asserted *"that class of move is
brittle"* — and let the class grade stand in for a check of the instance.

⇒ **a capability graded by its archetype is a capability graded without a look.** *"transcript
parses are brittle"* is a true generalization and a false verdict on a shipped, contracted surface.

## .the shape of the fix

add a fourth anti-pattern to `rule.always.reuse-pavement-before-improvise`, plus a cue row and an
enforcement clause:

```md
| when… | then… |
|---|---|
| you would grade an option **unavailable**, **brittle**, or **not shipped** | 🔴 a `--help` is cheaper than the paragraph you are about to write |
| you would grade a whole CLASS of approach rather than the instrument in front of you | that is a grade of the genus. check the instance |
| a design record lists **candidates** | 🔴 each row is a claim about what EXISTS. it needs the same check a citation does |

blocker: an option graded unavailable with no look at the tool · a class grade offered as a verdict
on a specific shipped surface.
```

and one line in `.the anti-patterns`:

```md
- **the phantom absence** — an option ruled out as absent, with no look. the phantom path's inverse,
  and the more expensive one: it fails silently, because nobody dereferences a road not taken
```

## .why it is not done in this round

**dirt.** it edits a booted, published `say` brief in `bhrain/role=learner`, which changes the boot
payload of every consumer of that role. this route's diff is `.behavior/` and its own dreams; the
amendment ripples outside it.

⚠️ **and it is one rung short of the real fix.** a cue makes an author look; **a checker makes the
gap visible.** a candidate table whose rows are capability claims is mechanically detectable — that
is a second dream, not this one.
