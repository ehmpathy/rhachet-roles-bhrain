# case=3 — the approval halt still names the human

feel: happy · care: critipath (a no-regression check)

## .narrative

the driver finishes the vision, and the peer reviews pass. one gate is left: the stone wants a human's sign-off. the driver reads `please ask your human to`, then `rhx route.stone.set --stone 1.vision --as approved`.

it knows this one is not its own. it has just watched the urgent halt hand it a top-up as "yours to spend". this halt has kept its old words, and the difference is the signal. if it tries the approval anyway, the command refuses it, since it has no TTY. so it stops, tells its human the stone is ready, and waits.

its human reads the vision, approves at its own terminal, and the driver runs `--as passed`. two halts, two owners, and each halt names the right one.
