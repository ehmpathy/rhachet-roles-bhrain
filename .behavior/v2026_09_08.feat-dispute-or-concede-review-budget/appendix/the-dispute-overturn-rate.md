rarely overturned** — and that the only data available contradicts it. this appendix carries the
measurement trail behind that claim. the yield states the output; this states how it was reached.

## the honest bounds

- **n = 6, one route.** a rate, not *the* rate
- **selection bias, unknown sign.** 9 of 18 stayed open; if the adjudicated set is the contentious
  set, the rate over all fulcrums is nearer **5/18 ≈ 28%**. that is the conservative floor, and it is
  still not *"rare"*
- **these are fulcrums, not disputes.** a dispute is one kind of judgment call; its rate may differ

⇒ **28% to 83%. the wish's economics assume a rate near zero.**
