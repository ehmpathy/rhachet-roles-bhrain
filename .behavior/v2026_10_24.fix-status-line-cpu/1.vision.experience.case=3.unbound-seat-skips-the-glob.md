# case=3 — a bind change: an unbound seat skips the glob, and a bind or unbind shows at once (happy + sharp · critipath)

## .narrative

a seat works on `main`, no route bound. today every repaint globs the whole repo, `.git` and
caches included, to learn there is no bind flag — then prints an empty line. after: the first
repaint globs once, caches the blank line keyed on the branch, and the repaints that follow read
the cache until it ages past 30s.

when the driver runs `rhx route.bind.set`, the bind op drops the branch's cache, so the next
repaint finds the new route at once.

the sharp half: the route finishes and the driver runs `rhx route.bind.del`. the cache still
holds `🗿 5.3.verification, yield 🌾`. two guards stop that stale line: the bind op drops the
cache, and the bind flag's own stat is in the fingerprint — so even an unbind by hand (`rmsafe`
of the flag) makes the next repaint a miss. the line goes blank on the next repaint, never later.

## .bdd sketch

```
given('[case3] a repo on a branch with no bound route')
  when('[t0] the line repaints 10 times')
    then('every repaint prints an empty line')
    then('the bind glob runs once, not 10 times')
  when('[t1] a route is bound to the branch via route.bind.set')
    then('the next repaint prints 🗿 <first stone>, with no wait for the max age')
  when('[t2] the bind flag is removed (route.bind.del, or by hand)')
    then('the next repaint prints an empty line')
```
