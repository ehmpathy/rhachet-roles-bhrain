# razor: sagan

shaves: a claim with no owner or weight · settled by: rivals (the settled base is the rival)

## .the question

> **how far does this claim sit from what is already settled, and does the evidence at hand match that distance?**

## .the source

- Sagan, *Broca's Brain*, 1979; *Cosmos*, 1980: "extraordinary claims require extraordinary evidence"
- Truzzi, 1975 and 1978: "extraordinary claims require extraordinary proof"
- Laplace, 1814: the weight of evidence "must be proportioned to its strangeness"
- Hume, *Enquiry*, sect. 10, 1748: "A wise man, therefore, proportions his belief to the evidence"
- read via https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sagan_standard and https://davidhume.org/texts/e/10

## .fails when

- "extraordinary" is undefined (Deming); name the settled base it departs from
- it can suppress a real discovery (Cardeña)
- the claim is the best explanation at hand; then no extra weight is owed (Schick)

## .run by

`rule.always.test-a-claim-against-the-whole-it-speaks-for`
