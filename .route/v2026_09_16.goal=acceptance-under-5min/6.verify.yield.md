## 🔴 .two claims in THIS STONE's own text are wrong. corrected here

the stone was written mid-route and holds inference I later refuted by measurement. a
future traveler reads the stone, so the correction belongs beside it.

### ❌ *"93m55s compressed into 11m50s = 7.9x. the suite is WAIT-bound."*

**wrong.** per-file wall clock inflates under contention, so a parallel run's summed file
time is not a measure of work:

| run | per-file total | real work? |
|---|---|---|
| serial | 42m55s | ✅ yes |
| 8 workers | 93m55s | ❌ inflated |
| 16 workers | 154m20s | ❌ inflated |

against the **serial** denominator the true figures are **3.63x at 8** and **4.36x at
16** — real, and sublinear. ⇒ **a derived ratio needs both terms measured under the same
conditions.**

### ❌ *"review.join-intersect = 5m32s, so ONE file misses the bar by itself."*

**wrong twice over.** 5m32s was a contention-inflated figure; serial it is **5m06s**. and
more importantly the floor does not yet bind — the wall obeys
`max(longest_file, aggregate / parallelism)`, and at 4.36x that is
`max(306s, 591s)` = **591s**. the aggregate term dominates. the longest file becomes the
floor only once the aggregate falls below ~1330s.

⇒ the stone's conclusion ("split the long poles") is premature, not merely mis-numbered.
**split is lever two, after the aggregate falls.**
