# case=4 — a first take meets its counter

feel: happy · care: critipath · source: #834 case `a-fix-text-for-another-seat`

## .narrative

a push fails. a credential helper prints three fixes for a server-tier token. the seat's first take:
relay those fixes to the human. the min of `rule.always.question-your-first-take` fires: what is the
strongest reason this is wrong? counter-take: this box is a laptop, which pushes over ssh and never
holds that token. the read that could break the take: the error line. it shows an https remote. the
take dies; the seat reports the real cause (remote url) and its one-line fix.

## .bdd sketch

```
given a seat enrolled with skeptic, and a tool fix-text in view
  when the seat forms a take to relay the fix-text
    then [t0] it names the counter-take in one line
    then [t1] it runs the one read that could break the take (the seat, env, path the tool assumed)
  when the read refutes the take
    then [t2] the take shipped is the read's, not the tool's
  when the human corrects a take
    then [t3] the next take names why the prior passed and runs the read that would have caught it
```

note: no mechanism holds [t0]–[t3]; the brief is the guard, graded in review. the cue rests on two
foundations the seat boots beside it: `trust-no-one-especially-not-yourself` (name two ways the first
thought fails, read the likeliest) and `trust-but-verify`'s row for a tool verdict (its case
`the-fix-command-a-refusal-printed`). #834's rule adds the stop condition: the take does not leave
until both halves hold.
