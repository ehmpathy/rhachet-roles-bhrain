# S25 · `.agent/.notes/` is the overflow valve — chronicle the path there, ref to it

## .said

verbatim, 2026-09-04, mid-drive at `1.vision`:

> yes. do all. hey, also, should we teach the telepath to leave its chain of thoughts in the .agent/.notes/ dir? i.e., rather than tell the human or write in comments or briefs, they can chronical their path of thought in .agent/.notes and just ref to it. that may help give the overflow that the brains instinctively seem to desire - and keep it out of the dense comms that we require from them

## .settled

**a chain of thought gets a HOME rather than a ban.** it is chronicled in `.agent/.notes/`, and the
message refs it by path.

### the fundamental — a forbid with no destination is a DAM

every rule in the canon so far treats the urge to narrate as a **defect to suppress**. the wisher's
read is sharper: **the urge is real, and suppression has a price.**

| the forbid alone | the forbid plus a destination |
|---|---|
| the author fights the urge on every line — costly, and it fails under load | the urge is **routed**, and it costs one line |
| ⇒ or it leaks into the comms, which is the defect | ⇒ the comms stay dense **without a fight** |

⇒ **a dam holds until it does not. a channel holds always.**

### it is a RELOCATION, never an addition

the note replaces prose that would otherwise have sat in the message. so the tokens were already
spent — what changes is **who pays**:

| | who pays for the chronicle |
|---|---|
| in the comms | **every reader, every read** |
| in `.notes/`, ref'd | the one reader who fetches it |

🟡 that is what parts this from an ordinary *"write more docs"* ask, and it is why it does not fall
to `philosophy.pavement-saves-nature`'s *"an unwalked path is the worst case"* — an unread note costs
its readers naught, where the same prose in a message costs them all.

### the gap it fills is NARROW, and the extant homes are named

| the reader | the home |
|---|---|
| me, later this session | `.scratch/` — extant, ephemeral |
| **a human who may audit this message now** | **`.agent/.notes/`** — the gap |
| a future traveler on this **concept** | a **brief** — that is what a brief is |
| a future traveler on this **route** | `$route/archive/` · `$route/appendix/` — extant |

⇒ **the last row is the closest pavement**, from `rule.always.yield-the-output-not-the-archaeology`
(driver). what `.notes/` adds is **repo scope**: a chat reply has no route, so it has no `archive/`.

### it is the DESTINATION half of three extant forbids

`forbid.narration` · `forbid.chronological-accretion` · `forbid.diffusion` each ban a shape and name
no home for what they displace.

⇒ so this is one shared peer rather than a clause repeated in three rules
(`rule.prefer.decompose-a-subject-via-suffixes`).

## 🟡 .the open question the ask does not settle — is a note TRACKED?

**a ref must dereference for its reader, or it is a phantom path.**

| | the ref works for | the cost |
|---|---|---|
| **tracked** | every reader, forever | diff noise |
| **gitignored** (like `.agent/.cache/`) | **only the author, on that machine** | the ref is a phantom for all others |

⇒ the recommendation is **tracked, never booted, prune-able** — the whole value is a ref that
dereferences, and diff noise in a dir no reviewer reads is cheap.

🟡 **this is a fulcrum, not a settlement.** the wisher's call.
