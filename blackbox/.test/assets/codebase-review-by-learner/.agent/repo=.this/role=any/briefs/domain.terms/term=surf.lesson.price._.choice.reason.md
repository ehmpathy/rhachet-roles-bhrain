# domain.term.choice.reason: price

## .etymology
`price` is what the surf school quotes a surfer for a lesson. chosen over `cost` (what the school
pays, the other side of the trade), `fee` (an add-on charge), and `rate` (a per-unit figure, not
the amount owed).

## .disputes
none.

## .evidence
- discovery: the surf school's quote — "a lesson's price is set by its minutes"
