## 🔴 .the wisher never mentioned a reason field. I invented one

`0.wish.md` names two signals and their effects. it says naught about a rationale, a justification,
or a `--why`. the contract this vision proposes reads:


⚠️ **that snippet is the call AS FILED, and `S05` superseded its third part** — `--why` now takes a
**path**, so the `@stdin` shape is retired. it is kept verbatim because **an entry records the call
that was made**, and the verdict below is legible only against it. ⇒ the live contract is at
§*and the SHAPE changed the same day*.

⇒ **the flag, its requiredness, and its stdin shape are all inferences**, and until r4 not one of the
three was itemized. `rule.always.itemize-the-fulcrums-you-best-guess` fires on *"a premise you did

⚠️ **it hid because it looked derived.** `--why` reads as an obvious companion to a judgment, and the
yield discusses its weakness at length in `.what is awkward` § 2 — so the *flaw* was on the page
while the *choice* was not. **a decision discussed is not a decision recorded**, and only the record
gives a council a call it can overrule.

## 🔴 .the verdict — A holds, and the ENTRY's whole case against it was aimed at the wrong party

> *"yeah unread is fine. we just want them to justify"*
> *"its an opportunity to nudge them to reconsider"* — the wisher, 2026-09-09

**A stands: required, ungated.** and the reason is not that C's cost was judged too high — it is that
**C's target was never the point.**

### what this entry got wrong

every argument above prices `--why` by **who reads it**: the fulcrum needs a body · a council needs
evidence · 3 of 3 reversals turned on a stated reason. ⇒ so an ungated field reads as the design's
central hole, since no reader can trust prose no gate checked.

🔴 **the field's beneficiary is the AUTHOR, not the reader.**

| the beneficiary | what the field buys | can a gate improve it? |
|---|---|---|
| a **reader** | evidence to judge by | 🔴 no — substantiveness is a judgment |
| 🔴 the **author**, at the moment of the stance | a forced articulation, and thus a chance to find the call is wrong | ✅ **the authorship IS the check** |

⇒ **a gate cannot improve a mechanism whose whole action completes before any reader arrives.** so
ungated is not a compromise struck against cost — it is the correct shape.

### why the articulation is a real mechanism

the same claim `rule.require.brevity` makes of prose — *"brevity proves grasp … cannot say it
briefly? you do not yet grasp it"* — applied to a judgment. a driver who cannot state why they
disagree has not established that they do, and the draft runs **before** the stance lands, so a
driver who fails it can still concede.

⚠️ **the field's yield is the reconsiderations it causes, and those leave no artifact** — a dispute
abandoned mid-draft writes no `--why`. ⇒ **an audit of every `--why` on disk measures only the cases
where the nudge did not work.** unfalsifiable by its own output, and that is not a defect.

### what the measured evidence becomes

**not overturned — re-scoped.** *"3 of 3 reversals turned on a stated reason"* is evidence about a
reason a **later round read**. this verdict is about a reason the **author drafted**. two mechanisms;
only the first needs a reader, and F04's fork C restores that one anyway — a disputed lane returns
each generation, so a later reviewer does read the `.taken` that accompanies the stance.

### 🔴 and the SHAPE changed the same day — `--why` takes a PATH

> *"but the `--as disputed --why $path` shoould reference fulcrums"* — the wisher, 2026-09-09

**`--why` is not prose. it is a path to a fulcrum entry**, required, and it must resolve

⇒ that **strengthens** this verdict rather than complicates it. the nudge's whole force is the

| the field | what an author must produce |
|---|---|
| prose at a terminal | one sentence |
| 🔴 a **fulcrum entry** | `.the fork, stated fairly` · `.taken, and why` · `.rework, and why` · `.what would settle it` |

⇒ **you cannot fill that in without you state the counter-argument.** far harder to fake past
yourself than a sentence.

🟡 **and the requiredness half is answered by the same shape.** *"is `--why` owed on every
re-declaration, or only the first?"* — **every one, and it costs naught**: if the argument still
holds the driver passes the same path, which the wisher named as the reuse.
