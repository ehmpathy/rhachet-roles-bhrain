# razor: segal

shaves: a number or a sample · settled by: observation (a second read from an independent source)

## .the question

> **is there a second, independent measure that could disagree with this one?**

one source cannot show its own error. two that disagree show that one is wrong; two that share a root count as one.

## .the source

- *San Diego Union*, 20 Sep 1930: "a man with one watch knows what time it is, and a man with two watches could never be sure"
- misattributed to Lee Segall; misquoted by Bloch as "Segal's Law"
- read via https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Segal%27s_law

## .fails when

- the two sources share a root; then they are one
- more sources without a way to weigh them confuse rather than settle

## .run by

`rule.always.trust-but-verify`
