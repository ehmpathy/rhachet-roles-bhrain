# domain.term.choice.reason: attributed

## .etymology

the past participle of its family's noun: a prompt the census **attributed** to its inputs. the
family word and its position share a stem, so a reader of either finds the other.

rejected:

| rejected | why |
|---|---|
| `whole` / `complete` | the rows ALWAYS sum to the prompt — `markup` is the residual, so they do by algebra. "complete" would be true of every census and so names no position |
| `clean` | implies a defect check passed. the grade claims less: only that the residual is too small to hide an input |

## .the boundary

"attributed, of WHAT?" — of a prompt's chars, to its named inputs, by the census.
`review.grade.attribution` answers it, beside the cache family's `review.grade.cache.*`.

## .disputes

none.
