## .settled

**every term carries its boundary in its filename — always, and as the full ancestry path from the
root.** not on collision, and not one segment.

the rule as written had split the demand: *answer the boundary in the header always; carry it in
the filename only where a word collides.* it had named the wisher's own example,
`term=route.guard.rung`, as **wrong** — on the grounds that a boundary answers *"which one?"*
rather than the whole taxonomy above it.

⇒ the wisher's form is the correct one. three properties the split form gave up:

| property | what the split form cost |
|---|---|
| **a computable path** | under a conditional rule the path depends on whether a word collides *elsewhere*, so every lookup becomes a search — for all 27 terms, not merely the ones that collide |
| **prefix slices** | one segment gives a reader the leaf's parent and no slice above it. `term=route.*` needs the full chain |
| **a checkable gap class** | a full path demands every boundary name itself reach a root, which surfaces boundaries this glossary owes and had never named |

⚠️ **`repo` is dropped from the path** — it is the universal ancestor of every term here, so it
distinguishes none of them.
