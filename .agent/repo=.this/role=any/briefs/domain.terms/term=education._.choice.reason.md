# domain.term.reason: education

## .etymology

latin `educere`, to lead out: a student is led out of what they know toward what others found.
the real-world territory is a student, a course, a canon, a lab, and a credential; each maps to
one piece of the suite.

rejected:
- `study` — a forbidden synonym of `learn` in this repo
- `drill` — names repetition toward a fixed behavior, not the acquisition of a canon
- `school` — names the institution, not the act
- `enroll` — overloaded: `rhx enroll` boots a brain with roles
- `lesson` — overloaded: a document kind (`kno201.documents.lessons`)

## .evidence

- five whys on "send a role to study": the act that survives is to acquire the greats' lessons
  on purpose, test them, keep them. `learn` already holds the role's own lessons, so the new act
  earns its own word
- dimensional walk (subject breadth × bound × source): one bounded subject from an external
  canon is a `course`; an open subject from the canon is `research`; an open subject from one's
  own experience is `learn`. `education` names the bounded, canon-sourced half
- full record: `.behavior/v2026_11_23.feat-learner-education-suite/1.vision.domain-discovery.md`

## .disputes

### dispute: learn — raised 2026-11-23 — status: RESOLVED (canonical holds)

- claim      = the learner role already `learn`s; a second word is a synonym
- counter    = `learn` retains one's own lessons; education acquires others'. two sources, two acts
