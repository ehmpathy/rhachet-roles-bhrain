# rule.always.skepticize-the-layer-of-a-cure.example=the-drip-and-the-main

## .what happened

a tap in an upstairs bathroom dripped. a handyman swapped its washer; a week later it dripped again. he swapped the cartridge; two weeks later, again. then the kitchen tap began to drip, and a pipe joint in the basement wept.

a plumber read the water pressure at the main: twice what the house's pipes were rated for. the regulator on the main had failed. every tap was the symptom; the main was the cause. she replaced the regulator, and every drip stopped.

## .what the first cures did

- each sat at the layer where the symptom showed: the tap
- each worked for a while, which read as proof the layer was right
- the cause sat at a layer no one read, owned by a different trade

## .the lesson

where a symptom shows is a claim about where its cause starts. read the cause's layer, and hand the cure to whoever owns that layer.

## .the question that would have caught it

**where does the cause start, by what read, and who owns that layer?** — one gauge on the main, read before the first washer, would have answered it.
