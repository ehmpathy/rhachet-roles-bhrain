- soft: the ask runs detached; the flag lands as a `rhx clone say` message on the next turn
- hard: the hook awaits the ask and holds the stop until the seat answers the flag
- the daemon stays disabled by default; once enabled, `hard` is the default nudge
- a `hard` entry runs soft until its replay clears the gate, since a held stop is a block
