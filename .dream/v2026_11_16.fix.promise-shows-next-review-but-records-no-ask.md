# 🌙 dream: a promise that shows the next self review should record its ask

kind: fix · owner: bhrain/driver (`route.stone.set --as promised`) · caught 2026-11-16

## .the cue that fired

twice in one stone (`1.vision`, route `v2026_11_16.feat-disputes-reconcile-at-human-gate`): after a
successful `--as promised`, the output rendered `review.self N+1/5` with its guide and its path. I
wrote the review to that path and promised. refused: `🍃 no ask on record`. I had to run
`--as arrived` to record the ask, which then made my file "predate the ask" (`🍂`) and forced a
rewrite of a review written in good faith.

## .the gap, precisely

the promise path renders the next review's ask but does not persist it. only `--as arrived` /
`--as passed` persist an ask. so the one render a driver reads at that moment is an ask the guard
does not know it made.

## .the shape of the fix

either (a) the promise path records the ask for the review it renders, at the moment it renders it,
or (b) it renders no next review, and says "run `--as arrived` for the next ask". (a) is the pit of
success. clamp: promise N, write N+1 at the rendered path, promise N+1 → accepted.

## .why it is not done in this round

this route's scope is disputes at the human gate; the self-review ask ledger is a different
subsystem, and a fix there ripples through the self-review snapshots (fails CLEAN).
