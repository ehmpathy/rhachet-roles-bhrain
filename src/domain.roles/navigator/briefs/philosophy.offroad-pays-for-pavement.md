# philosophy: offroad pays for pavement 🧭

> **the exploration gets paid for either way. a route is what turns that one-time cost
> into a permanent asset.**

## .the asymmetry

| | cost | paid by |
|---|---|---|
| **pave it** | once | the first traveler |
| **improvise it** | every time | every traveler, forever |

the ratio improves **without bound** the more a path is walked. no other move in the
practice has that shape.

⇒ so the navigator's whole job sits on one claim: **you would pay the exploration cost
regardless.** the route is not extra work. it is the receipt for work already done.

## .why a role, and not merely a habit

the deposit rules already exist — a driver is told to leave a route behind. and yet an
offroad brain, mid-exploration, is the **least** likely party to write one: it is deep
in a problem, the path is not yet clear, and the artifact feels premature.

⇒ the route gets written at the end, from memory, or never. the navigator exists to
hold the outline **while** the ground is still unlearned, which is the only moment the
cues are still recoverable.

## 🔴 .the sanitized route is worth less than no route

a route reconstructed at the end reads clean: six stones, each one obvious, no wrong
turns. it is also a **lie of omission**, and it costs the next traveler in the exact
place they will need help.

| what the live outline keeps | what the reconstruction drops |
|---|---|
| the wrong turn, and what reversed it | (gone — it looks like nobody took it) |
| the cue that made you notice | (gone — the stone reads as foresight) |
| the order that turned out to matter | (gone — re-orderable for tidiness) |
| the guard you could not name at first | (gone — every stone looks well-cut) |

⇒ **a traveler who hits the same wrong turn and finds no record of it concludes they are
uniquely confused.** that is worse than an empty tree, which at least tells the truth.

## .the two failures this role guards

**the braid** — a second path laid beside a serviceable one, because nobody looked
first. the navigator's first move is a search for extant pavement, never a fresh cut.

**the rut** — conformity to a found path with no test that it leads anywhere. precedent
is a reason to look, never a reason to conform. a route inherited and never questioned
is how `maxWorkers: 1` survives four years in a config nobody diagnosed.

🟡 the first is a gap of attention; the second a gap of **skepticism**. no amount of
extra care catches the second — only a test does.

## .the limit

**not every task earns a route.** a one-step fix does not. the trigger is *judgment at
several points, in an order that matters* — that is precisely the shape a route carries
and a linear skill cannot.

⇒ **do not route what could be entooled.** a route laid over deterministic work charges
imagine cost at every stone and buys no judgment, because none was needed.

> pave it once, so it is never paid for twice.
> then **walk it** — an unwalked path is the only wasted one. 🧭
