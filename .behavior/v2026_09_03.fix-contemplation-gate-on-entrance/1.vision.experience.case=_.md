## 🔴 .the invariant the forbidden cells encode

> **no entry to a review round while a blocker stands unanswered and unforgiven.**

⚠️ **the phrasing was tightened at 5.3; the mechanism never moved.** it read *"an unanswered blocker
that no overrule forgives"* — which named the human valve as the only forgiveness, and so implied a
blocker some reviewer deliberately raised. two finds falsified both halves: a `retired` reviewer is
unforgivable **because it has no level to overrule** (F8), and an **unreadable** verdict gates with
no blocker raised at all (`verdict-legible?`, dimensions §1b).

⇒ the predicate in code was always `blockers > 0`, and both cases satisfy it. **the prose was
narrower than the mechanism it described** — the same defect class r7 caught in the render copy,
found here in a contract instead.

all six sit in `enter-review`. that row **is** the invariant's whole surface:

| cell | why refused | demoed? |
|---|---|---|

| `carried-unanswered × live` | carried across an edit — the hatch this behavior closes | ✅ case=1 |
| `carried-unanswered × exhausted` | exhaustion does **not** discharge (F1, kept) | ✅ case=5 |

| 🔴 `fresh-unanswered × retired` | raised, then retired before the next entry — same halt | ✅ case=11 |

✅ **provably complete**: forbidden iff **`enter-review`** `∧ unanswered ∧ ¬forgiven`. **A is a
conjunct, never a coincidence** — drop it and the predicate selects `answer × fresh × live` (demoed,
