## .the gap, precisely

`.agent/repo=.this/role=any/briefs/define.invariant.review.peer.exhausted.md` carries six sections:
`.what` · `.invariant` · `.why` · `.detection` · `.consequence` · `.enforcement`.

measured against the shape that brief prescribes, it omits **five**, and they are the five a
re-litigation needs:

| owed | present | why its absence costs |
|---|---|---|
| `.kind` — nature or nurture | ❌ | with no label, it gets argued as if negotiable on taste. it is **nurture** — a deliberate choice about what a verdict word means |
| `.scope` — what it does NOT cover | ❌ | 🔴 **the most expensive omission.** the invariant governs what a verdict READS. #420 also complains the driver is left at a hard human-only block — an ergonomics matter the invariant is silent on. with no `.scope`, the invariant reads as though it settles both, and #420's second half gets closed by an argument that never addressed it |
| `.the counter-argument`, stated fairly | ❌ | #420 and #422 each had to construct it from scratch |
| `.what would overturn it` | ❌ | so a disputer cannot tell what evidence would count |
| `.the litigation` — who argued what, verbatim | ❌ | the argument that produced it is unrecoverable |

## .the shape of the fix

1. **label it `nurture`** — a choice about verdict semantics, not a fact about the domain
2. **add `.scope`** — it governs *what a verdict reads*. it does **not** govern the halt message,
   the budget-lever guidance, or whether a driver is left with a next move
3. **add the counter-argument fairly**, in #420's and #422's own words:
   > *"the arrive that spends the last budget still shows the lenses as `rejected` and the next
   > level as `awaits l1 terminal`. the NEXT arrive — free, every lens cached — flips them."*
   >
   > *"the moment the L1 reviewers `exhausted`, the ladder should have instantly kicked off the L3."*
4. **add what would overturn it** — per the nurture row: a demonstration that a level opened on
   the round that spends its last budget causes no harm, i.e. that fresh unaddressed blockers on
   the cheap level never invalidate an expensive level's work
5. **add the litigation record** — cite #420 and #422 as dated disputes, with the resolution
6. **cite the clamp** — `blackbox/driver.route.peer-budget-exhaustion-unlocks-level.acceptance.test.ts`,
   whose docblock already restates the invariant. a brief that names its own clamp is checkable

⇒ ⚠️ and route #420's **second** complaint separately. it is a real ergonomics issue about the
halt render and it is **not** answered by this invariant.

## .why it is not done in this round

**safe** — yes. it is a brief edit; no code, no test, no contract moves.

**clean** — 🔴 **no.** the repair must state a counter-argument fairly and declare what would
overturn the invariant. that is a **litigation record**, and per the learner's own rule the
argument is worth more than the conclusion — so it must be drawn from the disputers' actual words
rather than paraphrased by the round that decided against them. to author it here would be one
party who records both sides of a dispute it has an interest in.

**and the scope bound**: this round's stone is `1.vision`, whose artifacts glob is
`$route/1.vision*.md`. a vision stone emits no briefs.

⇒ the fulcrum that decided to preserve the invariant is
`.fulcrums/inventory.of=fulcrums.case=F6-preserve-the-unlock-moment.md`, at 93%. **this dream is
what that fulcrum owes back** — to preserve a rule and leave the reason for it unwritten is how it
comes to be re-litigated a third time.
