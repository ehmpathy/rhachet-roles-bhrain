looseness is visible rather than silent.

## .why it fails safe

**fast** — the predicate is a ledger read, already implemented, already tested · **loud** — the grant
prints its warrant and repeats the human warn, so a run of urgent grants is countable in the PR
rather than invisible · **bounded by a written claim** — an `urgent` grade with no nameable harm is
the mis-grade `rule.forbid.overzealous-blockers` and
`rule.require.grade-a-concession-by-its-harm` already forbid, and a council reads it.

🔴 **the restraint is social and countable, never mechanical — and the invariant says so.**
`define.invariant…urgent-earns-budget` §the counter-argument states the abuse plainly: *"a driver
could grade every concession `urgent` to buy rounds."* ⇒ this gate does not close that; it makes each
use **cost a written harm claim and a human's warn**, which is strictly more restraint than today's
free lever.
