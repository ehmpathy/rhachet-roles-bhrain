## .settled

- a check that flags honest replies as often as stale ones is noise; a reader learns to skip it, so it catches naught, as a hard block or as a notice
- `skeptic.claims` and `skeptic.answer.check` grade words, never whether the thought was checked; both leave the role
- a claim's staleness is a property of its source, never of the turn it was read in; a cited evidence file is a read
- every skeptic hook that grades words leaves too: `razor.stop.check`, `razor.hold.check`, `skeptic.notice`, `razor.brief.guard`. the role ships as persona and briefs; the doubt lives in the brain
- a future Stop check must flag a contradiction with recorded facts (exit codes, call order), never a word pattern, and ships as a notice until a labeled corpus shows ~9 in 10 of its flags are true
