# razor: hitchens

shaves: a claim with no owner or weight · settled by: observation (of what was offered)

## .the question

> **what evidence came with this claim?**

none → the claim earns no credence. dismissed is not refuted: it states no counter-claim.

## .the source

- Hitchens, *God Is Not Great*, 2007, pp. 150, 258: "What can be asserted without evidence can also be dismissed without evidence"
- Latin root: *quod gratis asseritur, gratis negatur*
- read via https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hitchens%27s_razor

## .fails when

- the claim carries evidence at first sight; the razor then does not apply
- both sides invoke it and talk stops (Rauser)
- it binds your own claim too

## .run by

`rule.always.trust-but-verify`
