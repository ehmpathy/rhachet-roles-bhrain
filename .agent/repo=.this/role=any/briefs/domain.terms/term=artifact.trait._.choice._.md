## .what
a `trait` is an artifact that confers a **default a reader falls to with no decision**.

three parts, and **no recall** is what parts it from every peer:

- **declared** — a file a role boots, never tacit temperament
- **general** — it colors every passage rather than one act
- **no recall** — one you must fetch has already lost the round

## .the three artifact kinds

`en$X` produces a `$X`, and the family names three artifact kinds. each confers a different form of
prethought:

| the artifact | confers | the reader gains |
|---|---|---|
| 📚 a brief | prethought concepts | a thought to reason with |
| 💪 a skill | prethought capacity | a step they need not do |
| 🧬 a trait | a prethought default | a choice they need not make |

⇒ a brief and a skill are **consulted**; a trait is not. that is the whole boundary, and it is
checkable: a brief you must open, a skill you must invoke, a trait you already obeyed.

## .why it is not a rung on the entoolment ladder

the ladder ranks by imagine cost per use, and a trait's cost is zero — which reads as rung 5 and is
not. the ladder and entraitment move the work in opposite directions:

| the act | where the work goes |
|---|---|
| **entoolment** | OUT of the brain, into an artifact |
| **entraitment** | INTO the brain, below decision |

⇒ so they are **orthogonal axes, never one scale**. `trait.bulletize-reflexively` carries the same
content as `rule.require.bulletize` and retires the recall rather than the act.
