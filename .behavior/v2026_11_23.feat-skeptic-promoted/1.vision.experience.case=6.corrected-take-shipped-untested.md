# case=6 — a corrected take ships untested

cell: take × sharp × +subconscious · care: critipath · source: #834 case `a-correction-shipped-untested`

## .narrative

the human corrects a seat's diagnosis. the seat, eager, ends its next reply with a new culprit: *"the cause is the retry wrapper."* no read stands behind it.

the `question-your-first-take` cue should have caught it first: a corrected take owes the read that would have caught the prior one. the seat skipped it.

the instinct catches what the cue missed. it reads the reply and flags one claim: a cause, stated as fact, with no basis and no record. the owed basis, per `trust-but-verify`'s row for a cause: the code or brief that would refute it.

the flag reaches the seat at the start of its next turn, before the human replies. the seat reads the briefs on the retry wrapper. it learns the wrapper is deliberate and harmless where it sits, and drops the culprit. its next reply names the read and the cause the read supports, and cites the record it wrote. the human never acts on the bad culprit.

## .the cue and the instinct, each at its grain

- the cue fires at the take, inside the seat; only the seat can heed it
- the instinct fires at the reply, outside the seat; it sees only what the seat said
- a seat that heeds the cue meets no flag. the flag is the outside signal for the seat that did not
