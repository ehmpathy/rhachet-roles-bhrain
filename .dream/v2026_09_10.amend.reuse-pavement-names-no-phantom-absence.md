## .the gap, precisely

the rule names three anti-patterns — the braided trail, the rut, the phantom path. the fourth is the
phantom path's inverse:

| | the claim | how it surfaces |
|---|---|---|
| phantom path | *"X exists"*, and it does not | a reader globs and finds naught — loud |
| 🔴 phantom absence | *"X does not exist"*, and it does | never — nobody dereferences a road not taken |

a second shape: **a grade of the genus applied to the instance.** *"transcript parses are brittle"*
is a true generalization and a false verdict on a contracted surface.

## .the shape of the fix

add to `rule.always.reuse-pavement-before-improvise`:

```md
| when… | then… |
|---|---|
| you would grade an option unavailable, brittle, or not shipped | 🔴 a `--help` is cheaper than the paragraph you are about to write |
| you would grade a whole CLASS of approach rather than the instrument in front of you | that is a grade of the genus. check the instance |
| a design record lists candidates | 🔴 each row is a claim about what EXISTS. it needs the same check a citation does |

blocker: an option graded unavailable with no look at the tool · a class grade offered as a verdict
on a specific shipped surface.
```

and in `.the anti-patterns`:

```md
- **the phantom absence** — an option ruled out as absent, with no look. the phantom path's inverse,
  and the more expensive one: it fails silently, because nobody dereferences a road not taken
```
