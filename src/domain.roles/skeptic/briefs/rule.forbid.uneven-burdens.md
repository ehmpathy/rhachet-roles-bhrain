# rule.forbid.uneven-burdens

> **no claim is held to a lighter burden because it is the one you favor. no rival is refuted in a weaker form than its holder states.**

twin of `rule.always.weigh-each-claim-with-one-burden`. razor: `razor=rapoport`.

## .the shape

| 👎 | 👍 |
|---|---|
| your fix accepted on one green run; the peer's alternative asked for three | both held to three runs |
| "fixed" relayed at once; "regressed" re-run twice before it is believed | each run the same number of times |
| the rival refuted as "they just want to skip tests" | the rival stated as its holder would: "a slow suite costs more than the rare bug it catches" — then weighed |

## .the test

> **would this answer have moved me as far if it pointed the other way?**

## .enforcement

blocker: a favored claim accepted on less proof than its rival was asked for, and a peer acted on it
nitpick: a rival refuted in a form its holder would not recognize
false positive: two claims with different stakes, each sized to its own harm (stated)
