# catalog.of=glyph.axis=phase

## .what

the glyphs that tip a route status line when the route **self-drives** — they say where the work
is, and that no human is owed anything.

declared in `src/domain.operations/route/statusLine/asStatusLine.ts`, `asCalmGlyph`.

## .the members

| glyph | phase | names |
|---|---|---|
| 🌾 | `yield` | the yield grows — the stone produces its artifact |
| 🔍 | `review.self` · `review.peer` · `judge` | the machine's turn — an artifact under examination |

```
🗿 1.vision, yield 🌾
🗿 1.vision, review.self, r7/r10 🔍
🗿 5.3.verification, judge 🔍
```

## ⚠️ .the axis is CALM by construction

a phase glyph appears only on a `push` disposition. the moment the route halts, the phase glyph is
replaced by a **halt** glyph plus the word that names why (`catalog.of=glyph.axis=halt.md`).

⇒ so the two axes are **mutually exclusive on one line**, which is what lets a reader triage by
glyph alone: 🌾 or 🔍 means read no further; any other glyph means you are needed.

## .why 🔍 is also the reviewer's marker

it is the same concept. the review phase IS the reviewer at labor, so the glyph that marks the
phase is the glyph that marks the role — not a second claimant
(`catalog.of=glyph._.md`, *.the two-axis entries are NOT collisions*).
