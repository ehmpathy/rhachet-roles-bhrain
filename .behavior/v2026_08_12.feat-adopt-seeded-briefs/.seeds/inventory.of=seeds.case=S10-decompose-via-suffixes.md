## .settled

1. **a sub-claim that stands on its own is ejected into a suffixed peer**, never accreted into its
   root — `$subject._.md` + `$subject.$zoom.md`
2. **the suffix names the zoom**, in `via-$x` form where the zoom is a lens onto the root
   (`via-routes`, not `for.routes`)
3. **the reason is boot economics** — an inline zoom-in is costed on every boot; a suffixed peer
   at `ref` is costed only when dereferenced
4. **this belongs to the librarian**, as a `prefer` rule beside the cluster-root rule
