# case=5 — the driver learns the grant owner from its briefs

feel: happy · care: critipath

actor: the driver, at boot, before any halt

## .narrative

a fresh driver boots on a route. before it meets one halt, its briefs tell it how a review budget works: a `better` concession earns no round; an `urgent` one earns more, and the top-up is its own lever, run once the lane is dry, with a note to its human.

an hour later the judge halts on an urgent concession and prints `increase budget — yours to spend`. the halt holds no surprise. it says what the briefs already said, so the driver runs the command, then writes its human a line.

on another route a full `.md` brief, one the boot does not load, still says "a budget hit needs a human". a driver that reads it on demand meets two owners for one lever. it either asks its human for a grant it holds itself, or it trusts the halt and doubts the brief. either way the briefs cost it a round trip or its trust.

## .bdd sketch

```
given the driver and reviewer briefs, as shipped
when [t0] the driver boots
  then each booted brief that names the budget lever names the driver as its owner
  then the urgent lever carries the note to the human
when [t1] the driver reads a full brief on demand
  then no brief claims a human grants the budget
when [t2] the urgent judge halt renders
  then its owner line agrees with the briefs
```
