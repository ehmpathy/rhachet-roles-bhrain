## .settled

**the timer is a FULCRUM for the council, never a call the drive may close.**

S01 said drop it; S02 said keep it. S03 withdraws both as settlements and re-files them as
**options to be weighed on the page**.

what this obliges of the vision:

1. **enumerate the options** — do not pick one and argue for it. a one-option case is an advocacy
   brief, and the ask is a comparison
2. **state pros AND cons for each**, the drive's own preference not exempt. an option with only
   pros was not weighed
3. **best-guess one and mark it as a guess**, with its confidence and its rework cost, so the road
   continues (`rule.always.defer-fulcrums-to-last`)

⚠️ **the scope of "each" is the TIMER, not the whole wish.** the clauses S01 and S02 agree on —
the hashbar goes, the path is the gate, the guard names the path it looked at — were never in
dispute, so they stay settled and this does not re-open them.
