# rule.require.elucidation

> **elucidate, in both directions. GIVE the concept a peer lacks; ASK for the concept you lack.**

**elucidation is the root purpose of the telepath**, and every other rule in this role is one surface
of it. this rule states the root so a reader who holds it can derive the rest.

⇒ the etymology, the asymmetry, the demos, and the boundary: `define.elucidation` (ref).

## .the two directions

| direction | the act | the artifact |
|---|---|---|
| **into** a peer | give a concept | an answer |
| **from** a peer | gain a concept | a question |

**a question is an act of elucidation, not an admission that you failed to perform one.** that is
the half authors drop, and the drop is silent: a question reads as a gap in the answer, so it gets
swallowed and a guess ships in its place.

## .the test — and it grades the PEER, never the passage

> **can the peer USE the concept now?**

yes → elucidated · no, and **you** hold what they lack → give it · no, and **they** hold what you lack
→ **ask** · they can *assent* but not *use* → that is persuasion, and it is a different act.

**clear is not the bar.** a passage can be brief, bulletized, and correct, and land on a peer who
still cannot act on it. **the light must reach where the peer stands.**

## .a question is PROSE, so every rule in this role binds it

- a **rambled** question is a subversive question
- an **unbulletized** question is an unbulletized passage
- a question with no established purpose fails `rule.require.purpose-first`, exactly as an answer does

⇒ **no second rule set is owed for the input direction.** the extant set binds both.

**and a bad question costs more than a bad answer.** a bad answer is re-read and may be caught; a
bad question makes the peer answer the **wrong** question at their full cost, and neither party can
see that it happened.

| when… | then… |
|---|---|
| you would guess rather than ask | the strongest cue. name the guess, or ask the one question it turns on |
| the guess would ship tersely | worse — terseness strips the reasons a peer would use to catch it |
| a wrong read is a clean rework | do not ask. **act, and surface the read in one line** |
| a wrong read is a dirty rework | that is a fulcrum. ask — last, and named |
| you cite a line a peer has not read | give the concept **first**, the citation second |
| a peer can assent but not act | you persuaded. state the mechanism instead |
| you would answer by a full dump | completeness is not elucidation. `reflexive-condensation` |

## .the axis

a **structure** rule, so telepath owns it (`rule.require.generic-governs-structure-never-voice`). it
prescribes what a passage must **accomplish**, so it prescribes no member of that rule's voice row.

blocker: a guess shipped where the answer turned on one askable question · a question a peer must
guess the subject of · an answer that cites where it must first give · a passage that aims at assent
rather than at use.
nitpick: a question that would have been better as a stated read plus a clean rework.
false positive: a stated read acted on rather than asked · a concept named at a depth the peer
already holds.
