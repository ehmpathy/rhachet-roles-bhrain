# 🌙 dream: a passage record cannot attribute a brain to a stone

- **kind** = amend
- **owner** = `ehmpathy/rhachet-roles-bhrain` (this repo)
- **caught** = 2026-09-09

## .the cue that fired

the vision for a prescribed brain per stone makes switch **visibility** a critipath: a prescription
nobody can verify is a preference, not a control.

the vision bounds that to **one output line** on stone entry (fulcrum `F9`), because
`rule.require.status-feedback` demands exactly that and neither the wish nor the issue asks for more.

⚠️ **the counter-argument was strong enough to record rather than discard.**

## .the gap, precisely

a line lives in a transcript. **a transcript is not queryable.**

the foreman's real question — *"did 5.3.verification actually run on opus?"* — is asked **later**:
after a defect ships, possibly after the session is gone, plausibly by someone who was never there.

- a line answers a reader who was present
- it does not answer one who was not

⇒ and the route **already keeps a per-stone append-only record**: `passage.jsonl`. the brain a stone
ran under is arguably a property of that passage. so the record is less invented than it first looks —
which is what makes the deferral a real judgment rather than an obvious scope cut.

## .the shape of the fix

on a switch (or a deliberate no-op), append the brain to the stone's passage entry — the brain, and
its provenance (prescribed by this stone's guard, versus inherited from an earlier one).

⚠️ **`rule.forbid.timestamps-in-route-artifacts` binds it**: a date is fine, a time is not.

⇒ the whole cost is small, and it is entirely additive: no change to the output line, the guard
schema, or the hook.

## .why it is not done in this round

**scope.** neither the wish nor the seed issue asks for a durable record, and
`rule.forbid.scope-leaks` is a live rubric on this route. `rule.prefer.wet-over-dry` agrees: one
usecase today; wait for the second.

⇒ the deferral is a **judgment about scope**, so it carries a fulcrum as well as this dream —
`F9`, `rework = clean`.
