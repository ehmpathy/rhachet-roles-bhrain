# enbrief → a provenance clause for `rule.always.archive-the-wishers-words-verbatim`

**status:** caught here, **not written into the rule**. the bound wish is briefs-only and this IS a
brief edit — but it belongs to the driver rule this round already touched, and it wants a worked
example the round has now produced. drafted here so the next round writes it once, with evidence.

**owner:** this repo — `src/domain.roles/driver/briefs/rule.always.archive-the-wishers-words-verbatim.md`.

🟡 **this file was first named `reseed.*`, and that was wrong.** a `reseed` goes to the repo that
owns the ask; this ask is owned **here**. it is an `enbrief` — a brief to write, deferred by the
wish's scope, never relocated. see `.the dream verbs`, below.

---

## .the gap

the rule says a `.said` block must be **verbatim** — unedited, typos and all. it says naught about
where the words come from, so it reads as a bar on a **tidied** quote and not as a bar on an
**invented** one.

⇒ **a driver can obey every clause of the rule and still archive a quote the wisher never said.**
that is exactly what happened.

## .the defect, dated

**2026-08-28.** seeds S13 and S14 were each written with a second `.said` block, presented as the
wisher's words. the wisher's reply: *"i never said this"*. both were removed.

the invented text was **not wrong** — it restated the driver's own argument correctly. that is
what made it hard to catch: it read as a well-argued endorsement, because it was the driver's
argument.

## .the clause to add

> **capture from the message, at the moment of speech. never reconstruct, and never archive an
> answer as an ask.**

with the three tells that separate a real capture from an invented one:

| the tell | a genuine wisher utterance | driver prose in a `.said` block |
|---|---|---|
| **form** | typos, lowercase, run-ons, raw paths, codediff pointers | clean, structured, hedged |
| **speech act** | an **ask**, a correction, a coinage, a contract | an **answer** — often an endorsement |
| **frame** | the wisher's own vocabulary | the driver's own vocabulary, verbatim from its last turn |

🟡 **the middle row is the sharp one.** a wisher asks; a driver answers. a `.said` that agrees with
the driver's work, in the driver's words, is the driver.

## 🟡 .the census cannot catch this, and the rule should say so

`inventory.of=seeds._.md` runs a gap check: every `case=` has a file, every file is in the table.
it passed cleanly while two entries carried invented quotes.

⇒ **a coordinate census proves completeness. it proves no word inside any entry is real.** the two
checks are orthogonal, and only one of them exists today. the rule should name the second as a
reviewer's job rather than imply the census covers it.

## .what it must NOT say

**do not cut driver prose from a seed.** the wisher settled that directly
(`.seeds/inventory.of=seeds.case=S15-settled-stays-in-the-seed.md`): the shape stays
`.said` · `.settled` · `.landed`. **the fix is the label, never the volume** — driver prose
belongs under `.settled`, in the driver's name.

## 🟡 .the dream verbs — what the second filename segment means

`.dream/` catches take `v$date.$verb.$slug.md`, and the verb says **what the catch is owed as**:

| verb | the ask is | owner |
|---|---|---|
| **enbrief** | a brief to write | **this repo** |
| **entool** | a skill or tool to build | **this repo** |
| **reseed** | a seed to send to the tree that owns the path | **a peer repo** |

⇒ the first two are the `en-` family's climbs (`term=enbrief`, `term=entool`); the third is not a
climb at all — it is a **relocation**, and it exists because *a tree adopts only what is scoped to
its own repo* (`.seeds/inventory.of=seeds.case=S02-adopt-only-what-is-scoped-here.md`).

🟡 **the verb is decided by the OWNER, never by the topic.** this file's topic is a brief about
seeds, which reads as re-seed-shaped; its owner is this repo, so it is an `enbrief`. that is the
same actor-over-subject test that moved
`rule.always.get-a-second-opinion-before-foreman` from reviewer to driver
(`.seeds/…case=S13-a-rule-belongs-with-the-caller.md`).

🟡 **and `enskill` is a legal fourth verb** that no catch has needed yet — a sequence to pave here.
the set is the `en-` family plus `reseed`, and it is open on the `en-` side.
