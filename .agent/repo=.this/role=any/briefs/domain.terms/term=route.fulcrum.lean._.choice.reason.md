# domain.term.choice.reason: lean

## .etymology

a **lean** is which way a call tilts before it is settled, the honest word for a call held at
under-certain confidence. it pairs with the fulcrum metaphor: the lever leans one way until a
human sets it.

| word | what it loses |
|---|---|
| `recommendation` | it reads as advice to someone with the authority; the driver also acts on it at once |
| `proposal` | it implies the work waits for approval, where a dispute never halts a stone with no human gate |

## .evidence

the vision's gate render (`1.vision.md`, case=2) shows the human one line per dispute: case,
title, confidence, and the call. the field that carries that call needed a word distinct from the
`verdict` a human returns.
