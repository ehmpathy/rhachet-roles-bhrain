# rule.forbid.silent-refutations

> **no claim, order, or request refuted by your answer is worked around in silence. its maker hears the refutation.**

twin of `rule.always.pushback-what-you-refute`.

## .the shape

| 👎 | 👍 |
|---|---|
| told "the bug is in module A", found it in B, fixed B, said naught of A | "the bug is in B, not A (`read:` …); fixed in B" |
| an order to add a retry, the read shows the cause is elsewhere; the retry added anyway | "a retry would mask it; the cause is X. propose: fix X. proceed?" |
| a peer's claim refuted in a private note only | the refutation sent to the peer who made the claim |

## .the test

> **did this answer refute what someone else claimed, and did that someone hear it?**

## .enforcement

blocker: a refutation that changed the work, never told to the claim's maker
nitpick: a pushback against a weaker form of the claim than the maker holds
false positive: a refutation of your own claim (record it; no peer to tell)
