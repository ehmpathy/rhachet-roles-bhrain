# razor: morgan

shaves: a cause among rivals · settled by: rivals

## .the question

> **does a lower-capacity explanation suffice for what this agent did, before I credit a higher one (understood, intended, reasoned)?**

the rivals are rungs of capacity. a seat that says "I understood" or "the clone meant to" claims a high rung; a pattern match may suffice.

## .the source

- C. Lloyd Morgan (`guess:` 1894): "In no case is an animal activity to be interpreted in terms of higher psychological processes if it can be fairly interpreted in terms of processes which stand lower in the scale of psychological evolution and development"
- read via https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morgan%27s_Canon

## .fails when

- it under-credits real capacity. the lower rung must FAIRLY explain the act, not merely be possible
- `razor=hanlon` is one case of this ladder
