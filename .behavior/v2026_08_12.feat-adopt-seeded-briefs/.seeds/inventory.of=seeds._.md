## 🔴 .the standard — a seed is the seed of a CONCEPT, never a chronicle of the round

settled 2026-08-31: *"it should be the ultimate seed of concept; not just historic curiosities
about the route tools themselves."*

`.said` is verbatim and exempt. **every other line states what now HOLDS** — with no reference to
this route, its tools, its fulcrums, or its errors. the trail lives in the yield and in `git log`;
a seed that carries it holds a third copy that will drift from both
(`rule.always.archive-the-wishers-words-verbatim`, `.a seed is the seed of a CONCEPT`).

### 🔴 .and a seed whose subject is the ENVIRONMENT is dropped, never rewritten

settled 2026-08-31: *"can we drop all the 'seeds' that mention radio or other environmental
minutia? those are not relevant to the route at all."*

**the standard above governs a seed's PROSE. this governs whether it is a seed at all.**

| the subject | verdict |
|---|---|
| a **concept** — a word coined, a contract set, a claim corrected | ✅ a seed |
| the **environment** — a channel's switch state, a credential's health, a tool broken that day | 🔴 **not a seed.** drop it |

⇒ an environmental utterance is real and it is a **directive for the session**, not a concept a
future traveler inherits. its facts expire with that session, so a seed built on one is a record
whose subject no longer exists by the time anyone reads it.

⚠️ **the tell: strip the machinery and NAUGHT is left.** a seed that survives the strip keeps its
concept — S08 kept the moai when its radio clause went — and one that does not was never about a
concept.

⚠️ **the arrears are real.** S01 was **dropped** under this standard, and S02 and S08 were stripped
of the environmental clauses they carried incidentally. **the other 21 have not been swept for the
prose standard above**, and several carry the chronicle shape.
