# 🌙 dream: route reminder nudge trail, handle sunset, pid identity, race seam

kind: feature · owner: rhachet-roles-bhrain · caught 2026-11-21

## .the cue that fired

rounds i003 and i004 of `fix-route-reminder-one-per-clone`, the enroll-impl lanes: a human cannot see the nudges; the clone home keeps files of dead sessions; the pid is the daemon's only identity; two composite races are clamped only at their leaves (fulcrums F12, F13).

## .the gap, precisely

- the daemon logs only its exit line. a human cannot check "one nudge per interval, about the route driven now" from the log: neither a nudge nor a focus move leaves a line
- each session leaves `.focus`, and sometimes `.log` and `.crashes`, in `.agent/.cache/repo=bhrain/role=driver/skill=route.reminder/`, with no sunset. the `.pid` is dropped by the daemon on every exit
- a pid file names a pid, not a daemon: a recycled pid held by another process reads as live, and a signal to it faults EPERM (`withRouteReminderProcessFaultMap`, the `kept` legacy outcome)
- the drive's dead arm reads the focus, then signals the daemon; a drive that lands between the two loses its daemon until its own next hook
- the sync's dead-arm focus gate and the tick's retarget are clamped at their leaves; no test moves the focus between the two reads of one call
- the parent claims the handle for the child, so a daemon exists with no handle between spawn and claim; the reconcile, retry, and orphan reap guard that gap

## .the shape of the fix

- a nudge line and a focus-move line in the clone log, with the log rotated or capped; `getRouteReminderLogPresence` reads presence alone, so it stays correct
- a sunset: on gen, remove the `.focus` / `.log` / `.crashes` of a clone handle whose `.pid` is absent and whose files are older than a bound
- an identity probe beside `isProcessAlive`: `ps -p <pid> -o command=` must name the `route.reminder` daemon with this `--clone-addr`; a stranger pid reads as stale, so EPERM stops to be a fault. weigh it against a new host boundary per platform
- decide whether the dead arm keeps its kill: without it, a daemon lingers up to one interval on a blocked route, and the focus gate, log wipe, and retarget re-read go with it
- the daemon claims its own handle at boot (`claimExclusiveWrite`); a loser exits itself, and the parent only spawns and then reads the handle. `claimHandleForSpawnedPid`, `reconcileExtantHandle`, `reapSpawnedPidOnFault`, `RouteReminderOrphanError` and the tick-1 ownership skip go. the boot-survival read must then wait out the child's claim
- an injected `onBetweenReads` seam (or a passed-in activity read) in `syncRouteReminderForDrive` and `stepRouteReminderTick`, so an integration case writes the focus between the two reads

## .why it is not done in this round

none ships a harm the wish names; each wants its own small design (log growth, a session-death signal, a host boundary per platform, a new seam on two contracts). the one item with a nameable harm, the daemon that outlives its handle, landed in round i004 (`isRouteReminderHandleOwnedBy`).
