# 🌙 dream: `declare each claim exactly once` stops at the yield's edge

- **kind** = enbrief
- **owner** = `ehmpathy/rhachet-roles-bhrain` — `repo=bhrain/role=driver`,
  `rule.always.yield-the-output-not-the-archaeology`
- **caught** 2026-09-07, `5.3.verification`, in this repo

## .the cue that fired

the wisher asked one question — *"how could we ever reach this? didnt we block re-entry if the
`.taken` is omitted?"* — and it falsified a clause in a **shipped code comment**.

then the sweep to propagate the correction found the fact **already stated correctly, twice, on the
same branch**. it had been right in two artifacts and wrong in a third for two whole stones.

## .the gap, precisely

the rule carries the right instruction and binds it to one file:

> **declare each claim exactly once.** a claim that belongs in two sections means one of the two is
> redundant.

🟡 **"two sections" — of the yield.** its own after-check confirms the scope: *"grep each decision's
central term — more than two or three hits and you retell rather than declare"*, with the worked
command scoped to `$route/*.yield.md`.

⇒ so a claim declared once per artifact and three times across the route passes the check
cleanly, and a correction to one copy leaves every other copy untouched.

a sweep of every artifact family on this route — yields, the halt file, the fulcrum inventory,
`src/` comments, the experience contracts, the published briefs, and the dreams — found 47
instances of exactly this: a claim correct at its source and stale in a restatement, with no check
that spans the gap.

### the six varieties

| variety | what happens | sharpest instance |
|---|---|---|
| corrected-elsewhere | a claim is corrected at its source; a restatement elsewhere is not | F3's confidence — corrected in its entry and the fulcrum summary, stale in `blocker/5.3.verification.md`, `5.3.verification.yield.md`, and two further yields — 4 of 6 homes never reached |
| never-right | a summary invents a claim the entry never made | F16's lowest-confidence row claimed *"judged dirty on a ripple estimate, never a measure"* — the entry makes no such claim. no correct ancestor exists to diff against, so no sweep finds it |
| overtaken-by-the-world | the claim was true when written; the world changed, no author error | F18's credential-absence note stayed true-when-written after the credentials cleared |
| half-propagated | one half of a claim updates, the other does not, and the pair still reads as coherent | F16's rubric-doubt row took its score from after a check and its reason from before it — internally plausible, wrong |
| source-edited-in-place | a citation's target text changes while the citation stays syntactically valid | F3's `where` cites `…ContemplationStatus.ts:84`; the line survived, its content was rewritten by this branch — the citation still resolves and now names the wrong claim |
| corrected-below-rather-than-at | a correction is appended below a stale claim, and never replaces it in place | the F9 dream corrects its own `:411` twice, ~130 and ~220 lines below the table that states it wrong — a reader who stops at the first table gets the wrong number |

three literals also cannot be maintained by any discipline, because the quantity moves on its own:
a route's `since-main` file count, a document's own line count, and any count that grows as the
route works. the repair for these is not a corrected value — it is the command that re-derives it
at read time (`wc -l <path>`, `git status --porcelain | wc -l`).

### the two-slot radius — measured over six confidence values, zero exceptions

a correction reaches exactly two homes: the entry's header field, and the summary row that indexes
it. it does not reach the entry's own section title, or any yield, or the halt file.

| home | held the correction? |
|---|---|
| the entry's header field | 6 / 6 |
| the summary row that indexes it | 6 / 6 |
| the entry's own `##` section title | 0 / 4 checked |
| every yield and the halt file | 0 / 4 (measured on F3) |

⇒ the radius is a distance in EDITS, not in files. a stale title sits in the very file the author
had open, a screen from the field they corrected, and is missed every time — proximity does not
help; the title was simply not part of the act that corrected the field.

🟡 F7 and F3 carry no metadata block at all, so their values lived only in a `##` title — invisible
to any sweep that reads header fields, and stale. F9's title, by contrast, reads `## .why the
confidence is low` — no literal — and survived two re-scores (`72 → 80 → 90`) untouched, while the
four titles that did carry a number went stale on one move each.

the repair applied: two of six homes (`5.3.verification.yield.md`, `1.vision.yield.md`) no longer
state F3's confidence at all — they point at the entry that authors it. two more
(`blocker/5.3.verification.md`, `5.1.execution.from_vision.yield.md`) keep the value, because they
are the wisher's triage surfaces where a follow-a-link cost is real; that exposure is deliberate.

### reversed drift — a stale ENTRY under a correct summary

not every drift runs source-to-summary.
- F11 holds four disagreeing confidence values (`84 · 76 · 84 · 91`) in one file
  - it was appended to from two directions over six rounds with neither side aware of the other
- ⇒ neither the entry nor the summary is reliably the source of truth
  - a reader who resolves conflicts by a fixed preference is wrong about half the time
- the only remedy that held in every case is a single machine-comparable field
  - a `grep` for `confidence` across 18 files found the reversed cases in a second
  - the never-right and half-propagated varieties took a manual audit and were found by accident

### the line-number citations — a class of its own

a `file.ts:NN` cited into a file the branch itself edits decays two ways: **displacement** (an
insert above the line pushes it down — a re-read shows plausible but wrong code) and
**substitution** (the line holds, its content changes — only a read of the content catches it,
since the number still matches). measured across this route:

| citation | wrong by | homes |
|---|---|---|
| `runStoneGuardReviews.ts:441` (exhaustion skip) | wrong branch — `:441` is the clean-cache `continue;` twenty lines above | 5, across a dream, two experience-contract files, and a mechanism list |
| `setStoneAsContemplated.ts:56-64` (slug rejection) | ~30 lines short | 4 |
| `setStoneAsContemplated.ts:92-95` (ack forms) | ~20 lines short | 1 |
| `route.ts:947` / `:834` | off by 2 each (`:949` / `:836`) | 2, both inside the same function so the wrong code still reads plausibly |
| `runStoneGuardReviews.ts:402/:405/:411` | each off by 5 | a test-file comment; the dream that originated the numbers corrected itself twice, ~130 and ~220 lines below the table that kept them |
| `route.ts:1024` vs `:1026` | one file, two citations of the same guard that disagree | 4 comments |
| `setStoneAsPassed.ts:1021` (judge branch) | 5 short | 1 |

three citations were verified accurate and rewritten to the symbol anyway, since "right today" does
not survive the branch's own edits: `getSelfReviewChallengeDecision.ts:98` (published brief),
`writeInputArtifacts.ts:20` (glossary census), `setStoneAsPassed.ts:332-337`.

🟡 the scope is narrower than it first reads: a `:NN` is fine in a file the branch does not touch
(foreign paths, `jest.*.env.ts`), and it is fine — required, even — in an append-only record (a
`.taken`, a `.given`, a `passage.jsonl` entry), where the number documents what was read at
iteration N and must not change. a generated file (a `.snap`) is the worst case: every number in
it expires together, on a schedule, for a reason unrelated to the claim.

### the append-only exemption — do not "fix" a ledger

- a yield, a summary, or a blocker file asserts what is true NOW, and a stale value there is a
  defect
- a `.taken`, a `.given`, or a `passage.jsonl` entry asserts what was said at iteration N
  - it is correct as written even after the world moves
  - to "fix" one falsifies the conversation the contemplation gate exists to force
- one measured instance: `.reviews/peer/…i027….taken.by_self…md:17` still reads *"itemized at F9 +
  F10 (88%, ruled together)"*
  - every part of that is now wrong — F9 is 90%, F10 is 88%, neither is ruled together
  - it must stay exactly as written, since it is the record of what was said, not a claim about
    what is true

### shape defects — a value sweep cannot see them

two surfaces held every value correctly and still failed:
- the fulcrum summary's lowest-confidence table sat unsorted on the column it sorts by
  (`1 · 2 · 2= · 5 · 2=`)
- `blocker/5.3.verification.md`'s `.what is open` section interleaved five asks and two reports
  with no marker of which was which
- neither is a stale restatement, and both defeat the surface as thoroughly as one would
- a rule that only grades individual claims (quote-or-cite, derive-or-command) will pass a page
  like this and call it clean

### the counter-case — a biased sample, not a general rule

- every row above is a defect, because a defect is what gets written down
  - a summary that was simply correct is read once and forgotten
- the check that corrects for the bias: the experience tally was re-measured against all 112
  cells of the walk, counted one at a time (20 demoed · 6 forbidden · 9 impossible · 77 itemized)
  - both the source (`…experience.case=_.md`) and the once-stale yield line now match it exactly
- ⇒ the target for a fix is narrower than "distrust every summary"
  - a summary drifts when its source is corrected and no mechanism re-reads it
  - the fix belongs at the moment of correction, never at the moment of read

### the structural counter-example — a symlink cannot drift

- the glossary census exists at two paths and in one file, via a symlink
- the corrected-elsewhere class is structurally impossible there — no sweep can ever find drift,
  because none can arise
- every cue below is a weaker approximation of this
  - wherever two homes must hold identical text, a symlink removes the class outright
  - the cues are for wherever they hold a *characterization* of each other instead

### the final measurement — the author who named the class still committed it

- on 2026-09-08, this dream's own author — who had swept sixteen surfaces for this exact class
  that week — undercounted a guarded-site count across five homes authored within one hour, when
  the true count was three
  - `one` (the S24 seed title)
  - `both`/`two` (the F9 entry, the A-dream's delivery table, the 5.3 yield's verdict row,
    `getLatestReviewArtifactForSlug.ts`'s docblock)
- the miscount traced to the wisher's sketch, which showed one site
  - every later artifact copied the count forward rather than derive it
- three careful passes missed the third site
  - one grep for the expression `cachedReviews.find((r) => r.index === …)` found it
- ⇒ to know the class is not to catch it
  - the only remedy that held in every measured case is mechanical: grep the expression before you
    edit any of its sites, and write the derived count, never a word carried over from a sketch

## .the shape of the fix

widen the declare-once clause from *one yield* to *the route plus what it ships*, and give it
checks that can run rather than a discipline to remember:

1. **state the scope** — a claim is declared once across the route's artifacts and any code
   comment the route authors, never once per file
2. **grep at the moment of correction** — the correction is when the author holds the exact
   phrase; a sweep later does not. this catches corrected-elsewhere, but not never-right,
   overtaken, half-propagated, source-edited, or corrected-below
3. **widen the after-check** — it currently greps `$route/*.yield.md`; it wants the route dir and
   `src/**` for any term the route's code comments assert
4. **grade a code comment above a route artifact** — a stale route artifact misleads the next
   traveler; a stale code comment outlives the route and misleads every future maintainer at the
   moment they are about to change the code
5. **quote or cite, never paraphrase from memory** — a summary row that characterizes another
   artifact must quote its words or cite its line, so a reader can check it against the source.
   this is the only cue that catches never-right and overtaken-by-the-world, since those are wrong
   at authorship or by drift rather than by a missed correction
6. **grade a mis-described summary above a stale one** — a stale row has a correct ancestor on
   disk; an invented row has none, so no sweep finds it, and both outrank their entries in reader
   trust
7. **derive or cite, never type a value another artifact determines** — write *"once per row of
   the table below"*, never a count; write *"see the F3 entry"*, never its percentage. a claim with
   no literal in it cannot go stale
8. **propagate a correction reader-first, never source-first** — start the grep for copies at the
   most-read surface and work inward; the natural order is the opposite, so it must be said
   outright
9. **where no artifact owns the value, write the command** — `wc -l <path>`,
   `git status --porcelain | wc -l`. a command re-evaluates at read time and cannot go stale
10. **cite the symbol, not the line number, in a file your own branch edits** —
    `runStoneGuardReviews.ts` "the `blockers === 0` fast-path" survives every edit; a bare `:NN`
    does not. a `:NN` is fine in a file the branch does not touch, and required — never rewritten —
    in an append-only record; a generated file (`.snap`) has no stable-number case at all
11. **date a sampled value rather than treat it as present truth** — a column header that reads
    "the truth" over three sampled values is true of one kind of cell and false of another; "as
    sampled `<date>`" cannot go stale
12. **write a section title with no value in it** — a title is the surface a reader's eye lands on
    and the one a `grep` stops at; a title that repeats a header field's number goes stale the
    moment the field is re-scored
13. **apply a cue in the same act you write it, at every home** — a cue written and not applied at
    all its own homes is a cue written to nobody
14. **title a check by the scope it ran, never the verdict it reached** — a scope-titled check
    invites the next question; a verdict-titled one closes it and hides the gap from every later
    reader — the one who ran it, too
15. **sweep `src/` comments, not only markdown** — code comments carry the most trust and the
    least audit, and this dream's own first defect was a false clause in a shipped comment
16. **repair a claim in place; note the prior text beneath it, never append the correction
    below** — an appended correction does not replace a stale claim, it sits beside it, and a
    reader who stops at the first answer never reaches the second
17. **prefer a symlink to a discipline wherever two paths must hold identical text** — it applies
    only where the two homes want the same text, never where one merely summarizes the other, but
    where it applies the class becomes structurally impossible
18. **grep the citation before you assume it has one home** — a wrong citation propagates by copy
    and clusters; the two-slot radius applies here too, so a fix reaches the artifact you had open
    and the one you set out to find, and stops there
19. **sweep the dreams** — a route artifact is read while its stone is live, with the branch open;
    a dream is read months later, by whoever picks the ask up, with no tree to check it against
    and no author to ask

### the bound this fix must carry

an append-only artifact — a `.taken`, a `.given`, a `passage.jsonl` entry — asserts what was said
at iteration N, not what is true now, and none of the cues above apply to it. a rule phrased as
"keep restatements current" would sweep these in and falsify the record the contemplation gate
depends on; it must name its scope as *artifacts that assert present truth* up front.

and a value sweep cannot see a shape defect (an unsorted table, an unmarked mix of asks and
reports) — a page can pass every cue above and still be unusable. a rule from this dream should say
what it does not cover, or a green run reads as a clean page when it is not one.

## .why it is not done in this round

**out of the wish's fence.** this route fixes the contemplation gate; its `0.wish.md` carries an
explicit `.scope — do NOT let these expand it` block, and a rewrite of a booted driver brief is not
on it.

🟡 and the halt makes it costlier to defer than usual: the route is halted at `5.3.verification`,
with every wisher ruling still open — F3 · F9+F10 · F16 · F17 — per the fulcrum summary's own
`[wisher]` tags. to enlarge a booted brief now adds files to a `since-main` diff that already
overflows five review lanes, and the drive would grade its own new rule with no lane able to read it.

⇒ every stale restatement found on this route is repaired at the source, one repair per instance
above. what is deferred is the rule that would have caught them before a human had to.

six distinct varieties were measured on one route (corrected-elsewhere · never-right ·
overtaken-by-the-world · half-propagated · source-edited-in-place · corrected-below-rather-than-at),
plus two shape defects and three unmaintainable literals. the sixth variety recurred in this
dream's own authorship, by the same hand that had just named the class, inside the artifact built
to record the recurrence — the strongest evidence that a discipline alone will not hold:
**a discipline its own most motivated practitioner cannot hold for one hour is not a discipline;
it is a wish.**

the one counter-case in this whole dream stays the shape of the answer: the glossary census lives
at two paths in one file, via a symlink, and no sweep can find drift there because none can arise.
derive or symlink, over restate — argued here by 47 measured failures and confirmed by the one
structure among them that cannot fail.
