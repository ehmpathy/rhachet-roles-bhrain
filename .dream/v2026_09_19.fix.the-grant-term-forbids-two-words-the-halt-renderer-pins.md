# 🌙 dream: the `grant` term forbids two words the halt renderer pins as canonical

- **kind** = `fix`
- **owner** = whoever next touches `route.guard.budget` copy, or the `route.guard.budget.grant` term cluster
- **caught** = 2026-09-19, from `.behavior/v2026_09_17.fix-budget-grant-needs-urgent-concession`,
  stone `5.3.verification`, raised as `i004/r001` nitpick.1 + nitpick.2 by `repo-rules`

## .the cue that fired

the `repo-rules` reviewer approved the stone with 0 blockers and raised two nitpicks that name the
same collision from two sides: **published cli copy uses `increase` and `top-up`, and this route's
own new term cluster declares both forbidden synonyms of `grant`.**

⇒ the defect is self-inflicted. `term=route.guard.budget.grant._.choice._.md:6-11` was authored on
this route, and its `.refs` at `:35` names `route.guard.budget.sh` as *"the published cli"* — one of
the exact files that carries `top-up`.

## .the gap, precisely

`rule.forbid.domain-term-synonyms` binds **contracts**, and names published interfaces as the most
expensive place to drift:

> *"a **contract** = a dobj/dop name, an internal contract … and **above all the external interfaces
> we publish** (api, sdk, cli)"*

a rendered halt remedy and a `--help` body are published cli bytes ⇒ contract-tier.

| the word | where it ships |
|---|---|
| `increase` | `formatBlockRemedyGroups.ts:95` — the label `increase budget — yours to spend` |
| `top-up` | `route.ts:2330` (help text) · `formatBudgetGrantRefusalLines.ts:20` · `route.guard.budget.sh:6` |

### 🔴 .the part that makes this a DISPUTE rather than a rename

`increase budget` is not loose prose — **an extant test pins it as the canonical term**:

```
src/domain.operations/route/drive/formatRouteDriveHalts.test.ts:118
  then('the term is `increase budget`, never a second word for it', () => {
```

and a second clamp asserts its **absence** on the converge path:

```
src/domain.operations/route/drive/formatRouteDriveHalts.test.ts:92
  expect(output).not.toContain('increase budget');
src/domain.operations/route/stepRouteDrive.integration.test.ts:481
  expect(result.emit?.stdout).not.toContain('increase budget');
```

⇒ **the codebase already declared `increase budget` the one word for its concept, and pinned it.**
the new `grant` cluster declares the opposite. two declarations, one concept, and each has a test or
a glossary entry behind it.

## .the shape of the fix

🔴 **it is a `.reason` dispute, never a find-and-replace.** `rule.forbid.domain-term-synonyms` offers
exactly two moves — *"adhere, or dispute"* — and the extant set is explicitly left alone:

> *"it may be **left in place until disturbed** — no forced mass-rewrite."*

so the fix is one dated entry in `term=route.guard.budget.grant._.choice.reason.md`, under
`## .disputes`, that settles **one** of three options:

| option | what it would mean |
|---|---|
| **A — two concepts** | `grant` = the permitted raise (the gate's verdict); `increase budget` = the **remedy label** a driver reads on a halt. both canonical, each under its own boundary. ⇒ the forbidden list drops `increase`, and the term gains a `.see also` that names the split |
| **B — one concept, `grant` wins** | the labels are rewritten, the two clamps are updated, and ~90 sites move. 🔴 **8 of them are driver BRIEFS that publish to other repos** |
| **C — one concept, `increase` wins** | the term cluster is amended; `grant` keeps the gate-verdict sense alone |

**best guess = A.** the halt label answers *"what may you do?"* and the gate's verdict answers
*"was it earned?"* — genuinely different questions, and `rule.forbid.domain-term-ambiguity`'s own
sort (*"the senses differ by **context** — one concept, two boundaries ⇒ **qualify**"*) points here.

## .why it is not done in this round

the SAFE/CLEAN test (`rule.always.fix-forward-under-scouts-honor`):

- **safe?** 🔴 **no.** option B flips two **negative** clamps and one **positive** one, and
  `rule.require.review-test-changes` grades an unasked assertion change a red flag by name
- **clean?** 🔴 **no.** measured with `rhx grepsafe --pattern 'increase budget|top-up|topup|Topup'`:
  **43 sites in `blackbox/`** (≈20 snapshot files + 8 live assertions) and **~47 in `src/`**,
  **8 of them driver briefs** (`rule.always.spend-own-levers-before-escalation`,
  `rule.always.converge-to-terminal`, `howdoes.the-review-gates-you-drive-within`,
  `define.invariant.review.peer.judge.urgent-guides-the-budget-ask`, and their `.min` twins) that
  **publish to other repos**

⇒ and the rule forbids the sweep outright: a find-and-replace cannot part a **citation** of the word
from an **instance** of it, and several sites quote shipped bytes verbatim.

🟡 **the concern was graded `nitpick` by the reviewer that raised it, and the stone's judge cleared
the tally** (`--allow-nitpicks 7`, 3 distinct concerns landed). ⇒ under this behavior's own shipped
thesis — *inside the budget, `better` is enough; past it, only `urgent` is* — a `better` concern
below the floor earns no further round. to re-open a sealed, passed route for it would be the exact
churn loop the behavior exists to stop.
