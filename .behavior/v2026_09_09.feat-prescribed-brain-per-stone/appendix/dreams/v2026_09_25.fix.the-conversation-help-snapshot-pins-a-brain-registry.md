## .the gap, precisely

the test's own header states its subject:

```ts
//        drift in the --conversation flag docs is visible in the pr diff
//        (rule.require.snapshots + blueprint test-tree)
expect(sanitizeTimeForSnapshot(result.stdout)).toMatchSnapshot();
```

⇒ 🔴 **it snapshots the WHOLE `--help` stdout to guard ONE block of it.** the `options:` block is
this repo's to own; the `🔭 available brains` block is a render of whatever brain packages happen
to be installed, and it is **three times the size** of the block under test.

so every brains-package bump breaks a test whose declared subject did not move.

### 🔴 the real cost is not the noise — it is the MASK

a resnap diff here is 10 lines of registry churn. a genuine `--conversation` regression would be
**one line inside that churn**, in the same hunk, reviewed by someone who has already learned that
this snapshot's diffs are routine.

⇒ **the guard's own signal is diluted by what it does not guard**, and the dilution grows every
time a brains package adds a model.

## 🔴 .why a `--resnap` was correct here, and is NOT the general answer

⚠️ **`.dream/v2026_09_16.fix.foreground-guard-snapshot-pins-a-jq-version.md` says, in bold, "do NOT
`--resnap`."** that is right for that case and wrong for this one, and a traveler who carries the
rule across without the reason will take the wrong act on one of them.

| the pinned foreign input | varies by | a resnap… |
|---|---|---|
| `jq`'s parse-error text | 🔴 the **HOST** | pins *this machine* and moves the failure to whichever host runs the other build — the same defect, a new victim, harder to find |
| this brain registry | ✅ a **PINNED DEP** in `package.json` | is **deterministic** — every host on `0.1.7` renders the identical list |

⇒ **the resnap was taken, and the diff was read to confirm it: registry rows only, `options:`
untouched.** the snapshot now matches the dep the branch declares.

🟡 **the durable line: a resnap is honest when the foreign input is VERSIONED IN THE REPO, and
dishonest when it is a property of the machine.** both look like "a third party's text leaked into
our snapshot"; only one of them has a single right answer to record.

## .the shape of the fix

the same move the jq dream prefers, one repo over: **assert on the part we own.**

1. 🔴 **snapshot the `options:` block alone** — split stdout at the `🔭 available brains` header and
   snapshot the head. the subject is the flag docs; the registry is scenery
2. **assert the registry's SHAPE, not its contents** — that the header renders and that every row
   matches `/^ {3}[├└]── [↻○] \S+$/`. that catches a broken renderer and ignores a new model
3. resnap on every bump — 🔴 **what happens today by default**, and it is the option that degrades

⇒ prefer **1**, with **2** beside it if the registry render is itself worth a guard. a snapshot is
a contract about **our** output, and a brain catalog is not ours.

🟡 and check the neighbours: any other snapshot of a full `--help` stdout carries this same pin.

## .why it is not done in this round

- **SAFE?** ❌ — it rewrites an acceptance assertion whose subject is the reviewer's `--conversation`
  contract. this drive's stone is the prescribed-brain feature; a change to what that guard asserts
  would ride along under a diff nobody bound it to
- **CLEAN?** ❌ — option 1 changes the assertion shape and its snapshot; option 2 adds a matcher.
  neither lands inside the diff this round already has

⇒ deferred per `rule.always.fix-forward-under-scouts-honor`. **no fulcrum is owed** — this is not a
judgment this round made, it is a defect this round found in work it does not own. the resnap it
*did* take is recorded above with its reason, so the next traveler need not re-derive why it was
the right act here and the wrong one for `jq`.
