# howto.winnow-the-ideas

> **separate the wheat from the chaff in a set of ideas: for each idea, a verdict a reader can check, with the evidence beside it.**

the human asked for it on 2026-10-19: teach bhrain's skeptic the winnow act, to separate the wheat from the chaff in ideas. the wish that holds the contract: nheuron, tree direction/rhachet-accelerate, `src/stream/2026.Q4/2026-10-19.seed.repo=rhachet-roles-bhrain.skeptic-winnow.wish.md`; the task: ehmpathy/rhachet-roles-bhrain#647.

**how it applies the basis:** an idea in a pile is trusted because it is there. winnow doubts each one by the same test, the author's favorite too, and keeps only what the evidence lets stand.

## .what

winnow takes a set of ideas (a brainstorm, a list of proposals, the options of a plan) and returns one verdict per idea: `kernel` (keep) or `chaff` (discard), with the reason and the evidence. a reader acts on the kept set without a re-read of the pile.

## .the contract

| field | holds |
|---|---|
| the idea | as given, so the reader can match it back |
| verdict | `kernel` or `chaff` |
| the kernel | where an idea is part sound, the part that survives, in fewer words than the idea |
| why | one or two sentences |
| evidence | a fact, a measurement, a contradiction, a duplicate |

## .what makes a kernel sound

all of:

- it serves the stated goal (or, with none given, a goal the reader can name)
- it rests on a claim that evidence supports, or that a cheap read could check
- it is not a restatement of a peer idea already kept
- it survives the strongest counter-argument against it (`define.skeptic.lessons-from-the-greats`, lesson 13: refute the strongest form)

chaff fails one of them. a duplicate folds into its peer and is named, never dropped in silence.

## .how it differs from the other skeptic acts

| act | the unit | the question |
|---|---|---|
| `rule.always.trust-but-verify` | one claim | true, or stale? |
| winnow | a set of ideas | which to keep, which to drop, and the sound part inside a mixed one |

a claim can be true and its idea still chaff (true, and serves no goal). an idea can hold a kernel inside a false wrapper. winnow uses verification as one source of evidence and does not replace it.

## .how to know it worked

- every idea in the input gets exactly one verdict; none is dropped or invented
- every verdict carries evidence a reader can check, not only an opinion
- a mixed idea yields its kernel, not a blanket keep or discard
- duplicates are folded and named

## .out of scope

- a rank of the kept kernels by value (a prioritizer's job)
- execution of any kept idea
