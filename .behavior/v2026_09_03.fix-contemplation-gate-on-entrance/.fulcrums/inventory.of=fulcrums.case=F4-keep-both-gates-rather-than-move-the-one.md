## .the fork, stated fairly

the wish says the gate is "on the exit, never the entrance", which reads as a call to move it.

- **move it** — one gate, at the entrance. simplest to explain.
- **keep both** — an entrance gate and the extant exit gate.

## .taken, and why at the time

**keep both.** they catch different debts:

| gate | catches |
|---|---|
| entrance | debt carried **into** this round |
| exit | debt created **by** this round — the blockers the reviewers just raised |

if the exit gate were removed, a driver would pass on the very critique it just received: the
reviewers run, raise blockers, write givens, and passage proceeds because the entrance check ran
before those givens existed. that is a strictly worse hole than the one under repair.

so the wish describes where the gate is **absent**, not where it is misplaced.

## .rework, and why

**clean.** to drop the exit gate later is a deletion of one block. to re-add it is a revert.
