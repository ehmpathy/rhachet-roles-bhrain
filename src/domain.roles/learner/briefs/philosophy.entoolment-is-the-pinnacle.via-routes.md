# philosophy.entoolment-is-the-pinnacle.via-routes

## .what

a **zoom-in** of `philosophy.entoolment-is-the-pinnacle._.md` onto **the rung-3 artifact this org
reaches for most.**

> **a route IS a 🔩 rigid skill.** not *"like one"*, not *"adjacent to one"* — it satisfies
> rhachet's definition of rigid exactly: *"deterministic entrypoint, mixed operations +
> orchestration."*

🟡 **it is the most GENERAL shape at rung 3, never the rung itself.** rung 3 also holds a skill
that computes its setup then hands one probabilistic step to a brain, and a wrapper that batches
the deterministic half — and **both are specializations of a route**: a route is an ordered set of
stones with guards, so a one-stone route is the first and an all-deterministic route is the second.

⇒ a route earns its own zoom-in for that generality, never for a preference. any rung-3 shape can
be expressed as a route; the reverse does not hold — and the case it fits most often is
**judgment that recurs, in an order that matters.**

the parent brief ranks artifacts by brain cost per use. this one answers the question that rank
raises: **what do you pave when the brain cost cannot fall to zero?**

## .why the answer is a rung and not an exception

rung 4 asks for zero imagine cost, and much real work has an irreducible floor above zero — it
needs judgment at several points, and no 🪨 solid skill can take that judgment.

that floor is easy to read as a **failure to reach rung 4**. it is not. a route is a full
destination on the same ladder, and the reason is mechanical:

| the route's part | its determinism |
|---|---|
| the stones, their order, their guards | 🪨 **deterministic** — the guard refuses identically, every time |
| the work performed at each stone | 💧 **probabilistic** — a brain judges |

⇒ that blend is the definition of 🔩 rigid. **the route has a rung, it is a tool, and rung 3 is a
destination rather than a shortfall.**

## .the deterministic half DRIVES the probabilistic half

this is the property that earns the rung, and it is stronger than *"there is some determinism
somewhere."* **the deterministic mechanisms push the brain through.** the brain does not choose to
advance; a mechanism advances it, or refuses to:

| the mechanism | what it deterministically does to the brain |
|---|---|
| the **guard** | refuses passage until its conditions hold — the brain cannot proceed by decision |
| the **stone order** (numeric prefixes) | computes what comes next, so the brain never recalls it |
| the **stophook** / `route.drive` | re-emits the current stone unprompted — the brain is *pulled back* to the road it drifted from |
| the **passage ledger** (`passage.jsonl`) | holds state on disk, so progress survives a brain that forgot it |
| the **review ladder** + judge | tallies verdicts by rule, so *"is this done?"* is computed, never argued |

⇒ **a route is a deterministic transport with probabilistic cargo.** the rails are solid; the
passenger thinks.

## .what a route removes — variance, not cost

a 🪨 solid skill's benefit is that the work costs **zero**. a route's benefit is that the work
costs **the same every time**:

| without a route | with a route |
|---|---|
| what comes next? | the stone says |
| did i skip a step? | the guard refuses to let you |
| how did we do this last time? | the route IS last time |
| every traveler improvises their own order | one order, walked and refined |

⇒ **a 🪨 solid skill removes the brain. a route removes the brain's overhead** — the recall, the
order, the second-guess, the rediscovery of a path somebody already walked.

three properties follow, and none of them is cost reduction:

- **fewer trips** — the guard catches the misstep before it compounds
- **less rediscovery** — the order of operations is the artifact, not tribal memory
- **a floor under quality** — the worst run of a paved route is far better than the worst
  improvisation, because the stones carry the lessons of every prior traveler

and it compounds like any pavement (`philosophy.pavement-saves-nature`): the first traveler pays to
lay it; every traveler after walks it for the cost of a read.

## .the other rigid skills — a route is not alone at rung 3

| the rung-3 artifact | what it is |
|---|---|
| a **route** | a directory of stones with guards, walked by a brain |
| a **published thought route** | a skill that interleaves compute steps and imagine steps |
| a **computed-setup skill** | one that derives its inputs deterministically, then asks a brain to judge |

all three spend a brain only where judgment is real, and all three drive it deterministically
between those points. **reach for the route when the judgment recurs in an order that matters** —
that order is the part a route removes and the others do not.

## .the choice — what to pave, given what is left over

| the leftover work is… | pave it as… |
|---|---|
| a step — same every time | 💪🪨 a **solid skill** (rung 4) |
| a sequence of commands | 💪💧 or 💪🔩 a **fluid or rigid skill** (rung 2–3) |
| judgment, at several points, in an order that matters | 💪🔩 a **route** (rung 3) |

🟡 **do not route what could be entooled.** a route around deterministic work is ceremony that
charges imagine cost at every stone. reach for a route when the judgment is real.

## 🗿 .offroad work earns its keep by what it paves

sometimes there is no route, and a brain must go **offroad** — genuine exploration, no map. that is
legitimate and often necessary.

but offroad travel is the most expensive mode there is, and it is paid **per traveler**. so:

> **an offroad brain is at its best when it leaves a route behind.**

the exploration happens either way. to end it with a paved route rather than a finished task is
what converts a one-time cost into a permanent asset — the same move as `enskill`, applied to work
whose steps need judgment rather than commands.

⇒ **`declapract.upgrade` is the worked example.** an improvised upgrade — run it, see what breaks,
fix in whatever order occurs to you — is now `init` plus a route of stones: invoke, detect hazards,
repair test defects, reflect, repair cicd defects, reflect. **the brain still performs every
repair.** it no longer has to recall that a reflection follows each repair, or that hazards are
detected before any repair begins.
