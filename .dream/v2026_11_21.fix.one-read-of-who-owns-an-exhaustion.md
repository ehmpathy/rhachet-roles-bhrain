## .the cue that fired

the F8 fold-in had to patch the same `better || urgent` test into two files, and a reviewer (5.1 r011 i004 nitpick.1) counted five places that decode one fact.

## .the gap, precisely

- `asRouteStoneDisposition.ts` and `isReminderLiveForPassage.ts` each spell `isRouteGuardConcessionExhaustion || isRouteGuardUrgentConcessionExhaustion` over the persisted reason
- `isRouteDriveExhaustionStopBlocked` spells `kind !== 'none'` over the live read
- `formatRouteDriveBudgetExhausted.ts` holds a local `isConcession` (better-only) and reads `display.isConcession` (better-or-urgent) 38 lines apart
- `isRouteGuardConcessionExhaustion` is named for any concession but means `better` only

a fourth surface can forget a severity.

## .the shape of the fix

- `asConcessionExhaustionKind({ reason }): ConcessionExhaustionKind` — the persisted mark, as the type `getStoneConcededLaneSlugs.ts` already declares
- `isExhaustionDriverOwned({ kind })` — one predicate; disposition, reminder, and stop-block read it
- rename the better-only predicate to say `better`; drop the second `isConcession`

## .why it is not done in this round

a rename across the reason-mark readers ripples every caller and its tests (fails CLEAN). the behavior is correct today and clamped at integration grain (`setStoneAsPassed.exhausted.integration` `[case1][t3]`).
