# domain.term.choice.reason: unattributed

## .etymology

the negation of `attributed`, so the pair reads as one axis at a glance
(`rule.prefer.symmetric-term-pairs`).

rejected:

| rejected | why |
|---|---|
| `defective` / `broken` | assert a found fault. the census cannot part a real unnamed input from legitimate scaffold, so it claims neither |
| `partial` | the rows sum to the whole prompt by algebra, so no census is partial. the doubt is about which input the residual belongs to, never about its size |

## .the evidence

a realistic prompt's tags and headers run ~1% of its chars; the threshold sits at a tenth, far
past that. a census measured at 70.8% residual on 293 small targets is graded `unattributed`
rather than thrown on — a corpus shape is unvouchable, never impossible, and a throw there would
kill every review of a repo shaped that way.

## .the boundary

"unattributed, of WHAT?" — of a prompt's chars, by the census. `review.grade.attribution`.

## .disputes

none.
