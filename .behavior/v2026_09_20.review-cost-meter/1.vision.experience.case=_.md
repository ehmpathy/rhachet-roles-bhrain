## .`cold` has two causes, and the grade names one

the `cold`/`warm` grade holds so a 0.0% first round does not read as a defect. a live probe
measured a second cause, and the two want opposite responses:

| the cause | what a driver should do |
|---|---|
| first round — no prior prompt to share with | no act. correct and unavoidable |
| elapsed time — the prefix was resident and aged out | a real loss, and no prompt edit repairs it |

the evidence is monotone in elapsed time and noisy in prefix size: reach held 90.0% at 20m,
27.8% at 33m, and 0% at 66m, 67m, 118m — while a 195.5k prefix at 67m served zero and a
prefix 6.4× smaller at 33m served. a direct probe confirmed a 240k prefix dead at 78m.

so a 0.0% round 5 reads exactly like a 0.0% round 1, and only the second is benign. the
grade as it stands tells a driver their round was cold; it does not tell them whether that
is the weather or a defect they introduced.

the cell is demoed (`case=2`) and this does not un-demo it — it is a distinction inside the
extant grade, not a new coordinate. what would serve it: the grade carries the elapsed time
since this lane's prior round, which `getOneReviewRunMeterLatest` can already reach. `cold
(first round)` and `cold (aged 67m)` are one render string apart, and they are the
difference between *"as expected"* and *"you just paid full rate for a prefix you had."*

## .agency = `engine` (the system acts between invocations)

two engine acts exist: the warmer, and the prime meet. each ACTS at run breadth — one guard,
one lane, one tree. but the warmer's VALUE is read at tree and grove breadth:
`getOneWarmerValue` weighs every settled poke against what its followers' cache saved, and
`asWarmerValueLines` renders the verdict in both `--of tree` and `--of grove`. so the act is
impossible past run breadth; the read of its cost is not.

| breadth | quantity | intent | verdict | feel × care | note |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| run | chars | observe | itemized | happy × alter | unserved — *"what did the warmer warm?"* in chars. the row carries `promptTokens`, a post-call quantity on the `cost` side of this axis's own split |
| run | chars | defend | itemized | sharp × **alter** | unserved — the prediction clamp (see the re-grade below) |
| run | cost | observe | itemized | happy × alter | *"what did the warmer spend?"* — `metrics.size.tokens.cache.get` per poke |

| run | * | trace | impossible | — | one invocation has no second point, exactly as the driver half |

| {tree, grove} | chars | * | impossible | — | the warmer replays one lane's prefix; no composition exists past run breadth |

the prime meet spends one poke per shared head. `setOneReviewPrefixPrime` writes its realized
metrics to a run dir of its own, so its figure sits in the extant `engine × run × cost × observe`
cell, and an unmeasured prime grades `unknown` in the `defend` cell
(`setOneReviewLanePrime.integration.test.ts` — "its spend is recorded in a run dir of its own" /
"the meter grades the prime unknown — never a low cost"). no new cell.

### why the value verdict is alter, not criti

the harm cell is `engine × run × cost × defend`, demoed at `case=7` — the spend is recorded. a
wrong verdict costs a misread advisory, and `--warmer off` is the fallback, so care is alter
(`F232`).

### the prediction cell — re-graded from criti to alter

`rule.require.experience-coverage` says a critipath owes a demonstration; an alterpath may
stay itemized. `engine × run × chars × defend` was first graded criti with no demo, which
was a contract violation on its face. the origin dream's own measured table refutes that
grade, though:

| the prediction is | consequence |
|---|---|
| over-warmed | excess bytes warmed and never hit — inefficient, never broken |
| under-warmed | protects fewer bytes than it could — inefficient, never broken |
| the wrong system prompt | wrong replica. protects naught, silently |

only the third row is a critipath, and it is a different cell entirely — a split error, not
a prediction error — and it is already clamped two ways: `asWarmerPokePrompt` refuses
outright where the head does not prefix the prompt (`rule.require.failfast`), and the loop
suite asserts `call.head` is byte-identical on every poke. so the `criti` grade belonged to
the cell next door; this one is alter and unserved. what would serve it: the warmer row
carries the chars it replayed, and the next round's `metrics.realized.json →
prompt.sharedPrefix` says how many of them the round actually shared — one join apart,
measured on neither side today.

### why `run × cost × defend` is the one demoed cell on the engine half

the driver half's costliest cell is `run × chars × defend` (`case=3`) — an input that
enters the prompt with no row of its own. the engine half's costliest is its cost twin:

> a `driver` cost that goes unrecorded is a figure someone asked for and did not get. an
> `engine` cost that goes unrecorded is money nobody knows was spent.

a warmer that fires 3× per lane across 11 lanes, every round, with no row in any meter, is a
spend line that grows with use and appears in no surface this branch built — bounded (3
pokes × 11 lanes × ~$0.0013 ≈ $0.043 per abandoned route), which is exactly why it would go
unnoticed. `case=7` is the demo that forbids it.

`case=6` moved cell from `tree × cost × observe` to `tree × cost × trace`: its `[t3]` does
not price a round, it differences two of them, which no `observe` surface can do. the
re-file is what makes wish bar 5's instrument a named obligation rather than a line of yield
prose.

## .the nineteen itemized cells, and why none earns a demo

a demo is owed by care (criti vs alter), never by whether a surface has been built — so a
cell can be served and still carry no `case=N`.

**served — a surface answers them; a demo would restate a neighbour's fixture:**

- `run × chars × observe` — eight rows off one run's census; the trivial read `case=3` already defends
- `run × cost × observe` — one run's tokens and spend, the atom `case=1` sums
- `run × cost × defend` — `asAccountedCost` on a single run, with its own unit test; `case=2` demonstrates the same grade where it bites (a total)
- `grove × chars × observe` / `grove × chars × defend` — both alter-path; `[case8]`/`[case9]` in `genReviewGroveStdout.test.ts` clamp the caveat in both directions
- `engine × run × cost × observe` — *"what did the warmer spend?"*, alter-path; its `defend` twin (`case=7`) carries the harm and is demoed
- `tree × chars × observe`, `tree × chars × defend` — both alter-path; the by-input census and its caveat on `--of tree`, the same render as the grove pair
- `tree × chars × trace` — alter-path; `rhx census.review-shared-prefix`, a repo-internal instrument whose two acceptance suites are its record. a miss costs a misread rate, never a wrong bill
- `engine × {tree, grove} × cost × {observe, defend}` — alter-path; the warmer value block, one render at both breadths; `case=7` carries the harm (`F232`)

**unserved — no surface answers them, so there is no behavior to demo:**

- `grove × chars × trace`, `grove × cost × trace` — both alter-path; a ruled decline (`F22` fork **c**), since a grove cross-tab keys on `<stone> i<NNN> · <slug>`, which two trees can share
- `engine × run × chars × observe`, `engine × run × chars × defend` — the warmer's own `chars` column, unfilled; a mis-predicted prefix is inefficient, never broken, which is the honest reason it stays alter rather than criti
- `engine × {tree, grove} × cost × trace` — alter-path; no surface differences the value verdict round over round, and the grove cell meets the same `F22` fork **c** key collision

## .the forward terminus — the one demoed critipath whose sketch is not yet proof, per this catalog

`rule.require.experience-catalog-evolution` grades this catalog in both directions. the
forward direction outlives the vision stage: by the time verification passes, every
critipath in the catalog owes a real test. `demoed` above means named + sketched, never
proven.

|---|---|---|
| `case=1` | largely proof | `genReviewMeterStdout.test.ts [case1]`/`[case2]` — a mixed corpus, and an all-unknown one that reads absent rather than zero |
| `case=2` | proof | `asAccountedCost.test.ts`, all 5 cases |
| `case=3` | proof | `getOneAttributionGrade`, in the shipped path, clamped red under a 5,000-char unattributed block |
| `case=4` | proof | `asCacheHitRate.test.ts [case1]` — the denominator pinned |
| `case=5` | proof | `asAccountedCost.test.ts` — the honesty census, at assertion grain |
| `case=6 [t3]` | open, per this row | naught — no test re-drives a stone after a repair and asserts the second round's cache share exceeds the first's; the instrument exists (`--trace`), the two-round test does not |

`[t3]` needs a live brain, a real guard, and two rounds with a repair between them — a
verification-stage act by construction. a faked proof of a cache gain is worse than an
absent one, because wish bar 5 rests on exactly this figure. verification does not pass
while this row reads open; it is the contract's own unmet clause.
