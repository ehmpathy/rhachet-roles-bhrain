correction, read 2026-11-24 (pass 3, `.temp/research/skeptic.sources.pass3.play.ts`): the razor page lists rows 2, 3, 4, 5 only as "see also" links, so their originators were not on it. each was then fetched: row 2 → "Law of the instrument" (Kaplan 1964, Maslow 1966); row 3 → Sagan's baloney kit ("excluded middle, or false dichotomy"); Morton's fork is a narrower form (contradictory observations lead to one verdict) and is dropped as a synonym; row 4 → "Occam's razor" § anti-razors (Chatton; Menger 1960); row 5 → "Zebra (medicine)" (Woodward, late 1940s) and "Reference class forecasting" (Kahneman and Tversky; the outside view). row 1's quote cites Miles, *Inroads* (2003), p. 543; the page names no Hume work.
