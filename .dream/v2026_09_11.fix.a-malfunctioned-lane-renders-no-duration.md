# 🌙 dream: a malfunctioned lane renders no duration, so it cannot be compared to its co-members

- **kind** = fix
- **owner** = `rhachet-roles-bhrain`
- **caught** = 2026-09-11

## .the cue that fired

peer review i002/r9 (`arch-hazards-behavior`) conceded the blocker on the wall-clock timeout and kept
one half open: *"the timeout message names the bound and not the contention — was this lane slow, or
were seven co-members?"*

the obvious answer was that the **guard tree already shows it**: settled lanes render
`rejected [TIME]`, so a driver could compare the timed-out lane against its level-mates with no new
plumb-through at all.

⇒ **the snapshot was opened to confirm that, and it refuted it.**

## .the gap, precisely

`blackbox/__snapshots__/driver.route.peer-review-timeout.acceptance.test.ts.snap:6-10` is the whole
rendered lane:

```
   ├─ r1: slow-reviewer (l1, 0/2)
   │   ├─ malfunction 💥
   │   ├─ terminal — does not block higher levels
   │   ├─ given: …r001._.given.by_peer.slow-reviewer.md
   │   └─ taken: …r001._.taken.by_self.slow-reviewer.md
```

beside a settled lane from `…peer-budget-multimember-l1…snap:8`:

```
   │   ├─ rejected [TIME]
```

| the lane | carries a duration? |
|---|---|
| `rejected` · `approved` | ✅ yes — `[TIME]` |
| 🔴 `malfunction 💥` | 🔴 **no** |

⇒ **the one lane whose duration is the diagnosis is the one lane that does not report it.** the
`timed out after 21 minutes` text exists, in the `.given` artifact's stderr — one file away from the
tree the driver reads.

⚠️ **and the omission is worse under a pour than it was serially.** serially there was one lane and
its duration was the whole run; concurrently the question is *relative* — slow against what? — and the
render supplies neither term: not the timed-out lane's own duration, and so no basis to weigh the
co-members' against it.

## .the shape of the fix

render `[TIME]` on a malfunction line, exactly as `rejected`/`approved` already do:

```
   │   ├─ malfunction 💥 [TIME]
```

the elapsed time is **already held** — `GuardProgressEvent.inflight` carries `{ beganAt, endedAt }`
and the formatter reads it for every other outcome. so this is a branch in the formatter that stops
to suppress a field it already has, never a new measurement.

⇒ start at the outcome branch in `formatGuardTree` / `genContextCliEmit` that emits the settled
detail block, and find why the malfunction arm omits the duration the others print. it may be
deliberate (a killed process has no honest end instant) — **check that before you change it**, since
if so the honest render is the bound rather than the elapsed.

## .why it is not done in this round

the SAFE/CLEAN test, answered honestly:

| question | answer |
|---|---|
| **safe?** | ✅ yes — an additive field on one render branch |
| **clean?** | 🔴 **no** — it re-cuts every malfunction snapshot among the 36 the vision froze |

🔴 **the cost is now a NUMBER, not an estimate — 16 acceptance snapshots.** counted by peer review
i032/r10, which reached this same gap independently and priced it: *"this is locked in by 16
acceptance snapshots (so it's deliberate/stable, not accidental)."*

⇒ **that parenthetical is the sharper half.** 16 committed snapshots is a declared contract rather
than an oversight, so the fix is a contract change and owes a round that says so in its own title.
price it at 16 before you open the formatter.

🔴 **and the dirt is a DECLARED commitment, not merely a cost.** vision acceptance 2 is that the
settled tree survives the pour unchanged, and the 36 committed snapshots are its oracle. a render
change to a malfunction line moves bytes in `peer-budget-malfunction`,
`peer-malfunction-nonterminal-higher`, `peer-contemplation-malfunction`, and `peer-review-timeout` —
⇒ **the round whose whole claim is "these did not move" cannot be the round that moves them.**

it belongs to a round that owns the render, where the re-baseline is the point rather than a
contradiction.

⇒ its peer is `v2026_09_09.fix.a-timeout-message-names-the-bound-and-not-the-contention.md`, which
carries the other half — the message. **these are two repairs of one diagnosability gap**, and the
tree half is the cheaper of the two once a round is free to re-cut snapshots.
