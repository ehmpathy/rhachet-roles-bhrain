# 🌙 dream: decouple the thinker's briefs from the telepath's

**kind** = amend
**owner** = this repo (`ehmpathy/rhachet-roles-bhrain`)
**caught** = 2026-09-04

## .the cue that fired

the wisher, mid-drive, as `howto.razor-a-thought` was scoped into telepath:

> *"and catch a dream to better decouple thinker briefs from telepath briefs in the future. with for
> now, overlap is fine, since the thinker role needs some heavy cleanup. (but razors seem like a
> thinker concept, applied to telepathic communication)"*

## .the gap, precisely

**a razor is a THOUGHT operation, and telepath is about to own one.**

| the act | whose subject it is |
|---|---|
| cut a candidate cause from a set until the root is left | 🪐 the thinker — how a mind reaches a conclusion |
| cut a line from a draft until the point is findable | 🔮 the telepath — how a concept reaches a peer |

⇒ **they are the same move at two scales**, which is why `howto.razor-a-thought` lands cleanly in
telepath and still reads as borrowed.

🟡 **it is not the only one.** `require.fundamentalism` has the same shape — *decompose to the root*
is a thought operation, and telepath carries it because the canon needed a method under
`condensation` and `elucidation`. the boundary between the two roles is drawn by nobody today.

**verified 2026-09-04:** `thinker` is declared in `getRoleRegistry.ts` and is **not linked** in
`.agent/repo=bhrain/`, so this repo publishes it and does not consume it. its state is unaudited
from here.

## .the shape of the fix

1. **audit the thinker** — the wisher names it as owed *"heavy cleanup"*; the boundary cannot be
   drawn against a role whose contents are unknown
2. **name the seam in one line**, the way `generic-governs-structure-never-voice` names telepath's
   other seam. the candidate: **the thinker owns the move on a CONCEPT; the telepath owns the move
   on a PASSAGE**
3. **re-home by that seam** — a razor set that grades candidate causes is the thinker's; a razor set
   that grades candidate lines is telepath's. they may well be **one root plus two zoom-ins**
   (`rule.prefer.decompose-a-subject-via-suffixes`), never two forks
4. **leave a pointer both directions** — whichever half moves owes a back-pointer, so the split does
   not become a phantom path

## .why it is not done in this round

**safe: yes. clean: no.**

- the seam cannot be drawn without the thinker audit, and that audit is out of this behavior's scope
- a split drafted against a misread of the thinker's contents would move files and repair no defect
  — the exact failure `im_an.obsessive_learner.for.domain.glyphs` records for a glyph swap
- and the wisher settled the interim explicitly: **"for now, overlap is fine"**

⇒ so the overlap is a **declared** state, never an oversight. this dream is what keeps it declared.
