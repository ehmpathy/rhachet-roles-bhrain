## .the gap, precisely

🔴 **a fulcrum's whole value is that a human eventually reads it, and no mechanism ever tells them
it is there.** `rule.always.defer-fulcrums-to-last` gathers every best-guessed call into one council
at the close — and *"the close"* is a moment only the driver recognizes.

⇒ so the discipline has a **write** half that is disciplined and a **surface** half that is not:

| the half | what drives it |
|---|---|
| itemize the call as you make it | `rule.always.itemize-the-fulcrums-you-best-guess` — a rule the driver holds |
| 🔴 **raise it to the human before they leave** | **naught.** the driver must remember, at the exact moment they are about to rest |

**measured on this route:** the wisher asked *"questions for me?"* **unprompted**, and five rows came
back that had sat open across several rounds. two of them — F03 and F04 — were ruled within
minutes, and one was a **reversal that changed the architecture**. ⇒ **the questions were ripe the
whole time and the surface was a human's own initiative.**

🟡 **and the extant `onStop` hook is the proof this seam works.** `elucidate.summary --when
hook.onStop` already blocks a rest until the driver states where things stand, and its own text asks
for *"⭐ before your ask, if you have one"* — **so the slot for an ask exists and its contents are
unchecked.**

## .the shape of the fix

an `onStop` hook that reads the bound route's fulcrum board and refuses a silent rest while a row is
open:

```
🦉 before you rest — 3 fulcrums stand open on this route

🔮 fulcrum.nudge --when hook.onStop
   ├─ F004 · a disputed lane goes quiet    · dirty · 55%   ← 🔴 the dirty row reads first
   ├─ F013 · a dirty dispute drives on     · clean · 75%
   └─ F007 · stance is an invented umbrella · clean · 70%
   │
   └─ ⭐ if any of these is the wisher's to rule, ask now
```

three properties it owes:

- **it nudges, never gates** — the parallel is `--why`'s own verdict (`F014`): the value is the
  reconsideration it prompts, so a check on the ask's *content* would buy naught
- 🔴 **it sorts on `rework`**, so the `dirty` rows lead. that is the column's whole purpose
  (`rule.always.itemize-the-fulcrums-you-best-guess`), and no surface reads it today
- **a ruled row falls off** — which is why it needs `fulcrum.get --status open`, and why this dream
  depends on its peer

## .why it is not done in this round

**SAFE** — no. a new hook fires on every rest, in every session, on every route.

🔴 **CLEAN** — no, and this is the harder half: **the query it needs does not exist.** `--status
open` is a field no code can read, since `.fulcrums/` has no operation at all
(`.dream/v2026_09_09.entool.a-fulcrum-inventory-has-no-operation.md`). ⇒ a hook built now would parse
markdown tables by regex, which is the free-text-parse hazard this route already names as a defect
class.

⇒ **so the two dreams are ordered: the operation, then the hook.**
