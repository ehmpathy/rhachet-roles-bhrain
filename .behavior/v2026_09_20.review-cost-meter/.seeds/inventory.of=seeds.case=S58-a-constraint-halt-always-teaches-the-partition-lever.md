## .settled

- the lead remedy of a constraint halt is the partition lever, in every case
  - where the .given proposed partitions, the halt pastes them, ready to run
  - where it proposed none, or the .given was not read, the halt shows the template, so the driver
    still learns the lever exists
- "clear the cause" is not a remedy row: it names no command, and it hides the lever the driver
  owns
- the order stays by owner: the partition lever, then the narrower bind, then the human overrule
