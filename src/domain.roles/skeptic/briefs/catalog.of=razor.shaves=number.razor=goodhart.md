# razor: goodhart

shaves: a number or a sample · settled by: observation (the number against an outcome read apart from it)

## .the question

> **now that someone aims at this number, does it still track the outcome?**

the rivals: a real gain vs a gamed gain. a count of acts is not the outcome the acts were for.

## .the source

- Charles Goodhart, 1975, on UK monetary policy: "Any observed statistical regularity will tend to collapse once pressure is placed upon it for control purposes"
- "when a measure becomes a target, it ceases to be a good measure" is Marilyn Strathern, 1997, from Hoskin, 1996
- cousins: Campbell's law (from 1969); the Lucas critique (1976)
- read via https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goodhart%27s_law

## .fails when

- no one aims at the number; then it may still track. ask who aims

## .run by

`rule.always.trust-but-verify`
