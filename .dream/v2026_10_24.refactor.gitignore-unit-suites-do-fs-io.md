## .the gap, precisely

`src/domain.operations/route/gitignore/findsertReview{Peer,Self}Gitignore.test.ts` build temp dirs
under `os.tmpdir()` and call `fs.mkdir` / `writeFile` / `readFile` / `stat` / `rm`. each has an
`.integration.test.ts` twin with near-identical cases (`[case1]`–`[case8]` overlap on the error
variants). both pairs are main-era; the fix-status-line-cpu pr touched them for one literal and one
label.

## .the shape of the fix

merge each `.test.ts` into its `.integration.test.ts` twin: keep the union of cases, one snapshot
name per variant, then delete the `.test.ts` and its `.snap`. if a pure helper is worth a unit
test, test it with an injected fs.

## .why it is not done in this round

SAFE: yes. CLEAN: no — a merge of two suites per pair, outside the wish (F29 of
`fix-status-line-cpu`).
