# seed S18 — each ask earns a task and a seed

## .said — verbatim, 2026-09-07

> creat a task and archive each ask

## .settled

**a wisher ask is received in two artifacts, always: a task that carries the WORK, and a seed that
carries the CONCEPT.** neither substitutes for the other.

| artifact | holds | lives |
|---|---|---|
| a **task** | what must be done, and its blockers | the session |
| a **seed** | what now holds, and the words that settled it | the route, forever |

## 🔴 .why it had to be said — the asks were answered in prose, one at a time

six asks arrived in quick succession. each was answered with an accurate paragraph, and **not one
produced a durable artifact.** a prose answer feels like a record, which is the whole failure:

> the answer was given, so it seemed captured — and it was captured nowhere.

⇒ `rule.always.archive-the-wishers-words-verbatim` names exactly this: *"a spoken followup is a
deletion in a polite coat."* the rule was in the driver's booted context throughout.

⚠️ **the ask that hides is the QUESTION.** a wisher's *"why is X?"* whose answer changes the work reads,
in hindsight, as though the **driver** found the gap. every ask in this cluster was a question, and
every one changed the work — the guard lever, the conversation payload, the level skip, the budget
scope. the rule flags this cue as the cheapest kind to lose, and it was very nearly lost.

## ⚠️ .the discipline

on each ask, before the answer is composed:

1. **is there work?** → a task, with its blockers wired
2. **did a claim settle?** → a seed, `.said` verbatim, typos and all
3. **then** answer in prose

the prose is the cheapest of the three and the only one anybody reaches for unprompted.
