# domain.term.choice.reason: dispute

## .etymology

latin *disputare* — *to weigh, to examine by argument* (`dis-` apart + `putare` to reckon). the
sense is **to reckon apart**, never to refuse.

⇒ that is precisely the act: the driver removes one concern from the tally **and states the
argument**. a dispute with no argument is not a dispute; it is a `--why` the boundary refuses.

## 🔴 .why `refute` is forbidden — the words sit on opposite sides of one gate

`[REFUTE]` is already declared: it is one of two `.taken` shapes
(`rule.always.converge-with-reviewers.via-a-taken-per-point`), prose the **reviewer** re-reads.

| | who reads it | what it moves |
|---|---|---|
| `[REFUTE]` in a `.taken` | the **reviewer**, next round | whether the reviewer re-raises the concern |
| a **dispute** | the **judge**, this round | whether the concern counts in the tally |

⇒ **a driver may owe both on one concern**, and a glossary that let one word cover both would delete
the distinction the whole design rests on.

## .why `override` and `veto` are forbidden

both name **authority**, and a driver has none. the human's lever is `overrule` / `forgive`; a
dispute is an **argument**, which is what a party without authority offers instead.

🟡 **the distinction is not cosmetic — it is the defect this route found.**

## 🔴 .the grain a forgive's seam would have imported

`S06` settled the mechanism as a **tally exclusion** and named it *"the driver's forgive, at lane
grain"*. the analogy carried the implementation in with it: `getNonOverruledReviewFiles` drops a
whole **review FILE**, so one dispute shed every concern the lane raised — the driver's own
concessions among them.

⇒ **a forgive is legitimately coarse BECAUSE A HUMAN DECLARES IT. the breadth is the authority.**
copy the grain onto a driver's move and you copy the grant along with the code.

⚠️ **the check that would have caught it** is `S06`'s own — *"what does the downstream operation
already read?"* — asked of the **grain** rather than of the axis. it returns *"a file list"*: the
correct answer, and the wrong grain. ⇒ a second half is owed: *"and is that the grain the DOMAIN
settled?"* — `S05` had settled it, on the page, one day earlier.

⇒ the full record: `S07` · `1.vision.experience.case=11` ·
`rule.forbid.suppression-of-undeclared-concerns`.

## .the words it beat

| candidate | why not |
|---|---|
| `refute` | 🔴 already declared, on the other side of the gate. see above |
| `override` / `veto` | ⛔ each names authority the driver does not hold |
| `reject` | reads as a verdict on the reviewer rather than on the concern |
| `appeal` | implies a higher body will rule. no such body runs before passage |
| `waive` | ⛔ a waiver forgoes a right; a dispute asserts one |
