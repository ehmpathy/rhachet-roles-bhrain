## .the gap, precisely

`rule.require.given-when-then` (ehmpathy/mechanic) asks a `then` title to name the claim it
verifies. `then('stdout has good vibes')` names none — it states that output exists, which
is true of every step in the corpus that passes.

the cost is real and specific: the title is **baked into the snapshot key**, so a reader who
walks `.snap` keys to reconstruct a journey reads the same non-claim at every frame. the key
becomes a position marker rather than an assertion.

**measured 2026-09-06:**

| where | count |
|---|---|
| `blackbox/**` | **375 lines** |
| `blackbox/achiever.goal.triage.acceptance.test.ts` alone | ~20 |

## ⚠️ .what this dream does NOT claim — r005's premise was refuted, the observation kept

r005 argued the branch had already **adopted** a better convention, and cited
`driver.route.journey [t10.5]`, retitled to `an allowed bounce is silent on stderr —
snapshot pins the silence`.

**that retitle was forced, not chosen.** `[t10.5]` snaps **both** streams inside one `when`
(`:651` stdout, `:655` stderr), and a jest key derives from the title — so two frames in one
`when` must carry distinct titles or collide. the titles name the stream because they had to.

🔴 **the proof is in the same file:** `[t10]` still reads `then: stdout has good vibes`
(`driver.route.journey.acceptance.test.ts.snap:297`). a branch that had adopted a convention
would not leave the adjacent step on the old one.

⇒ **the observation survives its refuted premise.** the title is still a non-claim in 375
places. what is false is that this branch created, or half-adopted, the inconsistency.

## .the shape of the fix

per-frame, name what the snapshot pins — `matches snapshot — the round that turns over
budget`, in the shape the corpus already uses elsewhere.

⚠️ **a retitle re-keys the snapshot**, so each one needs a mint. that couples this fix to the
credential gate, which is the second reason it cannot land here.

## .why it is not done in this round

it fails the SAFE/CLEAN test on **clean**, twice over:

1. **375 instances, overwhelmingly outside this branch's contact set.** most sit in files
   this branch never opened. `rule.require.review-test-changes` grades a corpus-wide rewrite
   smuggled into an unrelated change a **blocker**, and `rule.prefer.wickup-touched-prose`
   bounds fix-forward to prose already under the diff
2. **every retitle re-keys a tracked baseline**, and the mint sits behind the five absent
   keyrack keys that already hold this stone. so the sweep could not be **verified** here
   even if it were in scope — and an unverified 375-key edit is the fabrication this drive
   has refused twice

⇒ the decision to defer is itemized as fulcrum **F12**, per
`rule.always.catch-dreams-for-followups`' dirt clause.

### 🔴 reason 2 above does not survive, and it is left visible rather than deleted

*"an unverified 375-key edit is the fabrication this drive has refused twice"* **conflates two

**reason 1 — scope — stands alone, and is sufficient for the 369.**
