## i013 — l3 (r10 behavior-intent) blocker: the LIVE judge tree still contradicted the overrule

the i012 r10 fix corrected the **persisted** tree (`🗿 route.stone.set` via `formatGuardTree`) to render

**root cause (two coupled seams):**
  - **`runStoneGuardJudges.ts` cached emit** always emitted `{ decision: prior.passed ? 'allowed' :

    the renderer as a bare `blocked`.

    only `'decision' in judge`, so even a fresh malfunction fell through to `✗ blocked`. the peer side
    already softens a broken reviewer to `malfunction 💥` (via `asReviewerTreeState`); the judge live
    renderer had no equivalent.

**fix (root, parity with the peer side):**
  - cached emit now mirrors the fresh-run emit — a malfunctioned judge (cached or fresh) carries the
    `{ malfunction }` variant, never a bare decision (one shape either way).
  - `handleJudgeEvent` derives its mark+status through one shared `asJudgeLiveMarkStatus` transformer
    (used by both the cached and completed branches — `rule.forbid.duplicate-format-tree-operations`):
    a malfunction reads `💥 … malfunction`, never `✗ blocked`. the live event never carries the overrule
    (a human action on a halted stone), so — exactly as the peer side documents — it renders the FACTUAL
    verdict (`malfunction`) while the persisted tree renders the FORGIVENESS (`overruled ✓`). no
    contradiction: the judge crashed (live), the human forgave it (persisted).
  - **snapshot-stability:** the live judge malfunction line carries a duration (`… malfunction <dur>s`);
    added the absent `malfunction \d+\.\d+s → malfunction [TIME]` mask to `sanitizeTimeForSnapshot`
    (the `malfunctioned` verb was masked, the `malfunction` noun was not — a sub-second mock-judge
    duration would otherwise flake, cf. the constraint-verb fix).

**regression cover:** `judge-overrule-peer` case1 `[t2]` gains an explicit non-snapshot assertion —
`not.toContain('✗ judge.1 - blocked')` + `toContain('malfunction')` + `toContain('overruled ✓ — forgiven
by human')` — so the contradiction can never silently re-bake into a whole-stdout snapshot again.
