# case=11 — a miss the harness cancels still warms the cache (sharp · critipath)

## .narrative

a box at load 56 makes a cold repaint slow. the harness sends SIGTERM to a repaint that runs past
its window. were the process to die at once, the cache write would never land, and every repaint
would be a cold miss: the cost the wish removes, back in full. so the cached entry holds a short
grace on the first SIGTERM: the render finishes, the cache file lands, and the process exits. the
next repaint hits. a render that hangs past the grace is killed at the grace (exit 143), and a
second SIGTERM kills at once, so the harness is never held hostage.

## .bdd sketch

```
given('[case11] a cold seat whose repaint the harness cancels')
  when('[t0] the harness sends SIGTERM mid-render')
    then('the cache write lands within the grace, and the next repaint hits')
  when('[t1] the render hangs past the grace')
    then('the process exits 143 at the grace')
  when('[t2] the harness sends a second SIGTERM')
    then('the process exits at once')
```
