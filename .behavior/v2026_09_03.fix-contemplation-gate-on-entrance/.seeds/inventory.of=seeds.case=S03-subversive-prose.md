# seed S03 — subversive prose

## .said — verbatim

> this yield has subvsersive prose still. do you know what i mean by that ?

## .settled

**prose can pass every terseness test and still work on the reader rather than inform them.**

a metaphor stands where a mechanism belongs · an aphorism dresses a limitation as a principle · a
self-issued ✅ decides what the reviewer exists to decide · a read-order directive steers the review to
the author's strongest exhibit. each is a **substitution**: rhetoric occupies the slot a derivation
should hold, and the reader is handed a conclusion in place of the concept that would produce it.

⚠️ **the defect is invisible from the inside.** the author reads their own persuasion as clarity, which
is why a human names it after peer lanes tuned for terseness pass the artifact clean.

🔴 **length does not distinguish it.** measured head to head, the rhetorical form and the mechanism it
displaced cost the same words. **the test is the substitution, never the word count** — *does this
transfer a concept, or a conclusion?*

⇒ ⚠️ **the causality is in S06, and it is the opposite of what this seed first claimed.**
