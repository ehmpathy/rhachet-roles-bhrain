# case=5 — a lane that misses pays as today

feel = sharp · care = critipath · cells = S1.within `group` × `whole` × `cold` · S1.route `unpinned` ×
`within` · cross `group` × `cold` × `unpinned`

the spotlight on the fail-safe of `case=1`: what a lane pays, and what the driver sees, when the
shared head does not reach it from cache.

## .narrative

two ways a lane of a shared-head group misses its peers' prefix:

- **the meet goes cold.** the poke faults, the claim times out, or the lane's deadline leaves the
  meet no time. the meet names the fault on stderr and the lane pours at once
  (`genReviewPrefixPrime.ts:65-68`). a prime is an optimization, never a gate
- **the replica is the wrong one.** the poke primed a replica of the cheapest host, and the floor
  sent this lane to the same host but no pin sent it to the same replica. its request lands cold

either way the lane sends the same bytes it would have sent on a hit, and pays the corpus at the
full rate — what every lane pays today. no branch costs more than the current layout, save the
1.5s gather the meet already spends. the verdict is unaffected: a hit and a miss deliver identical
bytes to the model.

the miss is not hidden. the meter grades the lane's pair `missed` inside the reach, with 🔴, and
the `by provider` and `by brain` rows show the share it lost. a driver who reads a 🔴 `missed` on a
group lane reads the route, not the layout: the pin is the dispatch owed to
`rhachet-brains-openrouter`.

## .[tn] sketch

```
given a level of 8 lanes that share one head, after cure 1
  [t0] the level pours, and the first lane claims the head
  [t1] the meet settles cold for lane 5
    when the poke faults
      then the fault is one line on stderr, named
      then lane 5 pours at once, and its review runs whole
    when lane 5 lands on a replica the poke never reached
      then its request sends the same bytes as its peers
  [t2] lane 5's review returns
    then lane 5 paid the corpus at the full rate — the figure of today, never more
    then its verdict is the verdict a hit would have produced
  [t3] the driver runs `rhx review._.cost --of tree`
    then lane 5's pair grades `missed`, with 🔴
    then the provider row and the brain row each show the share lost
```
