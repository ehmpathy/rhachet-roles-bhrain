## .the fork

the decomposition is an invented taxonomy, which `rule.always.itemize-the-fulcrums-you-best-guess`
names as a fulcrum in its own right.

taken: `actor(2) × goal(3) × declaration(6) × reach(3)` = 108 cells.

a fifth axis — **entry ordinality** `{first, re-entry after a halt, re-entry after a rewind}` — is
admitted as axis `E` and does **not** multiply the product: the hook's act is identical on every
entry. the one exception, re-entry after a rewind (the live brain is a later stone's, and sticky
keeps it), is itemized as `📋 sharp·alter` in `case=_`.

⇒ so a reviewer checks two claims: are the four orthogonal, and is the collapse of the fifth sound?

## .taken, and why

- **`reach`** — its `unenrolled` value is measured, is today's common case, and is the value a
  free-list drops
- **`declaration` has six values** — `absent-from-launch` vs `absent-after-switch` makes stickiness
  (`F6`) a cell, and `misspelled-key` makes `case=4` visible at all
- **`actor`** — a driver authors guards too, and it splits the `audit` slice into two readers

rejected axes live in `1.vision.experience.dimensions.md`, cited rather than copied here.

## .the counter-case

`actor` is the weakest axis. slice 4 (`human × prescribe`) mirrors slice 1 (`driver-clone ×
prescribe`) exactly — 18 cells, identical verdicts, since a guard on disk does not know who typed it.
three axes would yield 54 cells and lose no demoed critipath. it is kept because the mirror shows
author time is actor-blind — the blindness `F3` is about.
