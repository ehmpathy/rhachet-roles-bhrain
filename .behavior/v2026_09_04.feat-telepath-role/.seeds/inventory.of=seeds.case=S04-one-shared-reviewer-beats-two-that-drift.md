# S04 · one shared reviewer beats two that drift

## .said

> *"⇒ if the right shape is one lane shared across repos rather than one per repo, say so in your yield. that is a better outcome than two rubrics that drift."*

🟡 verbatim. it is phrased as a permission and it functions as a question — which is why it earns a seed.

## .settled

a rule that is **structural** is repo-agnostic, so it takes one home and is adopted by reference.

the concept, stripped of this round:

- two rubrics over one concern **drift on the half that was worth a shared home**
- a fork is earned by a **voice** difference, and voice is a separate axis from structure
- ⇒ so a shared rubric is the default and a fork is the exception that must show cause
- the cost side agrees: adoption of a shared reviewer costs a declaration, never an implementation, so a local copy buys no ergonomic win

🟡 the seam it implies: the **rules** have one owner; **which artifacts each consumer grades** stays with the consumer. one is a prose fact, the other is a scope decision about that repo's own work.
