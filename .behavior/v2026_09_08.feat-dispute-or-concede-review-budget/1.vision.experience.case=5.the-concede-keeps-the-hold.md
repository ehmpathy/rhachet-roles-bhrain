# case=5 · the concede keeps the hold

**the other half of acceptance #1**, and the half that reads as a no-op until you see what it

🔴 **the concede-tailored guidance lands in the ACK, not the re-run.** `[t0]`'s ack already said

the moment I need it. a second concede-worded halt at `--as passed` would repeat guidance I already

**the aha:** *the concede looks like it does naught, and that is exactly its value. today a driver
that adds budget and re-arrives leaves a ledger where a number shrank for no stated reason. now the
ledger reads `conceded — mechanic`, and a human who scans ten rounds can see at a glance which were
churn and which were agreement.*

⇒ the wish says it outright: **the signal is the value, never the permission.**

✅ **the ack prints NO budget command** — *"the hold stands. fix, then re-arrive"*. `S11`'s sequence
is `concede → fix → budget → re-arrive`, and here the mechanic has 2 rounds left, so the third term
elides.

⚠️ **that elision is why this cell cannot check the ORDER.** with no budget ask in play no order can
be got wrong; `case=6` is the same act at `C = exhausted`, where it can. ⇒ **a defect at one value of
an axis is invisible to every demo elsewhere on it.**

🟡 **and `S11` re-scopes the aha above, which is right about the MECHANISM and short about the ACT.**
a concede changes no state, and it is not therefore passive: *"concede is to fix and run again"* — a
declaration **with work attached**. ⇒ the emit's *"fix, then re-arrive"* is not advice beside the
stance; **it is what the stance MEANS**, and `[t1]`'s refusal is that meaning enforced.

## .why it fails safe

**fast** — the hold is the extant hold; no new subprocess, no new latency · **loud** — the halt
names the lane and the reason in the driver's own words (*"you conceded"*) · **teaches the fix** —
one command, and it is the one the driver already knows.

⚠️ **the deadlock check.** a concede narrows the driver's options by design — it forecloses the
dispute on that given (F09). **the exit is real and it is `[t4]`**: fix, re-arrive, and the new
given carries a fresh stance slot. a driver is never trapped by its own agreement.
