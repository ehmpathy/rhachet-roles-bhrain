## .the cue that fired

a peer reviewer (`enroll-impl-arch-defects`, i006 r011 hazard 2) grepped for the consumers of
`getSelfReviewTriggeredReport`'s return fields and found two of the three read by nobody. the count
is checkable and I re-ran it: `uptilMtime` and `attempts` appear in that file, its own test, and no
other production line.

## .the gap, precisely

```ts
getSelfReviewTriggeredReport(...) → { sinceMtime, uptilMtime, attempts }
```

its one production caller is `getSelfReviewChallengeDecision.ts`, and that caller reads
**`.sinceMtime` alone**. the other two are vestigial: they date from before this round moved the
gate off a counter read and onto the atomic `firstAdjudication` claim, and the read side was never
slimmed to match the write side.

the cost is concrete rather than stylistic:

| what the dead fields cost | per |
|---|---|
| an `fs.stat(uptilPath)` for a value no line reads | every gate check |
| a `.uptil`-absent fallback branch that exists only to keep `uptilMtime` populated | every gate check |
| a reader who must decide whether `attempts` is load-bearing here | every read of the file |

🟡 the third row is the real harm. `attempts` **is** load-bearing in `setSelfReviewTriggeredReport`
(it is written there, and its docblock carries a 🔴 caution against a gate keyed on it). a reader who
meets it on the READ side too has two places to hold, and only one of them matters.

## .the shape of the fix

split by consumer, so each operation's contract is what its caller actually needs:

```
getSelfReviewAskedAt({ stone, slug, route })
  → Date | null
  the gate's operand, and the whole of it. one `fs.stat`, one branch

getSelfReviewTriggeredDiagnostic({ stone, slug, route })      ← only once a caller appears
  → { sinceMtime, uptilMtime, attempts }
  the human's read. today it has no caller, so do NOT write it speculatively
```

⇒ **the honest first move is the narrow one**: drop `uptilMtime` and `attempts` from the return, and
let the diagnostic reader be written when a caller asks for it (`rule.prefer.wet-over-dry`).

## 🔴 .the split — the two fields are NOT one item

the dream's own test — *"is this field read by a production caller?"* — returns **no** for both, and
that is why it grouped them. it is the wrong test. the right one is:

> **does anything OBSERVE a property that matters?**

| field | what observes it | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| `uptilMtime` | 🔴 **naught.** not a production line, not an assertion, not a docblock claim past its own | **removed** |
| `attempts` | the yield's **invariant #5** — *only a promise the guard adjudicated may increment the attempt count* — asserted **7×** in `getSelfReviewChallengeDecision.test.ts` and 3× here | **kept** |

the three sharpest, and each pins a distinct clause of that invariant:

```
:94   expect(report?.attempts).toEqual(0);   // after an ask — the ask burns no attempt
:211  expect(report?.attempts).toEqual(1);   // after an adjudicated promise — and only then
:491  expect(report?.attempts).toEqual(0);   // per lane, under a fork — no cross-lane leak
```

⇒ a test IS a caller, and the property it holds is the one a leaked increment would break. **a
production grep cannot see that**, which is the whole reason the dream read the two as one.

## ✅ .what shipped

removed, with its subject: the `uptilMtime` field · the `fs.stat(uptilPath)` it required · the
`.uptil`-absent fallback branch that existed only to populate it · `uptilPath` from the destructure.

🟡 **the `.uptil` FILE is untouched and still carries the load** — its exclusive `wx` create is what
wins `firstAdjudication`. only its MTIME was dead.

🟡 **`[case4]` was RE-AIMED, never deleted.** it read *".uptil absent (graceful fallback)"* and
asserted `uptilMtime equals sinceMtime`. its **subject** — a `.uptil` that is absent, the ordinary
state of every fresh ask — is live; only the quantity it asserted about is gone. a case deleted with
a live subject is a coverage gap, whatever its old assertion said.

⚠️ **the transferable part: a CLEAN verdict grades RIPPLE, and ripple is observable.**

from a gate into a rationalization, and it fails in the expensive direction: toward a deferral
nobody re-examines.

## ⚠️ .the transferable part the dream got RIGHT

**a write-side repair leaves a read-side contract stale, and no test fails.** this round moved the
gate from a counter to an atomic claim — a real improvement — and the reader that served the counter
kept its old shape. ⇒ when a mechanism moves off a datum, **grep the datum's readers in the same
pass**; a field with no reader is invisible to every gate the suite has.
