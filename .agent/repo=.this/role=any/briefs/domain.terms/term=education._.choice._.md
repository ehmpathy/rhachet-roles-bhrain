## .what

**education** is a role's deliberate acquisition of lessons the domain's greats already paid for:
read the canon, test its claims in vitro, keep what holds as briefs and skills. it is the
boundary root of the `education.*` suite (`education.pursuit`, `education.pedigree`).

it is distinct from `learn`: `learn` retains the role's own lessons, one defect at a time;
`education` acquires others' lessons on purpose.
