## .settled

**one instruction to compress does not reach the floor. six did.** each rung found a *different* class
of defect, and no earlier rung could have found the later ones:

| the ask | what it exposed |
|---|---|
| *"not ruthless enough"* | slack inside sentences |
| *"still too many words"* | whole passages that restated their own tables |
| *"maximum density"* | sections that were archaeology, not answer |
| *"maximum directness"* | claims hedged behind qualifiers |
| *"zero fluff"* | two headings over one list |
| *"zero exceptions"* | the carve-outs I had granted myself |

⇒ **the author stops at the first rung that feels done, and it is never the floor.** the artifact went
615 → 210 → 155 lines across the ladder, and the largest structural cut — the ejection of the
groundwork to `appendix/` — came at rung three, not rung one.

⚠️ **each rung must be answered with a re-read, never a further trim of the last edit.** the defects
sat at different scales; a second pass over the same sentences would have found none of them.
