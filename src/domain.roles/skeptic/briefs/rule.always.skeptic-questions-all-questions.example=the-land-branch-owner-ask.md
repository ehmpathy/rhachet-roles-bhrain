# rule.always.skeptic-questions-all-questions.example=the-land-branch-owner-ask

> direction: outbound, a question that leaves the session. 2026-11-18, nheuron. a director put a question to the surdirector. the ask names no read. a read could have settled the owner part.

## .the shape, in any life

a clerk emails the whole floor, "whose lunch is in the fridge?" the bag has a name on it. what could I read myself before I spend a stranger's turn?

the case below: a director asked who owns two branches, when the owner was one lookup away and only the ci re-run was truly open.

## .the ask (inbox 2026-11-18, from the rhachet-efficiency director)

> the spawn-storm fire tree holds every cure item, 15 to 18, behind two unmerged land prs: nheuron #238 (land/main-2026-11-18-role-any) and #239 (land/main-2026-11-18-role-director), each open and mergeable since 2026-11-18, with its one ci check cancelled at 30m. per the pioneer exemption they merge on green ci, no review. who owns those land branches, so their ci re-runs green and they merge?

## .the gate, applied to the ask

| step | the ask | the skeptic's verdict |
|---|---|---|
| 1 name the read | none named | "who owns a branch" is a read: the ledger row of the tree that holds the branch, and `rhx git.pull.get` of each pr |
| 2 run it | no read shown | the ask carries no `read:` line, so the receiver cannot tell whether the lookup ran |
| 3 settled or open | the ask puts the lookup to the receiver | the part a read settles (the owner) goes back as a read; the part left open is the action: the ci re-run is refused for the robot (the receiver's answer on the entry) |
| 4 show the read | absent | the ask shows no `read: … · open: …` line |

the receiver's recorded answer (the entry's `why`): the land branches are the surdirector's own, a re-run is refused for the robot, and a fire-tree fallback stands. that answer was a lookup the asker could have made.

## .the cost

one turn of the surdirector spent on a lookup, on a day the fire tree held four cure items behind it.

## .the same ask, in the form the law owes

> read: `rhx git.pull.get` of #238 and #239, and the ledger row for the land branches · open: the ci re-run on each is refused for the robot, and no seat holds the right to re-run it. ask: which seat re-runs the two checks by 2026-11-19?

the owner lookup is gone from the ask; the open part is the one call a person holds.

## .the lesson, by law

- a question that names an owner is a lookup; a lookup is a read
- a question that shows its read lets the receiver spend the turn on the open part
- a brief alone did not hold the director; a Stop check was built to refuse a reply that asked the human with no `read:` line, and it left the role on 2026-11-26: it matched a question's shape and flagged honest asks as often as lazy ones
