# domain.term.reason: lapsed

## .etymology

*lapse* is to pass by, of time — a term or a right that ran out because the clock ran past
it. it names an age, which is what the cache reach measures, and it was already the meter's
word (`cold (lapsed Nm)`), read by every driver who opened `rhx review._.cost --of tree`.

## .disputes

### dispute: lapsed (prime meet outcome) — raised 2026-11-01 — status: RESOLVED (split)

- raised.by  = the `fix-review-input-cacheability` vision, in its walk of the meter
- claim      = the prime meet's `ReviewPrefixPrimeOutcome` already spells `lapsed`: the lane's
               deadline left the meet no time. a pair verdict named `lapsed` would put one word
               over two concepts (`rule.forbid.domain-term-ambiguity`)
- counter    = the two senses differ by concept, not by boundary — one clock is a lane's
               deadline, the other the cache reach — so a split is owed, not a qualifier
- resolution = the cache sense keeps `lapsed`: it is the published one, rendered in stdout and
               read by drivers. the meet outcome is renamed `expired`. at the time of the rename
               no artifact or stdout carried it, so the rename moved no stored byte; the meet
               now rides `metrics.realized.json` and every breadth of `review._.cost`, under
               `expired` from its first write (`ReviewRunMeet`, `term=prompt.cache.meet`)

## .evidence

- the rename landed before the meet outcome reached any disk or render, so no stored run
  carries the old word
- the meter's `cold (lapsed Nm)` is a published stdout surface since the cache grade landed
