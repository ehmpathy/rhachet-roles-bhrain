# wish: the reviewed? judge passes an exhausted lane once every concern is absorbed and conceded better

## .the defect

a peer review lane that is exhausted (its budget spent), with every concern absorbed and every concession graded `--severity better`, still counts its blockers in the stone-wide tally. the `reviewed?` judge then halts the stone with no lever left for the driver.

the measured halt, from the bhrain clamp tree `rhachet-roles-bhrain.beav.fix-clamp-role-boot-budgets` (bhrain 0.42.1):

```
passage = blocked · reason = blockers exceed threshold (3 > 0)
j1: rhachet run --repo bhrain --skill route.stone.judge --mechanism reviewed? --stone $stone --route $route --allow-blockers 0 --allow-nitpicks 7 · finished 3.3s ✗
```

- the 3 blockers: r8 behavior-intent-coverage blocker.1, r9 ergo-friction-hazards blocker.1 and blocker.2
- r8 and r9 are both exhausted at 3/3
- every concern on them is conceded `--severity better` and absorbed; 0 `.taken` owed
- r1 to r7 are approved with 0 blockers

## .the contract the judge must hold

per `define.invariant.review.peer.exhausted` and `define.invariant.review.peer.budget.urgent-earns-budget`:

- exhaustion unlocks the level; it does not discharge the debt. once the debt IS discharged (every concern absorbed), a `better` concession is maintenance, and the floor is good enough
- so: an exhausted lane + every concern absorbed + every concession `better` + no live `urgent` = that lane's concerns do not count toward the stone-wide tally, the same way a disputed concern is shed
- a live `urgent` concession on an exhausted lane still halts, and the halt names the driver's lever: `rhx route.guard.budget --for review --add N --peer <lane> --stone <stone>`
- an unabsorbed concern still holds the stone, exactly as today

## .done

1. a clamp built from the halt above (r8 blocker.1, r9 blocker.1 and blocker.2, both exhausted, all absorbed and conceded better): red before the fix with `blockers exceed threshold (3 > 0)`, green after
2. a clamp where one concession on an exhausted lane is `urgent`: the judge still halts, and the halt names `route.guard.budget`
3. a clamp where a concern on an exhausted lane is NOT absorbed: the judge still halts
4. a PR on ehmpathy/rhachet-roles-bhrain, CI green, ready for the commander's review. no merge, no `--apply`

## .bounds

- fix at the source of the tally; the suspect to read first is `guard/judge/computeStoneGuardExhaustedApprovalBypass` and wherever the judge sums blockers per lane
- do not raise `--allow-blockers`, mute a lane, or narrow a lane's paths to pass; the judge contract is the cure
- a constraint given (e.g. a depth refusal) tallied as a blocker is a separate defect; leave it out of this tree unless the same line of code carries both
