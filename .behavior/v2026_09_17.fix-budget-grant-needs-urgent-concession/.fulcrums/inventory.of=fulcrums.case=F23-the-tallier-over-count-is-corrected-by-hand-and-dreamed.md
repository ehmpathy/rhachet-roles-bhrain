## ⚠️ .why it is not 93%

**the miscount is safe in one direction only, and that is what makes A defensible** — an over-count
can hold a stone that should pass; it cannot pass a stone that should be held. so no artifact on this
route is falsely green.

the counter, and a council may fairly take it: **the route's own record now disagrees with itself.**
the guard's output says 6 and this board says 3, and a later reader who trusts the guard will
re-derive naught — the walk's stdout is the more authoritative-looking of the two. ⇒ the correction
lives only in prose, which is exactly the shape `rule.always.archive-the-wishers-words-verbatim`
distrusts.

🟡 **and the boundary is argued rather than obvious.** `rule.require.review-test-changes` forbids the
smuggle; a reviewer may fairly hold that a **harness** defect found during verification is precisely
what a verification stone exists to surface, and that the surface is its own deliverable.
