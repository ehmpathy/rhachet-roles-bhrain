# rule.always.trust-no-one-especially-not-yourself.example=the-first-thought-on-skill-mods

> 2026-11-18, nheuron. two first thoughts, one hour apart. each one was wrong. each one had a read that was one call away.

## .the shape, in any life

a traveler is sure the shop shuts at five, from a visit years ago. he skips it, and the sign on the door says seven. what is the cheapest look that would test this limit?

the case below: two limits stated at once ("out of reach", "it refuses"), and each one call of a read would have tested them.

## .case one: the pnpm-patch style skill mods

the human asked whether a task must be dispatched to teach rhachet to mod a consumed role's skills, pnpm-patch style (nheuron inbox `2026-11-18`; a question, not a seed, so its words stay in the inbox).

| step | the record |
|---|---|
| first thought | an "I do not know" answer: the seat held no read of whether rhachet supports a mod of a consumed role |
| the failure it assumed | that the answer was out of reach |
| the read | `rhx radio.task.pull --via gh.issues --from ehmpathy/rhachet --exid 702` returned the title: `feat(roles): mod a consumed role in place, via .agent/repo=.mods` |
| what the read settled | the feature is already an open issue. the answer to "do we need a new task" is "no new task; cite #702, and check whether its scope covers a patch of a skill file" |

the first thought stated a limit that was a choice not to read. one call named the issue.

## .case two: the grepsafe refusal

the first thought: "grepsafe refuses a path under `.agent/repo=.this`" (the text of a guard in `goal.tree`). the failure it assumed: that the guard text describes the tool as it is today. the read: `rhx grepsafe --pattern 'role=pioneer' --path .agent/repo=.this/role=pioneer/briefs` exited 0. see `rule.always.trust-but-verify.example=the-stale-grepsafe-guard.md`.

## .the cost

two answers that would have shipped a false limit to the human: one a dispatch of a duplicate task, one a guard kept that refuses a correct check.

## .the lesson, by law

- a first thought of the form "it is out of reach" or "it refuses" is the cheapest to test and the most often wrong
- the two cases share a shape: a limit stated, no read of the limit. the read took one call each
