## .settled

**two rulings, and the first is a correction of a drift inside a settled record.**

### the pair is `tree` | `grove`, and it stayed `fleet` in the places that matter

`grove` was settled two rounds earlier. it was then written into a fulcrum's *reasoning* and
**not** into that same fulcrum's *verdict*, which kept `fleet` — and every downstream artifact
inherited the verdict rather than the reasoning.

⇒ the surviving set:

```
review.cost.tree     # this tree's realized spend
review.cost.grove    # every tree's realized spend
```

🔴 the lesson is not the word. it is that **a record can contradict itself and still read as
settled**: one file argued `grove` at length, concluded `fleet`, and no reader caught it,
including the author. a verdict line is what downstream copies, so a verdict that drifts from
its own argument propagates unchecked.

### `review.corpus` is removed, not repaired

the fork was open at 65% for deletion. ruled: **delete**.

the reasons that already stood, now binding:

- it reports a figure that is **no longer true** — it sums every new-file diff unconditionally
  and never reads the decision the renderer makes, so its headline describes a prompt this
  branch stopped sending
- the drift **will recur**: it re-derives what the renderer decides, and nothing forces the
  pair to move together
- the capability it uniquely held — a cold-branch, pre-run estimate — has no demonstrated
  consumer

⇒ and the real gap it papered over stays open and stays honest: *"across this tree, what share
of chars is target files?"* wants a roll-up of measured data, never an estimate from declared
globs.
