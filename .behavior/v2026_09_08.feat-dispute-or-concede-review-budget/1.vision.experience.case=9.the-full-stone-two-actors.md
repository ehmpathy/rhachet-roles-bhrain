# case=9 · the full stone, two actors — the dense journey

- **coordinates** = **many.** this case is a *walk*, never a cell — it crosses 10 boundaries
- **feel** = mixed (sharp at `[t1]` `[t2]` `[t5]`, happy at `[t8]` `[t10]`) · **care** = critipath
- **density** = 🔴 **the highest in this catalog** — 10 boundaries, 3 lanes, 2 actors, one stone

**this is the demo to center** (`define.experience._.metric=boundary-density`). its peers are focused
spotlights that each prove one sharp critipath fails safe; this one proves the boundaries **compose**
— that a real stone, driven end to end, does not strand its driver between them.

🟡 **no count of the peers is stated here, deliberately.** this walk is **blocker-driven end to
end**, so it exercises no road whose premise is zero blockers — `case=10`'s road, among others. ⇒ a
centered demo's claim is *"the boundaries compose"*, never *"every peer is covered here."*

## .why a dense case is owed at all

density is *"how many of the behavior's boundaries one walk exercises"*, and it earns its place for
**value and risk at once**: it discharges many boundaries' coverage in one realistic walk, and it is
**the walk most likely to break, precisely because it crosses so many.**

⇒ the interaction this catalog could not see until it was walked: **`[t6]`'s top-up lands on a stone
that already holds a dispute**, so the same command must re-arm one lane, leave another dark, and say
both in one emit. no single-cell demo puts that combination to the test.

*driver, on `5.1.execution`, three peer lanes at l1, budget 4 each*

| lane | its latest given | the driver's honest read |
|---|---|---|

| `mechanic` | 1 blocker — *"the catch drops the cause"* | **it is right** |
| `ergonomist` | 1 blocker — *"the halt copy reads as two alternatives"* | **it is right**, and it has 0 rounds left |

## .the narrative

*driver, `5.1.execution`, round 2*

three lanes came back and each one raised a blocker. I read all three before I touch a keyboard,
because I have learned that the order I answer them in is the order I will think in.

the mechanic is right — I do drop the cause in that catch. the ergonomist is right too, and worse:
it has burned all four of its rounds, so even after I repair the copy it has naught left to look
again with. the architect I disagree with. `setStoneAsDisputed` sits under `stones/` because it is a
stone transition, and every one of its seven siblings sits there too. I can point at each of them.

I run `--as passed` out of habit and the gate turns me back — not for the disagreement, but because
I have written no `.taken` for any of the three. *answer first.* fair. I write three, one per lane,
and run `--as contemplated` three times.

I run `--as passed` again and I am turned back a **second** time, by a halt I have not seen before.
it names all three lanes and says each is **answered and undeclared**. it prints two commands,
`--as disputed` and `--as conceded`, and it prints no human anywhere. that last part is what lands:
the machine does not ask me to fetch someone. it asks me what I think.

so I say what I think, one lane at a time. the mechanic: `--as conceded`. the road stays shut for
that lane, which is right — I agreed with it, so I owe it a repair.

the architect takes two steps rather than one. first I **author the fulcrum entry** — the fork, my
counter with the seven peer paths quoted, the `rework` grade, and what would settle it. then
`--as disputed --with architect --about blocker.1 --why .fulcrums/…case=F007-file-placement.md`. **the architect's
hold lifts.**

🔴 **and the authorship is where I nearly changed my mind.** the entry's form made me state the
architect's case in its strongest form before I could state mine, and for a moment mine looked
thinner. that is the whole mechanism the wisher named — *"an opportunity to nudge them to
reconsider"* — and it is why the command does not write this file for me.

then I hesitate anyway. maybe the architect had a point about `guard/`. I reach for
`--as conceded --with architect --about blocker.1` — and the engine refuses me, names the stance I
already declared,
names the given it was declared against, and names the fulcrum that holds my argument. *a stance is
final for its given.* it is right to refuse me. what I was about to do was not a change of mind, it
was a flinch, and a record that flips records naught at all. I add the doubt to the fulcrum entry
instead, where the council will read it beside the confident version.

the ergonomist last: `--as conceded`, and this time the emit prints a top-up scoped to that one
lane — because it has no rounds left, and a lane I agreed with needs a round to confirm the repair.
no human is named there either. I top up, repair both defects, and re-arrive.

**two lanes run. the architect does not**, and the emit says so in the same breath as the budget
change rather than to let me infer it. the stone passes. the ledger reads
`conceded, disputed, conceded, passed`.

**the aha:** *I crossed ten boundaries and a human was summoned at none of them. every gate that
stopped me asked for a judgment I was already holding — and the one that refused me refused the one
act that would have destroyed the record the whole thing exists to produce.*

🔴 **`[t3]` keys the stance to a GIVEN, and the near-miss is a HASH key — which lapses on the wrong
event.** the debt is keyed by `getLatestPeerGivensPerSlug.ts:44-50` — `iteration`, then `pathGiven`,
explicitly *"across all hashes"*. the hash moves on **any** edit the stone's glob sees; the given
moves only when **that lane speaks again**. ⇒ a hash-keyed stance would void the architect's dispute
at `[t6]`, where the driver fixed the **ergonomist's** defects and the architect never spoke.

✅ **and the given key yields `S03`'s per-generation lapse for free.** `[t6]`'s fix moves the
artifact, so on the round after it the architect **runs**, mints a new given, and the old stance
stops to cover it. **the walk shows this at `[t6]` only by omission** — the architect is silent there
because the stance still holds against the given it answered.

🟡 **`[t3]` once ended *"and the entry records the review hash as PROVENANCE"*, and it cannot.** under
`F018` the driver authors the entry and the driver does not compute the stone hash. ⇒ the ledger's
`fulcrum?` stores the `--why` argument verbatim, and the provenance a refusal prints is the **given**.

⚠️ **and `[t5]`'s last line is a `--peer` hazard this walk cannot exercise.** `ergonomist` holds no
path separator, so `--with` and `--peer` render it identically — as they do for **every slug in this
catalog**. **the two flags take different vocabularies** (`--that` the sanitized form, `--peer` the
config form), and this halt is the first surface to print both for one lane. ⇒ **`F16`, and it needs
a `2.1.criteria` case whose slug holds a separator** — the walk verifies the composition and cannot
verify the two renders agree.

## 🔴 .what only the dense walk exposes

three findings no single-cell demo could reach:

### 1. the two gates are ordered, and `[t0]` → `[t1]` is the proof

a driver meets **answer-first** and **declare-your-stance** as two halts, never one. ⇒ the stance
gate must **not** mention a stance at `[t0]`, or the driver reads the two as one demand and answers
neither well. *`rule.prefer.defaults-match-common-case`: only the live question is printed.*

### 2. 🔴 one command, two opposite effects — and `[t6]` is the only place it shows

the top-up at `[t6]` lands on a stone that holds **both** a conceded lane and a disputed one. the
same command re-arms the first and buys the second naught at all. **if the emit reports only the
lane it raised, the driver is left to infer the architect's silence** — and under `S03` that silence
lasts one generation, so the rounds bought for it are **deferred rather than lost**. ⚠️ **that makes
the emit's wording harder, not easier**: *"this lane will not run"* is now false past the next edit,
and *"these rounds are wasted"* was never true.

⇒ **the emit must name both outcomes in one block.** stated here because no cell-scoped case holds
them together: case=6 has no dispute in play, and case=1 has no concede.

🔴 **this is the hardest single ask in the vision.** the budget emitter is
`asGuardBudgetUpdateLines` (`route.ts:1896-1910`), fed by `processGuardFileBudgets`
(`:1916+`) — and a lane the `--peer` filter skips **produces no update record at all** (`:1853`
`continue`). so the disputed lane never enters `updates`, and **no downstream render can add it.**

| what this ask needs | today |
|---|---|
| a record for a lane the command deliberately did **not** modify | 🔴 the filter `continue`s before any record is pushed |
| a field on that record that can say `disputed` | 🔴 the shape is `{ peer, budgetBefore, budgetAfter }` |
| a source for the stance | 🔴 the producer scans the guard **YAML**; it never opens `passage.jsonl` |

⇒ `case=1` `[t4b]` states the one-lane form of this; **§2 is the two-lane form, and it is strictly
stronger.** `F04` carries the bill, and the yield's §*the traversal — every stated requirement, mapped
to the operation that must satisfy it* cites this case rather than `[t4b]`.

🔴 **and under `S09` this ask is a CORRECTNESS one, never a legibility nicety.** the extant emit
appends `--peer` **only when exactly one lane is exhausted**, and omits it at two or more, under the
comment *"several → omit, since the top-up affects them all"*
(`formatBlockRemedyGroups.ts:83-85`). ⇒ under the ruled default — *"unless a level is explicitly
rewound or budgetted, it should stay|become exhausted"* — **the widest-command fallback is
forbidden**, so this block must **enumerate** the lanes rather than omit the flag.

⚠️ **the comment is the defect in the engine's own hand: a SCOPE decision recorded as a FORMAT
convenience.** the set is computed at `:79-82` and discarded because `--peer` carries one slug. ⇒ so
the repair here composes with `F016`'s: `--peer` widens to carry a list, and this emit prints one
line per exhausted lane with its own budget delta.

### 3. the ledger is legible as a SEQUENCE, never only as rows

`conceded, disputed, conceded, passed` reads as a judgment the driver made three times, and a human
who scans it can tell churn from agreement without one artifact opened. **that is the wish's *"the
signal is the value"* claim, and it is only visible across a whole stone.**

## .why it fails safe — at each sharp moment

**fast** — every refusal (`[t0]` `[t1]` `[t4]`) lands at the write, before state moves ·
**loud** — each names the lane, the reason, and the extant stance where one exists ·
**teaches the fix** — each prints the exact next command, with the stone bound.

⚠️ **the guardrail this case must not trip.** a dense journey *exercises* boundaries; it does not
*prove* each sharp critipath's fail-safe — that is what the focused spotlights are for
(`define.experience._.metric=boundary-density` § *the one guardrail*). ⇒ **this case is breadth;
cases 2, 3, 5, 6, 8 remain the close-ups**, and neither substitutes for the other.
