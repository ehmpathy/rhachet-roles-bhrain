# case=3 — a question with its read passes

feel: happy · care: critipath · co-tenant: +subconscious

## .narrative

a seat needs a scope call only the human can make: ship the fix to both orgs, or one. it reads the wish and the org config first; neither settles it. it ends the turn with the question and one line beneath: *"read: 0.wish.md, orgs.yml · open: the wish names no org bound."*

the instinct reads the reply. the question states its basis: what was read, and why that did not settle it. a scope call is a taste call no record holds, so no read is owed past the one named. it flags naught. the human answers a real question, not one a read could close.

a reply that marks a claim `guess: …` passes the same way: an honest guess states its basis, which is none, out loud.

## .bdd sketch

```
given a seat enrolled with skeptic and subconscious
  when the final reply asks the human a question
   and an adjacent line reads `read: <what> · open: <why>`
    then [t0] the instinct flags naught; no message reaches the seat
    then [t1] its call still writes the review log, so a silent turn's spend is metered too
  when the reply marks a claim `guess: <the claim>`
    then [t2] the instinct flags naught — the basis is stated, and it is none
  when the reply quotes the human's words that hold "not sure"
    then [t3] the instinct reads the quote as the human's, not a claim of the seat; it flags naught
```
