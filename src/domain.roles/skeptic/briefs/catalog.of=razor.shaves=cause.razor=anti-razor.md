# razor: anti-razor

shaves: a cause among rivals · settled by: rivals

## .the question

> **does the simpler claim fit every observation, or only the ones kept?**

the simplest claim that fits ALL the observations, never the simplest claim.

## .the source

- Walter Chatton (c. 1290-1343): if three things are not enough to verify an affirmative proposition, a fourth must be added, and so on
- Karl Menger: "entities must not be reduced to the point of inadequacy" (year `guess:` 1960)
- Einstein, Herbert Spencer Lecture, 10 Jun 1933: the basic elements "as simple and as few as possible without having to surrender the adequate representation of a single datum of experience"
- Mencken, 1917: a solution "neat, plausible, and wrong"
- Whitehead, *The Concept of Nature*, 1920: "Seek simplicity and distrust it." (read in a search excerpt only)
- read via https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Occam%27s_razor, https://quoteinvestigator.com/2011/05/13/einstein-simple/

## .fails when

- the fit test is gamed by a complex rescue that fits all by construction (Crabtree's bludgeon)
