# domain.term.reason: education.pursuit

## .etymology

the act of education, named as an act: one pursues an education. the term pairs with
`education.pedigree`: the pursuit is the activity, the pedigree is its record. that split
settled the suite's two faces (seed S6, fulcrum F2).

rejected:
- `degree`, `program`, `curriculum`, `learnership` — bundle nouns that name an institution's
  offer or a credential, never the activity; no bundle noun enters the contract
- `course` as the opener — a course is the unit inside a pursuit, one subject each

## .evidence

- a pursuit holds one or many courses, in order, each built on the last (seeds S5, S7)
- a pursuit is a stack of routes: plot, then one child route per course, then a review that
  either appends courses or graduates (seed S8)
- dimensional walk: a set of bounded, canon-sourced courses is the cell a pursuit fills; it adds
  the order and the aim a lone course lacks

## .invariants

- a course exists only within a pursuit
- one pursuit educates one role
- a pursuit graduates only once every course in it has exited
- a pursuit graduated on a date is never reopened; a later pursuit of the same slug opens a new
  dated route
