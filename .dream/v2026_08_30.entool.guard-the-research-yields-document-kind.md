# dream: a guard that refuses a research yield with no document kind

**kind**: entool · **owner**: this repo (`rhachet-roles-bhrain`) · **caught** 2026-08-30

## .the cue that fired

`rule.require.classify-the-yields-document-kind._.md` was written this round and states its own
bound: it is **taught** at `3.3.probes.absorb.gaps`, never guarded. that is correct for the value
it protects — a guard fires at yield time, after the probes close, so it can never catch a
**missed** kind.

⇒ but *"never guarded"* leaves the floor unenforced too, and the floor is cheap.

## .the gap, precisely

a research route can reach `5.briefs.curate` with briefs whose filenames carry no kind prefix, and
no artifact refuses it. the rule is present in the researcher's boot context and that is the whole
of its enforcement — a d≈0.05 intervention by this repo's own cited research
(`research.selfreview-effectiveness`).

## .the shape of the fix

a guard on the curate stone that greps the planned brief names for one of the five prefixes
(`whento` · `howto` · `howdoes` · `hazard` · `rule`) and blocks on an unprefixed name.

🟡 **it grades PRESENCE, never correctness** — and the rule must keep that caveat, or a green guard
reads as a classified set when it only proves each name starts with a legal word.

🟡 **and the prefix set is open** (`define`, `ref` are live elsewhere), so the guard reads its list
from the rule rather than hardcodes five strings.

## .why it is not done in this round

**SAFE: yes. CLEAN: no.** the bound wish is briefs-only — *no new skills, no new roles* — and a
route guard is a skill artifact plus a stone edit. it also needs the prefix list to be readable
from the rule, which is a contract that does not exist yet.

## .the fulcrum

> **the classify rule ships as a lesson with no floor beneath it.**
> rework: **clean** — the guard is additive, and the rule already declares where it would sit.
