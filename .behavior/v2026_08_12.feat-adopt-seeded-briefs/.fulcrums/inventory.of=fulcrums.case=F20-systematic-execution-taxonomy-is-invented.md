## .why the confidence is low — the wisher gave one clause, i wrote a model

the ask, verbatim:

> *"achiever should be enbriefed to always enumerate tasks from asks and **execute each of them
> systemically**"*

the first half — enumerate — was already a rule. **the second half is four words, and each item
below is mine:**

| what i invented | the wisher's words for it |
|---|---|
| the three drops — abandoned tail · absorbed remainder · new-ground jump | none |
| three terminal states — `completed` · `declined` · `deferred` | none |
| `declined` owes a reason; `deferred` owes a *when* | none |
| one `in_progress` at a time | none |

⇒ **the claim is right and the decomposition is a guess.** *"systemically"* could equally have
meant *in dependency order*, or *without a context switch*, or simply *finish them*.

## .the evidence FOR the model, and it is real

**i violated the new-ground-jump drop within the same session i wrote it.** the wisher asked *"and
did you complete all your tasks?"* and the answer was **3 of 9** — each new ask itemized and
immediately worked over an open list, exactly as the drop describes.

⇒ that is a worked case with a measured count, on the author, from the hour of authorship. it does
**not** validate the other two drops, and it does not validate the terminal-state model at all.

## ⚠️ .and the model's own floor is unenforceable

the parent closes with *"the mechanism is yours"*, and the only mechanism a clone reaches for is
the native task list — which **cannot outlive the session**. so `deferred` decays to `dropped` with
no trace, which is the exact defect the zoom-in forbids.

⇒ caught as `.dream/v2026_08_30.entool.task-object-persists-beside-the-native-list.md`, dispatched
as **#395**. **until that lands, the rule states a floor it cannot hold.**

## .what would overturn it

- the wisher says *systemically* meant one of the other senses → the drops re-frame, the file stays
- two of the three drops never fire in practice → fold the survivor into the parent and delete the
  zoom-in
- #395 lands and the states it declares differ from these three → conform the brief to the object
