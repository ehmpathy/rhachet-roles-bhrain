## .the gap, precisely

`rule.forbid.emphasis-noise` parts a mark that RANKS from one that CLASSIFIES by two properties,
and on this shape the two disagree:

```
⇒ **the gap was recorded first, and closed second.** an earlier pass left this cluster flat …
```

| the rule's test | its verdict here |
|---|---|
| **position** — at a line's LEAD → classifies | ✅ classifies. the bold opens the line |
| **extent** — spans its WHOLE container → classifies | 🔴 ranks. it spans the first sentence, never the line |

the rule states both and never says which wins, so two reviewers who read the same line reach
opposite verdicts — and a reviewer who takes the extent test alone will order a strip that
manufactures a narration hit.
