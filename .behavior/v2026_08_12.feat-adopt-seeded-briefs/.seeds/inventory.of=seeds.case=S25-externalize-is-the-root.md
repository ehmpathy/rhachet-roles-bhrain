## .settled

**the family's boundary is `externalize`, and `externalize` reaches the root directly.** so the five
flat clusters take their full ancestry in one segment:

```
term=externalize.enbrief   term=externalize.enskill   term=externalize.entool
term=externalize.pave      term=externalize.learn
```

⇒ the rejected candidate was `knowledge.` — *what the artifact holds*. it loses because **the family
is a family of VERBS.** `enbrief`, `enskill`, `entool`, `pave`, and `learn` each name an **act**;
`knowledge` names the substance an act operates on, so it is the boundary of the artifacts these
verbs produce, never of the verbs themselves.

**and `seed` is not in the family.** it is a noun and it is already settled as `artifact.seed` — the
row that listed it alongside the five read the `en-`-adjacency rather than each term's own declared
sense, which is the exact error `rule.require.boundary-qualified-terms` warns against.

⚠️ **`externalize` is a root, so it takes no ancestor of its own.** `repo` is the universal ancestor
and is dropped from every path, which makes `externalize.$verb` the complete chain rather than a
truncation.

## ⚠️ .the sweep stops at the LIVE briefs — a dated record keeps the path it cited

a rename of five clusters leaves citations behind it, and they split into two kinds that want
opposite treatment:

| the citation sits in | treatment | why |
|---|---|---|
| a **live brief** — a rule, a catalog, a readme, a dream still owed | **rewritten** | it is read as a pointer. a pointer that resolves to no file is a phantom path, and `rule.always.reuse-pavement-before-improvise` grades that a blocker |
| a **dated record** — a seed, a fulcrum, a stone yield | **left as it stood** | it is read as testimony of a moment. to rewrite it is to edit what was said, which is the defect `rule.always.archive-the-wishers-words-verbatim` forbids outright |

⇒ so `.seeds/…case=S04`, `S05`, `S06`, `S16`, `S22`, `.fulcrums/…case=F13`, `1.vision.yield.md`,
and `1.vision.yield.arrears.md` still name `term=enbrief`, `term=enskill`, `term=entool`, and
`term=pave`. **that is correct, and this row is what makes it legible** — without it the set reads
as a cluster of phantom paths the sweep missed.

⚠️ **a glob over one tree is not a sweep.** the citation pass ran over
`.agent/repo=.this/role=any/briefs/**/*.md`, which reaches neither `.dream/` nor `src/`, so one
live dream kept a stale pointer until it was caught by hand. **enumerate the trees a rename
touches before you glob one of them.**
