# 🌙 dream: a `--conversation` bind needs a depth bound

- kind  = reseed
- owner = `ehmpathy/rhachet-roles-bhrain` (the engine half)
- caught = 2026-09-17

## .the cue that fired

three peer lanes on stone `5.1.execution.from_vision` returned `unreadable — no numeric count found`,
at 75.2–75.4% against a 75.0% gate. decomposed:

| term | tokens | grows? |
|---|---|---|
| rules | 0.5k – 14.8k | no |
| targets | 339.6k | with the diff |
| 🔴 conversation | ≈436.2k | every round, without bound |

⇒ **the term that dominates the prompt is the one nobody chose the size of.**

## .the gap, precisely

`enumRouteGuardReviewPeerConversationFiles` unions every prior generation of `.given` + `.taken` on
the stone, and `rhx review` has no depth flag. a guard chooses **whether** a lane threads the
conversation, never how much.

the same cliff took a different lane eleven rounds earlier (758.1k against a 750k gate, 398.9k of it
conversation) — the signature of an unbounded term, never a mis-sized bind.

| lever | why it falls short |
|---|---|
| narrow `--paths-with` / `--paths-wout` | a one-time cut against unbounded growth |
| trim `--refs` | the refs are that lane's subject |
| drop `--conversation` | closes it, and costs the lane every argument answered back to it |
| a hand-run `rhx review` | ⛔ `rule.forbid.hand-run-reviews` |

## .the shape of the fix

```
--conversation <files> --conversation-depth <n>     # the last n generations only
```

or a `$conversation.recent(n)` expansion the guard can ask for.

- **n = 2 or 3.** a reviewer re-raises what it was never told, and it is told in the `.taken` of the
  round that answered it — so the live debt sits in the recent generations
- **per lane, never global.** a judgment lane wants depth; a coverage lane wants none
- **the counter-case:** a truncated conversation can drop a settled argument, and the reviewer
  re-raises it. a real cost on both arms — carried as fulcrum `F25`

## .why it is not done in this round

- **SAFE — no.** it changes `rhx review`'s public flags and the guard expansion contract, used by
  every adopter's routes
- **CLEAN — no.** `parseReviewArgs`, `stepReview`, the `$conversation` expansion in
  `runStoneGuardReviews`, bhuild's guard template, and every extant `.guard`
