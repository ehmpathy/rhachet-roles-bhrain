## .what
**reflect** is **the upstream issue a sure misread owes, as `owner/repo#n`.** a dispute on `wrong`
ground at ≥ 0.90 confidence names the reviewer, not the artifact, as the defect; the fix is to the
reviewer's rubric upstream, so the dispute owes that issue instead of a human verdict.

it rides in two places that must match: the fulcrum's `reflect:` field, and `--reflect` on
`rhx route.stone.set --as disputed`.
