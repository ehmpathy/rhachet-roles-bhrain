# 1.vision — dense role boots, clamped by a declared budget

## the outcome world

a director seat boots learner, driver, and librarian at about half today's tokens (66.6k → ~30–35k). every rule
still boots. every cue still fires at the act. one line per role says that each `X.md.min`
condenses the `X.md` beside it, and that file holds the argument the min no longer carries. each role's `boot.yml`
declares `budget: tokens:`, and the next edit that bloats a min turns the clamp test red, in the
author's run and in ci, with the fix printed. a seat links each role from `dist`, so its own boot
warns rather than halts.

### before / after (o200k_base, `npx rhachet roles cost --all`, this branch, 2026-10-21)

| role | before | proposed budget | cue share (measured) | cue-only floor | expected after |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| learner | 26,846 | 12,000 | 52% | ~14.3k | ~12–14k |
| driver | 25,672 | 12,000 | 47% | ~12.9k | ~11–13k |
| librarian | 14,116 | 5,000 | ~65% (rules 53%, `kno*` 70%) | ~8.3k | ~7–8k (F5) |
| **sum** | **66,634** | 29,000 | — | ~35.5k | ~30–35k |

🟡 **realized, at 5.1: learner 20,502 · driver 19,848 · librarian 11,912 = 52,262**, a 22% cut,
not the half this table expected. every argument-only section has moved out, with every cue
intact. the floor column overstates how far a move reaches: it counts each prose line as argument,
and most resident prose lines are one-line claims and tests, cues the markup heuristic cannot see.
so the rest needs a tighten of cue rows, deferred to its own pr so a cut cannot hide among
reworded rows (case=5, F10). each budget sits at what its role reached, not at its proposal.

the cue share is measured by `rhx census.cues` (paved this round, `.agent/repo=.this/role=any/skills/`).
it uses `rule.prefer.rules-that-refout`'s line classifier and counts only the booted say entries.
the floor scales chars/4 to o200k at each role's own ratio, plus its readme and chrome. **the floor
is what a pure say→ref move reaches. it is above the proposal for learner and driver.** the gap
to 12k is closed by tighter phrase choice inside the cue lines (dense, never cut), plus F6's
moves. that is ~15% of the learner's cue text and ~7% of the driver's. librarian cannot reach 5k
unless its ontology articles leave the boot (F5).

the wish cites different "before" figures (26,042 · 22,864 · 9,651). today's measure is the one
the clamp checks (F4).

### the aha

the argument already has a ref home. every learner say entry, and all but two driver say entries,
is a `.md` + `.md.min` pair, and both ship in `dist`. the min is the say tier; the `.md` beside it
is the ref. so the work is to **rewrite each min down to its cue**, plus one convention line per
role (F7). there are no new files to coin. the halt that `roles boot` already raises over budget is the clamp.

🟡 a min is a hand-made condensation of its `.md`, not a generated slice. two samples hold (each
min line has a source line in its `.md`), but the rest are unproven. so before a line leaves a min,
the execution confirms that its `.md` carries it, and moves it into the `.md` where it does not.

## the contract

per rule (a `.md.min` at say):

```md
# rule.$directive.$topic

> the claim, one or two lines.

the test, as a question.

| when… | then… |          ← every row kept
|---|---|

blocker: … · nitpick: … · false positive: …   ← kept verbatim

⇒ see also: …                                    ← kept as is
```

per role, once, in a say-level doc (the readme where one boots, F7):

```md
each `X.md.min` here condenses the `X.md` beside it. read the `.md` for the argument.
```

two moves, in order. **move** the argument out, which reaches the floor. then **tighten** the cue
lines that stay: a shorter row, one claim phrased once, a table cell trimmed to its noun. tighten
never removes a row, a test, or an enforcement clause, and the case=6 census proves it.

| stays in the min (cue) | moves out, already present in the `.md` (argument) |
|---|---|
| claim · test · every when/then row · shapes table · enforcement lines | etymology · measured cases and their dates · counter-arguments · long 👎/👍 demos past the first · "why this rule exists" sections · history of prior drafts |

per role (`boot.yml`):

```yaml
budget:
  # proposed <n>, reached <m>. <why, if they differ>
  tokens: <n where m ≤ n; else the least multiple of 250 strictly above m>
```

headroom is a limit boundary (case=7 `[t2]`). the budget is a clamp, not a target to fill. a
thin headroom means the first edit after the pr halts every boot here. the halt prints its own
fix, which is safe when the new tokens are argument. when they are a new cue row, every printed fix
would cut a cue, and the safe move is case=5 instead: raise the budget and say why. either way it
is friction. so if a role reaches under its proposal, the budget is the proposal, and the gap is
its headroom.

per tool-enforced rule (F6): a rule moves to ref only where the tool's own halt prints its cue
at the act, and the boot.yml comment names the tool. verified: learner `rule.forbid.native-memory`,
which `memory.guard` halts and redirects. refused: `rule.always.concede-with-a-severity`. the tool
demands `--severity` but does not carry the harm test that picks it.

per repo, once (F8): an integration test snapshots each role's booted cue lines, with the same
classifier `rhx census.cues` uses. the budget sees growth; the snapshot sees a cut. a cue dropped
or a rule deleted in any later pr is a snapshot diff a reviewer must read. the same test asserts
each budget is declared and runs the real `roles boot` under it, snaps each `not` list, and checks
every booted min has its `.md` beside it. it does not check that a min's cue matches its `.md`:
76% of min lines are paraphrases of the `.md`, so a verbatim check would fail on day one (F8).
that drift is reported by `rhx census.minpeer`, never gated; the gate is caught as a dream.

per subdomain: the driver's six `define.invariant.review.peer.*` files already sit at ref behind
the say-level gates map, which is the catalog shape. the librarian's unlisted briefs (467 tokens
of `<also>`) go under `not` (`rule.always.boot-an-oversized-subdomain-as-its-catalog`).

## mental model

- **to a friend:** *"the boot carries the index card for each rule. the full essay is in the drawer
  beside it, labelled, and nobody reads the essay unless they argue."*
- **metaphor:** a field manual and its reference volume. the manual says *do this when that*; the
  reference says *why, and how we learned it*.
- **their words vs ours:** they say *shorter briefs* and *cheaper boot*; we say *cue at say,
  argument at ref*, *budget*, *payload*.

## evaluation

| goal (wish) | how this meets it |
|---|---|
| 1. budget declared, cost under it | `budget.tokens` per role; `roles cost` reads `N / budget` |
| 2. learner first | order of execution: learner → driver → librarian |
| 3. cue at say, argument at ref | the min/`.md` split (F1), one convention line per role (F7), and tool-enforced rules to ref where the tool prints the cue (F6) |
| 4. never cut a cue for a number | budget set at what it reaches, with the reason (case=5) |
| 5. oversized subdomain → catalog | driver invariants already in that shape; librarian `not` list |
| 6. pr before/after table | from `roles cost --all`, before and after |
| 7. reviewer held, not cut | untouched; 9,454 today. a budget at that figure if touched |

**pros** — the same rules for about half the tokens on every call · zero new files · the halt the
tool already has becomes the clamp · the argument stays one Read away.

**cons** — each min becomes a hand edit that must stay in sync with its `.md` (no generator) ·
a min-vs-cue judgment per rule, which a reviewer must check · the librarian may land above its
proposed figure.

**edge cases and the pit of success**

- a min that drops a when-row → caught in this pr by a census diff that names the file and the
  line, and in every later pr by the cue snapshot test (case=6); each booted min is checked for
  its `.md` on disk in src by that test, and in the built package by
  `blackbox/roles.boot.consumer-seat.acceptance.test.ts`, which links each role into a consumer
  repo and boots it (case=3)
- a role whose cues outweigh its proposal → the budget is set at what it reaches, with headroom
  and a comment (case=5); never a cut
- a future edit that bloats a min → the clamp test halts, exit 2, with four fixes; a linked seat
  warns at exit 0
- a `.md` with no `.min` at say (driver: `rule.forbid.verbatim-quotes-outside-seeds.md`, 2,096
  tokens; `define.routes-are-gardened.[philosophy].md`) → give it a min. the verbatim-quotes one
  is a symlink to the learner's, so its min is a symlink too. the gardened one keeps no min for
  now: its min would be a new bracketed filename, which `rule.forbid.brackets-in-filenames`
  forbids, so the rename and the min ride a dream (F9)
- a consumer repo → its specs are `(linked)`; over budget warns there, never halts

## what is awkward

- the min and the `.md` become two hand-kept copies of the cue. if an author edits the cue in
  the `.md` and forgets the min, the boot drifts. the budget catches growth, not drift
- "cue vs argument" is clear for a when-table and fuzzy for a philosophy, where the claim and the
  argument are one paragraph (F2)
- the per-role budget can be met while the per-seat cost still double-counts the three borrowed
  rules (F3)
