# razor: survivorship

shaves: a number or a sample · settled by: observation (of the denominator), then rivals

## .the question

> **which cases did this sample drop, and what would the dropped cases show?**

`razor=base-rate` asks how often at large; this asks how the sample was filtered. a seat sees only the traces that passed, or that were kept.

## .the source

- Abraham Wald, Statistical Research Group, Columbia, WWII memoranda on damage to aircraft that returned
- read via https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Survivorship_bias (partly read)

## .fails when

- the sample was not filtered. name the filter, or say there is none

`rule.always.test-a-claim-against-the-whole-it-speaks-for`
