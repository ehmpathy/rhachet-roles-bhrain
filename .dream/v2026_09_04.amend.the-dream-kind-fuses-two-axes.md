## .the cue that fired

I picked `amend` over `reseed` **twice today** for dreams that satisfy both definitions, and recorded
the judgment neither time. that is a fork taken silently, which is the fulcrum cue.

## .the gap, precisely

the `$kind` slot carries two questions at once:

| the question | the markers that answer it |
|---|---|
| what **work** is owed? | `entool` · `enbrief` · `amend` · `fix` |
| where is its **home**? | `reseed` |

**one slot, two axes.** so a peer-repo amend must drop one answer, and which one drops is the
author's habit rather than a rule.

**measured on this repo's queue, 2026-09-04 — 35 dreams:**

| the dream | work | home | the marker it carries |
|---|---|---|---|
| `v2026_09_04.enbrief.telepath-owes-a-rule-…` | enbrief | 🔴 peer (bhrain issue) | `enbrief` |
| `v2026_09_04.amend.subversive-is-accretion-…` | amend | 🔴 peer (bhrain #428) | `amend` |
| `v2026_09_04.amend.terseness-lane-does-not-select-…` | amend | 🔴 peer (bhuild #358) | `amend` |
| `v2026_08_30.amend.telepath-owns-an-accretion-review-lane` | amend | 🔴 peer | `amend` |
| `v2026_09_04.reseed.radio-task-push-must-teach-…` | amend (the skill exists) | peer | `reseed` |

⇒ **five peer-repo dreams, two markers, no rule.** a `Glob` for `.dream/*.reseed.*` returns 12 of
them and misses the rest — so **the census of what this repo owes its peers is not computable**, which
is the one property `reseed` exists to provide.

## .the shape of the fix

three options, and the choice is a wisher call because it changes an extant filename convention:

1. **two slots** — `v$date.$work.$home.$slug.md`, with `$home` omitted when local. computable both
   ways; costs a longer name and a rename of 35 files
2. **`reseed` wins the slot** when a home is foreign; the work-kind moves into the body. cheap, and
   loses the work axis from the filename
3. **a `.owner` field is already in every dream body** — declare *that* the home axis, drop `reseed`
   as a kind entirely, and let the census be a grep of `owner` rather than a glob. cheapest; costs the
   glob

⚠️ **option 3 is likely right and I am not confident** (~70%). every dream already carries
`- **owner** = …`, so the data exists and only the *index* is absent. but `reseed` is 12 files of
extant convention and a rule cites it by name.

## .why it is not done in this round

it renames or re-scopes 35 files across a convention several travelers write into, on a behavior that
waits at a human-approval gate. that is dirt, not scope
(`rule.always.fix-forward-under-scouts-honor`).

⚠️ **the `fix` kind was NOT deferred** — it was in use 7 times across 5 days, so its row is extant
practice and landed in `term=artifact.dream._.choice._.md` this round. only the **axis** question
defers.
