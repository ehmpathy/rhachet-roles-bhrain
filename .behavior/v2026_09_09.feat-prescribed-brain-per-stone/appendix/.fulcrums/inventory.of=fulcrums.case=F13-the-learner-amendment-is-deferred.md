## .taken, and why at the time

## .the counter-case, stated fairly

⚠️ **the strongest argument against the defer is the rule's own anti-pattern list.** *the batch* —
small fixes gathered for later, where later does not come and the context that made each cheap is
gone. the dream mitigates that (it carries the exact `md` to paste) and does not remove it: this
repo's `.dream/` queue holds **80 files**, and a measured queue elsewhere ran 41 open, 0 claimed.

⚠️ **and the fix is genuinely small** — one bullet and three cue rows. a reader could fairly say the
CLEAN question was answered too strictly for a diff of that size.

⇒ the counter that does **not** hold: *"the rule is booted, so a driver who reads it now is unprotected."*
true, and the amendment protects a **future** author rather than this one — this route already found
the defect the hard way, and recorded it in three places.
