## .settled

**one hook, two glyphs — and it goes in the VISION, not in a sweep this round.**

| the glyph | why it is refused |
|---|---|
| `·` U+00B7 | it fuses two clauses into a run-on the reader cannot parse |
| `⚠️` U+26A0 + FE0F | width-ambiguous — a terminal reserves one cell, an emoji font paints two |

⇒ the two arrive together because they share a mechanism: a `Write|Edit` hook that refuses a
character at the moment it is typed, which is the one surface that fires before the defect ships.

## .the specimen is the human's own, and it is from this canon

> came for the puzzle → it is the point · came for the subject → it is theft of their attention.

`rule.forbid.riddles`'s boundary line. two full clauses, each with its own subject and verb, joined
by a mark that carries no grammar — so the reader must guess whether the second clause continues the
first or opposes it.

🟡 **the mark reads as a list separator and was used as a sentence joiner.** that is the defect
precisely: a reader who has seen `·` separate three short items now meets it between two sentences
and applies the wrong parse.

## .the hook is the mechanism, and the rule is rung 1

- a brief-tier forbid fires only for an author who recalls it
- ⇒ and the author of this canon typed `⚠️` ten times in one round while he wrote the rule against it
- so the hook is the fix and the rule is the record of why

## 🔴 .the hook is TELEPATH'S OWN — the fourth utterance reverses the first read

the first three utterances were taken as a **reseed** into `ehmpathy`'s extant
`claude.hooks/pretooluse.forbid-terms.blocklist`, since that hook already exists and already refuses
writes. the fourth settled otherwise, and the correction holds on the canon's own rule:

- telepath **declares** the glyph forbid and owns `catalog.of=glyph._.md`
- ⇒ so the mechanism belongs beside the rule it enforces
- an entry in a foreign blocklist is a rule enforced where it is not declared
  - the rule and its mechanism then drift, and the drift lands on whichever tree does not own it
  - and a one-glyph change in this canon would wait on another repo's release

⇒ **so it is an `entool`, over a `reseed`** — the climb from 📚 rung 1 to a mechanism, in the role
that already carries the brief. `ehmpathy`'s hook stays the precedent to copy rather than a home to
borrow.

🟡 **and it is deferred on a SCHEDULE rather than a ripple** — *"later"*. the reseed's old deferral
reason (the hook was another tree's) is void the moment the owner changed, so the dream states the
new one: the vision is unapproved, and the `·` predicate is an open design call.
