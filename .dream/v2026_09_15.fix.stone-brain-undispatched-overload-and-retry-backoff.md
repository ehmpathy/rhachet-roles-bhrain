# 🌙 dream: a failed brain apply retries every tick, with no dwell

**kind** = fix · **owner** = `ehmpathy/rhachet-roles-bhrain` · **caught** 2026-09-15

## .the cue that fired

an L3 architecture review (`enroll-impl-arch-defects`) raised two brain-module points: a term
overload, and an unstated retry tradeoff.

## ✅ .gap 1 — the `undispatched` overload — CLOSED

the cause was renamed `'spawn-failed'`; the outcome `'undispatched'` stayed.

🔴 **the durable lesson is the deferral reason, which was wrong.** it read *"a multi-file rename that
ripples through a snapshot and three suites."* one grep showed every site inside
`src/domain.operations/route/brain/`, already open in the diff. ⇒ **a ripple estimate recorded in a
dream is a guess, and a dream is the artifact that stops anyone from a re-check of it.**

## 🔴 .gap 2 — the retry has no dwell — OPEN

`applyStoneBrainOnEntry` does not mark entry on a failed dispatch, so the next onStop tick retries —
correct, per the wish's *"fail loud, never a silent no-op"*. but each retry on `unenrolled` or
`timed-out` re-pays the `whoami` probe (`WHOAMI_TIMEOUT_MS` = 10s cap) plus a fresh spawn, every tick,
for as long as the stone is parked.

the tradeoff is now stated in a `.note` on `applyStoneBrainOnEntry`. the dwell is not built.

## .the shape of the fix

skip the probe for n ticks after a failure whose cause is `unenrolled` or `timed-out`: a persisted
last-fail stamp beside the entry marker in `DriveBlockerState`. the window must not outlive a human's
fix, or a loud halt becomes a silence.

## .why it is not done in this round

| the test | the answer |
|---|---|
| **SAFE?** | 🔴 no — a dwell adds a time-based suppression to the one path whose contract is *"fail loud"* |
| **CLEAN?** | 🔴 no — it widens `DriveBlockerState`'s write contract (a new per-stone stamp) |

- ⇒ the decision record is fulcrum `F21`
- the number that would settle it — the real per-tick wall-clock on a parked stone — has not been
  measured
- the probe cap is an upper bound; `unenrolled` exits 2 in milliseconds
