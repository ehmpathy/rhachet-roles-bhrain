## .the shape, in any life

the case below: a verb printed red, and a look at the pane showed the compact was already in flight.

## .what happened

- the surdirector ran `git.crew.compact` on a director seat that burned past its token cut
- the verb exited 1: `compact UNCONFIRMED — the pane shows no compact row after the send`
- a pane read right after showed `Compacting conversation… 47%`, and a second `/compact` copy queued in the box

## .the claim and the read

the verb's red verdict was a claim about the pane. the pane read refuted it: the compact ran. a resend on the verb's word alone would have queued a third copy.

## .what the read owed

- read the pane before any second send (the verb's own fix line said so)
- treat the red verdict as a tool defect, filed to the owner of the verb (inc-20261122-002906), with the pane text as its red test case

## .the cost

one duplicate `/compact` already queued; a third avoided only by the pane read.

up: `rule.always.trust-but-verify` — a tool verdict guides where to read; only the read settles it.
