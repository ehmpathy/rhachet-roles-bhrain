# 🌙 dream: generate each `.md.min` from its `.md`, so the two cannot drift

kind: feat · owner: rhachet-roles-bhrain (or rhachet, if the boot owns the min) · caught 2026-10-21

## .the cue that fired

the vision for `v2026_10_21.fix-clamp-role-boot-budgets` planned a drift check: every cue row of a
min must appear verbatim in its `.md`. `rhx census.minpeer` measured the premise first: 1,195 of
1,581 min lines (76%) are paraphrases, absent verbatim from their `.md`. the check was dropped
(fulcrum F8).

## .the gap, precisely

a min and its `.md` are two hand-kept copies of one cue. an author who edits a cue row in the
`.md` and forgets the min boots the stale row, and no check sees it. the budget sees growth, the
cue snapshot sees a cut, and neither sees drift.

## .the shape of the fix

mark the cue lines inside each `.md` (a fence, a comment marker, or a `## .cue` section), and
generate the min from the marked lines at build. then the `.md` is the one source, the min is
an artifact, and drift is impossible by construction. `rhx census.minpeer` is the migration
meter: a min is ready to generate once its absent count is zero.

## .why it is not done in this round

not CLEAN: it touches every `.md` of three roles plus the build, and it changes how a min is
authored. it is its own design.

## .the tooling debts it retires (r011 i006 nitpicks, `better`)

- one predicate for "a min line is on record in its `.md`": moveout, `census.minpeer` and
  `census.cuesdiff` each hold their own, with different normalization. extract one
  `asLinesAbsentFromPeer` with a caselist test
- one fence scan: four loops (two in shipped `src/`), where moveout's `asLinesWithFence` is the
  shared transformer
- `asBriefLinesByCue` and `asHeadersOrphanedOfText` ship in `dist` with no production importer;
  the generator is their production caller, or they move to librarian `skills/`
