# F7 — one convention line per role, not a pointer line per min

## the fork
- (a) each condensed min ends with `⇒ argument: <name>.md`
- (b) one line per role, in a say-level doc that already boots: *"each `X.md.min` here condenses
  `X.md` beside it. read the `.md` for the argument."*

## taken: (b), and why at the time
- (a) costs ~15 tokens × ~60 mins ≈ 900 tokens, about 3% of the ~30k target, to state one
  claim 60 times
- the boot already prints each min's path (`<brief.say path="….md.min">`), so the `.md` beside it
  is computable from the header. (b) teaches that computation once
- a pointer per min is a claim restated 60 times, and a restated claim drifts (`declare-once`)

## where the line lives
learner and driver: their `readme.md`, which boots at say (434 · 223 tokens). librarian: its boot
lists no readme, so the line goes on the role's first say brief, or its readme joins say if
the budget allows. settled at execution.

## rework: clean
one line per min, if the wisher wants per-rule pointers back.

## confidence: 70%, low because
at the moment of doubt, a reader looks at the rule, not the readme. (a) is more discoverable,
and (b) leans on a reader who knows the min/md convention.
