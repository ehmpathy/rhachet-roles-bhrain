## .taken, and why — at the time

**re-arrive.** the stated reason was that other blockers remained answerable, so a halt would be
premature while the driver still had `.taken` work to do.

⚠️ **that reason is not wrong, and it is not sufficient.** `rule.always.raise-a-blocker-a-taken-cannot-close`
holds both at once: the halt does **not** excuse the answers, and the answers do **not** discharge
the absence. the correct move was always *do the takens, **then** halt on the residual* — never *do
the takens **instead of** the halt.*

## 🔴 .why it went unnoticed — the budget read healthy every round

the rule names this failure mode in its own words:

> *"budget left is not evidence of a remedy. a lane rejected for absent coverage re-raises

⇒ each round moved **something** — a lane's count fell, a guard got repaired, a term got itemized.
so the ladder never presented the flat signal that would have prompted the test. **the work was
real; it was simply not work on the point that gated.**
