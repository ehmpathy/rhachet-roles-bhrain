## .settled

**the self-review ladder has no override.** the levers a driver reaches for elsewhere — an
overrule, a budget top-up, an escalation to the foreman — do not exist on this ladder. every review
it triggers is the driver's to carry, and the count is not a budget the driver may spend down.

three consequences follow, and they are what a driver most often gets wrong:

| the temptation | what holds |
|---|---|
| *"the count keeps rising — the guard must be broken"* | the count is the work. a re-trigger is more review owed, never a defect to diagnose |
| *"i will ask for this one to be waived"* | there is no lever to grant it. an ask spends the foreman's attention on what they cannot give |
| *"the guard let me through, so it was enough"* | a mechanical allowance is not a verdict on the review's honesty. what the guard permits and what the driver owes are two different quantities |

⚠️ **the last row is the one with teeth.** an escape hatch a driver can reach on purpose is a
shortcut, and a shortcut taken because it was reachable is exactly what this settles against. the
mandate is on the driver's conduct, never on what the machinery happens to permit.
