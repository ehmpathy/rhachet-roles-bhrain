## .the gap, precisely

on the MIXED arm, `stepRouteDrive` renders two surfaces from one pass, and they disagree about
**who owns the next move**.

**the stdout** (`case25.t0.onstop`) carries three remedies, sorted by owner:

```
   └─ halted, reviewer malfunctioned; peer reviewer budget exhausted: limited
      └─ spend your own lever first, then ask a human
         ├─ increase budget — yours to spend
         │  └─ rhx route.guard.budget --for review --add N --peer limited --stone 1
         ├─ overrule the malfunction — a human must grant
         └─ approve as-is — a human must grant
```

**the stderr** (`case25.t0.onstop.stderr`) carries one:

```
reviewer or judge malfunctioned on stone 1. a human must fix the malfunction before the route
can proceed.
```

⇒ 🔴 **the first row of the stdout tree is a lever the driver owns and the stderr denies exists.**
`rule.always.spend-own-levers-before-escalation` turns on exactly that sort — *"the guard prints
what would unblock the stone; it never says who owns each lever"* — and here one surface sorts and
the other does not.

⚠️ **and stderr is the surface most likely to be the only one read.** `rule.forbid.stdout-on-exit-errors`
names the asymmetry: a harness on a non-zero exit renders stderr and may drop stdout. so the
surface that survives is the one that under-states the remedy.

## .the shape of the fix

the reason string is `formatRouteDriveEscalate`'s, and it is **shared by every malfunction
escalate**, not only the mixed arm. so the fix is one of two, and the choice is a contract call:

| option | what it changes | ripple |
|---|---|---|
| **A** — soften the one clause | *"a human must fix the malfunction"* → *"spend your own levers first, then ask a human"* | every malfunction stderr in the repo, and every snapshot of one |
| **B** — make the MIXED arm carry its own reason | the mixed return passes a reason that names the budget row | one arm, one new format op, two surfaces to keep in step |

⇒ **B is the narrower change and A is the truer one.** the bare malfunction arm has no
driver-owned lever, so A would over-promise there; the mixed arm does, so B is the only arm
whose stderr is actually wrong today.

## .why it is not done in this round

- **SAFE?** 🔴 **no.** option A rewords a string every malfunction escalate on every route emits.
  that is behavior beyond what this route came to change, and the route's own drivers read it.
- **CLEAN?** 🔴 **no.** either option ripples past the diff this feature opened — A into every
  code-1 snapshot in the repo, B into a new format operation and the shared render seam.
- ⇒ and the call itself — *does a stderr reason mirror its stdout's remedy tree, or state the
  terminal condition alone?* — is a **contract decision** rather than a repair. it is the same
  question `.dream/v2026_09_18.fix.the-push-stderr-bypasses-the-brain-halt.md` asks one arm over,
  and the two should be settled together rather than one at a time.

🟡 **caught rather than left spoken.** the divergence is now byte-pinned on both surfaces
(`case25.t0.onstop` and `case25.t0.onstop.stderr`), so it cannot change in silence while it waits.
