## .settled

### 1. a stance is scoped to ONE given — `F020`, fork A

> **stances do not survive rounds; only live in latest round**

a concern's identity is `(reviewer, severity, ordinal)` **within one given**. a fresh round
renumbers, and the driver re-declares.

⇒ this is what `S03` already implied and never stated: a disputed lane returns next generation, so
the design **already expected** a re-declaration. the verdict makes the identity scheme match the
lapse behaviour rather than fight it.

### 2. a declaration is EVIDENCE of a read — `F021`, fork A

> **a driver who types `all` has not read all**

so `--about all` is refused outright, and **not** as an ergonomic preference. the ground is what
the declaration IS: evidence that the driver read *that* concern.

⇒ 🔴 **and it is symmetric.** a bulk *dispute* suppresses concerns the driver never argued; a bulk
*concede* over-claims concerns they never read. the evidence property forbids both, which is the
same law `rule.forbid.suppression-of-undeclared-concerns` states over declarations rather than over
disputes.
