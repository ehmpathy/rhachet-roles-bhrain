# domain.term: subversive

term.chosen   = subversive
term.kind     = adj
term.boundary = prose
term.synonyms.forbidden:
- rhetorical
- weasel
- fluffy
- hand-wavy

## .what
`subversive` describes **prose that elongates or encircles its own purpose**, so a reader ends
unable to object rather than able to judge.

it is a boundary term with two shapes beneath it, and the shapes take opposite repairs:

| shape | what is absent | the repair |
|---|---|---|
| `subversive.shallow` | no substance beneath it — the sentence asserts none | cut it |
| `subversive.squishy` | no justification reaches it — it conforms to whatever the reader wants | replace it with the mechanism |

## 🟡 .the root never appears alone in a grade

> **a grade must SORT, never merely flag.**

a reader told only *"this is subversive"* cannot know whether to delete the line or to write a
different one — the two repairs are opposite, so a bare flag names a defect and leaves them unable
to act on it.

## 🟡 .the word claims INTENT, and the rule fires on the ARTIFACT

`subterfuge` is the etymological neighbour and it is exact — the prose masks the true message. but
an author can reproduce every shape by convention, with no intent at all.

⇒ the tell is what happens when the shape is named: **an honest author repairs it; a subversive one
argues that each word was true.** the grade is on the passage, never on the person.

## .why each synonym is forbidden

| word | why not |
|---|---|
| `rhetorical` | names a register, and the defect is ambiguity. a metaphor beside a stated mechanism is fine |
| `weasel` | grades the author, and this term must grade the artifact |
| `fluffy` · `hand-wavy` | each smuggles a volume claim. the measured instance ran 12 words against the mechanism's 13 |

## .the test it is defined by

> **what observation would settle this sentence?**

🟡 **never *"could a reader disagree with it?"*** — that grades taste, and a metaphor passes it. the
coarse test separates `shallow` from a real claim and passes `squishy` whole, which is the reason
this term needed two children rather than a sharper definition.

## .the peer defects it does not name

| word | what it grades | the seam |
|---|---|---|
| `obfuscation` | selection — what the author left off the page | this grades what is on it |
| `diffusion` | findability — the point is present and cannot be found | this grades whether a point was made at all |
| `narration` | shape — prose where a tree was owed | a bulletized passage can still be subversive |
| `condensation` | volume — the cut to the 95% bar | measured: de-subversion ran 155 → 163 lines |

⇒ they overlap on purpose. two cues cannot disagree; they can only both miss or one catch, so a
redundant detector costs a line and a missed one costs the defect
(`rule.require.a-cue-is-not-a-claim`).
