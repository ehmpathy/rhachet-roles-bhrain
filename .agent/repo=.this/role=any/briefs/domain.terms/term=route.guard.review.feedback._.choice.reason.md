# domain.term.choice.reason: feedback

## .etymology

plain english for "the response one gets" — here, one reviewer's whole round: what it critiqued,
and (once the driver has acted) what was said back to it. neither `given` nor `taken` alone
carries that whole; `feedback` is the word for both of them at once.

## .why it was not chosen the first time

`term=route.guard.review.given._.choice.reason.md`'s own `.why not the obvious alternatives`
table rejected `feedback` as a candidate for **`given`**: *"names the CONTENT, so it has no
complement — the pair collapses into a compound."* that argument is sound for what it was
asked — `feedback` cannot stand in for one symmetric half of a `given`/`taken` pair, because it
has no natural partner word the way `give`/`take` do.

it was never asked, at the time, whether `feedback` could name the PAIR ITSELF — a third
concept, one level above the two halves. it can, and the gap was real: no word named "one
reviewer's round, given and taken together" until this term existed.

## .disputes

### dispute: feedback vs given — raised 2026-09-16 — status: RESOLVED (settled at
