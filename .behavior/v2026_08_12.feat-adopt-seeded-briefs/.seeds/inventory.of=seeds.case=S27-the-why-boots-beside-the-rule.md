# seed: the why boots beside the rule

**2026-08-31. a two-sentence correction that overturned a tier policy — and named what a
fixed-footprint rule cannot trade away.**

## .said

> also, lets ensure that the philosophy briefs are say level

> the why often matters more than the rest

## .settled

**a philosophy boots at `say`, beside the rules it grounds — never one dereference away at `ref`.**

the policy it overturned was reasonable and is now recorded as wrong: `ref` had been justified as
*"a why is wanted once, not on every boot."*

⇒ **the mechanism that makes it wrong is the edge case.** a rule states WHAT to do, and it cannot
state what to do in a case it does not enumerate. **no rule enumerates every case.** so a reader
who carries the rules and not the why:

- can follow every rule exactly and still be wrong at the first edge
- **cannot know they are wrong**, because the rule they followed said naught about the edge

and a why held one dereference away is a why they do not have at that moment, because **the edge
arrives unannounced.** a reader who knew to fetch the philosophy would already have understood the
edge — which is exactly the knowledge the fetch was supposed to supply.

## ⚠️ .the d=0.05 objection, and why it argues the other way

`research.selfreview-effectiveness` grades a philosophical frame at **d = 0.05** and a when-then
cue at **d = 0.65** — which reads, at a glance, as a case for demotion.

**it is not, and the reason is in the measurement.** the 0.05 is for a frame with **no rule beside
it**. that is not this arrangement. every philosophy promoted here sits in the same tier as the
rules it grounds, so what was built is the pair rather than the frame alone.

⇒ so the research argues **for** the pair, and a shared tier is what makes the pair possible.

## 🔴 .the tier is not a budget to protect — the `.min` is what pays for it

the extant policy is *"`say` holds a fixed, small footprint; depth lives under `ref`."* the
promotion does not suspend it. **it moves the compression from the tier to the artifact.**

| the old lever | the lever now |
|---|---|
| demote the brief to `ref` | condense the brief into a `.md.min` |
| a reader must know to fetch it | it fires unprompted; the source is what is dereferenced |

⇒ **the min IS the say/ref split, applied within one artifact.** so *"this is too big for say"* is
almost never a tier question — it is an unpaved min.

⚠️ **and a philosophy resists that cut more than a rule does.** measured the same day: rules min to
17–38%, philosophies to 43–60%, because *a min may drop an argument and never a claim* — and a
philosophy is argument by construction. **a philosophy that mins to 20% was gutted, not
condensed.**
