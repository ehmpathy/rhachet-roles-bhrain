## .taken, and why

🔴 **candidate C, and specifically the extant `rhx route.stone.set --as conceded --severity urgent`.**

the wish asks for a signal that is *recorded and machine-readable — a fact the tool reads, never a
judgment a clone asserts* (req 3). **that signal already ships, and no other candidate on the list
does:**

| what req 3 demands | the extant concede |
|---|---|
| recorded | a `PassageReport` row in `passage.jsonl` — `{ status, reviewer, about, given, severity }` |
| machine-readable | `getStoneLiveUrgentConcessionSlugs.ts` already filters it |
| fresh — the LAST round | `getLiveReviewAbsorptions` keys it to the slug's latest `.given` |
| graded by harm | `--severity` is **REQUIRED** on every concede; there is no ungraded concede (`setStoneAsConcernAbsorbed.ts:103-113`) |
| the closed harm set | security · safety · monetary · reputation · behavioral, already declared |

⇒ **A, B, and D would each coin a second signal for a concept the repo already names**, which
`rule.forbid.domain-term-synonyms` forbids and `rule.always.reuse-pavement-before-improvise` fires on
before authorship.

**and the wisher already ruled the underlying law**, one week earlier:
`define.invariant.review.peer.budget.urgent-earns-budget` states *"only an urgent concession qualifies
for an increased budget"* — with `urgent` defined as the driver's `--severity` grade. ⇒ this wish asks
which signal earns a grant; the invariant already answered, and this fulcrum is a **confirmation**
rather than an invention.

🟡 **candidate A is the strongest rival and it is not dead.** a reviewer that narrows its own point is
better evidence of convergence than a driver's self-graded harm claim — it comes from the party that
raised the objection. ⇒ but the engine has **no reviewer-side narrow signal at all**: a `.given` is
counts plus prose, and no operation reads a reviewer's retraction. candidate A is a feature, never a
predicate, and it would be a wish of its own.
