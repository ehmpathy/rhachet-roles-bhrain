# case=10 · the nitpick-only rejection

- **coordinates** = `enter × answered × live` — reached by a road `case=2` does not walk
- **feel** = sharp · **care** = critipath · **verdict** = forbidden → demoed

**the boundary `F015` turns on.** a lane raises **zero blockers**, holds the stone anyway, and owes
the driver an answer it never asked for. this is the case that proves a stance keys on the
**verdict** rather than on a blocker count.

## .the narrative

*driver, on `1.vision`, round 3, an `experience-coverage` lane*

the lane approves my structure and files **eight nitpicks** — a stale `.see also`, two counts that
drifted, a header that names an emotion. no blockers. I read the meter and it says `rejected`.

I check the guard, and the arithmetic is on the page:

```
--allow-blockers 0 --allow-nitpicks 7
```

**eight is one over.** the lane holds the stone on nitpicks alone.

🔴 **and today I have no lever at all.** the four exits a driver knows are each shut, and each for a
different reason:

| the exit | why it is shut |
|---|---|
| answer it | 🔴 **no `.taken` is owed.** the debt predicate is `blockers > 0`, so the lane never enters the debt set — the contemplation gate does not even name it |
| let it exhaust | 🔴 **impossible.** `runStoneGuardReviews.ts:430` cache-skips on raw `blockers === 0`, **before** the exhaustion check. the lane never re-runs and never spends a round |
| top up the budget | ⚠️ buys rounds for a lane that is skipped anyway |
| `--as blocked` | the critique is answerable-by-repair, so a halt is the forbidden exit (`rule.forbid.unanswered-exits-from-a-blocker`) |

⇒ **the one road out is to repair all eight** — which moves the hash, misses the cache, and re-runs
the lane. that is fine when I agree. **it is a trap when I do not**, and two of these eight I do not:
the "drifted count" is a count the reviewer misread, and the "emotion header" is a declared
artifact-kind header its own contract fixes.

**now the halt asks me the question it asks everywhere else:**

```
🗿 route.drive
   └─ held — one lane answered, and undeclared
      ├─ experience-coverage (l1) : 0 blockers, 8 nitpicks  ██░░ rejected
      │  └─ nitpicks exceed threshold (8 > 7)
      │
      └─ declare a stance on each of the 8 concerns — nitpick.1 … nitpick.8
         ├─ concede — "the reviewer is right, I will repair it"
         │  └─ rhx route.stone.set --stone 1.vision --as conceded --with experience-coverage \
         │       --about nitpick.<n>
         └─ dispute — "this ONE is fine to continue"; it leaves the tally, the council rules
            ├─ write the argument into a fulcrum entry first
            │  └─ .fulcrums/inventory.of=fulcrums.case=F00N-<slug>.md
            └─ rhx route.stone.set --stone 1.vision --as disputed --with experience-coverage \
                 --about nitpick.<n> --why <that path>
```

I concede six and repair them; I dispute the two I hold. **8 declarations, not one** — and the
arithmetic is what makes that grain earn its keep:

| grain | what leaves the tally | the total | the stone |
|---|---|---|---|
| 👎 per-FILE | all **8** | 8 → 0 | passes, and my six concessions are erased (`case=11`) |
| ✅ per-CONCERN | the **2** I argued | 8 → 6, under the allowance of 7 | passes, and the six stay on record to repair |

⇒ **both roads pass the stone, and only one of them is honest.** that is why this lane is the sharp
demo: a per-file dispute reaches the right verdict by the wrong arithmetic, so the defect leaves no
trace in the outcome.

**the aha:** *a stance was never about blockers. it is about **what the judge counts** — and the
judge counts nitpicks. the moment the design keyed the stance to a blocker count, it built a cell
where the stone is held and the driver has no move.*

## 🔴 .the mechanism, as this case makes it legible

**the contract states it once** — `1.vision.yield.md` § *"the hold is lifted" is an OUTCOME — the
mechanism is a TALLY EXCLUSION*, with the code lines and the forgive/dispute grain table. **read it
there; it is not copied here.**

⇒ what this case adds is the one lane where the exclusion is the **whole** effect and naught else
moves: **the 2 disputed nitpicks leave the sum, the total falls to 6 under an allowance of 7, and no
blocker was ever in the arithmetic.** every other demo disputes a lane that also raised blockers, so
the count and the verdict fall together and a reader cannot see which one the mechanism touched.

🔴 **and that legibility surfaces a consequence no other case can: the tally is a sum ACROSS LANES,
never per lane.** the operation's own `.why` says so —

> *"aggregates counts **across multiple review files** for threshold check"*
> — `computeReviewTotalsFromFiles.ts:6-7`, a `+=` over every file (`:20-21`)

⇒ **one `totalBlockers` and one `totalNitpicks` for the whole stone**, handed to
`computeReviewThresholdVerdict` (`route.ts:1404-1414`) and compared to one allowance.

⇒ so a dispute lifts the hold only if **what remains** falls under the threshold. two lanes at 5
nitpicks each, under `--allow-nitpicks 7`, total 10 and hold the stone — **and a dispute of either
one leaves 5, which passes.** a third lane at 5 would leave 10 and the dispute would change naught
visible.

⚠️ **`--as disputed --with <reviewer> --about <concern>` is a per-CONCERN command whose effect is a
global arithmetic**, and *"the hold lifts for that lane"* states the grain of the command rather than
the grain of the effect.
⇒ **a driver who reads it that way will dispute a lane and find the stone still held**, with a halt
that names no reason they can act on. ⇒ **the halt owes an arithmetic line**, and **which** line is a
fork — the residual total, or the per-lane contribution. ⇒ `F019` at 68%, a `2.1.criteria` case this
vision names rather than settles.

## .the bdd timeline

```gherkin
given a stone `1.vision` with peer reviewer `experience-coverage`, budget 4, rounds 3
  and the guard declares --allow-blockers 0 --allow-nitpicks 7
  and the lane's latest .given raises 0 blockers and 8 nitpicks
  and its verdict computes `rejected`                             # nitpicks 8 > 7
  and NO .taken is owed                                           # blockers > 0 is false

[t0] when the driver runs --as passed
     then the stone is HELD                                       # 🔴 R4, via the verdict
      and the halt names the lane "answered, undeclared"          # 🔴 B = answered, not clean
      and it prints the threshold that was crossed                # "nitpicks exceed threshold (8 > 7)"
      and it prints concede and dispute, per concern              # 🔴 S07 + S11 — concede first

[t1] when the driver runs --as disputed --with experience-coverage --about nitpick.4 \
       --why <a fulcrum path>
     then it is ADMITTED                                          # ✅ invariant 2 passes TRIVIALLY
      and NO .taken was required first                            # 🔴 naught was owed to discharge
      and exactly ONE nitpick leaves the tally                    # 🔴 S07 — never the whole file
      and totalNitpicks falls 8 → 7, which MEETS the allowance of 7
      and the stone PASSES on that one declaration                # ⚠️ see [t1b]

[t1b] given the driver disputes a second, nitpick.7, and concedes the other six
      when all 8 declarations are on record
      then totalNitpicks reads 6, under the allowance               # ✅ the honest arithmetic
       and the six conceded nitpicks STAY owed as repairs           # 🔴 a concede subtracts naught
       and the council reads exactly TWO fulcrum-backed rows        # never one row for eight concerns

[t2] given the lane had raised 3 blockers and 8 nitpicks instead
     when the driver runs --as disputed --about blocker.1 with no .taken
     then it is REFUSED                                           # 🔴 R1 — answer-first still binds
      and the refusal names 3 blockers and the .taken path         # case=3's refusal, unchanged

[t3] given the driver repairs 2 of the 8 nitpicks, and does not declare
     when it runs --as passed
     then the hash moves, the cache misses, the lane re-runs
      and its new .given raises 6 nitpicks, so the verdict is `approved`   # 6 <= 7
      and NO stance is owed                                       # B moves to `clean`

[t4] given instead a lane with 0 blockers and 3 nitpicks, on DEFAULT thresholds
     when the driver runs --as passed
     then the stone is HELD identically                           # 🔴 allowNitpicks defaults to 0
      and a stance is owed at THREE nitpicks                       # ⚠️ the ordinary configuration
```

🔴 **`[t1]` is the step this whole case exists to make legible, and it is the one `F015` got
backwards.** the entry argued that a stance here *"would be demanded with no prior answer, which is
precisely the shape the wish's boundary forbids."* ⇒ **the boundary forbids a dispute that SKIPS an
owed answer.** naught is owed here, so there is naught to skip — invariant 2 finds no debt and
passes. **a gate that catches naught is not a defeated gate.**

⚠️ **`[t2]` is why that is not a loophole.** add blockers to the same lane and answer-first binds at
full strength. **the two steps differ on whether a debt EXISTS**, never on whether the gate is
enforced.

### 🔴 the hole `[t1]` opens under the per-concern grain, and it is NEW

**one dispute takes the total 8 → 7, which MEETS the allowance — so the verdict flips to `approved`
and the stance gate stops asking.** the other **seven** nitpicks are then never declared, never
repaired, and never seen by the council.

| what the driver declared | what the stone records |
|---|---|
| 1 dispute, fulcrum-backed | ✅ on the board, ruled at the close |
| 🔴 7 concerns, undeclared | **naught.** no row, no repair, no council row |

⇒ **this is `case=11`'s defect at a different joint.** there the suppression was *mechanical* — one
declaration shed four concerns. here it is **incidental**: the gate's own threshold arithmetic stops
demanding stances the moment the tally clears, so a driver may exit with the cheapest single dispute
that crosses the line.

⚠️ **and it is worse at the boundary than in the middle.** a lane 1 over its allowance needs exactly
**one** dispute to silence the rest; a lane 5 over needs five. ⇒ **the incentive runs backwards** —
the closer a lane sits to passable, the less of it a driver must answer.

🟡 **`rule.forbid.suppression-of-undeclared-concerns` names the law and does not reach this.** its
test is *"does this declaration discharge a concern it did not name?"* — and here no declaration
does. **the gate does**, by no longer asking. ⇒ the rule grades declarations; this is a property of
the **gate's exit condition**, which nothing on this route grades.

🔴 **recorded, not solved.** it belongs to the same fork as `F024` — whether the stance gate reads a
lane's residual verdict or its concern ledger — and a repair here (*"every concern owes a stance,
threshold notwithstanding"*) would change what acceptance #1 means. ⇒ `2.1.criteria` owes a case on
the **boundary** lane, and the fork is the wisher's.

🔴 **`[t4]` is the frequency claim, and it is what makes this a critipath rather than an edge.**
`getReviewedJudgeThresholds.ts:14` returns `null` where a guard declares no `reviewed?` judge, and
`computeReviewPeerVerdict.ts:55-56` then defaults **both** allowances to `0`. ⇒ **on default
thresholds one nitpick is enough.** the `--allow-nitpicks 7` guard makes the case *visible*; it does
not make it *possible*.

## .why it fails safe

**fast** — refused at the gate, before any subprocess · **loud** — the halt prints the threshold
arithmetic (`8 > 7`), never a bare *"rejected"* · **teaches the fix** — two commands, and the concede
branch names repair as the road it opens.

⚠️ **the ergonomic hazard this case must not cause.** a driver who reads *"0 blockers"* and *"held"*
in one emit will suspect a defect in the engine. ⇒ **the threshold line carries the whole weight**,
never decoration: it is the one line that converts an apparent contradiction into arithmetic.

## .the cells this case covers

`enter × answered × live` ❌→✅ `[t0]` · `dispute × answered × live` ✅ `[t1]` ·
`dispute × unanswered × live` ❌→✅ `[t2]` · `reviewer-runs × answered × live` ✅ `[t3]`

🟡 **`[t3]` is filed at `answered`, never at `clean`.** its `given` is a lane whose latest verdict is
`rejected` with no `.taken` owed; **`clean` is its OUTCOME**, and an outcome is not a coordinate
(`case=_` § A6).

🟡 **every cell here is already demoed elsewhere, and that is the point.** this case adds **no cell**
— it walks four extant ones by a road the other nine never take: **a rejection with no blocker
behind it.** ⇒ the census is unmoved, and `rule.require.experience-catalog-evolution` is satisfied by
a demo rather than by a new coordinate.

⚠️ **it earns a file anyway, because the cell it lands in was reached by the WRONG road for two
rounds.** under axis B's old text this lane sat at `clean`, so the grid said *permitted* where the
engine says *held*. **a case that only re-walks extant cells is exactly what catches a mis-defined
coordinate**, since the walk and the engine disagree at one cell and nowhere else.
