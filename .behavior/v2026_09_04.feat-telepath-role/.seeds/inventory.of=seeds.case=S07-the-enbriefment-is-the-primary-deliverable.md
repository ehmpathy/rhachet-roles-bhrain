## .settled

**a role's deliverable is its canon. a reviewer is a consumer of that canon, never its purpose.**

the primary deliverable is two artifacts, in this order:

1. the **briefs** — the traits and rules that teach the prose discipline
2. the **rubrics** — a `review.by` rubric per rule, so the canon is checkable

⇒ **a route or a guard that consumes the reviewer is downstream and separate.** it is where the canon gets *used*, never what the canon is *for*.

### the four traits the canon must teach, named by the wisher

| trait | direction |
|---|---|
| ban **subversive narration** | negative |
| ban **chronological accretion** | negative |
| enforce **concise, clear, direct prose** | positive |
| enforce **crystal clear compression** | positive |

🟡 **two bans and two mandates, and they are peers.** a canon with only the bans teaches what to avoid and leaves the reader with no target; a canon with only the mandates teaches a target and no defect list.
