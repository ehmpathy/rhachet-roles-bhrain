# seed S13 — the guard is the driver's own lever

## .said — verbatim, 2026-09-07

> you have the abiltiy to edit the guards

## .settled

**`route.mutate.guard` is a driver skill. a guard that overflows its own lanes is a driver defect to
repair, never a wisher call to escalate.**

the guard declares each lane's `--rules`, `--paths-with`, `--conversation`, `budget`, and `level`. all
five are the driver's to set. so when a lane cannot fit its own scope in a window, the driver holds
the whole remedy:

| the knob | who owns it |
|---|---|
| `--paths-with` — narrow a lane to its own subsystem | **driver** |
| `--conversation` — bound it, or drop it | **driver** |
| `budget` — the rounds a lane may spend | **driver** |
| `level` — where a lane sits on the ladder | **driver** |

## 🔴 .why it had to be said — the mis-sort is the exact one the extant rule names

`rule.always.spend-own-levers-before-escalation` states the trap outright:

> *"a halt is a diagnosis to make, not a message to relay. the guard prints what would unblock the
> stone; it never says who owns each lever."*

**the driver read that rule, then filed the overflow as `F11 — the guard binds are blind and i did not
edit them`, a fulcrum for the council, for six consecutive rounds.** the fulcrum's own title says the
binds were not edited. it does not say the driver could not edit them, because the driver believed it
could not.

⇒ **a fulcrum is for a call that is genuinely the wisher's.** to file a lever you own is to convert
your own unspent work into someone else's decision.

## ⚠️ .the subtler half — the rule's own example set is what let it slip

the extant rule's owner table lists three driver levers: `route.guard.budget`, a diagnosed reviewer
malfunction, and (by implication) the scoped re-run. **`route.mutate.guard` appears in none of them.**

⇒ so the driver applied the rule correctly against the levers the rule illustrated, and looked past a
lever it did not. that is precisely
`rule.require.specialize-a-rule-its-readers-look-past` — a failure of **retrieval**, not of attention.
the repair is the guard-edit row added to that owner table, not a louder `.what`.
