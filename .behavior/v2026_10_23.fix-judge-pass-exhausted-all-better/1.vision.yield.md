# 1.vision — a stance holds wherever the route is read from

## .the one-line vision

a concession or dispute keys to **which given it answers**, never to **where on disk the route sat
when the driver answered**. so an exhausted lane whose concerns are all absorbed and conceded
`better` is shed by the `reviewed?` judge on every machine, in every worktree, under every path
alias — and the stone passes, as `define.invariant.review.peer.budget.urgent-earns-budget` already
says it must.

## .the reframe — the contract the wish asks for already ships; the judge cannot see it

the wish asks the judge to stop to count an exhausted lane's concerns once every one is absorbed and
conceded `better`. **that rule is already code**, per concern, not per lane:

- `src/contract/cli/route.ts:1587-1604` — the judge subtracts `getStoneDisputedConcernCounts` +
  `getStoneConcededBetterConcernCounts` from its tally, then clamps at zero
- `getStoneConcededBetterConcernCounts.ts:23-43` — counts live stances with `status: conceded` and
  `severity ∈ {better, undefined}`; `urgent` is not shed
- `blackbox/driver.route.concession-exhaustion-severity.acceptance.test.ts` — an exhausted,
  all-`better` lane passes today, end to end

a hermetic repro of the measured shape (`appendix/repro.exhausted-all-better.md`) confirms it: two
l2 lanes at 3/3 — `intent` 1 blocker, `friction` 2 — every concern conceded `better` and absorbed.
the stone passes on round 4 and the judge reads `blockers: 0/0`.

**then the same judge, on the same route, same ledger, same givens, reached through a symlinked
alias of the route dir:**

```
passed: false
reason: blockers exceed threshold (3 > 0)
```

that is the measured halt, byte for byte.

### the root cause

a stance row stores `given` as the **absolute path** of the given it answers
(`setStoneAsConcernAbsorbed.ts:274` writes `given.pathGiven`; `enumRouteFiles` returns absolute
paths). every reader then compares by string equality:

| reader | compare |
|---|---|
| `getLiveReviewAbsorptions.ts:51` | `pathGivenLatestBySlug.get(stance.reviewer) === stance.given` — feeds the disputed shed, the `better` shed, and (via `getLiveConcededAbsorptions`, `getStoneConcededLaneSlugs.ts:24`) the urgent-slug halt **and the budget-grant warrant** |
| `computeUndeclaredConcernLabels.ts` (the `labelsDeclared` filter) | `one.given === input.pathGiven` — the entrance gate and the stance ack |
| `getStoneReviewAbsorptions.ts:116` (`getAbsorptionOnConcern`) | `stance.given === input.given` — R3 and the idempotent write |
| `getStoneReviewAbsorptions.ts:54` (`keyOf`) | the last-wins fold key |

so the moment the route's absolute path differs from the one the driver answered under, **every
stance lapses silently**. the absolute path moves more often than it looks:

- a worktree dir renamed, moved, or removed and re-created
- a route reached through a symlink (`~/git` vs its realpath, a `.temp/*.symlink` dir)
- a route carried into another tree by a merge — **this repo's own ledgers prove it**:
  `.behavior/v2026_09_17.fix-budget-grant-needs-urgent-concession/.route/passage.jsonl:11` lives in
  this worktree and names `/home/camper/git/ehmpathy/_worktrees/rhachet-roles-bhrain.beav.fix-budget-grant-needs-urgent-concession/…`
- a route cloned onto another machine

### two readers, two absolute paths — the shape of the measured halt

the stance is written, and the entrance gate reads it, in the **driver's** process. the `reviewed?`
judge reads it in a **subprocess** the guard spawns with `cwd: repoRoot` and its own `$route`
(`runStoneGuardJudges.ts:80-105`). each side builds its absolute paths on its own.

| who disagrees | what the driver sees |
|---|---|
| the whole route moved, both readers agree on the new path | the entrance gate names every orphaned concern as undeclared — a lever exists (re-declare), though it asks the driver to repeat stances already made. measured: repro `passed via alias` |
| **the set side and the judge side** see two different absolute paths in one run | the gate reads every concern as declared, 0 `.taken` owed; the judge reads none of it and halts `3 > 0`. **this is the measured symptom**, and here the lever is truly gone |

the repro reaches row 2 by a judge invoked through an alias of the route. which divergence the
clamp tree hit between its driver process and its judge subprocess is not observed — the tree is
gone (F1). the cure does not need to know: a path-free key makes **every** pair of readers agree,
whatever their cwd, their `$route`, or the machine.

### why it bites exhausted lanes, and only them

a live lane re-runs, mints a fresh given at the current path, and the driver re-declares against
it, so the orphan heals in one round. **an exhausted lane never re-runs.** its given is frozen, its
stances are frozen, and their key no longer matches. the tree still shows 0 `.taken` owed (givens
and takens are both enumerated from disk at the current path and paired by derived path,
`getOneRouteGuardReviewPeerFeedbackAbsorptionStatus.ts:99-103,212`, so neither side holds a stale
key), and the judge counts every blocker. the driver sees a stone with every concern
answered and no lever that moves it — and the lever is truly absent, not merely unnamed: the
`route.guard.budget` top-up is refused without a live `urgent` warrant, and that warrant is read
through the same lapsed key.

> absorbed. `route.drive` shows 0 `.taken` owed. the judge halts: `blockers exceed threshold

### after

> the same driver, the same ledger. the judge folds each stance by the given's **identity** — its

> passes, `blockers: 0/0`. no re-declaration, no human, no budget.

### the aha

the stance was never wrong; its **address** was. a stance answers *"blocker.2 of friction's
i003 given"* — a fact about the review, true in any clone. the absolute path was an accident of
where the shell stood.

## .the contract

| situation (exhausted lane, any route path) | judge |
|---|---|
| every concern absorbed, every concession `better` (or disputed) | sheds them — passes if the residual fits |
| ≥1 live `urgent` concession | holds; the halt names `rhx route.guard.budget --for review --add N --peer <lane> --stone <stone>` |
| ≥1 concern with no stance | holds, exactly as today |

all three already hold under an unchanged path. the cure makes them hold under **every** path.

### the shape of the cure (for the blueprint, not settled here)

- one transformer, `asReviewGivenKey({ path })` → the given's basename. unique within a stone, since
  the filename carries `$stone`, `i$iter`, `$hash`, `r$idx`, `$slug`
- every stance↔given compare routes through it on **both** sides — so legacy rows with a foreign
  absolute path match too, and the measured clamp tree unsticks with no re-declaration
- new rows write that same key (the given's filename), so a fresh ledger carries no machine path
  and old and new rows reduce to one key through one transformer. safe: every reader of
  `stance.given` is a string compare, none opens it as a file (`getLiveReviewAbsorptions.ts:51`,
  `getStoneReviewAbsorptions.ts:55,116`, `computeUndeclaredConcernLabels.ts:49`)
- the urgent halt names `--peer <lane>` for each lane that carries an urgent concession (today it
  passes `peer: null`, `route.ts:1644-1648`). not cosmetic: a bare `--add` lands on the latest level
  alone, so without `--peer` the grant can miss an exhausted lane at a lower level
  (`rule.always.spend-own-levers-before-escalation`, F022 fork E). the halt's **who** — "please ask
  your human" — is left untouched; that divergence is an unruled fulcrum of another route

## .mental model

- **the driver would say:** *"i answered every point, and the judge forgot my answers because the
  folder moved."*
- **the analogy:** a library card keyed to the shelf a book sat on, rather than to the book. move
  the shelf and every loan reads as unreturned.
- **their words vs ours:** they say *"my concessions didn't count"*; we say *"the stance lapsed — its
  given key no longer matches the latest given."* the cure keeps the lapse rule (a new generation
  still lapses a stance) and drops only the false lapse.

## .evaluation

**goals.** the measured halt passes (done #1); urgent still halts with the lever (done #2);
an undeclared concern still holds (done #3). the fix sits at the source of the tally — the key —
with no allowance raised, no lane muted, no path narrowed.

**pros.**
- one transformer cures every reader at once — the shed, the urgent read, the entrance gate, R3
- legacy ledgers heal on read; no migration
- a route artifact stops to carry a machine-local path — no false precision in a committed ledger

**cons.**
- two key forms coexist in old ledgers (absolute) and new (relative) until rows age out; the
  transformer hides it, but a human who greps the ledger sees both

**edgecases.**
- two givens with one basename in one stone → impossible by construction (`r$idx` + `i$iter` +
  `$hash` + `$slug` in the name)
- a rewind re-mints the same basename → already voided by `getPassageReportsSinceLastRewind`, which
  slices on the `rewound` row rather than the key
- a stance whose given was deleted → no latest given matches it, so it lapses, as today

## .what is awkward

- the wish's title names a rule that already holds, so the PR fixes a different line than the wish
  points at. the PR body must say so plainly, with the repro, or a reviewer will look for a lane rule
  and not find one
- the clamp must move the route's absolute path, which is an unusual fixture move; a symlinked alias
  is the cheapest honest stand-in for a rename, a carried tree, or another machine
