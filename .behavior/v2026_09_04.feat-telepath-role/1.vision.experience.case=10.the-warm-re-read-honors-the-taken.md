## .the edge, precisely

a reviewer is stateless between rounds. it re-reads the subject and grades it fresh.

| what the driver did | what a naive stateless reviewer does |
|---|---|
| fixed the defect | re-reads the artifact, finds the fix, drops the point |
| refuted the point with cited evidence | re-reads the artifact, sees no change, re-raises the identical blocker |

⇒ **the second row is the edge, and it does not converge.** the driver refutes, the reviewer re-raises, the driver refutes again — a loop that burns the budget without either party wrong. and the driver cannot part it from a real defect they failed to fix.

🟡 the mechanism that fixes it is already in the subject:

- `.taken.by_self` files live under `.behavior/`
- ⇒ so a reviewer whose scope keeps `.behavior/` reads the driver's articulation in the same pass as the artifact
- ⇒ the contract is that it must USE it

## .the narrative

the driver takes a blocker: *the taken slice's real-cell count contradicts itself across two artifacts.* it is right, and the repair is one file.

the second point is where the driver has to make a call. half of it is a real defect and half rests on a read of the artifact that no longer holds. the driver could quietly fix the whole of it — cheaper, and it would end the exchange. instead it repairs the half that is wrong and writes out the evidence for the half that is not, both into `.taken.by_self`, with the current file quoted.

🟡 that second act is a bet, and the driver knows the odds. a reviewer that re-reads cold will not see the `.taken` at all — the repair will look like no repair, the refutation like silence, and the driver will spend the next round on the two points it just closed. budget is finite, and a re-litigated point costs the same as a new one.

it re-arrives. and what comes back is one nitpick it has never seen before.

⇒ **the relief is the experience, and it is specific: neither of yesterday's two points is in the report.** the driver does not re-read them to check, does not re-argue the refutation, does not spend a round to re-close what it already closed. the budget bought forward motion.

🟡 and the driver learns where its own leverage sits. the cost of a careful `.taken` is repaid within one round, so on the next blocker it writes the evidence again rather than takes the quiet fix. the contract is what makes the honest move the cheap one — and a reviewer that ignored the `.taken` would teach the opposite lesson just as fast.

## .the fail-safe proof

a sharp critipath owes fast · loud · teaches-the-fix · holds-the-limit · drops-no-data.

| obligation | met at | how |
|---|---|---|
| fast | `[t3]` | the re-raise appears in the same round's report — within one iteration, never after a silent divergence |
| loud | `[t3]` | a re-raised point is a **counted blocker** in stdout, so the guard blocks the stone. it cannot pass unnoticed (`contract.reviewer-output`) |
| teaches the fix | `[t3]` | `rule.always.converge-with-reviewers.via-a-taken-per-point` states the diagnosis outright: *"a blocker that returns after you fixed it is a driver error, never a reviewer defect. check whether you ever told the reviewer, before you diagnose one."* |
| holds the limit | `[t2]` | an unanswered point is **correctly** re-raised. the reviewer's floor behavior is the safe one — it re-raises rather than forgets |
| drops no data | `[t0]` `[t1]` | the articulation is in the subject and survives every round; `case=8` `[t4]` proves the scope narrow preserves it |

### why `holds-the-limit` is the row that carries the most weight

**the degenerate case of this cell is the SAFE one.** a reviewer that ignores every `.taken` re-raises each point — noisy, slow, and **never wrong**. a reviewer that over-trusts a `.taken` would drop a real defect, which is the failure that ships.

⇒ so the contract is asymmetric by design: **the reviewer must not re-raise an ANSWERED point, and it must re-raise an UNANSWERED one.** `[t2]` is the guard on the second half, and it is why this cell fails safe even where the contract is imperfectly implemented.
