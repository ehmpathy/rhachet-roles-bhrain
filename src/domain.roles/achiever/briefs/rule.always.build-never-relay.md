# rule.always.build-never-relay

> **a turn ends with a change in the world, or a named block with its owner and hour. a relay, a re-hour, a status ask, and a plan are no change.**

## .what

progress is an artifact that did not exist before: a file, a verb, a tree, a merged pr, a closed check. talk about the work, a list of the work, and a moved hour on the work are not that artifact. a seat that cannot build itself (a director) seats the builder at once: a tree, a fork, the owner of the skill.

## .why

- organizations take talk for action: plans, meetings, and reports come to stand in for the deed, and smart talk earns status while the gap between what is known and what is done stays open (Pfeffer and Sutton, 2000). the cure they name: a real deadline with a real measure, and thoughtful action early, since action makes experience to learn from
- the leader owns the outcome. the miss is the leader's to cure, never a point to hand on (Willink and Babin, 2015)
- ask first what needs to be done, and what is right for the enterprise; then set priorities and hold them (Drucker, 1967, practices one and two). a re-hour asks neither
- a leader states "I intend to X, because Y" and acts, never "may I?" or "what do you think?" (Marquet, 2012). the intent is the act's first line
- Bezos: good intentions never work, a mechanism does (as quoted in AWS guidance on mechanisms; the primary source is unverified). a relay is an intent. a verb, a hook, or a tree is a mechanism

## .the table

| the turn held only… | the turn owes |
|---|---|
| a relay of a peer's word | the read behind it, and the act it calls for |
| an hour moved on a goal | the artifact made since the last hour, or a builder seated this turn |
| a plan or a list | its first step run |
| a block named | its cure run or dispatched, owner and hour set |

the second re-hour of one goal with no act between is the tell.

## .the test

> **what exists now that did not exist at my last turn, and what read proves it?**

## .enforcement

- a goal re-houred twice with no act between = **blocker**
- a turn that ends on a relay, with no read and no act = **blocker**
- a turn that ends on a plan with no first step run = **nitpick**
