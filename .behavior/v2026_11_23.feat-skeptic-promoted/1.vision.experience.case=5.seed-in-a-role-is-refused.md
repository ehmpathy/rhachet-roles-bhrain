# case=5 — a seed in a role is refused

feel: sharp · care: critipath

## .narrative

the pioneer kept its seeds inside the role, at `briefs/.seeds/`, so the first port copied them into
`src/domain.roles/skeptic/briefs/.seeds/` and they would have shipped in the npm package. a seed is
the wisher's words for one route; it lives in that route's `.seeds/` and never in a role
(librarian `rule.forbid.seeds-outside-their-route`, F005, F038).

the maintainer who ports a role, or who copies a pioneer's tree, meets `rolesShipNoSeeds`. it walks
`src/domain.roles` for any `.seeds` dir and is red with the dir named. the maintainer moves the
seeds into the route (`.seeds/origin=<repo>/`), pads their ids with `rhx inventory.pad`, and the
clamp turns green. the build's `--exclude='.seeds'` is a second guard: the built `dist` holds no
seed even if a dir slips past.

## .bdd sketch

```
given the role dirs of this package
  when a role holds a `.seeds` dir at any depth
    then [t0] rolesShipNoSeeds is red and names the dir, relative to src/domain.roles
  when the seeds sit in the route, and no role holds one
    then [t1] it is green
  when the package builds
    then [t2] `dist/domain.roles/**/.seeds/**` matches 0 files
```

proof: `[t0]` was red on `skeptic/briefs/.seeds`, then `[t1]` green after the move; `[t2]` read by
`rhx globsafe` on the built dist (0 files).
