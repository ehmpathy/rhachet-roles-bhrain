# 🌙 dream: the meet's wall time rides the realized record

- kind: fix
- owner: rhachet-roles-bhrain (review meter)
- caught 2026-11-05

## .the cue that fired

a peer review of `fix-review-input-cacheability` asked that the meet's wait be recorded, so the
after-measurement can state the latency the cure costs. the round put a spinner around the
meet, so a human sees the wait and its elapsed seconds; the figure is not persisted.

## .the gap, precisely

`metrics.realized.json` `time` covers the brain call alone. a lane that waited at the meet for
a leader's poke spent that wait inside the lane's bound, and no artifact holds it. so a
latency claim about the meet can be read only off a run's stdout, never off the corpus the
meter reads.

## .the shape of the fix

- add `meetTime: IsoDuration | null` to `ReviewRunMetricsRealized` beside `meet` (null for
  `skipped.*`, and for runs before the field), and to the Omit/Pick lists that already name
  `meet` (`setMetricsRealizedOrUnmeasured`, `asReviewRunMetricsRealized`,
  `asReviewRunMetricsRealizedOfResult`, `ReviewRunMetricsRealizedOfResult`,
  `asReviewRunMetricsRealizedOfAsks`)
- measure it in `stepReviewOfCompiled` around `getOneReviewRunMeet`
- render it on the `--of run` meet row (`meet: awaited (12s)`), and sum it per meet row at
  tree and grove
- clamp: `stepReview.casePrimeMeet.integration.test.ts` `[case3]` asserts each lane's
  `meetTime` is under the meet's own stop bound (`getOneReviewPrimeMeetStopMs`)

## .why it is not done in this round

the field threads through five record shapes and every fixture that builds one, which is a
contract change across the realized record, beyond the render this round repaired. the wait
is visible now (the spinner names its elapsed seconds), so no human meets it silent.
