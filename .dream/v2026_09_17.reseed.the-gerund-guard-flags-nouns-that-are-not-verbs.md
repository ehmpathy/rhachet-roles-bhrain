# 🌙 dream: the gerund guard flags `-ing` nouns that were never verbs

- **kind** = `reseed` — the guard lives in `ehmpathy/rhachet-roles-ehmpathy`, not in this tree
- **owner** = `ehmpathy/rhachet-roles-ehmpathy` · `role=mechanic` · `claude.hooks/pretooluse.forbid-terms.gerunds`
- **caught** = 2026-09-17

## .the cue that fired

a single vision stone on `v2026_09_17.fix-budget-grant-needs-urgent-concession` took **ten blocked
writes** before its artifacts landed. several blocks were correct and taught the rule. several were
not: the guard rejected the bare noun `thing`, and its `some-` and `any-` compounds.

⇒ the cue in `rule.always.catch-dreams-for-followups` — *"you hit a broken paved path"* — and the
sharper one: the guard is **right everywhere else**, which is exactly why its false positives cost
so much. an author who trusts it cannot part a lesson from a misfire without a stop to reason it out.

## .the gap, precisely

`rule.forbid.gerunds` declares its subject exactly: **"gerunds (-ing as nouns) forbidden"**, and its
own note bounds it — *"gerund: -ing as noun"*, *"participles in verb phrases ok"*.

a **gerund is a verb form**. so the test for one is *"is there a verb stem under this suffix?"*

| word | verb stem | a gerund? | the guard says |
|---|---|---|---|
| `loading` | to load | ✅ yes | ⛔ blocked — **correct** |
| `handling` | to handle | ✅ yes | ⛔ blocked — **correct** |
| `phrasing` | to phrase | ✅ yes | ⛔ blocked — **correct** |
| `thing` | 🔴 none | ❌ no | ⛔ blocked — **false positive** |
| `something` | 🔴 none | ❌ no | ⛔ blocked — **false positive** |
| `anything` | 🔴 none | ❌ no | ⛔ blocked — **false positive** |
| `ceiling` | 🔴 none — "to ceil" is no domain verb | ❌ no | ⛔ blocked — **false positive** |

⇒ **the guard matches a bare `-ing` suffix with no verb-stem test.** its own rule brief already
states the narrower subject, so this is a drift between the detector and the rule it enforces, never
a disagreement about the rule.

🟡 **checkable evidence, this session:** each of the three `thing`-family words produced a
`🛑 BLOCKED: gerund(s) detected` whose hint read *"remove -ing suffix"* — a hint that yields `th`,
`someth`, `anyth`. **a hint that cannot be followed is the proof the match was wrong.**

⚠️ **and `nothing` is a true positive by a different route** — the separate `forbid-terms.blocklist`
guard forbids it as a vague negation, with `naught` / `none` / `absent` as its alternatives. that
one is correct and must not be swept up in this repair.

## .the shape of the fix

a stem check, in the gerund detector, before the suffix match:

1. strip the `-ing`
2. **the residue must be a plausible verb stem** — `load`, `handl(e)`, `phras(e)` pass; `th`,
   `someth`, `anyth`, `ceil` do not
3. simplest sufficient form: a **small deny-list of known non-verb `-ing` nouns**, checked first —
   `thing`, `something`, `anything`, `everything`, `ceiling`, `string`, `king`, `ring`, `spring`,
   `wing`, `during`, `morning`, `evening`, `nothing`¹
4. keep the hint honest: where a word sits on the deny-list, the gerund guard emits naught

¹ `nothing` stays forbidden — by the **blocklist** guard, with its own reason and its own
alternatives. removal from the *gerund* list changes which guard catches it, never whether it is
caught.

⇒ 🟡 **the deny-list is the cheap, correct fix and the stem check is the general one.** the
deny-list closes the measured cases today; a stem check closes the class. either is a change to one
detector, with no caller hardened against either.

## .why it is not done in this round

**the guard is not in this tree.** it ships from `ehmpathy/rhachet-roles-ehmpathy` and reaches this
worktree through `node_modules`, so the repair is unreachable from here —
`rule.always.scope-onetime-lessons-to-the-behavior`: *"a lesson whose home is another repo is
re-seeded there, never adopted here."*

and the fix fails CLEAN on its own terms: a change to a shared prose detector ripples to **every
write in every repo that boots `role=mechanic`**, so it wants its own diff and its own review,
rather than a ride along a vision stone.

## .the cost, if it stays

- every author pays a stop-and-reason at each misfire, on a guard they otherwise trust
- 🔴 **the worse cost is the one that leaves no trace**: an author who cannot name a legal
  substitution edits around the word and **weakens the sentence** to satisfy a rule that was never
  broken. `.dream/v2026_09_13.feat.a-levels-actual-concurrency-is-undiscoverable-without-a-live-stderr-line.md`
  records exactly that outcome, in its own words — *"paraphrased to drop gerunds"*
- ⇒ the guard exists to sharpen prose. on these words it blunts it.
