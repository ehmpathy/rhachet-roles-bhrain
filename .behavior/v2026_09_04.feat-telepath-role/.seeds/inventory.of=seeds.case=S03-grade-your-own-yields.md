# S03 · grade your own yields against your own rules

## .said

> *"🟡 and hold yourself to it: **a role about terse prose whose own briefs ramble has failed its own rubric.** grade your yields against the rules you write."*

🟡 verbatim.

## .settled

a rule's author is its first subject.

the concept, stripped of this round:

- an artifact that prescribes a property and lacks it **refutes itself** — a reader who meets the gap discounts the rule, never the artifact
- so the author's own output is the rule's **first and cheapest test case**. it costs no extra pass; the work was authored anyway
- ⇒ **self-application is a correctness check, never a courtesy.** if the rule cannot be obeyed by the one actor who understands it best, it is unenforceable by anyone else
- 🟡 the check is falsifiable and must be run rather than claimed. *"i wrote it tersely"* is the same self-read that `case=3` measures as ineffective
