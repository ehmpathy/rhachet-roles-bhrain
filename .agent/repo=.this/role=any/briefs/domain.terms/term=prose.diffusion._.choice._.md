# domain.term: diffusion

term.chosen   = diffusion
term.kind     = noun
term.boundary = prose
term.synonyms.forbidden:
- burial
- dilution
- flatness
- vagueness

## .what
`diffusion` is **a passage whose purpose is spread evenly across its prose**, so no line outranks
its neighbours and the reader cannot point at the one that carries it.

the point is present. that is the whole definition — an absent point is a different defect, and
to cover both with one word is what each forbidden synonym does.

## 🟡 .it grades FINDABILITY, never presence or length

| the state | the word |
|---|---|
| the point was never made | absent — not this |
| the point is there, and cannot be found | **diffusion** |
| the point is there, and buried under surplus | still diffusion — the surplus is a separate charge |

⇒ so a three-line passage diffuses if it carries three claims of equal weight, and a long one
does not if its purpose sits in one place. **length is neither necessary nor sufficient**, which is
why `dilution` and `flatness` were both rejected: each smuggles a volume claim the word must not make.

## .the pole pair — one act, two failures

| word | what happened | the repair |
|---|---|---|
| **diffusion** | no line elevated | elevate one |
| `emphasis` noise | every line elevated | cut back to one |

⇒ they are peers under one act, so a passage can fail both at once — many marked lines, and the real
needle unmarked among them. the pair is declared in `rule.forbid.emphasis-noise`.

## .the peer terms it does not name

| word | what it is | why not `diffusion` |
|---|---|---|
| `narration` | prose where a tree was owed | a shape defect; a bulletized passage can still diffuse |
| `condensation` | the cut to the 95% bar | it changes the volume; this changes the gradient |
| `obfuscation` | what the author left off the page | this grades what is on it |
| `emphasis` | the device that ranks a line | its absence is what diffusion is |
