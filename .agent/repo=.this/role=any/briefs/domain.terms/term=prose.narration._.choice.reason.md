# domain.term.choice.reason: narration

## .etymology

**narration** — from latin *narrare*, "to relate, to recount", from *gnarus*, "one who knows". the
noun names **the act that recounts in sequence**.

⇒ that is precisely the defect: the concepts are related to the reader **in the order the author
walked them**, rather than at the depth their relations put them. the etymology names the sequence,
and the sequence is what the rule forbids.

## .why the ACT and not the artifact — `narration` over `narratives`

this is the whole reason the word was chosen, and it turns on the taxonomy in
`rules101.structure` (reviewer):

| directive | the claim | what a reviewer reads |
|---|---|---|
| `forbid.$state` | the artifact must not be X | the artifact |
| `forbid.$act` | you must not do X | what the author did |

`narratives` names an artifact, and the artifact is legal:

- a narrative in a `.demo=` file is correct
- a narrative a human asked for is correct
- ⇒ so `forbid.narratives` forbids an artifact this canon permits, and its own boundary section would have to take the name back

⇒ **`narration` names the act that offers one where a tree was owed** — which is the defect, and it
is the only form of the word that does not contradict its own exemptions.

## .why not the rejected synonyms

- `narratives` — see above. it names a legal artifact, so it cannot name a defect
- `prose-dump` · `wall-of-text` — both name length, and length is not the defect. a short
  chained paragraph is narration; a long outline is legal. 🟡 **a word that names the wrong axis
  teaches the wrong repair** — *"make it shorter"* rather than *"render the tree"*
- `story-mode` — jargon, and it reads as a config rather than a defect
- the `-ing` forms need no entry: `rule.forbid.gerunds` bans them outright, which is a second and
  independent reason that family is unavailable

## .evidence

- **code + contracts**: `rule.forbid.narration` (booted say-tier) composes the word;
  `rule.require.bulletize` and `define.bulletize` each name it as their counter.
  `rule.require.domain-term-itemization` binds all three
- **the pair is precedented, not coined**: `require.brevity` ⇄ `forbid.rambles` is the same
  target/smell shape in the ehmpathy canon, and `rules101.structure` prescribes the split
- **the peer boundary is checkable**: `forbid.chronological-accretion` grades time-order;
  this grades shape. a walkthrough trips both; a chained paragraph of timeless claims trips
  only this one. ⇒ the two are distinguishable by a test, so they are not synonyms
  (`S09`, the wisher: *"both should exist"*)
- **invariant**: narration is a defect **only** where no ask licensed it. the ask is the licence,
  and an implied ask is not one — *"explain this to me"* asks for a clear transfer, and an outline
  is the clearer one
