# define.bhrain-repo-mascot

## .what

this is the `rhachet-roles-bhrain` repo. the mascot is the **owl** 🦉, never the turtle 🐢 — the
turtle belongs to `rhachet-roles-ehmpathy`.

all stdout, treestruct headers, and vibes here use owl glyphs and owl-themed phrases.

## .the two axes — repo mascot vs role marker

🟡 **a repo mascot and a role marker are different things, and this brief governs only the first.**

| axis | what it marks | bhrain |
|---|---|---|
| **repo mascot** | which repo you are in — the voice, the vibe, the wisdom lines | 🦉 the owl |
| **role marker** | which role holds the floor — a header, a label, an error prefix | per role |

a role marker does **not** displace the owl. the owl remains the voice.

🟡 **and a marker does not carry the mascot with it.** a role marker is ONE glyph. to prefix the
owl onto it — `🦉📜 learner` — claims a mascot every other role holds equally, and it breaks the
one-glyph-per-role shape a reader scans by.

### the role markers

| role | marker | why |
|---|---|---|
| driver | 🗿 the moai | stones enroute — a driver walks a route one stone at a time, and a moai is a carved stone that marks a place |
| achiever | 💎 the gem | pressure into what endures — an achiever holds a goal under pressure until it crystallizes |
| learner | 📜 the scroll | what is written down outlives the round — the learner's whole subject is durable retention |
| librarian | 📚 the books | a collection, curated — the librarian operates between documents, over within one |
| researcher | 🔭 the telescope | far sight — the researcher's subject is what the repo does not yet hold |
| reviewer | 🔍 the magnifier | a close look — a reviewer examines an artifact against declared rules |
| telepath | 🔮 the crystal ball | the concept transferred whole, without the words — the closest a glyph gets to telepathy |
| thinker | 🪐 the ringed planet | cosmic, vast — the thinker's subject is the whole space of a problem |
| reflector | 🌕 the full moon | illumination — a reflector shines back what was already there |

⇒ **the test for this axis: does the glyph name what the role DOES?** the moai passes because the
driver's whole subject is stones. a glyph picked for looks does not pass.

🟡 **every other test is the glyph register's, not this brief's.** the palette rule, the
one-concept check, and the claim procedure live in `term=glyph._.choice.reason.md` and
`domain.glyphs/catalog.of=glyph._.md`. **read them before you claim a marker** — a grep alone
proves a glyph unused, never unclaimed.

## .where each one goes

```
🗿 route.mutate guard                              # role marker — a header, names WHO
🦉 to chase all paths, is to reach none. focus.    # repo mascot — the VOICE
🗿 route.foreground guard: failed to parse …       # role marker — an error prefix
```

⇒ **be consistent within a surface.** the same label must not be 🗿 in a header and 🦉 in an
error — a reader who sees two glyphs on one label reads two speakers.

🟡 **and do not coin a third glyph for a concept a role marker already covers.** a road for
*"pave a route"* is a second glyph for what 🗿 already marks
(`rule.always.reuse-pavement-before-improvise`).
