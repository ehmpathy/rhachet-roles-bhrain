## .the cue that fired

this round repaired the self-review trigger by the rule *remove the derived component from the key,
and give the key ONE owner* — `getSelfReviewTriggeredPaths`. a `has-consistent-mechanisms` self

it does not.

## .the gap, precisely

`${stone}.blocked.triggered` is spelled out at **four prod call sites**:

| site | shape |
|---|---|
| `blocked/getBlockedTriggeredReport.ts:16` | `path.join(routeDir, \`${stone}.blocked.triggered\`)` |
| `blocked/setBlockedTriggeredReport.ts:17` | the same, spelled again |
| `blocked/delBlockedTriggeredReport.ts:13` | the same, spelled again |
| `stones/delStoneGuardArtifacts.ts:46` | a **glob** — `.route/${stone}.blocked.triggered` |

**checkable:** `rhx grepsafe --pattern 'blocked\.triggered' --glob '*.ts'` over `src/`.

⇒ this is the **`c8′` defect class** the round's own vision names — *one derived quantity, computed
differently at several call sites* — unrepaired in the family next door. the round measured that
class twice first-party (the route segment, and the `rN` ordinal), so it is not hypothetical.

🟡 **it has NOT misfired yet, and that is luck rather than design.** all four spell the same string
today. the defect is that no mechanism holds them in agreement — exactly the state the self-review
family was in before this round, and that one *did* misfire, twice.

⚠️ **row 4 is a glob, not a path**, so an owner must either return both shapes or the glob site
stays hand-spelled with a pointer to the owner. do not force a path-shaped owner onto a glob
consumer — that is how a "single source of truth" quietly grows a second source.

## .the shape of the fix

1. add `getBlockedTriggeredPath({ stone, route })` beside the three `blocked/` operations, on the
   shape `getSelfReviewTriggeredPaths` already sets
2. wire the three `blocked/` call sites to it
3. decide row 4 deliberately — either the owner also yields the route-relative glob, or
   `delStoneGuardArtifacts` keeps its literal with a `.note` that names the owner
4. **bite-check**: alter the owner's filename segment, confirm the `blocked/` suites and
   `setStoneAsBlocked.test.ts` go red, restore, confirm green

🟡 the suites already exist — `getBlockedTriggeredReport.test.ts`, `getBlockedChallengeDecision.test.ts`,
`setStoneAsBlocked.test.ts`, and `delStoneGuardArtifacts.test.ts:507` all pin the literal — so the
clamp is there before the fix is.

## .why it is not done in this round

**SAFE ✅ / CLEAN 🔴.**

safe — it is pure path derivation, and four suites already pin the literal it would own.

**not clean** — the `blocked/` family is a subsystem this round never opened. this wish is a
self-review-trigger repair; its diff touches `guard/review/self/`, `guard/tree/`, and two
`stones/` operations. to open `blocked/` adds a fourth subsystem and a new operation with its own
tests, to a round whose yield **already flags scope as its own live concern** (four independent
repairs to one gate, stated as a question the council may want to split).

⇒ a fifth deliverable would worsen precisely what the round has already told the council to watch.

## .the lesson

> **a repair that names a defect CLASS has an obligation the repair itself does not discharge:
> to go look for the other members.**

the round wrote *`c8′` is not a one-off defect; it is a CLASS* into its own vision, and then
searched for further members only when a self review asked. **the class was named in one artifact
and un-swept in the next**, and one grep found a third member.

⇒ the transferable form: **when you write the words "this is a class, not an instance", the very
next act is the census.** otherwise the sentence is a claim about the codebase that nobody checked.
