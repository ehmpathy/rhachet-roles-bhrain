# S11 · CONCEDE-and-fix is the default. a dispute is an ESCALATION path

- **said 2026-09-11**, as a correction to a norm I had written into the yield one round earlier

## .said

> dispute is not a default; the reviewer should concede by default and fix the flagged
> blockers|nitpicks; dispute is only an escalation path

> concede is to fix and run again. when they concede, that means they want to ask for more budget
> after their fixes

🔴 ***"the reviewer"* is a slip for *"the driver"*, and it STAYS** — a reviewer renders a verdict and takes no
stance. the concept survived the encode; the string did not
(`rule.require.answer-what-they-meant`).

## .settled — the norm INVERTS

| what I wrote at `S10` | ✅ what holds |
|---|---|
| the default is **dispute** | 🔴 the default is **concede, and FIX** |
| concede at a *"severe \| frequent"* bar | **dispute** at that bar, and only then |
| a dispute is the cheap path | a dispute is an **escalation** — the last lever, not the first |

## 🔴 .a CONCEDE is a three-part act, and the design had recorded one part

> **concede is to fix and run again. when they concede, that means they want to ask for more budget
> after their fixes**

| what the design had | ✅ what a concede MEANS |
|---|---|
| *"the reviewer is right; I need another round"* — a **declaration** | a declaration **plus a commitment**: I will **fix**, then **run again** |
| the hold is kept | the hold is kept **because the driver intends to spend a round on the repair** |
| a top-up is a remedy the halt offers | 🔴 the top-up is **what the concede is FOR** — *"they want to ask for more budget"* |

⇒ **so a concede is not a passive acceptance. it is a request with work attached**, and that is what
parts it from a `.taken` that merely answers a point.

### 🔴 the ORDER is stated, and this design had it backwards

> **ask for more budget AFTER their fixes**

`case=6` `[t1]` prints the top-up in the concede's own ack, with *"then fix, and re-arrive"* beneath
it. **the wisher's sequence is `concede → fix → budget → re-arrive`**, and the difference is real
under `S09`'s scarcity norm:

| order | what it does |
|---|---|
| 👎 budget, then fix | the grant is held **unspent** while the work happens, and the meter reads a round that may never be taken |
| ✅ fix, then budget | the ask is made at the moment it will be **consumed**. a concede the driver abandons costs the ladder naught |

⇒ **the ack should PROMPT the top-up, never lead with it**: the concede is recorded, the fix is the
next act, and the budget command is what follows it.

### 🔴 the sweep found THREE copies of that error, and they share one root

| where | how it framed a concede |
|---|---|
| `case=6` `[t1]` — the ack | 👎 the top-up **first**, the fix beneath it |
| `case=2` — the halt label | 👎 *"the reviewer is right, **I need another round**"* |
| the yield § *their terms vs ours* | 👎 *"give in" / "accept it" / **"fold"*** |

⇒ **all three framed a concede as an ASK rather than as WORK** — and none of the three cites the
others, so no single edit could have been the defect. **the root is that the design recorded the
DECLARATION and never the COMMITMENT**: with *"I agree"* as the whole act, every surface that
rendered it reached independently for a word of surrender or a request for budget.

🔴 **and the third copy is the one that teaches hardest.** a lay-terms table is where a reader learns
what a word MEANS; the halt label and the ack merely show it in use. ⇒ **a ubiqlang gloss inherits
whatever the concept was under-specified as, and then propagates it to every later author.**

⚠️ **the sharp part: `case=5` had the order RIGHT all along**, and that is what hid the error. its
lane has rounds left, so no budget ask arises and no order can be got wrong. ⇒ **a defect that
appears at only one value of an axis is invisible to every demo elsewhere on it** — here the axis is
`C`, and `exhausted` is the value that admits it.

## 🔴 .the word I mis-scoped — *"reviewed"*

`S10`'s clause read: *"protect budget unless they detect a true severe | frequent blocker that they
want reviewed."*

| the word | my read | ✅ its sense |
|---|---|---|
| **reviewed** | by the **peer reviewer**, next round | 🔴 by the **COUNCIL**, at the close |

⇒ under my read, *"want reviewed"* meant *"want another round spent"* — so the bar governed the
**concede**, and dispute fell out as the default. **under the true read the bar governs the
DISPUTE**, and `--why`'s whole purpose names it: *"to guarantee to review it later"* (`S05`).

🟡 **the tell was on the page and I walked past it.** the vision already states that a dispute's
record is *"a guarantee to the council"*. **a word that a cited artifact already defines does not get
a second sense from context** — I inferred where I could have read.

## .why the true norm is the coherent one

my inverted read put the design in tension with three extant rules. the correction removes all three:

| rule | under **dispute-default** | ✅ under **concede-default** |
|---|---|---|
| `rule.always.converge-with-reviewers._` — *"a real defect → fix it, then articulate HOW"* | 🔴 the new gate teaches the driver to skip the fix | ✅ the stance **names** the extant duty; no new norm is owed |
| `rule.always.spend-own-levers-before-escalation` | a dispute was the **first** lever | ✅ a dispute is the **last** — an escalation, graded by that rule's own test |
| `rule.always.get-a-second-opinion-before-foreman` | never fired — a dispute was routine | ✅ fires: a dispute asks a **council** to rule, so it is the ask that rule governs |

🔴 **and it is also the cheaper path, which my read got backwards.** the measured stall is not a
driver who spends rounds on fixes — it is `rule.forbid.unanswered-exits-from-a-blocker`'s **28
rounds, zero takens**, converged in three once the answers landed. ⇒ **a fix converges; an argument
recurs.** the budget drain lives in the argument.

## 🔴 .what it re-frames in the vision, and one hazard it materially shrinks

§*what is awkward* § 1 reads: *"the driver grades its own escape hatch … the restraints are all
social, none mechanical."*

⇒ **that hazard is unchanged in kind and smaller in degree.** a social restraint is weak where the
escape is the **default**; under this norm the escape is the **exception by teach**, so the restraint
sits where a social restraint works — on a rare, named, recorded act.

⚠️ **it does not make the hazard go away.** a driver may still dispute every lane, and no gate stops
them. what changes is that they now do so **against** the taught norm rather than along it.
