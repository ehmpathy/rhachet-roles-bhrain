# 🌙 dream: the drive-state lock moves onto `withSimpleMutex`

kind = fix · owner = rhachet-roles-bhrain · caught 2026-10-23

## .the cue that fired

a full-suite red in `mutateDriveBlockerState.integration.test.ts [case2]`: eight queued writers,
and the tail threw at the acquire deadline though no hold ran long. the repair in this round makes
the deadline bound ONE hold (it restarts at each observed handoff, keyed on the lock file's inode
and birth time). the repair sits inside a lock this repo forbids by rule.

## .the gap, precisely

`src/domain.operations/route/drive/withDriveStateLock.ts` claims with `fs.open(lockPath, 'wx')`,
writes a pid into the file, and reaps on `kill(pid, 0)` behind a second `wx` claim
(`delAbandonedDriveStateLock`). each of those three is a shape `rule.forbid.hand-rolled-locks`
names: an exclusive create as a claim, a holder carried in a file, a crash rule invented per lock.

## .the shape of the fix

- the critical section is one read, one write, one rename — a watermark transition, so a short
  lease fits (`rule.require.locks-via-with-simple-mutex`)
- `withSimpleMutex(critical, { key: statePath, cache: <simple-on-disk-cache at .route/>, lease })`
- delete `delAbandonedDriveStateLock` and the `.reap` claim; the lease owns crash release
- keep the clamps: `[case1]`, `[case2]`, `[case4]` (a live hold refuses loud), `[case8]` (a queue
  that moves never throws); retire `[case6]`/`[case7]`, which test the pid reap

## .why it is not done in this round

safe, not clean. the move replaces the claim, the crash rule, and two error renders across every
writer of the drive state, and adds a dependency — a contract change in a PR whose subject is role
boot budgets. the queue-deadline repair is the part that made the suite red, and it landed here.
