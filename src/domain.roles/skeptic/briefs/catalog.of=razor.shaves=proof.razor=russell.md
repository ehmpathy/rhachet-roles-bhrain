# razor: russell

shaves: a claim with no owner or weight · settled by: logic

## .the question

> **am I the one who asserts? then the proof is mine.**

the burden sits with whoever asserts. a twin of `razor=hitchens`: hitchens asks what came with a claim, russell asks who owes it.

## .the source

- Russell, "Is There a God?", written 1952 for *Illustrated* magazine, published only after his death
- read via https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell%27s_teapot

## .fails when

- a negative claim is asserted too: "it is not X" owes proof as well
- Russell's own move is partly a base rate: nobody thinks the teapot "sufficiently likely to be taken into account in practice" (`razor=base-rate`)

## .run by

`rule.always.trust-but-verify`
