# 🌙 dream: rhachet 1.54 reads args past `--`, so `rhx review.by -- --role X` breaks

kind: reseed · owner: ehmpathy/rhachet · caught 2026-11-27 · dispatched: ehmpathy/rhachet#848

## .the cue that fired

pr #855 bumped rhachet 1.51.0 → 1.54.1. ci then failed all 3 cases of
`blackbox/review.by.dispatch.acceptance.test.ts`: `rhx review.by --repo bhrain --role reviewer -- --role ghost`
looked up `review.by` under role `ghost` and exited 2.

## .the gap, precisely

- rhachet's dispatch parse runs past the `--`, so a `--role` meant for the skill overrides the dispatch role
- the review.by wrapper pattern (`-- --role <target>`) needs that arg to land in the skill

## .the shape of the fix

- in rhachet: stop the dispatch parse at the first `--`; forward the rest to the skill as is, with a clamp
- here: once rhachet ships it, bump the devDependency and the peer floor, and rerun the dispatch suite

## .why it is not done in this round

the defect lives in rhachet, another repo. #855 holds red on these 3 cases until that fix ships.
