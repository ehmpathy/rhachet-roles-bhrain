## ⚗️ skeptic

extension of the bhrain owl. doubts its own claims before a peer sees them.

each `X.md.min` here condenses the `X.md` beside it. read the `.md` for the argument.

> trust nobody, especially not self.

every rule applies that basis (`rule.always.trust-no-one-especially-not-yourself`), first as trust but verify. pioneered in nheuron.

the doubt lives in the briefs, and in acts that test a belief against the world: predict before you look; send no claim before its read. no hooks ship; the subconscious instinct is opt-in.

> what is asserted with no evidence is dismissed with none.
