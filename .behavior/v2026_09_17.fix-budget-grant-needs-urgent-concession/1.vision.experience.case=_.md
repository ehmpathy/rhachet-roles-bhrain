🔴 **no cell is IMPOSSIBLE.** every cell is attemptable by construction — one command, two actors, any

🔴 **and the 12 `refused` cells ARE the rule's `forbidden` class — this section denied it until `r2`

## 🔴 .the four lenses — eight spotlights, one walk, one seam, one lever

`define.experience._.metric=boundary-density` asks for **both shapes**: *"dense journeys for
breadth, focused spotlights for each sharp critipath's fail-safe."* and
`howto.experience.enumerate` move 3 names a **third** place a sharp edge hides, which neither shape
reaches.

| lens | which files | what it buys |
|---|---|---|
| **spotlights** | `case=1` … `case=8`, `case=13` | each cell's **fail-safe**, proven — the safety obligation |
| 🔴 **the dense walk** | `case=9` | the **transitions between cells**, which is where this design's sequential risk sits |
| 🔴 **the seam** | `case=10` | 🔴 **a handoff the GRID cannot express** — cli → domain op, where `--stone` matches N and the predicate takes 1 |
| 🔴 **the peer lever** | `case=11` | 🔴 **a second command that moves the same QUANTITY** — which no axis over one command's inputs can reach |
| 🔴 **the overlap** | `case=12` | 🔴 **two movers in one cell** — which no product over *states* can reach, since the hazard is an order of arrival rather than a state |

🔴 **the last two lenses are ones a grid is structurally blind to, and they are blind in different
ways.** every axis is a **state of the gated command's inputs**. a **seam** is a type mismatch across
a handoff *inside* that command, which no cell of any state-product holds. a **peer lever** is an
entirely different command, which no product over *this* command's inputs can reach at all.

⇒ **each was found by a question the decomposition method does not supply.** `case=10` came from
`howto.experience.enumerate` move 3's locator — *"a rejection, a limit, a **seam**"*. `case=11` came
from the requirements guide's *"is the scope too large, too small, or misdirected?"*, and the answer
was **too small by one noun**: the wish governs a command, the outcome governs a number.

🟡 **the transferable lesson, since both landed at `r2` and neither at `r1`:** a decomposition
inherits the scope of the artifact it decomposes. **to walk the product is to assume the correct
subject was put on the axes** — and that assumption is checked by a different question than the walk
asks.

⚠️ **this catalog held only spotlights until `r2` self-review**, and that is the *thin* case the
stone's guide names. three risks were invisible to every single-cell demo and land hard as a walk:
🔴 the **`F07` re-grade** is a two-command sequence (`[b3]`→`[b4]`) · 🔴 **`F10` is reached by
ACCIDENT** (`[b5]`) rather than hunted · and the two **interact** — one urgent grade buys a round on
every dry lane on the stone, which neither fulcrum states alone.

⚠️ **and the property that redeems them is bounded PER LANE, never per stone** — `[b6]`: the warrant
is consumed by the round it buys, for each lane that **ran**; a lane that sat dry mints no given, so
no key moved for it and the warrant is still good there (`[b6x]`). ⇒ **one generation wide for each

## .the itemized alterpaths, and why each stays undemoed

### ⚠️ `driver × disputed × exhausted` — the correction this grid took at `r2`

**it was graded `criti` and left itemized**, on the claim that *"its refusal stdout, its remedy, and
its aha are identical to `case=1`"*, with a recorded trigger: *"if the refusal copy ever
discriminates by B, this cell owes a demo at once."*

🔴 **the trigger had already fired, inside this grid, before it was written.** `case=6` renders a
refusal that leads with a **C**-specific diagnosis and states at `[t3]` that *"the gate reads B; C
is copy, not verdict."* ⇒ if the copy discriminates on the axis the predicate does **not** read, it
certainly discriminates on the one it does.

and the claim was false on its own terms — `case=8` § *why it is not `case=1`* names three
differences, of which one is **structural**: `--severity` is forbidden on a dispute
(`setStoneAsConcernAbsorbed.ts:92-101`), so this is the one refused cell whose lift is shut **by
construction** rather than by a predicate over a field the driver writes.

⇒ **the cell is now `case=8`.** the lesson kept: *a claim that two cells are alike must be checked
against the copy the design actually renders, never against the predicate they share.*

### the `forgiven` column (8 cells, both slabs)

a human overruled the level, so the lane owes no rounds and a grant changes no outcome. the verdict
follows B unchanged; the *experience* is a no-op either way. ⇒ **happy, alter, itemized** — and the
refusal in the `driver` half is harmless, since the driver had no use for the round.

🟡 **it is still refused, deliberately.** a carve-out at `forgiven` would be a bypass: overrule a
level, then top up a *different* level under cover of it. the gate reads B, never C (`F10`).

### `driver × urgent × {live, malfunctioned, forgiven}` (3 cells) — 🔴 one is now DEMOED

permitted, and the permission is **pointless rather than wrong**: the lane in question does not need
the round.

🔴 **`urgent × live` is the residual hole, and `r1` peer review moved it from itemized to demoed.**
one urgent concession permits a grant of any size onto a lane with budget to spare. the gate bounds
*whether*, never *where* or *how many* — see `F10` (no exhaustion requirement) and `dimensions §6`
(no size cap). **both are recorded best-guesses, not oversights**, and `case=13` now walks the cell
rather than asserts it.

🟡 **`urgent × {malfunctioned, forgiven}` stay itemized**, and the reason is that they are the same
pointless permission with a lane that cannot use the round for a *different* cause — a break rather
than a surplus. `case=6` already walks what a malfunction changes, and the `forgiven` note below
covers the other.

🔴 **these three cells are itemized, and `case=9` demonstrates that the hole they leave is LARGER
than any one of them.** each cell alone reads as *"a pointless permission on one lane"*; `[b5x]` and

ran. ⇒ **a per-cell itemization understated the risk, and only the walk surfaced it** — which is the
enforcement line's own argument for a dense journey, met from the other direction.
