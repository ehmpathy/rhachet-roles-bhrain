# 🌙 dream: a permission denial echoes the whole allowlist into context

- kind = reseed
- owner = ehmpathy/rhachet-roles-ehmpathy (role=mechanic, `pretooluse.check-permissions.sh`)
- caught 2026-10-21

## .the cue that fired

`rhx reflect.on.token.spend` on its own drive session ranked `tool.result · Bash · cat` third in
repaid (5.6M tokens) and placed ~13k tokens of `Bash · chmod` results in context, though no
`chmod` ever ran. each was a denial payload.

## .the gap, precisely

- every command not on the allowlist is refused by the PreToolUse hook with the full allowlist —
  ~200 `[e]`/`[p]` rows, about 6k tokens — on stderr, which the harness feeds back as the result
- the payload is identical on every denial, and a retry of the same command is the documented
  way through, so one blocked command costs ~6k tokens to read and then that 6k is held in
  context and repaid on every later call until a compaction
- on a 428-call session the reflect tool measured ~13k tokens of denial payloads held, repaid
  per call; the allowlist is already printed once at SessionStart

## .the shape of the fix

1. on denial, print the one-line verdict, the closest 3–5 allowlist rows to the denied command
   (prefix match on its first word), and a pointer to the full list
2. keep the full list at SessionStart only, where it is read once
3. measure the delta with `rhx reflect.on.token.spend` before and after
