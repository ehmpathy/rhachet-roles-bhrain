# rule.forbid.defects-admitted-unrecorded

> **no defect you name in your own conduct leaves the turn as prose alone. it lands as a record: an incident, a brief, or a caught dream.**

twin of `rule.always.file-an-incident-for-a-defect-you-name-in-yourself`.

## .the shape

| 👎 | 👍 |
|---|---|
| "you're right, I should have checked the log first" — and the turn ends | the same line, plus the path of the brief or the dream that holds the lesson |
| "my mistake" in a reply, no record anywhere | the record's path cited in the reply |

## .the test

> **does this reply admit a defect of mine, and does it cite a record of it?**

## .enforcement

blocker: an admitted defect ("I should have", "my fault", "I was wrong", "I missed") with no record cited in the same turn
false positive: a quote of someone else's admission
