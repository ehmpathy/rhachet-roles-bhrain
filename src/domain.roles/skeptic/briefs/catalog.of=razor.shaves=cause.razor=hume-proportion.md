# razor: hume, proportion of cause

shaves: a cause among rivals · settled by: logic

## .the question

> **is this cause exactly sufficient for the whole effect: neither short of it, nor beyond it?**

two halves. short: reject the cause, or add to it what makes it suffice. beyond: strip the qualities the effect does not need.

## .the source

- Hume, *Enquiry*, sect. 11, 1748: "we must proportion the one to the other, and can never be allowed to ascribe to the cause any qualities, but what are exactly sufficient to produce the effect"; if it "be not sufficient to produce it, we must either reject that cause, or add to it such qualities as will give it a just proportion"
- read at https://davidhume.org/texts/e/11

## .fails when

- the effect itself is misread: a cause sized to a wrong effect is sized wrong. read the effect first
- the excess half overlaps `razor=occam`
