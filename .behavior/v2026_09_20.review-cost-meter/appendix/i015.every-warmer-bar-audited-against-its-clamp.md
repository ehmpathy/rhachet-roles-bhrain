constructor — and both `[case1]` assertions go red while `[case2]`'s two correctly stay green, since
they never call the constructor.

🟡 the guard's tallier (`getReviewCountsViaBrain`) sends `briefs: []` and was deliberately **not**
wired. it reads a review's stdout, shares no prefix with any round, and has no pin to preserve — an
empty system prompt is correct there and would be a defect at the other two. the distinction is
recorded in the constructor rather than left for the next reader to re-derive.

### the transferable lesson

🔴 **a clamp can only see the artifact it reads. an invariant BETWEEN two artifacts is, by
construction, invisible to both their suites.**

`stepReview`'s tests can prove its pin works — `stepReview.casePromptCache.integration.test` does
exactly that, live, with a cold control. `getOnePokeMeasured`'s tests can prove a poke is sent and
measured. **both suites pass, in full, with the pins parted.** no amount of per-file rigor closes
this, because neither file is wrong on its own.

⇒ the detector: **for every value derived at two or more call sites, ask what artifact holds them
equal — and if the answer is "the author's care", that is the gap.** the repair is not a test; a test
would clamp one site. the repair is to make the second site *impossible to write*, then clamp the one
place that remains.

🟡 and it names the limit of the ladder's first twenty-two rungs honestly: each read **one artifact
harder**. this one read **the space BETWEEN two artifacts** — which is why twenty-two passes of
ever-sharper rigor walked past it, and why the prose that names the harm has sat in
`asWarmerPokePrompt` unacted-upon since the day it was written.

🔴 **and the pass caught its own first draft in the act.** it found the pin, repaired it, ran the
gates, and wrote this section — on a verdict that rested on *"the warmer has exactly one such
value"*, a claim it had never checked. the enumeration above was added after, and it is what makes
this an audit rather than an anecdote. ⇒ the discipline the yield already states, turned on the
instrument that states it: **a read that halts on its first hit reports a sample with an audit's
confidence**, and the only cure is to write the population down BEFORE the verdict.

## 🔴 .the TWENTY-FOURTH pass — the wall-clock read, and a default nobody ran

### the instrument

pass 21 asked *"does this clamp still exercise what it was written for?"* of each clamp it found.
this pass aimed the same question at the **schedule as a whole**, and one rung further out:

> **which of the plan's DECLARED promises has no case that runs at its real values?**

the promise in question is the one the product is named for. `PLAN_OF_WARMER_DEFAULT` is
`{ pokes: 3, everyMinutes: 15 }`, and `0.wish` argues that pair at length: *"`arm→read` is the sum of
two gaps that must each stay under the ttl."* so the default is a **45-minute coverage window**, and
that window is the whole reason a warmer exists rather than a single poke.

### the four hypotheses that died on contact

each was a plausible clock defect, and each was already answered by the code:

| the hypothesis | why it died |
|---|---|
| a `fault` advances `lastTouchAt`, so the next gap drifts from a touch that never happened | 🟡 **already argued, at `runReviewWarmer.ts:334-345`** — a DELIBERATE overstatement with its cost named, chosen against the burst counterfactual |
| brain latency accrues across pokes, so poke 3 lands past the ttl | the drift is **conservative**: latency moves a poke EARLIER relative to its touch, never later |
| a `defer` waits the full cadence again, so a deferred round costs two gaps | 🟡 **found, fixed, and clamped** — `expect(scene.clock.waits).toEqual([15, 10])` |
| a suspended host resumes and fires the rest of the plan at once | bounded by `k`: the budget is spent, the spend is capped, and `[case1]` clamps it |

⇒ **four dead ends is the pass at work**, not the pass in default. each refutation narrowed the aim,
and the fourth is what produced the find: the defer clamp exists, it is real, and it runs **two**
wakes.

### the find — every multi-poke case ran at a cadence of ZERO

the sweep of the suite's plans:

| shape | cases | cadence |
|---|---|---|
| multi-poke | most of the suite | 🔴 **`everyMinutes: 0`** |
| nonzero cadence | `[case4]` alone | `15`, and it spends **ONE** poke |

so **no case had ever walked three pokes at a real cadence**, and the 45-minute window was asserted
in `0.wish`, argued in `asReviewWarmerPlan`, measured in production — and clamped by naught.

🟡 **the reason it hid is the defer clamp.** a genuine defect had been found there and fixed, and its
clamp reads `[15, 10]` — two wakes, a real cadence, an arithmetic assertion. a reader who finds it
concludes the cadence is covered. it covers **one** gap's arithmetic; it says naught about whether
three gaps compose into the window the default promises.

### the repair

`[case9]` — the default plan, run to completion, with three assertions:

| the assertion | what it pins |
|---|---|
| `scene.clock.waits === [15, 15, 15]` | every gap is a FULL cadence — never one, never accumulated |
| the poke stamps are `10:15 · 10:30 · 10:45` | the pokes land on the wall clock the plan PROMISES |
| `stub.calls.length === PLAN_OF_WARMER_DEFAULT.pokes`, acts `poke poke poke retire` | it spent exactly its declared budget, then terminated |

🟡 the third reads the constant rather than a literal `3`, so a change to the default cannot leave the
case green against a plan it no longer describes.

### 🔴 the discovery the neuter made — the cadence is defended TWICE

the neuter was `waitOfNextWake = 0` in place of `= input.plan.everyMinutes`. the docblock's first
draft predicted a burst: three pokes inside a minute, coverage over at t+16m, pokes 2 and 3 that warm
each other and no review. **the neuter refuted it** — 2 of 4 red, and the stamps assertion **passed**:

```
waits  [15, 0, 15, 0, 15]           acts  poke defer poke defer poke retire
stamps 10:15 · 10:30 · 10:45        — UNCHANGED
```

⇒ the wake verdict's `sinceTouch < cadence → defer` rule is a **second, independent guard** on the
same promise. a collapsed wait costs an extra free wake and buys the collapse naught: the warmer
wakes early, computes that the last touch is too recent, defers, and the poke still lands on time.

🟡 **defence in depth that no artifact had stated** — and it is only visible from a neuter, because
both mechanisms are correct, so no read of either one alone reveals that the other would cover it.

### the transferable lesson

🔴 **a clamp on a defect is not a clamp on the promise that defect violated.**

the defer defect was real, the fix was right, the clamp was proven red. and the clamp's subject is
**the defect's arithmetic**, never **the window's composition** — two different claims that share a
vocabulary. so the artifact that most looked like cadence coverage was the artifact that made the
absence invisible.

⇒ the detector: **for each promise the product makes in prose, name the case that runs it at its real
values.** where the answer is a clamp written for a defect near it, that is the gap — a defect clamp
is aimed at what went wrong once, and a promise needs a case aimed at what must hold always.

🟡 and the pass's own prose was wrong before its neuter corrected it, which is the third time on this
branch that **the instrument had to be verified before its output was a claim**. the docblock now
records what the neuter proved rather than what the author expected — so the case documents a real
architectural property instead of a harm the mechanism refutes.

## 🔴 .the TWENTY-FIFTH pass — the inherited-premise read, and a foundation that stops short

### the instrument

pass 23 asked which values our code derives twice. this pass asked the next question outward, and it
is the first on this ladder whose subject is **not in this repo**:

> **which of this subsystem's premises are INHERITED from a dependency — and does that dependency's
> own evidence cover the case we depend on?**

it was provoked by pass 24's find. the ledgers said poke 1 misses in 21 of 24, and a claim in
`getOnePokeMeasured.ts:44-47` stood between that measurement and any conclusion about why:

> *"the digest hashes the brief's TEXT, never its path, so a warmer-owned path costs the pin naught"*

a claim about a **dependency's** internals, asserted in a comment, load-bearing, and unsourced. pass
23's whole lesson is that such a claim is where a subsystem breaks.

### what the read CONFIRMED — two live hypotheses, both dead

`node_modules/rhachet-brains-fireworksai/readme.md`, verbatim:

> *"the key is coarse on purpose. it covers the model and the system prompt only — not the prompt,
> the episode, or the tool defs."*

| the hypothesis | verdict |
|---|---|
| the brief's **PATH** enters the key, so a warmer-owned temp path parts the pins | 🟡 **dead** — the key covers the composed system prompt, and `asRoleOfSharedPrefix.integration.test [case1]` independently clamps that the composed prompt is the content alone |
| the **SCHEMA** enters the key, and the poke's `schemaOfPokeAck` differs from the review's | 🟡 **dead** — *"not the tool defs"* |

⇒ the comment's claim is **true**, and pass 23's formula is **correct**. the code is now cited rather
than asserted.

### 🔴 the find — the adapter's evidence is same-process, and the warmer is not

the readme's measured table is its own proof of the pin, and its scope is stated plainly:

> *"measured on `fireworks/deepseek/v4-flash`, **6 calls per arm**, rate over the 5 follow-ups"*
> — control 0/5, treatment **5/5**

| | what the adapter PROVED | what the warmer NEEDS |
|---|---|---|
| process | **one**, back to back | 🔴 **two** — the review's, then the warmer's, then the next round's |
| gap | seconds | 🔴 **15-18 minutes** |
| result | 5/5 = 100% | 🔴 **3 of 24** in production |

⇒ **the warmer's foundational premise was inherited from a readme whose evidence does not reach the
case the warmer is built on.** the adapter demonstrates that a shared key routes two calls together;
it never demonstrates that a key reaches a *new process* into a replica warmed *minutes* earlier —
and that second claim is the entire product.

🟡 **the readme is not at fault.** it states its arms and its counts honestly, and 5/5 in-process is
a real and useful result. the defect is on the consumer side: **we read a proof of one route as a
proof of durable reach across processes**, which is a strictly larger claim.

### the transferable lesson

🔴 **a dependency's evidence has a SCOPE, and a consumer adopts the claim without the scope.**

the failure is not a misread of what the readme says — it says the right thing. it is that a measured
table reads as *"this works"*, and its arms, counts, and intervals read as provenance rather than as
**bounds**. so the premise crossed the package boundary and shed its conditions on the way.

⇒ the detector: **for each premise your subsystem rests on and did not measure itself, find the
measurement behind it and write down its ARMS.** where your case sits outside them, that is not a
proven foundation — it is an assumption with a citation attached, which is the sort that persuades
hardest.

🟡 and it names the honest shape of this pass: **it changed no code's behavior and it changed the
product's status.** the pin is correct, the bars are clamped, the clamps bite — and what all of that
rests on is measured at 3 in 24. an audit can end in a stronger artifact or in a weaker claim, and
this one ended in the second.
