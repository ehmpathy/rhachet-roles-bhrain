## .settled

the two were bundled in one mechanism and are separable: the prompt's *content* is what carries the
encouragement, and the clock was only the trigger that decided when to show it. **drop the trigger,
show the prompt unconditionally, and grade the promise on the articulation.**

| the elapsed measurement | the reflect prompt — the identity reframe, the when-then cues, the exact articulation path |

| the 30s threshold, and any number in its place | the **articulation gate** — present, at the owed path, and fresh |
| the plowthrough hatch's reason to exist | — |

⇒ **this is the separation the prior ruling could not see.** `S05` retained the timer because the
*"what's the rush?"* flow was worth a keep — and that was true about the **prompt**, which a clock
was assumed to be the only way to deliver. it is not: the prompt can fire on the first promise per
slug with no clock consulted at all.
