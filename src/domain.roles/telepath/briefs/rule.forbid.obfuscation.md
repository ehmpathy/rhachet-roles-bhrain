# rule.forbid.obfuscation

> **obfuscation is CONCEALMENT BY SELECTION. every part is true, checkable, and findable — and
> what was left out, ranked down, or framed away is what the reader needed.**

the **third** rule on this defect, and it exists because prose can pass the other two whole.

| rule | what it grades | its subject |
|---|---|---|
| `forbid.subversive-prose` | falsifiability — can the reader dispute it? | what is claimed |
| `forbid.diffusion` | findability — can the reader locate the point? | where the point sits |
| **this rule** | selection — is what the reader needs even here? | what is **absent** |

⇒ **it is the only one of the three that grades what is not on the page.** the other two read the
prose; this reads the prose against what the author knew.

## .the test

> **if the reader knew what you know, would they read this the same way?**

yes → honest · **no** → obfuscation, however true each sentence is.

**not *"is it accurate?"*** — accuracy is what obfuscation is built from. every shape below
survives a fact-check.

⇒ the second test, for the author: **what did I leave out, rank down, or frame away — and would the
reader have wanted it?**

## .the shapes — each is true, disputable, and findable

| shape | 👎 as written | 👍 the honest form |
|---|---|---|
| the decoy needle — a real result elevated so a larger one stays below | *"the reviewer costs $0.013 per run"* | *"the reviewer cannot enforce 2 of the 10 issues. it costs $0.013"* |
| the technically-true frame | *"the tests pass"* | *"the tests pass. 40 are skipped"* |
| the shifted sense — a term the reader will take differently | *"the blocker is removed"* | *"the blocker was regraded a nitpick. the defect stands"* |
| the displaced qualifier — present, findable, and where the reader will not carry it | *"unfixed"* (its bound two cells away in a header) | *"unfixed **at the vision**"* |
| the selective citation — the source that supports, cited; the one that refutes it, omitted | *"`#429` settles it"* | *"`#429` settles it; `S11` then overturned `#429`'s second claim"* |
| the weak settler — you name what would settle it, and pick the cheapest one | *"one file read would confirm it"* | *"one file read is suggestive; the run is what settles it"* |
| the volume defense — forty true elevated facts, so the reader stops before the one that counts | a yield that grew on a round where no decision changed | cut to the decisions. `reflexive-condensation` |

⇒ **every shape but the volume defense repairs to an ADDITION, never a cut.** that is the tell of
this rule against its two peers, whose repairs are a rewrite and a promotion.

## .why it is a rule of its own, and not a stricter `subversive-prose`

`subversive-prose` grades the **sentence** — its ten shapes each fire on a passage a reader cannot
dispute. **each shape here is fully disputable**, so not one of them fires.

⇒ and `diffusion` grades the **gradient** — each shape here has one. the decoy needle is *elevated*;
that is what makes it work.

🟡 **so the two clean rules pass this prose, and that makes it the most dangerous of the three** — it
survives a careful review by an author who applied both.

| when… | then… |
|---|---|
| you choose which true result to lead with | the strongest cue. is the one you elevated the one they need? |
| you state a result and its caveat sits elsewhere | move the caveat to the claim (`place-a-passage-by-its-subject`) |
| you cite the source that supports you | name the one that counters you, in the same breath |
| a word will be read in a different sense than you mean | say the sense. a shifted sense is a lie with an alibi |
| you name what would settle a claim | name the **strongest** settler, never the cheapest |
| your artifact grew on a round where no decision changed | volume is a defense. cut it |
| you would answer *"but every word is true"* | **that is this rule's premise, never a defense** |

## 🟡 .the boundary — brevity is NOT obfuscation

| a violation | not a violation |
|---|---|
| a caveat the reader must act on, omitted | detail cut because it ranks below the 95% bar |
| the counter-source, unnamed | a source ref'd out rather than inlined |
| a decoy needle elevated over a larger one | the largest needle elevated, the rest subordinated |
| a term used in a sense you know the reader will misread | a declared term used in its declared sense |

**the line that parts them: did the OMISSION change what the reader would do?** no → condensation,
and correct. yes → obfuscation, however small the words saved.

## .the axis

a **structure** rule, so telepath owns it (`rule.require.generic-governs-structure-never-voice`). a
concealed selection is concealed in every register, so it prescribes no member of that rule's voice
row.

🟡 **the word claims intent, and the shapes carry it** — a selection is chosen. but the rule fires on
the **artifact**: shown what was omitted, an honest author adds it; a concealed one argues that each
word was true.

blocker: an omission that would change what the reader does · a caveat separated from the claim it
bounds · a supportive citation given where a counter one was known · a decoy needle elevated over a
larger one · a term used in a sense the author knows will be misread · a weak settler named where a
strong one was known.
nitpick: an artifact that grew on a round where no decision changed.
false positive: detail cut below the 95% bar that changes no reader's action · a source ref'd out ·
a declared term in its declared sense.

`rule.require.place-a-passage-by-its-subject` (where a displaced qualifier belongs) ·
`rule.require.reflexive-condensation` (the boundary this must not eat).
