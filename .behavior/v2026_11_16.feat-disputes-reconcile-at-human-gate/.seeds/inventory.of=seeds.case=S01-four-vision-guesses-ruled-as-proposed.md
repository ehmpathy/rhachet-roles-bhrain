> dirty-ground dispute always goes to the human; a route with no human gate refuses its last stone
> while a dispute is open; a concession does not re-hold the stone; confidence lives in the fulcrum

## .settled

- a dispute on the dirty ground is `owed: human` at any confidence; only a wrong-ground dispute at
  ≥ 0.90 owes a reflection
- a route with no human gate refuses `--as passed` on its last stone while any `owed: human`
  dispute is open
- a human's conceded verdict does not restore the concern to the `reviewed?` tally; the halt names
  the repair, and the human withholds approval until it lands
- `confidence` lives only in the fulcrum frontmatter — no `--confidence` flag
- the human's verdict command is `rhx route.fulcrum.rule`
