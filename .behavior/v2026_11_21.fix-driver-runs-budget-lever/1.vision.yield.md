# 1.vision — the driver runs its own review budget lever

## .what this builds

the `reviewed?` judge's urgent halt hands the top-up to the driver who reads it, and names no human as its owner.

```
✋ halted, an urgent concession stands — friction
   ├─ its harm ships if unfixed, so this stone earns more budget
   ├─ increase budget — yours to spend
   │  └─ rhx route.guard.budget --for review --add 2 --peer friction --stone 1.vision
   ├─ 🟡 tell your human why this stone bought budget
   │
   └─ after the grant, address the concession, then run
      └─ rhx route.stone.set --stone 1.vision --as passed
```

the halt offers the grant only for a lane run dry. a lane with a round in hand gets the fix instead, so the printed lever always lands:

```
✋ halted, an urgent concession stands — friction
   ├─ its harm ships if unfixed, so this stone earns more budget
   ├─ a round is in hand — friction; a grant opens once it runs dry
   │
   └─ address the concession, then run
      └─ rhx route.stone.set --stone 1.vision --as passed
```

five changes, one story:

| # | change | where |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | the urgent halt's owner line becomes the drive halt's own label, `increase budget — yours to spend`, read from one shared const | `asJudgeUrgentHaltLines.ts`, `formatBlockRemedyGroups.ts` |
| 2 | the halt adds the note ask. it reads the same literal the grant render prints (`asBudgetGrantWarrantLines`), so one sentence lives in one place | same, + `asBudgetGrantWarrantLines.ts` |
| 3 | every driver brief that names the urgent top-up names the driver as its owner, and the human as the reader of a note | see `## .briefs touched` |
| 4 | the halt reads the stone's meters: a grant row only for a lane `hasReviewerRunDry` calls dry, else "a round is in hand" (F5) | `asJudgeUrgentHaltLines.ts`, `route.ts` `judgeReviewed` |
| 5 | an urgent exhaustion is a driver push, as a `better` one is: the onStop hook holds the session, the status line reads the push, the reminder stays live (F8) | `asRouteStoneDisposition.ts`, `isRouteDriveExhaustionStopBlocked.ts`, `isReminderLiveForPassage.ts` |

the gate does not change. `computeBudgetGrantRefusal` keeps its three conjuncts: warrant, moment, scope. with no live urgent concession, a top-up is still refused.

`JUDGE_HALT_ASK_HUMAN_LABEL` stays as is, and so does the `approved?` judge. approval is still a human act: `setStoneAsApproved.ts:45` refuses a caller with no TTY, and the budget path has no such check.

## .why it works

- **the lever is already the driver's, by mechanism.** `route.guard.budget` (route.ts:2560–2720) checks no actor. it runs only the three-conjunct gate. so a human who runs the pasted command meets the same gate the driver would. the ask buys a round trip and gives the human no power the driver lacks. the glossary already states this: `term=route.guard.budget._.choice._.md:41–42` says "a human who runs it is refused exactly as a driver is", and `term=route.guard.budget.grant._.choice.reason.md:42` says "who may earn it: a driver, with a warrant"
- **the drive halt already agrees.** `computeBlockRemedyGroups` labels the same command `increase budget — yours to spend` on an urgent exhaustion. today one stone can show two owners for one command, depending on which surface the driver hits first. after the change, both surfaces read one const
- **the human still hears about it, from the right actor.** the urgent-earns-budget invariant owes the human a written why. the driver writes it, at the moment of the act. no human round trip blocks the stone

## .the outcome world

**before.** a driver concedes a real harm as `urgent`. it re-arrives and the judge holds. the driver reads `please ask your human to`, stops, and pings its human. the human reads one line, pastes the command the driver could have run, and goes back to its work. one halt, one human round trip, and no decision in it needed a human.

**after.** the same driver reads `increase budget — yours to spend`. it runs the top-up, and the gate checks the warrant. it writes its human one line: "1.vision bought 2 rounds on `friction`: the concession is a data-loss path on retry". then it fixes the concession and re-arrives. the human reads the note when it chooses to.

**the aha.** the urgent grade was always the warrant. the driver made the harm claim, so the driver spends what that claim earned. a human's attention goes to the claim, never to the paste.

## .evaluation

**pros**
- removes one human round trip for every urgent judge halt
- one owner for one command, on every surface
- the clamp pins the owner line, so a later edit cannot slip it back

**cons**
- the driver now spends budget with no human in the loop at the moment of spend. the bound on that is social: the harm claim is written down and a council reads it. the gate checks that a claim exists. it cannot check that the claim is honest. that was already true of the drive halt; this change only removes the inconsistency

**edgecases**
- **the urgent lane still has rounds** (the judge holds on the tally, but the lane has not run dry). this is the halt's main path: a concede with no artifact change, then a re-arrive, keeps the lane cached with a round in hand. the halt reads the meter, offers no grant, and names the fix (F5)
- **the concession lapses between the halt and the top-up** (a new generation). the top-up is refused with `no-warrant`. that is the correct outcome
- **two urgent lanes** → one scoped command per lane, unchanged. the multi-lane snapshot pins it

## .mental model

> "an urgent concession is a receipt. you wrote it, so you cash it. you just send your human a copy."

| the wisher / human says | we say |
|---|---|
| "top up the budget" | `route.guard.budget --for review --add N` |
| "a real harm" | an `urgent` concession, the warrant |
| "tell me why" | the note: why this stone bought budget |

## .open questions & assumptions

**assumptions**
- the note is a plain written line to the human, sent by no mechanism (`asBudgetGrantWarrantLines` docblock)
- the urgent judge halt can fire with rounds in hand, so it must read the meter `judgeReviewed` already holds (`peerStatuses`)

**questions**
- **[answered]** does a human add any power when it runs the top-up? no. the command checks no actor (`term=route.guard.budget._.choice._.md:41–42`)
- **[answered]** is the urgent halt common enough to matter? yes. this repo's own routes hold 103 `severity: urgent` concession rows in `.route/passage.jsonl` (grepsafe over `.behavior/`, 2026-11-21)
- **[answered]** should the halt repeat the note ask, against `genRouteGuardExhaustedReason.ts:177–179`? yes. the wish asks for it, and this halt is where the driver decides to spend → F2
- **[answered]** rename the invariant brief? no. keep the filename, rewrite its body → F1
- **[answered]** align the top-up count (`--add N` vs `--add 2`) in this pr? no. it moves no owner and the gate makes a double run safe → F4, dream
- **[answered]** make the urgent halt meter-aware in this pr? yes. its main path is a lane with a round in hand, where a meter-blind grant row is refused → F5
- **[answered]** does an urgent exhaustion stay a human wait? no. the wait state let the session stop on the very halt that names the driver's grant → F8

## .what is awkward

- the invariant brief's whole argument is "the mirror IS the argument". after the change, the two judges match in shape but not in owner. the rewrite has to say plainly that a mirror of shape is what a driver reads, and that the owner follows the mechanism

- the two surfaces agree on the owner now, but not on the count: the drive halt prints `--add N`, the judge prints `--add 2`. a second top-up is refused (`rounds-remain`), so no double spend ships. the count is out of scope here (case=_ row 18)
