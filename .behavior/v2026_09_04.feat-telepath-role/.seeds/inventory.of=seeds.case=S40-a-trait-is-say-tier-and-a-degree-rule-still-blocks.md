## .settled

**two rules, and each overturns a call this route had argued at length.**

### 1 · every trait boots at say

- a trait is an instinct, and an instinct cannot be dereferenced
  - a rule is consulted; a trait fires with no decision
  - ⇒ a trait at `ref` is a trait nobody has, since nobody fetches an instinct mid-thought
- so the tier follows from the KIND, and no per-trait argument is owed
  - the say-tier boot comment for a trait states why that trait exists
  - it does not re-litigate whether a trait belongs at say

🟡 **it overturns a demotion this route proposed.** the case against was a `declare-once` claim:
`trait.bulletize-reflexively` restates *"bulletize by default"*, which `rule.require.bulletize`
already asserts at say, so the trait read as a third copy.

⇒ that claim is wrong, and `rule.require.a-cue-is-not-a-claim` is why: a rule and a trait cannot
DISAGREE, so they are two cues over one defect rather than two copies of one claim. the rule
states the bar a reviewer checks; the trait states the reach an author never decides on.

### 2 · a degree judgment does not soften a rule's severity

`rule.prefer.dense-prose` → `rule.require.dense-prose`, and its verdict → **blocker**.

- the nearest precedent is a blocker: `require.brevity` grades a degree at the LINE grain
  - so the passage grain is the same property one step coarser
- ⇒ the file's own severity argument was refuted by its own exemption table, two sections down
  - the argument: a blocker becomes a deletion mandate, so the driver cuts evidence to clear it
  - the table: *"a density flag with no legal refutation is a deletion mandate **at any
    severity**"*
- the exemptions, never the severity, are what make a density gate safe
  - a nitpick a driver cannot refute is still cleared by a cut
  - ⇒ nitpick buys no protection. it only makes the flag skippable

## .the general form

> **a rule's severity follows from the COST of the defect, never from the shape of the
> judgment.** a degree is harder to grade than a binary, and that is a fact about the reviewer
> rather than about what the defect costs.

⇒ and the mirror: **a tier follows from the KIND of the artifact.** a trait boots or it does not
exist.
