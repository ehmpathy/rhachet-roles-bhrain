# 🌙 dream: a passage record cannot attribute a brain to a stone

- **kind** = amend
- **owner** = `ehmpathy/rhachet-roles-bhrain`
- **caught** = 2026-09-09

## .the cue that fired

the vision makes switch visibility a critipath — a prescription nobody can verify is a preference,
not a control — and bounds it to one output line (fulcrum `F9`). the counter-argument was strong
enough to record.

## .the gap, precisely

a line lives in a transcript, and **a transcript is not queryable.** the foreman asks *"did
5.3.verification run on opus?"* later — after a defect ships, maybe after the session is gone, maybe
someone who was never there.

the route already keeps a per-stone append-only record, `passage.jsonl`, and the brain a stone ran
under is arguably a property of that passage.

## .the shape of the fix

on a switch, append to the stone's passage entry the brain and its provenance — prescribed by this
stone's guard, or inherited from an earlier one. a date may ride along, never a time
(`rule.forbid.timestamps-in-route-artifacts`). additive: no change to the line, the guard schema, or
the hook.

## .why it is not done in this round

**scope.** neither the wish nor the seed issue asks for a durable record (`rule.forbid.scope-leaks`),
and it has one usecase today (`rule.prefer.wet-over-dry`). a judgment about scope, so it carries
`F9` (`rework = clean`) beside this dream.
