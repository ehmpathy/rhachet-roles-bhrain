## .the gap, precisely — two defects, and they compound

verbatim from `.log/role=mechanic/skill=git.repo.test/what=acceptance/2026-09-19T17-53-32Z.stderr.log:4-21`:

```
      when: [t0] ... attempt 1
        ✓ then: the guard runs the term-aggregation peer and approves (39480 ms)
        ✕ then: the guard-tree stdout is stable (full snapshot) (69 ms)
        ✕ then: the captured peer artifact is stable (full snapshot) (55 ms)
      when: [t0] ... attempt 2
        ✓ then: the guard runs the term-aggregation peer and approves (128 ms)
        ✓ then: the guard-tree stdout is stable (full snapshot) (16 ms)
      when: [t0] ... attempt 3
        ✓ then: the guard runs the term-aggregation peer and approves (13 ms)
        ✓ then: the guard-tree stdout is stable (full snapshot) (4 ms)
```

### 🔴 defect 1 — the retry never retries

**39480 ms, then 128 ms, then 13 ms.** the guard subprocess is driven **once**. `useThen` memoizes
its result, and the attempts are its sibling blocks — so attempts 2 and 3 re-assert the *identical*
bytes attempt 1 produced.

⇒ **`criteria: 'SOME'` can never observe a second outcome**, because a second outcome is never
produced. the retry costs three passes over one result and buys no variance tolerance at all — which
is the whole purpose `rule.require.repeatable-for-llm-tests` assigns it.

### 🔴 defect 2 — attempts 2+ assert naught, and report green

the same bytes went **red at attempt 1 and green at attempts 2 and 3**. the only difference is the
snapshot KEY, which carries the attempt ordinal:

```
... attempt 1 then: the guard-tree stdout is stable (full snapshot) 1
... attempt 2 then: the guard-tree stdout is stable (full snapshot) 1
```

and `blackbox/__snapshots__/review.by.guard-peer.acceptance.test.ts.snap` holds **only `attempt 1`
keys** — `git diff` after the run confirms no `attempt 2` or `attempt 3` key was written.

⇒ **attempts 2 and 3 compared against no baseline and passed regardless.** a green with no assertion
behind it is the shape `rule.forbid.failhide` forbids by name, and here it is produced by the harness
rather than by an author.

### ⇒ the two together

| | what it costs |
|---|---|
| defect 1 | a real flake is **not** rescued — attempt 1's red is the suite's red |
| defect 2 | an un-baselined attempt **manufactures** green, so `SOME` is satisfied by a no-op |

🔴 **so the pair fails in both directions at once**: it cannot save a legitimate flake, and it
reports success from an attempt that checked naught. a reader who sees `when.repeatably` on a
snapshot suite will believe a tolerance exists that does not.

## 🔴 .defect 2 INVERTS under `--ci`, and the surface is 18 assertions repo-wide

`package.json:67`:


⇒ under `--ci` jest **refuses to write a new snapshot** and fails instead. so:

| where | an absent `attempt 2` baseline | the net effect on `SOME` |
|---|---|---|
| local | written in silence ⇒ attempt 2 **passes** with naught checked | `SOME` satisfied by a no-op — defect 2 as traced |
| 🔴 **CI** | 🔴 **a hard FAILURE** | 🔴 attempts 2 **and** 3 fail by construction ⇒ `SOME` **collapses to "attempt 1 must pass"** |

**both are the same root defect — the attempt ordinal in the snapshot key — and it is harmful in
each direction.** locally it manufactures green; in CI it removes the tolerance the retry was
adopted to provide.

## .the shape of the fix

three parts, and the first two are independent:

1. **re-derive the value per attempt.** a memoized `useThen` inside `when.repeatably` defeats the
   retry by construction. either `useThen` gains an opt-out when it sits inside a repeatable block,
   or such a case drives inline and pays the cost per attempt
2. 🔴 **keep `toMatchSnapshot()` OUT of a repeatable block.** the key carries the attempt ordinal, so
   exactly one attempt can ever hold a baseline. a snapshot is a single comparison against a pinned
   artifact; it has no meaning under a retry. the behavioral assertions may retry; the snapshot must
   sit outside, driven once
3. **audit the peer suites.** every `blackbox/**.acceptance.test.ts` that pairs `when.repeatably`
   with `useThen` or `toMatchSnapshot` carries one or both defects. the census is a grep, not a read

### 🔴 part 2 ALONE does not turn a red suite green — take parts 1 and 2 together

> **added 2026-09-20, after the red run's snapshot diff was read line by line.**

part 2 is the half that reads cheap, so it is the half somebody will take first. **on its own it
repairs the HONESTY of the retry and absorbs no variance at all.**

the measured red is a **brain verdict flip** — `approved → rejected`, `0 blockers ✓ → [N] blockers 🔴`,
`judge.1 - allowed → blocked` — on a case whose fixture and name both say *clean*:

| | after part 2 alone | after parts 1 + 2 |
|---|---|---|
| attempts 2–3 report an un-asserted green | ✅ fixed — one drive, one baseline | ✅ fixed |

| 🔴 a verdict flip on a pass case | 🔴 **still red** — one drive of a probabilistic brain flips at the same rate, now **deterministically** | ✅ **rescued** — `SOME` can observe a second outcome |

🟡 **and part 2's true price is the ordinal in the snapshot KEY.** to hoist is to rename every key it

hard failure rather than a silent rewrite. so part 2 is a one-shot repo-wide re-snap, never a
suite-local edit.

⇒ **part 1 is the decisive half.** it is also the one that lives in `test-fns`, which is why this
dream exists rather than a repair.

## .why it is not done in this round

the SAFE/CLEAN test (`rule.always.fix-forward-under-scouts-honor`):

- **safe?** 🔴 **no.** part 1 changes `useThen`/`when.repeatably` semantics, which every LLM-backed
  suite in this repo leans on. a change there can turn a green suite red on a box this route cannot
  test, and the fix lives in `test-fns` rather than here
- **clean?** 🔴 **no.** part 2 restructures a four-case verdict matrix this behavior does not own —
  it touches 8 snapshot keys, the case docblock's stated matrix, and the meaning of the suite

⇒ **defer, and the deferral owes a fulcrum** — the call is about RIPPLE, never SIZE, so
`rule.always.catch-dreams-for-followups` makes the fulcrum mandatory beside this dream.
