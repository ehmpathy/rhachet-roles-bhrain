# case=5 — disable turns it off

feel × care: happy × critipath, plus one sharp × alterpath edge · actor: human · ⇒ inventory rows `disable · on`
and `disable · on-rhachet-authored`

## .narrative

a month on, kai's glossary is mature and the nudges feel like noise. he turns it off:

```
$ rhx learn.terms.hook disable
🦉 as leaves fall

📜 learn.terms.hook disable
   ├─ status = disabled (removed)
   ├─ settings = .claude/settings.of.repo.json
   ├─ takes effect = at each session's next start; a live session keeps its old settings
   └─ share it = commit .agent/.actors/actor.via.repo=ambient/brain/.claude/settings.of.repo.json
```

the sharp edge: in another repo, someone had pasted the withdrawn hook into the settings by hand,
under the learner's own `repo=bhrain/role=learner` author. `disable` keys on the hook command, not
on the author, so that copy is removed too, and the output names it. `disable` means no stop in this
repo runs the term hook, whoever wrote the entry.

## .[tn] sketch

```
given('[case5] a repo where the toggle is enabled')
  when('[t0] `rhx learn.terms.hook disable` runs')
    then('exit 0; no Stop entry runs the learn.domain.terms hook command')
    then('every other hook and key is unchanged')
    then('stdout reports disabled (removed) and when it takes effect')
  when('[t1] a session that was live before t0 reaches a stale stop')
    then('it still holds with the nudge — it read its settings at start; the t0 output said so')
  when('[t2] a session started after t0 reaches a stale stop')
    then('no term hook runs, and the session rests')
given('[case5b] a repo with a hand copy of the hook under author repo=bhrain/role=learner')
  when('[t0] `rhx learn.terms.hook disable` runs')
    then('the hand copy is removed; stdout names its author')
```
