# domain.term: externalize

term.chosen   = externalize
term.kind     = verb
term.boundary = ROOT   # `repo` is the universal ancestor and is dropped; externalize takes no other
term.synonyms.forbidden:
- document     # names one OUTPUT shape (prose), where externalize spans a brief, a skill,
               # a route, and a tool alike
- capture      # names the moment of NOTICE, never the act of transfer to a durable home.
               # a lesson can be captured in a head and remain unexternalized
- record       # the passive twin of capture; same defect
- publish      # implies an audience beyond this tree. externalize holds whether or not
               # the artifact ever leaves the repo
- persist      # a storage word. it says where an artifact went, never that a future
               # traveler can now reach it

## .what

**externalize** = to move knowledge out of one actor's head and into a durable artifact a future
traveler can reach without that actor in the room.

it is the **root of a family of verbs**, each of which externalizes at a different rung of
`philosophy.entoolment-is-the-pinnacle`:

| the verb | what it produces | the rung |
|---|---|---|
| `externalize.enbrief` | a brief — prethought concepts | 1 📚 |
| `externalize.enskill` | a skill — prethought capacity | 2 💪💧 |
| `externalize.entool` | a tool — capacity at **no brain cost per use** | 3–4 🔩🪨 |
| `externalize.pave` | a reusable path, for a traveler who never had the lesson | any |
| `externalize.learn` | durable retention, so a lesson is not re-derived | any |

## .why it is the BOUNDARY of that family

the family is a family of **acts**. so its boundary must name an act, and `externalize` is the act
every member is a specialization of.

🟡 the rejected alternative was `knowledge.` — *what the artifact holds*. **it names the substance
an act operates on, never the act**, so it is the boundary of the artifacts these verbs produce and
not of the verbs themselves.

## .what is NOT in this family

`seed` is a **noun**, and it is already settled as `artifact.seed`. it sat on the family's gap row
for eighteen days on an `en-`-adjacency read rather than a read of its own declared sense — the
exact error `rule.require.boundary-qualified-terms` warns against.
