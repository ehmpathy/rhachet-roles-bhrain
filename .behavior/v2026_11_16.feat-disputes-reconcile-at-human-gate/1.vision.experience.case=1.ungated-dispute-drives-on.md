# case=1 — an ungated dispute drives on

cells: R1 · feel happy · care critipath (invariant 1 — the autonomy clamp)

## .narrative

the driver is on `3.blueprint`, a stone whose guard holds peer reviewers and a `reviewed?` judge,
but no `approved?` judge. the rules lane raises `blocker.2`: a name the driver believes the rubric
misreads. the driver is 70% sure.

it itemizes the fork as fulcrum F12, with frontmatter that the write derives `owed: human` from
(`0.70 < 0.90`), and disputes the concern with `--why` at that file. the dispute sheds the concern
from the tally. the stone is reviewed, the driver marks it passed, and the drive goes on to the next
stone. no halt. no human paged. no new review round bought.

the debt is not lost: F12 still says `owed: human, verdict: null`. it waits for the next gate on
the route (`case=4`).

```
  then the route moves to the next stone

```
