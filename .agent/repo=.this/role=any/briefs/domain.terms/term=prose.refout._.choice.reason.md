# domain.term.choice.reason: refout

## .etymology

`ref` + `out`, in the shape english already gives `factor out` and `farm out` — the object stays,
its home changes. the repo spoke the phrase before it declared the word: `#418` is titled *"a passage
REFS OUT"*, so the coinage is a nominalization of an extant verb phrase rather than a new word
(`rule.always.reuse-pavement-before-improvise`).

it was declared 2026-09-07 because it entered a **filename** —
`rule.prefer.rules-that-refout.md` — and a filename is a contract
(`rule.forbid.domain-term-synonyms`).

## .the rejected peers, and each was rejected on a different ground

| word | why not |
|---|---|
| **externalize** | already declared, and it names tribal → written. a refout moves between two written artifacts, so to reuse the word would overload a term whose family has six members (`rule.forbid.domain-term-ambiguity`) |
| **eject** | `rule.prefer.decompose-a-subject-via-suffixes` uses it for a **subject** split into a peer file. a refout moves a *passage* and changes no subject boundary |
| **demote** | names a tier change (`say` → `ref`), which is a *consequence* of a refout and not the act. a passage refs out of a resident file that stays resident |
| **offload** | implies the sender is relieved of a burden. the resident file is relieved of BYTES and keeps the claim, by way of the pointer |

## .the distinction it carries — a refout is available only where a target EXISTS

that is what parts it from a cut, and it is the whole reason the word earns its slot:

- a **cut** removes a passage. if the passage carried a distinction, the cut was *lossy*
- a **refout** moves it. the distinction survives at the target, and the resident file names the path

⇒ so *"refout"* asserts what *"shorten"* does not: **that the reader can still reach it.** an author
who says *"I refed it out"* and left no pointer did not refout — they cut, and mislabelled it.

## .evidence

- discovery: measured 2026-09-07 across `bhrain/role=telepath`'s 26 resident briefs — 108,701 chars,
  **46% argument prose**, which is the material a refout moves. the act was performed repeatedly
  before the word existed, which is the cue that a word was owed
  (`rule.require.enumerate-before-you-name`)
- the instances the enumeration walked, each a distinct sense-check on the word:
  - a rule's **argument** → its `define.$x` (7 pairs extant in telepath)
  - a rule's **argument** → its own `.md`, where no define exists (19 rules)
  - a route's **archaeology** → `.agent/.notes/` (`rule.require.archaeology-in-notes`)
  - a catalog's **occurrence record** → `example=$id.md` (`rule.require.catalog-is-an-index`)
  - a `.md.min`'s **depth** → its `.md` (the sidecar convention)
- ⇒ five instances across four artifact kinds, and the word covers each without stretch. it excludes
  its neighbours too: none of the five is an `externalize`, an `eject`, or a `demote`

## .the invariant

**a refout leaves a pointer, or it was a cut.** the pointer is what makes the losslessness claim
checkable — a passage moved with no path from its origin is a deletion with extra steps, and
`.dream/v2026_09_04.enbrief.a-lossy-min-boots-while-its-precise-source-does-not.md` records what a
violation looks like at scale.
