## .settled

### 1 · a summary answers the STATE, never the trajectory

a summary owes four things, in this order:

| # | the question | what it holds |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | what | the subject, or the answer, in one line |
| 2 | why | why it matters to them |
| 3 | where | where it stands — what holds, what is open |
| 4 | next | the move that follows, if there is one |

🟡 **what CHANGED since the last turn is archaeology.** it feels like the answer to
*"where did we land?"* and it is not — a reader who holds the delta and lacks the state
cannot act.

🟡 **and the four are worded for ANY clone, never for a builder.** a first draft asked for
*"the goal we are up to"*, *"what is built, what is owed, what blocks"*, and *"the one move
that follows"* — three build assumptions, and the wisher rejected all three at once:

> *"it shouldnt assume that the clone will build anything. the clone could be a researcher
> or just a friend; ... the purpose is to onstop ensure that they clearly communicate"*

⇒ **a researcher has a subject rather than a goal, an answer rather than a build, and often
no next move at all.** so `what` takes *the subject, or the answer*, `where` takes *what
holds, what is open*, and `next` is conditional — *if there is one*.

### 2 · substance ranks above density, and the order is the fix

> verbatim: *"substance before density is 100% right"*

a density rule grades HOW a passage reads. it cannot grade WHAT the passage holds, so a
dense recap of the wrong subject clears it perfectly.

⇒ **a bar the wrong artifact can clear is one notch too coarse.** that is
`rule.require.enumerate-before-you-name`, applied to a rule's own scope rather than to a
word — and it is the second time this route has met it (`S30` was the first, where
*"could a reader disagree?"* caught `shallow` and passed `squishy` whole).

### 3 · a nudge must instruct without a SHOUT — and the two are separable

the first repair conflated them: it cut the reminder to five lines, and the wisher
rejected it at once — *"that makes the hook not do anything."*

**the volume was never the length.** three cuts removed it and left the lesson whole:

| the shout | the cut |
|---|---|
| ALL-CAPS emphasis — `SUBSTANCE`, `STATE`, `TRAJECTORY` | lowercase. `rule.forbid.shouts` already forbade it |
| an imperative header — *"say it clear, say it dense"* | an invitation — *"before you rest, say where we stand"* |
| a 6-line list of canon brief paths, every turn | dropped. those briefs are **booted**, so to re-list them ranks below `reflexive-condensation`'s own bar |

⇒ **the seam is TONE, not SIZE.** a reminder to be dense that is itself 30 shouted lines
has refuted itself; one that instructs naught has forfeited its reason to fire.
