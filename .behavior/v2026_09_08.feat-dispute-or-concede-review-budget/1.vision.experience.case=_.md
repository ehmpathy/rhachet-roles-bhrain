### 🔴 the density ranking — the lens, applied

`define.experience._.metric=boundary-density` asks *how many boundaries one walk exercises*, and it
prescribes the mix: **a dense journey for breadth, plus focused spotlights so each sharp critipath's
fail-safe stays legible.**

| rank | case | boundaries in one walk | its job |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🔴 **1** | **9** | **10**, across 3 lanes and 2 actors | **the centered demo** — proves the boundaries compose |
| 2 | 6 | 4 — concede, meter, scoped top-up, the re-run | the fullest spotlight |
| 3 | 1 | 4 — dispute, mint, skip, the two top-up forms | acceptance #2 |
| 4 | 7 | 3 — the row, the verdict, the abuse signal | the human's read |
| 🔴 5 | **10** | 3 — the threshold verdict, the trivial gate, the refusal that bounds it | **the coordinate audit** |
| 6 | 2 · 5 · 8 · 3 · 4 | 1–2 each | the fail-safe close-ups |

⚠️ **density is not importance, and the two are easy to conflate.** a cell can sit at the seam where
a driver's judgment meets the engine's gate — case=1 and case=2 both do — and still cross few
boundaries in one walk. **density counts boundaries; care sets severity.** they are orthogonal, and a
ranking that reaches for *"which matters most"* has answered the care question twice and the density
question not at all.

⇒ **case=9 adds no new cell.** it is a walk across cells the tally already counts, so the arithmetic
above is untouched. its value is the **interaction** — chiefly `[t6]`, where one top-up
must re-arm a conceded lane and leave a disputed one dark, which no cell-scoped case can stage.

### why case=8 is an alterpath and still demoed

a flip-flop is **rare** (a driver rarely reverses a stance within one given) and its **cost is
high** (F09: it re-opens the escape). `care = freq × cost` puts it below the critipaths and well
above the itemized floor. ⇒ demoed because its refusal encodes an invariant no other case carries.
