## .settled

### 1. exhaustion is a state to accept, never a defect

> **its fine if l1 becomes exhausted after a conceded on l3**

a lower lane that runs out of rounds while a higher one converges is **the meter at work**. no
mechanism is owed to prevent it, and no emit should apologize for it.

⇒ this strikes the counter-argument I had built the whole row on: *"starve l1 and a lens is lost."*
the lens loss is real and it is **acceptable**, so the harm I priced had no buyer.

### 2. budget is SCARCE, and the scarcity is the point

> **we want to prioritize budget right now**

⇒ a blanket top-up is not a convenience with a small cost; it is a **spend of the scarcest resource
on the ladder**, made silently, on lanes nobody asked about.

🟡 **and it reads directly onto the wish's own thesis** — *"budget is the wrong instrument to end a
disagreement."* a design whose emit prints a command that inflates every level was in tension with
the premise it was built on.

### 3. 🔴 the default is NAUGHT, and the exits are enumerated

> **so unless a level is explicitly rewound or budgeted, it should stay|become exhausted**

| the property | what the verdict fixes |
|---|---|
| the default reach of a top-up | **none.** a level is not touched unless it is named |
| the exits from exhaustion | **two, and the list is closed** — an explicit rewind, or an explicit budget |
| what "explicit" excludes | an automatic extension of the exhausted set — even one keyed on a correct predicate |

⇒ **the word that carries it is `explicitly`.** it forbids not merely the wide default but every
*inferred* one, and that is what parts this verdict from the fork I had drafted in answer to the
challenge (`F022` fork D — *"auto-extend exactly the lanes already exhausted"*).
