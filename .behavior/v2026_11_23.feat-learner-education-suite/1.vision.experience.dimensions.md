E is split per act because the acts take different inputs; E3 is the argv every face shares. G is the stack depth: the bind is where the driver stands. M–S each surfaced in verification: each tests a refusal at the boundary where the input first enters, so each folds to one row per value rather than a product.

## .the full product

every axis, unrestricted: 3 (A) × 8 (B) × 4 (C) × 4 (D) × 4 (E1) × 6 (E2) × 8 (E3) × 3 (F) × 5 (G) × 3 (H) × 2 (I) × 4 (J) × 3 (K) × 2 (L) × 2 (M) × 4 (N) × 4 (O) × 4 (P) × 2 (Q) × 4 (R) × 3 (S) = 489,223,618,560 cells.

E1, E2, E3, F, H, J, K, L, M–S each bite few acts, so each multiplies only those acts' cells and is constant (n/a) elsewhere. that fold, plus the A × B pairs below, is the reduction.
