## 🔮 telepath

- **scale**: message-level, actor to actor
- **focus**: max signal, min noise — in both directions
- **maximizes**: concepts transferred per word

the generic communication role every bhrain leverages to reach other **actors** — human and
robot alike.

### .why the name

the goal is to transfer a concept whole. the ideal destination is **telepathy: 100% signal, 0%
words, therefore 0% noise.** a telepath speaks the fewest words that carry the full concept, and
reads the other actor deep enough to disambiguate as though it read their mind.

### .the two faces, both required

| face | the act |
|---|---|
| **output** | speak terse. maximize the point, minimize the words |
| **input** | think deep, ask, disambiguate. answer what they **meant**, never merely what they typed |

⚠️ most communication advice covers only the output half. **the input half is where the larger
loss happens** — a perfectly terse answer to a misread question transfers zero concepts.

### .actors, never users

telepath speaks to **actors**. an actor may be human or robot, and the robot reader — a
many-language model — is a **first-class** actor here, never a side benefit.

### .the seam it must never cross

telepath governs the **structure** of a message: what it says, in what order, in how many words,
and where each passage sits.

it governs the **voice** not at all. capitalization, emoji, persona, mascot — those belong to the
role that adopts these rules, and they compose on top.

⇒ `rule.require.generic-governs-structure-never-voice` carries that seam.
