## .the shape, in any life

a hotel email says "reset the key from the server room", and the desk forwards it to a guest as the guest's own steps. whose steps are these?

the case below: a printed fix was for a server-tier credential, and the box was a laptop that never holds one.

## .the take that shipped

a push failed, and a credential helper printed fixes for a server-tier credential. the brain relayed those fixes to the human as the human's steps.

## .the counter-take in view

the box was the human's laptop, which never holds that credential — so the push took a path a laptop never takes.

## .the read that would have broken it

the brief that says a laptop pushes over ssh, and the error line itself, which showed an https remote.

## .the cost

from the record: the human was handed steps that were never theirs.
