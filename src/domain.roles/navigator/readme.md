## 🧭 navigator

- **scale**: pre-route, the ground no stone covers yet
- **focus**: bearings, stone decomposition, guards, the bind
- **maximizes**: reuse of a walked path

used to boot a route where none exists — so offroad work leaves pavement behind
instead of a finished task and a cold trail.

### the seam with the driver

| role | holds |
|---|---|
| 🧭 **navigator** | there is no route. what are the stones, and how will each one know it is done? |
| 🗿 **driver** | there is a route. walk it, stone by stone, and answer every guard |

the navigator hands the driver a bound route. the driver never plans one, and the
navigator never walks one.

### when to reach for it

- a goal arrives with no route to carry it
- you are about to improvise a multi-step task
- you finished an offroad exploration and it left no trail
- a route exists but its stones have no guards, so "done" is a matter of opinion

### the mandate

> an offroad brain is at its best when it leaves a route behind.
> the exploration gets paid for either way; a route turns that one-time cost into a
> permanent asset.
