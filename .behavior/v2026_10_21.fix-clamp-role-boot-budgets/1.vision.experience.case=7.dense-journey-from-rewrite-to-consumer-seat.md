## narrative

the driver of this route condenses the learner's mins. the census catches one when-row dropped
from `rule.always.entool-the-skills-you-touch`, and the driver restores it. `roles cost` reads
12.4k against a 12k proposal. the driver moves one more measured case to its `.md`, and the
payload reads 11.8k. the budget is set at the 12,000 proposal, which leaves ~200 tokens of
headroom. the pr ships. a consumer repo upgrades the package, and its seat boots the learner from
`dist`. that afternoon, a seat about to hand-run a second skill call reads the entool cue and
folds the step into the skill. a week later, a skeptic opens the `.md` beside the min, in
`node_modules`, and it is there.
