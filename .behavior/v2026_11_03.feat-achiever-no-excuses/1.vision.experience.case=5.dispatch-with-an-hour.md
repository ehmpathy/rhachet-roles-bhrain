# case 5 — a blocker dispatched to its owner with an hour

feel: happy, then sharp past the hour · care: critipath · actor: achiever, subordinate (the owner) · seams: store, radio, stop hook, clock

## .narrative

the achiever's goal needs a fix in `rhachet` (another repo): its hook contract drops a field. the cure
is not in this repo's reach. the achiever patches what it can here (a pioneer patch that unblocks its
own work), then dispatches the real fix to its owner with `rhx radio.task.push` and an hour: the time by
which it will check back. it records the blocker on the goal as dispatched, with the radio task and
the hour. the stop is allowed; the dispatch is a live promise, not an excuse.

the hour passes with no progress logged. at the next stop the hook refuses: the follow-up is due. the
achiever pulls the task (`rhx radio.task.pull`), sees the owner claimed it, logs that, and sets a new
hour. had the owner not answered, it would chase once more or escalate the one act it cannot do.

## .[tn] — a sketch

```
given  an inflight goal whose cure lives in ehmpathy/rhachet
when   [t0] rhx radio.task.push --via gh.issues --into ehmpathy/rhachet --title '...' --description @stdin
     and rhx goal.memory.set --slug x --status.choice blocked
           --cure.via dispatch --cure.ref ehmpathy/rhachet#123 --cure.await 60m
then     the stop passes before the hour
when   [t1] the hour passes with no progress logged
then     goal.triage.next --when hook.onStop exits 2
     and names the goal, the radio task, how overdue, and the two moves:
           log the chase:  rhx goal.memory.set --slug x --cure.progress '<what came back>' --cure.await 60m
           or close it:    its done check passes
```

## .forbidden

`--cure.via dispatch` with no `--cure.await` is refused: a dispatch with no hour is a drop under a marker.
