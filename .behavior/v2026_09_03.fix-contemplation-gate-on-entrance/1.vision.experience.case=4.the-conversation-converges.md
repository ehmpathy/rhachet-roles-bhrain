## .the mechanism — extant, unchanged

| piece | where |
|---|---|
| a `.taken` newer than its `.given` triggers a re-run | `runStoneGuardReviews.ts:252-269`, `491-528` |
| `$conversation` unions givens + reports + takens | `enumRouteGuardReviewPeerConversationFiles.ts` |
| a reviewer's own fresh given is excluded from its own conversation | `runStoneGuardReviews.ts:136-140` |

**this behavior adds no transport** — it adds the `.taken` files the extant transport was built to
carry and never received. `[t4]` records the deliberate asymmetry: a blind reviewer reuses its
cache, so an answer costs it no round.

🔴 **the risk this case guards.** if the entrance gate blocked a reviewer's **re-run** as well as
the driver's entry, this cell would deadlock — the debt could never be discharged by a reviewer that
drops its own blocker. `[t0]` asserts the opposite: **the gate governs the driver's entry, never the
reviewer's execution.**
