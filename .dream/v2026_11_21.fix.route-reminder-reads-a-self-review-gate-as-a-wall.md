# 🌙 dream: the reminder reads a self-review gate as a human wall

kind: fix · owner: rhachet-roles-bhrain · caught 2026-11-21

## .the cue that fired

a byhand walk of `rhx route.reminder.get` in 5.3 of `fix-route-reminder-one-per-clone`, while the clone worked its own self reviews:

```
reminder is not live — its focus .behavior/v2026_11_21.fix-route-reminder-one-per-clone is not a live drive (route-not-live, status blocked); it re-registers on the next route.drive of a live route
```

## .the gap, precisely

- `--as arrived` on a stone that owes `review.self` writes `status: blocked` to passage
- `REMINDER_LIVENESS_BY_STATUS.blocked = false`: blocked reads as "a human must act"
- but a self-review gate is the DRIVER's to clear. a clone that stalls mid self-review is exactly the stall the nudge exists for, and it gets no nudge

## .the shape of the fix

- tell the self-review block apart from a hard wall: either a distinct passage status for "review.self owed" (then classify it live), or carry a `reason` on the blocked row that the liveness map reads
- the map's exhaustive `satisfies` keeps a new status a compile error until it is classified
- clamp: a route whose tail is the review.self block reads live; a human-wait block stays dead

## .why it is not done in this round

the liveness policy and the passage statuses predate this wish (one daemon per clone) and are shared by the guard; a new status or reason ripples into the judge and the status line.
