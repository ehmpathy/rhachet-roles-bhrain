## .settled

**an amendment, and it corrects an artifact I had already written.**

`rule.forbid.subversive-prose` shipped with **ten shapes** and no statement of what makes them one
class. the wisher's read: *"more than just metaphors"* — the rule read as a **list of surfaces**.

### the fundamental, in the wisher's own terms

> **subversive prose is any elongation or encirclation of the purpose of speech.**

two moves, and both are one mechanism:

| the move | what it does |
|---|---|
| **elongate** | take the long way, so the point arrives late or never |
| **encircle** | orbit the claim, so the reader infers what was never asserted |

⇒ **subterfuge**, and the wisher's word is exact: it **masks the true intent and the true message**.

### the aim is the sharpest half — TIRE, not persuade

> *"aims to **tire the reader into submission**, rather than **elucidate**"*

| the honest passage | the subversive passage |
|---|---|
| **elucidates** — the reader ends able to judge | **tires** — the reader ends unable to object |

⇒ *unable to object* and *convinced* are **different states**, and the difference is the whole rule.
a reader worn down assents; a reader informed decides.
