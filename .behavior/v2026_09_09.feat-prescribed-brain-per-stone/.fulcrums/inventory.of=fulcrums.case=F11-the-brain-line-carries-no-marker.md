# F11 — the brain line carries no glyph

**rework** = clean · **status** = open · **confidence** = 80%

## .the fork

the drive renders the brain as a bare `brain = <slug>` line in its `where do we go?` bucket. extant
surfaces put a marker at the head of a line a human triages — `🗿`, `✋`, `👋`. does the brain line
get one, and which?

## .taken, and why

**no glyph.**

- a claim is an act with artifacts: a palette entry, an index row, an axis-file row
  (`catalog.of=glyph._.md`); half a claim drifts the register
- the obvious pick is taken: `🧠` is claimed for rung 0, tribal knowledge. apt is not the test
- an unmarked line makes no claim; a wrong marker is a contract whose repair the catalog measured at
  68 occurrences across 10 files when `🔮` collided

## .the counter-case

`case=8` argues the switch must be visible, and an unmarked line beside marked peers is harder to
scan. the catalog warns that three bare lines beside one marked line are three sites owed a marker,
not one to strip. ⇒ a defensible defer, never a defensible end state.

## .rework

clean while the sites are few — one glyph at a fixed position, plus the three catalog rows. once the
line spreads through downstream snapshots, an insert churns every route.

## .confidence — 80%

the defer is sound. the 20% is that it is never picked up: an absent glyph raises no error.
