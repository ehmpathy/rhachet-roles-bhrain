# case=8 — a repo that never asks pays naught

feel × care: happy × critipath · actor: a developer, and the clones they run · ⇒ inventory row
`(no verb run)`

## .narrative

mira keeps a small repo with a settled vocabulary. she never wanted the glossary nudge, and in the
months it shipped she felt it: every hour, a clone's stop was held to distill terms she had no use
for. that charge is why the hook was withdrawn.

she upgrades to the release that ships the toggle and runs `rhx init`. she does not run `rhx
learn.terms.hook` — she has no reason to. that afternoon she runs three clones. each works, reaches
its stops, and rests at once, exactly as before the upgrade. no stop is held, no distill guide
prints, no hook she did not ask for spends a second. the only sign of the release is one more line
in `rhx list`.

## .[tn] sketch

```
given('[case8] a repo that links the learner, upgrades, runs `rhx init --hooks`, and never runs the toggle')
  when('[t0] a developer\'s clone works past an hour and reaches a stop')
    then('the stop is not held, and no term nudge prints')
    then('no Stop entry in the settings runs the learn.domain.terms hook command')
  when('[t1] the learner role as shipped is read')
    then('it declares no onStop hook')
```

⇒ the extant clamp `getLearnerRoleHookRegistration.integration.test.ts` already asserts t0's second
then and t1 for this repo; it stays green, since this repo does not enable the toggle.
