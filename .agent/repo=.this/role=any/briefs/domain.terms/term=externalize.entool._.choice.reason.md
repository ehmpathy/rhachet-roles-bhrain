# domain.term.choice.reason: entool

## .etymology

**`en-` + tool** — the english prefix that makes a verb of transformation *into* a form
(*encode*, *enshrine*, *entrust*). to entool is to make a tool of what was not one.

it was coined to join a family this org already speaks. the librarian's tactics are
**enbrief**, **catalogize**, **demonstrate**, **lessonize** — each a verb that names the act of
materialization into one document kind. `enbrief` in particular is the direct model: brief is the
target form, `en-` is the transformation. **entool** and **enskill** extend that family to the
two artifact kinds the learner produces beyond documents.

⇒ the word was not invented in a vacuum. it inherits a productive pattern, which is why it reads
as vocabulary rather than jargon.

## .why not the rejected synonyms

- **automate** — the near-miss, and the reason a coined word was worth it. `automate` names the
  *removal of a human*, and it is silent on what matters here: **which rung**. an automated
  process may still demand a brain to run it (rung 2 is "automated" in casual speech and still
  charges imagine cost on every use). `entool` names the target form — *a tool* — so the ladder is
  legible in the word itself
- **toolify** / **scriptify** — the `-ify` suffix reads as informal and breaks the `en-` family
  the org already uses. one concept, one word-shape (`rule.prefer.symmetric-term-pairs`)
- **productionize** — names a *readiness* bar (deployable, monitored), which is a different axis
  entirely. a rung-4 tool may never reach production; a productionized service may still be fluid

## .the seam — why `harden` is not a synonym either

`ehmpathy/rhachet` declares its own verb for the climb:

> *"to harden a thought route = to move it toward 🪨 solid on the determinism spectrum"*
> — `define.term.skill.thought-routes.md`

we adopt rhachet's rungs and glyphs, so the question is fair: is `entool` merely `harden` renamed?
**no — the two spans differ, and ours is strictly wider.**

| | the span | covers 🧠 → 📚? |
|---|---|---|
| **harden** | the three 💪 rungs: 💧 fluid → 🔩 rigid → 🪨 solid | no — rhachet declares no brief rung |
| **entool** | all five rungs, 🧠 tribal through 🪨 solid | **yes** |

⇒ **every harden is an entoolment; the reverse does not hold.** one word names a proper subset of
the other, so neither is a second name for one concept — the case
`rule.forbid.domain-term-synonyms` actually forbids.

⚠️ and the wider span is the whole point of this org's ladder: the 🧠 → 📚 step is where a lesson
leaves one brain, and it is the step rhachet has no word for.

## .the pair — why `enskill` is a separate term, not a synonym

settled against `rule.forbid.domain-term-synonyms`'s own test (*one concept or two?*): **two.**

| | act | the rung it moves |
|---|---|---|
| **enskill** | write down a repeated sequence as a runnable artifact | 0 → 2 |
| **entool** | remove the leftover brain work from an extant artifact | 2 → 4 |

**the decisive case:** a tactic can be enskilled and never entooled — a fluid skill that works,
used a dozen times, still charges imagine cost on every use. that is the common state, and it is exactly
what `rule.always.entool-the-skills-you-touch` exists to catch. an unenskilled tactic, by contrast,
has no artifact to entool at all.

⇒ they differ in **precondition and target**, so they are distinct concepts. neither is a
forbidden synonym of the other. `enskill` earns its own cluster.

## .disputes

none on record. both words were coined 2026-08-13, at the wisher's ask, and no contract has drifted from
either.

## .evidence

- **the wisher's ask (2026-08-13)**, which is the source of the concept and the reason it earns a
  word: *"although not all skills are tools … the best skills are tool based. no brains required
  is the ideal scenario … it lets deterministic brainless operations execute value"*, with the two
  costs named — *"energy → money, resources → nature"* and *"time → help people faster"*
- **the pair was requested explicitly**: *"entool the skills you touch should be paired with an
  enskill the tactics you discover … standard operational procedure is a common alias for this
  concept for humans"* — SOP is recorded in the enskill brief as the human-side alias, never as a
  rival term
- **immediate field instance:** `rule.always.entool-the-skills-you-touch` fired on the very next
  action after it was written. the boot manifests for learner + librarian had to be verified by
  hand, because the bidirectional boot test covers only driver and achiever. caught in
  `.dream/v2026_08_13.entool.boot-manifest-guard.md` — a rung-3 gap found by the rule that names it
- **invariant:** `entool` is a verb of THIS org's practice vocabulary (it composes learner rules
  and a learner philosophy published via `dist/`), not generic english like `run` or `build`
