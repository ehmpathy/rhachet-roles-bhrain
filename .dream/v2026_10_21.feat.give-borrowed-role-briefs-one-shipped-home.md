# 🌙 dream: give borrowed role briefs one shipped home, in place of symlinks between role dirs

kind: feat · owner: rhachet-roles-bhrain · caught 2026-10-21

## .the cue that fired

peer `arch-smell-scopeleaks` at `v2026_10_21.fix-clamp-role-boot-budgets` 5.1 i001 flagged the
driver's `rule.forbid.verbatim-quotes-outside-seeds.md.min` as a symlink into the learner's
`briefs/`: one role reaches into another by path. the pr followed the extant mechanism (fulcrum
F13); the design question stays open.

## .the gap, precisely

`git ls-tree HEAD src/domain.roles/driver/briefs/` lists symlinks into
`src/domain.roles/learner/briefs/` for `rule.always.archive-the-wishers-words-verbatim` (both
`.md` and `.md.min`) and `rule.forbid.verbatim-quotes-outside-seeds.md`. the learner also boots
`rule.always.catch-dreams-for-followups` from the driver by the same means. a rename in the owner
role dangles the borrower's link; `roleBootClamps.integration.test.ts` catches it, but only after
the fact, and the owner cannot see who borrows.

## .the shape of the fix

1. pick a shipped home both roles read, e.g. `src/domain.roles/_shared/briefs/` (the build copies
   `briefs/**` from any dir under `src/domain.roles/`; confirm)
2. move each borrowed `.md` and `.md.min` pair there, by hand, file first
3. point each role's `boot.yml` say entry at the shared path. `rhachet roles boot` reads briefs
   relative to the spec's own dir and dropped a `../../` path in a probe at this pr, so test a
   `../_shared/...` entry first; it may need a rhachet change
4. delete the symlinks; extend `roleBootClamps` to assert no booted brief is a symlink into a
   peer role

## .why it is not done in this round

not CLEAN: it moves briefs across three roles, changes their boot specs, and may need a rhachet
change if boot specs cannot read outside their own dir. a boot-budget pr is the wrong place for a
new convention.
