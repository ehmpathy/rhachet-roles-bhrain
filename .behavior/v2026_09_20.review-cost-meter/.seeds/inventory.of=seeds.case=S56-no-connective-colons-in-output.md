# S56 — a tree row never opens with a connective word and a colon

- said 2026-10-13, on the constraint halt's options block

## .said

> also, never ever use `:` again;    │  ├─ or: narrow its bind — yours to make

## .settled

- a tree row that opens with a connective or label word plus a colon — `or:`, `tip:`, `then:`,
  `also:`, `note:`, `hint:` — is forbidden in rendered output. the tree's own shape already says
  the row is an alternative, a hint, or a step; the colon is a second marker for one fact
- the repair is plain prose: `or narrow its bind — yours to make`
- a `key: value` field row is not this — there the colon parts a key from its value
- the ban is held by a clamp over snapshot bodies, not by a catalog row (the lesson of S55)
