## .the shape of the fix

`.agent/repo=ehmpathy/role=mechanic/skills/claude.tools/`:
- paths must sit within the repo (same guard as its peers)
- a closed set of modes (`+x`, `-x`), no octal
- plan by default, `--mode apply` to write; idempotent
- then add it to the permissions allowlist, so `permissions.get --head chmod` names it
