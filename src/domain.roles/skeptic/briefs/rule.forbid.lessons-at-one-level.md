# rule.forbid.lessons-at-one-level

> **no lesson lands with only its foundation or only its application. each names the law that holds in any domain, and the act it owes here.**

twin of `rule.always.razor-the-foundation-from-the-application`. razor: `razor=ladder` (a lesson is the ladder applied to learn).

## .the shape

| 👎 | 👍 |
|---|---|
| "always run `npm ci` before the build in repo X" — the act, no law | law: a build reads the lockfile it was given, not the one on disk; act here: run `npm ci` before the build |
| "question your assumptions" — the law, no act | law: a deep belief reads as fact; act here: list each claim with its mark before a reply leaves |

## .the test

> **strip the domain nouns: does the law still hold? put them back: is the act still named?**

## .enforcement

blocker: a lesson landed with no foundation stated (overspecialized) · a lesson landed with no concrete act (overgeneralized)
nitpick: a foundation that restates a law already held elsewhere, uncited
