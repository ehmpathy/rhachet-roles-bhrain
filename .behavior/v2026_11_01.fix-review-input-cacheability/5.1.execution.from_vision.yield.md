## .what a reader must act on

- 🔴 **the rubric's cross-round reuse is given up, by design.** the anatomy clamp measures it on a
  fixture whose rubric is 23% of its prompt: the shipped layout's cross-round share falls from
  76.8% to 51.7% (`reviewPromptCacheAnatomy` `[case6]`). on a heavy real prompt the rubric is ~7%;
  the cross-lane gain is what `5.3.verification` measures
- `rules` and `refs` stay marked `cacheable` in the census tables: one static table labels the
  whole historical corpus, where they led. the note at `IS_CACHEABLE_SECTION` says the claim is
  now partly true; a seam derived from the layout is a caught dream
  (`dreams/v2026_11_05.fix.cacheable-seam-derived-from-layout.md`) — it changes the `seam` field
  of the `review._.cost` json
- the reviewed code now rides the system prompt with the corpus (F5, open). the trade is stated
  at `asPromptHead`
- a split lane still skips the level meet (`dreams/v2026_11_01.feat.split-lanes-meet-across-the-level.md`);
  its runs now record `skipped.partition`, so `5.3` can count their share of spend off disk
- the census rank now prints the target rows ahead of `rules` and `refs`, the wire order
- `breaks in: head` is now the usual shape of a repair round (a shared target changed); the
  cache across lanes is carried by the meet, which the pair now grades
- the `--json` of `review._.cost` gains `meet` and `minutesOfCall` on each run row, `meet` and
  `expectedFrom` on each pair, `byMeet` on tree and grove, and `via` on a warm `cacheGrade`
- 🔴 **the lane meet's field figures sit at break-even.** across this route's own rounds
  (i001–i003, the level-1 pours) the cross-lane head hit on ~14% of followers (1/7 each round;
  `appendix/measurement.after.md` § p), against a 13%
  break-even for the poke, and each lane waited 17–38s at the meet. the poke is ungated, and with
  no replica pin (openrouter #34) its hit is chance. `5.3` measures it; a gate on the measured
  rate is a caught dream (`dreams/v2026_11_05.feat.lane-meet-gated-by-measured-hit-rate.md`)
- the meet's spinner shows only on a terminal: the guard runs its lanes progress-silent, so
  under the guard the wait shows naught and no record holds its seconds yet
- a trail behind a leader that went `solo` still reads `awaited` — warm when no head was
  warmed (`dreams/v2026_11_05.feat.meet-outcome-trailed.md`); a first round that met warm has
  no `--of run` prefix line yet (`dreams/v2026_11_05.feat.first-round-prefix-verdict-of-a-warm-meet.md`)
