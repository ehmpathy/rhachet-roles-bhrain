## 🔮 telepath

- **scale**: message-level, actor to actor
- **focus**: max signal, min noise — in both directions
- **maximizes**: concepts transferred per word

the generic communication role every bhrain leverages to reach other **actors**. an actor may be
human or robot, and the robot reader — a many-language model — is first-class here, never a side
benefit.

### .the root purpose — elucidation

> **elucidation is the transfer of a concept until the peer can USE it.**
> — quoted from `define.elucidation`, which declares it; amend it there

every rule here is one surface of that, and it runs both directions — each with a face, and both
required:

| direction | the act | the artifact | the face |
|---|---|---|---|
| into a peer | give a concept | an answer | output — speak terse |
| from a peer | gain a concept | a question | input — answer what they MEANT, never what they typed |

⇒ the name is the destination: telepathy is 100% signal, 0% words, therefore 0% noise.

⇒ `rule.require.elucidation` · `define.elucidation`

### .which face loses more — neither, without a scope named

most advice covers only the output half. the two lose at different scales:

| scope | the larger loss | why |
|---|---|---|
| one message | the input half | a misread transfers zero concepts, whatever the output half does |
| a system, over many artifacts | the output half | bloat compounds until review itself stops to function |

⇒ measured: a 4.5k-token wish grew 155.8k of yields, and four reviewers overflowed and rendered
**no verdict at all** — and an unreviewed reviewer is indistinguishable from a clean one.

## .the canon — declared ONCE here

each rule states its own position in one line and cites this table. the order lives here and
nowhere else, so a new rule changes one file.

🟡 the tables below ARE the enumeration, and no count of them is stated — a struck row shows in an
enumeration and cannot show in an arithmetic.

🟡 **every act carries its inverse clamp**, because a forbid names a shape a reader can recognize
where a require names a duty they must already suspect they failed.

⇒ **and a recognizable shape is what a CHECKER reaches too, so the clamp is the mechanizable half.**

- every shipped checker and every probe lands on a **forbid**, with two exceptions
  - `require.place-a-passage-by-its-subject` and `require.generic-governs-structure-never-voice`
    each name a shape outright, so each is reachable directly
- ⇒ so a require is verified **through its clamp**, and a require whose clamp is uncovered is a gap
  - measured 2026-09-07 over the 25 rule files on disk: 4 checked · 11 probed · 3 clamped ·
    7 unreachable · **0 unaccounted**
  - the settler is `.agent/.notes/tool.map-rules-to-verification.js`, which **computes** the clamp
    column rather than asserts it — an uncovered clamp is a promise, never a check

🟡 **the unreachable each carry a reason, and each is the rule's own**: two grade a live message
(D5), one grades what is **absent**, one is **the root**, one grades whether a **pass ran**, one
grades a **degree**, and one grades a **question never put**. a regex reaches a shape on the page,
and no one of those is on the page.

### the acts — in order, because each measures what the one before produced

| # | fires on | the act | its clamp |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | before a word exists | `require.purpose-first` — should this exist, and at what target | `forbid.purposeless-passages` — a section a template named |
| 2 | the domain | `require.domain-discovery` — what is the fundamental truth here | `forbid.surface-speak` — see below, it clamps both 2 and 3 |
| 3 | the concept | `require.distillation` — drilled to its root, condensed | `forbid.surface-speak` — an adjective for a mechanism |
| 4 | the container | `require.bulletize` — the tree rendered, one concept per line | `forbid.narration` — a tree serialized into a paragraph |
| 5 | the draft | `require.reflexive-condensation` — each line at the 95% bar | — none: an absent pass has no shape to forbid |

🟡 **acts 2 and 3 share one clamp, and that is a property of the defect rather than a gap.** an
undiscovered domain produces a claim pitched at its surface, which is the shape `forbid.surface-speak`
already reads — so the two acts fail into one recognizable form even though their subjects differ:
discovery maps the territory, distillation drills one point in it.

🟡 act 5 carries a second peer on a different axis — `rule.require.dense-prose`, the one rule here at
`ref` tier. it is a **degree** peer rather than an inverse clamp, so it sits beside the row rather
than in its clamp column:

- `require.reflexive-condensation` grades whether the pass RAN — binary
- `require.dense-prose` grades how dense the result IS — a degree
- ⇒ an artifact can fail one and pass the other, which is what earns it a rule rather than a clause
- 🟡 both are blockers. a degree does not soften a rule — `require.brevity` grades a degree at the
  line grain and blocks; this grades the same property over a passage

### the graders — fire on the result, in any order

| rule | the grain it reads |
|---|---|
| `require.disputable-claims` ←→ `forbid.subversive-prose` | falsifiability — could the reader disagree, and know what settles it? |
| `forbid.diffusion` ←→ `forbid.emphasis-noise` | rank — a pole pair. one buries the needle, one elevates every line |
| `forbid.width-ambiguous-glyphs` | render — does the mark occupy the cells the terminal reserved? |
| `forbid.obfuscation` | selection — is what the reader needs even here? |
| `forbid.decode-friction` → `forbid.riddles` | assembly — how many steps from these words to the claim? a genus and its species |
| `forbid.absolutes` | quantification — what is the set, and did you walk it? |
| `forbid.defensive-prose` | aim — is the reader modeled as an actor, or as a judge? |
| `require.place-a-passage-by-its-subject` | placement — is this section about that? |
| `require.archaeology-in-notes` | the third verdict — move and ref, where cut and keep both lose |

🟡 the three falsifiability-adjacent graders overlap on purpose. two cues cannot disagree; they can
only both miss or one catch, so a redundant detector costs a line and a missed one costs the defect
(`rule.require.a-cue-is-not-a-claim`).

🟡 **the assembly row is the one grain no density rule reaches.** a decode-friction sentence passes
`require.brevity`, `forbid.rambles`, and `require.bulletize` whole — no word in it is surplus, and
the defect is the ORDER and the REFERENCE. ⇒ so it is not a stricter brevity rule; it grades a
property the brevity rules are blind to by construction.

⇒ and its arrow is a genus→species, never a pole pair: a **fetch** reads as an oversight, so an
author cuts it; a **riddle** reads as the good part, so the same sweep passes over it. the genus
alone catches half of what it names.

### the root, the input face, and the seam

| rule | its subject |
|---|---|
| `require.elucidation` | the root every rule above is a surface of |
| `require.answer-what-they-meant` ←→ `forbid.genie-answers` | the input face — the concept they meant, not the string they typed |
| `require.generic-governs-structure-never-voice` | the seam — what telepath may and may not prescribe |

### .the seam it must never cross

telepath governs a message's **structure** — what it says, in what order, in how many words, where
each passage sits. it governs the voice not at all: capitalization, emoji, persona, and mascot
belong to the role that adopts these rules and compose on top, so every mascot adopts without a fork.
