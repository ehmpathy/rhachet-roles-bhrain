# 🌙 dream: `--as rewound` has no GRAIN — it wipes the self ladder to void the peer verdicts

- **kind** = feat · **owner** = `rhachet-roles-bhrain` · **caught 2026-09-10**

## .the cue that fired

the wisher, mid-design on `v2026_09_08.feat-dispute-or-concede-review-budget`, verbatim:

> *"and can we ad `--as rewound --grain peer|self|both` ? as part of this too"*
>
> *"since we typically want to rewound peer reviews in case of severe refactors, but not self
> reviews"*

⇒ **the second sentence is the whole dream.** it names a partition the engine has drawn and never
exposed, and it names the case where the absence costs.

## .the gap, precisely

`delStoneGuardArtifacts.ts:24-51` sweeps **five globs in one pass**, and three of the five are the
self ladder:

| the glob | its grain |
|---|---|
| `enumRouteGuardReviewPeerFiles` | **peer** — the `.given` / `.taken` corpus |
| `enumRouteGuardJudgeFiles` | the judge — shared, derived from both |
| `.route/${stone}.guard.promise.*.md` | 🔴 **self** |
| `.route/${stone}.guard.selfreview.*.triggered.*.md` | 🔴 **self** |
| `.route/${stone}.blocked.triggered` | the halt marker — neither |

**checkable:** the function takes `{ stone, route }` and no third parameter. `rewindAffectedStones`
calls it once per affected stone with no scope. `setStoneAsRewound.ts:16-20` takes
`{ stone, route, yield? }` — `yield` is the only knob, and it governs the **yield archive**, never
the artifact sweep.

⇒ **so a driver who restructures an artifact and voids its peer verdicts also re-arms every self
review on that stone**, plus every self review on every **cascade** stone
(`computeAffectedStonesForRewind`).

## .why it costs more than a re-run

`rule.always.bear-every-self-review` states it outright:

> *"the count is the WORK, never a budget … more reviews cost only yours"*

a peer re-run costs a **budget round**, and budget is the resource `S09` ruled scarce. a self re-bear
costs the **driver's attention**, uncapped, with no lever to waive it — *"there is no lever to grant
it."* ⇒ **the two are not interchangeable, so a sweep that treats them as one charges the wrong
meter.**

⚠️ **and the harm is a PULL toward the wrong move, over a cost paid honestly.** a driver who knows a
rewind re-arms eight self reviews will reach for a dispute they do not hold, or re-arrive on prose
they know is stale — both cheaper than the correct lever. ⇒ **a lever priced above its alternatives
is a lever nobody takes.**

## .the shape of the fix

1. **`delStoneGuardArtifacts` takes a `grain: 'peer' | 'self' | 'both'`**, defaulted to `'both'` so
   every extant caller is unchanged. the five globs sort as the table above; the **judge** goes with
   `peer` (it tallies peer verdicts) and the **halt marker** goes with `both`
2. **`rewindAffectedStones` and `setStoneAsRewound` pass it through** — one parameter each, no logic
3. **the cli gains `--grain`**, validated against the three values, defaulted to `both`
4. **the emit names what it deleted, by grain** — `formatRouteStoneEmit`'s `cascade` block already
   renders the four counts `delStoneGuardArtifacts` returns; it needs a grain line above them
5. 🔴 **the meter reset follows the grain too.** `resetRouteStoneGuardReviewPeerMeters` is a **peer**
   operation, so `--grain self` must not call it — a self-only rewind that refunds peer budget is a
   budget leak, and `S09` ruled budget scarce

🟡 **the default is `both`, deliberately.** the wisher's *"we typically want"* names the common case
and not the safe one: a driver who omits the flag after a refactor gets today's behaviour, which is
**over**-broad rather than under-broad. ⇒ `rule.require.safe-by-default` — the bare invocation keeps
the wider clear.

## .why it is not done in this round

**safe? no. clean? no.**

- 🔴 **`--as rewound` is SHIPPED and unguarded.** `setStoneAsRewound.ts` carries no human gate, so
  every route in every repo reaches it. a regression in what it deletes is a regression in the one
  operation a stuck driver reaches for
- **`delStoneGuardArtifacts` has a pinned test suite** (`delStoneGuardArtifacts.test.ts`) plus
  `setStoneAsRewound.test.ts`, whose cases assert the five-set sweep and its cascade
- 🔴 **it crosses the host wish's own boundary** — `0.wish.md`: *"do not touch the SELF-review
  ladder."* the grain flag's whole purpose is to change what a rewind does to that ladder
- ⚠️ **this bullet once read *"the self ladder's trigger state is the ripple nobody sees … a change
  to a state machine"*, and the read on 2026-09-11 found it OVERSTATED.**
  `setStoneAsPassed.ts:214` gates the ladder with
  `selfReviews.filter(r => !promisedSlugs.has(r.slug))` — a set-difference over promise files that
  `getStonePromises` stamps `hash: 'hashless'`, *"firm checkpoints that don't invalidate"*.
  ⇒ **on the self half, step 1 IS a filter on a delete list**, and this dream is cheaper than it was
  first priced
- 🟡 **the `.triggered` files are a separate axis and do not change that.**
  `getSelfReviewChallengeDecision` reads them for the 30-second timer and the plowthrough count —
  they gate **when** a promise is admitted, never **whether the review is owed**
- 🔴 **what stays dirty is the PEER half**: `resetRouteStoneGuardReviewPeerMeters` must not fire
  under `--grain self`. and `$stone.blocked.triggered`, the fifth glob, has a grain that is
  **neither** self nor peer, so `--grain` must rule on it outright rather than let it fall out

⇒ deferred for **dirt**, so a fulcrum is owed beside the dream:
`F023` on `v2026_09_08.feat-dispute-or-concede-review-budget`'s board.

## 🟡 .what the host route DOES deliver, so this dream does not hold the round

the wisher bounded it themselves — *"or should that be separate and we just teach `--as rewound`"*
— and the teach lands this round: the driver briefs gain `--as rewound` as the **third lever**
beside dispute and concede, **with its cost stated**. a driver who takes it knows it re-arms the
self ladder.

⇒ **so the gap is a priced lever rather than an absent one**, which is what makes the deferral
honest rather than convenient.
