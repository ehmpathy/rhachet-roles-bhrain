# 🌙 dream: skeptic.claims backs a claim by its source, never by the turn it was read in

kind: fix · owner: role=skeptic · caught 2026-11-26

## .the cue that fired

the human asked why a claim needs a tool call of THIS turn, and why an evidence file would not suffice. the vision (1.vision.yield.md:277) carried the turn scope over from the source unquestioned; its own research item (:480) says to measure the false-refusal rate before trust, and the dispatch corpus (`skeptic.stop.dispatch [case9]`, .why) records peer i006 judged 6 of 12 refusals false over 47 real replies. the hook shipped on by default (F002) with that rate unaddressed.

## .the gap, precisely

- a turn opens at each human prompt, so a new message makes every read of the prior turn "unread" with no state moved. staleness is a property of the SOURCE, not of the turn boundary
- the role's own yield law (`trust-but-verify`) accepts a yield that carries a proof per claim. the hook refuses the same proof when it sits in a file rather than in a call of this turn
- a list item under a hedged label ("I will:") does not inherit the hedge
- a reply that explains past refusals is all claims about past turns, so it is refused in a loop

## .the shape of the fix

- backed = a call of this turn whose text holds the key, OR a cited evidence path on the line (`path` or `path:line`) whose text the hook reads now and finds the key in. a citation the file does not hold stays unbacked, so a forged cite fails
- list items inherit a hedge from their parent label
- the hook's own refusal text, quoted, is no claim
- measure the false-refusal rate on the tagged corpus before default-on stays settled (reopen F002)

## .why it is not done in this round

it changes the vision's settled mechanism and a foreman fulcrum (F002); the human's call on the stance comes first.
