# case 1 — a driver's guard review on openrouter

happy × criti · the densest walk (5 boundaries)

## .narrative

a clone drives a route and arrives at a stone. the guard runs
`rhx review.by --role mechanic --for ...` with no `--brain` flag. `review.by` reaches for
`DEFAULT_REVIEW_BRAIN = openrouter/deepseek/flash`. rhachet finds the brain because the package
scan lists `rhachet-brains-openrouter`. keyrack supplies `OPENROUTER_API_KEY`, and openrouter
routes the ask to the cheapest fast zero-retention host.

this reviewer writes prose with no numeric count, so the guard builds its tally sub-brain lazily,
also `openrouter/deepseek/flash`, and reads `2 blockers · 1 nitpick` out of the prose. the
artifact footer reads `tallied by reviewer@openrouter/deepseek/flash`. the clone sees a normal
rejection and answers it. it never knew a vendor changed.

## .timeline

```
given('[case1] a route stone whose guard runs review.by with no --brain; OPENROUTER_API_KEY unlocked')
  when('[t0] the driver runs --as arrived')

  when('[t1] the reviewer emits prose with no numeric counts')

    then('the footer reads tallied by reviewer@openrouter/deepseek/flash')
```
