# rule.forbid.diffusion

> **do not diffuse the purpose of a passage among its prose. do not lose the needle in the haystack —
> ELEVATE the needle.**

the clamp on `rule.require.elucidation`. that rule names the target; this names the smell.

⇒ the etymology, the seven smells, and four worked 👎/👍 pairs: `define.diffusion` (ref).

## .the needle is PRESENT — that is what parts it from every peer

| the failure | the reader's state |
|---|---|
| the needle is **absent** | the point was never made — a different defect |
| **diffusion** | the point **is there, and cannot be found** |

⇒ it grades **findability**, never presence. an author who states the point once, truly, then
surrounds it with six facts of equal rank has diffused it — **and every per-sentence rule passes.**

## .the subject is PURPOSE, not words

| rule | its subject |
|---|---|
| `forbid.rambles` | a **line** that runs past its own point |
| **this rule** | a **passage** whose purpose is spread across rival points |

⇒ **a three-line passage diffuses** if it carries three claims of equal weight. length is neither
necessary nor sufficient, so a cut is not the repair.

## .the test

> **read the passage once, fast. can you point at the line that carries its purpose?**

yes → concentrated · no, and the point is absent → a different defect · **no, and the point is in
there somewhere** → diffuse. elevate it.

| when… | then… |
|---|---|
| a passage holds two claims of equal weight | the strongest cue. one is the needle; subordinate the other |
| you list five reasons at one depth | rank them. one carries the claim; the rest are its children |
| a reader must infer your claim | assert it. an implied point is an unfound point |
| the same point sits in three places | none is authoritative. elevate one, cite it from the rest |
| a bulletized passage still reads flat | correct — `bulletize` renders a tree and cannot tell a tree from a rake |
| your ask spreads across three unstated dimensions | the input-direction case. state the ground |
| every member answers the same question about a different subject | not diffuse — that is a **table**, and its header is the needle |

## .the repair is ELEVATE, and it is not a cut

| rule | its repair |
|---|---|
| `require.reflexive-condensation` | cut the haystack **down** |
| **this rule** | raise the needle **up** |

**the haystack may stay.** promote the point to a header, a lead line, a bolded claim, or a parent
bullet with the rest as its children.

⇒ one rule changes the **volume**, the other the **gradient**. **a passage can be perfectly condensed
and perfectly diffuse**, which is why the two do not merge.

## .the axis

a structure rule, so telepath owns it (`rule.require.generic-governs-structure-never-voice`). a
diffuse passage is diffuse in every register, so it prescribes no member of that rule's voice row.

blocker: a passage with no line a reader can point at as its purpose · two claims of equal weight,
neither subordinated · a claim the reader must infer · the same point in three places with none
authoritative · a flat list where one member carries the claim.
nitpick: a question whose ground is unstated.
false positive: a genuinely flat set on one axis — that is a **table** · facts placed **under** a
claim that ranks above them · a long passage whose purpose is concentrated in one place.

⇒ its pole is `rule.forbid.emphasis-noise`, which declares the pair: this rule buries the needle
(elevate one line), that one elevates every line (cut back to one). a passage can fail both at once.
