## .the gap, precisely

`enumRouteGuardReviewPeerConversationFiles` unions **every prior generation** of `.given` + `.taken`
on the stone. there is no depth parameter on it, and `rhx review --help` carries no depth flag
either:

```
--conversation <files>  comma-separated prior peer-review dialogue files (.given + .taken)
                        to thread as context (opt-in; guard expands $conversation)
```

⇒ so the guard can decide **whether** a lane threads the conversation, and cannot decide **how much
of it**. the only two settings are *all of it* and *none of it*, and this guard already spends the
second on `behavior-intent-coverage` with a fifteen-line justification for why no other lane may
copy it.

**the measured growth is 20–40k a round.** at i032 this same cliff took `behavior-intent-coverage`
(758.1k against a 750k gate, 398.9k of it conversation across 504 files). at i035 it took three more
lanes. **the identical failure, twice, eleven rounds apart, on different lanes** — which is the
signature of an unbounded term rather than a mis-sized bind.

### 🔴 why the extant levers do not close it

| lever | why it falls short |
|---|---|
| narrow `--paths-with` / `--paths-wout` | a **one-time** cut against an **unbounded** growth. the i036 narrow buys ~7–10 rounds |
| trim `--refs` | the i033 note already refuses it — the refs ARE that lane's subject |
| drop `--conversation` | ✅ closes it, and **costs the lane every argument ever made back to it**. a reviewer that cannot read a `.taken` re-raises settled points forever |
| a hand-run `rhx review` | ⛔ `rule.forbid.hand-run-reviews` — no budget, no `.given`, gates naught |

⇒ **every driver-owned lever is either a deferral or a coverage loss.** that is what makes this an
engine gap rather than a guard defect.

## .the shape of the fix

a depth bound on the conversation bind, expressed where the guard can reach it:

```
--conversation <files> --conversation-depth <n>     # thread only the last n generations
```

or, equivalently, a `$conversation.recent(n)` expansion the guard can ask for.

**n = 2 or 3 is the shape to aim at**, and the reason is in the contemplation contract rather than
in a token budget: a reviewer re-raises a point it was never told about, and it is told in the
`.taken` of the round that answered it. ⇒ **the generations that carry a live debt are the recent
ones**; generation 1 of 35 holds arguments that were settled, superseded, or repaired thirty rounds
back, and it costs the same tokens as the round that matters.

⚠️ **and the bound must be per-lane, not global.** a lane that grades a JUDGMENT wants depth; a lane
that grades COVERAGE wants none (this guard's own split). a single engine-wide constant would force
one of those two to be wrong.

### the counter-case, stated fairly

a truncated conversation can lose a settled argument, and a reviewer that cannot see it will
re-raise it — the exact failure `rule.always.converge-with-reviewers.via-a-taken-per-point` is about.
⇒ so the depth is a **fork with a real cost on both arms**, which is why it is also carried as a
fulcrum (`F25`) rather than only as a dream.

## .why it is not done in this round

**SAFE** — no. it changes `rhx review`'s public flag surface and the guard expansion contract, in a
repo whose consumers are every route in every tree that adopts these roles.

**CLEAN** — no. it ripples through `parseReviewArgs`, `stepReview`, the `$conversation` expansion in
`runStoneGuardReviews`, the guard template in `rhachet-roles-bhuild`, and every extant `.guard` that
spells `--conversation $conversation`.

⇒ both answers are no, so the deferral is correct — and per
`rule.always.fix-forward-under-scouts-honor` a deferral for **dirt** owes a fulcrum beside the
dream, which `F25` is.
