# domain.term.choice.reason: denial

## .etymology

`denial` is the act of the system that bars the read, which is what `EACCES` reports: "permission
denied". it is the word the contracts already use (`isErrorOfDenial`, `CODES_OF_DENIAL`).

rejected:

| rejected | why |
|---|---|
| `barred` | an adjective of the path. the contract names the error, not the path's state |
| `forbidden` | the repo's rule directive `forbid` already owns the word |
| `unreadable` | true of a denial, and also loosely said of an absence. a word that covers both halves of the pair parts naught |

## .why it is itemized with its counterpart

the argument for the pair — two fs failures that both arrive as an empty read, with opposite
answers — is stated once, in `term=fs.error.absence._.choice.reason.md`, and cited here rather
than restated.

## .disputes

none.
