# example: the `howdoes` gap

a demo of `rule.require.classify-the-yields-document-kind._.md`, and specifically of its claim that
**a kind picked mid-research reveals what you did not write.**

**2026-08 · `ehmpathy/declastruct-aws` · a set of six security briefs.**

## .what happened

the briefs were authored, then classified. the classification surfaced as a live question **three
separate times** across the round, and the third one —

> *"where's the `howdoes.` brief that explains that?"*

— named a prefix that **did not exist in the repo yet**.

the brief written to fill it was called *"the heart of the solution"* by its reader. it was written
**last**, and only because someone asked a mechanism question the other five could not answer.

## .the cost

the answer came from what happened to be remembered, rather than from a probe. by the time the gap
was visible the research was closed, so the most valuable brief in the set was the one written with
the least evidence behind it.

## .what the rule takes from it

a researcher who classified at `3.3.probes.absorb.gaps` would have found the missed kind while the
research was still open, and probed for it.

⇒ this is also the sharpest case for the `howto` / `howdoes` split: five howtos could not answer a
mechanism question, and no reader could tell from their prefixes that they could not.
