## .what the set shows

- **the foundation holds.** every candidate, the three new laws included, applies trust-no-one. none asks to sit beside it (F21)
- **the three laws share one gap: how a read is WEIGHED.** the canon says to read and to question; it does not say what a read is worth once in hand. diagnosticity, one burden, and the implication of a true fact all live there
- **one seam inside the canon.** lesson 7 (Tetlock, small steps) and the lightness virtue (update at once) read as opposites. both say the step equals the evidence: one forbids a jump, the other a rearguard retreat. lesson 7 states only half (see `verdict=row`, row 12)
- **the methods are claims too.** ACH, the best-known structured method here, has little evidence that it reduces bias. so each law's mechanism owes a measure before it ships (research 12's false-refusal measure is the shape)
