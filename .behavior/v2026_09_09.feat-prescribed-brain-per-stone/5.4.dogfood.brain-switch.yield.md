## 🔴 .why `clone get` cannot confirm the switch

- the clone **serial** is rhachet's; the transcript **exid** is the brain-cli's filename
- no map joins them, so `clone get` infers the file by cwd and spawn time
- two clones in one cwd tie, and it refuses rather than link the wrong transcript — 11 ids ambiguous here
- ⇒ `clone get` reads another clone from outside. a hook reads itself from inside, and the payload
  names its transcript: `transcript_path`, already parsed at `src/contract/cli/telepath.ts`
