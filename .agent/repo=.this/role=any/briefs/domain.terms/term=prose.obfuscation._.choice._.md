it is the one term in this boundary that names a property of what is **absent** from the page. the
shapes, the test, and the boundary against condensation live in that rule.

## 🟡 .why the word cannot be `omission`

the rejection is the glossary's own half, and it turns on one fact the rule states: accuracy is what
this defect is **built from**, never a defense against it.

⇒ `omission` grades the absence alone, and **a cut below the 95% bar is also an omission and is
correct**. what parts them is whether the absence changes what the reader would DO — a property
`omission` cannot carry and `obfuscation` does.

## .the trio it completes, and why three words are owed

| word | its grain | what it reads |
|---|---|---|
| `subversive` prose | falsifiability | what is claimed |
| `diffusion` | findability | where the point sits |
| **obfuscation** | selection | what is ABSENT |

🟡 **the two clean rules pass this prose.** each shape of obfuscation is fully disputable and fully
elevated, so its peers do not fire — which makes it the one defect that survives a careful review by
an author who applied both.

## .its repair inverts every peer in the boundary

| word | its repair |
|---|---|
| `condensation` | a cut |
| `diffusion` | an elevation |
| `narration` | a rewrite |
| **obfuscation** | an ADDITION |

⇒ that inversion is the tell, and it is why the word must not be `spin` or `misdirection` — each
implies the fix is to remove a distortion, where here the fix is to supply what was withheld.

## .the peer terms it does not name

| word | what it is | why not `obfuscation` |
|---|---|---|
| `omission` | any absence | most absences are correct — the 95% bar cuts by design |
| `diffusion` | the point is present and unfindable | this one grades a point that is not there |
| `emphasis` | the device that ranks a line | obfuscation may use it — a decoy needle is elevated |
| `condensation` | the cut to the bar | its cuts are the false positives of this term |
