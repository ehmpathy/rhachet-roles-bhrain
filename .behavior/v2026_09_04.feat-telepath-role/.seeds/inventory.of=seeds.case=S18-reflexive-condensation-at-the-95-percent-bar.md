# S18 · reflexive condensation — the 95% bar

## .said

verbatim, 2026-09-04, mid-drive at `1.vision`:

> and also separately enrule a reflexive condensation of communicated thought. always reflectively condense to maximize signal, eliminate noise. if its not 95%+ important to mention, dont mention it.

## .settled

**condensation is REFLEXIVE — a pass over what you already wrote, run every time, not a style you
try to hold as the words appear.**

the bar is stated as a number, and the number is the point:

- **95%+ important to mention** → it stays
- **all else** → 🔴 it goes, and the author does not get to argue for it

⇒ that is a **default-delete**, and it inverts how an author naturally reads their own draft: every
sentence is there because a past self judged it worth the words, so the burden normally sits on the
cut. the bar moves the burden onto the **line**.

### a NUMBER is what makes it operable

*"be concise"* is unfalsifiable — every author believes they were. *"95%+"* forces a per-line verdict
a second reader can check:

| the question | what it does |
|---|---|
| *"is this concise?"* | the author says yes. every time |
| *"is this line 95%+ important?"* | the author must **rank** it against the lines that stay |

⇒ the bar is high **on purpose** — it is not *"cut the obvious filler"*, it is *"cut each line that
is merely useful"*.

### the separation the wisher prescribed

> *"and also **separately** enrule…"*

⇒ condensation is its own rule, not folded into `fundamentalism` or `bulletize`. the word
**separately** is the settlement: it fires at a different **moment** than they do.

| rule | when it fires |
|---|---|
| `fundamentalism` | on the **concept**, before words exist |
| `bulletize` | on the **container**, as words are chosen |
| **condensation** | on the **draft**, after it exists |
