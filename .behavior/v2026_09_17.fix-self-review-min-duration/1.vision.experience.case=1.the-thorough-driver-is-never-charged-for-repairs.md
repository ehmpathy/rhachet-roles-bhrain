## .the narrative

a driver is asked for the `has-questioned-assumptions` self review on `3.3.blueprint`. they read
the blueprint slowly. it takes forty minutes, and they find nine defects — a contract that cannot
hold, a term used two ways, six claims with no cite, a section that narrates its own drafts.

they repair each one as they find it, because the guard's own prompt told them to: *"when you found
issues, fix them before you continue."* each repair edits the blueprint.

then they write what they found into the path the guard printed, and promise — with `--into`, which
states the path they wrote to.

**it clears. no prompt, no wait, and the nine repairs cost naught.**

⇒ **one command.** the forty minutes are measured by no clock that can refuse them — and the nine
edits that used to restart one now touch no key the gate reads.

🔴 **and the prompt is withheld on purpose, which is the part worth a second read.** the guard *does*
still block once per slug, and this driver does not meet it — because the block is bounded by
`elapsed-since-the-ask < 30s` and they arrived at T+39min. **the confrontation exists and they are
not its audience.** ⇒ that is `S13`'s whole design: the prompt is not removed, its **audience** is.

🟡 **so the honest cost is naught**, and an earlier draft of this demo said it was one extra command.
that was true under `S11` alone and false the moment `S13` bounded the block. the wish's
`.the outcome` — *"a driver who has genuinely reviewed is never held"* — is met **literally**, with
no round trip attached.

### .the "aha"

> **the repairs were never a reset. they were the review.**

the clock was never the defect. **the hash key was.** a gate keyed by artifact hash cannot tell a
driver who improved the artifact from a driver who just arrived — so it treated the best evidence
of review as evidence of none.

## .the invariants this case carries

🔴 **a verdict that MOVED because `D6 = changed` is impossible.** `[t0]` is the assertion: nine hash
changes, zero verdict changes. every other demo inherits it — none of them mentions the hash, and
that silence is the point.

🟡 **the invariant is deliberately NOT stated as *"`D6 × every cell` is impossible"***, which is what
this line said until a peer review flagged it. `D6 = changed` is a **reachable state** — this case's
own narrative is a driver who repairs nine times — so a bar on the axis reads as a claim the repair
cannot happen. **the bar is on the verdict's movement, never on the repair.**

🔴 **and `[t2]` now carries TWO assertions rather than one:**

| the clause | what it proves | whose |
|---|---|---|
| *no elapsed duration is reported **as a reason to refuse*** | the wish's requirement 1 — a promise is never refused for time | `S11` |
| 🔴 *the challenge condition is **false**, and one command sufficed* | the confrontation is **targeted** — it exists, and this driver is not its audience | `S13` |

⚠️ **the first clause was reworded by `S13` and the change is not cosmetic.** it read *"no elapsed
duration is reported"* under `S11`, when no elapsed read remained at all. one remains now — it
decides **who hears a paragraph**, never **who passes** — so the clause must name the use rather than
ban the read, or it would forbid the mechanism `S13` requires.
