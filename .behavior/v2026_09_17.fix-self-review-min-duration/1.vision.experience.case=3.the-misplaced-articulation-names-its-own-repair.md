forward, and `S12` removed the level — so the story described a pitfall the round had retired. the

today they are told: **the articulation is absent.** it is not absent. it is thirty minutes of real

### .the "aha"

> **"absent" was never the truth. it was the only sentence the guard had words for.**

a gate that knows one path can report presence. a gate that knows **two** can report a
**mismatch** — and a mismatch names its own repair, where an absence names only a symptom.

⇒ this is `rule.require.errors-name-the-fix` applied to a gate: the error states what, why, and the
concrete next move.

## 🔴 .the assertion that carries the most weight

**`and no rush is alleged`.** today the timer is checked before the path is fully diagnosed, so this
driver waits 30s to be told a falsehood. the path verdict must come first, always — a confrontation
about haste, on a driver whose real defect is a path, teaches them to wait when they should move a
file.

⚠️ **`S11` narrowed the assertion, `S13` narrowed it again, and neither retires it.** the clause
above is no longer *"no clock is consulted"* — one elapsed read survives, **and it can still refuse
this promise**. what the clause now asserts is **`the path verdict is reached before the clock is`**,
which is the property the invariant always wanted:

| | can the clock refuse this promise? | what the refusal costs | does this driver meet the short-circuit? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **B** — the clock gates | 🔴 yes, unboundedly | a **30s wait**, per fresh hash | every driver on a fresh trigger |
| `S11` | ✅ no — no read at all | n/a | every driver |
| 🔴 **`S13`** | 🔴 **yes, once per slug** | **one command**, no wait | **only a promise inside 30s of the ask** |

⇒ **so the residual violation is narrower and real:** the driver who is **both hasty and misplaced**
meets `challenge:first` at `:61-67`, above the file check at `:70`, and learns about haste rather
than about their path. **the assertion moved from *"check the clock last"* to *"the challenge must
know what the filesystem says"*** — the harder version of the same demand, now owed for one driver
rather than all of them.

🔴 **and `S15` makes the omission starker.** with `--into` required, the guard holds the **declared**
path on that very call — so it has both operands a diff needs, and the short-circuit renders none of
them.

## 🌙 .the cell IS RETIRED — `S12` ruled it out of existence

this whole narrative rested on one premise: that the path carries a level the driver cannot compute.
`S12` removed it — `for.$stone._.$slug.md`, keyed `(stone, slug)` exactly as the promise already is.

⇒ **so `c9` — *the driver counts the level forward* — is unreachable rather than repaired.** a driver
cannot guess a component the path does not have.

| what survives this cell | what does not |
|---|---|
| a driver who mistypes the **slug** or the **stone** — a typo | 🌙 the **level pitfall** — the guard-assigned ordinal nobody could compute |
| a driver with a **stale** path from a prior round | 🌙 the off-by-one between three call sites |

🔴 **and the demo is kept rather than deleted, because `--into` was NOT retired with it.** this cell's
own text argued that *"`--into` then has far less to catch, which is why `F09` dominates `F03`"* —
and `S15` ruled the flag **required** anyway, on a reason this cell did not hold: the declared path is
the second operand a **diff** needs, and a rarer mismatch is no easier to explain than a common one.
⇒ **the narrative above is now the demo of a typo rather than of a pitfall**, and that is exactly the
residual `--into` exists for.

⚠️ **and the narrative had to be REWRITTEN to make that sentence true.** this line stood for a round
above a story that still counted `r3` forward from `r2` — **the claim and the demo disagreed, in one
file.** the peer review caught it. ⇒ **the lesson: a retirement marker is not a repair.** to write
*"this cell is retired"* at the foot of a file changes no line above it, and a reader who starts at
the top meets the retired world first.
