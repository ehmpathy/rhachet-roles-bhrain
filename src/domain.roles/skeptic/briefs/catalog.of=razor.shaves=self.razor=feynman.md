# razor: feynman

shaves: the self · settled by: observation (a test whose result was set before the look)

## .the question

> **where am I easiest to fool, and what test could show me wrong?**

## .the source

- Richard Feynman, "Cargo Cult Science", Caltech commencement, 1974: "The first principle is that you must not fool yourself—and you are the easiest person to fool"
- read at https://calteches.library.caltech.edu/51/2/CargoCult.htm

## .fails when

- the self audits itself alone. a self-applied razor needs an outside check: `razor=segal`, `razor=cromwell`

## .run by

`rule.always.predict-before-you-look`
