# 🌙 dream: grepsafe must not cut its output when stdin or stdout is a slow pipe

- kind: reseed
- owner: ehmpathy/rhachet-roles-ehmpathy (role=mechanic, skill=grepsafe)
- caught 2026-11-23

## .the cue that fired
`rhx grepsafe ... | head -n 40` and `rhx audit.test.intentions | rhx grepsafe ...` each ended in `awk: ... fatal: print to "standard output" failed: Resource temporarily unavailable` and `💥 failed with an error`, with the result list cut short.

## .the gap, precisely
a writer on a non-block pipe gets EAGAIN when the reader drains slower than it writes. awk treats EAGAIN as fatal; node drops the queued bytes on a hard `process.exit`. the result is a partial list that reads as complete.

## .the shape of the fix
make stdout wait on a full pipe (or retry on EAGAIN), and clamp it with a case that pipes a >64KB result into `{ sleep 1; cat; }` and asserts the last line arrives. this repo's `audit.test.intentions` case8 is the worked clamp.

the skill lives in another repo; the route here is a practice upgrade.
