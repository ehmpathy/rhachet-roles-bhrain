### the prompt-shape move is the sharpest, and it names this route's own failure mechanism

> *"the stage prompt asks for the outcome world / day-in-the-life / before-after / the aha. **four sections, one fact.**"*

⇒ **a template with a dozen questions produces a dozen headers, and each header pulls prose to fill it.** that is the mechanism behind a 4.5k wish that grew 155.8k of yields — not an author who rambled, but a form that rewarded a section per question.

🟡 **and it is the one move a per-sentence rule can never catch.** brevity grades a sentence; this grades whether the section should exist.

### the `audience` lens generalizes

`#346` demands a `case=N` header declare **who encounters it and what they came for**, because *"a narrative can read in an actor's voice while the artifact never states which actor."*

⇒ **the specific form is behaver's; the generic claim is telepath's** — a passage declares who it is for. that is the `rule.require.generic-governs-structure-never-voice` seam applied: telepath states the obligation, behaver states which header field carries it.
