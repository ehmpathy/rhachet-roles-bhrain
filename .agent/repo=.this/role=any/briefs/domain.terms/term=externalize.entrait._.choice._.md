# domain.term: entrait

term.chosen   = entrait
term.kind     = verb          # the act is `entraitment`; it produces a `trait`
term.boundary = externalize   # settled 2026-08-31; externalize is a ROOT
term.synonyms.forbidden:
- entrain
- instill
- habituate
- internalize

## .what
to **entrait** is to materialize a default as a 🧬 **trait** — a declared file that a reader falls
to with no decision.

## 🔑 .the family's construction law, stated for the first time

> **`en$X` produces a `$X`.**

| verb | produces |
|---|---|
| `enbrief` | a brief |
| `enskill` | a skill |
| `entool` | a tool |
| `entrait` | a trait |

🟡 **the law was already true and had never been written down.** three members obeyed it before the
fourth was coined, which is why the fourth needed no argument — the pattern was supplied and the
member fell out of it.

⇒ so `entrait` is not an n=1 coinage. it is tested against a declared pattern with three prior
members, and it satisfies the pattern exactly (`rule.require.enumerate-before-you-name`).

## .what it retires, and why it is no rung

every other `en-` verb names a climb on the entoolment ladder — work moved OUT of a brain into
an artifact. `entrait` moves in the other direction:

| the act | where the work goes | what it retires |
|---|---|---|
| `enbrief` · `enskill` · `entool` | OUT — into an artifact a reader invokes | the derivation, the sequence, the execution |
| **`entrait`** | **IN — below decision** | the **recall** |

⇒ **a rule fires when you notice; a trait fires when you do not.** so entraitment is orthogonal to
the ladder rather than a fifth rung on it, and `catalog.of=externalization` lists it as such.

## .the cue that fires it

> **a rule you must recall has already lost the round.**

reach for `entrait` when the cue arrives **before** the artifact exists — a container chosen before
the first word, a form the author must already be in. a rule fetched afterward asks for a rewrite of
prose that should never have been written.
