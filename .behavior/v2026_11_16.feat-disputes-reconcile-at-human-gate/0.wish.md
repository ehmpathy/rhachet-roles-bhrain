# feat(driver): disputes reconcile with a human at a human gate, never halt without one; six invariants enbriefed + enforced (supersedes #744 #745 #746)

## .supersedes

this issue supersedes #744, #745, and #746. build from this one.

- **#744**: its shape stands and is restated below, with the gate scope made explicit
- **#746**: its driver rules are folded in below (invariant 4)
- **#745**: withdrawn. a free re-read of an exhausted lane costs money and has no verdict to give that changes passage. invariant 6 forbids it

## .what

a reviewer concern the driver defers is settled by the drive, never by the driver's memory. a disputed concern is reconciled with a human **at a human review gate**, and only there. a stone with no human gate never halts on a dispute, so the drive keeps its autonomy and its cost floor.

## .the invariants: each one enbriefed and enforced

each row ships as a `define.invariant.*` brief in the driver role, plus exactly one mechanical enforcer, with a clamp that goes red when the enforcer is removed.

| # | invariant | enforcer |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **a dispute never halts a stone that has no human gate.** the driver drives on; autonomy and cost stay as they are | the dispute path in `route.stone.set`: it records the dispute and never halts |
| 2 | **at a human review gate, every dispute under 90% confidence is reconciled with the human.** the approval halt lists each one (case, concerns held, confidence, the driver's lean), and `--as approved` is refused until the human rules on each: uphold, or concede and send the driver to fix | the approval halt reads the fulcrum frontmatter (`owed: human`, `verdict: null`) |
| 3 | **a dispute at 90% or more forces a reflection, never a halt.** the driver names the rule or rubric line that misled the reviewer and dispatches its fix upstream, so reviewers stop the misfire | `--as disputed --confidence >= 0.90` is refused without `--reflect <path>`, which names the upstream task |
| 4 | **deferrals accrue where the human reads.** a disputed or held concern is a named line in the gate's halt, never hidden in a list the gate counts as answered. driver brief pair: `rule.require.deferrals-accrue-in-the-blocker-list` + `rule.forbid.deferrals-hidden-from-the-human` | invariant 2's halt (the line exists because the halt derives it from frontmatter) |
| 5 | **the fulcrum file is the one home of a deferral.** its deterministic attributes live in frontmatter (or a json sidecar); the dispute record only links its fulcrum; the summary row is derived, never typed twice | an onStop linter: every `$route/.fulcrums/inventory.of=fulcrums.case=*.md` carries valid frontmatter, or the stop holds (exit 2) and names the file and the absent field |
| 6 | **a dispute earns no budget and no extra review round.** only an `urgent` concession earns budget (`define.invariant.review.peer.budget.urgent-earns-budget`, unchanged); a dispute is settled by a human at the gate or by a reflection, never by another paid read | the budget meter: a dispute adds no budget and triggers no re-run |

## .the frontmatter

```yaml
---
case: F44
rework: clean | dirty
confidence: 0.65
status: open | held | ruled | superseded
owed: human | reflect | none        # derived at write: < 0.90 → human, ≥ 0.90 → reflect
holds: [rules-lane/i003/blocker.2]  # the reviewer concerns it holds, by lane/round/ordinal
reflect: <org>/<repo>#NNN           # when owed: reflect
verdict: null | { by: human, at: <date>, ruled: ... }
---
```

`owed: human` matters only at a human gate. on a stone with no human gate it is carried forward to the next gate on the route, and never halts the stone it was raised on.

## .the incident (a private downstream route, a blueprint stone)

a rules lane raised a class of name defects. the driver disputed them onto one held fulcrum (a ~400-row rename table, rows at 55–90% confidence), and the stone passed `reviewed?`. at the approval gate the human read the blueprint and found three name defects by hand; two sat in that held table. the halt had shown no deferral, so the human approved what he was shown and found the rest himself.

## .the clamps

- a stone with no human gate and a dispute at 70%: the stone passes, no halt; with invariant 1 broken (a halt added), the clamp goes red
- a human gate with a dispute at 70%: the halt names it and approval is refused until it is ruled; with the fix reverted, the halt is silent and approval goes through
- a dispute at 95% with no `--reflect`: refused; with the fix reverted, accepted
- a fulcrum file with no frontmatter: the onStop hook holds; with the linter removed, the stop goes through
- a dispute on an exhausted lane: no budget added, no re-run
