# 1.vision.experience.dimensions

the experience space of a **deferred reviewer concern**: from the moment a driver disputes it, to
the moment a human or a reflection settles it.

## .the dimensions

| # | dimension | values | why it is its own axis |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | **actor** | `driver` · `human` · `system` (the dispute-time check, the onStop linter, the budget meter) | who meets the experience. the system acts with no human present |
| D2 | **gate** — does the stone hold a human review gate? | `none` · `human` | invariant 1 vs 2: the same dispute halts or drives on by this axis alone |
| D3 | **confidence band** of the dispute | `low` (< 0.90) · `high` (≥ 0.90) | the confidence alone. it does NOT by itself fix `owed` — `owed` derives from D3 × D8 (below) |
| D4 | **verdict state** of the fulcrum | `unruled` (`verdict: null`) · `upheld` · `conceded` | what the human has said. non-null only via a human verdict |
| D5 | **record state** of the fulcrum | `valid` · `absent / invalid` frontmatter · `summary hand-typed` (diverges from frontmatter) · `dispute record restates` the fulcrum | invariant 5's four ways the one home can be honored or broken. independent of the dispute's content |
| D6 | **lane state** of the disputed reviewer | `live` (budget left) · `exhausted` | invariant 6: a dispute on an exhausted lane must not buy a re-read |

## .the hidden dimensions the walk surfaced

| # | dimension | values | how it surfaced |
|---|---|---|---|
| D7 | **carry** — is the human gate on the stone that raised the dispute, or a later one? | `same` · `carried` | `gate=none × low` cannot be ruled where it was raised, yet invariant 2 says every `< 0.90` dispute is reconciled with a human. both hold only if the debt **travels** to the next gate. the wish names it in one line under `.the frontmatter` |
| D8 | **ground** of the dispute | `wrong` (the reviewer misread) · `dirty` (the reviewer is right; the fix costs too much) | the `high × invariant 3` cell asks "which rule misled the reviewer?" — for a `dirty` dispute the answer is none. the two grounds come from `term=route.guard.review.absorption.dispute`; the wish's band rule silently assumes `wrong` |
| D9 | **reflect** — was `--reflect <owner/repo>#N` given? | `provided` · `absent` | without it, the accepted and the refused high-band dispute sat on one coordinate with two verdicts. the flag is an input, so it is an axis |

## .the dimensions execution surfaced

| # | dimension | values | how it surfaced |
|---|---|---|---|
| D10 | **proof** of a verdict | `proven` · `reopened by an edit` · `reopened by a later dispute` · `forged` · `ledger unreadable` | D4 holds what the human ruled; review rounds i004–i007 showed the gate must also read whether that verdict still speaks for the entry it names. a verdict binds to the content (`seen`) and the disputes (`held`) the human saw |
| D11 | **approval order** against a verdict | `before` · `after` | a human may approve, then a later dispute is conceded. the earlier approval never saw the repair, so passage reads the order (`after`), i007 |

these cross only the `verdict ≠ unruled` cells of A, walked as sub-product E in `case=_.md`.

`owed` is derived, never an axis: `owed = reflect` iff `ground = wrong ∧ band = high`; else `owed = human`.

## .the orthogonality check

- D2 × D3: a 70% dispute and a 95% dispute both occur on stones with and without a gate
- D3 × D8: a sure dispute and an unsure one both occur on either ground
- D4 × D2: a verdict lands only at a human gate, so `gate=none × upheld|conceded` is **impossible** by
  nature. that void row is what surfaced D7
- D5 is independent of the dispute's path: a broken record can hold any dispute, at any gate
- D6 is independent of D3/D8: exhaustion is a budget fact; band and ground are stance facts

## .the walked product

the full product D1–D9 is 3·2·2·3·4·2·2·2·2 = 2,304 cells, almost all of them repeats. the walk
instead takes four **sub-products**, each walked in full, and crosses an axis into a sub-product
exactly where an enforcer reads it:

| sub-product | axes | cells | why these axes, and not the others |
|---|---|---|---|
| A — the path | D2 × `owed` × D7 × D4 | 24 | the halt and the approval refusal read gate, `owed`, carry, and verdict — never band or ground directly. so A is keyed on the derived `owed` |
| B — the ground | D8 × D3 × D9 | 8 | the dispute-time check reads ground, band, and the flag, and derives `owed`. B's `.B → A` table sends every accepted tuple to the A rows it walks, so ground × gate and reflect × gate are each verdicted through the composition |
| C — the record | D5 × D2 | 8 | the record is read at write (no gate), at stop (no gate), and at the halt (gate). band and verdict do not change whether a record is readable |
| D — the budget | D6 × D2 × D1 | 12 | the meter reads the lane and the actor (all three); crossed with the gate because invariant 6 must hold at a gate too. band and ground change no budget fact |

52 cells, plus four attempts that span every cell (R11–R14). every tuple of each sub-product is a
row, verdicted: `1.vision.experience.case=_.md`.

and a corollary the walk forces: **a route with no human gate at all**. a low-band dispute there is
carried to a gate that never comes (R14, yield Q1).
