## .settled

**1. the pattern.** four librarian briefs, with the path contract specified verbatim by the wisher
(hence a `forbid` rule as peer of the `require` rule). the two named benefits — **accrual** and
**dereference lookup** — became the article's spine.

**2. 🔴 the driver's catalog line was WRONG, and this retracted it.**

the driver had claimed the line was **completeness**: a catalog indexes what was found; an
inventory claims a census. the wisher's line is **what each entry carries**:

| | enumerates | each entry carries |
|---|---|---|
| **inventory** | every cell | **a reference only** |
| **catalog** | every member | a reference **+ a preview** |

**why the driver's line failed, on two counts:**

1. **completeness is common to both.** the periodic table and *Systema Naturae* — the librarian's
   own catalog exemplars, in a brief the driver had already read — are exhaustive by intent. a
   distinction was built on a property both archetypes share, from evidence already in hand
2. **it was the wrong KIND of line.** completeness is a claim about the world; per-entry content
   is a property of the document. **a reviewer verifies the second with a read and can never fully
   verify the first.** a term whose test cannot be run is a term that will drift

**and the correct line explains what the driver's could not:** it maps onto the say/ref boot
tiers. an inventory is ref-only, so it scales without bound at fixed cost; a catalog pays per
member for previews and buys a dereference saved. that is why the librarian's own `[catalog]` sits
at **say** while its per-archetype articles sit at **ref**.

**3. `inventory` pairs with `itemize`** — and that exposed that `itemize` had been declared here
for months (`rule.require.domain-term-itemization`) with **no noun for what it produces**.
`domain.terms/` was an inventory all along; the org had no word for it.

## .the lessons kept

- **a verb declared without its noun is a half-captured concept.** the act was governed; the
  artifact was unnamed, so nobody could state what a good one looked like
- **the driver reached for a plausible internal distinction rather than the observable one already
  present in the extant briefs** — the same habit every self-review round of this behavior found
