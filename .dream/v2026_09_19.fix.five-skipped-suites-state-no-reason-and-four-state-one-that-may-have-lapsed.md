### 🔴 the two halves fail in opposite directions

**the five bare ones are unreadable.** a `describe.skip` with no note is indistinguishable from an
abandoned test, a quota deferral, and a test its author knew was wrong. ⇒ a reader cannot tell
whether to restore it, delete it, or leave it, so the safe act is always *leave it* — which is how a
bare skip becomes permanent.

🟡 **their neighbours make the ambiguity worse, never better.** `stepDiverge` and `stepCatalogize`
sit two directories apart, are the same shape of brain-driven suite, and one explains itself while
the other does not. **a reader will assume the reason carries across, and that assumption is
unverifiable** — it may be right, and there is no way to check.

**the four documented ones may have LAPSED.** each names a condition — *"when quota restored"* — and
a condition is a trigger that someone must notice has fired. 🔴 **evidence it may have:**

- the brain provider account was suspended on this grove at `i004` (a `412`, `PRECONDITION_FAILED`)
  and is recorded **settled as of 2026-09-18**
- this stone's acceptance sweep ran **131 suites green**, and several make live brain calls
- ⇒ the quota those notes wait on appears to be back, and **no mechanism exists to tell them so**

## .the shape of the fix

**two independent halves; do not bundle them.**

**half 1 — the five bare skips, cheap and safe.** give each a `.note` in the shape its neighbours
already use: the reason, and the condition that restores it. 🔴 **do not guess the reason** — check
`git log` / `git blame` for the commit that added the skip and take the reason from there. if none
exists, say so explicitly: *"skipped <date>, reason not on record"* is worth more than silence,
because it converts an unknown into a known-unknown a reader can act on.

**half 2 — the four conditional skips, and this one needs a measurement first.** run them manually
against the current provider account before any un-skip lands:

```sh
rhx git.repo.test --what integration --mode apply --scope 'path://stepCatalogize'
```

⚠️ **the named scope is the whole selector, and it must stay that way.** an earlier form of this
line carried `--thorough` beside it; `rule.forbid.thorough-test-runs` forbids a brief, readme,
skill, or comment that recommends the flag at all — *"the recommendation is the mechanism by which
the flag survives."* ⇒ a `.dream/` proposal is exactly the guidance a later traveler reads and runs,
so the flag is struck here rather than in their hands. the `path://` scope already selects the
suite, and it is the tightest loop the rule's own table reaches for.

- they pass and the cost is acceptable → un-skip, and the corpus shrinks by four
- they pass and the cost is not → keep the skip and **replace the condition with a decision**:
  *"deliberately manual; cost N per run"* is a settled state, where *"until quota restored"* is a
  promise nobody watches
- they fail → the skip was load-carrying, and that fact is worth the run on its own

## .why it is not done in this round

| **clean?** | 🔴 **no** — 5 files in `domain.roles/thinker/**`, a tree this diff does not touch at any point | 🔴 **no** — the same tree, plus a ci budget decision that is not this behavior's to make |

⇒ **both fail CLEAN, and half 2 fails SAFE outright.** the deferral is correct, and the rule says a
deferral for dirt owes this record.

not noted"* — and its subject is **this branch's** coverage. this branch's skip delta is **zero**,

would smuggle an 8-file unrelated change into a verification gate, which
`rule.require.review-test-changes` forbids by name.
