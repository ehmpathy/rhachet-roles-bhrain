# rule.forbid.purposeless-passages

> **no passage exists because a form asked for it. a passage exists because a reader needs it.**

the clamp on `rule.require.purpose-first`. that rule names the target; this names the smell.

## .the lead smell — the header a template handed you

a template that lists a dozen questions produces a dozen headers, and **each header pulls prose to
fill it.** the author never chose to pad; the form rewarded a section per question.

| the form asks | what an author writes |
|---|---|
| *the outcome world* · *the day in the life* · *the before/after* · *the aha* | **four sections, one fact** |

⇒ **that is how a 4.5k wish grows 155.8k of yields** — not an author who rambled, but a container
that paid for length.

**this is the only rule in the canon that can shrink an artifact before a word of it is typed**,
because it is the only one that grades a container the author was handed rather than prose the
author wrote.

## .the repair — write `none`, and move on

a template section with no purpose is not shortened. **it is answered with `none` and dropped.**

> a section padded to look thorough is noise with a header on it.

## .the shapes

| shape | what it is | the repair |
|---|---|---|
| the template header | a section that exists because the prompt named it | write `none`. drop it |
| the lap | reaches its purpose the long way | cut to the line that carries it |
| the displaced passage | true, tight, well-made — and aimed at a **different** purpose | **move it** to the passage whose purpose it serves |
| **the restated `.what`** | a header, then a sentence that says the header again | drop one |
| the courtesy section | *"## .risks — none identified"* left in for symmetry | drop it |
| the parallel-structure fill | three peers get a section, so the fourth gets one too | the fourth may have naught to say |

## .the displaced passage is the one that survives every other rule

it is true. it is tight. it is bulletized. **no rule that grades a property of the words fires on
it** — only a stated purpose reveals that it is aimed elsewhere.

⇒ and its repair is a **move**, never a cut. that is what parts this rule from
`require.reflexive-condensation`, whose repair is always a cut.

## .the test

> **name this passage's purpose as the READER'S decision. now: does the passage serve it?**

- yes, directly → keep it
- yes, the long way → cut to the line that carries it
- it serves a **different** purpose, and serves it well → **move it there**
- you cannot name one → **cut it**

*"to explain the reviewer"* is a **subject**, and a subject grades naught. *"so an adopter can decide
whether to wire it"* is a **purpose**, and it grades every line.

| when… | then… |
|---|---|
| a **template** or a stage prompt named this section | the strongest cue. state its purpose, or write `none` |
| you would write *"for completeness"* or *"worth a mention"* | the passage just told you it has no purpose |
| a section is short and you feel it looks thin | thin is correct where the answer is thin. do not pad a header |
| a passage is true, tight, and you cannot cut it | check whether it is displaced. move, do not delete |
| you would call a passage *"too long"* | long compared to what? name the purpose first |
| a peer section has three bullets and yours has one | one is the right count where one is the truth |

## 🟡 .the boundary

| a violation | not a violation |
|---|---|
| a section written because the prompt listed it | a section written because a reader must make a decision |
| a passage whose purpose you cannot name | a passage whose purpose demands more words than you like |
| a header, then a restatement of the header | a header, then the claim it introduces |
| `## .risks — none identified`, kept for symmetry | `## .risks` with a risk in it |

**the line that parts them: would a reader ACT differently once they read it?** no → it is
purposeless, however true. yes → it earns its header.

## .the axis

a **structure** rule, so telepath owns it (`rule.require.generic-governs-structure-never-voice`). a
purposeless section is purposeless in every register, so it prescribes no member of that rule's
voice row.

## .enforcement

blocker: a section that exists because a template named it · a passage whose purpose the author
cannot state · a displaced passage left in a home whose purpose it does not serve · a header
followed by a restatement of itself.
nitpick: a passage that laps its purpose where one line would carry it.
false positive: a short section whose answer is genuinely short · a passage whose purpose demands
its length.
