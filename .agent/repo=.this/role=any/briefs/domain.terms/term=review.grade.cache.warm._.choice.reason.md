# domain.term.choice.reason: warm

## .etymology

`warm` and `cold` are the industry's own words for a cache with and without content — a "cold
start", a "warm cache". a reader who has run any service already holds the sense, so the pair
needs no gloss.

rejected:

| rejected | why |
|---|---|
| `hot` | names a cache under heavy load, a different axis. warm/cold is presence; hot/cold is traffic |
| `primed` | names the act that made it so, never the state. a reviewer can be warm with no prime at all — a real review is a touch (`term=prompt.cache.touch`) |
| `seen` | true of the reviewer, never of the cache. a reviewer seen once by a provider that evicts can be cold again |

## .the evidence — why a grade, not a bare rate

`0.0% cached` states two opposite things as one figure. a reviewer's first run cannot hit — there
was no cache — so its zero is a fact about the world. a reviewer's sixth run at zero is a defect.
the grade is what parts them, and it renders beside the rate so a reader never has to infer it.

## .the boundary

"warm, of WHAT?" — of a bucket's cache opportunity, as graded by the meter. one word answers
it, so the cluster is qualified `review.grade.cache`. the telepath sense answers "of a peer's
retained context", and no head is declared for that yet, so it is left out of this cluster
rather than overloaded into it (`rule.forbid.domain-term-ambiguity`).

## .disputes

none.
