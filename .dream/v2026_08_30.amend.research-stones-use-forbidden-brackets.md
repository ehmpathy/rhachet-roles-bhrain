# 🌙 dream: the research route's brief-name pattern uses forbidden brackets

**caught 2026-08-30, mid `.behavior/v2026_08_12.feat-adopt-seeded-briefs`, while the researcher
role was declared. deferred because the fix is DIRTY — see below.**

## .the cue that fired

`rule.forbid.brackets-in-filenames` (librarian) — read while the researcher's own brief-shape rules
were written, one file away from the stone that violates it.

## .the gap, precisely

`src/domain.operations/research/init/templates/5.1.briefs.curate.blueprint.stone` prescribes the
name pattern every research yield is authored under:

```
{code}XXX.{subtopic}._.[doctype].{variant}.md
```

🟡 **`[doctype]` is exactly the shape the librarian forbids.** `[` and `]` are glob metacharacters,
so `[article]` reads as *"any one character from `a,r,t,i,c,l,e`"* — the name a reader sees and the
name a tool matches are different strings. a `Glob` for `**/*.[article].md` returns **no match**
while the files sit on disk.

so **the research route actively prescribes the defect.** every curated brief it produces is born
unaddressable, and the volume is the problem: this stone is the one that generates brief names at
scale.

the extant `[article]`/`[catalog]`/`[demo]`/`[lesson]` files in `librarian/briefs/knowledge/` are
the accumulated output of exactly this pattern.

## .the shape of the fix

replace the bracket marker with the coordinate form the librarian rule prescribes:

```
{code}XXX.{subtopic}._.kind={doctype}.{variant}.md
```

⇒ `cons101.fundamentals._.kind=catalog.md`, `em251.electromagnetic-waves.kind=article.md`

three edit sites in that one stone:

| site | scale |
|---|---|
| the `## instructions` name-pattern line | 1 line |
| the `example format` treestruct | **~90 lines**, every one with `[doctype]` |
| the `## appendix: brief name pattern` — its table, tiers, doctypes, and examples | ~40 lines |

and one adjacent line: `5.2.briefs.curate.execute.stone:24` points at `librarian/briefs/` as the
pattern to match — which is the tree full of bracketed names, so it would still demonstrate the old
form after the blueprint is fixed.

## .why it is not done in this round

**SAFE: yes. CLEAN: no.** the deferral is a dirt call, so it carries a fulcrum
(`rule.always.fix-forward-under-scouts-honor`):

- the change is ~130 lines across two stones, in a namespace this round did not otherwise open —
  it would swamp the diff for its reviewer
- it forces a second decision the round has no mandate for: **what happens to the ~40 extant
  bracketed briefs in `librarian/briefs/knowledge/`?** the librarian rule says *leave them until
  disturbed*, but a stone that prescribes one form beside a tree that demonstrates another is a
  contradiction a reader will hit immediately
- and the librarian rule warns that the repair is **file-first and by hand** — a `sedreplace`
  sweep cannot tell a *citation* of a bracketed name from an *instance* of one, and this stone is
  ~90 lines of citations

⇒ so a hasty fix here plausibly makes it worse than it is.

## .the fulcrum this deferral raises
