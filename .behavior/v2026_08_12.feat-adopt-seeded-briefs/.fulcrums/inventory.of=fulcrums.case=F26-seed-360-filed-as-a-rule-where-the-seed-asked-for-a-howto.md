## .the seed's own words, which this call departs from

> *"crystallize this into a bhrain brief (**a howto or lesson**) so any traveler knows to: 1. pave
> a new route offroad… 2. record the intent to formalize… 3. extract a skill-stamped template only
> once 2+ real cases confirm the shape…"*

**three of four are obey-shaped**, which is why the archetype moved.

the driver role holds 12 `rule.always.*` files and, before this one, **zero** `rule.prefer.*`. so

| the option | the cost |

not settled.
