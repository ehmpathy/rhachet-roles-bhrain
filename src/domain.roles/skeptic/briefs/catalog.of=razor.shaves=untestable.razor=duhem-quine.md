# razor: duhem-quine

shaves: a claim that cannot be tested · settled by: rivals (each member of the bundle a suspect)

## .the question

> **this observation contradicts the claim: which part of the bundle failed, the claim or an assumption under the test?**

a test never meets a claim alone. it meets the claim plus the setup, the tool, and the premises beneath both. `razor=popper` says refute; this asks what the refutation actually hit.

## .the source

- Duhem, 1906: when experience conflicts, "what is disconfirmed is necessarily ambiguous"
- Quine, `guess:` 1951
- read at https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/duhem/

## .fails when

- it is abused to save a pet claim forever: blame the setup each time. Duhem claims only ambiguity, never that any claim survives
- name each suspect, and the read that would clear it

## .run by

`rule.always.weigh-a-read-by-what-it-rules-out`
