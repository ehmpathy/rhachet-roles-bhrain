# S2 — `repaid` in tokens and dollars, not one or the other

## .said

> why not both? - F4: repaid is reported in tokens, not dollars

## .settled

the report shows both units side by side. tokens stay the measured figure; dollars are an estimate
beside them — each call's usage times its model's rates, read and write priced apart. a token
count alone overstates what a re-read costs, since a cache read is priced well below a raw input.
