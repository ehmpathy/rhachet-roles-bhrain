- **fix**: the org cycles are eliminated: `pnpm.overrides` pins every transitive `helpful-errors@<1.7` to 1.7.5 and `domain-objects@<0.34` to 0.34.0 (a bump of the direct deps alone cleared none). `.dpdmrc.yaml` excludes the three dev packages, and holds two third-party prod packages with no clean release, each with its replacement: `bottleneck` (reached via wrapper-fns + @ehmpathy/as-command; dispatched to both) and `readable-stream` (a dream to replace archiver). types, unit, and integration pass under the overrides. fulcrum F4

### D2 — suites the repo keeps as records fail to load
- **what**: three sets fail with `Could not locate module` or import a retired api:

  - `src/domain.roles/thinker/.scratch/**/*.integration.test.ts`
  - `src/domain.roles/thinker/skills/**/step*.integration.test.ts`
- **how**: main's configs cut `.behavior/`, `.scratch/`, and the thinker stitch suites; the practice configs cut only `.agent/`

- **fix**: rekeyed the patch to `patches/test-fns@1.17.0.patch` (the 1.17.0 dist matches every hunk). the installed dist holds `runAttemptWithinBudget` at all 4 sites; the clamp test went red before, green after

### D5 — `stepRouteDrive` integration snapshots mismatch on temp dir paths
- **what**: 9 mask sites in `stepRouteDrive.simple.integration.test.ts` (6) and `stepRouteDrive.integration.test.ts` (3) leave the genTempDir path unmasked
