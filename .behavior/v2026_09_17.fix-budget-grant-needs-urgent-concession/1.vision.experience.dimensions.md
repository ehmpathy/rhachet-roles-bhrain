# 1.vision.experience.dimensions

the experience space of the **budget grant gate** — the refusal that meets
`rhx route.guard.budget --for review --add N`. factored into orthogonal axes and walked as a product.
the cells are verdicted in `1.vision.experience.case=_.md`.

🔴 **the subject is the GRANT ATTEMPT, never the budget hit.** the predecessor behavior
(`v2026_09_08.feat-dispute-or-concede-review-budget`) walked the *hit* and its halts. this walks the
one move that halt advises, and asks whether the mover is permitted to make it.

## .the axes

### A — the actor who attempts the grant (2)

| value | who | why it is an axis |
|---|---|---|
| `driver` | the bot at the keyboard | the party the gate binds (`0.wish.md` req 7: *"this binds the bot"*) |
| `human` | a person, with the route in view | the carve-out. never gated, at any B or C |

⚠️ **actor and move do NOT vary independently elsewhere in this domain** — the predecessor merged
them for that reason (`--as overruled` is a human act by construction). here they **do**: one command,
`route.guard.budget --add N`, is issued today by either party with identical syntax and identical
effect. ⇒ **the actor is the axis precisely because the move does not name it**, and that is the gap
this behavior closes.

### B — the live concession state of the stone's latest round (4)

the predicate the gate reads. **stone-scoped**, computed over the live absorptions —
`getLiveReviewAbsorptions` keyed to each slug's latest `.given`
(`getStoneLiveUrgentConcessionSlugs.ts:28`).

🔴 **the four values are a PRECEDENCE LADDER, read top-down — never four independent tests.**

| value | what stands, live, at the attempt |
|---|---|
| 🔴 `urgent` | ≥1 live `conceded` stance graded `urgent` — the one value that lifts the refusal |
| `better` | no live `urgent`, **and** ≥1 live `conceded` stance |
| `disputed` | no live concession at all, **and** ≥1 live dispute |
| `none` | no live absorption at all — the ordinary churn round |

🔴 **the ladder form is the `r1` peer review's correction, and the first draft was not a partition.**
it read each value as *"every live stance is of this kind"* — so a stone whose latest round carried
**one dispute and one `better` concede** matched no value at all. that state is lawful and reachable
(a driver disputes `blocker.1` and concedes `nitpick.2` `better` on one round), and the walked product
had no coordinate to give it a verdict.

⇒ under the ladder it lands on `better`, and the verdict is unchanged — **refused**, since no live
`urgent` stands. so the gap hid no critipath; what it broke was the axis's claim to be a closed set,
which is what `rule.require.dimensional-decomposition` grades. **the first row that fits wins, and
every stone state fits exactly one.**

🟡 **the precedence is not arbitrary — it is the predicate's own shape.**
`getStoneLiveUrgentConcessionSlugs` filters for `status === 'conceded' && severity === 'urgent'` and
reads the result as a boolean. ⇒ **one live urgent outranks any quantity of every other kind**, which
is exactly what the top row says.

✅ **every value here is already computable today.** `passage.jsonl` carries
`{ status, reviewer, about, given, severity }` per `PassageReport`, `--severity` is **required** on
every concede (`setStoneAsConcernAbsorbed.ts:103-113`), and the `urgent` filter already ships. ⇒ the
gate reads a **fact on disk**, never a judgment a clone asserts — `0.wish.md` req 3, satisfied by
extant machinery.

🔴 **`disputed` is a value, not a fold into `none`.** a dispute sheds its concern from the tally and
earns budget by no severity (`define.invariant…urgent-earns-budget` §scope). so it looks like
evidence of engagement and is **not** evidence a round converges — the exact confusion a merged value
would hide.

### C — the target lane's meter state (4)

**lane-scoped**, at the moment of the attempt.

| value | what |
|---|---|
| `live` | budget remains; the lane can run again |
| `exhausted` | `rounds >= budget`; the lane is skipped (`isReviewPeerSkippedForBudget.ts:57`) |
| `malfunctioned` | the lane broke or returned an unreadable verdict — scored 1 blocker, terminal-for-unlock |
| `forgiven` | a human overruled its level (`getStoneGuardOverruledLevels`) |

🔴 **`forgiven` is NOT a meter state, and this axis is a deliberate PROJECTION of two — `r2` peer
review caught it and the concern is exactly right.** the first three rows read one quantity (does the
lane have rounds?); the fourth reads a **level overrule**, which sits **on top of** the meter rather
than replaces it. a forgiven lane still has a meter state underneath.

⇒ the honest declaration is two axes, projected to one:

| the true axis | positions | scope |
|---|---|---|
| **C₁ — the meter** | `live` \| `exhausted` \| `malfunctioned` | the lane |
| **C₂ — the overrule** | `not-overruled` \| `forgiven` | 🔴 the **LEVEL**, never the lane |

**so `C = C₁ × C₂` is 6 positions, and the grid walks 4.** the two dropped are
`forgiven × {live, malfunctioned}` — a forgiven lane whose meter is live or broken.

🔴 **the projection is safe, and the argument is one line: `C₂ = forgiven` DOMINATES.** once a human
has overruled the level, no grant is sought at all — the level owes no further round, whatever its
meter reads. ⇒ **all 6 `forgiven` cells verdict identically to the 2 walked**, so the projection hides
no critipath and drops no verdict.

⚠️ **and the check that proves it is checkable rather than asserted:** the gate never reads C at all.
it reads `A` and `B` alone. so **every C position — all 6, projected or not — is a no-op for the
verdict**, and C earns its place in the grid for one reason: it is where a reader *expects* the
behavior to differ, and the walk shows it does not.

🟡 **the cost of the projection, stated rather than glossed:** a criteria author who reads C as a
partition will clamp 4 states and believe they covered the lane's whole state space. they covered 4
of 6, and the 2 they missed are the ones a human's overrule creates. ⇒ **the criteria stone owes a
clamp on `forgiven × live`** — cheap, and it pins the dominance claim above rather than trusts it.

🔴 **`live` is in the grid deliberately.** a gate that fires only at exhaustion leaves the **preemptive
pad** open — a driver who tops up at round 1 never meets a refusal, and the bound is void by
anticipation. ⇒ the `live` column is where the loophole would live, so it must be walked (`case=5`).

## .orthogonality

| pair | independent? | why |
|---|---|---|
| A × B | ✅ | either actor may attempt at any concession state. the actor does not author the ledger |
| A × C | ✅ | either actor may attempt at any meter state |
| B × C | ✅ **by scope** | 🔴 **B is STONE-scoped and C is LANE-scoped.** a stone may carry a live urgent concession from l1 while the l2 lane sits `malfunctioned`, `live`, or `forgiven` |
| 🔴 C₁ × C₂ | 🟡 **independent, and PROJECTED** | the two are orthogonal — a forgiven lane still has a meter state — and the grid walks 4 of their 6 cells. safe because `C₂ = forgiven` dominates the verdict; declared in full at § C above |

🔴 **the B × C scope split is what earns the ✅, and it deserves a plain statement because it looks
wrong.** a first read says *"you cannot concede against a malfunctioned given"* — true, and it is a
claim about **one lane** (`F030`: an unreadable given owes a re-run, never a stance). B asks a
different question: *does any lane on this stone carry a live urgent concession?* ⇒ the two never
constrain each other, and no cell is impossible.

⚠️ **no cell in this grid is IMPOSSIBLE. every cell is attemptable.** ⇒ **nature bars none**, so the
verdicts are `permitted` and `refused` — and a refusal is the engine that answers *no* to a move
already made, which is the whole behavior. a refused cell **confirms** orthogonality rather than
breaks it.

🔴 **`refused` IS the rule's `forbidden` — nurture-barred, never nature-barred.** the three invariants
those cells carry are recorded in `case=_.md` § *the invariants those 12 forbidden cells carry*, per
`rule.require.dimensional-decomposition`'s mandate that a forbidden cell ship a checkable rule.

## .the product

## .what the walk surfaced

### 🔴 1. this behavior falsifies the predecessor's sharpest ownership claim

`v2026_09_08`'s own dimensions file, §4, states it as a proven property of its walk:

> ### 4. the whole `top-up` slab is permitted — all 15 cells
> > **the budget lever is never gated by the stance.**
> a driver may top up at any debt state and any budget state, before or after a stance, exactly as it
> may today. **acceptance #5 is a property of the grid rather than of a new mechanism** — and the walk
> is what proves it, because a single forbidden cell in that slab would have falsified it.

⇒ **this behavior supplies that cell — twelve of them.** `driver × {none, disputed, better} × *` is
refused across the board. the predecessor did not overlook the question; it **walked it and answered
permitted**, on a wish that said so in as many words. ⇒ this is a **reversal on record**, and the
yield owes it an argument rather than a silent overwrite.

🟡 **the reversal is narrower than it reads.** the predecessor's claim was *"the stance does not gate
the budget"* — and it still does not. what gates the budget here is the **actor** (axis A), a
coordinate the predecessor's grid did not carry. ⇒ its 15 cells are all `driver` cells, so the claim
was true of the grid it walked and false of the grid it did not.

### 🔴 2. the `better` concession loses the round it was designed to buy

the predecessor demoed `concede × answered × exhausted` as a **critipath**, `case=6`, titled *"the
concede that buys a round"*, with this aha:

> *the top-up was never a favour asked of a human. it is the price of a round the driver decided it
> needs — and now the ledger says which rounds those were.*

⇒ under this gate, **that exact sequence is refused.** a `better` concession is the maintenance floor,
and the floor never earns budget — so `concede → fix → budget → re-arrive` loses its third step, and
the lane that raised the blocker has no round left to **confirm** the fix.

⚠️ **and the strand is permanent, never deferred.** budget is cumulative rounds, never per-generation:
`isReviewPeerSkippedForBudget` reads `rounds >= budget`, so an exhausted lane stays exhausted however
often the driver edits and re-arrives — **the edit restores no round, and the skipped lane never
speaks again.** ⇒ the fix ships **unconfirmed by the lane that asked for it**, forever.

🟡 **the skip is what makes the strand permanent, and the SAME skip is what holds a stance live at
this cell** — an exhausted lane mints no new given, so naught it could say voids an urgent concession
declared against it (`F11`). one mechanism, two consequences, opposite in sign.

✅ **it is coherent, and it is the declared trade.** a `better` concession is already **shed from the
tally** (`getStoneConcededBetterConcernCounts`), so the stone passes on its own arithmetic — *good
enough inside the budget*. ⇒ the loss is a **confirmation**, never a passage.

🔴 **but it is a real cost, it is not what the predecessor promised, and it owes a fulcrum** — `F04`.
demoed at `case=3`.

### 🔴 3. the gate INVERTS which case the bot may self-serve

today, at the budget hit, the two concession severities land opposite to where this wish puts them:

| the live concession | today's halt | under this gate |
|---|---|---|
| `better` | 🔴 *"what to do — **yours to run, no human needed**"* → `--add N` (snap `:107-109`) | **refused** |
| `urgent` | *"spend your own lever first, **then ask a human**"* + the warn → the judge says *"please ask your human to"* + the budget cmd (`route.ts:1530-1567`) | 🔴 **permitted, to the bot** |

⇒ **the two swap.** the case today's engine hands the driver is the case this gate refuses; the case
today's engine escalates is the case this gate lets the bot serve itself.

🟡 **both halves are internally consistent, and they answer different questions.** today's split asks
*"does this hit need a HUMAN'S GLANCE?"* — and `better` does not. this gate asks *"is one more round
EVIDENCE-BACKED?"* — and only a nameable shipped harm is. ⇒ the axes are `attention` and
`convergence`, and a design may hold both: **the bot buys the round, and the human is still warned
this PR** (`define.invariant…urgent-earns-budget` §invariant line 22 says *warn*, never *grant*).

⚠️ **that reconciliation is a best guess and it is the wish's reserved fulcrum** — `F03`.

### 4. every extant halt advertises the refused command

the change surface is wider than the gate. **every budget halt in the repo prints `--add N` as the
next move**, and three of the four print it as the driver's own:

| halt | the line it prints | fate under the gate |
|---|---|---|
| ordinary exhaustion (snap `:23-25`) | *"spend your own lever first … increase budget — yours to spend"* | 🔴 advertises a refusal |
| `better` concession (snap `:107-109`) | *"yours to run, no human needed"* | 🔴 advertises a refusal |
| `urgent` concession (snap `:136-138`) | *"spend your own lever first, then ask a human"* | ✅ still true, and now the bot may |
| malfunction + exhaustion (snap `:195-197`) | *"spend your own lever first"* | 🔴 advertises a refusal |

⇒ **a gate that ships without the halts is a pit of failure**: the engine tells the driver to run a
command the engine then refuses (`rule.forbid.surprises`, `rule.require.errors-name-the-fix`). the
halts are **in scope**, never downstream polish.

### 🔴 5. a latent axis the grid does not carry — `grant-scope`

`--add N` takes `--peer <slug>`, `--level N`, or neither (the bare form, which lands on the latest
level alone). three scopes, one gate.

⇒ **not a coordinate.** the gate's verdict is identical at all three — it asks *may you grant?*, never
*where?* — so a fold would triple the grid to 96 and duplicate every verdict. ⚠️ **but it is a real
question the gate must answer explicitly**: a refusal that fires on the bare form and not on `--peer`
is a bypass one flag wide. recorded as `F09`, and the invariant is *the gate precedes the **LANE**
scope parse*.

🟡 **that invariant read *"the scope parse"*, unbounded, until `r2`** — and `--stone` is a scope too,
looked up at `:2264`, after which the gate must sit. ⇒ §7 below, and `F09` now carries the boundary.

### 6. a second latent axis — `grant-size`

`--add 1` versus `--add 50`. **not a coordinate, and no invariant either** — the wish bounds
*whether*, never *how many*. ⚠️ recorded as an unmechanized residual: a permitted urgent grant of
`--add 99` is a bound one integer wide. the extant `ROUNDS_OFFERED_ON_TOPUP` const is the natural
place a cap would live, and the auto-grant dream already argues for *"one round, not a blank check"*.

### 🔴 7. a FOURTH AXIS the walk missed entirely — `--stone` scope

found at `r2` by `howto.experience.enumerate` move 3, which names a **seam** as one of three places a
sharp edge hides. **all three axes above treat *"the stone"* as a singleton. it is not.**

```ts
// getTargetGuardPathsForStone — route.ts:1948-1956
input.guardFiles.filter((f) => path.basename(f).startsWith(input.stoneName))
```

| the part | its contract |
|---|---|
| `getStoneLiveUrgentConcessionSlugs.ts:19-22` — the **domain op** | `stone: string` — exactly ONE |
| the **cli** | a `startsWith` **prefix**, which names N |

⇒ `--stone 5` names `5.1.execution`, `5.2.verification`, `5.3.playtest` alike. **the gate reads one
stone's ledger and the write touches N stones' guards.**

🔴 **this one IS a coordinate**, unlike §5 and §6 — the verdict genuinely differs across its two
positions, which is the test an axis must pass:

| `D` | the verdict |
|---|---|
| `exact` | the 32 cells above, unchanged |
| 🔴 `prefix-multi` | **every permitted cell becomes open**, and the refused cells stay refused |

🟡 **and that asymmetry is why it is recorded rather than expanded to 64 cells.** a refused grant is
refused however many stones it names (`case=10` `[t5]`), so the `prefix-multi` slab differs from the
`exact` slab in its **permitted** half alone — 20 cells, one question, one demo.

⚠️ **the lesson outlives the row.** `rule.require.dimensional-decomposition` earns completeness *by
construction* from the walked product — and **an unlisted axis silently voids that guarantee**, with
no signal from inside the walk. the seam locator is a **separate instrument**, and this walk needed
it. recorded as `F12`, demoed at `case=10`.

### 🔴 8. a FIFTH AXIS the walk missed for a different reason — the `lever`

found at `r2` by the requirements guide's own question — *"is the scope too large, too small, or

🔴 **this one is a coordinate too, and its second slab is UNIFORM:**

| `E` | the verdict |
|---|---|

🟡 **and the uniformity is the argument for one demo rather than 32.** where `D`'s second slab varies

⚠️ **the two missed axes fail the walk differently, and that is what makes them a pair worth one

⇒ **§7's lesson generalizes and gets sharper here.** an unlisted axis voids completeness-by-

### 🔴 9. a SIXTH AXIS the walk missed, and the rule names it by name — `concurrency`

found at `r1` **peer** review, by `rule.require.dimensional-decomposition`'s own list of usual
omissions: *"concurrency, actor = system, resource-absent states are the usual omissions."* ⇒ the
walk enumerates **states** and never the **order two movers land in**, which is the identical failure
§7 and §8 name, on a third face.

**the source, read rather than assumed:**

```ts
// processGuardFileBudgets — route.ts:2116-2131
for (const guardPath of input.guardPaths) {
  const content = await fs.readFile(guardPath, 'utf-8');        // :2117  read
  const result = updateGuardPeerBudgets({ content, addAmount, … });
  if (result.modified) await fs.writeFile(guardPath, result.content);   // :2127  write
}
```

⇒ **an unlocked read-modify-write.** no lock, no atomic rename, no compare-and-swap. and the gate
this vision adds sits at `:2264`-ish, further still from the write — so the **predicate** read and the
**budget** write are two separate reads of the world with a window between them.

| `F` | the verdict column |
|---|---|
| `serial` | the 32 cells above, unchanged |
| 🔴 `concurrent` | 🔴 **unchanged too — and what changes is whether a PERMITTED grant LANDS** |

🔴 **the peer review proposed a double-spend and the source refutes that direction.** the concern
read *"two top-ups issued in parallel can both observe B = urgent / quota = 1 and both write — one
granted quota funding two grants."* the write half cannot do that:

- each writer recomputes `budget + N` **from its own read of the file**, so the later `writeFile`
  overwrites the earlier with a total that never saw it
- ⇒ two concurrent `--add 4` yield **+4, never +8**. a **lost update**, and it errs toward *less*
  budget
- 🔴 **no order of two movers can manufacture a grant with no warrant**, because the gate ran for
  each of them independently

⚠️ **so the axis is owed and the hazard is the opposite one: a grant the command REPORTS and does not
make.** two concurrent `--peer A` and `--peer B` grants against one guard file — B's write is built
on a pre-A read, so **A's bump is silently discarded while A's stdout prints `budgetBefore: 18 →
budgetAfter: 22`.** that is a `rule.forbid.failhide` shape on a surface this design newly makes
load-carrying.

🟡 **it is `alter`, never `criti`, and the test is the one `case=11` used.** *what breaks if this
path breaks?* — a lost grant is **over**-refusal, and the wish's outcome is a **bound**. ⇒ the bound
is never loosened by an overlap, so the core usecase survives and the fallback is to re-run the
grant. recorded as `F16`, demoed at `case=12`.

🔴 **and the axis has a second half this design would ADD, which is why it could not be left as a
set-aside.** `F02`'s human `uses` meter is a **decrement** of a shared file — two concurrent human
grants at quota 1 both read 1, both write 0, and both land. that lost update errs toward **more**.
⚠️ it violates no requirement (req 7 is a permission, never a cap), and it is the one place the
concurrent slab is unsafe in the direction that matters. ⇒ **if a council rules `F02` in, the meter
owes an atomic decrement**, and that is a constraint the walk did not carry.
