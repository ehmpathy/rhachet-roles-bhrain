# dream: a term's `.evidence` should CITE, never re-narrate

**kind**: enbrief · **owner**: this repo (`rhachet-roles-bhrain`) · **caught** 2026-08-30

## .the cue that fired

a wick-dense review of two new term clusters found the same defect in both, then traced it to the
template: **`.evidence` restates prose that already sits above it in the same file.**

the numbers in `term=glyph._.choice.reason.md` were the sharpest case — `198` appeared three times
in one file, `68` twice — with the `.evidence` block a third statement of what `.the one-concept
test` and `.the palette rule` had already made.

## .the gap, precisely

`template.domain-term.md` prescribes an `.evidence` section and does not say what distinguishes it
from the body. so an author who has argued a point in prose writes the same point again under
`.evidence`, and both readings are defensible.

⇒ present in **~31 `*.choice.reason.md` files**, so it is a template defect rather than an
authorship one. and it is invisible per-file: each copy reads as a summary, and only a
whole-file read shows the claim stated twice.

## .the shape of the fix

state the split in `template.domain-term.md`:

| section | holds |
|---|---|
| the body | the **argument** — why this word, why not the others |
| `.evidence` | the **measurements and citations** — a number, a date, a path, an invariant. no prose the body already carries |

⇒ the test: **strip `.evidence` and re-read the file. did a claim disappear, or only a repeat?** if
only a repeat, the section held its contract.

## .why it is not done in this round

**SAFE: yes. CLEAN: no.** the template edit alone is two lines and clean — but a template that
declares a rule its 31 extant instances violate is a rule with a 31-file arrears attached, and the
sweep is a separate pass in a namespace this round did not open.

⚠️ and the repair is **file-first and by hand**: only a reader can tell a measurement from a
restatement of one.

## .the fulcrum

> **the `.evidence` slot has no declared contract, so it accretes restatement by default.**
> rework: **clean** — the template edit is additive, and no artifact hardens against it.
