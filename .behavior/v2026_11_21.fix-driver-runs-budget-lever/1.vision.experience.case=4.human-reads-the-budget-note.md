# case=4 — the human reads the budget note

feel: happy · care: critipath

actor: the human who sponsors the route

## .narrative

the human is deep in another repo. a line arrives from its driver: "1.vision bought 2 rounds on `friction`; the draft can be lost on retry." no question in it, no command to paste.

it reads the line in five seconds. the harm sounds right: a lost draft is a defect it wants fixed. so it goes back to its work. there is no decision to make.

an hour later it reviews the pr. the guard diff shows `friction`'s budget went from 3 to 5. the grant names its warrant, `friction · conceded urgent`. the note it read earlier explains the diff, so the diff holds no surprise.

on another day the note reads "bought 2 rounds on `mech-rules`; the name `getData` is unclear". the human sees a taste concern graded urgent. it says so in the pr, and the council has a written claim to read. the bound on the driver's spend is social, and the note is what makes it readable.

## .bdd sketch

```

  then the note names the stone, the rounds, and the harm
  then it asks the human for no action

  then the budget change matches the note
  then the grant names its warrant (`friction · conceded urgent`)
when [t2] a note names a harm the human judges to be taste
  then the human has a written claim to dispute in review
```
