# 5.1.execution.from_vision.yield

execution of `1.vision.yield.md`. the vision's four cache-layout levers already shipped; this
stone delivers the `review._.cost` surface, deletes the stale `review.corpus` estimator, adds a
fifth lever (the cache warmer), and repairs the whole review-cost subsystem through a long peer
review ladder.

## what shipped

### A — the surface: one command over the `breadth` axis

`rhx review._.cost --of run|tree|grove`

- one cli entrypoint (`src/contract/cli/review._.cost.ts`), `--of` required, dispatched by
  breadth position; `--help` documents all three
- `--of tree` and `--of grove` route to the extant meter/grove reports (behavior unchanged)
- `--of run` is new: it reads one run's `metrics.compiled.json` + `metrics.realized.json` and
  renders its per-input census
- `review.meter.ts`/`.sh` and `review.fleet.ts`/`.sh` are deleted, along with their package.json
  exports
- `fleet` is renamed to `grove` across dop names, type names, and the `grove/` dir — `fleet` is a
  forbidden synonym of the declared term `git.grove`, so this was a rule fix, not a taste call
- whether `meter/` stays `meter/` or moves to `cost/breadth=tree/`, and the `…Meter…` dop names
  that still render the `tree` breadth, is left open — see fulcrum `F5`

### B — the deletion

`review.corpus.ts`/`.sh` and `src/domain.operations/review/corpus/**` are deleted: the tool
estimated a prompt the renderer no longer sends. its one useful figure (a 39.7% markup share) is
kept as a dated record in `research.review-cost-and-cache`; its one live defect (`broken globs`) is
re-homed to a dream; the four `chars × {tree,grove}` cells it would have served stay itemized and
unserved in the experience inventory.

### C — terminology

`fleet` → `grove` is swept across help text, stdout headers, research docs, and dreams — a second
pass caught a live `🦉 review meter` stdout header too, the retired command's name, since the first
sweep's pattern was the word `fleet` and the rendered string carried none of it. the few `fleet`
occurrences left are archaeology (fulcrum/seed history, the `.what/.why` headers that argue why
the old name collided) or a genuinely distinct sense (a fleet of servers, the org).

### D — tests

- acceptance snapshots for `--of run|tree|grove` and `--help` (contract + snapshot coverage per
  `rule.require.test-coverage-by-grain`)
- `reviewFleet.acceptance.test.ts` is retired into `reviewCost.acceptance.test.ts`, which clamps
  the refusal of `fleet` as a forbidden synonym
- `grove/` moved whole, with its own tests; only the contract layer's shape changed
- the grove roll-up's `1 runs` pluralization bug is fixed on the way through

### E — the warmer (the fifth lever)

the four layout levers make a shared prefix exist; a live probe showed the bound that actually
bites is the clock — a prefix ages out of cache regardless of its size (0% hit at 66-118 minutes,
90% at 20 minutes). a two-arm probe (8 calls, $0.08) confirmed the eviction policy is LRU, so a
scheduled read (a "poke") holds a lane's prefix warm between review rounds.

shipped:

- `asReviewWarmerPlan` — the `off | on | <k>x,<m>m` grammar; one token, not two flags, so a poke
  past the ttl can't be typed
- a pid-file lock (`isReviewWarmerAlive`, liveness via `kill(pid, 0)`, not mere presence) so a
  guard that re-drives a lane many times spawns one warmer, not one per round
- a lane is root + slug + prompt head + brain, since the cache pin is model plus system prompt.
  one rule (`isReviewLanePrefixSuperseded`) decides when a head or brain moved, for the spawn and
  the value fold alike. a superseded warmer is stopped only on a confirmed `/proc` identity;
  where identity cannot be read it is left to finish its plan and renders `stale, not cover`
  with its pid, never as cover
- `getOneWarmerWakeVerdict` (poke / defer / retire) — a deferral never counts as an attempt, and a
  real review counts as a touch, so a poke sent moments after a nominal review is skipped
- `runReviewWarmer`, detached and `unref`'d, split its rows to two sinks from one write
  (`setOnePokeRow`): a per-run ledger (`review._.cost` reads it) and a per-reviewer log (a driver
  can `cat` it mid-flight)
- `getOneWarmerValue` — pairs each warmed run with the next priced review of its lane, and renders
  net value (spend vs. cache saved) at both `--of tree` and `--of grove`
- the gate is coarser than the wish's literal words (it fires on "was this run by a guard", not
  "does the guard have budget left"); the residual is bounded to ~3 pokes and caught as a dream
  rather than guessed at against a seam this branch does not own

dogfooded across eleven real guard rounds: **$3.32** spent on pokes, **at most $1.19** saved, net
**-$2.13**. re-measured 2026-09-27 over 22 lanes via `rhx review._.cost --of tree` (the measure
`--help` points a human to; the help states the risk, not these dated figures): **$3.68** vs **at
most $1.40**, net **-$2.28** — a loss on 18 of 22 lanes. the mechanism works (pokes hit 94.9%) and the 45-minute default window does not — real
rounds arrive 1.4-2.8x later than that. ruled: `--warmer` now defaults to **off** (`F45`); a fix
for the window is deferred to a dream, and to turn it back on by default is a wisher's call.

### F — repairs from the review ladder

the surface above converged through more than twenty peer review rounds. every blocker was
repaired at its cause rather than patched at its symptom:

- **decode-friction** is extracted into named operations throughout `stepReview`, the meter, the
  grove report, and the warmer (aggregation folds, flag reads, hash reads, sort keys)
- **failhides narrowed** to real allowlists: an `EACCES` on a target no longer reads as "no
  mtime", an unreadable log root prints a refusal instead of a bare stack, a torn poke row is
  read once and thrown rather than folded in as free spend, a lock mid-write no longer reads as
  stale
- **the warmer's two sinks fail in opposite directions**: an unwritable voice log is survived,
  since the ledger already holds the row; an unwritable ledger halts the warmer loud, after it
  logs the cause to the voice log, so no poke bills money `review._.cost` cannot see
- **a bad guard costs its own rows, never the report**: a malformed or barred `.guard` reads
  `(no level — guard unread)` and names itself on stderr; a hash-less stray peer-review file
  refuses as a constraint with the fix, never a raw stack
- **one honesty grade, one word**: `refExists` allowlists only git's "no such revision"; the
  markup-share bound gained a `FLOOR_OF_ATTRIBUTABLE_CHARS` so small, real prompts don't false-trip
  it; count agreement (`1 hit` / `2 hits`) is one transformer, reused everywhere
- **names brought to the get/set/gen convention**: `getOneSharedPrefixByteCount`,
  `getOneSharedPrefixOverlap`, `asFullRateTokens`, `getAllTopDirsByTokens`, and others replace
  ambiguous or unsanctioned verbs; five multi-export files split one-op-per-file
- **coverage clamps** added for every debt the vision itself declared open, plus several more the
  ladder found: a five-key census fixture that summed to 99,000 instead of 100,000, a `--limit`
  flag advertised in `--help` with no behavioral test, a `.gitignore` counted into a run census as
  a zero-cost review, `--limit abc` coerced to `NaN` and left a render empty with no notice
- **error headers qualified**: every terminal refusal across `review.ts`, `review._.cost`, and the
  warmer now opens with `✋ ConstraintError:` or `💥 MalfunctionError:`, and a caller-fixable
  fault is thrown as `ConstraintError` (`helpful-errors` 1.7.5), so the class matches the header.
  `ReviewTimeoutError` extends `MalfunctionError` with a bare message, so its header prints once
- **cross-context edges removed**: the peer-review filename grammar, `isTargetPolluter`, and
  `getGuardProvenance` each have readers in both `route` and `review`, so each is lifted to the
  `domain.operations/` root rather than read across a context line

### G — an overflow that names its lever, and a lane that may split

- **`rhx review --partition '<slug>=<globs>'`**, repeatable: the caller declares each partition,
  never the tool. partitions may overlap; a file two of them list is common — graded once, by the
  first that lists it, and read as context in the rest. the lane runs whole while it fits and
  splits only on overflow; partitions merge into ONE stdout tally and one `--output` report.
  a target in no partition, a partition that lacks the bound route, or a partition that still
  overflows alone refuses (exit 2) before any brain is called
- **`rhx route.guard.partition --for review --peer @all`**: a route declares its set once, at
  `$route/.route/partitions.json`, and every peer review on every stone reads it. each
  declaration is the WHOLE set for its scope (`@all`, or one `--peer --stone` override), checked
  for coverage against every changed file before it is written; `--partition none` clears a scope
  (`F105`). this route's own set is seven vertical domains — warmer, meter, partition, review,
  route, reflect, rest — through domains, never layers (`rule.forbid.horizontal-partitions`)
- **one glob matcher, standard semantics**: `*` stays within one segment, `**` spans them, `.` is
  literal, `{a,b}` alternates, `[ab]` / `[!ab]` takes one char of a class — under `--paths-wout`,
  the polluter exemption, and `--partition` alike, so it reads as `--paths-with` does (`F106`). a
  brace-list member may hold a brace list of its own
- **the overflow refusal names the lever**, read off the masses the prompt held: a bind cut
  (`--paths-wout`) for edited mass, a partition for added mass, a supply cut for no target
  mass, a narrower partition for a partition that still overflows. an unclaimed refusal names
  every directory, and opens any row too heavy to be one partition to the subdomains below it
- **a partition reads only its own patches**: the scope walk names the targets first, and a
  patch is read for a target alone — so a peer partition's prompt log, or any large untracked
  file outside the scope, can never overflow a read before the polluter filter drops it. each
  patch read allows 64 MiB, as the selection's own git reads do; its size is the overflow gate's
  to judge
- **a fault names whether a re-arrive cures it**: a brain 429/5xx or a dropped socket is asked
  again twice (10s, 30s) before it counts (`F121`); a partition section calls only such a fault
  transient, and names any other as a malfunction of the review with its cause to repair
- **upstream as a dispatch**: where the guard came from another repo's template, the refusal prints
  the `rhx radio.task.push --into <repo>` command to send the cure to its owner
- **enbriefed**: `howto.partition-an-overflowed-review` (reviewer), a cue in `review.tactics`, the
  lever table in `rule.always.diagnose-reviewer-malfunctions` (driver), a row in
  `rule.forbid.adhoc-scope-rollups`

### H — driver frictions found in dogfood, made unreachable

- **a rewind never deletes the givens of a guard round in flight**: each round holds a lock whose
  name carries its stone, its pid, and its writer's start time; `--as rewound` refuses (exit 2)
  while any live round touches a stone it would clear, and names the round, a `ps -p` probe, and
  the stale-lock exit. a round killed mid-pour leaves a lock that never blocks: a dead pid is
  stale, and a pid the kernel handed to a stranger is refuted by its start time (`/proc` field
  22, refute-only — where the kernel will not say, the pid alone decides) (`F118`)
- **a recorded stance exits 0**: `--as conceded` and `--as disputed` succeed; only a refused act
  exits 2, so a driver's `&&` chain of dispositions runs to its end (`F117`)
- **the overflow refusal is a type**: `ReviewOverflowRefusedError` extends `BadRequestError`, and
  the partition fault section narrows on it by `instanceof`, never by its message; the human
  header is unchanged
