# 🌙 dream: a budget grant records its why

kind: fix · owner: rhachet-roles-bhrain · caught 2026-11-21

## .the cue that fired

5.1 r010 nitpick.2 and r011 (omission): the urgent halt asks the driver to tell its human why the stone bought budget, but names no channel, and no record holds the answer.

## .the gap, precisely

`ASK_TEXT_BUDGET_NOTE` says what to write, never where. `rhx route.guard.budget` takes no reason, so the grant lands in the guard diff and `passage.jsonl` with no why beside it. on an unattended route the note has no reader, and the accountability for a lever the driver now owns rests on prose alone.

## .the shape of the fix

add `--why <text|@stdin>` to `route.guard.budget --for review`, persisted on the grant's ledger row. the urgent halt then prints the note as that flag, so the grant and its why are one act. the human reads it in the route's passage view. clamp: a grant with `--why` round-trips it to the ledger; the halt's command carries the flag.

## .why it is not done in this round

it is a new contract on a cli (a flag, a ledger field, a render) beyond the owner line this wish moves.
