# domain.term.choice.reason: archive

## .etymology

greek *arkheion*, the magistrate's house where public records were kept. an archive is a
**deliberate keep** — set aside on purpose, by someone who judged it worth retrieval later.
it is the opposite of a discard, and that connotation is the whole reason the word wins
here.

a rejected fork is not garbage. it is the proof that a question was asked and answered, and
the next traveler who reaches the same fork needs it.

## .why each synonym is forbidden

| the candidate | why it loses |
|---|---|
| `rejected` | an adjective in a noun's coat, and it names the VERDICT rather than the container. a dir called `rejected/` reads as "things that failed", which invites deletion |
| `discarded` | states the opposite of the truth. an archive was kept on purpose |
| `graveyard` | the same error with worse manners, and it teaches the next author that the contents are dead. they are not — they are the record of a live decision |
| `attic` | implies "put away and forgotten", so it undercuts the retrieval the word exists to promise |

⇒ three of the four fail the same way: **they name a fate rather than a purpose**, and the
fate they name is neglect. an archive is consulted.

## 🔴 .why it must be argued with `appendix`, and never alone

stated in full in `term=route.appendix._.choice.reason.md`. the short form: the two dirs
are declared in one rule with opposed senses, so a word settled alone inherits no boundary,
and the next author files a rejected fork under `appendix/` in good faith.

## ⚠️ .the failure this word does NOT prevent

`archive/` names a container for **ideas**. it does not license a chronicle of the round —
the drafts, the reversals, the blow-by-blow. that material has a home already, and it is
git.

⇒ the tell: an archive file that reads as *"first we tried X, then we tried Y"* rather than
*"Y was considered and ruled out, because Z"*. the first is archaeology; the second is an
archive. `rule.require.timeless-lessons` governs both.

## .the boundary — `route`, not repo-wide

`.dream/` is the repo-wide queue of **caught, undone work**, and it is a different concept:
a dream is owed, an archive is closed. a dream that turns out to be a bad idea is not moved
to an archive — it is answered where it lies.

## .evidence

- declared: `rule.always.yield-the-output-not-the-archaeology` (bhrain/driver), which
  states both dirs in one table and names the axis between them
- the pair discipline: `term=route.guard.review.given` / `…taken`, argued together
- distinguished from: `term=artifact.dream` (owed work) and
  `term=artifact.seed` (the wisher's verbatim words) — three route-adjacent record
  kinds, each already itemized except this one

## .invariants

- an archive holds a **rejected idea**, stated as an idea. never a chronicle of the round
- an archive is never a deletion. the record is kept because it will be consulted
- an archive never holds evidence beneath a decision that IS in the yield. that is
  `appendix/`
