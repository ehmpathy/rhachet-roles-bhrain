# razor: misplaced concreteness

shaves: a claim at the wrong level (a model stated as the world) · settled by: logic (can one point at the noun, or only at its instances?)

## .the question

> **is the noun this claim names a real item, or a label for a class, a score, or a model?**

the catch nouns: "the system", "the model", "the team", "the market", "the user". a label given a cause or a will is the defect: "the system wants", "the codebase resists".

## .the source

- Alfred North Whitehead, *Science and the Modern World*, 1925, p. 58: "the real error is an example of what I have termed: The Fallacy of Misplaced Concreteness"
- a reification: an abstraction "treated as if it were a concrete real event or physical entity"
- the same blade, in plain words: "a map is not the territory" (Korzybski, 1931; *Science and Sanity*, 1933)
- read via https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fallacy_of_misplaced_concreteness, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Map%E2%80%93territory_relation (no primary text read)

## .fails when

- the abstraction is the best handle at hand and carries real force ("a bug", "a team"). the razor shaves only a label given a cause or a will
- a useful model is not a defect: run `razor=box`

## .run by

`rule.always.trust-but-verify`
