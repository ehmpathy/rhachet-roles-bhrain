## .notes

- **care for rows 1, 6, 9, 17, 19:** every call of a work day walks them, and a break has no workable route around it — a blocked Edit is routed past only by a human who strips the hook. `freq × cost` is intolerable; that is the crews' incident exactly.
- **row 8** is an extant over-match, kept: the copy verbs are graded by the presence of a memory path, not by their target. it errs toward the guard's one job, it is rare, and its fix is a parse of each copy verb's argv — a separate change.
- **row 24, the one invariant.** a guard that could not judge has no authority to halt (nurture: the wish chose flow over a guard that cannot run). consequence: in such a tree a true memory write passes, and the notice on every call is what tells the human the guard is off.
- **row 30** cannot occur under claude code, which exports `CLAUDE_PROJECT_DIR` to every hook; it is reachable only from a bare shell, where the human chose the cwd.
- **row 31 is why the notice rides on exit 1, not exit 0.** claude code never shows a hook's stderr on exit 0; a non-0, non-2 exit renders a `hook error` notice to the human and lets the call proceed. see fulcrum F1.

## .density

row 20 is the densest: it crosses the settings file, the role link, the anchor, and claude code's exit contract in one call — and it is the experience that stalled two crews. row 27 is next: cwd, the anchor, the link, and the verdict. row 31 closes the loop the fail-open trade depends on. all three are centered as demos.
