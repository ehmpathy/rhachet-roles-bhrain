## .the cue that fired

a peer reviewer (5.1 execution, i004, enroll-impl-arch-defects nitpick.4) found the list of
render inputs held three times, and the new `statusLine/file/` path reliant on `statusLine/cache/`,
the module that survives only for seats on the old node entry.

## .the gap, precisely

- the cache fingerprint (`getOneRouteStatusLineFingerprint`) and the settle stamp share one list
- the bash reader's `is_stale` holds a second, narrower list (no route dir mtime, fulcrum F6)
- `file/` imports the fingerprint, `asRouteStatusLineRouteDir`, the HEAD read, and the flag check
  from `cache/`

no test holds the bash list and the ts list together, as the branch corpus holds the two
`sanitizeBranchName` twins.

## .the shape of the fix

lift the shared pieces to `statusLine/`; give the settle stamp exactly the reader's list; add a
parity test that reads `is_stale`'s inputs from the reader file and compares them to the ts list.

## .why it is not done in this round

a module move across ~10 files and every import of them, plus a parity harness. the comment on
`getOneInputStamp` now states the one gap and its bounded cost. it fails CLEAN.
