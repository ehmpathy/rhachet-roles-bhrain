wish =

# wish: clamp the boot of role=learner, role=driver, role=librarian to a declared budget

## the need

every director seat boots these three roles. today they cost, per `npx rhachet roles cost --all`:

| role | boot tokens |
|---|---|
| learner | 26,042 |
| driver | 22,864 |
| librarian | 9,651 |

that is 58.6k of a ~126k seat, re-read on every call of every seat. the order is to make them
**dense, not cut**: the same rules, at a fraction of the tokens.

## what done means

1. each of the three roles' `boot.yml` declares `budget: tokens: <n>`, and `rhachet roles cost`
   for each role reads under it. proposed budgets, to settle with evidence: learner ≤ 12k,
   driver ≤ 12k, librarian ≤ 5k.
2. **learner first**, then driver, then librarian, so the largest win lands first.
3. every rule keeps its **cue** at `say`: the claim, the test, the when/then table, the enforcement
   lines. its **argument** (etymology, measured cases, counter-arguments, long demos) moves to a
   ref file beside it (`rule.prefer.rules-that-refout`, this repo's librarian).
4. a cue is never cut to reach a number (`rule.require.a-cue-is-not-a-claim`, learner). if a role
   cannot reach its budget with every cue intact, set the budget at what it reaches and say why.
5. an oversized subdomain (a glossary, an invariant set, a catalog of examples) boots as its
   catalog at `say`, its members under `not` (`rule.always.boot-an-oversized-subdomain-as-its-catalog`).
6. the pr body carries a before/after table per role from `npx rhachet roles cost --all`.
7. reviewer, if touched, holds its budget too, but it is not in scope to cut.

## bounds

- no rule deleted. no rule's sense changed. a move from say to ref is the whole lever.
- a rule whose cue fires at the moment of action stays say; a rule a tool already enforces at
  that moment may move to ref, with the tool named in the comment.

## why now

the commander ordered it, top priority, 2026-10-21. a release that lags is covered by a pnpm
patch in the consumer repo, so the cure lands upstream here, first.
