## .what
a **fulcrum** is **a call made mid-drive that a human should see, recorded at the moment it is
made.**

it takes the itemization form every occurrence set takes:

```
$route/.fulcrums/inventory.of=fulcrums._.md                # the SUMMARY — axes, counts, gaps
$route/.fulcrums/inventory.of=fulcrums.case=F$n-$slug.md   # one ENTRY per fulcrum
```

⇒ `case` is an **occurrence axis** — a drive's calls cannot be listed before the drive, so the
positions are declared as they arise (`rule.require.inventory-for-enumerable-concepts`).

## 🔴 .a fulcrum is NOT a fork — a fork is one WAY to earn one

the two words are near enough to collapse, and the collapse loses the broader half:

| | what it is | does it have the other? |
|---|---|---|
| a **fork** | two options in view, one taken | a fork that is best-guessed **always** earns a fulcrum |
| a **fulcrum** | a call the council should see | a fulcrum **often has no fork** — see below |

a call under the **93% confidence floor** earns a fulcrum with no rival in view: you wrote the
claim, it read fine, and the shakiness never became a decision. so `fork` is a **field inside** an
entry (*"the fork"* — option A vs option B, stated fairly), never a second word for the whole.

⇒ **that is why `fork` is not on the forbidden list.** it names a part, and
`rule.forbid.domain-term-synonyms` bans a second word for one concept, not a word for a component.

## .the two triggers

| trigger | catches |
|---|---|
| a **fork** best-guessed mid-drive | a choice you weighed |
| a call under **93% confidence** | the invented claim, the unverified premise, the n=1 generalization — **and the fork too** |

⇒ the floor is the broader trigger. the threshold is the wisher's, set 2026-08-30.
