# domain.term.reason: education.pursuit.course.proof

## .etymology

a course exists to prove claims in vitro and to move a score; what it hands the pedigree is the
proof of both. the word names the content (evidence that held or was refuted), never the medium.

rejected:
- `record` — a forbidden synonym of `education.pedigree`; the pedigree is the record, and a
  course proof is one entry within it. a peer review caught the overload in a contract
  (`CourseRecord`), and the contract now reads `CourseProof`
- `result` — names only the outcome score; a proof also holds the claims refuted
- `report` — names a document a human writes; a proof is read from fixed tables
- `summary` — names a reduction; the proof keeps every claim in full

## .evidence

- the proof is read only from yields the course stones fix in shape (`score =`, `transfer =`,
  the greats, claims, and yields tables); a yield absent its table refuses with the shape owed
- a refuted claim stays in the proof, so the pedigree keeps it on record for the next reader

## .disputes

### dispute: record — raised 2026-11-24 — status: RESOLVED (canonical holds)
- raised.by  = peer review `repo-rules`, 5.1.execution.from_vision
- claim      = `CourseRecord` names what one course proved
- counter    = `record` is forbidden under `education.pedigree`; the pedigree is the record of
               many courses, so one course's part needs its own word
- resolution = `proof` is canonical; `record` stays forbidden

## .invariants

- a proof is read from the course's yields, never written by hand
- a proof keeps every claim, held or refuted
