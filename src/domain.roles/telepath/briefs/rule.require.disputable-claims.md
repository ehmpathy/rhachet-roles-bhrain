# rule.require.disputable-claims

> **every passage must give the reader a place to say *"no, and here is why"*. state the claim, and state what would settle it.**

the positive peer of `rule.forbid.subversive-prose` — that rule names ten shapes no observation
settles, and sorts each as **shallow** or **squishy**; this names the target all ten fall short of.

⇒ the ten shapes, the worked 70-word demo, and the accretion-vs-compression measurement live there.
**this file carries the target, and the two moves that reach it.**

## .why a target is owed beside the ten shapes

a forbid list is a **detector**, and a detector tells an author what to delete. it does not tell
them what to write in its place — so an author who learns the ten deletes ten sentences and ships a
shorter passage that still asserts none.

| the forbid gives | this gives |
|---|---|
| ten shapes to recognize and cut | the two properties the survivor must have |
| a repair per shape | a bar the whole passage must clear |
| *"that sentence says naught"* | *"here is what a sentence that says something looks like"* |

⇒ **the clamp pair is asymmetric on purpose.** the forbid is the sharper cue and the reader meets it
first; this is what they aim at once the cuts stop.

## .the two moves

a claim is disputable when it carries both. either alone is half a claim:

- **assert a position** — a statement that could be false
  - it names a subject, a predicate, and a bound
  - a reader who disagrees can point at which of the three they reject
- **name the observation that would settle it** — the check, the file, the run, the measurement
  - so the disagreement has somewhere to go
  - ⇒ **this is the move authors drop**, and it is the one that makes the claim actionable

## .the test

> **what observation would settle this sentence?**

one you can name, over a stated position → a claim · a position with no observation → **an opinion**;
name the check · an observation with no position → **a procedure**; state what you expect it to show
· neither → cut it, or convert it.

🟡 **not *"could a reader disagree with it?"*** — that grades taste, and a metaphor passes it. two
readers may disagree forever over *"it took the cheaper of two doors"* and no observation on earth
settles them. **the question names the check; disagreement alone does not.**

🟡 and not *"is it confident?"* — a **stated uncertainty is a claim**. *"70%, and one read of
`getExitCodeClass.ts` settles it"* names a position, a bound, and an observation. *"this should
probably mostly work"* names none of the three, and reads more assured.

### the same claim, three ways, and only the third names its observation

| the specimen | prose | a position? | what settles it? |
|---|---|---|---|
| the hedge | *"the review flow has some issues worth a look"* | **no** | — |
| the metaphor | *"it took the cheaper of two doors that both looked legitimate"* | yes | **none** |
| the mechanism | *"`runStoneGuardReviews` never calls `getStone…Unforgiven`, so the check runs at passage"* | yes | open the file |

⇒ the metaphor is the expensive instance, because it reads as insight and a disagreement test
passes it. its repair is the mechanism it stood in front of, never a cut —
`forbid.subversive-prose` sorts it as **squishy**, and the hedge as **shallow**.

🟡 the three specimens are SHARED with that rule, and graded on a different axis there.

- it sorts each into shallow or squishy; this grades each pass or fail
  - ⇒ so the hedge and the metaphor read differently between the two files
  - the mechanism is identical, because it is the control — it passes both, so neither sort applies
- ⇒ the overlap is `rule.require.a-cue-is-not-a-claim`'s exempt shape, never a `declare-once` defect

| when… | then… |
|---|---|
| you would write "it depends on context" | the strongest cue. name the axis it depends on, and which value you took |
| a sentence could be prefixed with "obviously" and lose no content | it asserts what no reader would contest. cut it |
| you name a claim and not what would settle it | half a claim. the settler is the half that is absent |
| you would name a cheap settler where a stronger one exists | name the strongest. a weak settler is `forbid.obfuscation`'s shape |
| a paragraph survives a swap of its subject for any other subject | it is a truism, and it discriminates no case |
| you feel a passage is unattackable | that is the alarm, never the goal. an unattackable passage said naught |

## .the caveat — a disputable claim may be WRONG, and that is the point

this rule raises the odds you are publicly incorrect. **that is its function, not its cost.**

| the passage | what happens next |
|---|---|
| a disputable claim, wrong | a reader refutes it, and the artifact improves in one round |
| an indisputable passage, wrong | it is never refuted, and every reader inherits the error |

⇒ **an artifact cannot be corrected on a claim it never made.** so a canon that only forbids
subversion, and never demands assertion, produces prose that is safe and useless.

## .the axis

a **structure** rule, so telepath owns it (`rule.require.generic-governs-structure-never-voice`) — a
claim is disputable or it is not, in every register, so it prescribes no member of that rule's voice
row.

blocker: a passage that asserts no position a reader could reject · a position with no named
observation that would settle it, where one exists · a truism that survives a swap of its subject ·
a hedge stack offered in place of a stated bound.
nitpick: a settler named where a stronger one was available.
false positive: a stated uncertainty with a bound and an observation · a literal the reader must
type, which asserts no position · a verbatim quote · a measurement, which is its own settler.

⇒ see also: `rule.forbid.subversive-prose` (the ten shapes, and the depth) ·
`rule.forbid.obfuscation` (the weak settler is one of its shapes) · `rule.require.distillation`
(a surface claim is disputable and still shallow) · `rule.require.elucidation`.
