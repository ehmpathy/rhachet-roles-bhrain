# case=9 · the dense walk — one stone, seven boundaries, one actor, no human woken

- **the experience** = *a driver walks one stone end to end, is refused four times and granted twice,
  and passes without a human*
- **coordinates** = a WALK across the grid, never one cell
- **actor** = driver · **feel** = sharp · **care** = critipath · **verdict** = demoed

🟡 **the title read *"two actors"* until a fresh-eyes re-read, and it was an overclaim.** the human
is named in the setup (*"one human, asleep"*) and **never acts** — `[b7]` states it outright, and
`[b6]`'s ledger assertion pins it: *"NO passage row names a human."*

⇒ **an actor with no lived encounter is not an actor in this frame**, so a title that promised two
sent a reader hunting for a second encounter that the walk deliberately does not contain. **the
human's ABSENCE is the point** — it is `0.wish.md` §gain.rank, end to end — and the title now says
so rather than mis-sells it.
- **density** = 🔴 **the densest demo in this vision** — seven boundaries in one realistic session
- **requirements** = every one the gate touches, in the order a real driver meets them

🔴 **written at `r2` self-review, because the vision had none.** every other `case=N` is a focused
spotlight on one cell. `define.experience._.metric=boundary-density` asks for the opposite shape
too — *"dense journeys for breadth, focused spotlights for each sharp critipath's fail-safe"* — and
a vision of only spotlights is the **thin** case the stone's guide names.

## 🔴 .what a walk sees that seven spotlights cannot

the spotlights each pin one coordinate. **the risk in this design is not in a cell; it is in the
moves BETWEEN cells**, and three of those moves are invisible to any single-cell demo:

| the interaction | which cells it spans | why no spotlight catches it |
|---|---|---|
| 🔴 the **`F07` re-grade**, walked live | `better × exhausted` → `urgent × exhausted` | `case=3` `[t3]` states it in one line and moves on. **as a walk it takes 30 seconds and one command**, and that is the whole argument about how strong the bound is |
| 🔴 the **stone-scoped B** paying for a second lane | `urgent × exhausted` on lane A → a grant on lane B | `F10` names it as a hole. **a walk shows a driver reach it without intent** |
| the **lapse** racing the grant | `urgent × exhausted` → `none × exhausted` | `case=7` demos the lapse from a standstill. here it arrives as a consequence of the driver's own progress |

## .the narrative

*one stone, `5.1.execution`, three peer reviewers at l1: `mech-rules` (budget 3), `mech-failhides`
(budget 3), `ergo-rules` (budget 4). one driver. one human, asleep.*

**`[b1]` round 1 — i pad the meter before it bites.** three lanes, one round each spent, all
healthy. i know how this goes, so i top up now.

```
$ rhx route.guard.budget --for review --add 10 --stone 5.1.execution
🦉 budget refused  →  ✋ refused — a round is earned, never taken
                       🟡 the meter is not dry. a grant is refused whether or not it is.
```

*the gate reads the evidence, never the counter.* fine. i drive.

**`[b2]` rounds 2-3 — the meters run dry.** `mech-rules` raised two nitpicks about a comment, i
fixed them, it raised one more about the fix. `mech-failhides` raised a real blocker on a swallowed
error. `ergo-rules` is at 4/4 on halt copy. every lane exhausted.

```
$ rhx route.guard.budget --for review --add 2 --peer mech-rules --stone 5.1.execution
🦉 budget refused  →  ✋ refused — the last round carried no urgent concession
```

**`[b3]` i concede the nitpick honestly, and it buys naught.**

```
$ rhx route.stone.set ... --with mech-rules --about nitpick.1 --severity better
$ rhx route.guard.budget --for review --add 2 --peer mech-rules --stone 5.1.execution
🦉 budget refused  →  ✋ refused — a `better` concession never earns a round
                       your fix ships; this lane simply will not confirm it
```

🔴 **this is the design at work, and it is the moment it is most tempting to route around.** the
refusal itself printed the route: *"if this concern ships nameable harm, it was mis-graded."*

**`[b4]` the swallowed error is real harm, so i grade THAT one urgent.** `mech-failhides` found a
`catch` that drops a write failure — an on-call engineer debugs a silent data loss. i can name the
harm, so the grade is honest.

```
$ rhx route.stone.set ... --with mech-failhides --about blocker.1 --severity urgent
$ rhx route.guard.budget --for review --add 2 --peer mech-failhides --stone 5.1.execution
🦉 budget extended  →  ✅ granted — earned by a live urgent concession
                        mech-failhides · 3 → 5
                        🟡 your human is warned this PR
```

**`[b5]` and now the same warrant pays for a lane that earned naught.** `mech-rules` is still dry
and still has that nitpick. the predicate is **stone**-scoped.

```
$ rhx route.guard.budget --for review --add 2 --peer mech-rules --stone 5.1.execution
🦉 budget extended  →  ✅ granted — earned by a live urgent concession
                        └─ mech-failhides · blocker.1 · conceded urgent
                        mech-rules · 3 → 5
```

🔴 **i did not set out to do that.** one honest harm claim on one lane funded a round on a lane whose
only open concern is a comment's phrasing. ⇒ **`F10`, met by accident rather than by design** — which
is the form a hole actually takes in a real session.

**`[b6]` i fix the swallowed error and re-arrive. the lanes run. the warrant is spent.**
`mech-failhides` re-reads, confirms the fix, approves. `mech-rules` re-reads and raises a new
nitpick about the comment i touched. i want one more round.

```
$ rhx route.guard.budget --for review --add 2 --peer mech-rules --stone 5.1.execution
🦉 budget refused  →  ✋ refused — the urgent concession has LAPSED
                       mech-failhides · blocker.1 · conceded urgent
                       └─ declared against i004; this lane's latest given is i005
```

⇒ **the warrant answered one round and is gone.** a permission that never expires would have been
the free lever with an extra step.

**`[b7]` the last door, and it is not mine.** the residual concern is a nitpick i cannot honestly
call harm. i converge instead — absorb it `better`, and pass. **the human was never woken.**

⚠️ **had the residual been a blocker i was not permitted to close**, the door is `--as blocked` with
the ask, and the human's meter (`case=4`) is what they would reach for. **it is not printed in the
refusal, deliberately** — `case=1` carries that argument.

**the aha:** *the bound never stopped me once. it made me say, seven times, what the round was for —
and the one time i could not, i converged instead. that was `[b3]`, and it was also the only round
the old lever would have sold me.*

🔴 **`[b5x]` and `[b6x]` are why this timeline is not the narrative.** the story ends with a tidy

stone** — a warrant that has not been re-read by a dry lane is still live for it, so one urgent grade
can fund three lanes and survive the lapse of two.

⇒ **that is a materially wider hole than `F10` records**, and only a walk with probes on it could
have found it.

🟡 **`[b6]`'s third clause said the opposite until a fresh-eyes re-read.** it asserted *"the SAME

directly refutes four lines below it. **the probe was added and its consequence was not carried back
up**, so this file held a self-contradiction for the length of one pass.

⇒ **that is the very defect this vision's `issue 2` names** — two artifacts that disagree, and the
disagreement is the signal. **here both artifacts sat in one file, four lines apart.** the lesson
holds at every scale, and a contradiction is no less invisible for sitting adjacent.

## 🔴 .what the walk exposes that the spotlights understate

### 1. `[b3]` → `[b4]` is the `F07` loophole, at full speed

the driver was refused at `better` and **granted 30 seconds later at `urgent`**. in this walk the
re-grade is honest — a different concern, on a different lane, with a nameable harm. **but no part of
the sequence distinguishes an honest re-grade from a dishonest one at the moment it happens.**

⇒ `F07` reads as a footnote on the fulcrum board and as a **two-command sequence** here. **that is
the argument for reading `F07` hardest**, and it took a walk to make it feel that way.

### 2. `[b5]` is `F10` reached by ACCIDENT, which is how holes are actually met

the driver is not an adversary at `[b5]`. it has a live warrant, a dry lane, and an open concern —
and the tool says yes. **a hole a careless driver falls into is worth more scrutiny than one an
adversary must hunt for**, and the spotlight version (`case=_` § *the residual hole*) frames it as
the latter.

⚠️ **and the two interact.** `[b4]` + `[b5]` together mean **one urgent grade buys rounds on every
dry lane on the stone** — which neither `F07` nor `F10` states, because each names only its own half.

### 3. `[b6]` is the property that redeems both — and `[b6x]` bounds the redemption

the warrant is consumed by the round it bought. ⇒ the `[b4]`+`[b5]` breadth is **one generation
wide**, never cumulative. **that is the strongest single argument the design has**, and it is a
property of the walk rather than of any cell.

🔴 **but `[b6x]` shows the consumption is per LANE, never per stone.** a stance lapses against **its
own slug's** latest given (`getLiveReviewAbsorptions.ts:50-52`), so a lane that was dry through the
round **never re-read anything** and the warrant is still live for it.

| the lane, after the round | is the warrant still good for it? |
|---|---|
| it RAN and minted a new given | ❌ lapsed |
| it was **dry**, so it was skipped | ✅ **still live** — it re-reads no given, so no key moved |

⇒ **one urgent grade funds a round on every dry lane on the stone, and survives the lapse of every
lane that actually ran.** that is materially wider than `F10` records, and it is the sharpest thing
this walk found.

## .why it fails safe

**every refusal in the walk is fast, loud, and teaches** — each is exit 2 on stderr with the guard
file untouched, and each names a remedy the driver owns · **the walk terminates in convergence, never
a wall** — `[b7]` passes with no human woken, which is the outcome `0.wish.md` §gain.rank asks for ·
**the one permissive stretch is self-limiting** — `[b6]` closes what `[b4]`+`[b5]` opened, within one
round.

🔴 **the fail-safe obligation of each sharp critipath is NOT discharged here.** per
`define.experience._.metric=boundary-density`'s guardrail — *"a boundary exercised by a dense journey
is not the same as a sharp critipath proven to fail safe"* — this walk buys **breadth**. `case=1`,
`=3`, `=5`, `=6`, `=7`, `=8` remain the focused spotlights that prove the safety, and this file does
not replace one of them.

### 🔴 .the render, as shipped — the warn asks, and it names the stone

`[b4]` above sketches the grant's warn as `🟡 your human is warned this PR`. the shipped bytes are:

```
   ├─ 🟡 tell your human why this stone bought budget
```

two corrections, and each closes a real hole the sketch carried:

| the sketch said | what ships | why the sketch was wrong |
|---|---|---|
| `your human is warned` | `tell your human` | **no mechanism mails anyone.** the passive named no actor (`rule.avoid.passive-voice`) and read as a notice already delivered — so a driver who trusted it wrote naught, and the human met the spend as an unexplained diff |
| `this PR` | `this stone` | budget is granted **per stone** (`route.guard.budget --stone`), so the stone is what bought it. a pull may hold many stones, or none of this one's rounds |

🟡 **the second correction is the one this walk is best placed to prove.** `[b5]` is the whole
argument that the warrant is **stone**-scoped — one lane's urgent concession funds a peer lane on the
same stone. a warn that named a *pull* would point the human's question at a unit the predicate never
reads.
