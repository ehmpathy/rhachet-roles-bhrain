# 🌙 dream: a learner peer keeps the term hook a toggle turned on

- kind: reseed (rhachet), or a fix in this repo's toggle
- owner: whoever next touches `learn.terms.hook` or rhachet's enroll narrow
- caught 2026-11-26, in `.behavior/v2026_11_23.feat-learner-education-suite` (fulcrum F14)

## .the cue that fired

`learn.terms.hook.integration.test.ts` `[case3]` went red after the rhachet 1.51 → 1.54 bump:
a derived peer that holds the learner holds no term hook entry. the suite now pins that state.

## .the gap, precisely

- the toggle writes its Stop hook with author `toggle=learn.terms.hook/role=learner`, so rhachet's
  orphan prune (role authors only) leaves it be
- rhachet 1.54 reads an author's role only through `asRoleSlugOfHookAuthor`, which needs a
  `repo=` segment. `isHookAuthorOfEnrolledRoles` returns null for the toggle author, and
  `getSettingsForRoles` keeps only `=== true`. so every enrolled peer loses the entry
- the repo actor keeps it (it takes its settings whole), so plain sessions still get the nudge;
  `rhx enroll`ed learner clones do not
- each author that would survive the narrow (`repo=…/role=learner`) is instead pruned as an
  orphan, or moved into the manifest the sync rebuilds from declarations. no author shape works

## .the shape of the fix

either, and the first is smaller:

1. rhachet: in `getSettingsForRoles`, keep a hook whose author names an enrolled role by its
   `role=` segment alone when it has no `repo=` (a non-role author the prune never owns). clamp:
   a `toggle=x/role=learner` hook survives a `--roles learner` narrow, and drops for `--roles driver`
2. this repo: the learner declares the term hook in `getLearnerRole` (onStop), so the sync puts it
   in the manifest and narrows it right; the toggle flips a state file the hook reads, and the hook
   exits 0 silent when off. the toggle's settings writes, locks, and drift reports retire

then `[case3]` `[t1]` goes red: it pins the defect (`toEqual([])`). flip it back to
`toHaveLength(1)`, point `[t3]` and `[t4]` (`runLearnerStop`) back at the peer's derived settings,
and re-snap.

## .why it is not done in this round

the fix is a contract change in either rhachet or the toggle, outside the education suite this
route ships. the defect arrived with the rhachet bump F11 keeps (the suite needs 1.54 for mods).
