## .settled

**the canon holds two mandates and no method.** `require.fundamentalism` says *decompose to the
root*; `require.reflexive-condensation` says *cut every line below the 95% bar*. neither says **how
to decide** which candidate is the root and which line is below the bar.

⇒ a **razor** is exactly that decision procedure: a heuristic that cuts a candidate from a set, so
what survives is what the razor could not remove.

| the mandate | the razor supplies |
|---|---|
| `require.fundamentalism` — reach the root | which of several candidate causes to cut |
| `require.reflexive-condensation` — cut below the bar | which of several true lines to cut |
| `require.elucidation` — transfer the concept | which distraction is between the reader and the point |

**so it is a `howto`, and it is the method under three rules at once** — which is why it belongs
in the canon rather than inside any one of them.

🟡 **and it is a TRAIT as well as a howto.** a razor applied once is a technique; a razor applied
reflexively, on every draft, is a disposition. the wisher asks for both — the howto teaches the
moves, the trait makes them habitual.

## .the shape it implies

- `howto.razor-a-thought` — the razor set, one per row: what it cuts, its test, and one worked case
- each razor named for **what it cuts**, never for a person
- ⇒ it sits at `ref` beside `define.fundamentalism` and `define.reflexive-condensation`, since a
