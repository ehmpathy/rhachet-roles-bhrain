# case=2 — the cache rate of each brain, and why each missed

feel = sharp · care = critipath · cells = S3 `tree` × `rate` × `per-brain` · `tree` × `cause` ×
`per-brain` · `grove` × `rate` × `per-brain` · `grove` × `cause` × `per-brain` · D5 `recorded` · D6 `switched`

## .narrative

a driver is asked "which brain wastes the most on input, and why?" today the meter answers by tree,
route, stone, reviewer, and provider, never by brain. its pair verdicts — why a round read or did
not read from cache — exist only as one count over the whole corpus. a hand census over the json is
the only path to either, and `rule.forbid.adhoc-scope-rollups` forbids it.

**after.** `rhx review._.cost --of grove` carries a `by brain` rollup beside `by provider`, and
`--of tree` a `by brain` bucket. each row states full-rate tokens, cost, runs, and cache share, and
beside them the brain's own pair verdict counts: `served`, `missed`, `lapsed`. a run whose args
recorded no slug sits under its own absent-key row.

the aha is the per-brain cause: a brain at 4% with `lapsed` ≫ `missed` needs no layout act — its
rounds came late. a brain at 4% with `missed` ≫ `lapsed` has a broken prefix or route, and the
driver knows where to look.

## .[tn] sketch

```
given a grove whose runs rode two brains, one run of which recorded no brain
  when the driver audits which brain wastes the most, and why
    [t0] the driver runs `rhx review._.cost --of grove`
      then a `by brain` section lists each slug, richest first
      then each row shows full-rate tokens, cost, runs, and cache share
      then each row shows its own `served` · `missed` · `lapsed` pair counts
      then the run with no recorded brain sits under `(no brain — unrecorded)`
    [t1] the driver reads the brain with the lowest share
      when its pairs are mostly `lapsed`
        then the driver reads a cadence fact, and owes no layout act
      when its pairs are mostly `missed`
        then the driver reads a prefix or route defect on that brain
      when one of its rounds rode a brain its prior did not
        then that pair counts `unshared` under the round's brain, never `missed`
        then the driver is never sent to hunt a layout defect two models could not share
    [t2] the driver narrows to the richest tree: `rhx review._.cost --of tree --at <tree>`
      then a `by brain` bucket shows each slug's prompt, cache share, cost, and pair counts
      then the slug the grove ranked first ranks first here too
    [t3] the driver pipes the same call with `--json` into a dashboard
      then the payload carries `byBrain`, with the same rows as the text
```

## .what cure 0 owes, for this case

a pair is two runs of one reviewer, keyed by the round's brain. a pair whose two runs rode
different brains (D6 `switched`) shares no cache by nature — two models — so cure 0 grades it
`unshared`, never `missed`, with a clamp: a switched pair with a long shared prefix and a 0 read
grades `unshared`.
