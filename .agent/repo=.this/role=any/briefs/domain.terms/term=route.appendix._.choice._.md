## .what

the route directory that holds **the evidence beneath a decision that IS in the yield**.

```
$route/appendix/
```

a yield states the answer. where the argument behind one of its claims is worth a record,
that record lives here, and the yield cites it **by path**.

## 🔴 .the pair — it is defined against `archive`, never alone

| dir | holds | the decision it backs |
|---|---|---|
| `appendix/` | the evidence beneath a decision | one that **IS** in the yield |
| `archive/` | the ideas considered | ones that were **REJECTED** |

⇒ **the axis is the fate of the decision, never the age of the document.** a note written
first and kept is an appendix; a note written last and dropped is an archive.

⚠️ argue the two together or the opposition is lost — exactly as `given`/`taken` would have
been. this file and `term=route.archive._.choice._.md` are one settlement in two files.
