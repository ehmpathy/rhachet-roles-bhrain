## .settled

**fundamentalism is concept decomposition and condensation into fundamentals** — then speech in
those terms, never the surface the concept happens to wear today.

two moves, in order:

- **decompose** — break the concept into the fundamentals it rests on
  - ask *"what does this stand on?"* until the answer stops to shift
- **condense** — state each fundamental in the fewest words that keep it whole
  - a fundamental that needs a paragraph was two fundamentals
  - ⇒ the condense step is what **proves** the decompose step finished

### the output is a tree, which is what bulletize renders

**fundamentalism produces the tree; bulletize draws it.** the two are one pipeline, and an author
who bulletizes an undrilled concept renders a tree of surfaces.

⇒ that composition was invisible while each was considered alone, and it is the reason the second
utterance's reframe matters: *"decomposition and condensation"* names the same act
`rule.require.bulletize` already assumed and never named.
