## .the blueprint questions, settled

- **Q7: can a clone enrolled without the learner run it?** Yes, wherever the repo links the learner. `rhx` finds a skill under `<cwd>/.agent/repo=*/role=*/skills`, the repo's linked roles, and never reads the actor's roles (`rhachet/dist/domain.operations/invoke/discoverSkillExecutables.js`). So any caller may toggle (F4, upheld by the director), and the `reaches = sessions that hold the learner` leaf tells that caller its own stops may not be the ones nudged.
- **Q8: does the toggle share rhachet's lock?** Yes. It takes rhachet's `withRepoMutex` key in rhachet's cache dir. Both sides use `with-simple-mutex@0.1.1` and `simple-on-disk-cache@1.8.1` (checked via `pnpm why`). Rhachet does not export the lock, so its rule is restated and clamped from outside. Caught as dream `v2026_11_07.reseed.rhachet-export-with-repo-mutex`.
