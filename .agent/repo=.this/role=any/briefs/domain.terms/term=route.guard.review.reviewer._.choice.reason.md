🟡 that is the gap this cluster closes, and it is the mechanism `rule.forbid.domain-term-inconsistency` describes: **a word so central that everyone assumed someone had declared it.**

## .disputes

### dispute: `lane` — raised 2026-09-04 — status: **RESOLVED (keep `reviewer`)**

- **raised.by** = the wisher, mid-drive on `.behavior/v2026_09_04.feat-telepath-role`
- **claim** — the wisher, verbatim:

  > *"do you mean a peer-reviewer? wtf is a lane"*

  `lane` was used ~312 times across 13 live artifacts in a vision yield, and the wisher — the reader the artifact was written for — could not parse it. that is `case=1`'s own terseness criterion falsified live: *"no internal jargon in the part the reader acts on."*

- **counter** — `lane` is not a coinage of that route. it was inherited from a booted brief: `rule.always.diagnose-reviewer-malfunctions` is *named* for `reviewer` and its body says *"the lane's prompt blew the window"* and *"an overflowed lane returns terminal."* ⇒ the extant canon is itself inconsistent, which is how the word spread.

- the substantive counter, and it survived the verdict — `reviewer` is overloaded across two concepts:

  | the sense | what it is |
  |---|---|
  | the **role** | `repo=bhrain/role=reviewer` — briefs, skills, a rubric contract |
  | **one run of one rubric on one guard** | `r1: dimensional-decomposition`, one of several, at one iteration |

  `rule.forbid.domain-term-ambiguity` holds that an overload *"hides an ABSENT DISTINCTION"*, and its stated repair is a distinct word for the second sense. `lane` is a candidate for exactly that — which would make it a legitimate new term rather than a forbidden synonym.

- **resolution** = `reviewer` holds. the wisher settled it 2026-09-06 — *"yes, fix it exhaustively"* — so `lane` is a forbidden synonym permanently, and the conform ran the same round. the seed is `inventory.of=seeds.case=S31-lane-becomes-reviewer-and-the-stophook-flag-is-hook-onStop.md`.

### 🟡 the verdict took the INCONSISTENCY repair, so the AMBIGUITY it names is now an open gap

the two rules prescribe opposite moves on the same pair, and only one could run:

| the rule | its repair | what ran |
|---|---|---|
| `rule.forbid.domain-term-inconsistency` | one concept, two words → choose one, conform the rest | **this** |
| `rule.forbid.domain-term-ambiguity` | one word, two concepts → coin a distinct word for the second | deferred |

⇒ so the overload above is not refuted; it is inherited. `reviewer` now names the role *and* one run of one rubric, and the second sense has no word of its own.

🟡 **`lane` is no longer a candidate for it.** the verdict makes the word a forbidden synonym, so the run-sense needs a word this repo has not yet reached for. that is recorded as a gap rather than left as a dispute, because a resolved dispute stops to be read.

## .the census, measured against disk 2026-09-06

🟡 the fulcrum estimated **~312** occurrences. a walk of the tree found **1211** across 179 files —
nearly four times the estimate, which is the census law at work: a number asserted from a partial
sweep is a lower bound, never a count.

| class | count | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| a use — `lane` used for the concept | 577 lines, 72 files | renamed |
| a verbatim record — a `.said`, a peer's own report, a closed round | 466 | never rewritten |
| literal english — a road lane, in a walkability demo | 12 | untouched |
| a cache or a trash dir | 13 | not a contract |
| a mention — the word as the subject of a sentence about the word | 66 | kept; it IS the record |
| a dated address — a `.dream/` slug, which is an address rather than a word in a body | 2 | never renamed |

⇒ the five classes were enumerated *before* one edit landed, and the enumeration paid for itself:
a blind sweep would have rendered `review lane` as `review reviewer`, rewritten the thinker's road
lanes, and falsified the peer reports under `.reviews/peer/`.
