# 🌙 dream: a promise renders the next self review, but does not record its ask

kind: fix · owner: rhachet-roles-bhrain · caught 2026-10-21

## .the cue that fired

`v2026_10_21.fix-clamp-role-boot-budgets`, stone `5.1`. three times in one round, a promise came
back `passage = progressed (review.self N/8 promised)` and then rendered review N+1's full guide,
its `--into` path, and its promise command. i wrote that review, ran the printed promise command, and
got `🍃 no ask on record`. the fix it printed is to ask with `--as passed`, then promise again.

## .the gap, precisely

- the promise emit (`formatRouteStoneEmit.ts`, the `progressed` branch near :518) renders the next
  review as though it were asked
- the ask is minted only on an `--as passed|arrived` call, so the clock and the freshness bar for
  N+1 have no start
- ⇒ the printed promise command fails, every time, for a driver who follows the output literally.
  a pit-of-failure: the right move is the one the tool did not print

## .the shape of the fix

pick one, and clamp it with a test that runs promise(N) then promise(N+1) and expects passage:

1. the progressed emit mints the N+1 ask as it renders it (the render IS the ask, so its clock
   starts then), or
2. the progressed emit stops at `progressed`, and prints one line: ask the next with
   `--as passed`. no guide, no path, no promise command until the ask is real

option 2 keeps "one mint per ask" (`howto.run-self-reviews`) literal; option 1 keeps the flow one
command shorter. either removes the dead command from the output.

## .why it is not done in this round

not CLEAN: this route clamps boot budgets. the fix is in the route guard's emit and its haste/
freshness clock, with its own tests, and touches no file this pr opened.
