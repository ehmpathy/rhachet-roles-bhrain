# case=1 — the driver spends an urgent top-up

feel: happy · care: critipath

## .narrative

the `friction` reviewer finds a retry path that loses a draft. the driver agrees, fixes half of it, and concedes the rest `--severity urgent`. `friction` has spent its last round. the driver re-arrives and the `reviewed?` judge holds.

the halt reads `increase budget — yours to spend`, with one command beneath it. the driver runs that command. the gate finds the live urgent concession on `friction` and grants 2 rounds. the grant names the warrant and asks for the note.

the driver writes its human one line: "1.vision bought 2 rounds on `friction`; the draft can be lost on retry." it finishes the fix and re-arrives. `friction` re-reads the fix, approves, and the stone passes. the human was never blocked.

## .bdd sketch

```
given a stone whose `friction` lane has run dry
  and the driver conceded a `friction` concern as urgent
when [t0] the driver re-arrives and the reviewed? judge holds
  then stderr shows `├─ increase budget — yours to spend`
  then stderr shows `rhx route.guard.budget --for review --add 2 --peer friction --stone 1.vision`
  then stderr shows `🟡 tell your human why this stone bought budget`
  then stderr does not show `ask your human`
when [t1] the driver runs the printed top-up
  then it exits 0
  then it shows `granted — earned by a live urgent concession` with `friction · conceded urgent`
  then it repeats `🟡 tell your human why this stone bought budget`
  then the driver writes its human one line that names the stone, the rounds, and the harm
  then the human reads it later, and is never asked to act
when [t2] the driver fixes the concern and re-arrives
  then `friction` runs again (a round was in hand)
```
