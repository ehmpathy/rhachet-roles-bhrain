# domain.term.choice.reason: diffusion

## .etymology

latin *diffundere* — *dis-* (apart) + *fundere* (to pour). **to pour apart: to spread a substance
evenly through a medium until no part of it is concentrated anywhere.**

that is the defect exactly, and it is why the word was reached for rather than coined:

| the physical sense | the prose sense |
|---|---|
| the substance is still present — none was lost | the point is still stated — the author did say it |
| it is evenly distributed, so no gradient remains | every line carries equal rank |
| you cannot extract it from any one place | the reader cannot point at the line that carries it |
| the repair is to re-concentrate, never to remove | the repair is to elevate, never to cut |

⇒ **the last row is the one that earned the word.** every rejected synonym below implies a repair by
removal, and removal is the one move that does not fix this.

## .the rejected synonyms

| word | why rejected |
|---|---|
| `burial` | implies a cover — so the repair reads as *remove the cover*, a cut. and it implies a burier, which imports intent the rule does not grade |
| `dilution` | a volume claim. a diluted thing is weaker per unit; a diffuse passage's point is exactly as strong, merely unfindable. it would collide with `condensation`, whose subject IS volume |
| `flatness` | close, and it names the symptom rather than the act. a table is flat by construction and correct, so the word would fire on its own false positive |
| `vagueness` | grades presence — a vague claim was never made precisely. this rule requires the claim be present and precise. the two are opposite defects |

🟡 **`dilution` was the near miss**, and it is worth the record: it reads well, it is the common
english word for the sense, and it would have quietly merged this rule with
`rule.require.reflexive-condensation`. the two must not merge — **a passage can be perfectly
condensed and perfectly diffuse**, which is the fact that keeps them separate rules.

## .disputes

### dispute: `dilution` — raised 2026-09-04 — status: RESOLVED (keep `diffusion`)
- raised.by  = the author, mid-draft
- claim      = `dilution` is the plainer english word and a reader meets it sooner
- counter    = it asserts the point is **weaker**, which is false — a diffuse point is exactly as
               strong. and its implied repair is a cut, which is `condensation`'s repair, so the
               two rules would read as one and the pair would collapse
- resolution = keep `diffusion`; record `dilution` as a forbidden synonym

## .evidence

### the wisher forced the word, and forced it as HALF of a pair

> `until we no longer have noise and diffusion of prose nor emjis;  🔴 ✅`

one sentence, two defects, and they are the pole pair. the sharp part is the order of consequence:
**a sweep that cuts every rank glyph does not land on clean prose — it lands here**, where no line
is elevated and the needle cannot be found.

⇒ so the emoji half owed the diffusion half, and the wisher named both in one breath because the
first repair walks you into the second. the term exists because that pair had to have a name.

### the seam against its three neighbours

`rule.forbid.obfuscation` declares the trio and the grain each one reads:

| rule | its grain | what it reads |
|---|---|---|
| `forbid.subversive-prose` | falsifiability | what is claimed |
| `forbid.diffusion` | findability | where the point sits |
| `forbid.obfuscation` | selection | what is absent |

⇒ three rules over one family of defect, deliberately (`S24` — *"as many angles as it takes"*, and
`rule.require.a-cue-is-not-a-claim`). two cues cannot disagree with each other; they can only both
miss, or one catch.

### the instance that proves the repair is not a cut

`contract.reviewer-output.md.min`, section `## .the contract` — four prose lines, each true, each
tight, and not one of them a line a reader could land on. the contract itself sat in a colon-sentence
between two bullets.

⇒ **no word in it was surplus**, so `require.brevity`, `forbid.rambles` and
`require.reflexive-condensation` all passed it. the repair added a lead blockquote and cut no word
at all.
