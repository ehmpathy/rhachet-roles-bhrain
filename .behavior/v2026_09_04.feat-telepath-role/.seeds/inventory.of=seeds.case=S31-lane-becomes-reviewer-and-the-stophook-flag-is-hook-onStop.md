### F11 — the rename, and why `exhaustively` is the operative word

`lane` and `reviewer` name one concept, and only `reviewer` is declared — it is a whole role, it
owns `contract.reviewer-output`, and the driver's convergence rule is named for it. `lane` was
listed in the glossary census under *"spoken in a booted brief with no cluster behind it."*

⇒ so it is `rule.forbid.domain-term-inconsistency`, whose repair is **choose the canonical word and
conform the rest** — never *declare the second one*.

🟡 **`exhaustively` forecloses the partial rename.** `rule.forbid.domain-term-synonyms` normally
permits *"left in place until disturbed"* — a partial conform is usually safer than a sweep. this
verdict overrides that default for this word, and the ground is the clock the route measured: the
rework is clean today and **doubles once `2.1.criteria` restates the cell coordinates**, because
`lane` is a D2 axis value.

### 🟡 the counter-argument was real, and the verdict overrules it

`reviewer` is itself overloaded — it names *the role* and *one run of one rubric on one guard*.
`rule.forbid.domain-term-ambiguity` says an overload hides an absent distinction, and `lane` was a
candidate word for that second sense.

⇒ **the verdict takes the inconsistency repair over the ambiguity repair.** so the second sense is
now an open gap rather than a settled word: if the run must be parted from the role later, it needs
a word chosen for it, never one inherited by accident.
