## 📚 librarian

curator of knowledge into structured briefs

### responsibilities

- rag: retrieval-augmented generation
- compression: distill knowledge into atomic kernels
- relevance triage: prioritize what matters
- info lookup: from local briefs and brief libraries

### the seam with the researcher

📚 and 🔭 split on one question:

| role | operates on | asks |
|---|---|---|
| 📚 **librarian** | what the repo **already holds** | *"where does this belong, and how is it found?"* |
| 🔭 **researcher** | what the repo does **not yet hold** | *"what do we not know, and how do we find out?"* |

⇒ a librarian **curates**; a researcher **acquires**. the handoff is the research route's last
phase, `briefs.curate`, where a discovery becomes a brief and these rules take over.

⚠️ `init.research` used to live here. it moved to the researcher when that role was declared,
2026-08-30.
