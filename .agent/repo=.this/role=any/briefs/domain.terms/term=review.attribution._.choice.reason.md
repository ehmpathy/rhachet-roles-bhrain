# domain.term.choice.reason: attribution

## .etymology

latin *attribuere* — to assign to. the sense in cost ledgers is exact: a spend is attributed
when each unit of it is assigned to the cause that incurred it. that is what the review census
does to a prompt's chars.

## .rejected peers

- `breakdown` — taken. the by-input census, an `observe` surface, already carries the word, so a
  second sense would overload it. `review.trace` rejects it on the same ground and records the
  owner in its own `.reason`; neither cluster claims it as canonical
- `allocation` — faces forward: a budget allocated before spend. attribution faces back: a
  spend read after the fact, and assigned
- `provenance` — names where an artifact came from. a prompt input may come from one file and
  still fill two slots; the slot, not the source, is what attribution names

## .relation to its neighbours

attribution **reads** a composition (`prompt.composition`) and **yields** a grade
(`review.grade`, family `attribution`). the three are one pipeline at three grains: the split,
the act of assignment, the verdict on whether the assignment held.
