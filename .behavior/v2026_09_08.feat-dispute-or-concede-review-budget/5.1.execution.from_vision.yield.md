## .what a driver can do

a driver declares **one stance per concern** on a peer review, rather than spends budget to
re-argue it:

```
rhx route.stone.set --stone <stone> --as conceded --with <reviewer> --about nitpick.4
rhx route.stone.set --stone <stone> --as disputed --with <reviewer> --about blocker.1 \
  --why .fulcrums/inventory.of=fulcrums.case=F00N-<slug>.md
```

- a **concede** says the reviewer is right and the driver owes the repair. it removes naught from
  the tally, so the hold stands
- a **dispute** says this ONE concern should not hold the stone. it leaves the judge's tally, and
  the `--why` fulcrum is what a human council rules on at the close

⇒ the words, their etymology, and the synonyms each forbids:
`term=route.guard.review.absorption._.choice._.md` and its two positions.

## .the contract

| the field | on | why |
|---|---|---|
| `status` gains `disputed` \| `conceded` | `PassageReport` | a stance is a passage act, never a flag on one (`F002`) |
| `reviewer` · `about` · `fulcrum` · `given` | `PassageReport`, all optional | the stance's four coordinates |
| `--with` · `--about` · `--why` | the cli | `--that` stays with `--as contemplated`, which keys on a reviewer rather than a concern |

🔴 **two unions carry the status and they are distinct** — the ledger's in
`domain.objects/Driver/PassageReport.ts`, the cli's inline in `stepRouteStoneSet.ts`. TS does not
relate them (the cli union is the narrower input, the ledger's the wider record), so a member added
to one does not reach the other.

⚠️ **`given` is what makes a stance lapse.** a stance is keyed to the slug's latest given, so a new
given lands and the key does not match — `S03`'s per-generation lapse falls out by construction
rather than needs code. *a value a refusal must PRINT is a value the record must HOLD.*

🔴 **a stance cannot be read through `getAllPassageReports`.** that operation folds
last-entry-wins **per stone**, and a stone carries many stances at once — one per concern per
reviewer — so the per-stone bucket keeps the newest and drops the rest. `getStoneReviewStances`
reads the raw ledger and folds on `(reviewer, concern, given)`. the precedent is `overruled`, which
buckets per `(stone, level)` for the same reason.

## .the three gates, in order

each refuses for its own reason with its own message. a shared message would send the driver to the
wrong next step — *"you owe an answer"* and *"no verdict stands to answer"* are opposite diagnoses
that both refuse a stance move (`case=3` `[t3]`).

| # | the gate | what it refuses |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | `assertStanceIsAnswered` | a stance before the `.taken`. names the blocker count and the exact taken path, both **derived** from `RouteGuardReviewPeerUncontemplated` |
| R2 | `assertStanceHasSubject` | a stance on a lane whose verdict does not hold the road. keys on the **verdict**, never a raw count (`F012`/`S06`); a forgiven level lands here by the same clause |
| R3 | `assertStanceIsNotContrary` | a contrary second stance on one concern. an identical repeat is idempotent; the refusal prints the extant stance, its given, and its fulcrum from **stored fields** — no `.fulcrums/` scan |

plus `--why` **required** for a dispute and **forbidden** for a concede, refused before every read.

🔴 **the thresholds come from the judge command strings**, parsed by
`getReviewedJudgeThresholds({ judges })` off the `--mechanism reviewed?` line. there is no
`guard.reviewed.allowBlockers` field. ⇒ the stance reads thresholds **through the operation the
meter uses**, so *"the stance keys on the verdict the judge computes"* holds by construction.

## .the predicate

both joints key on the **residual verdict**:

```ts
computeReviewPeerVerdict(lane, { sans: disputedConcerns }) === 'approved'
```

| the joint | what it decides |
|---|---|
| the per-generation **skip** | an `approved` residual does not gate, so the lane has no reason to run |
| the stance gate's **exit** | a lane whose verdict holds the road owes no stance; the gate has naught to ask |

🔴 **`skippedByDispute`, never a `disputed > 0` count.** the two diverge in the ordinary case: a lane
at 3 blockers with 1 disputed **still holds the road on its residual**, so it runs with its dispute
unresolved. a count-based read would promise a silence that never arrives.

## .the emits

| the surface | what it says |
|---|---|
| the stance **halt** | `formatRouteGuardReviewPeerStancePrompt` — concede first and one line, dispute second and two (`S11`, `case=2`). it names neither a budget top-up nor a human approval: the stance precedes the budget |
| the **concede** ack | `concede → fix → budget → re-arrive`. the top-up is prompted, never led with |
| the **dispute** ack | meter-**independent** — a disputed lane will not run again this generation, so a top-up buys it naught. it prints no budget command in either state |
| the guard **tree** | a `disputed` flag beside `overruled`, never a seventh `ReviewPeerVerdict` — that union is computed; a stance is declared |
| the **budget** emit | names a conceded and a disputed lane in one block (`case=9` §2) |
| the human-only **guidance** | `formatGuidanceForHumanOnlyCommand` — one list, three call sites, and the stance words land in it once |
| `setStoneAsBlocked`'s escalate check | asks whether a concern stands undeclared, because a stance is a lever the driver holds |

🔴 **the concede ack's remedy is METER-DRIVEN, and the two branches are two ACTS.** rounds left ⇒
**re-arrive**, and the budget term elides entirely — to print it would sell a round the lane does
not need. budget spent ⇒ **buy the round**, because the lane spent its last round to RAISE the
concern and has none left to CONFIRM the repair.

🔴 **the budget emit's dark row is ADDED, never an annotation.** a lane the `--peer` filter skips
produces no update record at all (`route.ts:1904-1906` `continue`), so no line exists downstream to
annotate. `disputeSkippedSlugs` is a **separate parameter**, sourced from
`getCurrentPeerMeters` → `getDisputeSkippedReviewerSlugs` — the live meters, never a guard-YAML
scan.

🟡 **`DISPUTED_NARRATIVE` and the two budget suffixes phrase the skip differently on purpose** — a
tree detail line against a suffix on an arithmetic. what is pinned is the **claim**, never the
sentence: both say `this generation`, and `this stone` would overstate the skip by every generation
past the next edit (`F004`'s exact cost).

### 🔴 the vocabulary surfaces that need NO edit, and why

the vision costed `stepRouteDrive`'s tea-pause menu as the sharpest surface — a closed three-way
headed *"you must choose one"*. **a five-member menu there is wrong.** all three drive surfaces call
`getRouteDriveBlockerMessage` **before** `formatRouteDrive` (onBoot `:214-219`, onStop `:277-291`,
direct `:380-385`), and the stance branch is live-recompute — so in every state where a stance is
owed the stance prompt renders and the menu is never reached. a member there would name a command
the driver cannot use.

⇒ **the distinction that makes this consistent with the guidance list, which DOES gain the words:**

| the block | its shape | stance members? |
|---|---|---|
| *"as a driver, you should"* | a role **VOCABULARY**, on a refusal | ✅ yes |
| *"you must choose one"* | a **NOW-MENU** | 🔴 no — a stance is pre-empted in that state |

🟡 the `route.drive` help's halt list and the `route.status.line` phase list also need no edit: the
stance prompt is a **prompt**, in the same family as the contemplate prompt, which those lists
already and correctly omit.

## .the concession halt (`S12`)

an exhaustion whose every skipped lane carries a **live** concession is the driver's own, so it says
so and names the top-up as its one remedy — the `approve as-is` tail is suppressed.

- `getStoneConcededLaneSlugs` — which exhausted lanes carry a live concession, keyed to the slug's
  latest given
- `genRouteGuardExhaustedReason` — the one builder **and** the one reader of the mark, in one file,
  so the literal written and the literal read cannot drift
- `asRouteStoneDisposition` — a concession exhaustion is `push`, never `halt(exhausted)`
- 🔴 **onStop BLOCKS the stop** here, where every other exhaustion allows it

🟡 **the level half was already structural** — `allTerminal` gates the halt and `exhausted` is
terminal-for-unlock, so a conceded l1 never holds l3 from a run. the words and the owner were the
gap.

## .the caveats a reader must act on

- 🔴 **the budget read sits BEFORE `processGuardFileBudgets`.** the annotation is advisory and the
  write is not: read first and a malformed route fails with the budget untouched; read after and the
  same failure leaves a budget raised and an emit that cannot say so. ⇒ **read-before-write is
  failfast; read-after-write is failhide.**
- 🔴 **`asRouteStoneDisposition`'s `reason` must stay OPTIONAL**, and the default is the asymmetry: a
  caller that omits it gets the human-wait answer. a missed concession costs a driver one human
  round; a falsely claimed one strands the stone with no human named.
- 🟡 **`findOneStoneByPattern` is rejected for the budget emit's stone filter** — it throws on a
  multi-match, so `--stone 5.1` would gain a throw the write path does not have. the filter reuses
  the write path's own `stone.name.startsWith(stoneName)`.
- 🟡 **a union's exhaustiveness is a property of its CONSUMERS, never its declaration.** three read
  `RouteStoneGuardBlockerType` through an `if`-cascade with a default tail, and a default tail is a
  `rule.forbid.failhide` shape by construction. ⇒ **grep the call site, never read the declaration.**

## .the scope leak, recorded rather than folded in

🔴 **the review `--goal` default flips `representative` → `exhaustive`**, wisher-directed in-session
and outside `0.wish.md`'s literal scope (`rule.forbid.scope-leaks`).

reviewers **sampled**: `rhx review --goal` defaulted to `representative`, and the route guard passes
`--goal` nowhere, so every peer review in every route ran at that default.

| the reason | the effect |
|---|---|
| the full picture → better decisions | 3 of 9 reads as three local edits; all 9 reveals the **class** |
| one round → cheaper and faster | a sample costs 3 rounds to converge on one class; the same corpus reported once converges in **one** |

🔴 **and it is what makes `--about` well-defined.** a stance targets ONE concern, which is a finite,
checkable obligation only if the given **enumerates** its concerns. under a sample the driver
declares against an unknown population and cannot know when a lane is answered.

🟡 **coverage, never severity.** the exhaustive prompt collects many locations under ONE violation,
so the guard's tally counts violations rather than locations and a wider report does not inflate the
blocker count.

## 🔴 .the absorb rename + the composition gate (2026-09-15/16, `S19` · `S20` · `F038`)

the wisher renamed the vocabulary and mandated a new gate. both landed after the body above, so the
body reasons in the OLD terms; the map:

| the body says | the shipped term |
|---|---|
| `stance` (umbrella) | **`absorption`** — the driver *absorbs* a concern; `stance` is a forbidden synonym |
| `setStoneAsStanced` | **`setStoneAsAbsorbed`** (per-concern, `--as disputed\|conceded`) |
| `contemplated` / `--as contemplated` | **`absorbed`** / `--as absorbed` (the feedback `.taken` act) |
| `setStoneAsContemplated` | **`setStoneAsGivenAbsorbed`** |
| "the contemplation gate/loop" | "the **absorb** gate/loop" |

### the composition gate — the genuinely-new mechanism

`absorb` composes TWO grains, and the coarse one is now GATED on the fine one:

- **absorb a concern** — one disposition (`disputed`\|`conceded`) with its argument (the fine grain)
- **absorb the feedback** — the `.taken` per reviewer, renamed `contemplated`→`absorbed` (the coarse grain)

⇒ **`--as absorbed` is REFUSED until every concern of that reviewer's given is absorbed**:
`feedbackAbsorbed(given) ⟺ ∀ concern : absorbed(concern)`. the authority is
`define.invariant.review.peer.absorb`.

🔴 **it fires AFTER the answer-first (`.taken`) check, never before** — a concern is absorbed via
`--as disputed|conceded`, and R1 (`assertAbsorptionIsAnswered`) refuses those until the `.taken`
exists. so the pit-of-success order is: write `.taken` → absorb each concern → absorb the feedback.
the gate lives in `setStoneAsGivenAbsorbed.ts`, inside its `status.ready` branch, folded through
`getStoneUndeclaredConcerns` (the same operation the passage gate reads, so the two grade one corpus).

🔴 **the cadence inverted, corpus-wide.** the between-rounds test assets (`answerEveryPeerGiven`) now
concede each concern BEFORE `--as absorbed`, where they previously acked then conceded — the gate
makes the old order impossible.

### the clamp

`setStoneAsGivenAbsorbed.integration.test.ts [case2]`: `--as absorbed` with the `.taken` present
but concerns un-absorbed → throws, and names each; then absorb-first → the gate opens. red before the
gate (old code returned `absorbed:true` regardless), green after.
