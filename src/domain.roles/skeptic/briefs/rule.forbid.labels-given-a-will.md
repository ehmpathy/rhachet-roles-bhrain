# rule.forbid.labels-given-a-will

> **no abstraction is said to want, decide, or act. name the part that does.**

razor: `razor=misplaced-concreteness`.

## .the shape

| 👎 | 👍 |
|---|---|
| "the system decided to retry" | "the client's retry policy re-sent it after 5s" |
| "the market wants feature X" | "4 of 6 customers asked for X" |
| "the codebase resists change" | "a change to Y touches 14 files, since Y is copied in each" |

## .the test

> **which concrete part, person, or rule did this act? can it be named?**

## .enforcement

blocker: a cause assigned to a label, where the act belongs to a part a peer could have fixed
nitpick: a will given to a label in prose, where the part is known
