> in the surface the concept happens to wear today.
> — quoted from `src/domain.roles/telepath/briefs/rule.require.distillation.md`, which declares it;
> amend it there

`define.distillation.md` beside it.

## .why it earns a cluster
the word is adopted, never coined — *domain distillation* and *system distillation* already name
this act at other scales. what the cluster settles is the **boundary**: `prose.distillation` is the
conceptual scale, and it is one act with the learner's `distill`, never a homonym of it.

## .the boundary, and why it is the whole declaration

`rule.forbid.domain-term-ambiguity` fires on one word over two concepts. it does not fire here,
and the reason is the settlement itself: these are one concept at two scales.

| the sense | boundary | what it distils |
|---|---|---|
| the learner's | `externalize` | a round's raw source into a durable brief |
| **this one** | `prose` | a concept into the fundamentals you speak in |

⇒ 🟡 **the learner's `distill` is undeclared, and this settlement makes it load-bearing** — a named
gap in the census.
