# S41 · discovery precedes distillation, and both precede elucidation

## .said

> also, we should enbrief a rule to require domain-discovery. in whatever domain you happen to
> find yourself in, the most effective mechanism to distill the fundmentals with is domain
> distillation via domain discovery. you must constantly question, what is the fundamental truth
> here? rather than the surface distraction.

> so, to be able to elucidate, one is required to discover the domain, before they can distill
> it, and elucidate it

> to domain discover, one must search for the fundamental truth

## .settled

**a new act, and it sits FIRST among the content acts.**

```
discover  →  distill  →  elucidate
```

- **discover** — what are this domain's fundamentals?
- **distill** — drill THIS claim to the root the discovery found, and condense it
- **elucidate** — transfer it until the peer can use it

⇒ the canon had the second and third and not the first, so `require.distillation` opened on a
drill with no map: *"break the concept into the fundamentals it rests on"* presumes you know what
the fundamentals ARE.

## .the two acts are distinct, and the seam is the SUBJECT

| act | its subject | its question |
|---|---|---|
| **discovery** | the DOMAIN | *what is the fundamental truth here?* |
| **distillation** | one CLAIM inside it | *what does this claim stand on?* |

⇒ discovery maps the territory; distillation drills one point in it. so a drill can run to
completion and still bottom out on a surface, where the layer beneath was a fundamental the
author never discovered.

## .the test the wisher stated

> **what is the fundamental truth here, rather than the surface distraction?**

- constant, never a phase — *"you must constantly question"*
- the failure is not a wrong answer. it is a question never asked
- ⇒ so its clamp is the extant `rule.forbid.surface-speak`, one grain up: that rule catches a
  claim pitched at its surface; this catches a DOMAIN never drilled at all

## .the pavement it must reuse, never re-derive

`ehmpathy/role=architect` already declares the act — `def.domain-discovery` +
`howto.domain-discovery`:

> *"the operative word = discover, not invent. the truth is already there, buried under surface
> mechanism/jargon/tool-shape."*

⇒ so telepath's rule is a **specialization**, per
`rule.require.specialize-a-rule-its-readers-look-past`. the seam:

| role | what it discovers FOR |
|---|---|
| architect | to NAME — dobjs, dops, ubiqlang, a term cluster |
| **telepath** | to SPEAK — you cannot elucidate a domain whose fundamentals you never found |

🟡 and the specialization is owed rather than optional: every example in the architect's brief is
a **schema, a dao, a db row**. a reader who writes PROSE looks straight past it — which is that
rule's exact trigger.
