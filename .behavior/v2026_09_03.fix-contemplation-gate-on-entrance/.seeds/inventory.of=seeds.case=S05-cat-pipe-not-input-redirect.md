the remedies are opposite:

| the cause | who fixes it |
|---|---|

⇒ **the caller's likely read is the wrong one.** *"declined"* invites *"my ask was refused"*, which
escalates to a human who has naught to grant. **read the meter before you conclude a human is needed**

form into the refused one on sight.** documented examples are not a bound — the ban must be stated.
