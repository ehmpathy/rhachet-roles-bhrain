# razor: cromwell

shaves: a claim with no owner or weight · settled by: logic

## .the question

> **have I left any room in my confidence to be wrong, as small as one in a million?**

a belief held at certain or impossible cannot move by any read. the room may be tiny; it must exist. the one razor that shaves the claimer's own certainty.

## .the source

- Oliver Cromwell to the General Assembly of the Church of Scotland, 3 Aug 1650: "I beseech you, in the bowels of Christ, think it possible that you may be mistaken"
- named a rule by Dennis Lindley
- read via https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cromwell%27s_rule

## .fails when

- it is read as a license for vague doubt. it asks for room, not for a hedge on every line
- logical truths are exempt

## .run by

`rule.always.trust-no-one-especially-not-yourself`
