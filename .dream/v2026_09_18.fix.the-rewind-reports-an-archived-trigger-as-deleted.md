## .the gap, precisely

`delStoneGuardArtifacts` now **archives** the self-review triggers rather than deletes them
(`delStoneGuardArtifacts.ts:66`, via `archiveStoneSelfReviewTriggers`). its caller still files that


⇒ so a driver who rewinds a stone with 8 live triggers reads **`deleted: … 8 triggers`** for 8 files
that sit intact under `.route/.archive/`.

## .the shape of the fix

the truthful repair is a **channel**, never a re-label — an archived count under a `deleted:` label
is wrong at any label, and to simply drop the count trades a lie for a silence
(`rule.require.status-feedback`: a mutation that does not report what changed is a blocker).

1. give the cascade item an `archived` datum beside `deleted` — the yield outcome and the trigger
   count both belong in it
2. render it as its own line under the cascade's stone, so the two verbs stay apart:
   ```
   │  ├─ deleted: 2 reviews, 1 judges, 0 promises, 0 blocked triggers
   │  ├─ archived: 1 yield, 8 selfreview triggers
   │  └─ passage: rewound
   ```
3. fold the extant `yieldPart` into that line, which retires the older half of the conflation
4. re-take `setStoneAsRewound.test.ts.snap` (11 rows) and any acceptance snapshot that renders a
   rewind cascade

🟡 **the cheap alternative is on record and was NOT taken**: rename the rendered label `deleted:` to
`cleared:` and leave the shape alone. one line plus a snapshot. it is rejected because it makes the
render disagree with the field it prints (`c.deleted`), which is
`rule.forbid.domain-term-inconsistency` traded for `rule.forbid.surprises` — a defect swapped, not
removed. the fulcrum carries the fork.

## .the lesson

> **a change of VERB — delete to archive — travels no further than the operation that made it. its
> callers keep the old word, and the old word is the one the human reads.**

the round changed what happens to a file and clamped that change in three suites. **not one of them
reads the sentence a driver sees**, because a return count is verb-neutral: `{ count: 8 }` is true
whether the eight were deleted or archived.

⇒ the transferable form: **when an operation's verb changes, grep its callers for the OLD verb, not
for its name.** the call sites compile; the prose around them lies.
