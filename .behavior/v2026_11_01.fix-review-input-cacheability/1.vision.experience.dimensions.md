# 1.vision.experience.dimensions

the experience space of a review round's input cost. three actors meet it: the **guard** sends the
round (the driver waits on it), the **reviewer** brain reads the prompt and renders the verdict, and
the **driver** reads what it cost (`rhx review._.cost`).

## .the latent axis — surface

the actor decides which other axes can vary at all. the reviewer sees one prompt, so the cache's
state is invisible to it. the driver sees a report, so the bytes are invisible to it. a single
product of actor × every lever would therefore hold whole columns of moot cells.

so the latent axis is **surface**: what an actor can observe. it is declared here, and the space
splits into one product per surface. each product crosses only the axes its surface can observe.
every cell of every product is verdicted in `1.vision.experience.case=_.md`.

## .surface S1 — the bytes the guard sends, and what the cache does with them

| # | axis | positions | closed because |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | lanes per head in the pour | `solo` · `group` (2+ lanes, one head) | a head has one lane or several |
| G2 | partitions per lane | `whole` · `split` | a lane splits or does not |
| G3 | meet outcome, this round | `leads` (holds the claim; pokes, or goes solo) · `follows` (reads a peer's warm release, or trails a peer's cold one that carried the head) · `cold` (a faulted poke, a peer's cold release, a timeout, or a spent deadline) | the meet's seven outcomes (`ReviewPrefixPrimeOutcome.ts`) map onto three paths: `primed` · `solo` → `leads`; `awaited` → `follows`; `faulted` · `cold` · `timeout` · `lapsed` → `cold` |
| G4 | prior round of this lane | `none` · `warm` (within the ~66m reach) · `lapsed` (past it) | a prior exists or not; if it does, it is within the reach or past it |
| G5 | edit since the prior | `unchanged` · `tail-edited` (a rubric or a ref) · `head-edited` (the template, or a target) | after cure 1 every input sits in exactly one of the head or the tail |
| G6 | brain route | `pinned` (a host that caches, reached by an affinity pin) · `unpinned` (the cheapest host, by the floor; replica by chance) · `unmarked` (a host that caches only when the adapter marks it, and the adapter sends no marker — C7) · `uncacheable` (a host that bills no cache) | a request reaches a cache it can hit, a cache by chance, a cache it never asks for, or no cache |

S1 is three products, not one, because its axes answer three separate questions and each question
reads only its own axes:

| product | axes | cells | the question | why its axes are orthogonal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S1.within | G1 × G2 × G3 | 2 × 2 × 3 = 12 | do the requests of ONE round share a prefix that reaches the cache before the peers arrive | G1 counts lanes per head and G2 requests per lane; a guard sets each by a separate flag (the corpus binds; `--partition`). G3 is the meet's runtime result, free of both — save one cell, `solo` × `whole` × `follows`, which has no peer to follow and is verdicted impossible by nature |
| S1.across | G4 × G5 | 3 × 3 = 9 | does this lane's prior round leave a prefix this round can read | G4 is the clock between rounds; G5 is what a human or driver edited. no edit moves the clock, and no gap edits a file — save the two `none` × edit cells, impossible by nature |
| S1.route | G6 × source (`within` · `across`) | 4 × 2 = 8 | is the cache the request lands on the one either source wrote to | the route is set by the brain choice and the adapter; the source is set by the pour and the clock. neither choice reads the other |

### .the composition law — why the three need not be crossed cell by cell

a request's cache read is the longest prefix it shares with **any** request already prefilled on the
replica it lands on (`research.review-cost-and-cache.md`). the two sources are the round's peers
(S1.within) and the lane's own prior (S1.across); the route (S1.route) gates whether either is on
the replica at all. so a cross cell's outcome is computed, not discovered:

```
read = route serves ? max(within, across) : 0
```

each term is set by a different mechanism — the meet, the reach, the host floor — so no cross cell
holds a boundary its terms do not. the law is checked at the cross cells where the max picks an
unexpected winner, each verdicted in the inventory:

| cross cell | within | across | read |
|---|---|---|---|
| `group` × `lapsed` | the corpus | 0 | the corpus, from a peer — the common case, since rounds land 1–3h apart (C5) |
| `group` × `warm` × `tail-edited` | the corpus | the corpus | the corpus — a rubric edit no longer voids it |
| `group` × `warm` × `head-edited` | the whole new head — every peer compiled the same one | up to the first changed target | the whole head, from a peer: the leader prefills the edits once, its followers read them back |
| `solo` × `lapsed` | 0 | 0 | 0 — cold by nature, named `lapsed` in the meter (`case=3`) |
| `group` × `cold` × `unpinned` | 0 | per S1.across | today's figure; never worse than today |

### .the forbidden transitions of G3

two meet behaviors an actor could attempt and the meet rejects. each is a rejection invariant
(`rule.forbid.cold-pour-of-a-shared-prefix`), shipped and clamped, and the cure moves no meet code:

- a `follows` lane pours ahead of its leader's settled poke, while the claim is live and the
  deadline allows the wait
- a prime fault blocks a lane's pour

## .surface S2 — the prompt the reviewer reads

| # | axis | positions |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | layout | `old` (rubric in the head) · `new` (rubric in the tail) |
| R2 | prompt shape | `whole` · `split` (a partition: context-only list, peer list) |
| R3 | focus | `push` (target bodies) · `pull` (a path list) |

product: 2 × 2 × 2 = 8 cells. **why orthogonal:** R1 is set by the compiler, R2 by the guard's
partition declaration, R3 by `--focus`; each is a separate input, and the compiler composes them
in separate sections, so no value of one constrains another.

an edit to the rubric or a target is not an axis here. it changes the content the reviewer reads,
which is what a review is for; it does not change whether the layout delivers that content whole.
the clamp compiles any input both ways, edited or not.

one more cell sits beside the product: **does the reviewer decide the same** under `new` as under
`old`? the product proves the content arrives whole; only a live call proves the decision, and the
wish's bounds forbid one in ci. it is verdicted as its own cell, so no reader mistakes the content
clamp for proof of the decision.

## .surface S3 — the report the driver reads

| # | axis | positions |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | report scope | `run` · `tree` · `grove` |
| D2 | view | `rate` (the cache share, full-rate tokens, cost) · `cause` (the pair verdict counts: why a pair did or did not read) |
| D4 | brain grain | `all-brains` (one figure over every brain) · `per-brain` (one row per brain slug) |

product: 3 × 2 × 2 = 12 cells. **why orthogonal:** D1 picks which runs, D2 picks which figure,
D4 picks whether the runs are keyed by brain; the report computes each figure over any run set
under either key. the cell the driver's whole question lives in is `cause` × `per-brain`: *on this
brain, did a 0% come late, or did the layout break?*

D4 is the brain grain alone, by intent. the report's other rollups — by tree, route, stone,
reviewer, provider — are extant and unchanged by this route; none is crossed here, since the wish
asks per brain and no cure touches them.

three value sets the cells render, each walked in full:

| set | positions | renders in |
|---|---|---|
| D3 prefix verdict | `served` · `missed` · `lapsed` (new) · `over-reach` · `self-cached` · `unshared` · `unmeasured` | every `cause` cell, at each scope and grain |
| D5 brain slug | `recorded` · `absent` (an older run that recorded none) | every `per-brain` cell, as its row key |
| D6 pair brains | `same` (both runs rode one brain) · `switched` (the round rode a brain its prior did not) | every `cause` × `per-brain` cell, since a pair is keyed by its round's brain |
