## .the shape of the fix

NOT a syntax swap — a conceptual re-cut. case=3's whole narrative is "the bound is scoped to the
LEVEL, so the roster is invisible to it." under the shipped model the bound is scoped to a GROUP,
and reviewers join it. so the demo's argument, its yaml, and its `[case1]`–`[case3]` given clauses
each move to `group:`/`groups:`. the F1 seed (`.seeds/`) and `term=route.guard.concurrency.group`
carry the settled shape to copy from.
