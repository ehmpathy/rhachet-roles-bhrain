# 1.vision.experience.dimensions

the experience space of a dense, budgeted role boot. four live axes, one collapsed.

| axis | positions (closed) |
|---|---|
| **actor** | `seat` — a brain that boots the role and acts on it, here · `author` — a maintainer who edits a brief or a boot.yml · `consumer` — a seat in another repo that boots the published package from `dist` · `system` — a process with no brain that runs `roles boot` or `roles cost` (ci, a test suite) |
| **moment** | `boot` — session start · `act` — the instant a cue must fire · `dispute` — the why is asked · `edit` — a brief or boot.yml changes |
| **budget at boot** | `under` — payload ≤ `budget.tokens` · `over` — payload > `budget.tokens` · `absent` — the boot.yml declares no budget, so no clamp is computed |
| **cue state** | `intact` — every cue line of every booted rule is present · `cut` — a cue line or a whole rule is absent |
| ~~**concurrency**~~ | collapsed — see below |

## two latent dimensions, surfaced

- **the budget is evaluated at `boot` only.** `act` and `dispute` happen inside a boot that
  already passed or failed its clamp, so they **inherit** the budget state of the boot behind them.
  an owned boot over budget halts (exit 2), so no `act` or `dispute` sits behind it. a `(linked)`
  boot over budget warns and proceeds, so a consumer can act and dispute over budget. that is
  the only reason `over` reaches `act` and `dispute` at all
- **a disputant is a seat at the `dispute` moment, not an actor.** doubt is a moment any brain
  reaches. so `dispute` is crossed with every actor, and no actor exists for one moment only

## the collapsed axis: concurrency / time

considered, and it collapses to one position. a boot is a pure read of a file set on disk, and
the budget check is computed per boot from that set, with no shared mutable state, no cache of
the payload, and no lock. two seats that boot at once read the same files and get the same
verdict. an edit mid-boot is an edit before or after the read, which is the `edit` moment of the
next boot. so concurrency adds no cell the live axes lack. *what would revive it:* a cached
payload, or a budget check that writes state.

a stale package in a consumer (an old `dist`) is the same: the consumer boots whatever version it
holds, and that version passed its own clamp in this repo before publish.

## actor × moment — all 16 pairs

| actor ↓ · moment → | boot | act | dispute | edit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **seat** | real | real | real | **empty by definition** — a brain that edits role briefs is, at that moment, an author |
| **author** | real — the author's own next boot | real — walks the seat's act | real — walks the seat's dispute | real |
| **consumer** | real, from `dist` | real, from `dist` | real — the `.md` in `node_modules/…/dist` | **impossible by nature** — a consumer's files are a published package; this repo's briefs are not in its tree. its own repo is out of scope |
| **system** | real — ci or a test runs `roles boot` / `roles cost` | **impossible by nature** — no brain reads a cue | **impossible by nature** — no brain asks why | **empty by definition** — a process that edits briefs runs on an author's behalf, so it is the author's edit |

⇒ 11 real pairs, 5 empty: 3 impossible by nature, and 2 empty by definition, where the actor
axis is defined by the act (an edit makes its actor an author), the same correlation that folded
the disputant into the `dispute` moment.

## three splits inside a cell, surfaced

three distinctions split one coordinate of the walk into two experiences. each applies at one
place only, so each is walked as a split of that coordinate rather than crossed with all 33:

| split | positions | applies at | why only there |
|---|---|---|---|
| **spec ownership** | `owned` (this repo's `src`) · `linked` (a published package) | budget = `over` | it decides halt (owned) vs warn (linked), and only an over-budget boot does either. seat and author are owned and consumer is linked by definition, so only `system` splits (cells 25, 27) |
| **`.md` peer** | `present` · `absent` | moment = `dispute` | only a dispute reads the `.md`. it splits every dispute cell (cells 3 / 14, 18 / 14, 20 / 26, 23 / 26) |
| **edit phase** | `rewrite` (this pr's move-out) · `later` (an edit to a `.md` cue after it) | author × edit × `under` | the move-out is where a cue is lost in bulk (cell 12); a later `.md` edit is where a min drifts (cell 13). an over-budget edit takes cell 6 whatever its phase |

the collapsed concurrency axis above is about two boots at once. edit phase is a different
quantity: when in the life of the briefs an edit lands. it does split a cell, so it is surfaced here.

after it (the wish: hold its budget only if touched).

## budget × cue — crosses every cell above

| budget | cue | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| under or absent | intact | the happy state of each cell above |
| over | intact | the cells in the `over` column above |
| under or absent | cut | cell 10 — **a guarded sharp state, not a rejection.** the boot accepts a cut min, and a cut cue costs fewer tokens, so the budget cannot see it. out-of-band checks reject it: the census in this pr (case=6), the cue snapshot test after it (F8) |
| over | cut | cell 10 as well. the halt fires on the budget, and the snapshot test on the cut |

⇒ by construction: 16 actor × moment pairs (5 empty), 33 budget cells on the 11 real pairs
(4 forbidden by inheritance, 1 forbidden by nurture as a halt (cell 9), the rest real; cells 4
and 25 are halts too, but each is an experience its actor meets, so each is real and sharp).
the three splits turn some of those cells into two, and every split position carries its own
cell number above. cue state crosses all of them, with `cut` as the one guarded sharp state
(cell 10). no axis holds an `etc.` value.
