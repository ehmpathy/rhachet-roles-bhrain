# rule.always.externalize.lessons.into_briefs

## .what

when you learn, write it down. externalize lessons into briefs before context fades.

## .when

externalize when:
- a concept was a surprise
- a concept was hard to discover
- you had to ask or research to understand
- you wish someone had told you sooner

## .why

- context fades — what's obvious now will be forgotten tomorrow
- lessons unwritten are lessons lost
- briefs compound — each one makes the next traveler faster
- the cost of capture is low; the cost of rediscovery is high

## .how

1. notice the lesson (surprise, friction, insight)
2. pause — don't wait until later
3. write a brief in `.agent/repo=.this/role=*/briefs/`
4. keep it short — future you will thank present you

## .the test

ask: "would i want to know this if i were new to this codebase?"

yes → write the brief
no → move on

## .mantra

> write it down before it fades 📜

## .enforcement

lesson learned but not externalized = violation of learner purpose

## .this is the PARENT — three zoom-ins sharpen it by lesson KIND

🟡 **this rule is named for the genus.** `externalize` is the family
(`term=externalize._.choice._.md`) and the act it governs is `enbrief` — one member of it. so a
reader who arrives here holds the general claim and not the cue that fires on their case.

| the lesson you hold is about | reach for |
|---|---|
| a **word** — a dobj/dop named, a synonym argued, a canonical term settled | `im_an.obsessive_learner.for.domain.terms` |
| an **invariant** — a must-hold rule argued against and settled | `im_an.obsessive_learner.for.domain.invariants` |
| a **glyph** — a marker claimed, changed, or found unregistered | `im_an.obsessive_learner.for.domain.glyphs` |
| any other concept that surprised you or was hard to discover | this rule, as it stands |

⇒ each zoom-in carries what this rule cannot: **the shape that kind of lesson owes.** an invariant
needs its nature/nurture label and its counter-argument; a term needs its `.reason` cluster; a
glyph needs its catalog row. the general claim *"write it down"* names not one of them.

🟡 **and capture the ARGUMENT, never merely the conclusion.** a conclusion with no argument
attached gets re-litigated — or quietly reversed by a reader who only ever saw the conclusion.
