# example: the seventh ask — a rule derived from its own violation

a demo of `rule.always.itemize-a-peers-asks-into-tasks._.md`: what it costs when a supervisor hears
every ask and emits no receipt.

## .the source

**2026-08-09** · `ehmpathy/rhachet-roles-bhrain` · seed
[#366](https://github.com/ehmpathy/rhachet-roles-bhrain/issues/366), filed 2026-08-10

## .what happened

a human sent a supervisor **six asks in rapid succession** while it was mid-task. each arrived as
an interjection; each was acknowledged only by the work eventually done.

after the sixth, the human sent a seventh:

> *"create a task for each ask and mark as you go"*

## .the tell

**the seventh ask is the whole demo.** the human had no way to see whether the prior six had
landed, so they spent a turn to ask for the receipt directly.

the supervisor had in fact heard all six. **the information existed and was simply never
surfaced** — so the failure was not comprehension, it was emission.

## .the cost

one human turn, spent on a question the supervisor's own internal state could already have
answered. that is the one resource the loop cannot make more of.
