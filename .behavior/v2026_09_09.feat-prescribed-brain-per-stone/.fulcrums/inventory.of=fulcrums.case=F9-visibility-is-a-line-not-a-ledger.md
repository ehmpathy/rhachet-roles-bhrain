# F9 — switch visibility is one output line, never a per-stone ledger

**rework** = clean · **status** = open · **confidence** = 85%

## .the fork

`case=8` makes the switch a critipath: a prescription nobody can verify is a preference, not a
control. how much record does that need?

- **a line** — the drive output names the brain and its provenance
- **a ledger** — a durable per-stone record beside `passage.jsonl`, queryable after the drive

## .taken, and why

**a line.**

- `rule.require.status-feedback` demands it and bounds it — the line satisfies it; a ledger exceeds it
- neither the wish nor the issue asks for a ledger, so one is a scope leak (`rule.forbid.scope-leaks`)
- `rule.prefer.wet-over-dry` — the durable record has one usecase today

## .the counter-case

a line lives in a transcript, and a transcript is not queryable. `case=8`'s foreman asks later —
after a defect ships, maybe after the session is gone. `passage.jsonl` is already a per-stone record,
so a brain field there is less invented than it looks.

## .rework

clean and additive — no change to the line, the guard schema, or the hook. a ledger may carry a date,
never a time (`rule.forbid.timestamps-in-route-artifacts`).
