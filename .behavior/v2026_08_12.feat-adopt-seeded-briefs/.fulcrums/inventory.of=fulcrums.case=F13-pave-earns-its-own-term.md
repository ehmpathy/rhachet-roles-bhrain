## .the test that settled it

`rule.forbid.domain-term-synonyms` asks: *is this one concept or two?* the case that separates them
is concrete rather than shaded — **a lesson retained by one clone and unavailable to the next** is
learned and unpaved. that a real case separates them is what makes them two terms.

## .what would overturn it

a reviewer who shows the beneficiary/direction split collapses on a second look — that `pave` is
`learn` seen from the reader's side rather than a distinct act. the dispute entry is already the
place for that argument.
