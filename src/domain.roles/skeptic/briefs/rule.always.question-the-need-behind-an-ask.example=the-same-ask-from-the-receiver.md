# rule.always.question-the-need-behind-an-ask.example=the-same-ask-from-the-receiver

> direction: inbound, a question that reaches the session. 2026-11-18, nheuron. the land-branch ask (inbox 2026-11-18), examined from the receiver's seat. the question was "who owns those land branches"; the need sat one layer down.

## .the shape, in any life

a neighbor leans over the fence and asks, "whose ladder is that?" what he wants is a clean gutter before the rain. what is this ask for, and is there a quicker way to that end?

the case below: the ask was "who owns those branches"; the need was a merged land by a due date, and the owner was only a means.

## .the ask

> who owns those land branches, so their ci re-runs green and they merge? item 15 is due released by 2026-11-19

## .the four layers

| layer | the read of the ask |
|---|---|
| the words | "owns" may mean the author, the seat that holds the branch, or the seat that may re-run its check. three readings, three different answers |
| the frame | the ask assumes a re-run by an owner turns the checks green. its own text says the one ci check was cancelled at 30m: a cancel at a fixed bound may recur on a re-run. a read of the check log tests the frame |
| the need | a merged land, so item 15 is released by 2026-11-19. the owner is a means to that end |
| the why of the ask | the asker holds a fallback (a merge of both branches into the fire branch) and asks only to avoid it. the question is a request to spend the receiver's turn instead of the asker's commit |

## .the answer the examination owes

- answer the need first: the two prs, their check state, and which seat may re-run a check for the robot (the receiver's recorded answer: a re-run is refused for the robot)
- answer the words: the land branches are the receiver's own
- name the frame test the asker can run: read the cancelled check's log for the reason it stopped at 30m

the answer on record covers the words and the fallback. it does not show the frame test. that is the gap the rule closes.

## .the cost

the frame went untested on both seats, so a re-run could fail at the same 30m bound and cost the same turn again.

## .the lesson, by law

- a question that asks for an owner is a request to act through the owner. the need is the act
- the frame an ask carries ("a re-run will go green") is a claim a read can test before anyone is asked
