## .narrative

field verbatim, and the rubric line has no carve-out for quotes. the driver is 95% sure the
reviewer misfired — and the misfire will recur in every repo that quotes an api.

it tries the dispute with no reflection. refused: at ≥ 0.90 a dispute is not a question for a human,
it is a defect report against the rubric, and the report is owed upstream. the refusal names the
rule file and the dispatch command.

the driver dispatches a task to the rubric's owner, gets back `ehmpathy/rhachet-roles-ehmpathy#812`,
and retries with `--reflect`. accepted. the fulcrum says `owed: reflect, reflect:
ehmpathy/rhachet-roles-ehmpathy#812`. no halt here, and no halt later: the next human gate shows it
as informational only, never as a line that blocks approval (R9).
