kind: repair · owner: rhachet-roles-bhrain · caught 2026-10-10

## .the cue that fired

i040 r030 nitpick.2 (`v2026_09_20.review-cost-meter`, stone 5.1): the conversation expander
re-parsed the peer-review filename by hand. that half was repaired by a declared conversation
grammar; the `taken` name it named still has no declared composer.

## .the gap, precisely

`._.taken.by_self.` is composed or parsed by hand in four prod modules, none tied to
`asPeerReviewOutputName`:

- `src/domain.operations/route/guard/review/peer/getRouteGuardReviewPeerPathTaken.ts` — composes by an infix swap (`INFIX_TAKEN`)
- `.../peer/getRouteGuardReviewPeerPathMeta.ts` — parses the name
- `.../peer/asPeerReviewSlugFromPath.ts` — parses the slug
- `.../peer/enumRouteGuardReviewPeerFiles.ts` — globs it

a rename of the grammar turns `asPeerReviewOutputName.test.ts` and
`asPeerConversationFileParts.test.ts` red, and leaves these four silently wrong — on the gate
that decides whether a driver may re-arrive.

## .the shape of the fix

1. widen `asPeerReviewOutputName`'s `kind` with `taken` (tail `._.taken.by_self.$slug.md`);
   the composer then names the side, not only the tail
2. `getRouteGuardReviewPeerPathTaken` = parse the given via `asPeerConversationFileParts`,
   then compose with `kind: 'taken'`
3. `getRouteGuardReviewPeerPathMeta` and `asPeerReviewSlugFromPath` read
   `asPeerConversationFileParts`
4. `enumRouteGuardReviewPeerFiles` builds its glob from `asPeerReviewOutputName` with
   wildcard parts, as the report glob in the expander already does
5. a round-trip clamp per kind, red when any half parts

## .why it is not done in this round

SAFE — yes. CLEAN — no: four modules on the absorption gate, plus their tests, ripple past a
`better` nitpick's diff. fulcrum F214 records the call.
