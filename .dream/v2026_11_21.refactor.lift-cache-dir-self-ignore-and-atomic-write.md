# 🌙 dream: lift the cache-dir self-ignore and the atomic temp+rename write to one shared leaf

- kind: refactor
- owner: rhachet-roles-bhrain (driver route ops)
- caught 2026-11-21

## .the cue that fired

the `has-consistent-mechanisms` self review of the one-per-clone reminder found two writers of clone-local state under `.agent/.cache/` that each carry their own copy of two small mechanisms.

## .the gap, precisely

- the `*` `.gitignore` findsert: `findsertCacheDirGitignore` (private, sync) in `statusLine/cache/setRouteStatusLineCache.ts`, and `setRouteReminderHandleDir` (async) in `reminder/`
- the atomic temp file + rename: the per-process temp path is one shared leaf (`route/asTempPathBeside.ts`), but the write + rename + temp cleanup is still a copy in each of `setRouteStatusLineCache.ts` (sync) and `reminder/setRouteReminderFocus.ts` (async)

two copies, a few lines each. a third clone-local cache writer would make three.

## .the shape of the fix

- one leaf at `src/domain.operations/route/` (their common ancestor), e.g. `setCacheDirSelfIgnored({ dirPath })` and `setFileAtomically({ filePath, content })`, each in sync and async faces or async only
- the status-line repaint path is sync by design (it renders inside a status-line hook), so either keep a sync face, or confirm an async one costs naught there
- re-point both callers; their tests already clamp the behavior (`setRouteStatusLineCache.integration`, the reminder focus cases)

## .why it is not done in this round

it is not clean: it ripples into the status-line domain and forces a sync-vs-async decision on its hot repaint path, outside this wish's scope. at two copies, `rule.prefer.wet-over-dry` holds the lift until a third appears.
