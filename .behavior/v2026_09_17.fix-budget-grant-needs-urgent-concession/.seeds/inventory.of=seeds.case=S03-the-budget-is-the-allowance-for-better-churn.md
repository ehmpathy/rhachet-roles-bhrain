# seed S03 — the budget IS the allowance for `better` churn

**kind:** settlement · **settles:** `F14`, and the yield's § *what is awkward* §0b

## .said

> the budget is the budget for a reason. right? we allow up to $budget churns of better, until we
> subsequently only care about urgent

## .settled

> **the `better` churn is not a defect. it is what the budget is FOR.**

a lane that raises taste, round after round, and a driver that fixes each point, is **the intended
use of the allowance the route author set**. the budget is not a cap on waste — it is a **paid
window** in which taste counts.

⇒ and the rule that follows is one sentence:

> **inside the budget, `better` is enough. past the budget, only `urgent` is.**

| the meter | what counts |
|---|---|
| rounds remain | **any concession.** `better` is a full citizen here — this is the window the route author bought |
| the meter is spent | 🔴 **`urgent` alone.** taste has had its allowance, and it is over |

### 🔴 what this REVERSES in the vision

the vision argued `F14` as a **gap**: the wish's cue is the nitpick loop, `allowNitpicks` defaults
to zero, so the gate stops the driver one step **after** the loop begins and every round before
exhaustion is unrecovered spend. it recommended *"both, in order"* — dissolve the loop by a default
change, **and** bound the lever.

🔴 **the settlement refuses the first half, and refuses the premise under it.** the rounds before
exhaustion are not unrecovered spend. they are **the budget, spent as designed.** there is no loop
to dissolve; there is a window, and a bound at the end of it.

⇒ so `allowNitpicks = 0` is not the floor's absence. **the floor is the meter**, and it was there
the whole time.

### 🟡 what it does NOT settle

it says naught about **how far one `urgent` warrant reaches** once the window closes — `F10`'s
stone-wide hole, `F12`'s prefix, `F07`'s re-grade. those bound the gate **past** the meter, and this
settlement governs the meter itself.
