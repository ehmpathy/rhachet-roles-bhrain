## .the pros and cons

### A — drop the timer

**pros**
- **zero elapsed friction.** satisfies `S04`'s *"eliminate"* clause outright, with no residual
- the wisher's premise in `S01`: *"brains have gotten way better since that feature was released"* —
  the population the friction was tuned for has changed
- the **two measured levers survive untouched** — forced articulation (`d = 0.55`) and situational
  cues (`d = 0.65`). only the unmeasured one is dropped
- **simplest code.** the threshold, the elapsed reads, and the rush/first split all fall out
- 🔴 the friction rows of `research.selfreview-effectiveness`'s own design table argue against
  today's timer more than for it: *"target interventions — only confront on repeated rapid
  attempts"* and *"preserve autonomy — guide but don't block"*

**cons**
- the gate reduces to **"a fresh file exists at a path"**, which is cheap to satisfy without a
  review. a one-line `looks good` clears it in two seconds
- 🔴 the `patience, friend` prompt — the artifact that carries **both** measured levers — is
  rendered only on a challenge. drop the challenge and the strongest lever in the system is never
  displayed. **A does not merely remove friction; it removes the delivery vehicle for the
  encouragement `S04` asks to keep**
- it concedes `S04`'s second clause with no replacement, and with no measurement to say the
  concession is safe
