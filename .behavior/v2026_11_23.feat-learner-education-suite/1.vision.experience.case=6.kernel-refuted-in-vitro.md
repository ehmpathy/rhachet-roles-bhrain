# case=6: a kernel refuted in vitro stays out of the role

inventory row: d3. feel: sharp. care: critipath — vitro exists to catch this; a refuted kernel that enters the role teaches it a falsehood every boot.

## .narrative

at `2.2.greats.read` the student distills a kernel from one great: *"a client-side retry with exponential backoff makes a write safe."* at `3.practice.vitro` it builds the toy consumer in a temp repo, replays one message with backoff, and still sees two rows. the kernel is refuted: backoff spaces the retries; it does not dedupe them.

the `3.practice.vitro` yield records it, with the evidence that refuted it:

```
| claim | verdict | evidence |
|---|---|---|
| k3 natural-key upsert dedupes a replay | held | replay ×3 → 1 row |
| k7 backoff alone makes a write safe | refuted | replay ×3 with backoff → 2 rows; backoff spaces retries, never dedupes |
```

the `4.1.entool.blueprint` stone reads the vitro yield and plans briefs from held kernels only. k7 appears in no brief. the pedigree entry lists k7 as `refuted`, and names the course route, whose vitro yield holds the evidence, so a later reader of the resume sees which advice failed here and can read why.

🟡 the entry carries the verdict, not the evidence text; the evidence stays in the course route's vitro yield. a fold of the evidence into the entry is held in fulcrum F20.
