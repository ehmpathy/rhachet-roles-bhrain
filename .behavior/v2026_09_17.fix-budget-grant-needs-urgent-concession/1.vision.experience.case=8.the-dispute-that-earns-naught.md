🔴 **the two clauses that must ship, or the driver escalates.** *"already on the board"* tells the
driver the argument was not lost, and *"no longer holds the stone"* tells it the passage is open.
without both, a driver reads the refusal as a wall and runs `--as blocked` on a stone that would
have passed — the exact false escalation `case=3` guards against, arrived at by a different road.

**the aha:** *a dispute buys a council's read, never a reviewer's re-run. i had already been paid —
in the currency a dispute is denominated in.*

## 🔴 .why it is not `case=1` — three differences, and the first is by construction

| | `case=1` — B = `none` | `case=8` — B = `disputed` |
|---|---|---|
| the reason line | *no concession stands* | *a dispute concedes naught, so it carries no severity* |
| **can this cell reach the lift?** | ✅ yes — absorb `--severity urgent` | 🔴 **no, by construction** — `--severity` is FORBIDDEN on a dispute |
| the remedy's step 1 | absorb the concern the lane raised | absorb the **residual** concerns; this one is already shed |
| the aha | *the round was never worth its price* | *i was already paid, in another currency* |

🔴 **row 2 is the structural fact and it is unique to this cell.**
`setStoneAsConcernAbsorbed.ts:92-101` refuses `--severity` on a dispute outright — *"a dispute
concedes naught, so it has none."* ⇒ B = `disputed` is not a rung below B = `urgent`; the two are
**disjoint by the absorb operation's own contract**, and no re-grade walks from one to the other.

⇒ **so this cell has a property `case=1` does not: the `F07` loophole cannot open here.** a driver
refused at `none` may absorb at `urgent` and proceed (`case=3` `[t3]`). a driver refused at
`disputed` must first **withdraw the dispute**, which is a contrary stance against one concern and
one given — the move the predecessor's `F09` already forbids.

⚠️ **that makes this the one refused cell the gate holds unconditionally**, and it is the strongest
evidence the design has that the bound is not merely a claim on the record.

## .why it fails safe

**loud** — the refusal names the dispute by slug and concern, so the driver sees which of its own
written stances produced the refusal · **teaches the fix** — two numbered steps, both the driver's
own, with `--as passed` as the terminus · **prevents the false escalation** — *"already on the
board"* and *"no longer holds the stone"* are the two clauses that stop a `--as blocked` ·
**unreachable lift** — `[t2]` and `[t5]` close both roads from a dispute to a grant, by construction.

🔴 **the failure mode this cell must avoid is a driver who reads the refusal as the council's
verdict.** it is not — the dispute stands, unruled, and the route's fulcrum board carries it to the
close. the refusal's *why* block says so in as many words, and a copy change that drops that clause
turns a procedural refusal into an apparent rejection of the argument.
