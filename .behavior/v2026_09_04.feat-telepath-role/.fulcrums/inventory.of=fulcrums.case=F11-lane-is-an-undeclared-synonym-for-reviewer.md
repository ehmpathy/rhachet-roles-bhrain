## .this is a synonym, not a gap — and the two have different repairs

`reviewer` is *declared*: it is a whole role (`.agent/repo=bhrain/role=reviewer/`), it owns `contract.reviewer-output`, and the driver's own convergence rule is `rule.always.converge-with-reviewers`.

`lane` is *not*. this repo's glossary carries it as its own census row — *"an undeclared synonym … it names what `reviewer` names, so the repair is choose one word"* — filed against `term=route.guard.review.reviewer._.choice.reason.md`.

⇒ so the repair is *not* *"write the absent cluster."* two words for one concept is `rule.forbid.domain-term-inconsistency`, and its stated repair is **choose the canonical word and conform the rest** — not declare the second one.

## 🟡 .the one real argument for B

`reviewer` is itself **overloaded** — it names two concepts:

| the concept | what it is |
|---|---|
| the **role** | `repo=bhrain/role=reviewer` — briefs, skills, a rubric contract |
| **one run of one rubric on one guard** | `r1: dimensional-decomposition` — one of several, on one stone, at one iteration |

⇒ `rule.forbid.domain-term-ambiguity` says an overload *"hides an ABSENT DISTINCTION"* — and its repair is a distinct word for the second sense. **`lane` is a candidate for exactly that.**

🟡 **so the wisher's question has a second, better answer than *"yes, a peer reviewer."*** it is: *"yes — and if you want the run parted from the role, the run needs a declared word, and `lane` is one."*

**that is the actual fork, and it is theirs.** either way the status quo — two words, neither declared — is the one option that is wrong.
