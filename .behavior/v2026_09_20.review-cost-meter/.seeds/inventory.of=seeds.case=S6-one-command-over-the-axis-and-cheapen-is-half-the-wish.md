## .settled

### the surface is ONE command over the `breadth` axis

```
rhx review._.cost --of run|tree|grove
```

two commands that differ **only** in one axis position are the axis, spelled as a namespace.
the proof it was wrong: the axis has **three** positions and the command set expressed
**two** — `run` had no surface at all, because a per-run figure was an artifact nobody could
ask for by name.

and `_` is not decoration. `src/contract/cli/index.ts` carries `review` as a **leaf** beside
`reviewBy`, `reviewMeter`, `reviewCorpus`, `reviewFleet` — flattened to camelCase because
`review` is already taken, where `route` nests cleanly as a pure namespace. ⇒ **the flat set
IS the collision**, already shipped; `_` marks the subject itself as a peer of its parts and
dissolves it.

🟡 the **verb** stays open. `F1`'s argument demanded one; the ruled shape has none.

### 🔴 the vision buried half its own wish

the wish is **MEASURE → ATTRIBUTE → CHEAPEN**. the third desire changes what `rhx review`
sends — four shipped levers, three of the eight bars. the vision described **three
instruments** and left the tune to appear only as caveats in cons and open questions.

⇒ so a reader took this branch for a metrics project. the instruments exist **to serve the
tune**: the hit rate is the defect, the levers are the repair, and the meter is how a lever
is known to have worked rather than merely to have sounded right.
