## .settled

- the mechanism is a separate cheap brain, an instinct, not a word pattern: it reads only what the main brain said, plus the role's briefs, and is asked which claims should be skepticized
- it flags claims that were not skepticized, or that state no basis where one is expected
- the duality teaches the main brain to state the basis of its claims
- bhrain ships it as its own role, `role=subconscious`: a daemon that makes instinct decisions; skeptic is a consumer of it
- it must be cheap and highly cached; its inputs are fed the way `rhx review` feeds reviewer brains
- skepticized claims are recorded in a `.skepticism/` directory (under the route, or similar); the main brain cites which records back a claim, and the subconscious feeds those citations into the decision
- the `.skepticism/` records are a durable record of skepticism: they outlive the turn and the session
