# 🌙 dream: one halt is punctuated two ways, split cleanly by surface

- **kind** = fix
- **owner** = `ehmpathy/rhachet-roles-bhrain`
- **caught** 2026-09-05, at `5.3.verification` i017 r011 (`enroll-verif-snapshot-blemishes`)

## .the cue that fired

a snapshot-blemish lane read the `.snap` corpus directly and found the **same halt line** rendered
with two punctuations, split by which surface prints it:

| surface | renderer | the line |
|---|---|---|
| `route.stone.set` — the live round | `src/domain.operations/route/guard/genContextCliEmit.ts` | `└─ halted: ${reason}` |
| `route.drive` — the replay | `formatRouteDriveMixedHalt.ts`, `formatRouteDriveBudgetExhausted.ts` | `└─ halted, ${reason}` |

each surface is **internally consistent**, which is why no reviewer caught it until one read the
corpus rather than a diff. the two forms even appear in the **same snapshot file** —
`driver.route.peer-budget-multilevel.journey.acceptance.test.ts.snap` carries both, in different
steps of the one journey.

⚠️ **it cited `:199` and `:313` until 2026-09-08, and a snapshot is the most fused surface there
is** — a resnap rewrites every line number in the file at once. ⇒ **a `:NN` into a generated file is
a citation with a scheduled expiry.**

## .the gap, precisely

a driver who moves between `route.stone.set` output and `route.drive` output sees one concept
worded two ways. `rule.forbid.ambiguous-labels` (ergonomist) asks for *"same word, same action,
everywhere"*; nielsen's consistency heuristic is the ground.

it is a **blemish**, not a defect: both forms are legible, and neither misroutes a driver. the
cost is a small re-read on every surface change.

## 🔴 .a second blemish rides the SAME six sites — the halt glyph

the register (`catalog.of=glyph.axis=halt.md`) claims a glyph per halt kind: 👋 approval owed **or**
a reviewer exhausted · ✋ blocked · 💥 malfunction · 🌙 skipped. **one site carries its glyph and
five do not:**

| site | header | owed |
|---|---|---|
| `stepRouteDrive.ts` (malfunction) | `└─ 💥 halted, guard malfunction` | ✅ carries it |
| `stepRouteDrive.ts` | `halted, stone marked blocked` | ✋ |
| `getRouteDriveBlockerMessage.ts` | `halted, human approval required` | 👋 |
| `formatRouteDriveBudgetExhausted.ts` | `halted, peer reviewer budget exhausted` | 👋 |
| `formatRouteDriveMixedHalt.ts` | `halted, ${reason}` | **composed** — thread the glyph from whatever computes the reason |
| `genContextCliEmit.ts` | `halted: ${reason}` | **composed**, same |
| `goal.ts` | `halted, triage required` | 🔴 **no row claims it** — an arrear in the register |

⚠️ **each row carried a `:NN` until 2026-09-08, and every one was redundant** — the `header` column
beside it is a **literal string a grep finds**, so the number added a way to be wrong and no way to be
found. ⇒ **the sharpest case for cue 10 in the whole set: the symbol was already in the table.**

⚠️ **the majority-count is the wrong instrument here, and it inverts the repair.** one marked header
beside five bare ones reads as *one site to strip* — that read was acted on once and reverted
(`term=glyph._.choice.reason.md` `.evidence`). **a register makes absence the defect, never
presence**, so the five are owed markers and the one is correct.

⇒ it rides here rather than in its own dream because it is the **same six sites** and the **same
repo-wide resnap**. two dreams over one resnap is a braid.

## .the shape of the fix

pick **one** form and unify. the comma form has the larger footprint (the whole `drive/` family,
`stepRouteDrive`, `asStatusLine`) and it reads better in the halt sentences that follow it
(`halted, peer reviewer budget exhausted`), so the cheaper and clearer direction is:

1. change `genContextCliEmit.ts` from `halted: ${reason}` to `halted, ${reason}`
2. resnap `src/contract/cli/__snapshots__/routeStoneSetContemplation.acceptance.test.ts.snap` and
   every `blackbox` snapshot that pins the `route.stone.set` halt surface
3. add the unified form to whichever brief pins halt copy, so the next author does not re-split it
4. give the five bare halt headers their register glyphs (table above); for the two **composed**
   sites, thread the glyph beside the reason rather than hardcode one
5. claim a row for `triage required`, or re-word it to a halt kind the register already holds

⚠️ **the resnap is the real cost.** `genContextCliEmit` is the live-round emitter, so it fans out
across the `blackbox` corpus well past any one route's diff.

## .why it is not done in this round

**safe? yes. clean? no.** the edit is one character; the blast radius is a repo-wide resnap of the
live-round surface. that is the same reason the *"two snapshot dialects"* and *"hash-mask"*
blemishes were shelved at i016, and it is the same fence the wisher drew.

⇒ and the reviewer that raised it **recommended exactly this**: *"catch it as a dream rather than
fix now."* the deferral is the reviewer's own call, not a driver's convenience.
