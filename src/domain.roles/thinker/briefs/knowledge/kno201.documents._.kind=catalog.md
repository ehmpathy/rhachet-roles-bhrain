# 🧩 comparative catalog of document archetypes

| **archetype** | **what** | **forms, examples, role** |
|---------------|----------|---------------------------|
| 📖 **article**   | defines *what a concept is* | `kno201.documents.articles.kind=article.md` |
| 🗂️ **catalog**   | organizes *how concepts/instances relate* | `kno201.documents.catalogs.kind=article.md` |
| 🎬 **demo**      | exemplifies *how an instance shows a concept* | `kno201.documents.demos.kind=article.md` |
| 🎓 **lesson**    | teaches *how a concept is learned/practiced* | `kno201.documents.lessons.kind=article.md` |

## ✨ insight
- 📖 **articles define**
- 🗂️ **catalogs organize**
- 🎬 **demos exemplify**
- 🎓 **lessons teach**

each archetype materializes concepts from instances with a distinct treatment style; together they form a complementary system of knowledge.
