## .settled

**the `rN` level in the self-review articulation path is a failure that repeats across drivers, not
a one-off.** a driver cannot compute the level, guesses it, and writes to a path the guard does not
read.

⇒ the level is **guard-assigned** (`howto.run-self-reviews` calls this *"the level pitfall"*), and
the guard's own two emits disagree on its value — so *"copy the path verbatim"* is advice a driver
can follow and still land wrong.

🔴 **so the `rN` level earns a cell of its own, not a footnote on `c9`.** `c9` assumed the driver
chose the wrong path; this says the path is hard to get right **by construction**, for everyone.
