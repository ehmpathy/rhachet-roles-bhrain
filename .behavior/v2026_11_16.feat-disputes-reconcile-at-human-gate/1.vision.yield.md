# 1.vision — disputes reconcile with a human at a human gate

## .the outcome world

a driver who disagrees with a reviewer says so, on one concern, and drives on. the disagreement does
not vanish into a file the human never opens. it rides, as structured data, to the first place a
human looks: the approval halt. there it is a named line — case, concerns held, confidence, the
driver's lean — and approval waits until the human has ruled on each.

the drive keeps its autonomy: a stone with no human gate never halts on a dispute. the drive keeps
its cost floor: a dispute never buys a review round. and a dispute the driver is sure the reviewer
**misread** (≥ 90%, on the wrong ground) stops to be a question for this human and becomes a defect
report for the rubric's owner, so the same misfire stops in every repo. a sure dispute on the dirty
ground — the reviewer is right, the fix costs too much — is a scope call, and still goes to the human.

### .before / after

| | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| a dispute on an ungated stone | recorded, stone passes | same — plus `owed: human` in the fulcrum, carried forward |
| the approval halt | `halted, human approval required` and the ask — no word of any dispute | the same branch, with `N disputes owe your verdict` beneath it, one line each, before the ask |
| `--as approved` with disputes unruled | goes through | refused, names each dispute and the command to rule on it |
| a 95% dispute, wrong ground | recorded like any other | refused without `--reflect <owner/repo>#N` — the rubric fix is dispatched upstream |
| a 95% dispute, dirty ground | recorded like any other | `owed: human`, no `--reflect` (refused if given) — the human rules at the gate (F4) |
| a fulcrum file | free prose; summary row typed by hand | frontmatter is the record; the summary row is derived; onStop holds a file without it |
| budget after a dispute | none (already) | none — now enbriefed and clamped |

### .the aha

the incident's human approved what he was shown. two of the three defects he then found by hand sat
in a fulcrum the halt never named. **the fix is not to make the human read more — it is to make the
halt show what was left to him.** the same halt, with two more lines, would have pointed him straight
at the table.

### .a day in the life

at dawn: the driver drives `3.blueprint` (no human gate), disputes a name concern at 70% onto F12,
passes, drives on (`case=1`). a 95% gerund misfire on `5.1` is refused until the driver dispatches a
rubric fix and passes `--reflect` (`case=3`). a fulcrum it wrote in a hurry lacks frontmatter; its
turn's end holds and names the absent fields (`case=5`). by afternoon: `5.3.verification` halts for
approval. the halt lists F12 (raised on `3.blueprint`) and F44. the human upholds F12, concedes F44,
the driver repairs, the human approves (`case=2`, `case=4`). the gerund misfire shows as "reflected
upstream", never as a line that blocks.

## .the contract, as proposed

| surface | change |
|---|---|
| fulcrum file | yaml frontmatter, one canonical list (the same as case=5): required `case · rework · ground · confidence · status · owed · holds · lean · verdict`; optional `reflect` (required when `owed: reflect`). `ground: wrong \| dirty` (F4). `owed` derived from `ground × band` |
| verdict provenance | the lever writes `verdict` AND appends a `PassageReport` row; the onStop linter refuses a `verdict` with no matched row — so a driver's hand edit of the yaml cannot fake a human verdict (R13) |
| `route.stone.set --as disputed` | reads `confidence` from the `--why` fulcrum (F1); at ≥ 0.90 requires `--reflect <owner/repo>#N`; links the fulcrum, restates none of it; never halts |
| the `approved?` halt | lists every open `owed: human` fulcrum on the route (F3), each as a branch; lists reflect-owed ones apart, informational |
| `route.stone.set --as approved` · `--as forced` | both refused while any `owed: human` fulcrum has `verdict: null`. `forced` is approve + overrule (`setStoneAsForced.ts`), so it must not be a side door. the refusal renders the full dispute lines, not only the case ids: this refusal is the surface the human surely reads — the drive halt is read by the driver |
| an unreadable held fulcrum at the gate | listed as `unreadable, owes repair`, never skipped; approval refused until it reads. a silent skip is the incident rebuilt (R45) |
| one render | the dispute lines come from one formatter, shared by the drive halt (`formatRouteDriveNeedsApproval`), the judge (`judgeApproved`), and the approval refusal |
| fulcrum `confidence` | the weakest call the fulcrum holds, never an average. a table of rows at 55–90% is a 0.55 fulcrum — the incident's exact shape |
| fulcrum `lean` | why it is in the list above: the halt shows the driver's lean, and the wish's frontmatter has no field for it |
| human verdict lever | `rhx route.fulcrum.rule --case F44 --as upheld|conceded`, human-only (F2) |
| onStop | a fulcrum frontmatter linter; exit 2 names the file and absent fields. the backstop for every fulcrum, disputed or not |
| `--as disputed`, at write | refuses a `--why` fulcrum whose frontmatter is absent or invalid — the cheapest moment to catch it, since F1 already reads it there; the same field list and fix as the linter, one formatter |
| budget meter | unchanged; a clamp proves a dispute is no warrant and triggers no re-run |
| briefs | six `define.invariant.*` in the driver role; `rule.require.deferrals-accrue-in-the-blocker-list` + `rule.forbid.deferrals-hidden-from-the-human`; `rule.always.itemize-the-fulcrums-you-best-guess` updated for frontmatter |

## .mental model

- to a friend: *"when the bot disagrees with its reviewer, it can carry on — but it has to put the
  disagreement on the sign-off sheet, and I can't sign until I've checked each one."*
- analogy: **a contractor's punch list.** the contractor may defer an item ("the inspector misread the
  code"), but the item goes on the list the owner signs, never in a drawer. an item the contractor is
  certain about goes back to the code body as an appeal, not to the owner.
- terms: the human says *"the stuff the bot pushed back on"*; we say **dispute**, **fulcrum**,
  **verdict**, **owed**. the halt line should open with the plain words: *"disputes owe your verdict"*.

## .evaluation

- goal coverage: each of the six invariants has a demo, a named enforcer, and a clamp (`case=_`)
- pros: closes the exact incident; keeps ungated stones autonomous; turns sure disputes into upstream
  repairs; makes the fulcrum machine-readable, which every later tool can reuse
- cons: one more skill (F2); every fulcrum write grows a frontmatter block; a late gate on a long
  route may list many disputes (F3)
- pit of success:
  - the human cannot approve past an unruled dispute — the refusal names each and the command
  - the driver cannot hide a dispute — the halt derives its lines from frontmatter, not from the
    driver's prose (invariant 4)
  - the driver cannot omit the record — onStop holds (invariant 5)
  - only a human writes `verdict` — the lever reuses `getDecisionIsCallerHuman`

## .what is awkward

- **🔴 a settled term invariant is reversed.** `term=route.guard.review.absorption.dispute` says no
  gate reads the fulcrum's content. invariant 2 makes the approval gate read its frontmatter. the
  wish wins (it is the later, explicit settlement), but the term's `.reason` owes a dated dispute
  entry, RESOLVED, that names this route — never a silent edit (`howto.domain-term-disputes`)
- **🔴 invariant 3 assumes the reviewer misfired.** a dispute has two grounds: WRONG, or MASSIVELY
  DIRTY. a 95% "dirty" dispute — the reviewer is right, the fix costs too much — has no rubric to
  repair, so `--reflect` has no honest target. best guess: a dirty dispute is `owed: human` at any
  confidence, because the call is a scope call the human owns. **validate with the wisher** (Q4)
- **two lines, 90% and 93%.** a fulcrum is itemized under 93%; a dispute owes a human under 90%. they
  answer different questions (record it vs. who settles it), so both stand — but a reader will ask
- **the fulcrum `status` drifts.** the brief's `best-guessed → wisher-ruled` and the wish's
  `open | held | ruled | superseded` must converge to one closed set, in the brief update
- **two homes for confidence.** the wish shapes the flag `--confidence` and also the frontmatter
  field. F1 takes the frontmatter alone; the wish's flag form may be what the wisher wants for audit
- **`conceded` means two things.** a driver concedes a concern; a human concedes a dispute (rules
  the driver wrong). same word, two actors, opposite parties. the lever's `--as conceded` should
  perhaps be `--as overturned` — `rule.forbid.domain-term-ambiguity`. raised for the criteria stone
- **the 0.90 line is a self-grade.** a driver who wants to skip the human can grade 0.91 and owe a
  reflection instead. the reflection's cost (a real upstream issue) is the brake; a misuse is
  visible in the upstream tracker, not at the gate
- **a late gate can pile up.** a long ungated stretch drains every dispute onto one halt (F3)
