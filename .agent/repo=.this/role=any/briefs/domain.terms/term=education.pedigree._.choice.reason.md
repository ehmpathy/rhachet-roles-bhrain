# domain.term.reason: education.pedigree

## .etymology

a pedigree is a recorded lineage: who came before, in order. a role's pedigree names the greats
it learned from and the courses that built on one another, which is a lineage of teachers, not a
list of jobs. it is the wish's own word, and it pairs with `education.pursuit`: the pursuit is the
activity, the pedigree is its record (seed S6).

rejected:
- `resume`, `cv` — a self-authored claim of experience; a pedigree is stamped from proof (yields
  and passages), never written by hand
- `transcript` — grades per class; it omits the greats, the lineage the pedigree exists to show
- `logbook` — a chronology of events; a pedigree holds only graduated pursuits
- `record` — generic english; it names no concept on its own

## .evidence

- zero matches org-wide before this suite, so no overload
- the pedigree is the one artifact a role boots that says where its briefs came from, so a
  director can audit a role's education without a read of every route

## .invariants

- an entry exists only for a pursuit whose every course has exited
- a re-stamp converges: same entry, same index, no twin row
- the index lists every entry the role holds, rebuilt whole on each stamp
