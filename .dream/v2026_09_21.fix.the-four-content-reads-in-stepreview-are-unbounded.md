## 🔴 .the amendment — this dream argued the wrong subject, and that is why it stalled

every line below reasons about **heap**, and the heap is not what a concurrency bound touches
here. `stepReview` RETAINS all four arrays at once (`stepReview.ts:705-708` hands rules,
targets, refs, and conversation to `compileReviewPrompt` together), so **the peak is set by
RETENTION and no wave width moves it.**

⇒ the 2gb precedent does not transfer for the reason this dream assumed. `getAllReviewRunMeters`
bounds at 8 because *"each is DISCARDED the moment its chars are counted"* — **the wave is what
frees the last one.** that mechanism is absent here.

so the hazard splits, and only one half was ever a bound's business:

| half | repair | status |
|---|---|---|
| descriptors + threadpool | a bounded wave — SAME subject as `getAllTargetsTouchedAt`, so **64 is one justification reused, not a guess** | ✅ **DONE** — `getAllFileContentsBounded.ts`, one communicator at four sites |
| 🔴 heap | a fold that counts and discards, or a prompt compiled by stream | 🔴 **OPEN** — and `.what to measure first` below still applies to THIS half only |

🔴 **the `SAFE — no / CLEAN — no` below was answered against the heap premise, and does not
survive it.** once the subject is descriptors, the number has a source, the template is in the
package, and the change touches one function — which is safe and clean by the test.

🟡 **the clamp found a second defect en route**: `[case3]` of
`getAllFileContentsBounded.integration.test.ts` went red first, because the inherited
`if (!(error instanceof Error)) throw error` guard reads **false across module realms** and let
a raw `ENOENT` escape unwrapped. `failloud` had been silently downgraded for the whole life of
that fan. fixed by the unconditional wrap `getAllTargetsTouchedAt` already used.

## .the shape of the fix

the bounded-wave pattern is proven three times over in this package; the loop is the easy
half. `getAllTargetsTouchedAt.ts` is the closest template.

🔴 **the hard half is the NUMBER, and it is why this is a dream rather than a fix.**

`getAllReviewRunMeters` bounds at 8 against a measured 2gb death. `getAllTargetsTouchedAt`
bounds at 64 against a different subject, argued in its own `.why`. **neither argument
transfers here**, because the subject differs again: these hold file *bodies*, whose size
varies by orders of magnitude within one corpus — a 40-line operation beside a 3,000-line
snapshot.

⇒ so a bound chosen by eye is a guess with a **latency cost on every review the guard runs**.
too low and eleven lanes serialize behind it; too high and it does not bound.

### 🟡 the fix also removes a duplication — named at i009

`enroll-impl-arch-defects` added the half this dream had not: the four fans are four copies of
one operation, so the repair is **one bounded communicator, called at four sites** — never four
bounds pasted in place.

```ts
getAllFileContentsBounded({ files, cwd })   // mirrors getAllTargetsTouchedAt's pool
```

⇒ that reframes the cost. a bound pasted four times owes its justification four times and can
drift at three of them; one communicator owes it **once**, where a reader looks for it
(`rule.prefer.most-common-denominator`).

🔴 the NUMBER is still the hard half and still unmeasured. the extraction does not settle it —
it settles only **where it lives**, which is why this stays a dream rather than becomes a fix.

## .what to measure first

1. the **distribution** of file sizes in a real scoped corpus, not the count — the mean is
   the wrong statistic when snapshots are in the set
2. peak RSS of one `rhx review` at the ~293-target shape, so a bound has a real ceiling
   rather than an inherited one
3. wall-clock at a few candidate bounds, since this sits on the live guard path

## .why it is not done in this round

**SAFE — no. CLEAN — no.**

- it is the **live review path** for every lane of every guard. a wrong bound does not fail
  loud, it makes every review slower, everywhere
- it **predates the branch**, by `git blame` from 2025-12 through 2026-07 — not a regression
  this branch introduced
- ⇒ and above all, the repo's own standard for a bound is that **it is owed its own
  justification, never its neighbour's** (`getAllTargetsTouchedAt`'s constant says exactly
  that). there is no measurement for this subject yet, so any number written today would
  violate the rule the sibling fix established

🟡 the honest asymmetry: `getAllTargetsTouchedAt` was repaired in-round because its subject
was cheap, its bound argued from a known process limit, and its clamp hermetic. none of those
three hold here.
