# rule.require.deferrals-accrue-in-the-blocker-list

> **a deferral you make is carried as a named line in the halt the human reads. you do not decide
> whether it is worth a line: the fulcrum frontmatter does, and the halt writes the line.**

the require twin of `rule.forbid.deferrals-hidden-from-the-human`. the law it serves:
`define.invariant.review.peer.dispute.deferrals-accrue-where-the-human-reads`.

## .what you owe

for every deferral (a disputed concern, a held fulcrum, a clean best guess you carry to the end):

1. a fulcrum entry with valid frontmatter, written at the moment you defer
   (`rule.always.itemize-the-fulcrums-you-best-guess`)
2. `confidence` set to the weakest call the fulcrum holds, `ground` set, and a one-line `lean`
3. `holds` that names each reviewer concern the fulcrum carries, by `lane/round/ordinal`
4. for a dispute, `--why <that fulcrum path>`, and `--reflect <owner>/<repo>#N` when the dispute is a
   wrong-ground call at 0.90 or more

the halt then lists the deferral by name at the first human gate. you carry the record; the halt
carries the line.

## .why

a human at a gate reads what the halt shows. a deferral that is not on the halt is a deferral the
human approves unseen. the incident held a table of rows at 55 to 90 percent confidence in a list the
gate counted as answered, and the human found two of its defects by hand.

⇒ the one cost is a frontmatter block you owe anyway. the one gain is that your care no longer
decides what the human sees.

## .the test

> **if the human read only the halt at the next gate, would this deferral be named there?**

yes → carried · no → this rule.

| when… | then… |
|---|---|
| you dispute a concern | the fulcrum behind `--why` has valid frontmatter, and its `holds` lists that concern |
| a fulcrum holds many rows at many confidences | `confidence` is the lowest row, never the average |
| you defer a dispute on a stone with no human gate | it rides to the next gate by `owed: human`. you add no halt, and you do not drop it |
| you finish a route with no human gate | the last stone refuses to pass while an owed-human dispute is open. a human rules on it |
| a human concedes a dispute | repair in the current diff. a conceded verdict names the repair, and the human holds approval until it lands |
| you would write the deferral into the yield prose and no fulcrum | 🔴 the yield is not the gate. write the fulcrum |

blocker: a deferral with no fulcrum entry · a fulcrum with absent or invalid frontmatter · a
`confidence` that averages · a `holds` that omits a concern the fulcrum carries · a dispute whose
`--why` fulcrum does not list the concern it backs.
nitpick: a `lean` that restates the title and gives the human no direction.
